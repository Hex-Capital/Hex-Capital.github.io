# Taan.ai -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

The strongest route is a strategic sale to a video-editing or video-creation platform. Taan’s ability to align music to cuts and revise individual instruments or cues could reduce rework for editors, giving a platform a reason to own the capability if the savings hold in production. Taan reports 91.3% of events within ±500 ms in TimingBench, while its launch reports 73.2%. Those company-reported results show what to test, and the memo supplies no measured editor time savings. (The Idea, Key Risks)

Music for Video AI identifies Taan as its scoring provider. That integration gives Taan a starting point for a paid production pilot measuring time to an accepted score and revisions per edit. Reported seed financing suggests some capacity to run a pilot, though runway and pilot costs remain unknown. Taan says it licenses its training music, and Rohith lists two published patent applications. Corporate ownership and change-of-control rights remain unverified. The memo gives no support for an independent liquidity route. (Market & Traction, Defensibility)

The shared benchmark assumes a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, with 5% ownership diluted to 3% and no follow-ons. A 5× gross cash return requires $2.5 million within eight years, or about $83.3 million in distributable equity at 3%. The sale cases below are illustrative assumptions. Both assume an all-cash closing by year eight, an eligible holder selling their entire stake, and a pro rata waterfall after debt and deal adjustments with no senior preference reducing the holder’s share.

| Scenario | Proceeds bridge | Cash to holder |
|---|---|---:|
| Modest sale | $40M enterprise value less $5M in debt and deal adjustments yields $35M in distributable equity | $1.05M (2.1×) |
| Strong sale | $100M enterprise value less $10M in debt and deal adjustments yields $90M in distributable equity | $2.7M (5.4×) |
| Prolonged private | Cash is reinvested, with no distribution or eligible share sale by year eight | $0 |

If the holder can sell only 75% of their stake for cash in the strong case, proceeds fall to about $2.03 million. The memo provides no buyer valuation, transaction comparable, or Taan revenue with which to substantiate a clearing price. (Market & Traction, Key Facts)

### Countercase

Adobe already offers music generation inside Premiere, and ElevenLabs and Sonilo offer video-to-music APIs. A platform can use its own feature or procure a substitute. Taan’s benchmark uses short instrumental requests and relies on Taan reference stems for some events, leaving its advantage on real edits uncertain. Those facts weaken the reason to buy. The memo leaves both a structural block and a ceiling on credible proceeds unestablished, so neither Pass gate is triggered. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a video platform’s paid production pilot show that Taan saves enough editing time over the platform’s current option to justify a purchase that clears the holder’s proceeds hurdle? Buyer-verified savings across representative edits and a buyer-side estimate supporting at least $83.3 million in distributable equity under the benchmark would move the action to Reach out.
