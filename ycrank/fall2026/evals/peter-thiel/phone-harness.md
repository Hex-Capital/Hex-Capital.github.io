# Phone Harness -- Peter Thiel Evaluation

Phone Harness demonstrates that an agent can operate a personal iPhone without Xcode or a phone-side installation. What important belief does this overturn? The dossier identifies an implementation advantage over iphone-use’s XCTest runner, but no disputed proposition that named peers reject. A simpler setup is useful. It does not establish that Phone Harness has discovered something competitors misunderstand. I assign the secret criterion its absent-evidence default rather than inventing a contrarian thesis for the founder.

The decisive problem is ownership of the opportunity. Phone Harness supplies MIT-licensed controls in a market where Droidrun/Mobilerun and iphone-use already offer alternative implementations. Those alternatives differ technically; they nevertheless demonstrate that access to phone interfaces is reproducible. Open source can support a valuable business, but this dossier establishes no proprietary performance advantage, network effect, or scale economy in the paid layer. Persistent logins and device configurations could eventually create switching costs. Today that mechanism is explicitly an inference, and the initial customer segment remains broadly defined as developers building mobile agents.

The strongest argument against passing is that Shawn Pana might already understand how this market will be won. He previously led growth at Browser Use, including launches and developer partnerships. Approximately 4,000 GitHub stars and 403 forks show that his current project has attracted developer attention, while self-service cloud signup supplies a direct route from discovery to purchase. Removing installation work could make Phone Harness the default starting point for mobile-agent developers. This is the strongest dimension of the company. But repository attention has no documented conversion into recurring paid workloads, and the September 16 telemetry report—covering scripts, OCR output, and unredacted typing arguments—raises a specific trust obstacle when selling persistent access to customer phones. Current remediation remains unverified.

The founding structure is unknown rather than adverse. A single founder does not create a co-founder alignment problem, and there is no evidence of part-time commitment, outsourced core engineering, excessive secondary liquidity, or investor-dominated ownership. Equally, the dossier does not establish economic exposure or employment status, so I retain the founding criterion’s default. My unstated preference for operator-led company formation gives Pana’s background relevance, principally under distribution; it does not establish the missing ownership facts. Phone Harness also participates in the broader AI infrastructure pattern, but the dossier has yet to identify an essential production bottleneck it uniquely controls.

There is one intelligible revenue engine: hosted phone usage at $0.05 per minute, with dedicated devices priced separately. That is $3 per occupied phone-hour. Reaching $100 million in annual revenue would require roughly 33 million billable phone-hours at the published rate, before considering discounts. This is an illustrative scale requirement, not a forecast. The dossier provides neither device economics nor customer expansion evidence supporting it. Free personal-device use also gives developers an alternative to buying cloud minutes. Published pricing earns some credit; a power-law outcome remains unsupported.

I would pass with my own money. The adverse monopoly evidence triggers a primary gate, and the strongest known-exception tests do not remove it: there is no independently demonstrated technical breakthrough or realized customer economics showing that Phone Harness can capture exceptional value despite reproducible controls. The product’s installation advantage and developer reach make the bull case credible enough to revisit. My flip condition is a verified six-month paid cohort in one defined workflow demonstrating that proprietary accumulated state produces materially better task completion than a fresh competing harness. That would establish a reason customers become progressively harder to take away.
