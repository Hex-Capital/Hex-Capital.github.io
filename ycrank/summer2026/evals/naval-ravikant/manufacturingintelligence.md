# HERA -- Naval Ravikant Evaluation

Meera Patel and Noelle So combine industrial engineering, data science, factory exposure, and 200-plus customer conversations, yet HERA remains a single-customer tool whose next user adds little value for the last. HERA is specific-knowledge-heavy but network-light. That signature produces a credible industrial software business, not yet the asymmetric architecture that turns one angel check into 10,000x.

Stack Overflow is the high rung for non-trainable specific knowledge: practicing developers built for developers after living the problem publicly. HERA shares the mechanism through Patel’s TE Connectivity and Pfizer projects, So’s industrial-park water facility adding 40 L/s, and both founders’ industrial-engineering training. HERA diverges because internships and 200 interviews are exposure, not mastery demonstrated by a shipped artifact; no public repository, accuracy benchmark, or years-long drawing-review record proves that Patel and So know something F4, Axial, NexCAD, and RapidDraft cannot learn.

Perplexity’s low-rung mechanism on network value is single-sided utility: one user’s query does not automatically improve the next user’s experience. HERA inherits that weakness more severely. HERA’s company-specific rules and drawing-error records might compound inside one factory, but NDAs, plant-specific requirements, and the promise that prints never leave customer walls prevent those records from becoming a shared network. More installations create more accounts, not necessarily more value per account.

A free under-NDA review of 20 drawings, a founder visit, and deployment within one week reveal HERA’s leverage structure over the next 18 months. HERA uses code to perform the work, but labor and permission distribute it: demos, security review, rule configuration, and factory-by-factory trust. Each deployment may become cheaper as the product improves, yet customer acquisition does not replicate at zero marginal cost. Code inside the product is not the same as permissionless code distribution.

Aerospace, medical devices, semiconductors, pressure vessels, and custom machinery give HERA more pivot-room than a one-industry niche. HERA would move into the top market tier if one common rules engine could expand from drawing review into CAD release, supplier quality, first-article inspection, and revision control without restarting the enterprise sale. The current evidence points the other way: no TAM is published, standards differ, customer rules require maintenance, and NexCAD already owns CAD integration while RapidDraft already retains review decisions across revisions.

Noelle So built physical infrastructure for her family’s industrial park, while Meera Patel combined manufacturing projects with healthcare-ML research; HERA therefore clears intelligence and energy more convincingly than pedigree-only founders. Twitter’s founder rung was stronger because Jack Dorsey had shipped and Ev Williams had completed Blogger’s exit cleanly, giving an observable integrity and execution history. HERA’s best-friend co-founder relationship and named Founder/CTO roles avoid the unresolved-authority red flag, but HERA offers too little public history to establish the ten-year-partner test rather than merely leave it unanswered.

F4, Axial, NexCAD, and RapidDraft already attack overlapping drawing checks, so my falsifiable forecast is: **by July 2029, HERA will have narrowed into an on-premises compliance workflow for one regulated manufacturing vertical and reached $1 million–$3 million ARR, rather than becoming a horizontal drawing-intelligence network.** HERA’s domain knowledge can win a demanding niche; HERA’s customer-isolated data and founder-led distribution constrain compounding beyond it.

Perplexity is the strongest objection: a single-sided product can still become enormous when specific knowledge, category timing, and permissionless software distribution overwhelm the missing network. HERA has real ingredients for that exception—200-plus interviews, So’s factory experience, source-traced findings, and a 2026 vision-model step-change reported at 85%–95% extraction accuracy. HERA does not yet complete the Perplexity pattern: four competitors are already visible, no precision or recall is published, and every pilot appears to require NDA-bound founder involvement. I would flip to Invest if HERA demonstrated greater than 90% recall across customer drawings and acquired 100 factories through a repeatable self-serve or partner-free motion.
