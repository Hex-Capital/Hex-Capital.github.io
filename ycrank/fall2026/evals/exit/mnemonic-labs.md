# Mnemonic Labs -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

The strongest exit route is a strategic sale to a quantitative trading firm. The memo records a November launch and prior quant roles for both founders. Mnemonic says it built its training pipeline and model architecture in under six weeks and had more than 25 TB of tokens in its launch training run. A trading firm could have a reason to buy the model if it delivers repeatable net trading profits beyond what the firm can achieve internally. The team’s experience and reported build speed support a live test as the next milestone. The memo lists $125,000 in reported YC financing and leaves the test’s capital needs and runway unresolved. (*The Idea*, *Market & Traction*, *Founders & Team*, *Key Risks*)

The shared benchmark assumes a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, with ownership diluted from 5% to 3% and no follow-ons. The eight-year 5× objective requires $2.5 million in cash, or about $83.3 million in distributable equity value. The memo supplies no acquisition pricing evidence, so the following prices are illustrative. Assume a sale within eight years, with no net debt, deal adjustments, or senior claims, and an eligible holder selling their entire stake for cash at closing. A **modest sale** at $50 million enterprise value yields $50 million in distributable equity and $1.5 million to the holder (3×). A **strong sale** at $100 million yields $100 million in distributable equity and $3 million to the holder (6×). If 20% of the holder’s consideration rolls into buyer equity, cash at closing falls to $2.4 million (4.8×). In the **prolonged-private case**, trading profits are reinvested and the holder realizes $0 within eight years. The memo identifies a possible market-data licensing issue, leaves transfer permissions unverified, and supplies no evidence of an independent route to holder liquidity. (*The Idea*, *Market & Traction*, *Key Risks*, *Key Facts*)

### Countercase

A quant firm could build its own model using published market-event pretraining methods or choose a competitor with live deployment. Prodigy Research and Standard Signal report trading live, while Mnemonic disclosed training scale and left live returns and execution costs unquantified. This weakens the case for paying an acquisition premium. The Pass gate remains unmet because the evidence leaves open whether buyers have a ready substitute and whether a credible sale could clear the proceeds hurdle. (*Defensibility*, *Market & Traction*, *Key Risks*)

### Decisive diligence question

Can Mnemonic demonstrate repeatable live trading profits above a prospective buyer’s existing strategies at the capital level that buyer would deploy? A buyer-validated test that measures the gain after fees, slippage, and market impact and supports a purchase price above the proceeds hurdle after deal adjustments would move the action to Reach out.
