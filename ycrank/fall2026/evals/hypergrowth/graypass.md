# GrayPass -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Medium

### Company case

Assuming strong product-market fit, GrayPass could serve application teams that need to verify who controls a session before a payout, data export, or permission change. The documented decision API and signed proofs bound to specific actions are reusable across applications. That supports a plausible path to buyers in other markets, although the memo leaves open how much biometric consent and data handling would need to change. The evaluation form and single-action shadow rollout give buyers a repeatable starting point. The path from discovery through purchase and production enforcement without substantial vendor help remains unproven. (The Idea, Key Risks)

The listed $500 monthly starting price and annual contract terms support recurring billing for an ongoing authorization workflow. Customers could expand usage by protecting more actions, and embedded policies and customer-specific enrollment could support retention. The terms describe metered allowances, so whether higher usage raises the bill remains uncertain. GrayPass separately reported $50,000 in October 2026 revenue as of launch and more than 900,000 sessions processed across customer applications. The revenue is an early commercial signal for a two-person team, with no comparable periods or breakdown of recurring versus evaluation revenue. The session count indicates use, with billing status unspecified. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Facts, Market & Traction, Founders & Team)

For a buyer paying the listed starting price, monthly contribution would start at $500 and subtract decision processing, behavioral-template handling where enabled, ongoing support, and an allocation of acquisition and onboarding effort. Reusing the same API and starting with one action in shadow mode could limit vendor effort. The memo gives no workload or labor measurements that establish positive contribution. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Countercase

Shadow-mode evaluations could generate substantial activity while few customers move into paid enforcement. Customers must wire each protected action into server-side authorization, the core decision API is in beta, and experimental behavioral signals are limited to sandbox and shadow evaluation. GrayPass’s Illinois policy also requires affirmative consent and specifies retention and deletion procedures. These are concrete rollout constraints. The structural downside gate remains open because the memo shows no required market-by-market rebuild or unavoidable vendor work that would make growth uneconomic. (Key Risks, Market & Traction)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a cohort of target buyers in more than one market move from evaluation to paid production enforcement using the same API, with limited consent changes and little GrayPass staff time? Dated rollout records showing paid activation, the changes each deployment required, and vendor hours would answer it. Repeatable paid rollouts under those conditions would move the action to Reach out. Evidence that deployments require substantial rebuilding by market would move it to Pass.
