# hiloop -- Cyan Banister Evaluation

Move the clock to 2032. Models are not merely answering questions; they are continuously proposing experiments, allocating compute, testing changes, and preserving every failed branch so another agent can resume from it. That future requires trustworthy evaluations, reproducible execution, and durable experiment lineage. hiloop has produced a real leak from that world: 4,188 agent-directed trials on 50 B200 GPUs in two days, followed by 25 paired confirmation runs with interleaved controls. This is more meaningful than the $16.61 billion MLOps forecast because it shows the machinery operating. The distance remaining is equally concrete: the evidence comes from one narrow autoresearch campaign, request-time secret delivery is unfinished, and no external customer has demonstrated that the loop generalizes.

Brar and Boser understand the machinery. They worked together on ML, post-training, and platform systems at Reducto; Brar also led ML infrastructure at DynamoAI, while Boser accumulated engineering experience across Discord, Sentropy, and SoFi. That is credible domain tuition and a complementary CEO/CTO arrangement. It is not yet the deeper first-believer proof I look for: the dossier contains no dated pre-hiloop experiments, prior independent artifacts with meaningful adoption, or evidence that either founder recruited a difficult technical community before the market became fashionable. Their 4,188-run campaign proves execution, but it cannot also serve as proof that the obsession preceded the company.

The broken system is recognizable but insufficiently decoded. Researchers stitch together homegrown evaluations, compute provisioning, traces, and restoration logic; hiloop unifies those pieces into forkable campaigns. But whose rent disappears? How many researcher-weeks or GPU-dollars are wasted? Who gains new economic agency rather than a nicer workflow? The 12.54% NanoGPT runtime improvement hints at measurable value, yet the dossier does not quantify the underlying institutional cost or show that a user can now do something previously withheld by a gatekeeper.

The strongest bull case is that the founders have already built the concealed infrastructure beneath the visible autoresearch spectacle. Snapshottable compute, harness-independent lineage, and queryable records of model calls, tools, tokens, and costs could become the control plane for many research domains. Failed paths and verified outcomes might eventually compound into proprietary data. The pre-revenue exception would be justified if a functioning prototype were paired with signed external proofs and unusually deep founder obsession. Here the prototype qualifies; the other two conditions do not. Erdos Miller’s logo has no disclosed engagement, and the only documented costly behavior is hiloop spending its own compute.

The bear case stings because Prime Intellect already reports more than 10,000 beta training jobs and raised a $130 million Series A in July 2026. OpenPipe/CoreWeave and Runloop can also combine environments, evaluations, training, and orchestration. hiloop’s published campaign depended on Claude Fable 5, GPT-5.5 variants, and scarce Nvidia accelerators, so today it controls the experiment layer but not the models or compute beneath it. Harness agnosticism limits lock-in risk, while complete lineage is a plausible wedge, but neither is yet a structural barrier. Recording every tool action and secret-bearing run also creates a data-custody obligation; incomplete request-time secret delivery is an important warning, even if it is not yet a severe privacy violation.

I would not write the personal check today. This is a technically serious, thesis-adjacent company caught between a compelling artifact and an unproven customer truth. The fashionable-AI red flag is softened by the working system and rigorous controls, but platform convergence and absent repeat usage keep hiloop at Neutral. One verifiable condition flips me: two unaffiliated customers each pay for and repeat at least three hiloop campaigns in their own clouds. That would show the lineage layer changes external behavior rather than merely producing an impressive research demonstration.
