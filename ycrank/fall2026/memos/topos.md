## The Idea

- **Problem:** [Inferred]: Biology researchers assessing open-ended hypotheses rely on literature review and expert judgment, which become harder to apply consistently as the number of plausible explanations grows ([YC Swarm AI directory](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/industry/Swarm%20AI), [Google DeepMind, May 2026](https://deepmind.google/blog/co-scientist-a-multi-agent-ai-partner-to-accelerate-research/)).
- **Approach:** Topos says its Egregore harness can coordinate thousands of agents on one research task, with its broader research program focused on how agents communicate, formalize problems, and check each other’s reasoning ([Egregore research beta](https://toposlab.com/egregore/), [YC Swarm AI directory](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/industry/Swarm%20AI)).
- **Differentiation:** Topos emphasizes formalizing and checking reasoning, Aster emphasizes agents that define and pursue open-ended research problems, Acyclic Labs emphasizes general-purpose swarm infrastructure and benchmarks, and Edison Scientific’s Kosmos combines literature search and data analysis in a research workflow ([Topos](https://toposlab.com/), [YC Aster profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/asterlab), [Acyclic Labs](https://acyclic.dev/), [Edison Scientific](https://edisonscientific.com/news/announcing-kosmos)).
- **Business Model:** No public data found.
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** Topos recruits a limited group of researchers through an Egregore access-request form that asks for their affiliation and intended research task ([Egregore research beta](https://toposlab.com/egregore/), accessed November 2026).

## Key Risks

- **Swarm performance:** [Inferred]: Coordination overhead could limit Egregore on sequential research tasks, given Google Research’s finding that agent coordination improved parallelizable tasks but degraded performance on sequential ones ([Egregore research beta](https://toposlab.com/egregore/), [Google Research, January 2026](https://research.google/blog/towards-a-science-of-scaling-agent-systems-when-and-why-agent-systems-work/)).
- **Empirical validation:** [Inferred]: Agents can check reasoning within a formal specification, but biological conclusions still require empirical validation, as illustrated by Google DeepMind’s use of expert-led experiments to test outputs from Co-Scientist ([YC Swarm AI directory](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/industry/Swarm%20AI), [Google DeepMind, May 2026](https://deepmind.google/blog/co-scientist-a-multi-agent-ai-partner-to-accelerate-research/)).
- **Research workflow competition:** [Inferred]: Edison’s agreement to deploy Kosmos across Pfizer’s R&D pipeline gives a competing tool access to scientists and internal data in Topos’s stated initial field ([Edison Scientific, October 2026](https://edisonscientific.com/news/edison-scientific-announces-agreement-with-pfizer-to-accelerate-research-and-development), [YC Swarm AI directory](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/industry/Swarm%20AI)).
- **Name collision:** Dealroom combines `toposlab.com` and the YC batch with a geospatial business description, creating a risk of misattributing financing or acquisition records from the separate Topos acquired by LandGate ([Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/topos_3), [LandGate, December 2025](https://www.landgate.com/news/landgate-corp-acquires-topos), [Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/topos)).
