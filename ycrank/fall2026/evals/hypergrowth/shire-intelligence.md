# Shire Intelligence -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path B — Repeatable sales or partner distribution with account expansion.** The first buyer is a full-service restaurant operator who needs to spot tables awaiting service or clearance and balance server workloads during busy shifts. Shire’s proposed workflow uses existing CCTV to update table status and alert staff. The memo documents a demo offering and a reseller inquiry path. If a POS distributor can sell and activate the same workflow across restaurants, a group could start with one location and expand to others. That distribution and expansion pattern remains a scenario. (The Idea)

Shire reports 13 live restaurants at launch and a 12% average reduction in table-turn time. The deployments are an early sign that operators will try the workflow. Paid status and repeat usage are unknown, and the reported improvement lacks a disclosed baseline or measurement window. Mathews Restaurant’s reported savings came from payment processing, leaving the value attributable to camera alerts unresolved. (Market & Traction, Key Risks)

The same buyer and recurring job could plausibly travel. A second market would test whether Shire can reuse its camera setup and staff workflow with local POS systems and footage-handling requirements. The memo leaves cross-market demand, channel conversion, retention and marginal deployment costs open. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Countercase

Each restaurant may need a camera coverage review, site-specific calibration and POS integration before staff can rely on the alerts. Shire’s prototype reported inconsistent classifications, and Toast’s bundled table status already covers part of the host workflow. If each rollout adds substantial setup labor, video-processing costs and ongoing support, Shire could face both limited willingness to pay and weak contribution per site. The evidence identifies those risks. Their effect on current deployments remains unmeasured, so the structural Pass gate is unproven. (The Idea, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a POS reseller bring a paid cohort of independent full-service restaurants live in a second geography using the same onboarding process? I’d move to Reach out if deployment records showed that the reseller could activate the sites with limited founder involvement, staff kept using alerts across normal shifts, and site revenue covered incremental reseller, setup, video-processing and support costs. I’d move to Pass if those rollouts showed that every site required substantial custom work with no credible way to reduce it.
