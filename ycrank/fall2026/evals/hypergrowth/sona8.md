# Sona8 -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Medium

### Company case

**Path B: Repeatable sales or partner distribution with account expansion.** Consulting teams and enterprise transformation offices need to map how employees actually work before changing workflows. s.Oliver Group’s CEO office says it used Sona8 to map processes and uncover differences in teams’ planning requirements. Sona8 separately reports that a signed top-three consultancy is using the product to map client processes and that it has completed more than 10,000 employee interviews. The interview count shows reported usage volume, while the memo gives no realized revenue or account-level usage history. (*The Idea*, *Market & Traction*)

If the consultancy can reuse the interview-to-map workflow across clients, each engagement could bring in a new buyer. Within an account, post-rollout interviews and additional workflows could expand usage under the stated platform-plus-usage pricing model. A second-country rollout assumes the same buyer and core workflow, with local-language interview quality and employee consultation, privacy, and security requirements to verify. The memo documents multilingual capability but gives no comparable buying evidence across countries. (*The Idea*, *Key Risks*)

### Countercase

Sona8’s first customers came through the founders’ network, and consulting demand may end when a project closes. Each new rollout could require fresh employee approvals and hands-on map validation, while interview and query volume could add voice, compute, and support costs. The memo gives no repeat paid workloads or measured rollout costs. That leaves a specific test for Watch. The Pass gate requires observed evidence of a structural ceiling or proportional scaling costs. (*The Idea*, *Market & Traction*, *Key Risks*)

### Decisive diligence question

Can the signed consultancy bring a second client live on a paid deployment in a new country using the same rollout process? A paid order, completed interviews, and a workflow map the client uses, with documented setup and support hours and voice and compute costs, would answer it. Move to Reach out if the rollout shows limited founder involvement and delivery costs that fit within the fees. Stay on Watch if payment, live use, or rollout effort remains unresolved.
