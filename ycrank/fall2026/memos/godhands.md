## The Idea

- **Problem:** Operations, sales and support teams lose knowledge of undocumented exceptions when experienced employees leave, then reconstruct workflows through handover documents, SOPs and shadowing ([Launch YC, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Vn7-godhands-agents-that-learn-by-watching-your-best-people)).
- **Approach:** A Mac or Windows app captures text and interface structure from administrator-approved apps without requiring API integrations for capture, builds source-linked playbooks, and makes them available to employees and agents ([GodHands Security](https://godhands.ai/security), [Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Vn7-godhands-agents-that-learn-by-watching-your-best-people)).
- **Differentiation:** GodHands uses the same source-linked playbooks for employee handovers and agent execution, while Scribe pairs guide authoring with background optimization, Skan AI models workflows in an enterprise context graph, and Fluency emphasizes automation opportunities and ROI measurement ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Vn7-godhands-agents-that-learn-by-watching-your-best-people), [Scribe](https://scribe.com/library/100-million-arr-press-release), [Skan AI](https://www.skan.ai/in-the-news/skan-ai-raises-series-c-announcement), [Fluency](https://usefluency.com/blog/fluency-raises-6m-seed-accel)).
- **Business Model:** The standard terms specify a monthly subscription with fees quoted in writing or on an order form, with user and usage limits set by the plan ([GodHands Terms, Oct 2026](https://godhands.ai/terms)).
- **TAM/SAM:** The adjacent global task-mining software market is estimated at $420M in 2026, with a forecast 32.0% CAGR through 2034, and no public SAM estimate was found for GodHands’ specific segment ([Stratistics MRC, 2026](https://www.strategymrc.com/report/task-mining-software-market)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** The company takes demo bookings and uses Launch YC to solicit introductions to heads of operations, support executives and private-equity operating partners ([GodHands website](https://godhands.ai/), [Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Vn7-godhands-agents-that-learn-by-watching-your-best-people)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** GodHands retains customer-specific playbooks and learned knowledge after raw recordings expire, with source links and per-customer data isolation ([GodHands Security](https://godhands.ai/security), [GodHands Privacy Policy](https://godhands.ai/privacy)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Reconstructing a customer’s accumulated workflow exceptions elsewhere could create switching costs, but no public retention or migration evidence quantifies that effect ([GodHands Security](https://godhands.ai/security)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** Scribe already offers background workflow capture and an interface for agents to retrieve that context, and Skan AI already sells workflow discovery and execution products ([Scribe](https://scribe.com/library/100-million-arr-press-release), [Skan AI](https://www.skan.ai/in-the-news/skan-ai-raises-series-c-announcement)).

## Key Risks

- **Employee data capture:** Monitoring text displayed in work apps can expose sensitive information, and GodHands documents app allowlists, on-device redaction, configurable retention and on-premises deployment ([GodHands Security](https://godhands.ai/security)).
- **Agent execution reliability:** [Inferred]: Incorrectly learned exceptions could cause agents to propose the wrong changes in connected apps, and GodHands requires users to review and approve changes or messages before execution ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Vn7-godhands-agents-that-learn-by-watching-your-best-people), [GodHands Privacy Policy](https://godhands.ai/privacy)).
- **Substitution:** [Inferred]: Buyers may meet overlapping needs within Scribe or Skan AI, which already offer workflow observation and agent products ([Scribe](https://scribe.com/library/100-million-arr-press-release), [Skan AI](https://www.skan.ai/in-the-news/skan-ai-raises-series-c-announcement)).
- **Name ambiguity:** The GodHands name also appears in the unrelated GodHands Clinic filing, while the YC listing identifies this startup by godhands.ai and its founders ([Norwalk Patriot legal notice](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/57df855ff7e0ab74193e26a8/t/644ad7449ad3f9674b7c06f4/1682626375223/2023-04-28.pdf), [YC directory](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/godhands)).
