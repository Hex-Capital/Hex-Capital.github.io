## The Idea

- **Problem:** Employees implementing workplace AI rely on colleagues who discover useful workflows, but those colleagues must balance teaching with their existing jobs, limiting continued knowledge sharing ([Kevin Law, LinkedIn, accessed Nov 5, 2026, via search snippet](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kvnlw)).
- **Approach:** Coach operates through Slack and Teams, joins calls to guide employees through tasks, adapts to company tools and workflows, and incorporates employee walkthroughs into subsequent guidance ([YC, via search snippet](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/championeffect-inc)).
- **Differentiation:** ChampionEffect emphasizes redistributing internal champions’ practical knowledge, whereas Section combines coaching with transformation planning and adoption measurement, and Workera emphasizes skills assessment and development ([ChampionEffect](https://www.linkedin.com/company/championeffect), [Section](https://www.sectionai.com/), [Workera](https://www.workera.ai/blog/series-b)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Employer-paid coaching contracts are a possible monetization path given the enterprise positioning and signed customer, but public pricing and billing structure: No public data found ([ChampionEffect](https://www.linkedin.com/company/championeffect), [founder announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/emresimdi_first-customer-new-investor-and-we-launched-activity-7524203318890385408-vjmn)).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment; the broader AI-powered corporate-training market is estimated at $7.49B in 2026, reaching $18.19B in 2031 at 19.43% CAGR, and includes applications beyond teaching employees to use AI ([Mordor Intelligence, 2026](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/ai-powered-corporate-training-market)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Founders solicit conversations through LinkedIn and website call bookings, and Emre Simdi attributes the first customer’s purchase to outreach around the company’s employee-enablement mission ([Kevin Law, via search snippet](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kvnlw), [Emre Simdi](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/emresimdi_first-customer-new-investor-and-we-launched-activity-7524203318890385408-vjmn)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** The company describes collecting company-specific workflow knowledge from employees and reusing it in coaching, but public evidence of exclusive data rights or measured learning advantages: No public data found ([ChampionEffect](https://www.linkedin.com/company/championeffect)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Accumulated company-specific walkthroughs could improve contextual guidance and create migration costs, although the claimed learning mechanism lacks published comparative results ([YC, via search snippet](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/championeffect-inc)).
- **Market structure:** [Inferred]: No structural barrier identified at this stage, given Section’s existing personalized workplace-AI coaching offering ([Section](https://www.sectionai.com/)).
- **Commoditization risk:** [Inferred]: Enterprise-learning vendors could combine coaching, workflow libraries, and collaboration integrations, with Section already offering coaching and Arist offering employee-interview agents and organizational-data analysis; implementation difficulty is not publicly quantified ([Section](https://www.sectionai.com/), [Arist](https://arist.com/)).
