## The Idea

- **Problem:** Forward-deployed and implementation engineers must reconstruct customer requirements from GitHub, support tickets, and meeting recordings, which the founders say slows deployments ([Launch YC, Nov 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/XPD-openscaffold-superagent-for-deployment-teams)).
- **Approach:** OpenScaffold turns calls, emails, and tickets into a continuously updated, source-cited customer plan, briefs coding agents through Model Context Protocol (MCP), checks changes against customer rules, and proposes reusable playbooks for human approval ([OpenScaffold product and FAQ](https://openscaffold.ai/)).
- **Differentiation:** OpenScaffold passes deployment-specific requirements into existing coding agents and reviews their work against those requirements, while Perspective AI centers on discovery interviews, Rocketlane Nitro automates professional-services delivery, and Harness Cosmos uses codebase context to take engineering tickets through pull requests ([OpenScaffold](https://openscaffold.ai/), [Perspective AI](https://s.getperspective.ai/blog/ai-deployment-tools-forward-deployed-engineering-teams-2026), [Rocketlane Nitro](https://www.rocketlane.com/nitro-agents), [Harness Cosmos](https://www.harness.io/press-and-news/harness-acquires-augment-code)).
- **Business Model:** The public $0 plan includes source connectors, coding-agent briefings, review checks, and playbooks, with email inquiries for larger teams, single sign-on, or contracts ([OpenScaffold Pricing, Nov 2026](https://openscaffold.ai/pricing)).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** The company offers self-serve signup and founder calls through Launch YC, and Knagaram identified heads of forward-deployed engineering and implementation as buyer roles ([OpenScaffold Pricing](https://openscaffold.ai/pricing), [Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/XPD-openscaffold-superagent-for-deployment-teams), [Knagaram on X, Nov 2026, via Teapawt](https://teapawt.net/RushilKnagaram/status/2122721020914319399)).

## Key Risks

- **Technical feasibility:** Knagaram said that in a pilot test the product failed to distinguish customer requests from the deployment team’s requests 82% of the time, while the product provides source citations and a review workflow for checking requirements ([Knagaram on X, Nov 17, 2026, via Teapawt](https://teapawt.net/RushilKnagaram), [OpenScaffold product](https://openscaffold.ai/)).
- **Customer data access:** The product ingests customer communications and sends document text to a model subprocessor, while the privacy policy describes workspace isolation, per-deployment agent access, and checks for customer data in reusable playbooks ([OpenScaffold Privacy Policy, Oct 2026](https://openscaffold.ai/privacy)).
- **Platform substitution:** [Inferred]: Teams using Harness Cosmos may consolidate context and code review there, while OpenScaffold supports multiple coding agents and checks work against customer-specific requirements ([Harness, Oct 2026](https://www.harness.io/press-and-news/harness-acquires-augment-code), [OpenScaffold FAQ](https://openscaffold.ai/#faq)).
- **Name ambiguity:** GitHub’s similarly named Open Scaffold Labs publishes a separate portfolio spanning law practice, payroll, and fire-service software ([GitHub organization](https://github.com/Open-Scaffold-Labs), [YC OpenScaffold profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/openscaffold)).
