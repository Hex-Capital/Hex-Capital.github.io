# Forward -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path B — New Economies.** Commercial debt advisors manually contact lenders through existing relationships and can spend time pursuing funds whose mandates have changed. Forward says it automates financing briefs, mandate matching, and submission follow-up. The CEO of Capital Partners reports that Forward introduced unfamiliar lenders and helped secure term sheets. That is evidence of broader lender reach on an existing assignment. The market-opening hypothesis is that lower labor costs could make smaller financing assignments viable for advisors who previously declined them. The memo gives no comparative labor or closing data for that segment. (The Idea, Market & Traction)

Forward reports that 100 U.S. funds signed its fee agreement within three months. At pre-seed, those agreements show willingness to accept a fee contingent on funding. The reported annualized and contracted revenue figures leave cash collections unspecified. The founders bring private-credit legal, fund diligence, and AI workflow experience, and the team reports qualifying 63 advisory firms before starting its controlled rollout. (Market & Traction, Key Facts, Founders & Team, Key Risks)

At the stated 25-basis-point fee, a hypothetical $1 million loan that funds and pays the fee yields $2,500 in gross revenue. For smaller loans to work at that price, revenue per funded loan must cover lender screening, human review, compliance, and follow-up across both funded and unsuccessful submissions. Funded conversion and delivery costs remain unknown. (The Idea, Key Risks, Key Facts)

### Countercase

Forward may be making existing brokerage assignments easier to service. With equivalent models, advisors could use competing discovery tools or contact lenders directly. Forward’s strongest evidence of protection is its reported exclusivity with one Southern California advisor after the first transaction. The scope and duration of that agreement are unknown, and the memo reports no measured improvement in matching as lenders join. A single customer testimonial and conditional fee agreements leave both market opening and buyer preference under model parity unresolved. Both conditions are testable, so the decision gate supports Watch. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can Forward profitably fund repeat loans that independent advisors previously declined as uneconomic, while lenders keep paying Forward’s 25-basis-point fee when they have a functionally equivalent AI alternative? A pilot outside the exclusive advisor relationship would answer this by comparing full delivery cost per funded loan with the manual workflow, counting failed submissions, and tracking fee collections when lenders have that alternative. Repeat funded loans with positive contribution and continued lender payments would move the action to Reach out. Representative evidence that those loans remain uneconomic or that lenders switch and preserve the outcome would move it to Pass.
