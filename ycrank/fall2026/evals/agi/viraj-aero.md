# Viraj Aero -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path A — Scarce complements.** If cheap frontier intelligence accelerates data center construction, capacity that lets developers open sites sooner becomes more valuable. Viraj Aero’s strongest case is at sites where both grid delays and cooling-water limits constrain commissioning. The memo documents developers procuring on-site gas generation to bridge grid delays. Viraj claims GENA can also supply cooling water recovered from its exhaust and lists specifications of 10 MW, 45% electrical efficiency, and 2 ppm NOx. The inference is that the combined system could bring a site online sooner if gas supply and air approvals are available and the unit exports enough water after steam reinjection to relieve the site’s constraint. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

Viraj reports a patent on its compact condenser, which could protect the integrated design if the patent covers practical alternatives. The company reports more than 1.3 GW of interest under letters of intent, with undisclosed terms, and one paying engine manufacturer whose contract scope is unclear. The aviation ground test and subsequent full-scale prototype integration show relevant hardware execution, and the founders bring propulsion and power-project experience. GENA’s first unit was reported under construction, leaving continuous-duty performance at a data center unverified. That gap keeps confidence low. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Founders & Team, Key Risks)

A 10 MW module gives Viraj a concrete unit to replicate. The economic case depends on whether earlier commissioning and avoided cooling-water costs outweigh installed equipment costs, fuel, maintenance, and backup at realistic utilization. The memo provides no measured continuous-duty performance or installed costs to establish that bridge. (The Idea, Key Risks)

### Countercase

A developer could pair Boom’s turbine or VoltaGrid’s modular engines with a separate cooling solution. If that package meets the same schedule, water, and permitting requirements at comparable cost, buyers can bypass Viraj’s condenser even if the patent protects its design. The memo establishes credible alternatives for on-site power and leaves their performance at a site constrained by both power and water untested. That unresolved comparison warrants Watch. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Will an independent developer at a site constrained by both grid access and cooling water commit to a paid deployment after a continuous-duty trial shows that GENA meets the site’s power, net usable water, and emissions requirements with a commissioning or cost advantage over the best available power-and-cooling package? A paid commitment on those results would move the action to Reach out and make the durability of that advantage the focus of the conversation. Evidence that a readily available substitute matches the site outcome at comparable cost and schedule would close the path and move the action to Pass. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)
