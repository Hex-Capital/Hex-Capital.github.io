## The Idea

- **Problem:** Regulated companies building agents that can access sensitive data rely on security findings and remediation tickets, while Cobalt reports that roughly two-thirds of high-risk AI findings remain unresolved. ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/W5k-armorer-labs-continuous-security-verification-for-ai-agents-in-regulated-industries), [Cobalt, 2026](https://resource.cobalt.io/ai-pentesting-pulse-report-2026))
- **Approach:** Armorer maps agent permissions, tests for unsafe behavior, generates fixes for engineer approval after checking existing workflows, and uses a CPU-based runtime guard to allow, hold, or deny actions. ([Armorer Labs](https://armorerlabs.com/), [Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/armorer-labs))
- **Differentiation:** Armorer emphasizes workflow-tested fixes, while Straiker markets separate testing and runtime products, Zenity emphasizes agent identity and governance across platforms, Noma inventories AI assets across the development lifecycle, and Check Point offers standalone runtime screening. ([Armorer Labs](https://armorerlabs.com/), [Straiker](https://www.straiker.ai/blog/straiker-raises-64m-series-a-to-secure-the-agentic-workforce), [Zenity](https://zenity.io/), [Noma](https://noma.security/blog/introducing-noma-the-unified-platform-for-ai-security), [Check Point](https://docs.lakera.ai/guard))
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Negotiated enterprise contracts are the likely monetization path given the demo-led sales process and terms that place commercial details in signed customer agreements. ([Armorer Labs](https://armorerlabs.com/), [Terms of Service](https://armorerlabs.com/terms))
- **TAM/SAM:** Grand View Research projects the global agentic AI security market at $1.8B in 2026 and $17.8B in 2033, with 38.9% CAGR, while the serviceable segment for regulated companies building their own agents remains unquantified. ([Grand View Research, 2026](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/agentic-ai-security-market-report))
- **GTM / Distribution:** Armorer solicits demos from security and engineering leaders through its website and YC launch, and publishes security research and an email threat-intelligence briefing. ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/W5k-armorer-labs-continuous-security-verification-for-ai-agents-in-regulated-industries), [Armorer Labs Threat Intel](https://armorerlabs.com/threat-intel))

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Armorer reports 98% accuracy at 1.9 ms median latency versus JEV’s 80% at roughly 168 ms in a company-run comparison of 50 documents. ([Armorer Labs on LinkedIn, Oct 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/company/armorer-labs/))
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Customer-specific regression tests and approval histories could create switching costs as customers accumulate them, with no public retention data to establish that effect. ([Armorer Labs Trust Center](https://armorerlabs.com/trust))
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** NVIDIA already offers open-source runtime policy enforcement, and Check Point sells standalone agent guardrails, giving buyers alternatives for runtime controls. ([NVIDIA, Mar 2026](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/run-autonomous-self-evolving-agents-more-safely-with-nvidias-openshell/), [Check Point](https://docs.lakera.ai/guard))

## Key Risks

- **Benchmark transfer:** [Inferred]: The 50-document JEV comparison leaves production detection accuracy and latency across customer workloads uncertain. ([Armorer Labs on LinkedIn, Oct 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/company/armorer-labs/))
- **Data residency:** [Inferred]: Customers with geographic processing restrictions may face deployment work because Armorer’s hosted service uses one AWS region in Virginia, with customer-hosted deployment available as an alternative. ([Armorer Labs Trust Center](https://armorerlabs.com/trust))
- **Bundled substitution:** [Inferred]: Check Point’s combined agent discovery, testing and runtime offering can reduce the need for a separate purchase where its controls meet buyer requirements. ([Check Point](https://docs.lakera.ai/guard))
