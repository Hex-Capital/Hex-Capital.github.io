## The Idea

- **Problem:** Distributor buyers manually reconcile inventory, orders, deliveries, and sales history across email, spreadsheets, and legacy software before preparing replenishment orders, with operating rules often retained by experienced employees ([YC launch, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/W5s-lark-ai-agents-for-distributors)).
- **Approach:** Lark combines AI and inventory optimization algorithms with distributor data and operating rules to recommend purchases, prepare supplier communications, execute approved ERP actions, and monitor inventory and sales ([YC launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/W5s-lark-ai-agents-for-distributors)).
- **Differentiation:** Lark advertises purchasing, forecasting, inventory, sales, and analytics agents, versus Didero’s procurement execution, Proton.ai’s CRM/catalog/ecommerce platform, and Cavela’s manufacturer sourcing service ([Lark](https://larkagents.com), [Didero](https://www.didero.ai/), [Proton](https://www.proton.ai/), [Cavela](https://cavela.com/press-coverage)).
- **Business Model:** Subscription plans are referenced in the terms, but public tier prices, per-seat charges, and consumption rates were not found ([Lark Terms, Sep 2026](https://larkagents.com/terms)).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** Lark offers a meeting-to-pilot-to-expansion process, while Michael publicly solicits distributor introductions and announced attendance at the steel pipe distributors’ industry conference ([Lark](https://larkagents.com), [company LinkedIn, Oct 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/company/larkagents/)).

## Key Risks

- **AI execution errors:** Lark permits approved ERP writes, while its terms require customers to validate model outputs; approval limits, logs, and pause controls provide documented mitigations ([Terms](https://larkagents.com/terms), [Security](https://larkagents.com/security)).
- **Integration burden:** Setup generally takes one to two months and requires mapping customer data, vendors, and rules, making deployment dependent on customer systems and participation ([Lark FAQ](https://larkagents.com/faq)).
- **Competitive overlap:** Didero already executes procurement workflows inside existing systems, overlapping Lark’s purchasing proposition ([Didero announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/didero-raises-30m-series-a-to-bring-ai-agents-to-global-supply-chains-302686927.html)).
- **Name ambiguity:** A separate Lark office suite operates at larksuite.com, creating attribution risk for funding, downloads, and social metrics ([Lark Suite](https://www.larksuite.com/site/job), [YC company identity](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/lark-2)).
