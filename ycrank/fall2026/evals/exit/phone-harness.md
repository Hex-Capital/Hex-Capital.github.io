# Phone Harness -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

The strongest route is a strategic acquisition by a real-device testing platform that wants to add native-app automation for agents. Phone Harness reports that its shared command interface spans personal and hosted phones and that saved cloud devices preserve apps and logins across runs. A buyer could pay for a managed fleet and recurring customer workflows if acquiring them would be faster or cheaper than building the service. The memo records a November cloud launch, 5.1K GitHub stars, and 522 forks. Those figures show developer attention. Paying-customer count, realized revenue, and repeat usage are unverified. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Facts)

The seed-stage milestone is several months of repeat paid cloud usage on workflows that third-party apps permit, with enough utilization and contribution margin to show that the fleet can scale. The live beta and YC backing make an initial test plausible. Fleet, hosting, and support costs remain unquantified, as does the capital needed to scale. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

The proceeds benchmark assumes a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, 5% initial ownership diluted to 3%, and no follow-ons. The eight-year objective is $2.5 million in gross cash, requiring about $83.3 million in distributable equity value at 3%. The sale values below are illustrative assumptions. The memo provides no transaction pricing or buyer economics to support them.

- **Modest sale.** At $30 million in enterprise value, $5 million in assumed net debt and transaction costs leaves $25 million in equity value. A full cash sale of the holder’s stake would return $750,000, or 1.5×, assuming pro rata treatment and no senior claims diverting proceeds.
- **Strong sale.** At $100 million in enterprise value, the same deductions leave $95 million in equity value. A full cash sale on the same terms would return $2.85 million, or 5.7×, if it closes within eight years. Preference payments, a partial sale, or deferred consideration could bring cash received within the horizon below the objective. Holder eligibility and actual waterfall terms are unverified.
- **Prolonged private.** Continued operation with cash reinvested yields zero realized proceeds within eight years. The memo provides no evidence of an eligible secondary or funded payout.

### Countercase

A buyer can fork the MIT-licensed local harness or use an existing service. Mobilerun already bundles hosted phones with an agent task runner. Apple Mirroring limits local iPhone workflows, and third-party app rules can exclude workloads the product can technically perform. These constraints weaken the case for paying a premium and leave cloud contract transfer and continued workflow access open. The memo establishes neither a structural closing block nor a credible sale-price ceiling below the proceeds hurdle. Watch fits that unresolved gap. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)
