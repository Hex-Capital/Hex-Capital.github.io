## The Idea

- **Problem:** Restaurants seeking recurring local social-media discovery currently rely on agencies, individual influencer negotiation, self-produced content, or a full-time hire, each requiring money or owner time ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Src-trynearby-every-local-business-needs-to-be-discoverable-that-150b-market-is-moving-toward-local-creators), Aug 2026).
- **Approach:** TryNearby matches restaurants with vetted nearby creators, automatically schedules visits, and uses iMessage-based agents for onboarding, invitations, bookings, reminders, rescheduling, and restaurant updates ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Src-trynearby-every-local-business-needs-to-be-discoverable-that-150b-market-is-moving-toward-local-creators), Aug 2026).
- **Differentiation:** Hummingbirds rewards everyday local creators for campaigns spanning brands and retailers, while Hyper pays Los Angeles restaurant creators according to verified engagement; TryNearby instead sells restaurants recurring scheduled creator visits managed through AI agents ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/30/hummingbirds-platform-that-connects-local-creators-brand-campaigns-raises-5-4-m/), Oct 2024; [Hyper](https://thehyper.co/), Aug 2026; [Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Src-trynearby-every-local-business-needs-to-be-discoverable-that-150b-market-is-moving-toward-local-creators), Aug 2026).
- **Business Model:** Restaurants pay a $399 monthly subscription for automatically scheduled visits from a new group of local creators ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Src-trynearby-every-local-business-needs-to-be-discoverable-that-150b-market-is-moving-toward-local-creators), Aug 2026).
- **TAM/SAM:** The adjacent global influencer-marketing-platform market was $34.2B in 2025 and is projected to grow at 14.4% CAGR from 2026 through 2033, but no public SAM was found for local restaurant creator marketing ([Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/influencer-marketing-platform-market), Jun 2026).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Approximately half of new restaurants reportedly originate from owner referrals, supplemented by a Southern California creator sign-up funnel and California Restaurant Association membership ([YC/LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/trynearby), Aug 2026; [creator site](https://socal.trynearby.com/), Aug 2026; [California Restaurant Association](https://web.calrest.org/bg-prod/Advertising-Services/TryNearby-42580), Aug 2026).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Public moat signals comprise thousands of participating creators and an agent processing over 10,000 creator texts monthly while retaining preference, travel, availability, and rejection data for future matching ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/trynearby), Aug 2026).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Dense restaurant-and-creator coverage within each city and accumulated interaction data could improve booking reliability, but operation is currently disclosed only across Southern California ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/trynearby), Aug 2026).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** Hyper and Tablevert already market local-creator campaign products to restaurants, including automated campaign operations and performance tracking ([Hyper](https://thehyper.co/for-restaurants), Aug 2026; [Tablevert](https://www.tablevert.com/), Aug 2026).

## Key Risks

- **Platform dependency:** Customer output depends on TikTok and Instagram distribution, whose local-discovery features and commercial-content rules are platform-controlled; publishing across Google and Yelp provides partial channel diversification ([TikTok](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/introducing-the-local-feed?lang=en), Feb 2026; [California Restaurant Association](https://web.calrest.org/bg-prod/Advertising-Services/TryNearby-42580), Aug 2026).
- **Geographic liquidity:** Supply and demand must be co-located, while disclosed operations remain concentrated in Southern California; the existing pool of thousands of creators mitigates this within the current region but not in new cities ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/trynearby), Aug 2026).
- **Endorsement compliance:** Hosted restaurant visits constitute a material connection requiring clear disclosure, and the FTC assigns responsibility to both influencers and brands; no public disclosure-monitoring process was found ([creator site](https://socal.trynearby.com/), Aug 2026; [FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking), 2023).
- **Outcome attribution:** TryNearby reports approximately 35M views and individual sales outcomes but publishes no independent conversion methodology; its owner agent monitors videos, comments, reviews, and operating events as a partial measurement mechanism ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Src-trynearby-every-local-business-needs-to-be-discoverable-that-150b-market-is-moving-toward-local-creators), Aug 2026).
- **Website availability:** Website not accessible at time of research; the YC page and Southern California creator sign-up subdomain remained accessible ([TryNearby](https://trynearby.com), Aug 25, 2026; [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/trynearby), Aug 2026).
