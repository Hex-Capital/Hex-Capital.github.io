# RouterPlus -- Sam Lessin Evaluation

RouterPlus’s most interesting asset is the person who has spent four years building GPU infrastructure, not the promise of cheaper tokens. Narayana’s Samsung infrastructure work, Bishoyi’s edge-AI deployment experience, and the shipped EVO products give this team credible access to both application developers and inference operations. That fits my preference for founders translating between systems. But proposed post-money valuation, competing offers, and whether my check supplies genuinely scarce capital are all undisclosed. I cannot underwrite cheap optionality without knowing the strike price. YC membership alone establishes neither scarcity nor an auction.

There is a potentially useful business inside the product: customers specify workloads; RouterPlus solicits bids, tests capacity, and arranges backups. The founder-reported network of **40+ providers** could make fragmented supply easier to buy. The question is who captures the resulting savings. OpenRouter already offers customer-specific evaluations and dedicated deployments. If providers can sell through multiple marketplaces and customers can rerun the same procurement exercise elsewhere, RouterPlus performs valuable work without necessarily owning the relationship. Its permission to retain de-identified requirements creates a possible data asset; it does not establish an advantage today.

The strongest objection to passing is that this team may already be getting embedded where procurement becomes operationally painful. RouterPlus reports a largest customer running **3 million agent conversations daily**, and a founder reports reducing one consumer application’s P95 latency from **4.8 seconds to under 4 seconds** through a dedicated deployment. Those are materially better signals than October’s **244,000 launch views**. The latency result documents an intervention that helped a customer; the conversation figure does not establish how much traffic RouterPlus serves or whether anyone pays. I give credit for observed deployment and customer use, while keeping the earlier EVO product’s **10,000 optimized repositories** out of current marketplace traction.

My exceptions to missing deal evidence require unusually hard conversion, proprietary access, or cash economics. RouterPlus has enough operating evidence to make that possibility credible, but insufficient evidence to invoke it. Prepaid credits describe collection mechanics; they reveal nothing about the spread after provider costs. Free BYOK inference could attract useful demand, but no evidence connects that demand to profitable reserved-capacity contracts. Without realized revenue, margins, burn, or runway, deleting the next financing leaves an unanswered question. The AI red flag here is incumbent convergence and shared access to improving models—not demonstrated dependence on error-free agents.

Nor does the dossier identify what new truth this round purchases. Hiring advertisements are not evidence that the financing merely funds headcount, but they cannot substitute for a hypothesis, deadline, and measurable result. A useful venture moment would test whether pooled demand produces repeatable procurement savings that customers will share with RouterPlus through a durable margin. That is my proposed test, not a disclosed company plan, so it earns no score credit. Strong engineers can build this service; the unresolved investment question is whether capital creates an owned economic position.

I pass today. My working expectation is that, within 36 months, RouterPlus shifts its primary business toward managed dedicated-capacity procurement rather than a broad self-service inference marketplace: the founders’ operational experience looks more differentiated than their routing features. That could become a good business. My verifiable flip condition is a contracted capacity book whose collected contribution profit, after provider charges, failover, and customer operations, covers the company’s operating burn. That would demonstrate the durable base business currently missing from the option.
