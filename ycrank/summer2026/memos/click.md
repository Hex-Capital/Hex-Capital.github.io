## The Idea

- **Problem:** ChatGPT and Claude users cannot obtain sources such as LinkedIn activity, YouTube transcripts, financial data, Maps information, and live travel fares through ordinary web search alone and must otherwise consult those services separately ([Click website](https://www.useclick.ai/), Aug 2026).
- **Approach:** Click operates a remote MCP service that routes agent requests to web, professional-network, social, financial, mapping, travel, and extraction providers through one installation ([Click connector directory](https://www.useclick.ai/connectors), Aug 2026; [Click terms](https://www.useclick.ai/terms), Aug 2026).
- **Differentiation:** Click combines Exa search with Crustdata, Google Maps, Reddit, X, YouTube, Apify, Fiber AI, Financial Datasets, Jinko, and Context.dev, whereas Exa, Tavily, Linkup, and Parallel primarily sell web-search or research APIs ([Click connector directory](https://www.useclick.ai/connectors), Aug 2026; [Exa](https://exa.ai/blog/announcing-series-c), May 2026; [Tavily](https://www.tavily.com/blog/big-news-from-tavily-announcing-25m-series-a-to-power-the-internet-of-agents), Aug 2025; [Linkup](https://www.linkup.so/), Aug 2026; [Parallel](https://parallel.ai/blog/series-b), Apr 2026).
- **Business Model:** The Individual plan costs $49 per month for 500 credits, while the site displays annual and Business options without a public Business price ([Click pricing](https://www.useclick.ai/pricing), Aug 2026).
- **TAM/SAM:** The broader global AI-search-engine market was estimated at $16.28 billion in 2024 and forecast to grow at a 13.6% CAGR through 2033 ([Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/ai-search-engine-market-report), 2025).
- **GTM / Distribution:** [Inferred]: Self-service installation for ChatGPT and Claude users is the initial path, while the Business pricing selector and separate-order-form language indicate a parallel company-sales route ([Click pricing](https://www.useclick.ai/pricing), Aug 2026; [Click terms](https://www.useclick.ai/terms), Aug 2026).

## Key Risks

- **Upstream-provider dependency:** Click routes requests to external connector and data providers whose terms and privacy practices apply, so provider access, pricing, or availability changes can affect the service ([Click privacy statement](https://www.useclick.ai/privacy), Aug 2026).
- **Native-platform substitution:** OpenAI already offers built-in web search and remote MCP-server support, allowing the host platform to reproduce part of Click’s retrieval and connection workflow ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/new-tools-and-features-in-the-responses-api/), May 2025).
- **Data-handling exposure:** MCP requests, tool arguments, responses, and results may be retained temporarily and transmitted to connector providers, and Click instructs customers not to submit regulated or highly sensitive information ([Click privacy statement](https://www.useclick.ai/privacy), Aug 2026).
- **Product-positioning changes:** Click’s public description has moved from a frontend cursor for assistants to browser automation for operations teams and then to the current research/connectors service, creating continuity risk for integrations and customer targeting ([founder launch post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/aditya-asgaonkar_im-excited-to-launch-click-a-cursor-for-activity-7413642046030151680-zlCl), 2026; [Click LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/use-click), 2026; [Click website](https://www.useclick.ai/), Aug 2026).
- **Name disambiguation:** “Click” is a common term and YC separately backs “Clicks,” an AI back-office-automation company, which can interfere with branded search and company identification ([YC Clicks page](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/clicks), Aug 2026).
