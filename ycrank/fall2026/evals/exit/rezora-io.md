# Rezora IO -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Strategic acquisition is the strongest supported route. A brokerage software provider could buy Rezora to add outbound voice follow-up and appointment booking to its lead-management product. Rezora offers that workflow today, and the memo records Yelp’s acquisition of Hatch as evidence that a strategic buyer has acquired an adjacent lead-management product. The rationale for buying Rezora depends on its workflow producing incremental results worth paying for. (The Idea; Market & Traction)

Rezora reports 1,000 meetings booked on its current platform. The founder-reported $40,000 in 40 days came from the prior custom-agent pilot. Those signals support testing whether paying brokerage cohorts renew and consistently generate incremental appointments. The memo gives no current platform revenue, retention, or runway figures to establish the capital and time needed to reach that milestone. Buyer funding, contract assignment, and continuity of outbound consents after a sale remain unverified. The memo offers no evidence of an independent liquidity route. (The Idea; Market & Traction; Key Facts; Key Risks)

The benchmark assumes a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, 5% initial ownership diluted to 3%, and no follow-ons. A 5× gross return requires $2.5 million in cash within eight years, or about $83.3 million in distributable equity value at 3%. The sale cases below are illustrative. Both assume a full cash sale by year eight, holder eligibility for the full stake, and pro rata distribution after the stated deductions, with no further senior claims or holdbacks.

- **Modest sale.** $40 million enterprise value less $5 million in assumed net debt and deal adjustments leaves $35 million distributable. The holder receives $1.05 million, or 2.1×.
- **Strong sale.** $100 million enterprise value less $10 million in assumed net debt and deal adjustments leaves $90 million distributable. The holder receives $2.7 million, or 5.4×. If the holder can sell only half the stake and receives no other payout within eight years, realized cash falls to $1.35 million.
- **Prolonged private operation.** If Rezora reinvests available cash and no eligible share purchase occurs, the holder realizes $0 within eight years.

### Countercase

A strategic buyer may be able to build or bundle the core workflow cheaply. A founder says the original paid pilot used commercial tools, Jobber already bundles inbound booking, and Avoca already books directly into ServiceTitan while Rezora lists that integration as forthcoming. This weakens the case for a price that clears the proceeds hurdle. The evidence supports Watch because it leaves that price unsubstantiated and establishes neither a structural closing block nor a credible upper bound below the hurdle. (Defensibility; Market & Traction; Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

What acquisition value could a brokerage software buyer justify from Rezora’s retained, paying customer base? Renewal data and measured gains in appointments that actually took place versus the buyer’s existing workflow would let a buyer quantify that value. Buyer economics supporting at least $83.3 million in distributable equity under defensible deal assumptions would move the action to Reach out. Evidence that the strongest credible sale falls materially short would move it to Pass.
