# Biomes -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Path B — New Economies. Biomes says it embeds engineers with portfolio operating teams, connects agents to existing tools, and turns failures on real tasks into evaluations it can reuse. The scenario is that an acquired company now handles a routine workflow manually at low frequency or leaves some cases undone because staff time and custom integration are too expensive. If Biomes can reuse integrations and evaluations across acquisitions, it could lower the cost per successful outcome enough for that company to pay for recurring delivery. With equivalent models widely available, the buyer would still have a reason to pay Biomes if its field-tested integrations and handling of exceptions deliver reliably at a lower full cost. Both conditions remain untested. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Risks)

The expansion unit is one workflow deployed at another portfolio company. The cost comparison has to include discovery and integration spread over completed tasks, plus inference, review, retries, exception handling, and ongoing support. The advertised $400,000 annual engineering salary plus equity is a staffing cost signal. The memo gives no deployment hours, successful task volume, or realized price. The founders’ prior product launch and infrastructure work support early execution, while the current deployment business has no reported paid pilot or buyer commitment. (Market & Traction, Founders & Team, Key Facts)

### Countercase

Biomes could be selling bespoke implementation to sponsors already buying AI services. Different systems and data formats could force engineers to rebuild much of each deployment, while live failures could keep human review costs high. Topsail already markets embedded deployment to sponsors and operating companies, giving buyers a credible alternative under model parity. The memo contains no measured cost comparison or observed buyer switching, so neither Path B condition is established or defeated. The unresolved gates support Watch. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Will a second portfolio company pay for recurring delivery of a previously deferred workflow after comparing Biomes with an implementer using equivalent models? A paid repeat rollout that measures full cost per successful outcome against the former manual approach, shows positive contribution for Biomes, and demonstrates a buyer preference tied to Biomes’ execution would move the action to Reach out. A representative trial showing that integration and review erase the savings, or that the buyer can switch and retain the outcome on equivalent terms, would close this path and move the action to Pass.
