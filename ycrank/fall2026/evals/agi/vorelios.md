# Vorelios -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Vorelios has seed-stage support for a newly viable use of AI in aerospace design. The company says conventional simulations take hours or days per run. It reports subsecond predictions from clean CAD on full-scale designs and initial autonomous searches over thousands of candidates, with predictions within a few percent of an independent solver in those runs. If that performance holds on customer geometry, an aerospace team could screen thousands of coupled-physics design variants within a design cycle that previously allowed far fewer. That supports the lower end of the 25–37 band for early design exploration. The cost per solver-validated design remains unknown. (The Idea, Market & Traction)

The building-blocks path scores zero. The memo describes a bottleneck in hardware design and supplies no evidence that Vorelios relieves a scarce input needed to deploy or run a specific AI workload. (The Idea)

Under model parity, a buyer could still pay for a workflow that takes a written specification and delivers a shortlist of CAD designs checked against an independent solver. Vorelios’s in-house geometry and simulation pipeline shows relevant operating know-how, while its prototype and the founders’ physics and cyber-physical systems backgrounds support early execution credit. The memo provides no Vorelios buyer commitments, paid deployments, pricing, or delivery costs, so customer demand and scale economics stay at their defaults. (The Idea, Defensibility, Founders & Team, Market & Traction, Key Facts)

### Countercase

Vorelios reports that an earlier architecture failed on unfamiliar design families. On customer CAD, geometry cleanup, engineer review, and solver reruns could consume enough time and budget to keep broad searches impractical. Competing design workflows and open-source surrogate tooling also give buyers ways to reproduce parts of the workflow. The reported prototype results support the mechanism at seed, while the gap on unseen customer geometry and end-to-end cost triggers **Watch**. (Key Risks, Defensibility, Market & Traction)

### Decisive diligence question

Can Vorelios enable a search an aerospace buyer would otherwise skip on a previously unseen design family? A buyer-run comparison should show that Vorelios delivers a shortlist meeting pre-agreed solver tolerances within the buyer’s deadline and cost ceiling, including CAD cleanup, engineer review, and solver reruns, while the existing workflow cannot do so within those limits. A successful test would move the action to **Reach out**.
