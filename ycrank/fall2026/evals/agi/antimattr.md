### Company case

**Industry enabled by superintelligence.** The strongest seed case is hands-free troubleshooting for factory technicians in noisy environments. The memo describes conventional earbuds losing dictation and commands in background noise and records a Geocarbonite partnership to integrate Mnemosyne with IntelliPro. The scenario assumes technicians currently interrupt work to enter or repeat queries and that an agent can provide usable guidance during the task. If voice isolation makes that interaction reliable, AI could reduce technician time per resolved incident enough to make hands-free assistance practical on noisy shifts. Even with equally capable models, the workflow would still require reliable speech capture on the floor and integration with the troubleshooting system. The memo gives no field baseline for cost per resolution or evidence of sustained use. (*The Idea*, *Market & Traction*)

**Building blocks of superintelligence.** Mnemosyne captures speech for people using agents. The memo shows no AI workload whose deployment is constrained by scarce voice-capture hardware or by slow access to equivalent inputs. (*The Idea*, *Defensibility*)

The company describes its voice isolation as proprietary and reports below 5% word error versus 30% on AirPods. The undisclosed test protocol leaves the size and durability of that advantage uncertain. The CEO reports more than 2,000 preorders and two enterprise pilots. The listed deposit is refundable, and pilot payment and repeat use are unknown. The reported first hardware shipment and the founders’ prototyping experience support early execution. Listed prices establish an asking price, while manufacturing yield, returns, and AI service costs remain unknown. (*Defensibility*, *Key Risks*, *Market & Traction*, *Founders & Team*, *The Idea*)

### Countercase

Mnemosyne may make an existing workflow more convenient while leaving the economics of factory troubleshooting largely unchanged. Subtle already offers voice-isolating earbuds for dictation, and Aina offers ring-based agent controls. The company’s benchmark measures transcription errors, leaving open whether its device resolves more factory incidents than a conventional headset or the existing workflow. The integration supports a concrete use worth testing, so the Pass gate stays open pending field evidence. (*Defensibility*, *Key Risks*, *The Idea*, *Market & Traction*)

### Decisive diligence question

Does Mnemosyne make routine hands-free troubleshooting economical on noisy factory shifts? A paid side-by-side pilot should compare Mnemosyne with the same agent on a conventional headset and with the plant’s existing workflow. Measure fully loaded cost per successful resolution, including retries, escalations, and device costs. Move to **Reach out** if the plant documents that the savings make routine use viable and pays to continue. Move to **Pass** if either comparator delivers equivalent outcomes at comparable cost.
