# Latent -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path A, product-led adoption,** is Latent’s strongest path. The likely first buyer is an ML platform team running vLLM agents in a regulated workflow. Its recurring job is to catch unsupported answers or unauthorized actions before release. The memo documents a public self-serve launch and a published Python SDK. If teams can install the SDK and calibrate it on their own traffic, the same route could bring in more buyers. Paid tiers and metered overages could grow revenue as retained teams score more production traffic. The memo records no independent buyer activation, payment, or repeat usage. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Defensibility, Key Facts)

Cross-market portability is a scenario assumption. A buyer abroad using the same serving stack could plausibly use the same integration, subject to local approval for handling prompts and answers. Deployment in the customer’s environment is available according to the memo. Its rollout effort and cost in another market remain unknown, as does willingness to pay there. (The Idea, Key Risks)

### Countercase

Latent reports stopping 99% of rule-breaking agents versus 69% for a frontier judge in its sandbox benchmark. Production teams could still see too many legitimate actions held, or need hands-on recalibration for each workload. The next customer may require setup and security review. Additional workloads may add calibration and review effort, and more hosted traffic may add scoring compute. The memo gives no buyer-confirmed production benefit or measurements of those incremental costs. That leaves the growth mechanism untested at this launch stage and supports Watch. The structural Pass gate requires observed evidence of a ceiling or proportional scaling costs without a supported path to improvement. (Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can an independent vLLM team outside the U.S. use the published SDK to activate a paid production workload and keep scoring through its first billing cycle with minimal founder help? Setup and support logs, paid usage records, and the buyer’s confirmation that erroneous holds were tolerable would move the action to Reach out. A trial that stalls at calibration or security approval would keep it on Watch.
