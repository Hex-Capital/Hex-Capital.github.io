## The Idea

- **Problem:** [Inferred]: Women consuming fanfiction, dating sims, and reality television want participation in character relationships beyond watching or reading predetermined stories; measured unmet demand: No public data found ([Yam About](https://www.hiyam.ai/about), accessed Oct 27, 2026).
- **Approach:** Creators describe a scenario, edit AI-generated characters and settings, generate artwork, and publish a playable link ([Yam Creator Guide](https://www.hiyam.ai/guide), Oct 2026).
- **Differentiation:** [Inferred]: Yam combines a women-focused romance proposition with generated games, versus Character.AI’s general character roleplay, Dorian’s creator-authored games, and Hidden Door’s licensed fictional worlds ([Yam](https://www.hiyam.ai/about), [Character.AI](https://aventure.vc/companies/character-ai-redwood-city-ca-us/fundraising), [Dorian](https://republic.com/dorian), [Hidden Door](https://www.caplight.com/company/hiddendoor)).
- **Business Model:** Free gameplay with optional currency packs priced $0.99–$19.99, Yam Plus at $9.99/month with a $4.99 introductory month, and Plus+ at $19.99/month ([Yam pricing](https://www.hiyam.ai/pricing), Oct 27, 2026).
- **TAM/SAM:** Broader otome-games proxy: $3.46B estimated for 2026, with 7.98% CAGR through 2032; Yam-specific SAM: No public data found ([360iResearch, 2026, via search snippet](https://www.360iresearch.com/library/intelligence/otome-games)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Public creator pages and shareable game links support distribution, while referrals award up to 150 virtual Yam per activated friend ([Creator Guide](https://www.hiyam.ai/guide), [pricing](https://www.hiyam.ai/pricing), Oct 2026).

## Key Risks

- **Character rights:** [Inferred]: Generating games around existing anime or book characters creates rights-clearance exposure; Yam permits named characters while requiring users to hold necessary rights and providing a takedown process ([Creator Guide](https://www.hiyam.ai/guide), [Terms](https://www.hiyam.ai/terms)).
- **Private interaction data:** [Inferred]: Romantic prompts may contain sensitive disclosures, while Yam permits processing by model providers and model improvement; its policy advises against sensitive inputs ([Privacy Policy](https://www.hiyam.ai/privacy), July 2026).
- **Content moderation:** Yam acknowledges potentially offensive AI output and uses automated publication review plus age verification for mature stories, without guaranteeing every item receives review ([Terms](https://www.hiyam.ai/terms), Oct 2026).
- **Name ambiguity:** An unrelated video-Q&A app also used YAM; this dossier attributes company data through hiyam.ai and YC founder links ([MamStartup, Nov 2016](https://mamstartup.pl/quora-w-wersji-wideo-tak-w-skrocie-dziala-yam/), [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/yam)).
