# Shepherd Robotics -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

A **strategic acquisition by an industrial automation vendor** is the strongest private-company path. The memo documents Shepherd’s public launch and demo, and Teradyne’s strategic investment in Bright Machines shows adjacent strategic interest. Shepherd reports bench success rates of 95% for cable routing, 60% for cable insertion, and 62.5% for fuse-holder handling. Those results show uneven task performance and leave production economics unverified. (Market & Traction, Key Risks)

A buyer could pay to own Shepherd’s local adaptation software, deployment workflow, and any reusable data rights if integrating them across its installed base materially reduced the time and labor needed to automate variable wiring tasks. The operating milestone is a paid rollout of the same workflow at two sites, with customer-verified reliability, technician intervention time, and integration cost. YC backing and the founders’ deployment experience support a path to testing. The memo leaves pilot funding, runway, and the transferability of software and customer data rights unresolved. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction, Founders & Team, Key Risks)

**Proceeds bridge.** With no applicable check terms in the memo, use the illustrative $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation. The initial 5% stake falls to 3% after 40% relative dilution, with no follow-on checks. A 5× gross cash return within eight years requires $2.5 million to the holder, or $83.3 million in distributable equity proceeds at 3% ownership. The hurdle is $125 million at 2% and $62.5 million at 4%. The memo supplies no usable acquisition pricing or measured buyer savings, so the sale prices and timing below are scenario assumptions. (Market & Traction, Key Facts)

Both sale scenarios assume zero net debt and zero other price adjustments, so enterprise value equals equity consideration. The assumed waterfall allocates all equity consideration pro rata as cash at closing, with zero preference deductions, stock, escrow, earnouts, rollover, and compensation. Holder proceeds are before investor taxes.

| Scenario | Timing | Enterprise value and equity consideration | Holder cash at 3% | Gross multiple |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Modest cash sale | Year 5 | $50 million | $1.50 million | 3.0× |
| Strong cash sale | Year 7 | $100 million | $3.00 million | 6.0× |
| Prolonged private | Through year 8 | No sale or payout | $0, shares retained | 0.0× realized |

The strong sale clears the hurdle at 3% but falls to $2 million, or 4.0×, at 2%. In the prolonged-private scenario, growth plateaus, buyers stay away, public scale remains out of reach, and cash is reinvested. The memo provides no usable evidence of an independent route that would pay this holder. (Market & Traction, Key Facts)

### Countercase

An automation vendor could license Shepherd’s tools, build from open manipulation checkpoints, or partner with an overlapping vendor. If each site still requires extensive integration and technician supervision, owning Shepherd may buy little that a commercial agreement cannot deliver. The 60% bench insertion result and the need to adapt to customer-specific equipment make that countercase credible. The memo leaves open whether those substitutes can match a proven production deployment and cap the acquisition price below the hurdle. That gap warrants Watch. The modeled $0 private outcome alone does not trigger Pass. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Would a paid two-site rollout show enough economic value from **owning** Shepherd’s adaptation stack to support at least $83.3 million in distributable equity proceeds at 3% ownership? Customer-verified savings after integration and supervision, paired with a credible buyer-side estimate of what ownership adds over licensing or building, would move the case to Reach out if the necessary rights appear transferable. Evidence that a readily available substitute caps the price materially below the hurdle would move it to Pass.
