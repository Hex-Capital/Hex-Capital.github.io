# rekursiv.ai -- Elad Gil Evaluation

Joshua V. Dillon created TensorFlow Probability and co-created the Veo prototype, while Dan Kondratyuk led Luma AI’s World Models group and authored an ICML 2024 Best Paper. That makes rekursiv.ai an unusually strong research-shipping machine with an unusually weak commercial signal: rekursiv.ai has completed hundreds of experiments and produced real benchmark artifacts, but has disclosed no revenue, customer, LOI, or repeat external usage. The analytical signature is high founder execution paired with no visible buying curve. In the current AI cycle, that distinction separates something “just working” from a sophisticated false signal.

**Perplexity** is the high rung on founder shipping because Aravind Srinivas repeatedly turned conversations into working product within a week. rekursiv.ai shares the load-bearing mechanism: rekursiv.ai reports eight system iterations before establishing a 984-experiment continuous research loop, followed by 235 ARC experiments and 684 Sudoku experiments. Dillon and Kondratyuk also shipped consequential systems before founding rekursiv.ai—Veo, TensorFlow Probability, VideoPoet, and Luma world models—putting rekursiv.ai above a credentials-only research spinout. The divergence from Perplexity is that Perplexity’s shipping reached users; rekursiv.ai’s visible loop still terminates in company-run benchmarks.

**Mistral** is the low revenue rung that ordinarily worries me: a capital-intensive AI research effort launched before a buying curve was visible. Mistral escaped that failure mechanism because its founders had shipped Llama, ChatGPT had unmistakably reopened the market, and hyperscaler distribution reduced the independence risk. rekursiv.ai has equally credible technical builders but none of those commercial overrides yet: the dossier identifies no revenue, no named design partner, and only 25 GitHub stars for sagent. The $5M financing is capital supply, not demand. For rekursiv.ai, benchmark accuracy of 71.4%–75.5% on ARC-AGI-1 is evidence that the machinery works, not evidence that anyone will buy it.

Commercial coding models and single-GPU training enabled rekursiv.ai to sustain 984 experiments over 30 days in late 2025. That is a specific recent catalyst, and over the next 18 months it should compound through cheaper inference, longer agent horizons, better coding models, and accumulated failed-experiment data. rekursiv.ai is skating for the puck rather than citing an $8.60B TAM in steady-state terms. The discount is that the “why now” remains inferred and competitors including Sakana AI, Edison Scientific, Periodic Labs, and Lila Sciences saw the same opening, so rekursiv.ai has identified a technological re-opening without yet proving a uniquely ownable window.

Sakana AI has raised $412M and open-sourced its AI Scientist systems, while rekursiv.ai Apache-2.0-licenses sagent, configgle, and technical discoveries. For rekursiv.ai to become an index on autonomous research, every serious ML organization would need to route experiments, evidence, leaderboards, and institutional memory through rekursiv.ai regardless of which frontier model wins. The score flips into invest territory if three named AI labs adopt that workflow and generate proprietary cross-project data that compounds inside rekursiv.ai. Today, 14 GitHub followers, founder-gated access, and no structural barrier imply a product fighting for share rather than a Stripe-like rail benefiting automatically from category growth.

**Color Genomics** is the relevant single-anchor parallel: workflow infrastructure on top of a validated hard layer can avoid taking direct science risk. rekursiv.ai similarly builds orchestration, review, evidence tracing, and experiment management on top of commercial models and commodity compute, so rekursiv.ai does not require new physics or a proprietary foundation model. The remaining dependency chain is still uncomfortable: autonomous hypotheses must transfer beyond puzzles, 48-hour experiment cycles must become economical, benchmark gains must survive semi-private evaluation, and research organizations must trust agent-generated claims. The failed literal transfer of Hypothesis-Pinning Search from Sudoku to ARC shows that the central generalization miracle has not happened consistently.

The 36-month forecast is that by July 2029 rekursiv.ai remains below $5M ARR and is acquired by Google DeepMind primarily for its founders and autonomous-experimentation stack, rather than becoming an independent research-platform category leader. rekursiv.ai’s mechanism is straightforward: exceptional builders keep producing valuable algorithms and tooling, but slow experiments, weak cross-domain transfer, open-source leakage, and no index-like distribution prevent rekursiv.ai from converting technical output into a broad enterprise revenue curve before a frontier lab internalizes the capability.

Dillon’s 13 years at Google Research and DeepMind, Kondratyuk’s VideoPoet record, $5M of financing, and the 99,768-puzzle stress test make the strongest opposing case sting: rekursiv.ai could be the **Decagon** of AI R&D, converting a newly capable model layer into the system of record for every research organization. rekursiv.ai has already demonstrated more iterative output than most pre-seed companies, and the Mistral exception shows that pre-revenue capital intensity can work when elite model builders enter immediately after a step-function. The objection loses today because rekursiv.ai’s evidence stops exactly where enterprise validation begins—no named user, paid deployment, retention cohort, or proprietary customer corpus. Three named research organizations paying rekursiv.ai while usage or revenue compounds above 20% month over month would flip my verdict.
