# Spacesheep -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Strategic acquisition is the strongest path. A team collaboration vendor could buy Spacesheep to bring the review of work from Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor into its own workspace. Spacesheep describes a hosted workflow where teammates comment on agent-generated work, request revisions, and search past output. Its launch showcases an AI Foundry lab dashboard and a live thermal-camera stream. The memo records 28 Chrome extension users and 3 Firefox extension users, with paid adoption and retention undisclosed. The potential buyer benefit is higher workspace retention or paid-seat adoption. Owning the product could bring an established review workflow and its team accounts into the buyer’s workspace if those teams use it repeatedly and can transfer. The memo provides no measured buyer benefit, buyer interest, or adjacent acquisition prices. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Facts)

A measurable seed-stage milestone is a paid team cohort that remains subscribed at 90 days and publishes from at least two agent ecosystems weekly. The live launch and reported $125,000 seed entry support some early execution. Burn, runway, and the capital needed to reach the milestone are unknown. A whole-company sale is conceivable, with IP ownership, integration continuity, and customer and data transfer permissions unverified. (Market & Traction, Key Risks, Key Facts)

**Proceeds bridge.** With investor terms undisclosed, assume a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, 5% initial ownership diluted by 40% to 3%, and no follow-ons. The 5× gross cash target is $2.5 million within eight years. It requires about $83.3 million of distributable equity at 3%, $125 million at 2%, or $62.5 million at 4%. With $5 million of illustrative net debt, the 3% hurdle is about $88.3 million of enterprise value. The memo gives no basis for pricing a Spacesheep acquisition or adjusting the bridge for company-specific debt, fees, or senior claims. (Key Facts)

| Eight-year scenario | Illustrative gross holder cash |
|---|---:|
| $25 million enterprise-value sale, after $5 million of net debt | $600,000 (1.2×) |
| $100 million enterprise-value sale, after $5 million of net debt | $2.85 million (5.7×) |
| Growth plateaus, buyers stay away, public-market scale remains out of reach, and all available cash is reinvested with no secondary or payout | $0, with shares still held |

The memo supplies no predictable cash generation for a sponsor buyout, no eligible secondary demand, no evidence of a path to IPO scale, and no distributable cash or payout commitment. The prolonged-private case is a sensitivity, and the action remains Watch. (Market & Traction, Key Facts)

### Countercase

Claude Artifacts and ChatGPT Sites already provide native publishing, and Type.com already connects Claude and Codex to a shared workspace. Export to Drive or GitHub lowers switching costs, while Claude Code on the web restricts access to Spacesheep’s domains by default and requires a connector or allowlisting. These are concrete reasons a prospective buyer could build, partner, or rely on existing tools. The memo leaves the value of Spacesheep’s cross-agent review workflow unmeasured. A structural block or a realizable sale price below the holder’s hurdle would trigger Pass. The memo establishes neither, and the modest-sale shortfall is illustrative. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Would owning Spacesheep’s cross-agent review workflow generate enough incremental retention or paid-seat revenue for a collaboration vendor to justify a sale near $88.3 million in enterprise value under the modeled terms? A retained paid cohort with weekly use across at least two agent ecosystems, together with a buyer-side build-versus-buy estimate supporting that price range, would move the action from Watch to Reach out.
