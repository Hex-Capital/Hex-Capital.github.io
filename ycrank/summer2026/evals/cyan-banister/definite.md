# Definite -- Cyan Banister Evaluation

The $10.84 billion accounting-and-close market makes Definite a regulated-finance swing whose strongest quality is also its disguise: deterministic checks, source lineage, and hash-chained receipts live underneath an everyday AI-agent wrapper. Definite is highest on the unsexy-regulated axis and lowest on physical-world verifiability. I can see why Definite matters to a controller, but I cannot touch it, observe it in the wild, or yet see the strange human biography that makes these three founders inevitable.

**SpaceX** is the high rung because rockets made capital intensity and technical difficulty into compounding moats while conventional investors recoiled. Definite shares the useful half of that mechanism: banks, credit unions, and insurers care about immutable snapshots, deterministic verdicts, and auditable lineage precisely because trust blocks finance-agent adoption. Definite diverges where the analogy becomes load-bearing—Definite is read-only software with no patent, proprietary dataset, customer switching evidence, or regulatory burden that Numeric, Rillet, FloQast, or an ERP incumbent could not eventually absorb.

**DeepMind** sits on the low physical-verifiability rung: an abstract technical product ordinary people could not install, ride, scan, or photograph, rescued by Demis Hassabis’s extraordinary polymath compounding. Definite has the same abstraction problem without the known exception. Definite’s public documentation describes canonical transactions and hash-chained receipts, but no deployed artifact, repeat usage, or customer outcome lets me feel the product the way I could feel **Niantic** through Ingress; a controller must testify that it works.

Mazin Al-Ani’s Optiver and Boosted.ai internships, Farhan Ur Rehman’s Meta recommendations work, and Gurshabd Singh Varaich’s BitGo controls experience put Definite above founders who merely discovered finance automation in a market memo. Definite still lacks evidence that any founder personally ran a close, reconciled a broken ledger, or owned regulatory reporting inside a bank. The founder score flips upward if Definite can identify the specific month one founder repeatedly suffered this problem as the accountable operator—not merely the engineer adjacent to that operator.

Numeric’s $89 million raised, Rillet’s more than $100 million, and FloQast’s $100 million Series E make Definite look less magically weird with every quarter those vendors expand. Over 18 months, Definite’s context layer could compound through maintained connectors, mapping rules, and reconciliation history; without disclosed connector volume or mapping accuracy, the opposite compounding loop is more likely, with Definite maintaining integrations while incumbents bundle similar features. Definite is a rebuttal, not a hard no, but “AI agents for finance” is already a crowded sentence rather than an aesthetically transgressive secret.

**HQ Trivia** taught me that naming founders is not the same as naming the tiebreaker. Definite identifies Gurshabd as CEO, which is materially better than shared titles among three Waterloo friends, but Definite provides no evidence of majority operating authority, founder vesting, or an exit-conflict protocol. HQ Trivia earned a temporary governance exception through tactile, explosive user love; Definite has no comparable usage signal to override the unresolved authority question.

The YC launch’s bank-reporting origin predicts that within 36 months Definite will abandon the broad customer-built-agent framing, narrow into continuous-control and audit-evidence software for banks, and reach $1 million to $3 million ARR. Definite’s read-only boundary is constraining for a general back-office agent but useful in regulated environments where drafting, flagging, and proving provenance are safer than posting entries or moving money. Definite is never playing the broad-agent game it seems to be playing; the valuable product is the evidence trail underneath it.

Immutable snapshots, per-value lineage, BitGo approval-control experience, and a named CEO form the strongest bull case: Definite could become the trusted finance context layer that every more glamorous agent needs. Definite could even invoke the **DeepMind** exception if the founders reveal unusual cross-domain technical compounding, but the dossier shows internships rather than a shipped-history equivalent, while the **HQ Trivia** exception fails because no repeat product love is documented. I pass today; three named regulated-finance customers renewing after twelve months at more than $100,000 ACV each would verify the hidden infrastructure moat and flip me to Invest.
