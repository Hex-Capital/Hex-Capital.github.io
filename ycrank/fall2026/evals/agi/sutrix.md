# Sutrix -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch
**Evidence confidence:** Medium

### Company case

**New Economies** is the strongest path. Research teams can spend weeks or months manually reviewing charts and reconciling EHR, REDCap, and wearable records before a study can proceed. Sutrix applies study-specific rules to that work and routes unresolved records to reviewers. Sutrix reports an 80% reduction in preparation time. One customer reported going from questionnaire records to findings in days instead of weeks. That supports a faster workflow. Whether the savings are enough to make previously unaffordable studies viable remains open. (The Idea, Market & Traction)

**Scenario assumption.** If a manually prepared dataset takes 100 staff hours and the reported time reduction applies to total labor at equal investigator-verified quality, a successful dataset would take 20 staff hours. That could put a previously shelved multi-source study within an academic lab’s budget. For Sutrix, the expansion unit is one additional investigator-accepted dataset. Its contribution depends on the engagement price less data onboarding, inference, exception handling, and delivery support. Production EHR access and the labor required to resolve exceptions remain unmeasured. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

Sutrix reports paying teams at more than 10 institutions and over $150,000 in revenue before its software release. That supports seed-stage willingness to pay for the work. Repeat software purchases and payment for newly viable studies remain unverified. The founders bring clinical study leadership and production AI experience, and they shipped software after delivering studies for labs. Repeatable production EHR deployment remains an execution gap. Sutrix’s versioned rules, source provenance, and retained reviewer decisions could give teams a reason to stay under model parity. A customer reported that reconciliation steps were visible for expert review. Preference under parity remains untested. (Market & Traction, Key Facts, Founders & Team, Key Risks, Defensibility)

### Countercase

Sutrix could be a faster way to prepare datasets for studies that buyers already fund. Its early revenue came before the software release, and study-specific scoping, production data access, and expert review could keep delivery labor-intensive. Competing abstraction products and open-source extraction show that parts of the workflow are replicable. The memo establishes neither end-to-end substitution nor control of a scarce complement. Both New Economies conditions remain open, so the action is Watch. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks, Key Facts)

### Decisive diligence question

Will independent biostatistics cores repeatedly pay Sutrix to produce datasets for studies they previously shelved on cost grounds when a functionally comparable alternative is available? Move to Reach out if a paid cohort documents the prior budget barrier, lower all-in cost per investigator-accepted dataset at comparable quality, and repeat purchases that cover delivery costs after buyers evaluate the alternative. Move to Pass if the test shows the savings still leave those studies unaffordable or buyers readily switch at parity.
