## The Idea

- **Problem:** AI-compute buyers and stalled-project owners face multi-year data-center timelines while completed grid connections remain unused, with Pacific claiming these delays cost the U.S. $1B weekly and contrasting its pods with one-off construction ([Pacific website](https://gopacific.ai/), Aug 2026).
- **Approach:** Pacific proposes factory-standardized containerized supercomputers deployed on already-powered sites, using diamond wafers for cooling without evaporating water and vertically integrating transformers, switchgear, and power distribution ([Pacific website](https://gopacific.ai/), Aug 2026).
- **Differentiation:** Versus Armada’s rugged mobile Galleons, Crusoe’s energy-to-cloud infrastructure, and Starcloud’s orbital-first system, Pacific targets stalled terrestrial sites with claimed diamond-wafer dry cooling before extending the same architecture to orbit ([Pacific](https://gopacific.ai/), [Armada](https://www.armada.ai/blog/armada-230m-series-b-johnson-controls-galleon-edge-ai), [Crusoe](https://www.crusoe.ai/resources/newsroom/crusoe-announces-series-e-funding), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/30/starcloud-raises-170-million-series-ato-build-data-centers-in-space/), 2026).
- **Business Model:** Pacific says it will sell hosted GPU hours, tokens, and training capacity through short- or long-term contracts, plus managed pods for on-premise defense workloads, but publishes no rates ([Pacific website](https://gopacific.ai/), Aug 2026).
- **TAM/SAM:** JLL estimates annual modular-system and micro-data-center sales will grow from $11B in 2025 to $48B in 2030, a 35% revenue CAGR; no public SAM was found for stalled sites with unused interconnects ([JLL](https://www.jll.com/content/dam/jllcom/en/global/documents/reports/research-reports/26-research-global-data-center-outlook-new.pdf), 2026).
- **GTM / Distribution:** [Inferred]: Founder-led sales likely target stalled-project owners, compute buyers, and defense/aerospace organizations, consistent with Pacific’s statement that it is already working with unnamed large aerospace companies ([Pacific website](https://gopacific.ai/), Aug 2026).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Pacific claims diamond-wafer cooling and vertical integration, but the reviewed public materials disclose no patents, certifications, production volume, or comparative performance data ([Pacific website](https://gopacific.ai/), Aug 2026).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Repeated pod production could create manufacturing learning, supplier relationships, site-access pipelines, and operational data, but no deployed-fleet evidence is public.
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage, as funded competitors already manufacture mobile or modular data centers and sell integrated compute infrastructure ([Armada](https://www.armada.ai/blog/armada-230m-series-b-johnson-controls-galleon-edge-ai), [Crusoe](https://www.crusoe.ai/resources/newsroom/crusoe-announces-series-e-funding), 2026).
- **Commoditization risk:** Armada has deployed modular units internationally and Crusoe is allocating capital to prepackaged modular data centers, demonstrating that established infrastructure companies can pursue similar deployment models ([Armada](https://www.armada.ai/blog/armada-230m-series-b-johnson-controls-galleon-edge-ai), May 2026; [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/annatong/2026/03/12/from-gigawatts-to-grab-and-go-crusoe-leans-into-modular-ai-data-centers/), Mar 2026).

- **Competitors (minimum 3, up to 5):**
  - Armada (nearly $500M raised, revenue unknown): ruggedized mobile and megawatt-scale data centers with documented field deployments, versus Pacific’s stalled-site and diamond-cooling focus ([Armada](https://www.armada.ai/blog/armada-230m-series-b-johnson-controls-galleon-edge-ai), May 2026).

  - Panthalassa ($210M raised, revenue unknown): combines wave-powered offshore platforms with compute, versus Pacific’s land-based unused-power sites and later orbital plan ([Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/05/silicon-valley-bets-on-floating-ai-data-centers-powered-by-ocean-waves/), May 2026).
- **Why now:** JLL reported that 57% of data-center projects experienced delays of at least three months in 2025 and forecast a shift from build-to-suit construction toward assemble-at-scale modular systems ([JLL](https://www.jll.com/content/dam/jllcom/en/global/documents/reports/research-reports/26-research-global-data-center-outlook-new.pdf), 2026).

## Key Risks

- **Product-pivot validation:** Pacific reported £10K MRR in May 2026 for a digital-twin product, whereas its current website markets physical compute pods, so the earlier revenue does not validate the current product ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/harper-dammann-smith_the-ceo-of-the-financial-times-invited-us-activity-7457720884032884736-LsMJ), May 2026; [Pacific](https://gopacific.ai/), Aug 2026).
- **Thermal feasibility:** Pacific claims diamond-wafer cooling can operate in deserts without evaporating water, but publishes no thermal capacity, power-density, reliability, certification, or field-test results ([Pacific website](https://gopacific.ai/), Aug 2026).
- **Component and manufacturing exposure:** Pacific plans to internalize transformers, switchgear, and power distribution, while JLL reports a 33-week average data-center-equipment lead time and elevated transformer and switchgear delays ([Pacific](https://gopacific.ai/); [JLL](https://www.jll.com/content/dam/jllcom/en/global/documents/reports/research-reports/26-research-global-data-center-outlook-new.pdf), 2026).
- **Competitor capitalization:** Armada has raised nearly $500M and reports deployed units, while Crusoe has raised approximately $3.9B and operates integrated energy, construction, and cloud businesses ([Armada](https://www.armada.ai/blog/armada-230m-series-b-johnson-controls-galleon-edge-ai), May 2026; [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/crusoe/), 2026).
- **Siting and permitting:** Reusing an interconnection may shorten power access but does not remove local approvals, and JLL reports only 35% local support for data centers compared with 93% national support ([JLL](https://www.jll.com/content/dam/jllcom/en/global/documents/reports/research-reports/26-research-global-data-center-outlook-new.pdf), 2026).
