# Rezora IO -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path B — Repeatable sales with account expansion.** The strongest initial buyer is a real-estate brokerage with a recurring queue of CRM leads that agents would otherwise call manually. Rezora offers web signup and assisted demos, and its sales posting describes a role focused on closing leads qualified by marketing and AI systems. Brokerage pilots are reported, but the memo does not show how an independent buyer went from that channel to paid activation. If lead intake and calendar booking can be configured the same way across brokerages, the same sales and setup process could serve the next wave of buyers. A brokerage could then add agents or increase call volume on the same deployment. Neither form of expansion is evidenced at the account level. (The Idea, Key Facts)

The strongest early fit signal is founder-reported. The prior custom-agent pilot generated $40,000 in 40 days, and a founder says one customer closed $65,000 after using Rezora for CRM follow-up. The pilot suggests willingness to pay for the job, while the customer outcome is anecdotal. Rezora also reports 1,000 meetings booked on the current platform, without a paid customer count, renewal data, or a breakdown by account. Current platform revenue is undisclosed. (Market & Traction, Key Facts)

**Second-market scenario.** Assume an English-speaking brokerage abroad has the same CRM follow-up job and can document consent acceptable to Rezora. The test is whether the existing CRM and calendar workflow still works once Rezora handles local phone-number provisioning and consent requirements, and how much setup and support that adds. Each new account can add sales and onboarding time. Each extra call minute adds telephony and voice-model costs. Both published schedules list $0.20 per billable minute, but they differ on included usage, and the memo provides no measured costs to establish contribution. (The Idea, Key Risks, Key Facts)

### Countercase

The prior paid pilot was a custom service. If the current product still needs account-specific scripting and founder help, each brokerage could add sales and delivery labor in proportion to revenue. Rezora’s documented consent and campaign certification requirements also make leads without qualifying consent ineligible, which could shrink the usable lead pool in a new market. The memo does not establish a structural ceiling on demand or proportional delivery costs on the current platform, so the Pass gate is untriggered. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can Rezora take a small cohort of independent brokerages in a second geography from its intended sales channel through standard paid setup and first monthly renewal? Move to **Reach out** if billing and call records show renewed, recurring use and the rollout records show founder-light setup, with paid revenue covering measured sales, onboarding, telephony, voice-model, and support costs over the test period.
