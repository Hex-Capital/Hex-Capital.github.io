## The Idea

- **Problem:** Mortgage-title processors coordinate parties, request documents, search county records, and repeatedly enter the same data using spreadsheets and email, with the founders reporting that this work took a processor 8–10 hours per file ([Aahil Valliani on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/aahil-valliani), Jun 2026).
- **Approach:** Vestris ingests emailed contracts, creates party portals, retrieves land and tax records through browser agents, calculates prorations, drafts title schedules and commitments, balances settlement statements, and prepares lender packages ([Vestris](https://vestris.ai/), Jul 2026).
- **Differentiation:** Vestris describes end-to-end autonomous pre-closing processing, while Qualia provides title workflow, accounting, reporting, and collaboration software, Snapdocs connects closing participants digitally, and Dono supplies property-record intelligence with human verification ([Vestris](https://vestris.ai/), [Qualia](https://www.qualia.com/title-and-escrow/), [Snapdocs](https://www.snapdocs.com/resource-center/press-release/snapdocs-series-d-funding-announcement), [Dono](https://www.dono.ai/)).
- **Business Model:** Vestris offers one enterprise plan with custom pricing based on order volume and closing jurisdictions, including unlimited orders, users, and jurisdictions ([Vestris pricing](https://vestris.ai/pricing), Jul 2026).
- **TAM/SAM:** The U.S. title-insurance industry generated $18.5 billion in premiums in 2025, up 13.8% year over year, although this measures industry premiums rather than Vestris’s software-addressable revenue ([ALTA](https://www.alta.org/news-and-publications/press-release/ALTA-Reports-2025-Market-Share-and-Title-Insurance-Premium-Volume), May 2026).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Vestris uses a waitlist, demo-led enterprise sales, and direct outreach to title and settlement professionals, initially serving Washington, D.C. and Montgomery County, Maryland ([Vestris](https://vestris.ai/), [Vestris pricing](https://vestris.ai/pricing), Jul 2026).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Vestris says its Schedule B system was trained on decades of approved and rejected titles, but it does not disclose the dataset’s ownership, size, or exclusivity ([Vestris](https://vestris.ai/), Jul 2026).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Firm-specific corrections, county-system integrations, and accumulated closing outcomes could create proprietary workflow data, but no usage scale or exclusive data rights are publicly documented.
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** Qualia already offers title-production workflows and built-in AI, Dono automates property-record extraction and underwriting intelligence, and RealPact builds AI agents for real-estate transaction paperwork ([Qualia](https://www.qualia.com/title-and-escrow/), [Dono](https://www.dono.ai/), [YC housing directory](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/industry/housing-and-real-estate), Jul 2026).

## Key Risks

- **Geographic integration burden:** Public coverage is limited to Washington, D.C. and Montgomery County, Maryland, while U.S. property records span more than 3,700 county systems, requiring jurisdiction-specific integrations ([Vestris](https://vestris.ai/), [Dono](https://www.dono.ai/blog/dono-raises-6-5m-seed-round), 2026).
- **Title-output accuracy:** Vestris drafts title abstracts, Schedule A/B, commitments, prorations, and closing-disclosure reviews, but its website leaves commitments ready for underwriter sign-off rather than claiming autonomous underwriting approval ([Vestris](https://vestris.ai/), Jul 2026).
- **Incumbent overlap:** Qualia has $160 million in disclosed funding and offers title workflow software with built-in AI, while Snapdocs has $260 million and connects the mortgage-closing ecosystem ([Qualia](https://www.qualia.com/press-releases/real-estate-tech-platform-qualia-becomes-latest-tech-unicorn/), [Snapdocs](https://www.snapdocs.com/resource-center/press-release/snapdocs-series-d-funding-announcement)).
- **Workflow-data sensitivity:** The product processes contracts, land records, settlement charges, closing disclosures, and lender packages, but no public security, compliance, or certification documentation was found ([Vestris](https://vestris.ai/), Jul 2026).
- **Name ambiguity:** Search results also identify an unrelated New York software organization using Vestris.com, creating potential company-name confusion ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/vestris), Jul 2026).
