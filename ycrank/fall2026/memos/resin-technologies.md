## The Idea

- **Problem:** Hardware engineers manually research components, draw schematics, and route boards in conventional ECAD tools, with errors discovered after fabrication requiring another manufacturing cycle ([YC launch, October 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/VG7-resin-codex-for-electronics)).
- **Approach:** Resin converts requirements into editable schematics, generates symbols and footprints from datasheets, and places and routes boards within specified design rules ([Resin Product](https://resineda.com/product)).
- **Differentiation:** Resin combines schematic generation and routing in its own editor, whereas Quilter concentrates on physics-driven layout automation and CELUS emphasizes component selection, schematics, and BOM generation ([Resin](https://resineda.com/product), [Quilter](https://www.quilter.ai/blog/series-b), [CELUS](https://www.celus.io/)).
- **Business Model:** Pro costs $199 monthly for one user with 2,000 AI credits, Team costs $149 per user monthly for 2–5 users with pooled credits, Enterprise has negotiated terms, and new accounts receive a seven-day trial with 500 credits ([Resin pricing, accessed October 27, 2026](https://resineda.com/)).
- **TAM/SAM:** The broader global PCB design software market is estimated at $6.1 billion in 2026, with 12.8% CAGR through 2033; No public data found for Resin’s specific SAM ([Grand View Research, 2026](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/pcb-design-software-market-report)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Resin solicits introductions to electrical engineers and hardware leads through its YC launch, offers promotional signup credits and founder onboarding, and supports self-service trials ([YC launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/VG7-resin-codex-for-electronics), [Resin FAQ](https://resineda.com/faq)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Resin describes its routing engine as proprietary, but publishes no independent performance comparison establishing an advantage ([YC launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/VG7-resin-codex-for-electronics)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Accumulated workspace libraries and design history could create workflow switching costs, although customer retention evidence is unavailable and Resin supports editable exports ([Resin](https://resineda.com/)).
- **Market structure:** [Inferred]: No structural barrier identified at this stage, given overlapping AI editing and design assistance already offered by Flux ([Flux documentation](https://www.flux.ai/COPILOT)).
- **Commoditization risk:** [Inferred]: Competitors can access general-purpose models similar to those Resin uses, while reproducing its complete editor, file interoperability, and routing system requires additional engineering ([Resin Terms](https://resineda.com/terms), [Resin Product](https://resineda.com/product)).

## Key Risks

- **Technical correctness:** Resin warns that generated output can contain incorrect wiring, unsuitable components, or misread datasheets; required engineering review and reversible edits are its stated mitigations ([Terms](https://resineda.com/terms)).
- **Routing capability:** [Inferred]: Beta autorouting creates execution risk for demanding boards; Resin provides rule checks and manual editing, but public comparative validation is unavailable ([Product](https://resineda.com/product)).
- **Enterprise data acceptance:** [Inferred]: External processing of design context may restrict adoption for confidential projects; Resin discloses model providers, prohibits training on customer designs, and reports SOC 2 readiness in progress without an issued report or completed third-party penetration test ([Security, October 2026](https://resineda.com/security)).
- **Competitive overlap:** [Inferred]: Flux’s integrated editor and AI schematic editing create direct substitution pressure for Resin’s workspace proposition ([Flux documentation](https://www.flux.ai/COPILOT)).
- **Name ambiguity:** Searches also return Refined Resin Technologies, a cannabis-processing business whose funding must be excluded from this electronics company’s record ([FinCanna announcement](https://investornews.com/member_news/fincanna-advances-first-tranche-of-capital-to-refined-resin-technologies-inc/?print=pdf)).
