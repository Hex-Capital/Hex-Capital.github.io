# Axonnic -- Naval Ravikant Evaluation

Williams has done work that makes this problem worth taking seriously: HD-sEMG research, PCB development and supply-chain work at Voxblock, followed by a demonstrated connection between Axonnic’s software and a Proception robotic hand. Patankar brings robotics software experience from Oxford’s OxRAM project. These are relevant actions, not just relevant degrees. The strongest founder signal is that the team has crossed from studying human signals to connecting them to a machine. That earns substantial specific-knowledge credit, although the dossier does not yet establish repeated success delivering autonomous manipulation.

Axonnic’s decisive question is what accumulates after each deployment. Its reported **4.58° average joint-angle error, 50 predictions per second and 78 minutes of training data** establish a concrete sensing implementation. They do not establish that a robot can sew successfully or regulate grip force in production. Held-out Apple Vision Pro recordings validate the tracking claim within that test; autonomous task performance remains another step. Hardware and a trained decoder give Axonnic more substance than copyable application code, but Hemlock already offers overlapping EMG capture. The durable asset would be transferable manipulation knowledge collected under usable rights. The dossier establishes neither exclusivity nor demonstrated improvement across customers.

The market has room to maneuver. Force-sensitive sewing is a specific starting task, and recordings involving electricians, plumbers and demolition crews identify adjacent work where the same sensing capability could matter. Laboratory datasets offer another buyer alongside manufacturers. That is a credible expansion path, but recordings establish access to demonstrations, not demand for an automated replacement. The Italian manufacturing LOI and reported Perloro Packing Srl pilot are useful engagement signals. Neither establishes a price, a completed paid transaction or contribution economics. I give Axonnic credit for a plausible route through industrial manipulation, with monetization still at the absent-evidence default.

The strongest bull case is that leaving the fingers uncovered captures demonstrations that gloves distort or discourage. If muscle signals supply information that video misses, Axonnic could own a valuable input to robot learning. Its prototype and manufacturer engagement make that possibility concrete enough to investigate. My exceptions for an unusual prototype or exceptional founders matter here: a company can deserve investment before revenue or a user network exists. But those exceptions require unusually persuasive evidence elsewhere. Axonnic has demonstrated sensing and integration; the evidence does not yet show the transferable autonomous capability that would justify overriding its weaker dimensions.

My unstated preference for embodied bottlenecks favors this company. My preference for participants creating value for one another remains unfulfilled. A new factory could contribute useful training data, but cross-customer rights are negotiated individually, and no measured benefit to another customer is reported. I cannot count a possible shared dataset as an operating network effect. The adverse scenario is technically competent founders repeatedly collecting and calibrating bespoke datasets, with company labor growing alongside customer count. The dossier does not prove that outcome, but it supplies no demonstrated compounding loop to defeat it.

I see no documented integrity problem, artificial financing deadline or dependence on investor reassurance. Those red flags do not explain my hesitation. The unresolved issue is whether Axonnic’s learning becomes reusable property. **My flip condition is a paid deployment at a second manufacturer showing that a model trained on contractually reusable first-customer data improves autonomous task success against a video-only baseline, without repeating the original collection effort.** That would turn the central moat hypothesis into evidence. At present, I would keep learning from these founders and withhold my check.
