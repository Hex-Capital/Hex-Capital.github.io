# Hesper AI -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path B — New Economies.** Hesper could make full investigations economical for claims that carriers or MGAs currently give only a cursory review. Hesper says outsourced investigations cost $1,500–$2,500 per claim and reports that one MGA cut average adjuster time per claim by 82% over two weeks. The cost quote describes outsourced cases. The MGA result supports a labor-saving mechanism, while the memo leaves open whether the customer investigated claims it previously skipped. Hesper reports paying production customers, but their use cases, prices, and repeat orders are undisclosed. The founders’ document automation experience and their rebuild after the initial fraud-scoring product found no buyers support seed-stage execution. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Founders & Team)

If the reported time reduction held on previously skipped claims, every 100 baseline adjuster minutes would fall to 18. That scenario covers adjuster time alone. Market opening requires the buyer’s all-in cost per accurate, auditable investigation, including Hesper’s fee, review, retries, exceptions, and compliance, to fall within what the buyer will pay for those claims. Usage-based pricing makes the claim a plausible unit of expansion. Hesper’s contribution per completed investigation remains unknown. Under model parity, Hesper’s listed claims-system integrations and source-linked findings could earn repeat business if buyers value how they fit carrier-specific approval workflows. That preference remains an inference. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Countercase

Hesper may save time on investigations buyers already perform while leaving previously skipped claims uneconomic once review and exception work are counted. Its launch offer includes 200 free claims and 90 days without a platform fee for eligible buyers, which limits what subsidized adoption can establish about willingness to pay. Duck Creek’s native agent infrastructure and FRISS’s Guidewire integration give buyers credible alternatives within their claims systems. The memo provides no head-to-head evidence of equivalent investigation outcomes or actual switching. The short, founder-reported MGA result and limited buyer evidence leave both required conditions unproven. The available evidence supports Watch. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Will an independent MGA place a paid repeat order at a price that yields positive contribution for Hesper to investigate claims the MGA previously skipped, after comparing Hesper with a functionally equivalent option? Move to Reach out if buyer-verified results show acceptable accuracy and auditability, viable all-in cost per completed investigation, and a concrete reason the buyer chooses Hesper under parity. Move to Pass if the comparison shows the skipped claims remain uneconomic or the buyer can readily switch and retain the same outcome.
