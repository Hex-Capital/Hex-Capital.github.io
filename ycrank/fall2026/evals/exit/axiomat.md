# Axiomat -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

The strongest route is a strategic sale to a data warehouse or governed analytics platform such as Snowflake. Axiomat says its Lean proof gate checks generated queries against approved business rules before execution. A platform could have a reason to buy that capability if it measurably reduces review work or costly errors in pricing and financial reporting. The memo documents a public launch and a team with formal verification and systems experience, making a paid production comparison plausible. The cost and timing of that test remain open, as do the rights and consents needed for a sale. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction, Founders & Team)

Under the rubric benchmark, a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation starts at 5% ownership and dilutes to 3% with no follow-ons. The holder needs $2.5 million in gross cash within eight years, which requires about $83.3 million in distributable equity value at a full pro rata payout. For both sale scenarios, assume an eligible holder sells its entire stake at closing within eight years, all consideration is cash, and the waterfall distributes the remaining equity value pro rata after the stated deductions.

- **Modest sale.** At $50 million in enterprise value with no debt or other deal deductions, $50 million reaches the equity waterfall. The holder receives $1.5 million, or 3×.
- **Strong sale.** At $110 million in enterprise value less $10 million in assumed debt and other deal deductions, $100 million reaches the equity waterfall. The holder receives $3 million, or 6×. If the holder can sell only 80% of its stake on the same terms, cash received falls to $2.4 million, or 4.8×.
- **Prolonged private operation.** If Axiomat reinvests available cash and there is no eligible share sale or funded payout, the holder realizes $0 within eight years.

The sale values are scenario assumptions. The memo provides no measured customer savings or acquisition pricing evidence to support the strong case and no evidence of an independent liquidity route. (Market & Traction, Key Facts)

### Countercase

Snowflake already offers semantic views and approved SQL examples, giving it a built-in way to address the same customer need. Axiomat’s proof can also certify a query against an incorrectly specified rule, while retries and abstention can reduce the share of questions it answers promptly. If the proof gate adds little value in production, a platform has little reason to buy it. That risk supports Watch. The memo leaves open whether the built-in tools are sufficient for the highest-stakes workflows, and the modest scenario alone cannot establish that the strongest credible route falls short. Neither Pass gate is triggered. (Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can Axiomat show in a paid production deployment that its proof gate reduces costly errors or manual review enough for a platform buyer to underwrite a sale above the holder’s proceeds hurdle? A side-by-side comparison against the platform’s approved-query workflow, measuring review time, error rates, coverage, and latency, would answer the operating question. If those results support buyer economics consistent with at least $83.3 million in distributable equity value, move to Reach out.
