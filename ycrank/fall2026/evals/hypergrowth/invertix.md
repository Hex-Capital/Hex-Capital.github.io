# Invertix -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Assuming strong product-market fit with solar operations teams, Invertix has a plausible route to serving the same buyer abroad. Fault triage, technician coordination, and post-repair verification are recurring jobs that its shared operational model could support across markets. Altenia’s CEO reports that some work went from days to minutes, evidence of workflow value at one customer. The model is the reusable component. How much work it takes to map a new operator’s tools, procedures, and approval rules remains uncertain. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

Referrals from Italian anchor customers give Invertix a repeatable source of introductions. The ordinary buyer still goes through a demo and an engineer-led rollout, staged one workflow at a time. Once deployed, asset histories and approval rules could make the product harder to replace. Portfolio-based quotes suggest a way to charge more as an account adds sites. Billing cadence, renewals, and realized expansion within existing accounts remain unknown. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Risks)

Invertix reports approximately 2 GW of agent coverage in October. That indicates deployment activity, while billable capacity, paying-customer counts, revenue, and commercial growth remain unverified. For the next portfolio, the relevant cost bridge is contract revenue less sales effort, integration labor, model usage, and ongoing support. Reusing integrations could improve contribution if it reduces engineering hours on subsequent rollouts. The memo gives no realized prices or delivery costs to size that effect. (Market & Traction, The Idea, Key Risks, Key Facts)

### Countercase

Strong fit could deepen demand while leaving each new portfolio dependent on Invertix engineers. Customer-specific system mapping and workflow-by-workflow rollout could make delivery labor grow with each sale, slowing expansion even when operators see substantial time savings. The structural gate stays open. The memo documents hands-on deployment and leaves both the amount of reusable work and contribution on subsequent rollouts unresolved. Those gaps support Watch and low confidence. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can Invertix repeat a paid solar portfolio rollout in a second market using the same core product, with engineering hours falling on successive deployments? Look for recurring invoices and deployment records across independent operators that show reused integrations, time to first value, and positive contribution after sales, onboarding, model usage, and support. That milestone would support rescoring for Reach out if the revised score reaches 65 and confidence rises to at least Medium. Evidence that each market requires substantial rebuilding with no credible near-term transfer path would change the action to Pass.
