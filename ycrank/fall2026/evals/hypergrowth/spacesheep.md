# Spacesheep -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Medium

### Company case

Assuming strong product-market fit, Spacesheep’s strongest path starts with developers on teams that use multiple coding agents. The same MCP connector or CLI lets developers publish agent output to a hosted link that teammates can review and revise. That recurring workflow can travel across markets with the same product. Self-serve signup and browser extensions give developers a way to start, and each shared link can introduce the product to another teammate. The memo documents signup and pricing. Self-serve paid checkout and conversion through shared links remain unverified. (The Idea, Key Risks)

Searchable work and revision history could support repeat use. Team pricing at $10 per member per month lets accounts spend more as colleagues join, while higher individual tiers give heavy users another way to expand spend. Whether expansion can offset churn remains uncertain. Following the October 21 public launch, the two-person team had 28 Chrome extension users and three Firefox extension users in November. The company showcased an AI Foundry dashboard, and realized revenue and paying-customer counts remain unknown. The traction score uses the absent-evidence default for commercial traction. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Facts)

At the listed Team price, each additional paid seat brings in $10 per month before incremental hosting, storage, model inference, and attributable acquisition, onboarding, and support costs. Tiered model and deployment allowances give Spacesheep a way to charge more for heavier workloads. Contribution remains uncertain because the memo gives no representative workload costs or estimate of the cost of serving free users who enter through shared links. (The Idea, Key Risks)

### Countercase

Claude Artifacts and ChatGPT Sites already let teams publish inside the tools where they generate the work. Spacesheep may win teams that regularly work across agents yet see weak paid conversion if those teams can handle most reviews in their existing tools. Claude Code on the web also requires a connector or allowlisting, and sharing coding-session content can add governance friction. The structural downside gate clears because the documented setup gives the strongest buyer segment a credible path without bespoke rebuilding by market or required staff-led onboarding. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can shared Spacesheep links produce a cohort of teams that subscribe through self-serve checkout and expand paid seats? A cohort outside the free YC offer, with several teams independently subscribing after receiving a link and growing the cohort’s recurring revenue after churn and contraction over three billing cycles, would move the action to Reach out.
