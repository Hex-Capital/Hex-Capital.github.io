# Avout -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Strategic acquisition is Avout’s strongest path. Avout says it uses borrower operating data to underwrite frontier hardware companies and match them with lenders. An equipment lender could pay for a repeatable source of borrowers it would otherwise miss and for underwriting data that improves credit decisions. Ownership could make sense if acquiring the workflow and its data produces more incremental lending profit than a referral or licensing agreement. That benefit remains an inference. (*The Idea*, *Defensibility*)

The seed-stage milestone is repeat funded placements with documented fees and repayment performance. Avout says it helped arrange a $1.5 million data-center term sheet. Closing and disbursement are undisclosed. The founders’ experience in real-asset investing and regulated financial infrastructure supports their ability to run that test. The operating capital needed is unknown, and loan funding depends on third-party lenders. Avout’s rights to transfer borrower data and lender relationships remain unverified. (*Market & Traction*, *Founders & Team*, *Key Risks*, *Defensibility*)

With investor terms undisclosed, assume a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, 5% initial ownership diluted to 3%, and no follow-ons. The eight-year target of $2.5 million in gross cash requires $83.3 million in distributable equity at 3% ownership, $125 million at 2%, or $62.5 million at 4%. At 3% ownership, with illustrative net debt of $5 million and before any fees, senior claims, or investor taxes, a $25 million enterprise-value sale yields $600,000 (1.2×) and a $100 million sale yields $2.85 million (5.7×). The memo provides no basis for a realizable sale price. (*Market & Traction*, *Key Facts*)

If originations plateau, buyer interest fails to emerge, public scale stays out of reach, and Avout reinvests all available cash, the holder realizes $0 over eight years and retains the shares. The memo establishes neither predictable cash generation for a sponsor buyout or company-funded payout, eligible secondary demand, nor a path to public scale. (*Market & Traction*, *Key Facts*)

### Countercase

Trinity Capital already offers equipment and asset-based financing, and GPU Capital already underwrites server loans using appraisals and customer contracts. A lender could handle the underwriting itself and take referrals from Avout, leaving little reason to buy. This supports Watch. The available evidence leaves both a structural closing barrier and materially inadequate sale proceeds unestablished, so the Pass gate does not trigger. (*Defensibility*, *Market & Traction*)

### Decisive diligence question

Could an equipment lender justify paying enough for Avout’s origination and underwriting platform to clear the holder’s eight-year cash target? Evidence from repeat funded placements that quantifies the lender’s incremental profit from owning the platform, and supports a sale range clearing the modeled $88.3 million enterprise-value hurdle at 3% ownership and $5 million of net debt, would move the action to Reach out.
