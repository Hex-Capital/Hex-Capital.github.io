# Axiomat -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

New Economies is the strongest path. Axiomat says it translates business rules and questions into formal definitions, shows users its interpretation, and uses Lean to verify that a generated query matches the definition before execution. If it cannot prove the match, it retries or abstains. The memo records a public launch and product demo. Performance on representative customer workloads remains unmeasured in the memo. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Facts)

The scenario is that finance teams leave some recurring pricing questions unanswered because analyst time for writing, checking, and reconciling queries costs too much. Axiomat could make those questions economical to answer if it lowers the cost per correct, timely answer after accounting for rule setup and maintenance, model and proof retries, and human review or fallback. Customer-approved rules could give buyers a reason to keep paying Axiomat when comparable models are widely available. The memo describes the approval workflow, while the cost reduction, newly viable use, and buyer preference remain untested. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Risks)

The founders bring formal verification, applied ML research, and real-time utility software experience. That experience and the launch support seed-stage execution credit. The memo gives no independent buyer commitment or deployment cost data, so customer demand and scale economics take their absent-evidence defaults. (Founders & Team, Market & Traction, Key Facts)

### Countercase

A valid proof can certify a query against the wrong business rule. If finance teams still need to check the interpretation and resolve frequent abstentions, verification could preserve much of the analyst workload. Snowflake’s semantic views and approved query examples also give buyers a warehouse-native option that may satisfy their accuracy requirements. The memo provides no comparative labor results or observed buyer switching. Both required conditions remain open, and the memo identifies no independently supported scarce complement. That supports Watch. (Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Will an independent finance team renew a paid Axiomat deployment for recurring pricing questions it previously left unanswered after comparing it with a warehouse-native workflow using an equally capable model? Move to **Reach out** if a production pilot shows that Axiomat brings those questions below the buyer’s cost threshold at the required accuracy, including setup and exception handling, and the buyer renews at a price that supports delivery costs. Move to **Pass** if a representative trial shows that manual review still makes the use uneconomic or that buyers readily switch to a comparable alternative.
