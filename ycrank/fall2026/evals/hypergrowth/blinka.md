# Blinka -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Usage-based expansion** is Blinka’s strongest path. The first buyer is a U.S.-incorporated business that needs to pay overseas teams and vendors on a recurring basis. Blinka’s documented offering combines remote USD account opening with stablecoin settlement and local payout routes where available. The same account and workflow can serve businesses paying into multiple markets, subject to account eligibility and local payout coverage. (The Idea, Key Risks)

Early traction reports cite more than 400 businesses onboarded in two months and more than $18 million in payments on the current banking product. Moving real funds is a meaningful early commitment, although the memo does not tie that volume to specific customers or show what they paid. The growth hypothesis is that businesses start with one payout workflow, then route more payments through Blinka as the job recurs. The first unproven link is whether initial payment activity turns into repeat, fee-bearing use. (Market & Traction, Key Facts)

### Countercase

The reported volume could come from a few large customers or one-off transfers. The memo gives no account-level repeat activity and does not connect the website or referral channels to funded accounts. Even if customers return, Blinka relies on third parties for banking and settlement, and the memo gives neither realized payout revenue nor the cost of serving additional volume. These gaps support Watch. The structural downside gate does not apply because the memo shows provider dependence without evidence that growth requires costs to rise in proportion to revenue. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Facts, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Does the first funded cohort return for fee-bearing payouts on its normal payment cycle? An account-level ledger showing several independent businesses repeating the same payout workflow across consecutive cycles, with realized revenue covering incremental settlement and support costs, would move Blinka to Reach out.
