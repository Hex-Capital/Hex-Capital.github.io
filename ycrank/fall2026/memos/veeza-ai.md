## The Idea

- **Problem:** Egyptian passport holders face repeated paperwork and scarce appointments, using government portals themselves or paying travel agents and brokers for assistance ([EnterpriseAM, Aug 10, 2026](https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2026/08/10/one-egyptian-founder-and-ai-enthusiast-is-out-to-fix-the-broken-visa-process-plaguing-residents-of-the-global-south/)).
- **Approach:** Computer-use agents navigate government portals, verify uploaded documents, complete forms, and book appointments, with human review before submission ([YC, accessed Oct 27, 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/veeza-ai)).
- **Differentiation:** Veeza starts with MENA travelers and Arabic/English assistance, whereas Atlys has an India-centered distribution base and iVisa/HelloGov combines visas with passport services ([Veeza](https://www.veeza.ai/en/about), [Fortune India, Jun 2026](https://www.fortuneindia.com/business-news/how-former-pinterest-engineer-mohak-nahta-is-building-travels-ai-concierge/143342), [HelloGov](https://www.hellogov.com/hellogov-ivisa)).
- **Business Model:** One-time per-applicant service fees include EGP 1,500 for several Schengen destinations and EGP 4,000 for UK/US applications, with government and visa-center charges separate ([pricing, Oct 27, 2026](https://www.veeza.ai/en)).
- **TAM/SAM:** Coherent Market Insights estimates global tourist visa services at $4,050.5M in 2026, with 15.5% CAGR through 2033, a broader market than Veeza’s initial geography; company-specific SAM: No public data found ([CMI, Mar 2026](https://www.coherentmarketinsights.com/industry-reports/tourist-visa-service-market)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Acquisition includes founder LinkedIn/TikTok content, WhatsApp conversations, and advertising, while Veeza solicits travel-agency B2B2C introductions ([SceneNow, Oct 17, 2026](https://scenenow.com/Business/Veeza-AI-Wants-to-Make-Global-Mobility-Easier-for-the-Global-South), [Keiki, Oct 20, 2026](https://onkeiki.com/blog/veeza-customer-story), [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/veeza-ai)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** [Inferred]: Reusable passport/document storage provides workflow continuity, but does not establish exclusive data access or switching costs ([privacy policy, Sep 28, 2026](https://www.veeza.ai/en/privacy)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Route-specific exception handling and repeat-application workflows could improve with usage, although a measured advantage has not been disclosed.
- **Market structure:** [Inferred]: No structural barrier identified at this stage, given existing commercial platforms combining AI assistance and human review ([HelloGov](https://www.hellogov.com/hellogov-ivisa)).
- **Commoditization risk:** Atlys already offers AI assistance and iVisa/HelloGov combines automated checking with specialists, demonstrating overlapping capabilities without establishing how cheaply they could reproduce Veeza’s workflows ([Fortune India, Jun 2026](https://www.fortuneindia.com/business-news/how-former-pinterest-engineer-mohak-nahta-is-building-travels-ai-concierge/143342), [HelloGov](https://www.hellogov.com/hellogov-ivisa)).

## Key Risks

- **Appointment supply and portal dependency:** Veeza’s terms acknowledge that unavailable appointments and unannounced portal changes can interrupt service, with retries or notification as mitigations ([terms, Oct 13, 2026](https://www.veeza.ai/en/terms)).
- **Application accuracy:** [Inferred]: Incorrect extraction or form completion could affect an application; Veeza requires human review and lets applicants inspect prepared forms before submission ([company](https://www.veeza.ai/en/about)).
- **Sensitive-data exposure:** [Inferred]: Retaining passports and optional financial documents creates exposure if access controls fail; Veeza states it encrypts stored data, restricts access, and permits deletion requests ([privacy policy, Sep 28, 2026](https://www.veeza.ai/en/privacy)).
- **Outcome and refund mismatch:** Embassies control visa decisions, while service fees generally remain non-refundable after rejection; inability to book an appointment has a refund exception ([terms, Oct 13, 2026](https://www.veeza.ai/en/terms)).
