# Nitroduck -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

The strongest path is a strategic sale to a specialty-crop breeder. Nitroduck proposes fitting editing into breeders’ existing crosses and avoiding a separate plant-regeneration step. It estimates that a new trait costs $3 million to develop. A buyer could have a reason to own the service if it repeatedly lowers costs or shortens development across its breeding programs. Enza Zaden’s license from Pairwise shows that a vegetable breeder will pay for editing tools. Nitroduck’s own breeder demand, measured savings, and acquisition interest remain unverified. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction)

The company’s stated milestone is edited seed in a commercially relevant specialty crop, demonstrated in a breeder’s existing program. The founders’ prior plant-engineering work and a reported $500,000 convertible note support attempting the milestone. The test budget, timeline, and crop-level results remain undisclosed. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

For the proceeds comparison, assume a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, 5% ownership diluted to 3%, and no follow-ons. The 5× objective requires $2.5 million in cash within eight years, or about $83.3 million in distributable equity. Both sale scenarios assume an eligible holder sells its entire stake for cash within eight years, with pro rata distribution and zero deductions for senior equity claims. The reported note’s conversion and priority terms are unknown. (Market & Traction)

| Scenario | Assumed proceeds bridge | Holder cash |
|-----------|-------------------------|-------------|
| Modest sale | $40M enterprise value less $5M in net debt and deal adjustments gives $35M distributable equity | $1.05M (2.1×) |
| Strong sale | $100M enterprise value less $5M in net debt and deal adjustments gives $95M distributable equity | $2.85M (5.7×) |
| Prolonged private | Cash is reinvested throughout the eight years, with no share sale or payout | $0 |

The sale prices are illustrative. The memo leaves both a realizable price range and any independent route to holder cash unevidenced. (The Idea, Market & Traction)

### Countercase

A breeder can license existing editing tools, as Enza Zaden has done, while Nitroduck still needs to demonstrate a commercially relevant result. Plantik’s published patent application raises a specific freedom-to-operate risk a buyer would need to resolve. The memo leaves actual claim overlap and achievable sale proceeds uncertain, so neither the structural-block nor the inadequate-proceeds Pass gate is met. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a paid pilot in an existing specialty-crop breeding program produce edited seed and document repeatable savings against the breeder’s current workflow? A breeder-verified result with savings that could plausibly justify a sale above the modeled proceeds hurdle would move the action to Reach out to test the acquisition case.
