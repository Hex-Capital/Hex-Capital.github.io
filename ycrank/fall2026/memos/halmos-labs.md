## The Idea

- **Problem:** Biotech R&D teams spend lab time on trial-and-error and exhaustive screens, and Halmos identifies access to external experiments as a bottleneck ([Launch YC, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Uck-halmos-labs-intelligent-experiment-design), [YC company profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/halmos-labs)).
- **Approach:** Physical Validation connects AI research software with external labs, while the earlier Halmos Bio offering used literature analysis and simulation to help biotech teams choose experiments ([YC company profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/halmos-labs), [Swedish Tech News, Sep 2026](https://www.swedishtechnews.com/swedish-startup-halmos-labs-joins-y-combinator-to-build-automated-biotech-r-d/), [Launch YC, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Uck-halmos-labs-intelligent-experiment-design)).
- **Differentiation:** Halmos emphasizes lab sourcing for AI research software, Science Exchange bundles sourcing with enterprise purchasing and supplier compliance, and Ginkgo and Medra operate their own automated labs ([YC company profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/halmos-labs), [Science Exchange Marketplace](https://www.scienceexchange.com/platform/rd-marketplace), [Ginkgo Q3 results](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1830214/000162828026071022/ex991earningspr.htm), [Medra Lab 001](https://www.medra.ai/medra-lab-001)).
- **Business Model:** Physical Validation describes project-specific lab quotes, with no public platform fee found, and Halmos separately offers ADoor on a free tier or at $10 per workspace per month for Pro ([Halmos Labs on X via Teapawt, Nov 2026](https://www.teapawt.net/halmoslabs), [ADoor Pricing, Nov 2026](https://adoor.run/pricing/)).
- **TAM/SAM:** The broader global preclinical contract research organization market is estimated at $7.3B in 2026 with 8.4% annual growth through 2033 ([Grand View Research, 2026](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/preclinical-cro-market)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Halmos announced Physical Validation on X and used its earlier YC launch to solicit experiment-scoping inquiries from biotech R&D, process development, assay development, and manufacturing teams ([Halmos Labs on X via Teapawt, Nov 2026](https://www.teapawt.net/halmoslabs), [Launch YC, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Uck-halmos-labs-intelligent-experiment-design)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Physical Validation restricts bulk redistribution of its catalogue and reviews supplier-submitted corrections before publication ([Physical Validation Terms](https://physicalvalidation.com/terms), [Physical Validation Privacy](https://physicalvalidation.com/privacy)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Repeat orders could improve lab matching through accumulated fulfillment data, but repeat-order volume and matching performance are undisclosed ([Physical Validation Privacy](https://physicalvalidation.com/privacy)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** [Inferred]: An existing R&D marketplace could add comparable sourcing features using its supplier relationships, and Science Exchange already offers an AI assistant within its marketplace ([Science Exchange Marketplace](https://www.scienceexchange.com/platform/rd-marketplace)).

## Key Risks

- **Wet-lab validation:** The quantified Halmos Bio benchmark measures simulated evaluations, and the YC profile gives a future readout date for the company’s Physical Validation demonstrations ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Uck-halmos-labs-intelligent-experiment-design), [YC company profile, Nov 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/halmos-labs)).
- **Supplier execution:** [Inferred]: Service quality depends on external labs, and Halmos’s terms say buyers should verify provider information directly ([Physical Validation Terms](https://physicalvalidation.com/terms)).
- **Research confidentiality:** Physical Validation’s privacy notice says diagnostic records can retain scientific materials without automatic expiry and provides a process for requesting deletion ([Physical Validation Privacy](https://physicalvalidation.com/privacy)).
