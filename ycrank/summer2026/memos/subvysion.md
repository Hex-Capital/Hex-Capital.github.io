## The Idea

- **Problem:** Construction contractors rely on 811 markings and legacy drawings that may be inaccurate, with SubVysion stating that locator errors or inaccurate maps cause one-third of utility strikes ([SubVysion LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/subvysion), Aug 2026).
- **Approach:** A rover collects multi-sensor ground-penetrating-radar and surface-positioning data, which SubVysion aligns, validates and converts into georeferenced 3D utility maps viewable through a mobile AR application ([SubVysion](https://subvysion.com/), Aug 2026; [Penn Engineering](https://www.linkedin.com/school/penn-engineering/), Aug 2026).
- **Differentiation:** SubVysion emphasizes phone-based AR for crews, while Exodigo combines records, on-site scanning and engineering services, 4M primarily compiles public and private utility records into a planning map, and RodRadar detects utilities from excavation equipment in real time ([SubVysion](https://subvysion.com/); [Exodigo](https://www.exodigo.com/); [4M Analytics](https://www.4manalytics.com/); [RodRadar](https://rodradar.com/about/), Aug 2026).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Project-based mapping and recurring access to site maps are possible monetization paths because the website requests site, utility and timeline details for a tailored demonstration but publishes no pricing ([SubVysion](https://subvysion.com/), Aug 2026).
- **TAM/SAM:** The global underground-utility-mapping market was estimated at $1.57 billion in 2025 and forecast to reach $4.08 billion by 2034 at an 11.18% CAGR, while the U.S. segment was estimated at $294.4 million in 2025 ([Fortune Business Insights](https://www.fortunebusinessinsights.com/underground-utility-mapping-market-105689), Jul 2026; [Global Industry Analysts](https://www.marketresearch.com/Global-Industry-Analysts-v1039/Underground-Utility-40831005/), May 2026).
- **GTM / Distribution:** The founders report acquiring their first customer and investor through cold calling, securing job-site contracts and working directly with contractors and crews ([Wenson Tang on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/wenson-tang), Aug 2026; [SubVysion](https://subvysion.com/), Aug 2026).

## Key Risks

- **Environmental sensing limits:** FHWA states that moisture and clay attenuate GPR signals and that small-diameter utilities can be difficult to distinguish, directly constraining the conditions under which centimeter-level maps can be produced ([FHWA](https://infotechnology.fhwa.dot.gov/gpr-ground-penetrating-radar-metal-steel/), Aug 2026).
- **Accuracy-validation gap:** SubVysion advertises centimeter accuracy and “100% visibility,” but no independent accuracy study, detection-rate dataset or certification was found on its public website ([SubVysion](https://subvysion.com/), Aug 2026).
- **Field-operations scalability:** [Inferred]: Each project requires sensor collection, data alignment, quality control and validation, making throughput dependent on rover deployment and processing capacity ([SubVysion](https://subvysion.com/), Aug 2026).
- **Capitalized competitors:** Exodigo reports more than $200 million in venture funding, 86.1 million square feet mapped and over 100 customers, while 4M and RodRadar have funded overlapping systems ([Exodigo](https://www.exodigo.com/), Aug 2026; [4M funding announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/4m-analytics-secures-30-million-in-series-a-extension-to-become-the-google-maps-of-the-subsurface-301628430.html), Sep 2022; [CTech](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/l07otifyb), Mar 2025).
