# Obia -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path B — New economies** is Obia’s strongest path. Obia says its AI compares rents, checks permitted uses, and screens for loading and power constraints before a local broker handles tours and negotiations. It reports screening sites for a logistics operator by permitted use, service-area coverage, and projected throughput per dollar of rent. **Scenario assumption:** If automated screening materially cuts broker hours at equivalent accuracy, Obia could profitably serve smaller industrial tenants whose searches are too labor-intensive for the fees they generate. The memo provides no like-for-like labor comparison or evidence that Obia has reached this segment. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

Obia’s founder reports $1.05M in closed lease value over 22 days. Obia says landlords pay its brokerage fee, so the reported lease value establishes neither collected fees nor repeat demand. The scale test is collected fees per close less the cost of acquiring, screening, and servicing all searches needed to produce that close, including failed searches, broker review, tours, negotiations, and local compliance. The memo leaves those economics unmeasured. The founders’ growth and software engineering backgrounds and prior work together support early execution. San Francisco and Fall 2026 YC participation make a U.S. seed-stage fit plausible. (Market & Traction, The Idea, Key Facts, Key Risks, Founders & Team)

### Countercase

Obia may make existing brokerage work faster while serving the same transactions. Stale property data can send uncertain results back to brokers, and tours, negotiations, and state-specific requirements preserve human work. With equally capable models, a tenant could choose another local broker. The memo documents adjacent AI tools at CBRE and TenantBase, while Obia’s claimed reuse of tenant requirements has no measured matching gains. Local execution gives tenants a reason to use a broker, but the memo provides no evidence of why they would choose Obia under parity. It also identifies no bottleneck Obia controls for a separate Path A case. Both Path B conditions remain unproven, and the memo contains no direct evidence that defeats either. A feasible test supports Watch. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can Obia repeatedly close fee-paying leases for small industrial tenants whose searches conventional brokers decline at comparable fees, while those tenants still choose Obia when another local broker offers equivalent AI screening? A matched cohort trial should document prior broker declinations, verify comparable screening accuracy, and compare collected fees and all-in costs per close, including failed searches and broker exceptions. Repeat profitable closes and a demonstrated preference for Obia under parity would support Reach out if the updated score reaches 65 with at least medium confidence. A representative trial showing that review costs erase the savings or that buyers readily switch under parity would close Path B and move the action to Pass. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Risks)
