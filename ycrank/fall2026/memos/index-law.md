## The Idea

- **Problem:** Startups and growing businesses that rely on hourly outside counsel can face contract delays while finding counsel, explaining their business and coordinating reviews ([YC launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/XP1-meet-mike-1-your-company-s-ai-lawyer), Nov 2026).
- **Approach:** Mike‑1 uses company documents and past decisions to review agreements, prepare redlines and signatures, and brief vetted independent lawyers when professional judgment is needed ([Mike features](https://usemike.com/features), [YC launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/XP1-meet-mike-1-your-company-s-ai-lawyer)).
- **Differentiation:** Mike‑1 takes assignments directly from businesses and routes selected matters to independent counsel, while Harvey sells workflows to law firms and in-house legal teams and Crosby delivers contract work through its own law firm ([Index Law](https://indexlaw.com/), [Harvey](https://www.harvey.ai/blog/harvey-raises-at-dollar11-billion-valuation-to-scale-agents-across-law-firms-and-enterprises), [Crosby](https://crosby.ai/blog/series-b-planting-our-flag)).
- **Business Model:** The research preview offers free early access and advertises upcoming Pro at $149/month, Teams at $499/month and custom Enterprise pricing, with independent lawyer fees agreed separately ([Mike pricing, via search snippet](https://usemike.com/?_hop=1), [Mike changelog](https://usemike.com/changelog), Nov 2026).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** Index Law solicits contracts from founders and general counsel through Launch YC and founder-led setup calls, offers a free preview waitlist and publishes a public contract template library ([YC launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/XP1-meet-mike-1-your-company-s-ai-lawyer), [Mike site, via search snippet](https://usemike.com/?_hop=1), [Index Law template library](https://templates.indexlaw.com/)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Mike’s workspace retains company documents, redlines and matter history for use in later assignments, providing a potential source of switching costs ([Mike features](https://usemike.com/features), [Mike Privacy Policy](https://usemike.com/privacy)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: A counsel network with repeat assignments across jurisdictions could improve specialist matching over time, although Index Law’s public recruitment page gives no figures for network size or repeat assignments ([Mike’s Partners](https://lawyers.usemike.com/)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** Existing AI vendors can offer customers basic contract review and NDA triage through tools they already use, as Anthropic does through Claude Cowork’s configurable legal plugin ([Anthropic Legal plugin](https://claude.com/marketplace/plugins/legal)).

## Key Risks

- **Licensing boundary:** Contract workflows can reach questions that require licensed legal judgment, so Index Law routes professional advice and representation to independent licensed lawyers ([State Bar of California](https://www.calbar.ca.gov/public/concerns-about-attorney/avoid-legal-services-fraud/unauthorized-practice-law), [Index Law](https://indexlaw.com/)).
- **Technical reliability:** Errors in multi-step legal agents can propagate into later outputs, while Mike‑1 lets users approve work and escalate matters to counsel ([LexAgentHallu](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09754), [YC launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/XP1-meet-mike-1-your-company-s-ai-lawyer)).
- **Document confidentiality:** Mike sends relevant portions of customer documents to model providers, with encryption in transit, access controls and provider terms that prohibit training on customer content ([Mike Privacy Policy](https://usemike.com/privacy), Nov 2026).
- **Name confusion:** The Canadian firm INDEX Law Firm Inc. uses the similar domain `indexlaws.com`, while the YC company identifies itself as ASI Labs, Inc. doing business as Index Law ([INDEX Law Firm Inc.](https://indexlaws.com/), [Mike Privacy Policy](https://usemike.com/privacy)).
