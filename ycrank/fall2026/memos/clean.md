## The Idea

- **Problem:** Sellers using exported contact lists must manually qualify buyers and refresh stale records; customer Vivameda reported that half one list’s contacts had bounced or changed roles ([Clean case study](https://www.tryclean.ai/customers/vivameda), retrieved Oct 2026).
- **Approach:** Clean combines dated customs shipments, permits, loans and contracts into company timelines, identifies buying windows, and supplies decision-maker contacts ([Clean](https://www.tryclean.ai/)).
- **Differentiation:** Clean positions itself against Clay’s configurable workflows and Apollo’s contact database through completed research using operational records, although both competitors also offer research automation ([Clean FAQ](https://www.tryclean.ai/faq); [Clay](https://www.clay.com/blog/series-d); [Apollo coverage](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/30/apollo-debuts-ai-app-builder-intelligence-layer-and-signal-based-outreach-system/)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Recurring software subscriptions, supported by the CEO’s ARR claim; public tier, seat and consumption prices: No public data found ([Pavan’s LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/pavankumarny)).
- **TAM/SAM:** Broader intent-data software spending is estimated at $4.75B in 2026, growing 16.84% annually through 2031; Clean-specific SAM: No public data found ([Mordor Intelligence](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/intent-data-software-market), 2026).
- **GTM / Distribution:** The CEO describes handwritten letters, personalized outbound and founder events, while access requires booking a demo ([Pavan’s LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/pavankumarny); [Clean contact](https://www.tryclean.ai/contact)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Clean claims its own datasets and source-linked record aggregation, but public evidence of exclusive data rights or patents was not found ([Clean FAQ](https://www.tryclean.ai/faq)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Linking observed signals to subsequent purchases could improve prediction, but public longitudinal validation was not found.
- **Market structure:** [Inferred]: No structural barrier identified at this stage, given Clay’s announced combination of company context, external signals and research agents ([Clay](https://www.clay.com/blog/series-d), Sep 2026).
- **Commoditization risk:** [Inferred]: Clay and Apollo could extend existing research workflows into operational-record analysis; implementation difficulty cannot be established publicly ([Clay](https://www.clay.com/blog/series-d); [Apollo coverage](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/30/apollo-debuts-ai-app-builder-intelligence-layer-and-signal-based-outreach-system/)).

## Key Risks

- **Prediction accuracy:** [Inferred]: Operational events may indicate expansion without demand for a particular seller’s product; Clean exposes underlying records, but public predictive benchmarks were not found ([Clean](https://www.tryclean.ai/)).
- **Data dependency:** [Inferred]: Licensed company/contact providers create availability and commercial dependencies; Clean retains record provenance and verifies contacts ([FAQ](https://www.tryclean.ai/faq)).
- **Incumbent overlap:** Clay announced context-aware research agents and signal monitoring in September 2026, overlapping Clean’s workflow ([Clay](https://www.clay.com/blog/series-d)).
- **Workspace confidentiality:** Connected prospect emails become visible to teammates; Clean states that storage is encrypted and connections are optional ([Privacy policy](https://www.tryclean.ai/privacy), Oct 2026).
