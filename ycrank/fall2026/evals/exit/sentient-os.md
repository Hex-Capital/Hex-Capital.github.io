# Sentient OS -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

A strategic acquisition by a desktop productivity vendor is the strongest supported route. Sentient’s shipped Mac app builds context overnight on 8 GB Apple silicon Macs and uses it for reply drafts and cross-app actions. A vendor such as Raycast could acquire Sentient to add those workflows faster than building them internally. That rationale is an inference. The memo supplies no measured workflow savings or buyer interest. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction)

Sentient reports more than 10,000 users at its October launch and more than 30 enterprises using the app, with payment and retention unverified. That adoption and the reported pre-seed funding make a focused paid deployment plausible. The milestone is sustained use and measured time savings in a paid deployment approved by the customer’s security team. The memo leaves runway and deployment costs open. The public AGPL release and commercial license offering make a sale of the business and the rights it controls plausible. Code and model rights, customer consents, and holder participation would need verification. (Market & Traction, Key Facts, Key Risks)

The proceeds benchmark assumes a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, with 5% starting ownership diluted to 3% and no follow-ons. The holder needs $2.5 million in gross cash within eight years. The illustrative sale cases assume an all-cash sale of the entire company within that window, full holder eligibility, and a pro rata waterfall after debt, deal costs, and senior claims. The sale prices are scenario assumptions.

- **Modest sale.** A $50 million enterprise value with zero deductions yields $50 million in distributable equity and $1.5 million to the holder, or 3×.
- **Strong sale.** A $100 million enterprise value with zero deductions yields $3 million to the holder, or 6×. It can absorb about $16.7 million in deductions before falling below the 5× objective. With $20 million in deductions, the holder receives $2.4 million, or 4.8×.
- **Prolonged private.** Continued operation with cash reinvested and no eligible share purchase or payout yields zero realized proceeds within eight years. The memo supplies no supported independent route to holder liquidity. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Facts)

### Countercase

A strategic buyer could build overlapping features using public local-inference tools. Apple already documents overlapping capabilities, and Sentient’s editable Markdown makes user context portable. A buyer may therefore see little reason to pay a premium for the technology or user base. This is a concrete risk to buyer willingness to pay. The evidence leaves the acquisition price unresolved and does not trigger the Pass gate for a structural block or materially inadequate proceeds. (Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can Sentient show a strategic buyer that acquiring it is worth enough to leave at least $83.3 million in distributable equity? A buyer’s build-versus-buy assessment grounded in repeat use and measured savings from a paid production deployment, with a price range that clears the hurdle after deductions, would move the action from Watch to Reach out.
