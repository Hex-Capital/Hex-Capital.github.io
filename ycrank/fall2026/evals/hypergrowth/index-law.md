# Index Law -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path B — Repeatable sales with account expansion.** The initial buyer is a startup or growing business that relies on outside counsel and regularly needs commercial contracts reviewed. Index Law’s free preview uses Launch YC, a waitlist, and founder-led setup calls to bring buyers in. The company reports turning one contract around in under 60 minutes. That is an early signal of value on a live task, with paid conversion and repeat use still unverified. (The Idea; Market & Traction; Key Facts)

The growth hypothesis is that a buyer starts with contract review, then sends more agreements through the same workspace as it accumulates documents, redlines, and prior decisions. For the next thousand buyers, that would require a standard way to turn a prospect into an active account and configure its first workflow. In a second jurisdiction, the scenario assumes the same buyer and core workflow carry over. Index Law would still need to validate local review rules, the boundary for licensed advice, and access to counsel for escalations. The memo documents no paid test of that rollout. (The Idea; Defensibility; Key Risks)

### Countercase

Each new buyer could require founder time to configure its playbook, while additional contracts drive model usage, support, and counsel coordination. A new jurisdiction could add legal validation and counsel recruitment. The advertised plans are upcoming, and independent counsel fees are billed separately, so the reported savings on legal quotes leave Index Law’s own contribution per account unknown. Founder-led setup and the licensing boundary make this a credible constraint. The memo has no measured rollout showing that effort scales proportionally with volume, so the structural Pass gate remains unmet. (The Idea; Market & Traction; Key Risks; Key Facts)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a small cohort of independent businesses in a second jurisdiction convert to paid use through the same acquisition and setup process, then submit a second live contract at its normal buying cadence? Reach out if account records show that pattern with a shared workflow and measured localization, setup, model, support, and counsel-routing costs consistent with positive contribution as volume grows. Keep watching while that result remains untested.
