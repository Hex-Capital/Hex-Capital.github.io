# Tire Swing -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**New Economies** is the strongest path. Affordable housing operators spend staff time collecting documents, checking income and eligibility, chasing missing evidence, and assembling certifications. Tire Swing says it automates extraction, rule checks, and packet assembly, with staff reviewing the source evidence and approving each file. A plausible new use is timely certification at understaffed properties that currently defer files because manual review is too costly. That use remains a scenario assumption. The memo gives no measured baseline for staff time or cost per accurate, approved certification. (The Idea, Key Risks)

The company reports thousands of compliance checks and support for property-specific rules. Configured rules, existing forms, and accumulated review histories could give operators a reason to keep paying under model parity. That switching-cost hypothesis remains untested. The founders report dozens of live properties across eight states, and the subscription terms document how fees are set. Paid adoption, renewals, and demand from previously excluded operators remain unverified. The documented pivot into housing and the founders’ backgrounds in forensics and rules-based engineering support seed-stage execution credit. Field accuracy and exception-handling performance remain unmeasured. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Facts, Founders & Team)

A repeat deployment would add a property under a supported program. For the buyer, labor savings per accurate, approved file must cover the allocated subscription fee after staff review and rework. For Tire Swing, subscription revenue must cover onboarding, rule maintenance, AI and OCR processing, and support for exceptions. Reusing rules across properties could improve contribution, but the memo gives no per-property cost or file-volume data to test that assumption. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Countercase

Tire Swing may be making an existing compliance workflow more efficient while leaving staff approval as the binding constraint. Roost markets source-linked AI income calculations, and Yardi bundles certification with property accounting. If those alternatives deliver comparable results, operators may have little reason to buy or renew a separate subscription. The overlap makes substitution credible. Functional parity and actual switching remain untested, so the gate stays open for Watch. The memo establishes neither scarcity nor control for a separate scarce-complement path. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Will independent operators that previously deferred certifications because of labor cost renew paid Tire Swing subscriptions after testing an alternative with comparable functionality? Reach out if a cohort shows that lower all-in cost per accurate, approved certification, including staff review and rework, makes the deferred work viable and operators renew because Tire Swing handles their property-specific workflows better. Pass if the trial shows the cost reduction leaves the work uneconomic or buyers readily switch at parity.
