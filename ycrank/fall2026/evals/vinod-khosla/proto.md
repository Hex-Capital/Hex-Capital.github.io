# Proto -- Vinod Khosla Evaluation

Proto automates interface states, restores design context through comments, and turns prototypes into pull requests. The consequential possibility is collapsing the work between a product requirement and functioning software. But the dossier establishes only part of that chain: engineers still complete the remaining connections. I cannot turn faster prototype generation into a claim that software production becomes 100x cheaper. The cited $590.38 million prototyping market measures an adjacent software category; it does not quantify the labor or expertise Proto could eliminate.

The technical question is whether automatic state construction removes a binding constraint that Figma and coding agents cannot readily remove themselves. Figma opened its canvas to agents in March 2026; Magic Patterns now combines design context, variants, and coding-agent handoff. Proto’s state-restoring comments are a concrete product distinction. They are not evidence of a 5–10x discontinuity. There is no comparative result on completion time, state coverage, or engineering rework. Equally, there is no evidence establishing that this is merely a standard-model wrapper. I assign neither technical superiority nor an architectural red flag on speculation.

Maayan Albert is the strongest reason to challenge my pass. Co-creating Stamper, an artboard-oriented programming environment, and launching Browserbase’s Director demonstrate relevant building experience across design and developer tools. That combination could let Proto discover a better abstraction for making software, rather than compete feature by feature. Figma’s reported $370.1 million quarterly revenue also establishes substantial existing expenditure in the surrounding workflow. My willingness to fund execution-heavy software businesses matters here: scientific novelty is not the only route to a large outcome. But this record does not document belief revision or complementary recruiting, and the dossier does not establish the exceptional instigator, quantified consequence, or technical trajectory needed to override the missing primary gates.

The first experiment should attack engineering handoff. Faster prototypes have limited leverage if integration consumes the saved hours. Proto needs to measure the complete journey from requirement to accepted production change against the existing Figma/Cursor workflow. The dossier supplies no threshold, experiment budget, or stop condition, so I cannot credit a risk-removal process. Its 65 company-account followers and October 31 coverage do not resolve that uncertainty. Missing revenue at this stage is ordinary; those publicity signals simply provide little evidence about the mechanism.

Economics remain unspecified: subscription pricing is inferred, and inference costs, human intervention, and customer savings are undisclosed. This earns the absent-evidence default, without assuming subsidy dependence. I would pass with my own money today. My one verifiable flip condition is an independently auditable pilot across five unrelated teams showing a sustained 10x reduction in total labor per accepted production change versus their Figma/Cursor baseline, with unchanged defect rates and positive contribution margin at the price paid. That result would connect Proto’s promising workflow to both technical leverage and economic consequence.
