The 4,188-experiment campaign is the strongest case against that forecast: hiloop already demonstrated autonomous iteration at meaningful scale, beat the cited autoresearch result with 0.9016 validation bits-per-byte, and can deploy inside customer clouds with brokered secrets. The analogy breaks because hiloop has no identified structural barrier, no retained proprietary dataset, and no equivalent of Ro’s 50-state prescription rails. I would flip from Pass if hiloop verifies at least $1 million ARR from three unaffiliated customers at greater than 60% gross margin by July 2027.
