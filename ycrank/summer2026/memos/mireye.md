## The Idea

- **Problem:** Developers building agents for real estate, data-center, industrial, energy, insurance, and other physical-world workflows must combine fragmented GIS maps, utility portals, census tables, and government documents that were designed for human use ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SBp-mireye-the-easiest-way-to-build-agentic-applications-for-the-physical-world), Aug 2026).
- **Approach:** Mireye accepts natural-language questions, addresses, or coordinates through one API and MCP server, resolves locations, retrieves typed fields, and returns each result with its source, fetch timestamp, and confidence level ([Mireye](https://www.mireye.com/), Aug 2026).
- **Differentiation:** Mireye emphasizes cited government and county facts plus on-demand construction of missing fields, while Mapbox supplies global maps, navigation, search, and location agents, CARTO operates agentic GIS over customer warehouse data, and Fused runs programmable serverless geospatial workflows ([Mireye](https://www.mireye.com/), [Mapbox](https://docs.mapbox.com/help/getting-started/location-ai/), [CARTO](https://www.carto.com/ai-agents/), [Fused](https://docs.fused.io/blog/fused-redefines-geospatial-with-instant-maps/), Aug 2026).
- **Business Model:** Credit-based plans include Free at $0 for 5,000 monthly credits, Build at $19 monthly for 25,000 credits, Growth at $99 monthly for 120,000 credits, $1 per additional 1,000 credits on paid plans, and custom enterprise pricing ([Mireye pricing](https://www.mireye.com/pricing), Aug 2026).
- **TAM/SAM:** The broader global geospatial-analytics market was estimated at $117.0 billion in 2024 and projected to reach $232.9 billion by 2030 at a 12.2% CAGR, but no public SAM estimate was found for agent-oriented physical-world data APIs ([Global Industry Analysts via MarketResearch.com](https://www.marketresearch.com/Global-Industry-Analysts-v1039/Geospatial-Analytics-38387694/), 2025).
- **GTM / Distribution:** The founder reports manually identifying the first 50 prospects, testing outbound messaging until reaching a 10% response rate, and focusing initially on data-center developers, supplemented by self-service signup and demo booking ([Ansh Chokshi](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/ansh-chokshi_im-a-technical-founder-who-had-never-sold-activity-7482836368432459777-7ohR), Jul 2026; [Mireye](https://www.mireye.com/), Aug 2026).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Mireye says its catalog combines 85 sources and returns a source URL, fetch timestamp, and confidence level for each field, while accepted field requests become available to future callers ([Mireye](https://www.mireye.com/), Aug 2026).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Repeated customer field requests could expand a normalized, provenance-linked data catalog and reduce future query-fulfillment costs, but Mireye discloses no request volume, repeat-use, or customer-retention data.
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** Mapbox already offers hosted and self-hosted MCP access to global geocoding, routing, travel-time, and location metadata, while CARTO and Fused provide alternative agentic or programmable geospatial infrastructure ([Mapbox](https://docs.mapbox.com/help/getting-started/location-ai/), [CARTO](https://www.carto.com/ai-agents/), [Fused](https://docs.fused.io/blog/fused-redefines-geospatial-with-instant-maps/), Aug 2026).

## Key Risks

- **Geographic coverage:** The main catalog covers only the United States, with Canada supported solely for drive-time analysis, limiting workflows requiring broader coverage ([Mireye FAQ](https://www.mireye.com/), Aug 2026).
- **Source freshness and dependency:** Refresh timing follows underlying providers such as FEMA and USDA rather than a uniform Mireye schedule, although responses expose fetch timestamps and source provenance ([Mireye FAQ](https://www.mireye.com/), Aug 2026).
- **Incumbent substitution:** Mapbox already offers a unified MCP endpoint with global maps, navigation, search, and hosted or self-hosted deployment, creating functional overlap for agent location queries ([Mapbox](https://docs.mapbox.com/help/getting-started/location-ai/), Aug 2026).
- **Name collision:** Search results for Mireye overlap with MiREYE Imaging, a veterinary and human imaging company founded in 2021 with 1,210 LinkedIn followers, creating potential discovery and brand confusion ([MiREYE Imaging LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/mireye-imaging), Jul 2026).
