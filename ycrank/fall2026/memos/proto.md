## The Idea

- **Problem:** Proto says product designers building interactive prototypes have moved into developer IDEs because canvas-based design tools have lagged their AI workflows ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Whf-proto-the-product-designer-s-ide), Nov 2026).
- **Approach:** Proto turns prompts and inputs such as Linear tickets or Notion requirements into working prototypes whose variants designers can compare, link into user flows, and share for collaboration ([Proto on X, mirrored by teapawt](https://www.xitter.cf/proto_ide), Sep 2026, [Bitmap Studio](https://www.bitmapstudio.com/work/proto), Q4 2026).
- **Differentiation:** Proto adds visual variant comparison and flow mapping to the coding-agent workflow represented by Claude Code, while Figma Make’s local-code beta puts direct edits to production code inside Figma ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-4), [Bitmap Studio](https://www.bitmapstudio.com/work/proto), [Figma](https://www.figma.com/blog/figma-make-now-on-your-local-code)).
- **Business Model:** No public data found for pricing ([AI Tool Index](https://aitoolindex.org/tool/proto/), Nov 2026). Website not accessible at time of research.
- **TAM/SAM:** The Business Research Company estimates the broader global prototyping software market at $1.81B in 2026 and forecasts 19.5% CAGR through 2030, including segments outside product design such as hardware emulation ([The Business Research Company](https://www.thebusinessresearchcompany.com/report/prototyping-software-global-market-report), 2026).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Proto solicits alpha signups on its website through Launch YC and Albert’s X launch post ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Whf-proto-the-product-designer-s-ide), [Albert on X, mirrored by teapawt](https://www.nitter.gq/maayanalbert?sort=popular), Nov 2026).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** No defensibility signals found in public sources.
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Shared variants, flow maps, and review history could create switching costs if teams return to Proto across projects, a usage pattern for which no public data was found ([Bitmap Studio](https://www.bitmapstudio.com/work/proto), Q4 2026).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** Figma put code-backed prototype comparison on its Design canvas in October 2026, and Onlook already offers an open-source editor that writes visual edits back to code ([Figma Q3 results](https://investor.figma.com/news-events/news/news-details/2026/Figma-Announces-Third-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results/), [Onlook on YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/onlook)).

## Key Risks

- **Incumbent substitution:** [Inferred]: Figma’s Code Layers can absorb prototype exploration and review within the canvas where design teams already work ([Figma Q3 results](https://investor.figma.com/news-events/news/news-details/2026/Figma-Announces-Third-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results/), Nov 2026).
- **Prototype-to-production handoff:** [Inferred]: Agent-generated changes can create engineering review work when they must fit an existing codebase, which Proto addresses by splitting prototypes into smaller pull requests ([Founderland quoting Albert](https://founderland.ai/articles/proto-launches-ai-design-tool-with-cursor-accuracy-figma-fre-mvwyokth), Oct 2026).
- **Name ambiguity:** [Inferred]: Product discovery can be confused with Proto.io, which also sells web-based prototyping software, while Proto’s YC listing identifies it by `prototypes.fun` ([Proto.io](https://proto.io/), [Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/proto)).
