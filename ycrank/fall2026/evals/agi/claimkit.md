# ClaimKit -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Medium

### Company case

**Path B — New economies.** ClaimKit uses AI to assemble R&D evidence from engineering and accounting records, then has a tax specialist review the claim package. Fluer’s COO reports that the team could hand over raw data without writing the report. That supports a reduction in customer labor. The seed-stage hypothesis is that smaller eligible claims, previously skipped because preparation cost too much, could become worth filing. The memo gives no like-for-like measure of total labor or completed-claim quality, and ClaimKit’s stated outreach targets teams with 50+ engineers. (The Idea; Market & Traction)

Edexia’s COO reports switching from its accountants and completing the work faster at lower cost. ClaimKit reports 80+ clients across three countries, with payment and repeat filing unverified. ClaimKit’s specialist-reviewed service gives buyers a reason to pay for a completed filing, while preference for ClaimKit specifically remains untested under model parity. The founders’ R&D tax and engineering automation experience supports a plausible U.S. seed case. (Market & Traction; The Idea; Founders & Team; ClaimKit)

For the smaller-claim scenario, the buyer’s expected benefit must exceed fees and remaining internal work. ClaimKit’s contribution on the next filing would be collected fees less integration, inference, specialist review, rework, and filing costs. Its Australian terms document an upfront software fee and a tax-agent fee with a minimum. That minimum could preserve contribution while still pricing smaller claims out. The memo has no measured inputs for either side of that calculation. (The Idea; Key Risks)

### Countercase

ClaimKit may make an existing service faster for buyers who already file. Specialist review on every claim and rework from incomplete records could absorb the preparation savings. Neo.Tax already automates work from source records, RAD already collects evidence continuously, and ClaimKit says customers can export their evidence files. With comparable models and capital, another provider could plausibly offer similar preparation and specialist review. The memo establishes neither easy switching at full service parity nor control of a scarce specialist bottleneck that could support Path A. Both Path B conditions remain open, which supports Watch. (Market & Traction; Defensibility; Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Will a cohort of eligible companies that previously skipped filing because of cost pay ClaimKit a price that supports positive contribution when offered a comparable AI-enabled, specialist-reviewed alternative? A paid trial should track buyer and provider hours through lodgement, including exceptions and rework, and compare completed claims at equivalent quality. Consistent paid preference and positive contribution would support Reach out if the revised score clears 65. A representative trial showing that realistic costs erase the opening or that buyers readily switch at parity would move the action to Pass.
