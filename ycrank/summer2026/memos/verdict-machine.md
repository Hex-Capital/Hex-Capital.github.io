## The Idea

- **Problem:** Financial institutions operating digital assets must inventory on-chain exposure, screen wallets and prevent policy-violating transactions, while custody platforms, compliance screening and runtime-threat products address separate portions of that workflow ([YC profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/verdict-machine); [Hexagate product page](https://www.chainalysis.com/product/hexagate/)).
- **Approach:** Verdict Machine builds a live AI model of each institution’s on-chain environment, derives tailored controls and evaluates proposed transactions against active policies before execution ([YC profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/verdict-machine)).
- **Differentiation:** Blockaid emphasizes wallet-interface transaction simulation, Hypernative and Hexagate monitor protocols and ecosystems, while Verdict Machine describes an institution-specific model spanning inventory, posture, diligence, screening and pre-execution policy enforcement ([Blockaid](https://blockaid.io/transaction-security); [Hexagate](https://www.chainalysis.com/product/hexagate/); [founder announcement via LinkedIn](https://il.linkedin.com/in/or-steinitz-425b98a1)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Enterprise subscription or usage-based pricing sold to financial institutions and infrastructure providers, because the website was not accessible at the time of research and no public pricing was found.
- **TAM/SAM:** The global blockchain-security market was estimated at $6.37 billion in 2025 and forecast to reach $31.25 billion by 2033 at a 22.4% CAGR, while no public estimate isolates Verdict Machine’s financial-institution segment ([Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/blockchain-security-market-report)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** The company is using founder-led POCs with asset managers and digital-asset infrastructure providers and evaluations with bank digital-asset teams ([YC profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/verdict-machine)).

## Key Risks

- **Former-employer product overlap:** Check Point publicly deployed an AI-assisted blockchain firewall with monitoring and planned prevention under Danay’s leadership, creating direct feature overlap; no public IP dispute or commercial arrangement was found ([Rare Network interview](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/rare-evo-a-blockchain-event_rareevo25-activity-7366952746173804546-t_zM)).
- **POC conversion:** Public traction consists of POCs and active evaluations rather than disclosed contracts or revenue, so enterprise procurement and production conversion remain unresolved ([YC profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/verdict-machine)).
- **Policy-enforcement error:** Verdict Machine evaluates transactions with AI before execution, so [Inferred]: false positives could block legitimate institutional activity and false negatives could permit irreversible transactions ([YC profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/verdict-machine)).
- **Competitor data scale:** Blockaid processes more than 180 million transactions monthly and Hexagate draws on over 10 years of Chainalysis data across 75-plus chains, while Verdict Machine has disclosed no comparable dataset or chain coverage ([Blockaid](https://blockaid.io/transaction-security); [Hexagate](https://www.chainalysis.com/product/hexagate/)).
