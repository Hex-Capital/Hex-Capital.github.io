# BioDelivera -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Pass  
**Evidence confidence:** Medium

### Company case

BioDelivera has no supported path on either side of the AGI barbell. Its Guided Chimeric Vehicles (GCVs) are designed to deliver therapeutic cargo to selected cells. The memo identifies no AI workload that depends on GCVs or any scarce input they could supply, so Building blocks of superintelligence earns zero. The memo also identifies no AI-driven change in the cost per successful delivery program that would make a previously excluded buyer or use viable, so Industry enabled by superintelligence earns zero. (The Idea, Market & Traction)

The strongest commercial case is program-by-program licensing to pharma, an inferred business model. The company describes GCV as proprietary and rooted in five years of research. That supports modest credit for specialized know-how, with control of the underlying rights and differentiation against competing delivery programs still unverified. The founders’ co-authored drug-delivery study establishes relevant research execution. The company reports efficacy in tumors, muscle, and brain, while human selectivity and consistent manufacturing potency remain open. The memo provides no evidence of a BioDelivera buyer trial, paid commitment, or revenue-to-cost bridge for the next program, so customer demand and scale economics receive their defaults. Confidence is medium because the thesis gate is clear on the supplied evidence and the platform’s commercial performance remains largely untested. (The Idea, Defensibility, Founders & Team, Market & Traction, Key Risks, Key Facts)

### Countercase

An acquisition in adjacent targeted delivery and an existing pharma delivery-platform collaboration support the possibility of a licensing market. If BioDelivera’s reported efficacy translates into reproducible selectivity in humans, its physical delivery platform could give pharma partners a reason to license it. That case supports the underlying therapeutics business. The AGI gate remains closed because the memo establishes neither a scarce AI input nor a newly viable use enabled by AI. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a pharma partner show that an AI-enabled GCV workflow brings a previously shelved program below its threshold for funding? A partner-run comparison showing a lower fully loaded cost per program meeting the same qualification standard, followed by a commitment to proceed using BioDelivera’s delivery platform, would change the action to Reach out to test whether the result can repeat across programs.
