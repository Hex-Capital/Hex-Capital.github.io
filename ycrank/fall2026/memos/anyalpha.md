## The Idea

- **Problem:** Prediction-market traders manually combine data sites, news, X, and Discord, identify relevant contracts, and monitor entry conditions, making research and execution dependent on their availability ([company launch, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WOO-anyalpha-the-agentic-trading-platform)).
- **Approach:** AnyAlpha converts a trading thesis into an agent that identifies related markets, defines trading conditions, backtests historical performance, and executes through connected accounts ([AnyAlpha website](https://anyalpha.ai/)).
- **Differentiation:** AnyAlpha starts with prediction markets and user-defined theses, whereas Composer offers automated investment strategies and Olas Polystrat offers autonomous Polymarket trading ([AnyAlpha](https://anyalpha.ai/), [Composer](https://www.composer.trade/), [Olas](https://olas.network/blog/introducing-polystrat-an-autonomous-ai-prediction-agent-on-polymarket)).
- **Business Model:** Published terms describe automatically renewing subscriptions with possible trials, while the US iOS listing offers a free download and purchases labeled “BetAlpha” at $4.99, $6.99, $29.99, $69.99, and $79.99 without specifying their billing periods or current agent entitlements ([terms](https://anyalpha.ai/terms), [App Store, accessed Oct 27, 2026](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/anyalpha/id6762422950)).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** The company distributes through mobile apps and its YC launch, solicits direct conversations with power traders, and invites data providers to contact the CEO ([website](https://anyalpha.ai/), [YC launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WOO-anyalpha-the-agentic-trading-platform)).
