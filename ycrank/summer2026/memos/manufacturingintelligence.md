## The Idea

- **Problem:** Aerospace, medical-device, pressure-vessel, semiconductor-tooling, and custom-machinery manufacturers manually assign senior engineers to review drawings against ASME/ISO and GD&T requirements, taking days and risking errors that can scrap a $40,000 part ([Y Combinator, Aug 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/manufacturingintelligence)).
- **Approach:** Customers upload drawings and Hera checks GD&T conformance, code compliance, tolerance stacks, datums, contradictions, title blocks, and assemblies, returning traceable violations in minutes ([HERA website, Aug 2026](https://www.manufacturingintelligence.org/)).
- **Differentiation:** CoLab AutoReview checks CAD context and adds collaborative markup, Axial runs five specialized PDF-review passes, Redprint offers self-service per-drawing GD&T review, and Glypad adds raster-to-CAD conversion, while Hera emphasizes company-specific rules, assembly-package review, an evidence chain, and deployment where drawings remain on customer premises ([CoLab](https://www.colabsoftware.com/product/autoreview), [Axial](https://www.getaxial.com/), [Redprint](https://redprint.app/), [Glypad](https://glypad.com/), [HERA](https://www.manufacturingintelligence.org/)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Founder-led enterprise software sales likely follow a free, NDA-covered review of 20 drawings, followed by a paid deployment with company-specific rules; no paid pricing is published ([HERA website, Aug 2026](https://www.manufacturingintelligence.org/)).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** [Inferred]: Hera is pursuing design partners through factory visits, founder-led demos, and a free 20-drawing review aimed at aerospace, semiconductor, medical-device, pressure-vessel, and custom-machinery plants ([HERA website](https://www.manufacturingintelligence.org/), [Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/manufacturingintelligence)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Hera says each review contributes to a system of record for drawing errors and that checks are tailored to each customer’s internal standards, creating customer-specific data and workflow configuration ([Y Combinator, Aug 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/manufacturingintelligence)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Accumulated adjudicated violations, internal-rule mappings, and engineer feedback could improve customer-specific accuracy and switching costs, but no dataset scale, retention, or benchmark is public.
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** CoLab AutoReview, Axial, Redprint, and Glypad already advertise automated checks for dimensions, GD&T, drawing completeness, or ASME/ISO compliance ([CoLab](https://www.colabsoftware.com/product/autoreview), [Axial](https://www.getaxial.com/), [Redprint](https://redprint.app/), [Glypad](https://glypad.com/)).

## Key Risks

- **Automated-review errors:** A separate 2025 engineering-drawing parser reported a 5.23% hallucination rate, illustrating category-level accuracy risk; Hera mitigates this by stating that AI reads, math checks, evidence is traced, and the engineer retains sign-off ([arXiv, May 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.01530), [HERA website](https://www.manufacturingintelligence.org/)).
- **Competitive overlap:** CoLab, Axial, Redprint, and Glypad advertise overlapping drawing-completeness, dimension, tolerance, and standards checks, including self-service pricing as low as $1.98 per review ([CoLab](https://www.colabsoftware.com/product/autoreview), [Axial](https://www.getaxial.com/), [Redprint](https://redprint.app/), [Glypad](https://glypad.com/)).
- **Drawing confidentiality:** [Inferred]: Engineering drawings require deployment and procurement controls because Hera advertises that prints never leave customer walls and offers reviews under NDA, potentially lengthening onboarding ([HERA website, Aug 2026](https://www.manufacturingintelligence.org/)).
- **Name ambiguity:** “Hera” is also used by unrelated YC-backed calendar and motion-design companies, creating search and brand-disambiguation risk ([YC calendar Hera](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/hera), [YC motion-design Hera](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/hera-video)).
