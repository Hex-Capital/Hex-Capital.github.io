## The Idea

- **Problem:** AI-agent developers need executable travel search and booking, while browser automation encounters anti-bot blocking and conventional travel APIs impose structured queries and search-to-book constraints, according to [Jinko](https://gojinko.com/).
- **Approach:** Jinko exposes discovery, live pricing, booking, payments, and post-booking management through API, MCP, and CLI integrations ([Documentation](https://docs.gojinko.com/introduction)).
- **Differentiation:** Jinko combines flights, hotels, and cars with flexible discovery; [Duffel](https://duffel.com/) emphasizes flights and stays, [Nuitée Connect](https://liteapi.travel/) offers hotel/flight APIs and MCP, and [Etraveli Flight MCP](https://www.mynewsdesk.com/etraveli-group/pressreleases/etraveli-group-opens-its-flight-engine-to-ai-agents-with-new-flight-mcp-3474454) focuses on flights, leaving overlapping booking capabilities.
- **Business Model:** Flight discovery is free, confirmed flight bookings incur 1% of booking value, searches exceeding a 1,500:1 search-to-book ratio cost $0.005 each, and enterprise pricing and hotel/car commission sharing require sales contact ([Pricing, retrieved October 2026](https://gojinko.com/pricing/)).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** Jinko targets personal agents, work agents, banks, and platforms with existing audiences, with distribution through [Exa Connect](https://exa.ai/connect) and developer onboarding through its [documentation](https://docs.gojinko.com/introduction).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Jinko documents an operating travel-business layer encompassing bookings and servicing, and identifies itself as the contracting travel operator with professional liability coverage ([API terms](https://gojinko.com/api-terms/), [Sales terms](https://gojinko.com/terms/)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Integrations spanning search, checkout, and servicing could create switching costs, but retention and migration data are unavailable.
- **Market structure:** [Inferred]: No structural barrier identified at this stage, given existing supplier platforms’ ability to expose booking infrastructure through MCP ([Etraveli announcement, October 2026](https://www.mynewsdesk.com/etraveli-group/pressreleases/etraveli-group-opens-its-flight-engine-to-ai-agents-with-new-flight-mcp-3474454)).
- **Commoditization risk:** Adding MCP access is already occurring at travel-inventory providers, including [Nuitée](https://liteapi.travel/) and [Etraveli](https://www.mynewsdesk.com/etraveli-group/pressreleases/etraveli-group-opens-its-flight-engine-to-ai-agents-with-new-flight-mcp-3474454), although neither source establishes the effort required to reproduce Jinko’s complete product.

## Key Risks

- **Incumbent substitution:** [Inferred]: Existing booking providers can sell directly to agent developers, as demonstrated by Etraveli’s October 28, 2026 Flight MCP launch ([Company announcement](https://www.mynewsdesk.com/etraveli-group/pressreleases/etraveli-group-opens-its-flight-engine-to-ai-agents-with-new-flight-mcp-3474454)).
- **Search economics:** [Inferred]: Agent exploration can generate processing costs without bookings, reflected in Jinko’s excess-search fee; that fee provides a disclosed cost-recovery mechanism ([Pricing](https://gojinko.com/pricing/)).
- **Supplier and servicing dependency:** Jinko sells services fulfilled by independent suppliers while assuming contracting-operator responsibilities, with professional liability insurance described as mitigation ([Sales terms](https://gojinko.com/terms/)).
- **Name ambiguity:** [Inferred]: Search discovery can confuse this business with the unrelated Jinko cancer-care app, whose downloads and funding must be excluded ([Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=en_GB&id=care.jinko.v2)).
