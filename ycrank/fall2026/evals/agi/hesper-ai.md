# Hesper AI -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Medium

### Company case

Hesper has a plausible path on the industry side of the AGI thesis. P&C adjusters and special investigation teams manually chase records, verify providers, and reconcile evidence. Hesper quotes $1,500–$2,500 for an outsourced investigation. Its agents gather records, call providers, query databases, and assemble a source-linked case file for adjuster review. Hesper reports that one MGA spent 82% less adjuster time per claim during a two-week test. The investment assumption is that these savings could make routine investigation viable for lower-value claims that buyers currently leave uninvestigated. The memo establishes neither that these claims entered the workflow nor the fully loaded cost of completing them. (*The Idea*, *Market & Traction*)

Under model parity, customers would still need evidence gathered from providers and databases, findings returned to their claims system, and an auditable review process. Hesper lists integrations with Guidewire, Duck Creek, and Majesco. Those integrations could support switching costs if carriers embed their own rules and approvals, though retention is unverified. Hesper’s claims workflow supplies no evidenced scarce input to AI deployment, so the building-block path earns zero. (*The Idea*, *Defensibility*, *Key Risks*)

Hesper reports paying production customers and pilots across carriers, MGAs, and TPAs, with repeat demand and pilot conversion unverified. The team rebuilt its initial fraud-scoring product around investigations and brings relevant document automation and insurance sales experience. Dependable handling of investigation exceptions remains unproven. Usage-based pricing is described, but realized prices and delivery costs per completed investigation are missing, so scale economics stays at the absent-evidence default. (*Market & Traction*, *Founders & Team*, *Key Facts*, *Key Risks*)

### Countercase

The MGA result could reflect faster handling of claims it already investigated. A two-week reduction in adjuster time leaves open how much review, rework, and missed or incorrect flags cost at routine volume. Duck Creek’s launch of native insurance-agent infrastructure also gives customers a path to build similar workflows inside their existing system. If the savings only accelerate existing investigations, the newly viable use gate fails and the action becomes Pass. The short operating test supports keeping the mechanism on Watch while that gap is tested. (*Market & Traction*, *Defensibility*, *Key Risks*)

### Decisive diligence question

Will an MGA repeatedly pay Hesper to investigate a defined class of claims it previously skipped on cost grounds? A buyer-verified cohort at commercial pricing would move the action to Reach out if it meets the buyer’s quality standard and affordability threshold after adjuster review and rework. If it only speeds up investigations the MGA already performed, move to Pass.
