# Reific -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path A — Scarce complements.** Reific offers clocked replay of real operating records so labs can train agents to act on the information available at the time of a decision. Static benchmarks that reveal the full record can obscure whether an agent acts too early or too late. Reific reports that training one open model raised its full-solve rate on unseen tasks from 25.6% to 39.5%. That is seed-stage evidence that the environments can improve performance on timed tasks. Whether the improvement transfers to a lab’s own workload remains unverified. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

**Scenario assumption.** If cheaper frontier intelligence leads labs to train more agents for time-sensitive work, labs may spend more engineering time sourcing records they have rights to train on, reconstructing what was knowable at each decision point, and validating the scoring. Reific could capture that spend by licensing environments built from recurring records that labs would struggle to obtain and prepare themselves. The product is live across three task families, and the founder’s time-series research is relevant to the work. Reific also reports adding transformations and release checks after a test model reconstructed personal details. A licensed environment is a plausible unit of expansion if multiple labs can use it and license revenue covers record access, privacy checks, and practitioner review. The memo gives no realized prices, delivery costs, or buyer commitments. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction, Founders & Team, Key Risks, Key Facts)

### Countercase

Labs with suitable records could build comparable environments using HUD’s SDK or Prime Intellect’s open tooling. Reific’s supplier intake asks about exclusivity, and the memo identifies no signed exclusive rights or independent buyer comparison showing that Reific saves meaningful time or delivers better results. The updated industry leaderboard leader reached 60.2%, weakening the launch’s claim that every task family was below 50%. The decisive uncertainty is whether Reific controls a scarce input that matters to buyers. Substitution is credible and remains untested, leaving the case at Watch with Low confidence. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Will an independent lab commit budget for a follow-on license after testing a Reific environment built on verified, hard-to-replicate recurring data rights against the lab’s best in-house or open-tooling alternative? A paid commitment backed by materially less engineering time at comparable task quality, or better performance on the lab’s timed workload at comparable cost, would move the action to Reach out. If the alternative matches both quality and delivery effort using records the lab can readily access, the tested scarce-complement case closes and the action moves to Pass.
