# Summer Engine -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

A strategic acquisition by a game distribution platform is the strongest path. Summer’s Godot-compatible editor brings agent-assisted creation, hosted multiplayer, publishing, and analytics into one workflow. A buyer could use that workflow to acquire creators and increase the supply of games on its platform. Summer reports 60,000+ organically acquired users, 240,000+ reusable community assets, and 40% revenue growth over 30 days. Repeat publishing, paid retention, and the revenue base remain unknown. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction)

The seed-stage milestone is a creator cohort that repeatedly publishes games and pays for hosted features. The memo records 2,966 weekly package downloads and 13 entries in Summer’s first game jam, giving Summer an initial pool for testing that behavior. The cost and runway for reaching the milestone are unknown. The MIT license covers the MCP and CLI. Rights to transfer the hosted service and community assets, any required consents, and holder participation terms remain unverified. The memo supplies no evidence of an independent liquidity route. (Market & Traction, Defensibility, Key Risks)

Under the shared benchmark, a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation buys 5%, diluted to 3% with no follow-ons. A 5× gross cash return within eight years requires $2.5 million to the holder, or about $83.3 million in distributable equity value. The sale scenarios assume an eligible holder sells the entire stake for cash within eight years, with pro rata distribution after the stated deductions and no further senior claims.

| Scenario | Assumed proceeds bridge | Cash to holder |
|---|---|---|
| Modest sale | $40M enterprise value less $5M in net debt and deal adjustments leaves $35M distributable | $1.05M (2.1×) |
| Strong sale | $100M enterprise value less $10M in net debt and deal adjustments leaves $90M distributable | $2.70M (5.4×), or $1.80M (3.6×) at 2% ownership |
| Prolonged private | Cash is reinvested, with no distribution or eligible share sale within eight years | $0 |

The strong case clears the hurdle at 3% ownership. The memo leaves buyer economics and achievable pricing unknown, so the scenario establishes the price required to meet the objective. (Market & Traction)

### Countercase

Unity, Roblox, and GDevelop already offer agent access in established editors. A platform could keep creators in its own workflow and replicate enough of Summer’s tooling to make an acquisition unnecessary. Summer’s strongest reason to buy would be a creator base that consistently publishes games through its hosted workflow, and the memo leaves that behavior unverified. Substitution is credible, but the evidence establishes neither a structural block nor a price ceiling below the proceeds hurdle. The Pass gate therefore remains untriggered. (Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

What would a game distribution platform pay to acquire Summer’s repeat-publishing creator base given the cost of building the same workflow and attracting those creators itself? Cohort data showing repeat publishing and paid retention, coupled with a credible buyer valuation that clears the holder’s $2.5 million cash hurdle after deal deductions and senior claims, would move the action to Reach out. A supported price ceiling materially below that hurdle would trigger Pass.
