## The Idea

- **Problem:** Commercial sites such as construction yards and dealerships face theft and intrusion, while conventional after-hours monitoring can leave daytime incidents and follow-up to site staff ([Synara](https://synaraops.com/), [Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WAp-synara-the-ai-monitoring-station-that-makes-the-problem-go-away)).
- **Approach:** Synara analyzes feeds from existing cameras and recorders, sends flagged incidents to operators for verification, and supports live voice intervention, verified police alarms, and incident documentation ([Synara](https://synaraops.com/), [Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WAp-synara-the-ai-monitoring-station-that-makes-the-problem-go-away)).
- **Differentiation:** Synara offers integrator-branded incident response using existing recorders, Deep Sentinel connects compatible third-party cameras through its own hub, Solink links footage to point-of-sale data, and Becklar bundles dealer-sold remote guarding with wholesale fire and alarm monitoring ([Synara](https://synaraops.com/), [Deep Sentinel](https://www.deepsentinel.com/third-party-camera-integration/), [Solink](https://solink.com/solutions/convenience-store/), [Becklar](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/10/3309851/0/en/Becklar-Expands-Its-Unrivalled-Wholesale-Critical-Event-Monitoring-Solutions-Portfolio-with-the-Acquisition-of-Legacy-Security-Services.html)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Synara can charge integrators a recurring wholesale fee because its partner program has integrators bill customers monthly and retain the margin ([Synara](https://synaraops.com/)).
- **TAM/SAM:** Mordor Intelligence estimates the broader global video-surveillance-as-a-service market at $7.62B in 2026, with 15.47% CAGR through 2031 ([Mordor Intelligence](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/video-surveillance-as-a-service-vsaas-market)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Synara recruits security integrators through a partner inquiry form and lets them resell monitoring to their installed camera base under their own brand, with control over packaging and billing ([Synara](https://synaraops.com/)).

## Key Risks

- **Detection and response performance:** [Inferred]: Missed events and operator queues could affect response in occupied sites, where Synara relies on AI flags and human verification. Synara advertises under 30 seconds from alert to action and labels its example footage illustrative ([Synara](https://synaraops.com/)).
- **Dispatch dependency:** [Inferred]: Police response depends on local dispatch rules, such as Issaquah’s commercial-alarm verification procedures, while Synara’s workflow uses human review and video to substantiate incidents ([City of Issaquah](https://www.issaquahwa.gov/m/newsflash/Archive/Item/5873?arcId=14267), [Synara](https://synaraops.com/)).
- **Dealer competition:** [Inferred]: Integrators can source remote guarding from Becklar’s wholesale dealer channel, which overlaps with Synara’s partner-led route to market ([Becklar](https://becklar.com/resources/monitoring/benefits-of-live-video-monitoring/), [Synara](https://synaraops.com/)).
- **Name ambiguity:** [Inferred]: The Synara Ops name can be confused in public searches with SynaraOps, a workforce-management product offered by SynaraSystems at synara.uk ([Synara](https://synaraops.com/), [SynaraSystems](https://www.synara.uk/)).
