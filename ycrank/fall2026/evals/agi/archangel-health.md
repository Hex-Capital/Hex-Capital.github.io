# Archangel Health -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

The strongest path is **scarce complements**. If more clinical AI developers need to verify that agents complete real workflows, longitudinal records with reusable rights and specialist-graded simulations could become more valuable. Archangel describes sandboxed electronic health records that test whether an agent actually places an order and can use later outcomes to grade multi-visit tasks. The memo documents a public nephrology data listing. It contains no comparative test showing that Archangel’s offering finds consequential failures that available alternatives miss. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

Archangel reports grading by board-certified specialists and tracking dataset provenance. Those practices support a qualification advantage, while the scope and exclusivity of record reuse rights remain unknown. The company also reports use by frontier labs and medical AI developers, with no disclosed paid commitments or repeat orders. The founders’ reported clinical-agent and training-data experience, together with the documented launch, support seed-stage execution credit. The memo provides no field results or economics per additional workflow, including licensing, de-identification, and physician grading costs. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Founders & Team, Key Facts, Key Risks)

### Countercase

Stanford’s public PhysicianBench already covers multi-step EHR workflows, and competitors offer overlapping record access, evaluation environments, and specialist annotation. Buyers may be able to assemble adequate coverage from those options. That weakens the scarcity case, particularly for standard benchmarks. Whether buyers can readily replace specialist-graded longitudinal tasks remains untested, so the scarce-complements path stays open under Watch. New Economies has no independently supported case because the memo gives no change in cost per successful outcome or evidence of a previously excluded buyer or use. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, The Idea)

### Decisive diligence question

Will an independent clinical AI developer place a second paid order for a longitudinal evaluation because it detects consequential workflow failures that its best available substitute misses? A documented repeat order, buyer-confirmed comparative results, and reusable rights to the underlying records would move the action to Reach out. A buyer demonstration that alternatives provide comparable coverage and can readily replace Archangel would close the remaining path and move the action to Pass.
