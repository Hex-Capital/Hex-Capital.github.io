# Fleety -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Medium

### Company case

Fleety’s strongest AGI path is New Economies. It automates technician assignments and schedule changes that plumbing and HVAC shops handle with dispatcher labor. At Albert Nahman Plumbing, Heating & Cooling, Fleety reports making roughly 92% of assignments, allowing one dispatcher to manage more than 30 technicians and saving about $60,000 annually in overhead. That result supports the labor-saving mechanism at one existing shop. The potential new market is smaller shops that cannot justify dedicated staff for continuous dispatch. The memo gives no example of such a shop adopting Fleety and no before-and-after cost per correctly dispatched job. (The Idea; Market & Traction)

The memo documents a Panther Services Group customer video and identifies two other named partners. Those are engagement signals. Contract values, paid renewals, and the cohort behind Fleety’s zero-churn claim are undisclosed. Both founders worked as trades dispatchers, and the CTO previously automated operations serving more than 3,000 units. That experience supports seed-stage execution, with repeatable rollout still untested. (Market & Traction; Founders & Team)

Each shop is a rollout unit, and Fleety says its team goes on-site for every deployment. If the reported $60,000 is gross labor savings and dispatch volume and quality hold, subscription fees, onboarding, and any incremental review and rework would need to total less than $60,000 on an annualized basis for that customer to save money. Fleety’s own contribution depends on what it charges relative to deployment, support, and model costs. The memo provides no figures for that calculation. (The Idea; Market & Traction; Key Risks)

### Countercase

Fleety may reduce staffing costs in an existing market while relying on a platform that already sells revenue-based technician assignment. ServiceTitan controls access to its dispatch board and offers native matching by sales potential and skills. Fleety’s transcript and equipment checks and shop-specific rules provide a plausible reason to choose it today. The memo shows neither preference at functional parity nor evidence that adding shops improves the product for existing customers. It also identifies no scarce asset or service Fleety controls. The native overlap leaves the model-parity gate unresolved, and the memo shows no customer switching or rejection that would close the New Economies path. (The Idea; Defensibility; Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Will shops that previously could not afford continuous dispatch pay for and renew Fleety after evaluating a functionally comparable alternative? A paid cohort with documented prior staffing constraints, a measured reduction in fully loaded cost per correctly dispatched job, and renewals after a side-by-side trial would move the action to Reach out. Evidence that the shops were already economically served or readily switch at parity would move it to Pass.
