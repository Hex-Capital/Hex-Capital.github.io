## The Idea

- **Problem:** Construction teams discover pipes, cables, and structural elements after work begins, which the company says causes more than $20 billion in annual avoidable rework ([YC launch, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SAN-applied-electrodynamics-a-new-kind-of-camera-that-can-see-through-walls)).
- **Approach:** WaveSight transmits radio waves through walls, reconstructs hidden objects as a scale-native point cloud claimed to be accurate to 1 mm, and is planned to combine radio, RGB, and thermal imaging in a handheld device ([YC launch, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SAN-applied-electrodynamics-a-new-kind-of-camera-that-can-see-through-walls)).
- **Differentiation:** Applied Electrodynamics claims stand-off imaging from across a room and 1 mm point-cloud accuracy, while Vayyar’s $199.95 Walabot DIY 2 M must be moved against a wall and penetrates up to four inches ([Applied Electrodynamics LinkedIn, Aug 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/company/applied-electrodynamics); [Walabot, Feb 2024](https://walabot.com/blogs/guides/see-through-the-wall-with-walabot-diy)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Hardware sales and enterprise or government deployments are the likely paths because the site solicits preorders with “launch pricing,” while the YC launch requests introductions to contractors, industrial inspectors, and U.S. defense organizations ([company website, Aug 2026](https://www.ae-dyn.com/); [YC launch, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SAN-applied-electrodynamics-a-new-kind-of-camera-that-can-see-through-walls)).
- **TAM/SAM:** Global through-wall radar was estimated at $134 million in 2025 with 6.3% CAGR through 2032, but no public SAM was found for construction-focused wall imaging ([Global Info Research, Jan 2026](https://www.marketresearch.com/GlobalInfoResearch-v4117/Global-Wall-Radar-Manufacturers-Regions-43541620/)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** The company is seeking direct introductions to general contractors, industrial-inspection teams, and defense organizations and has exhibited at the Nashville Build Expo and BuiltWorlds ConTech Conference ([YC launch, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SAN-applied-electrodynamics-a-new-kind-of-camera-that-can-see-through-walls); [LinkedIn, Aug 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/company/applied-electrodynamics)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** The technical-complexity signals are company-built radio hardware and imaging algorithms plus a founding team credited with 250+ patents, although public sources do not establish that those patents belong to Applied Electrodynamics ([YC launch, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SAN-applied-electrodynamics-a-new-kind-of-camera-that-can-see-through-walls)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Scans paired with verified wall contents could create a proprietary training and calibration dataset, but no customer deployments or dataset scale have been disclosed.
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** Vayyar already commercializes RF wall scanners and Camero-Tech sells 3D tactical through-wall systems, so radio-based penetration is available from existing vendors ([Walabot](https://walabot.com/pages/about-us); [Camero-Tech](https://camero-tech.com/xaver-products/xaver-1000/)).

## Key Risks

- **Performance validation:** The company claims 1 mm accuracy across multiple materials but has published no independent benchmark, product specification sheet, or field-validation dataset, and the product remains at preorder registration ([YC launch, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SAN-applied-electrodynamics-a-new-kind-of-camera-that-can-see-through-walls); [company website](https://www.ae-dyn.com/)).
- **Competitive substitution:** Vayyar sells a $199.95 RF wall scanner and has raised more than $300 million, giving customers an available alternative for studs, pipes, and wires ([Walabot](https://walabot.com/blogs/guides/see-through-the-wall-with-walabot-diy); [Vayyar funding](https://www.prnewswire.co.uk/news-releases/vayyar-imaging-secures-108m-in-series-e-funding-led-by-koch-disruptive-technologies-821875765.html)).
- **Patent ownership:** The company cites 250+ team patents, but public patent records show at least some founder inventions assigned to former employers such as Apple rather than Applied Electrodynamics ([company website](https://www.ae-dyn.com/); [Justia Patents](https://patents.justia.com/inventor/paul-u-leutheuser)).
- **Cross-market scope:** [Inferred]: Construction mapping, manufacturing inspection, cargo screening, and defense require different performance validation and sales channels, while the launch currently requests introductions across all three groups ([YC launch, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SAN-applied-electrodynamics-a-new-kind-of-camera-that-can-see-through-walls)).
- **Name collision:** A separate Kansas-based Applied Electrodynamics LLC has operated in electromagnetic-scattering consulting since 2017, creating search and counterparty-disambiguation risk ([LinkedIn company page](https://www.linkedin.com/company/applied-electrodynamics-llc)).
