## The Idea

- **Problem:** US importers using trading companies face undisclosed factory identities, 15–30% container markups, and duties calculated on the intermediary’s invoice rather than the factory price ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/donkey), Aug 2026).
- **Approach:** Donkey reads public shipment records, identifies the original manufacturer from a product photo or markings, returns a delivered-duty-paid quote within 72 hours, takes title, inspects before factory payment, clears customs, and delivers to the buyer’s dock ([Donkey website](https://donkey.trade/), Aug 2026).
- **Differentiation:** Sourcify matches buyers with vetted factories, Inspectorio supplies quality-management software, and Flexport manages freight and customs, while Donkey combines factory identification, purchasing, title, inspection, customs, and delivery in one transaction ([Sourcify](https://www.sourcify.com/), [Inspectorio](https://www.inspectorio.com/about/), [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/donkey), Aug 2026).
- **Business Model:** Donkey embeds its margin in one delivered USD price, with no fees, retainers, or percentages added on top, and offers net-45 terms to qualified buyers ([Donkey website](https://donkey.trade/), Aug 2026).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** Benjamin Martindale reported driving from Vermont to San Francisco to visit independent wholesale distributors, while the website solicits referrals and product photos for free supplier checks and quotes ([Benjamin Martindale on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/benjamin-martindale/), Aug 2026).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Donkey reports inspectors covering seven Chinese provinces and says settled orders add pricing and supplier-reliability data to its system ([Donkey website](https://donkey.trade/), [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/donkey), Aug 2026).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Repeated orders could produce proprietary factory pricing, defect, lead-time, and reliability histories, but public sources do not establish the current depth or exclusivity of that dataset.
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** [Inferred]: Sourcify, Flexport, or Inspectorio could assemble overlapping sourcing, freight, and inspection workflows, although replicating Donkey’s title-taking and credit terms would also require working capital and trade-risk operations.

## Key Risks

- **Tariff-calculation freshness:** Donkey’s website still uses an illustrative 37.5% duty calculation containing a 10% Section 122 surcharge, but the authorizing proclamation limited that surcharge through July 24, 2026 absent congressional extension, so published savings examples require updating by entry date and HTS code ([Donkey website](https://donkey.trade/), [White House proclamation](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/02/imposing-a-temporary-import-surcharge-to-address-fundamental-international-payments-problems/)).
- **Working-capital and credit exposure:** Donkey takes title, pays factories, remains responsible until delivery, and offers qualified buyers net-45 terms; it states that terms are credit-insured and unlock after an operating history ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/donkey), [Donkey website](https://donkey.trade/), Aug 2026).
- **Physical execution risk:** The company coordinates factory payment, inspection, ocean freight, customs, and final delivery across seven Chinese provinces; mitigation includes inspection before balance payment and a stated credit-or-replace policy for incorrect goods ([Donkey website](https://donkey.trade/), Aug 2026).
- **Entity-resolution risk:** The sourcing mechanism depends on distinguishing actual factories from shells and forwarders in public customs filings, making classification errors capable of affecting supplier selection and pricing ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/donkey), Aug 2026).
- **Pivot and name ambiguity:** LinkedIn search metadata continued to describe the prior Prelude product after the Donkey launch, while “Donkey” is a generic shared name, creating discoverability and identity-confusion risk; the company now uses `donkey.trade` and “Donkey (YC S26)” for disambiguation ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/thedonkeytrade), Aug 2026).
