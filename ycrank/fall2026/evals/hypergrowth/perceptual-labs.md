### Company case

**Path B, repeatable sales with account expansion, is the strongest hypothesis.** The initial buyer would be a model-platform team with access to editable weights and a recurring need to fix specific model behaviors. Perceptual proposes mapping those behaviors to model internals, applying targeted edits, and logging changes for audit. The observed commercial signal is an early-access waitlist. The founders also reported custom training work for Apple and Framer through Bryel Foundry, which suggests a possible source of sales introductions. Paid activation and repeat use for weight editing remain unverified. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Facts)

**Growth scenario.** If a direct sales motion can repeatedly win teams with editable models, a reusable setup could get the first fix into production and turn subsequent fixes into paid workloads. Serving a buyer in a second geography assumes the same model support and editing workflow carry over. Weight licensing, secure deployment, and support requirements could still vary by buyer or market. Each new account could add sales and setup time. Each edit could add compute and regression testing, with specialist labor rising if the team has to rebuild its method for each architecture. The decisive unknown is whether implementation and validation can be reused enough for paid workloads to grow faster than delivery effort. (The Idea, Key Risks)

The Pass gate remains unmet because the memo contains no customer rollout showing a structural demand ceiling or proportional delivery effort. (Market & Traction, Key Risks)
