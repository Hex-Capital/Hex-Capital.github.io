## The Idea

- **Problem:** AI labs need human-generated voice, video, computer-use, and robotics data, but open-web and public contributor systems can introduce manipulated submissions, uncertain consent, and unclear provenance ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/datoric); [Datoric data-rights page](https://www.datoric.com/data-rights-and-provenance), Aug 2026).
- **Approach:** Datoric recruits consented contributors, captures data through modality-separated private systems, isolates customer projects, applies specification-based QA, and attaches contributor, consent, license, collection, and review records to each sample ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/datoric); [Datoric workflow](https://www.datoric.com/how-it-works), Aug 2026).
- **Differentiation:** Scale AI supplies labeling and AI applications, Turing and Mercor match labs with technical experts, and Prolific operates a participant platform, while Datoric emphasizes private project-specific capture of voice, video, computer-use, and physical-interaction data with source-level rights records ([Datoric](https://www.datoric.com/about); [Turing](https://www.turing.com/blog/turing-raises-111m-to-accelerate-the-future-of-agi); [Prolific](https://www.prolific.com/resources/series-a-fundraise), Aug 2026).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Revenue comes from catalog-dataset and build-to-spec enterprise engagements under direct, engagement-specific commercial licenses, with no public prices displayed ([Datoric licensing](https://www.datoric.com/licensing), Aug 2026).
- **TAM/SAM:** The global AI-training-dataset market was $3.2 billion in 2025 and is projected to reach $16.3 billion by 2033 at a 22.6% CAGR, while the U.S. data-collection-and-labeling market was $677.6 million in 2023 with a projected 24.5% CAGR through 2030 ([Grand View Research global](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/ai-training-dataset-market); [U.S.](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/us-data-collection-labeling-market-report), 2026).
- **GTM / Distribution:** [Inferred]: Datoric uses founder-led sales to voice-model, robotics, and world-model teams through introductions, direct project scoping, gated Hugging Face repositories, and buyer review packages ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/datoric); [Datoric workflow](https://www.datoric.com/how-it-works), Aug 2026).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Datoric reports more than 300,000 active contributors operating through private apps, with project isolation and sample-level provenance records ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/datoric), Aug 2026).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Repeated modality-specific collections could create proprietary contributor qualification histories, QA records, and buyer workflow switching costs, but no retention, exclusivity, or repeat-purchase data are public.
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** Scale AI, Turing, Mercor, and Prolific already recruit or manage human contributors and experts for model training and have funded or revenue-producing operations ([Scale AI](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240521674964/en/Scale-AI-Raises-%241-Billion-Series-F-to-Push-The-Frontier-of-AI-Data); [Turing](https://www.turing.com/blog/turing-raises-111m-to-accelerate-the-future-of-agi); [Mercor](https://www.mercor.com/newsroom/); [Prolific](https://www.prolific.com/resources/series-a-fundraise)).

## Key Risks

- **Revenue concentration transparency:** Datoric reports nearly seven figures of revenue in 30 days but names no customers or customer count, preventing assessment of concentration or repeat revenue ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/datoric), Aug 2026).
- **Contributor integrity:** The founders previously bypassed QA systems across paid annotation platforms, demonstrating the attack path Datoric must control across more than 300,000 contributors; mitigation includes approved-only access, private apps, contributor-linked samples, and pre-scaling tests ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/datoric), Aug 2026).
- **Rights-chain execution:** [Inferred]: A missing or invalid contributor consent or license record could impair a buyer’s right to train on delivered data; Datoric establishes consent before capture and supplies reviewable provenance and licensing records ([Datoric data rights](https://www.datoric.com/data-rights-and-provenance); [licensing](https://www.datoric.com/licensing)).
- **Product-history continuity:** The founders publicly presented Arzule as a YC Winter 2026 company before Datoric’s Summer 2026 listing, but no public transition explanation was found, so Arzule metrics are excluded from Datoric traction ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jeff-j-lin_thrilled-to-announce-that-we-are-backed-by-activity-7425561481208938497-N4Es); [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/datoric)).
- **Competitor scale:** Turing reports $300 million ARR, Mercor reports a $2 billion annualized revenue rate, and Scale AI received a $14.3 billion Meta investment, giving competing suppliers larger disclosed operating resources ([BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250128158063/en/Turing-Annual-Revenue-Runrate-Nearly-Triples-to-%24300M-in-Profitable-Year-as-Company-Sets-Stage-for-AGI-Advancements-in-2025); [Investing.com via Bloomberg](https://m.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/mercor-valuation-doubles-to-20b-on-revenue-growth--bloomberg-93CH-4784532); [AP](https://apnews.com/article/4b55aabf7ea018e38ffdccb66e37cf26)).
