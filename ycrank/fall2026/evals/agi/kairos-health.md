# Kairos Health -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Medium

### Company case

Kairos has no supported building-blocks path. It uses voice AI to deliver dental front-desk services, and the memo identifies no scarce input Kairos supplies to AI workloads. (The Idea, Defensibility)

The supported path is systematic voice recall for practices whose front desks lack time to call overdue patients consistently. Those practices rely on manual callbacks or automated texts. Kairos says its software identifies overdue patients, contacts them, and books directly under the practice’s appointment rules. At seed, the plausible mechanism is fewer staff minutes per completed recall visit, making routine outreach viable for practices that previously lacked the capacity. Under model parity, schedule write-back and practice-specific rules still have to be implemented and maintained. (The Idea, Defensibility)

The memo records live deployments and a customer testimonial citing 1,200+ calls handled and $21K+ in average monthly dental production. The production figure leaves Kairos’s incremental contribution unresolved. The company reports 13 paying practices and 85% gross margin. As a scale scenario, if that margin applies at the $1,200 list price, it implies $180 in monthly delivery cost per location. At the $600 launch price, the same cost would leave $420 before any support or rework excluded from the reported margin. Actual realized prices and fully loaded costs remain uncertain. The evidence supports early adoption and delivery, with the cost per incremental completed visit still decisive. (Market & Traction, Key Facts, The Idea, Key Risks)

### Countercase

Kairos may be doing existing recall work more efficiently without making a new outreach cadence viable. The memo provides no comparison with callbacks and texts on completed visits or staff rework. Arini already offers overlapping recall workflows, and communications vendors bundle AI reception. Those alternatives limit confidence that Kairos’s integrations will protect pricing. The live usage and specific labor-saving mechanism support Watch. If the product only speeds up work practices already do at the same cadence, both AGI paths would score zero and trigger Pass. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a paid deployment at a practice that previously lacked capacity for routine voice recall generate enough incremental completed visits to justify its recurring price? A comparison with the practice’s prior callback and text workflow should count completed visits and include staff time for corrections and exceptions. Sustained outreach with positive economics at the recurring price would move the action to Reach out.
