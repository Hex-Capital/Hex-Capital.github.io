# Aucert -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

A strategic sale to a mobile QA vendor such as QA Wolf or Momentic is Aucert’s strongest supported route. Aucert says its agents explore mobile builds and check functional behavior, design, accessibility, and store policy. A buyer could sell those checks to existing customers and shorten its own development timeline. The memo documents a public launch and records a private beta listing. It contains no measured customer savings, buyer interest, or transaction benchmarks that would anchor a sale price. (The Idea, Market & Traction)

The seed-stage milestone is paid production use across several mobile release teams, with repeated reductions in manual review time and evidence that the agents catch defects the existing QA stack misses. The launch and beta make that test plausible. Conflicting financing reports and undisclosed runway leave the capital and time required uncertain. A product acquisition is conceivable, with IP ownership, customer consents, and holder participation unverified. The memo provides no evidence for an independent liquidity route. (Market & Traction, Defensibility, Key Risks)

Under the shared benchmark, a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation starts at 5% ownership and dilutes to 3%. The eight-year objective is $2.5 million in gross cash, requiring about $83.3 million in distributable equity value at 3%. The sale cases below assume a full cash sale within eight years, $5 million in net debt, $5 million in other deal deductions, and an eligible holder receiving its full pro rata share at closing without a senior preference reducing it. The values are scenario assumptions.

- **Modest sale.** $40 million in enterprise value yields $30 million in distributable equity and $900,000 to the holder, or 1.8×.
- **Strong sale.** $100 million in enterprise value yields $90 million in distributable equity and $2.7 million to the holder, or 5.4×. At 2.5% ownership, proceeds fall to $2.25 million, or 4.5×.
- **Prolonged private.** Continued operation with cash reinvested and no eligible share purchase or funded payout yields zero realized proceeds.

### Countercase

QA Wolf already maps mobile workflows and runs and repairs mobile tests, while Google Play covers some accessibility and stability checks. A buyer could extend its own tooling and pay little for Aucert’s incremental capabilities. Aucert also says it sends unredacted screenshots and UI hierarchies to AI providers, which could complicate enterprise adoption. These risks weaken the case for a premium sale. The memo establishes neither a structural block nor a credible upper bound on sale proceeds, so the Pass gate stays open. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Would acquiring Aucert create enough incremental value for an established mobile QA vendor to support a cash price that clears the holder’s proceeds hurdle? Move to Reach out if repeated paid production results show measurable review savings and incremental defect detection, and a credible buyer-side estimate of build costs and cross-sell value supports a price that leaves at least $83.3 million distributable under the benchmark terms.
