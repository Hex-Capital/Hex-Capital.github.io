# Dydema -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Product-led adoption** is Dydema’s strongest path. The clearest initial buyer is a frequent computer-agent user who wants to confirm prompts hands-free. The research-cap demo shows three imagined movements mapped to actions, and Dydema reports that its pretrained decoder works on previously unseen users after brief calibration. If that performance carries over to the planned lower-cost headset, users could discover and order it online, calibrate it themselves, and reach first value by mapping a command to an existing keystroke. That workflow could travel across markets with limited software changes, though hardware fulfillment and any neural-data requirements remain unresolved. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

The first link likely to break, even assuming strong fit with early users, is independent activation on consumer hardware. The launch drew attention, and Wizard documents self-serve API keys and usage pricing for the earlier research offering. The memo records neither a consumer buyer reaching paid value through the proposed workflow nor subsequent use, expansion, or delivery costs. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Facts)

### Countercase

The research-cap demo may overstate what buyers can achieve at home. If cheaper sensors make a small command set unreliable, calibration and troubleshooting could cost users more effort than the keystrokes they save and add support work with each sale. Dydema is still adapting the decoder to cheaper sensors and improving command count and speed. The structural downside gate does not apply because the memo establishes neither persistent dependence on hands-on setup nor proportional delivery costs. A paid test on the intended hardware can resolve the activation question. (Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can independent, frequent computer-agent users pay the intended price, set up the lower-cost headset without founder help, and reliably confirm prompts in their normal workflow across multiple sessions? A small paid cohort that repeats this process through the same onboarding flow would move the action to Reach out. Continued dependence on hands-on calibration would keep it at Watch while that limitation remains testable.
