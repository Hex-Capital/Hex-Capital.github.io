# Foreshadow Industries -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

A strategic acquisition is Foreshadow’s strongest supported route. A defense contractor serving existing fleets could buy Zulu and its integration work if ownership would reduce the time and cost of delivering naval retrofits. Foreshadow reports three military-vessel integrations and repeated overnight demonstrations, including simultaneous operation on two vessels. Those claims support testing the acquisition rationale. The memo documents a YC founder-panel appearance and backing of an undisclosed amount. Buyer interest, measured deployment savings, and reuse across vehicle classes remain unverified. (*The Idea*, *Defensibility*, *Market & Traction*)

The next milestone is a funded naval production qualification that records customer-accepted reliability and integration cost. The reported demonstrations make that milestone plausible, and the memo describes a Navy program that progressed from at-sea testing toward production. Foreshadow’s qualification funding, capital needs, and schedule remain unknown. The memo supplies no evidence on asset transfer, required approvals, or holder eligibility, and no independent liquidity route is supported. (*Market & Traction*, *Key Risks*, *Key Facts*)

Using the benchmark $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, ownership falls from 5% to 3% with no follow-ons. The 5× objective requires $2.5 million in gross cash within eight years, or about $83.3 million in distributable equity. The sale cases below use illustrative prices. Both assume an eligible holder sells the full stake for cash within eight years, $10 million in combined net debt and deal adjustments, and a pro rata waterfall with no preference diversion. The memo supplies no buyer price or holder terms to substantiate those assumptions. (*Market & Traction*, *Key Facts*)

### Countercase

A strategic buyer could use an existing retrofit product or develop its own. Sea Machines already sells vessel retrofit systems and is eligible for follow-on Navy production agreements. Foreshadow’s reported demonstrations leave its production economics and cross-platform advantage unverified. That weakens the case for a strategic premium, especially when the illustrative strong sale clears the equity hurdle by only about $6.7 million before any further deductions. The memo leaves both a structural closing block and a materially inadequate payout unestablished, so neither Pass gate triggers. (*Defensibility*, *Key Risks*)

### Decisive diligence question

What acquisition price could a plausible strategic buyer justify after a funded naval production qualification? A buyer-backed range tied to measured integration savings or production contract value would move the action to Reach out if it supports at least $2.5 million in holder cash within eight years after deal deductions and the waterfall.
