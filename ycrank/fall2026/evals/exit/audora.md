# Audora -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Audora’s strongest exit route is a strategic sale to a reading or audiobook platform. Its account-free preview turns a story link into a full-cast sample, which could help a platform convert existing readers into paying listeners. The memo records a public launch and 23,573 views on the founder’s launch post. The founder reports 10,000 waitlisted readers and authors. Paid conversion, repeat listening, and the value of that audience to a buyer remain unverified. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Facts)

A paid pilot on a small, rights-cleared catalog could measure repeat paid listening and production costs against a platform’s existing casting option. The launched product makes a pilot plausible at seed. The memo’s differing reports of YC backing leave available runway uncertain, and the time and capital needed to secure broader catalog rights are unquantified. (Market & Traction, Key Risks)

Using the shared benchmark, a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation starts at 5% and dilutes to 3% with no follow-ons. The holder needs $2.5 million in gross cash within eight years, requiring about $83.3 million in distributable equity value. Both sale scenarios assume an eligible holder sells the entire stake for cash within eight years and receives 3% of distributable equity, with no additional senior claims diverting proceeds. These are scenario assumptions. The memo gives no supported sale price or confirmed waterfall. (Market & Traction, Key Facts)

| Scenario | Assumed proceeds bridge | Holder cash |
|---|---|---:|
| Modest sale | $40 million enterprise value less $5 million in debt and deal adjustments leaves $35 million in distributable equity. | $1.05 million, or 2.1× |
| Strong sale | $100 million enterprise value less $10 million in debt and deal adjustments leaves $90 million in distributable equity. | $2.70 million, or 5.4× |
| Prolonged private | Audora reinvests available cash and the holder receives no payout or share purchase within eight years. | $0, or 0× |

Even in the strong sale, eligibility to sell only 90% of the stake would cut cash proceeds to $2.43 million. The memo establishes no independent liquidity route. (Key Facts)

### Countercase

ElevenLabs already offers automated character casting and direct publishing, giving a plausible buyer a ready substitute for the core production function. Audora’s terms require authorized access, personal use, and compliance with source-site rules, so a buyer would need separate rights to commercially reuse imported stories. These constraints weaken the case for buying Audora for its current workflow or audience. The Pass gate remains open because a sale built around a rights-cleared workflow is still possible and the memo leaves its realizable price unresolved. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can Audora show that a reading platform would gain enough from owning its workflow to justify a purchase above the holder’s proceeds hurdle? A paid pilot with documented commercial rights and measured gains in repeat paid listening over the platform’s existing casting option would answer it if the resulting economics supported a price above the hurdle after deal adjustments. That evidence would move the action to Reach out.
