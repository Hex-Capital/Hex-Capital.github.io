# Tire Swing -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Strategic acquisition is the strongest path. Tire Swing turns tenant documents into certification packets that staff can review against the underlying evidence. The memo describes EliseAI’s affordable-housing prequalification product, which makes a leasing platform like EliseAI a plausible buyer for the downstream certification workflow. Tire Swing reports thousands of compliance checks. Owning that rules library and workflow could help a buyer sell a more complete product to housing operators. The value of ownership over licensing remains unmeasured. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction)

The founders report dozens of live properties across eight states. A paid rollout across multiple operators that shows lower staff review time per certification while maintaining accuracy would test whether the product creates repeatable value a buyer could monetize. The reported deployments give Tire Swing a starting point. Paid adoption, burn, and the capital needed for that milestone remain unverified. Subscription terms establish a billing model. A sponsor buyout or company-funded payout would require evidence of sustained cash generation, while a tender or IPO would require evidence of eligible share demand or public-market readiness. Each remains unevidenced. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks, Key Facts)

Under the illustrative $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, 5% initial ownership falls to 3% after 40% relative dilution, with no follow-ons. The eight-year 5× target is $2.5 million gross. It requires $83.3 million in distributable equity at 3% ownership, $125 million at 2%, or $62.5 million at 4%. At 3%, assuming $5 million in illustrative net debt and simple pro rata allocation before taxes, a $25 million enterprise-value sale yields $600,000 (1.2×), and a $100 million sale yields $2.85 million (5.7×). Both prices are scenarios. A realizable sale range and actual shareholder terms remain unknown. If growth plateaus, buyer interest fails to emerge, public scale stays out of reach, and cash is reinvested, eight years of private operation yields $0 realized with shares still held. In a wind-down, holder cash would depend on the residual after obligations and preferences, which the memo leaves unquantified. (Market & Traction, Key Facts)

### Countercase

Yardi already bundles certification with property accounting, and Roost offers AI-generated certification packets. A leasing platform could build comparable features or license certification, especially if staff review and ongoing rule maintenance limit the savings from ownership. Tenant-data and customer-contract transfer rights also remain unverified. These facts weaken the case for an acquisition premium and leave closing feasibility open. The Pass gate requires a supported structural block or materially inadequate proceeds, and the memo establishes neither. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)
