# Dydema -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path A — Scarce complements.** Dydema’s strongest case is supplying a reliable hands-free input channel for computer agents. The research-cap demo shows three imagined movements mapped to commands. Dydema claims its pretrained decoder works on previously unseen users after brief calibration. That makes shorter setup plausible, while reliability on cheaper consumer sensors remains unverified. *(The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)*

**Scenario assumption.** If cheaper intelligence makes agents common in workflows where users’ hands are occupied and voice is impractical, a reliable confirmation signal could become a bottleneck. Even with equally capable models, competitors would still need to acquire and decode that user’s EEG signal reliably. Dydema reports standardizing approximately 128,000 hours of existing recordings. Its ability to retain value would depend on showing that its sensor and decoder combination delivers an advantage competitors cannot readily match. *(The Idea, Defensibility, Key Risks)*

At seed, the founders’ EEG and machine-learning experience and the available research API support their ability to build and ship early software. Wearable hardware remains the next execution gap. The memo records launch attention and API list prices, with buyer commitments, repeat use, and consumer hardware economics still unknown. The relevant scale unit is a supported headset deployment, including hardware, calibration, and ongoing support. The memo gives no basis to estimate contribution per deployment. *(Founders & Team, The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)*

### Countercase

Conventional inputs may cover most agent confirmations, while Emotiv and Neurosity already offer programmable EEG commands. Dydema’s internal benchmark compares its pretrained decoder with models trained on individual users’ data. It leaves open whether Dydema beats competing systems on consumer hardware. The research-cap demo and limited command set make that gap consequential. Path A’s control gate remains open because equivalent substitution has yet to be demonstrated. Path B offers no separate supported case because the memo does not establish that automation reduces end-to-end delivery labor enough to open a new market. *(The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)*

### Decisive diligence question

Can Dydema’s lower-cost headset earn a paid repeat deployment from an independent agent integration partner by outperforming that partner’s best feasible conventional input and a programmable EEG alternative? A head-to-head trial in one hands-busy confirmation workflow should measure setup and recalibration time, missed and false commands, and cost per correctly completed confirmation. A repeat commitment tied to an advantage traceable to Dydema’s sensor and decoder combination would justify rescoring for Reach out if the score reaches 65 and confidence reaches at least Medium. Comparable performance from readily available substitutes would defeat this Path A case and move the action to Pass. *(The Idea, Defensibility, Key Risks)*
