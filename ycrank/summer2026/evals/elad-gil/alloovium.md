# Alloovium -- Elad Gil Evaluation

**A$400 million of active-project exposure but no disclosed revenue curve makes Alloovium a structurally clean workflow-infrastructure bet whose weakest signal is whether customers are actually buying.** Alloovium uses validated retrieval models, existing repositories, and human approval rather than requiring new science; Alloovium has not shown whether those deployments are “just working” or are enterprise-pilot tourism.

**Color Genomics** is the high rung on single-miracle architecture: workflow and data infrastructure captured value on top of commodity sequencing without betting on new chemistry. Alloovium shares that mechanism by connecting SharePoint, Procore, Aconex, and Outlook, then layering builder-specific IMS checks and cited evidence over existing models. Alloovium diverges because clause-level accuracy, contractual liability, third-party API access, and distribution must all remain tractable; human approval contains the risk but also limits automation leverage.

**Lyft’s Series C** exposed the low-rung failure mechanism for traction quality: category growth can be real while the company-specific economic curve remains unproven. Alloovium’s A$400 million figure measures the value of construction projects touched, not Alloovium revenue, expansion, or usage growth. The BMD subsidiary deployment and 30-year construction auditor design partnership are meaningful pre-seed validation, but Alloovium provides no ARR, paid-contract count, retention, or month-over-month curve. That is a false signal until converted into buying behavior.

**Trunk Tools’ $70 million funding and June 2026 drawing product** show that multimodal construction-document intelligence has crossed a technical threshold, but the catalyst is category-wide rather than proprietary to Alloovium. Over 18 months, cited retrieval across drawings, contracts, RFIs, and correspondence could compound into correction histories and compliance workflows; alternatively, Procore can bundle the same capabilities into its $1.3 billion system of record. Alloovium has a credible recent technological catalyst, but Alloovium is skating for a puck with several well-funded players already there.

**Cielo Nicolosi’s four founding-engineer roles and 2025 hackathon win** establish independent shipping ability beyond a résumé credential, while Zander Schweitzer’s site-engineering experience supplies a direct feedback loop. Alloovium would move into my top founder-execution tier if Zander and Cielo could show dated releases driven by BMD or auditor feedback—specific workflows built between customer conversations, followed by repeat usage. Without that chronology, Alloovium demonstrates that the founders can ship a product, not yet the Perplexity pattern of shipping iteratively every week.

**Stripe** became an index on e-commerce because every new internet business could enter through the same developer rail. Alloovium has an early version of that position: one head contractor reportedly exposes Alloovium to roughly 50 subcontractors annually, and the cross-system layer can benefit as each project adds documents and counterparties. Alloovium is not yet a default rail, however; Procore controls a system of record, while Trunk Tools and Document Crunch already contest the intelligence layer. The index outcome requires contractor-led distribution to compound faster than incumbent bundling.

**Alloovium reaches A$3 million–A$7 million ARR by July 2029, concentrated among Australian head contractors, but does not become the global construction-intelligence default.** Alloovium’s single-miracle architecture should produce a saleable compliance and document product, while the missing revenue curve, crowded specialist field, and dependence on Procore and Aconex constrain category ownership. That is a durable vertical-software outcome, not yet one of the dozen companies likely to matter globally in ten years.

**A$400 million of deployed project value** supports the strongest bull case: construction enterprise sales are lumpy, so revenue silence may conceal paid rollouts, and the 30-year auditor could turn builder-specific IMS checks into a distribution standard. Alloovium could follow the Color Genomics exception—workflow infrastructure on top of a hard capability—while the 50-subcontractor channel makes Alloovium an index on contractor networks. The dossier still loses that argument because no retention, paid expansion, or proprietary dataset scale distinguishes Alloovium from Trunk Tools, Document Crunch, or Procore. Evidence of at least A$1 million ARR growing above 20% month over month through three consecutive months would flip my verdict to Invest.
