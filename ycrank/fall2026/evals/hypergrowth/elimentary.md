# Elimentary -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Medium

### Company case

**Channel-led or repeatable sales** is the strongest path. Private credit fund CFOs and operations heads have a recurring month-end job that includes reconciling records, checking covenants, and preparing reports. Elimentary’s agents work on top of existing systems and route calculations for human sign-off. That design makes the workflow plausible across markets if local agreements can be handled through configuration and buyers can clear vendor diligence. The memo gives no buyer geography or cross-market rollout evidence. (The Idea, Key Risks)

The memo records a multi-year TresVista partnership with initial agents live in fund administration and financial planning. A listed business development company reported that a reconciliation covering more than 500 checks went from roughly 20 hours of work to under 30 minutes of agent runtime plus about an hour of human sign-off. Separately, an Elimentary case study reports that a direct lender expanded covenant testing to more than 150 portfolio companies. That shows broader use within one account, with paid expansion still unverified. The growth case assumes TresVista can bring another fund to paid use by reusing the setup and validation process. The memo gives no customer-level record tracing that journey. (Market & Traction, Key Facts)

### Countercase

The strongest countercase is that each fund deployment requires substantial custom work. Client-specific calculations and deal-by-deal validation could make implementation effort rise with each new agreement structure. Customer time savings establish a benefit, while Elimentary’s configuration, validation, and support costs remain unmeasured. Repeatable onboarding is the first unresolved link. The structural downside gate stays open because the memo gives no measured pattern of proportional delivery costs and the ability to reuse the workflow is testable. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can TresVista bring an independent private credit fund to a paid month-end go-live using the existing reconciliation workflow? Move to Reach out if the rollout record shows that a partner or implementation team can reuse the setup, with measured configuration and validation hours. Keep Watch if the rollout is pending. A completed rollout that requires substantial bespoke work would strengthen the case for Pass.
