## The Idea

- **Problem:** Retail investors with jobs and families lack the time, tools, and expertise to monitor market news, SEC filings, and portfolio risks, leaving passive index investing or self-directed research as their principal alternatives ([Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/RJy-ekpa-ai-native-investment-platform-for-everyday-traders), Jul 2026).
- **Approach:** Ekpa connects to brokerage accounts through SnapTrade with read-only permissions, aggregates holdings, and generates position-specific research, alerts, and suggested actions from news, analyst ratings, SEC filings, and economic data ([Ekpa](https://goekpa.com/), Jul 2026).
- **Differentiation:** Ekpa emphasizes research across existing brokerages without custody or trade execution, whereas Composer provides brokerage execution and no-code strategies, Public provides an agentic multi-asset brokerage, and PortfolioPilot provides registered investment advice plus tax, retirement, and estate-planning tools ([Ekpa](https://goekpa.com/), [Composer](https://www.composer.trade/), [Public](https://public.com/), [PortfolioPilot](https://portfoliopilot.com/), Jul 2026).
- **Business Model:** Basic access is free during early access, while Strategy Lab is a paid subscription whose price is not publicly displayed ([Ekpa](https://goekpa.com/), Jul 2026).
- **TAM/SAM:** The global investment-research-software market was estimated at $3.64 billion in 2025 and projected to reach $9.2 billion in 2032 at 14.2% CAGR, while no public SAM estimate was found for consumer portfolio-specific research agents ([VPA Research](https://www.marketresearch.com/VPA-Research-v4245/Investment-Research-Software-Size-Share-40859043/), Apr 2025).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Ekpa uses self-service free signup, YC launch distribution, and founder-led outreach to traders while onboarding early customers ([Ekpa](https://goekpa.com/), [Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/RJy-ekpa-ai-native-investment-platform-for-everyday-traders), Jul 2026).

## Key Risks

- **Regulatory boundary:** Ekpa says it is not an investment adviser while describing position-specific instructions and exact share counts; the SEC identifies algorithmic online recommendation providers as typically registered advisers, although Ekpa mitigates execution risk through read-only access and user-controlled decisions ([Ekpa](https://goekpa.com/), [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2017-52)).
- **Model and data accuracy:** Ekpa’s terms state that market data, news, and analysis may be delayed, incomplete, or inaccurate, while FINRA identifies hallucination, authority, and auditability risks in financial AI agents; Ekpa says its analysis includes reasoning and public evidence ([Ekpa terms](https://goekpa.com/terms.html), [FINRA](https://www.finra.org/media-center/newsreleases/2025/finra-publishes-2026-regulatory-oversight-report-empower-member-firm)).
- **Third-party dependency:** Brokerage connectivity depends on SnapTrade, while payments, email, and analytics depend on Stripe, Resend, and PostHog; read-only permissions and deletion upon disconnection limit the data and custody exposure ([Ekpa privacy policy](https://goekpa.com/privacy.html), Jul 2026).
- **Bundled substitution:** Public, Composer, and PortfolioPilot combine overlapping research or automation with brokerage execution or registered advice, allowing them to bundle functions that Ekpa supplies separately ([Public](https://public.com/), [Composer](https://www.composer.trade/), [PortfolioPilot](https://portfoliopilot.com/)).
- **Positioning transition:** Third-party listings still describe Ekpa’s prior product as an S&P 500 trend-following strategy, while the current website presents a multi-broker portfolio dashboard and states automated execution is not live ([Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/ekpa), [Ekpa](https://goekpa.com/), Jul 2026).
