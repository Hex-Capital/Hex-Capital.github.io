## The Idea

- **Problem:** Enterprise marketing teams use research firms and content agencies for buyer evidence and campaigns, and half of the 56 marketing and insights professionals in Gather’s study said they could not fast-track research when needed ([Gather study, Feb 2026](https://www.gatherhq.com/resources/customer-research-crisis), [YC directory](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/gatherhq)).
- **Approach:** Gather queries existing calls, surveys and research, recruits and adaptively interviews real buyers when evidence is thin, and uses the findings to refresh buyer simulations and produce campaign content ([Gather methodology](https://www.gatherhq.com/methodology), [Launch YC, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WI6-gatherhq-an-api-for-how-buyers-think-built-for-agents-and-teams)).
- **Differentiation:** Gather uses fresh buyer interviews for both simulations and campaign assets, while Listen Labs emphasizes searchable interviews and research deliverables, Outset emphasizes continuous customer-experience measurement, and Simile specializes in modeling human decisions ([Gather](https://www.gatherhq.com/), [Listen Labs](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/listen-labs-raises-69-million-series-b-to-bring-customer-voices-into-every-decision-302661000.html), [Outset](https://outset.ai/series-b), [Simile](https://www.simile.com/blog/series-b)).
- **Business Model:** Emma is free to install in Slack, with usage priced at $10 per simulation, $50 per consumer interview and $100 per B2B interview, plus volume pricing by inquiry ([Gather pricing, accessed Nov 2026](https://share.gatherhq.com/pricing)).
- **TAM/SAM:** Kantar estimated global market research at $54B for 2024, growing about 4%, as a broader benchmark, with no public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment ([Kantar lender presentation](https://www.kantar.com/investor-relations/financial-announcement/-/media/020A84BE683F48A7AA05E523229EBF74.ashx)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Gather targets enterprise marketing teams through demos and customer-published research, with self-service distribution through Slack Marketplace and agent access through its Resonance API and MCP ([Gather](https://www.gatherhq.com/), [Slack Marketplace](https://slack.com/marketplace/A0BC06WJV4M-gatherhq)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Gather says its models draw on data from 300,000+ real interviews across customers, with thousands added monthly ([YC directory, accessed Nov 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/gatherhq)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: A customer’s history of interviews and sales calls could make a buyer model harder to replace if that history measurably improves predictions, with no public retention or switching-cost data found ([Gather methodology](https://www.gatherhq.com/methodology)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** Listen Labs and Outset already offer AI-moderated interviews and automated analysis, making those components available from multiple vendors ([Listen Labs, Jan 2026](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/listen-labs-raises-69-million-series-b-to-bring-customer-voices-into-every-decision-302661000.html), [Outset, Dec 2025](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/12/10/3203401/0/en/outset-secures-30-million-series-b-to-launch-the-world-s-first-ai-native-customer-experience-management-platform.html)).

## Key Risks

- **Prediction validity:** [Inferred]: Simulations may misestimate reactions from buyer segments with sparse or stale interview evidence, which Gather addresses by labeling simulated estimates and commissioning fresh interviews when evidence is insufficient ([Gather methodology](https://www.gatherhq.com/methodology)).
- **Data permissions:** [Inferred]: Restrictions on reusing interview recordings and customer CRM data can limit model inputs, and Gather’s privacy policy ties processing and model improvement to customer agreements and applicable law ([Gather Privacy Policy](https://www.gatherhq.com/privacy-policy)).
- **Platform substitution:** [Inferred]: Salesforce’s acquisition of Listen Labs gives Slack’s owner an overlapping research product it could bundle, while Gather also distributes through its web app and API ([Salesforce announcement, via search snippet](https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/salesforce-signs-definitive-agreement-to-acquire-listen-labs/), [Gather](https://www.gatherhq.com/)).
- **Name ambiguity:** [Inferred]: Search results for Gather can surface Gather’s virtual-office product or the restaurant-events platform merged into Tripleseat, making product and traction attribution ambiguous ([Gather.town](https://www.gather.town/blog/new-chapter), [Tripleseat](https://tripleseat.com/articles/tripleseat-acquired-by-vista-equity-partners/)).
