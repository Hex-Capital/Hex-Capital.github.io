## The Idea

- **Problem:** Process EPCs and industrial operators manually reconcile engineering drawings, lists and datasheets after design changes, with missed updates potentially causing incorrect equipment orders and schedule delays ([Verno announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/verno-inc_verno-yc-f26-is-in-y-combinator-fall-2026-activity-7507794284075466752-EL6w)).
- **Approach:** Verno converts requested changes into native CAD edits, checks drawings against project documents, and incorporates changes after engineer acceptance ([Verno FAQ](https://www.verno-ai.com/faq)).
- **Differentiation:** Verno describes editing existing native files and synchronizing downstream deliverables, whereas Autodesk Plant 3D supplies CAD authoring and rule-based validation, and PlantFCE Maps emphasizes searchable navigation of existing PDFs ([Verno integrations](https://www.verno-ai.com/integrations), [Autodesk](https://www.autodesk.com/products/autocad/included-toolsets/autocad-plant-3d), [PlantFCE](https://maps.plantfce.com/)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Customer contracts suggest enterprise licensing; published pricing: No public data found; Website not accessible at time of research applies to the homepage, although contact and legal pages loaded ([Verno privacy notice](https://www.verno-ai.com/privacy)).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** Verno solicits demonstrations using prospects’ own drawings and builds connectors with design partners ([Verno FAQ](https://www.verno-ai.com/faq), [Verno integrations](https://www.verno-ai.com/integrations)).
