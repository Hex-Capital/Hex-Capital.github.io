# Kanopy -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Strategic acquisition is Kanopy’s strongest path. ACRT has introduced Arborcision, which combines Kanopy’s video-derived 3D clearance models with ACRT’s vegetation management services. That gives ACRT a company-specific reason to consider buying the reconstruction technology and bringing clearance measurement and work verification in-house. The economic case would depend on how much owning the technology improves contract margins or helps ACRT win and retain utility work beyond what its current partnership delivers. Schneider Electric’s announced AiDASH acquisition shows strategic interest in an adjacent capability. Kanopy’s realizable sale price remains unevidenced. (*The Idea*, *Market & Traction*, *Key Risks*)

The operating milestone is a paid utility renewal through Arborcision with field-validated clearance accuracy and measured savings from fewer repeat visits. Kanopy reports mapping 3,093 cumulative miles. The memo also reports six LOIs or master service agreements covering 270,000 miles of potential scope. Those figures support a path to testing the milestone. Committed volume, delivery costs, burn, and the capital needed to repeat the result remain unknown. Kanopy’s control of its hosted models makes an acquisition plausible in principle. Rights to transfer customer footage and contracts, required consents, and buyer funding remain unverified. (*Market & Traction*, *Defensibility*, *Key Risks*)

**Illustrative proceeds.** Assume a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, 5% initial ownership diluted by 40% to 3%, and zero follow-ons. The 5× target is $2.5 million gross cash within eight years. At 3%, that requires $83.3 million of distributable equity, or $88.3 million of enterprise value with $5 million of assumed net debt. The equity hurdle rises to $125 million at 2% ownership and falls to $62.5 million at 4%. Kanopy’s actual shareholder terms, debt, fees, and senior claims are unknown. The sale scenarios use simple pro rata distributions before investor taxes and assume zero additional deductions.

| Eight-year scenario | Gross holder cash |
|---|---:|
| Modest sale at $25 million enterprise value, less $5 million assumed net debt | $600,000 (1.2×) |
| Strong sale at $100 million enterprise value, less $5 million assumed net debt | $2.85 million (5.7×) |
| Growth plateaus, buyer interest fails to emerge, public scale stays out of reach, and Kanopy reinvests all available cash | $0, with shares retained |

A sponsor buyout lacks evidence of recurring cash generation. A secondary lacks evidence of eligible share demand. An IPO lacks evidence of operating scale, reporting readiness, and a holder resale path. Dividends or buybacks lack evidence of distributable cash and a policy to pay holders. A wind-down recovery would depend on what remains after obligations and preferences, which the memo does not quantify. (*Market & Traction*, *Key Facts*)

### Countercase

ACRT already offers Kanopy’s capability through Arborcision, giving it a working path to market while Kanopy stays independent. Utilities may also retain manual checks if video-derived clearances fall short in field validation, reducing the savings a buyer could capture. That leaves the ownership premium and the price needed for a 5× return unproven. The evidence supports Watch because a sale remains plausible and the memo establishes neither a structural barrier nor a price ceiling that puts the target out of reach. (*The Idea*, *Key Risks*)

### Decisive diligence question

Would the economics of a paid, field-validated Arborcision renewal support a strategic acquisition at a price that clears the holder’s 5× target? Measured contract margins and repeat-visit savings, together with a credible estimate of what ACRT or a comparable buyer gains from owning the technology beyond a commercial partnership, would answer it. A supported sale range that clears roughly $88.3 million in enterprise value under the illustrative terms would move the action to Reach out.
