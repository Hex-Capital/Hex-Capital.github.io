## The Idea

- **Problem:** Travelers applying from Egypt assemble documents, re-enter information and repeatedly check for appointments, with some paying local agencies or brokers to handle the process ([EnterpriseAM, Aug 2026](https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2026/08/10/one-egyptian-founder-and-ai-enthusiast-is-out-to-fix-the-broken-visa-process-plaguing-residents-of-the-global-south/)).
- **Approach:** Veeza extracts passport details, flags missing documents and uses browser agents to complete government forms and book appointments on full-service packages, with expert review before submission ([YC launch, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/VrY-veeza-ai-ai-tourist-visa-agent-for-people-with-weak-passports), [Veeza AI](https://www.veeza.ai/en)).
- **Differentiation:** Veeza pairs Cairo appointment booking with Arabic and English WhatsApp support, while Atlys publishes an India-focused Schengen appointment tracker and iVisa offers a global catalog of e-visas and entry authorizations ([Veeza AI comparison](https://www.veeza.ai/en/guides/ivisa-vs-atlys-vs-veeza), [Atlys](https://passport.atlys.com/appointments/schengen/india), [iVisa](https://www.ivisa.com/)).
- **Business Model:** Veeza lists one-time per-person service fees of EGP 1,500 for France early access and EGP 4,000 for UK and US full service, with applicable government or visa-centre fees itemized separately and group discounts up to 25% ([Veeza AI pricing, Nov 2026](https://www.veeza.ai/en#choose-your-destination)).
- **TAM/SAM:** Veeza estimates 200 million annual tourist visa applications from the Global South, while Schengen consulates in its initial Egyptian market received 226,865 short-stay applications in 2025 ([YC launch, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/VrY-veeza-ai-ai-tourist-visa-agent-for-people-with-weak-passports), [European Commission data via Traveloffsy, Oct 2026](https://traveloffsy.com/schengen-visas-egypt)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Veeza started with Egyptian travelers, acquires users through founder-led social content and creator collaborations, accepts applications through WhatsApp and solicits travel-agency partnerships ([EnterpriseAM, Aug 2026](https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2026/08/10/one-egyptian-founder-and-ai-enthusiast-is-out-to-fix-the-broken-visa-process-plaguing-residents-of-the-global-south/), [Aly Moursy, Nov 2026](https://www.teapawt.org/alymoursy/article/2121410998141886502), [Keiki, Oct 2026](https://onkeiki.com/blog/veeza-customer-story), [YC launch, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/VrY-veeza-ai-ai-tourist-visa-agent-for-people-with-weak-passports)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Veeza collects and publishes appointment availability by destination and visa centre in Egypt ([Veeza AI appointment tracker](https://www.veeza.ai/en/schengen-visa-appointment-availability)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Repeated applications could build a record of portal changes and document corrections by consulate, with no public evidence yet that this improves booking or review performance.
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** [Inferred]: Visa processors can combine commercially available browser-control models with existing document review and support operations ([OpenAI, Mar 2026](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-4/), [iVisa](https://www.ivisa.com/)).

## Key Risks

- **Appointment supply:** Consulates and visa centres control slot availability, so bookings can remain pending and trigger full order refunds under Veeza’s policy ([SceneNow, Oct 2026](https://scenenow.com/Business/Veeza-AI-Wants-to-Make-Global-Mobility-Easier-for-the-Global-South), [Veeza AI refund policy](https://www.veeza.ai/en/refund-policy)).
- **Application accuracy:** Changes in consular requirements can make generated files incomplete, and Veeza uses human review and applicant corrections before submission ([EnterpriseAM, Aug 2026](https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/2026/08/10/one-egyptian-founder-and-ai-enthusiast-is-out-to-fix-the-broken-visa-process-plaguing-residents-of-the-global-south/), [Veeza AI](https://www.veeza.ai/en)).
- **Sensitive documents:** A breach could expose passports and supporting records, which Veeza says it encrypts in transit and at rest ([Veeza AI privacy policy](https://www.veeza.ai/en/privacy)).
- **Name overlap:** Veeza.com operates a separate US immigration attorney referral service, creating potential confusion for travelers searching by name ([Veeza.com](https://veeza.com/about-veeza-inc/), [Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/veeza-ai)).
