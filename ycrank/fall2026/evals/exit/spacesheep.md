# Spacesheep -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

The strongest path is a strategic acquisition by a collaboration software vendor seeking a shared review workflow for outputs from competing coding agents. Spacesheep describes a hosted workspace where teams can publish from Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor, comment on specific elements, and request revisions. A vendor such as Gamma could use that workflow to extend its document and site products into team review of agent-generated work. That buyer rationale is an inference. The memo records a public launch and 28 Chrome extension users, and the company shows an AI Foundry dashboard as a usage example. Repeat team use, paid adoption, and buyer interest remain unverified. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Facts)

A practical seed milestone is a paid team cohort that repeatedly publishes and reviews work from at least two agent platforms. The live product gives the two-person team a way to run that test. The memo reports a $125,000 seed entry, with runway and the cost of reaching the milestone unverified. A sale of the hosted product is plausible, with connector continuity and rights to transfer or continue processing customer content still to verify. The memo supports no independent liquidity route. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Founders & Team, Key Risks, Key Facts)

Under the shared benchmark, a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation starts at 5% ownership and dilutes to 3% with no follow-ons. A 5× gross cash return within eight years requires $2.5 million to the holder, or about $83.3 million in distributable equity. Both sale scenarios assume a full cash acquisition within eight years, an eligible holder paid pro rata, and no preference or other waterfall diversion. In a **modest sale**, $30 million of enterprise value with no net deductions yields $30 million of distributable equity and $900,000 to the holder (1.8×). In a **strong sale**, $100 million of enterprise value less $10 million in assumed net debt and deal deductions yields $90 million of distributable equity and $2.7 million to the holder (5.4×). At $20 million of deductions, that same sale falls to $2.4 million (4.8×). In the **prolonged-private case**, continued operation with cash reinvested and no eligible share sale yields $0 realized. These are illustrative sensitivities. The memo gives no basis for underwriting a sale price or an investor waterfall. (Market & Traction, Key Facts)

### Countercase

Anthropic and OpenAI already ship native publishing, and Type.com already connects Claude and Codex to a shared workspace. Those substitutes weaken the reason to buy. Exports to Drive and GitHub reduce switching costs, while Claude Code’s web restrictions add setup friction. Without evidence that teams repeatedly use the cross-agent review workflow, a buyer has little demonstrated reason to pay a premium for it. This supports Watch. The memo establishes neither a structural closing block nor a credible sale-price ceiling that would trigger Pass. (Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can Spacesheep show that sustained paid cross-agent review creates enough value for a specific collaboration vendor to justify a purchase that clears the holder’s proceeds hurdle? Paid cohort data showing repeated cross-agent review, paired with a buyer-specific build-versus-buy case that supports at least $83.3 million in distributable equity under reasonable deal adjustments, would move the action to Reach out. Until then, Watch.
