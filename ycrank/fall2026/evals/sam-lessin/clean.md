# Clean -- Sam Lessin Evaluation

Clean has evidence that somebody wants the product. I still cannot underwrite the deal. Proposed post-money valuation: undisclosed. Competing term sheets: undisclosed. Whether my money supplies a genuinely scarce check: unknown. The reported $50K from Jackson Square Ventures and $500K from YC establish financing, not attractive entry terms or an auction. I give the missing terms my stated default. The same applies to proceeds: the dossier names no capital-funded experiment with a metric and deadline. Those are consequential blanks when I am investing my own money.

The strongest argument for writing the check is Vivameda. Its reported reply rate rose from approximately 4% to 19%, and it closed $60K in contracts within two weeks using Clean’s research ([case study](https://www.tryclean.ai/customers/vivameda)). That is an actual customer action connected to the workflow. Dated shipments, permits, loans, and contracts could tell a seller something economically useful that a contact list cannot. This fits my pattern of backing businesses that translate between disconnected systems: operational activity on one side, a seller’s immediate decision on the other. But one company-published testimonial does not establish repeatability, and aggregating source-linked records does not establish ownership of the underlying access. Clean earns meaningful distribution-and-workflow credit, short of a demonstrated proprietary dark pool.

The founder-reported $240K ARR before YC makes the bull case substantially harder to dismiss ([Pavan’s LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/pavankumarny)). A five-person team that pivoted during summer 2026 and already sells the resulting product may have found a compact, useful business. That revenue earns economics credit; Vivameda’s contracts belong to Vivameda. Without Clean’s data costs, research labor, gross margin, burn, or retention, I cannot tell whether another customer generates cash or another servicing obligation. Delete the next financing from the spreadsheet: the dossier does not yet show what happens. It also does not show that another round is necessary, so I will not invent that liability.

The team has more substance than a collection of school names. Pratham shipped Lokus, and the prior Clean MCP product reportedly attracted 10,000-plus unique sessions in a month. Those are engineering receipts, although neither establishes demand for the current sales product. Pavan’s Cisco sales experience supplies relevant access to the other side of the workflow, but the dossier gives no concrete sales operating result from that role. I see a plausible bridge with some shipped proof. The 25K LinkedIn followers do not strengthen it without evidence that those relationships converted into Clean customers.

My concern is where the value accumulates after this research becomes easy. Clay announced context-aware research agents and signal monitoring in September 2026; Apollo also combines research and outreach. Clean’s licensed-data dependencies and unproven exclusivity leave those incumbents a credible route into the same buying-window workflow. This activates my AI application red flag, without proving that Clean is merely a disposable wrapper. I checked the exception case: paid demand is present, but the dossier lacks the complete conversion, retention, independent acquisition validation, or unusual financing structure that has justified overriding my gates elsewhere. The strongest bull argument remains that customers already buy before the moat is obvious. I accept that signal; it does not establish what shareholders will retain.

I pass on the evidence available. Clean could become a worthwhile business, and its early customer outcomes deserve attention. My one verifiable flip condition is a documented financing offer below $10 million post-money whose budget funds a six-month test of whether a defined customer cohort renews at contribution margins sufficient to reach cash break-even without another raise. That would connect the price, the experiment, and the functioning business. Today those pieces remain disconnected.
