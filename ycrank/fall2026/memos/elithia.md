## The Idea

- **Problem:** Physician expert witnesses and medical-legal firms manually reconstruct patient histories from thousands of pages, spending hours or days reviewing records ([YC, Nov 2026, via search snippet](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/elithia)).
- **Approach:** Agents remove blank and duplicate pages, date and organize documents, and generate chronologies with findings linked to source pages and Word/PDF exports ([company LinkedIn, accessed Nov 2026](https://uk.linkedin.com/company/elithia)).
- **Differentiation:** Elithia targets clinician expert review, whereas [EvenUp](https://www.evenuplaw.com/about/) and [Supio](https://www.supio.com/about) cover broader plaintiff-law workflows and [DigitalOwl](https://www.digitalowl.com/about) combines analysis with retrieval; its cited-chronology mechanism also appears in competitors’ offerings ([Elithia LinkedIn](https://uk.linkedin.com/company/elithia)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Paid professional access follows an introductory trial covering the first 1,000 pages, but paid rates and billing units are undisclosed ([company LinkedIn, Nov 2026](https://uk.linkedin.com/company/elithia)).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** The company reports sales to expert witnesses and medical-legal firms, with third-party administrators and insurers identified as subsequent targets ([YC, Nov 2026, via search snippet](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/elithia)).
