# machine0 -- Elad Gil Evaluation

Barnaby Malet’s cloud-resource-allocation research, Upflow CTO/CPO tenure through a $15M Series A, and 1.1K-star Takana repository give machine0 a strong founder-shipping profile attached to the weakest possible revenue signal: none disclosed. machine0 is an operator-shaped infrastructure bet whose founder has repeatedly built, but whose current product has not shown that it is “just working.” That high-founder/low-velocity pairing is machine0’s analytical signature.

**Perplexity** is the high rung for founder iteration because Aravind Srinivas repeatedly shipped discussed features within a week. Malet places machine0 below that rung but materially above the credential-only founder: he built payment products at Hike, co-created the Director MCP gateway, scaled Upflow as CTO/CPO, and pivoted machine0 toward persistent VMs when late-2025 agents began running for 6–8 hours. The divergence is important: machine0’s public repositories have only 13 and one stars, so the dossier establishes prior shipping capacity, not Perplexity-like evidence that machine0 is compounding user feedback between conversations.

**Mistral** occupied the low revenue rung at entry: pre-revenue and capital-intensive, with the weakness overridden by a post-ChatGPT catalyst and founders who had already shipped Llama. machine0 shows the same missing-curve problem without an equivalent exception. The $0.013-per-hour CPU price and $0.836-per-hour GPU price prove that machine0 can charge, while 96 Show HN points prove only curiosity; machine0 discloses no customer, usage, retention, or revenue count. In an AI infrastructure market where RunPod reached $120M ARR and Modal approximately $50M annualized revenue, silence is a negative signal, not normal pre-seed ambiguity.

Late-2025 improvements in coding agents created a credible market catalyst for machine0: sessions expanded from ephemeral calls into 6–8-hour or always-on workloads. Over the next 18 months, that shift could compound machine0’s demand because longer-running agents need persistent state, snapshots, static IPs, and suspend-resume economics rather than disposable sandboxes. The problem is catalyst ownership. E2B and Daytona can extend persistence faster than machine0 can establish a category, so machine0 is skating for the puck but has not shown that the puck requires a new vendor.

E2B’s roughly $32M and Daytona’s roughly $31M in funding frame the counterfactual for machine0’s index position. machine0 becomes an index on agent activity only if persistent identity and state become a standard layer that developers address through machine0 regardless of the underlying workload; reusable images, snapshots, and telemetry would then compound across every agent. Today machine0 resells DigitalOcean-backed KVM/QEMU compute in a market also served by Modal, RunPod, hyperscalers, and ordinary VPS providers. A cross-cloud state layer or a de facto agent-computer API would flip this score; a cheaper persistent VM does not.

**Color Genomics** is the relevant single-anchor parallel: workflow infrastructure on top of commodity sequencing avoided requiring a new scientific breakthrough. machine0 similarly builds on validated NixOS, Ubuntu, KVM/QEMU, and DigitalOcean rather than inventing a new compute substrate. machine0’s technical architecture therefore needs no science miracle, but the business still needs persistent agent workloads to become distinct from conventional VMs while simultaneously overcoming funded substitutes, security demands from root SSH and public static IPs, and commodity-cloud margins. That is closer to one market miracle plus execution than a multi-science-project dependency chain, but it is not yet as clean as Color’s workflow abstraction.

DigitalOcean supplies machine0’s underlying capacity, and I expect machine0 by July 2029 to have pivoted from generic persistent VMs into an agent-state and workspace control plane while remaining between $1M and $3M ARR. The mechanism is the dimensional signature: Malet’s shipping history makes continued iteration more likely than death, while machine0’s absent buying curve and weak structural differentiation make an independent agent cloud unlikely. machine0 can build a useful layer without becoming the Stripe-like index on agent compute.

Upflow’s $15M Series A, Malet’s cloud research, the late-2025 workload shift, and machine0’s functioning pay-as-you-go product form the strongest bull case: machine0 could be the **Mistral** exception, where a founder who has shipped enters immediately after a category catalyst and turns apparent capital disadvantage into focus. That case stings because persistent agent identity may matter more than raw compute, and machine0 is early enough to define it. The evidence that wins today is the combination of 13 GitHub stars, zero disclosed customers, DigitalOcean dependence, and heavily funded substitutes already converging on “computers for agents.” I would not write a check yet; six consecutive months of greater than 20% month-over-month revenue growth culminating above $100K ARR from third-party customers would verifiably flip my verdict.
