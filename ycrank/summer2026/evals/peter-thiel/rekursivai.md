# rekursiv.ai -- Peter Thiel Evaluation

The contested truth is precise: a coordinated team of AI agents can perform consequential ML research more efficiently than human researchers working serially. The falsifier is equally clear—performance collapses on private, unfamiliar research problems once benchmark familiarity and hundreds of inexpensive trials are removed. rekursiv.ai has moved this beyond slogan: 235 ARC experiments across 15 directions and 684 Sudoku experiments produced reported 100% Sudoku-Extreme accuracy and competitive ARC results at 16–11,600 times lower computational cost than selected peers. But the evidence remains company-reported, the ARC evaluation was public, and the founders expect a four-to-six-point decline on semi-private tasks. This is a testable natural secret, though not yet one whose rejection by the field—or independent validation—the dossier establishes.

The founders are the strongest part of the company. Joshua Dillon and Dan Kondratyuk worked together at Google Research and Luma AI, co-authored VideoPoet, and have directly built foundation models, TensorFlow Probability, Veo, and world-model systems. This is a company formed by researchers around a problem they repeatedly encountered, not strangers assembled around a fashionable category. Their technical work appears in-house and both present themselves as current co-founders. The missing cap table, salaries, and ownership prevent a complete test of economic exposure, but there is no documented founding-structure red flag.

Does the technical result create a monopoly, or merely publish a better recipe for competitors? The initial market—software-only autonomous experimentation for ML teams—is appropriately narrow. Yet Sakana AI already open-sources an AI Scientist, several heavily financed companies are pursuing adjacent autonomous-science systems, and rekursiv.ai releases discoveries and components under Apache 2.0. The 235-experiment orchestration and unusually low reported compute costs suggest proprietary know-how, but no exclusive data, retention effect, or durable performance curve has emerged. The future experiment corpus could compound; today it is a hypothetical moat. The company participates in the emerging AI production stack without controlling the underlying models, compute, or an exclusive research dataset.

The strongest bull case is that the benchmark work is an early zero-to-one technical event: two unusually aligned researchers have built a system that generates discoveries they would not have produced themselves, while hundreds of autonomous experiments create the beginnings of a proprietary research memory. A verified technical discontinuity can override an initially uncertain monopoly. That exception does not yet apply here. Hypothesis-Pinning Search failed to transfer directly from Sudoku to ARC, and the absence of semi-private validation leaves open the possibility that rekursiv.ai has constructed an excellent benchmark optimizer rather than a general research system.

The commercial question is still almost entirely unanswered. There is no named buyer, price, revenue, contract, sales cycle, or implementation burden. Technical posts, eight public repositories, 16 GitHub followers, and gated access requests constitute an experiment in distribution, not a repeatable route whose economics can be evaluated. Nor can one calculate a power-law revenue engine from “enterprise access” or usage-based pricing inferred from a website. The $5 million financing funds the search for that engine; it is not evidence that the engine exists.

I would investigate, but I would not yet invest my own money. rekursiv.ai is an exceptional founding team attached to a plausible secret, with commercialization and value capture lagging far behind technical creation. The verifiable flip condition is one paid deployment at a named frontier-model laboratory that produces an independently confirmed improvement on a previously private research problem while reducing researcher-hours by at least half. That would simultaneously distinguish general discovery from benchmark search and reveal who pays for it. Until then: credible secret, uncertain monopoly, absent business.
