# Pomerado -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path B — New economies** is the strongest path. Pomerado turns a legacy website task into a reusable MCP or REST tool and attempts repairs when it fails. If reuse and repair reduce labor per successful task, smaller dealerships could automate recurring work in dealer portals that was previously too costly to integrate. The founders report dealership use, and the company tested prebuilt integrations on 30 tasks with one attempt per task. That test leaves setup, retries, human review, and maintenance labor unmeasured. The two San Francisco founders bring enterprise AI experience, and the memo records a shipped catalog of 584 tools across 447 sites. That supports seed-stage execution, while willingness to pay and repeat use remain open. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks, Founders & Team)

**Scale scenario.** At the listed $50 monthly Premium price, an account completing an assumed 1,000 successful tasks per month would generate 5¢ per successful task before browser, model, retry, repair, and support costs. The customer would also need to save enough manual labor to cover the subscription and any remaining review. Managed repairs could give customers a reason to keep paying under model parity if they measurably reduce that review after portals change. The memo leaves both sides of this cost comparison unmeasured. (The Idea, Key Risks, Defensibility)

### Countercase

Pomerado may make existing integrations cheaper while leaving previously excluded buyers uneconomic once exceptions and access changes are counted. Competitors with the same models and ample capital can build similar tooling. Pomerado’s core is MIT-licensed, and Skyvern has announced a similar reuse-and-repair approach. Portal operators control access, so Path A lacks a supported case for Pomerado controlling the bottleneck. These facts weigh against durable value capture. Equivalent delivered performance and buyer switching remain untested, so the Path B override has yet to trigger and the outreach gate remains unmet. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a paid trial with independent small dealerships that previously found a recurring portal workflow uneconomic show that they renew at a price that covers delivery costs when offered a comparable alternative using the same models? Measure human labor and total cost per successful task against the prior workflow, including exceptions and at least one portal change. Verified savings and renewals under those conditions would support Reach out if the reassessment clears 65 with at least medium confidence. If exception costs erase the savings or buyers readily switch to equivalent delivery, move to Pass.
