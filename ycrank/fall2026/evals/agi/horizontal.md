# horizontal. -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

New Economies is the strongest path. Factory teams manually reconcile records across plant systems when quality holds interrupt production. Horizontal says its agents can assemble evidence and coordinate release while people retain critical sign-off. A plausible new use is prompt, case-by-case investigation of routine holds that plants currently defer because manual coordination costs too much. That use is a scenario assumption. The memo gives no baseline or measured reduction in staff time per correctly resolved hold, including approvals and rework. Horizontal reports tests in operating plants and advertises 50% shorter release times without customer names or measurement baselines. The reported tests and manual workarounds give an early demand signal, with willingness to pay still open. (The Idea, Market & Traction)

Under model parity, a plant might still pay Horizontal for validated connections and approval workflows. Its reported on-premises deployment option and ISO 27001 certification support that possibility. The memo documents a public launch and relevant prior work in industrial enterprise sales and modeling. The proposed expansion unit is one workflow at one plant, with contribution depending on whether contract revenue covers site integration, validation, inference, support, and human exception handling. The memo supplies no deployment cost, pricing, or repeat rollout figures to ground that bridge. (Defensibility, Founders & Team, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Countercase

Horizontal may make existing factory work faster while leaving the same buyers and uses in place. SAP’s announced scheduling features overlap with part of the offering, and NVIDIA’s published blueprint gives competitors a starting point for quality investigations. Plant-specific validation could create recurring implementation costs without securing buyer preference. The memo shows neither functional parity nor buyers switching, so the substitution gate remains open. The scarce-complement path lacks evidence that Horizontal controls a scarce asset or service capacity. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Will an independent plant renew a paid deployment for a class of holds it previously deferred? Move to Reach out if a plant trial shows that Horizontal lowers the fully loaded cost per correctly resolved hold enough to make routine investigation viable, and the plant renews at a price that supports positive contribution after testing a comparably capable alternative. Stay on Watch if the result is inconclusive. Move to Pass if the trial establishes that the use was already economical and the plant readily substitutes the alternative.
