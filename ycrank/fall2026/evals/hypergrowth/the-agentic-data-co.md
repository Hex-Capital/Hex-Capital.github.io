# The Agentic Data Co. -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Assuming strong product-market fit with speech-model labs, the most scalable path is to license the same consented corpus to many labs. Open Yap’s synchronized speaker tracks address the same turn-taking and interruption problems wherever labs train models for comparable conversations. The free release under a data-use agreement gives labs a practical way to try the product. The reported benchmark result supports English conversational timing, while multilingual performance and tool-based task completion remain unverified. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

Hugging Face and GitHub provide bottom-up discovery and free access. The documented paid path starts with a founder inquiry. The two-person team recorded 8,204 downloads in the preceding month, and the company says unnamed AI labs buy its data. That claim provides a limited commercial signal. Paid volume and growth pace remain unknown. Recurring model training gives labs a reason to license fresh recordings, and YapRoom provides a way to collect them. Repeat orders and recurring billing remain unverified. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Facts)

**Cost scenario.** If recording rights permit licensing the same corpus to several labs, contribution equals combined license revenue less collection, screening, review, storage, delivery, and attributable sales and support costs. Participant payouts alone run $20 per clear two-person hour. Reusing the corpus spreads collection and review costs across licenses. The company’s license prices and realized contribution remain unknown. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Countercase

Labs may use the free release to evaluate the data and pay only for custom collections. In that case, new orders could bring fresh speaker payouts, human review, and founder-led selling. A competitor’s published entry price of $10 per audio hour signals pricing pressure, though the memo establishes neither comparable terms nor the company’s realized price. The structural gate stays open because the catalog offers a credible route to corpus reuse and the memo leaves the amount of required custom work unresolved. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Risks)

Can multiple labs buy and reorder standard catalog data with minimal founder involvement and positive incremental contribution? A paid cohort that demonstrates this from first purchase through reorder would support Reach out.
