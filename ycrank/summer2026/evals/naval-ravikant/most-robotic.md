# Most Robotic -- Naval Ravikant Evaluation

The founders may possess specific knowledge, but the dossier proves only its outline. Most Robotic describes two middle-school friends as MIT computer scientists with robotics research and collective experience at NASA JPL, SpaceX, and AWS. Those are relevant environments, not evidence that either founder personally built a physical-AI system, produced a research result, collected robot-interaction data, or deployed into a customer workflow. Credentials indicate where knowledge might have formed; artifacts reveal whether it did.

The strongest case is that secrecy hides the important evidence. A two-person physical-AI lab fits the movement toward embodied intelligence, and prior robotics work could support a technically strange product before the market understands it. The closest exception is Bolt: little commercial output was overridden by action-revealed founder behavior. The material difference is that Bolt had demonstrated recruiting and rapid, organized execution; here, even the founders’ names, individual contributions, and technical mechanism remain unverified. Pre-seed status excuses missing revenue. It does not turn missing action into demonstrated specific knowledge.

If a competitor copied the code today, the dossier identifies nothing that would remain. There is no disclosed hardware, proprietary model, exclusive dataset, benchmark, deployment experience, or accumulating feedback loop. Meanwhile, NVIDIA’s open Cosmos 3 stack reduced physical-AI training and evaluation cycles from months to days in May 2026. Skild AI has an omni-bodied model, approximately $30 million in reported 2025 live revenue, and $2.215 billion raised; FieldAI reports deployments across hundreds of industrial environments. Capital is not a moat, but deployed data is. Most Robotic has disclosed neither.

The market cannot yet be separated from the technology category. “Research lab for physical AI” names a substrate, not an initial buyer, task, or transaction. Without an embodiment or workflow, there is no causal path from a narrow wedge into adjacent demand. There is also no permissionless system, fragmented supply network, or mechanism by which one participant improves the product for another. The company matches the embodied-infrastructure direction in my recent allocation, but none of the unstated compounding patterns is observable.

There are no disclosed founder-integrity problems, artificial financing pressure, or reassurance-seeking behavior, so I do not manufacture a red flag from silence. Economics receive the same treatment: no payer, price, LOI, transaction, or revenue is public, so monetization remains unknown rather than disproven. The asymmetry may eventually be attractive, but an option still needs an identifiable source of nonlinear upside.

I pass today. One verifiable condition would flip the decision: a repeatable benchmark showing Most Robotic’s proprietary system outperforming Cosmos 3 on a real robot after learning from deployment data unavailable to competitors. That would simultaneously reveal shipped specific knowledge and a substrate that compounds after the code is copied.
