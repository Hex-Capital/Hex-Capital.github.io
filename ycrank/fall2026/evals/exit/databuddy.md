# Databuddy -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

A strategic sale to an analytics vendor is Databuddy’s strongest supported exit route. Plausible is a potential buyer on product fit. Databuddy describes scheduled Slack investigations that combine traffic, errors, and revenue data, an extension to Plausible’s cookieless traffic and conversion reporting. Owning that workflow could give a vendor a premium feature to sell to existing customers. The economic benefit and buyer interest remain unverified. (The Idea, Market & Traction)

The Stripe-connected snapshot shows 38 active subscriptions and $3,345 in company-wide MRR. Reported funding of more than $560,000 and an existing paid base make it plausible to test whether paying teams repeatedly use investigations and measurably shorten the time it takes to diagnose conversion problems. The company’s claim of serving more than 3,000 businesses leaves paid adoption and retention of the investigation feature unresolved. With a solo founder and unverified runway, the cost and timing of reaching that milestone remain uncertain. (Market & Traction, Key Facts, Founders & Team)

The shared benchmark assumes a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, 5% starting ownership diluted to 3%, and zero follow-ons. The holder needs $2.5 million in cash within eight years, which requires about $83.3 million in distributable equity at a full pro rata payout. The memo supports a revenue baseline. Buyer pricing and investor terms remain unevidenced. (Key Facts, Market & Traction)

| Scenario | Assumptions and cash to the holder |
|---|---|
| Modest sale | An illustrative $30 million enterprise value less $5 million in net debt and deal adjustments leaves $25 million in equity. If the holder participates in full at 3%, the waterfall pays pro rata, and all cash arrives by year eight, the holder receives $750,000, or 1.5×. |
| Strong sale | An illustrative $100 million enterprise value with the same deductions and payout assumptions leaves $95 million in equity. The holder receives $2.85 million, or 5.7×. Code and contract transfer rights, data permissions, and holder eligibility remain unverified. The memo leaves independent liquidity routes unevidenced. (Key Risks, Market & Traction)

### Countercase

Mixpanel already offers proactive metric monitoring, and PostHog exposes analytics to AI agents. Both can deliver parts of Databuddy’s workflow on data they already hold. The memo identifies no demonstrated switching costs, so a potential buyer could build the remaining features or accept its existing coverage. This weakens the case for a price that clears the holder’s hurdle. The Pass gate remains open pending evidence of a structural barrier or a supported proceeds shortfall on the strongest route. (Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

What would a plausible analytics acquirer pay to own Databuddy’s investigation workflow once a retained cohort of paying customers shows repeat use and measurable time savings? A buyer-side build-versus-buy assessment grounded in those results would move Watch to Reach out if it supported a price that clears the $2.5 million holder objective after deal deductions and the payout waterfall.
