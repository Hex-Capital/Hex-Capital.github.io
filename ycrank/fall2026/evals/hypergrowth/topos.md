# Topos -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Conditional on strong product-market fit with biology research teams, Egregore could serve the same recurring job across markets by helping researchers compare hypotheses and check the reasoning behind them. Topos says its harness can coordinate thousands of agents on a research task. Reusing that software across labs is plausible, but the beta evidence leaves open how much adaptation each lab’s data and workflow would require. (The Idea)

The memo records a limited beta with an access-request form that asks for affiliation and intended use. That gives Topos a reusable intake process. Independent activation, first value, and payment remain unverified. The memo provides no usable evidence of paid adoption or commercial growth, so initial traction takes the default. Hypothesis review could recur, but the memo establishes neither repeat use nor a mechanism for expanding spend within existing accounts, so retention also takes the default. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Facts)

If Topos charged per research run, incremental contribution would be the fee less inference and coordination costs, attributable onboarding and support, and any expert review Topos had to supply. The memo identifies coordination overhead as a risk but gives no price, workload cost, or delivery labor estimates to assess contribution. Cost economics takes the default. (The Idea, Key Risks)

### Countercase

Even with strong initial fit, Egregore could require substantial compute and expert involvement for each useful result. Coordination overhead could erode the value of swarms on sequential tasks, and biological conclusions still require empirical validation. The structural downside gate stays open because the memo does not establish that Topos must supply that validation or that ordinary workloads are uneconomic to serve. (Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a paid cohort of research teams in more than one market use the same Egregore setup, reach first value independently, and return for paid runs that earn positive incremental contribution? Onboarding records, repeat invoices, and per-run costs from that cohort would answer it. If the results support at least 65 points and Medium confidence with the structural gate clear, the action changes to Reach out.
