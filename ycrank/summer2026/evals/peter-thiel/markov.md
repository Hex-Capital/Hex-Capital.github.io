# Markov -- Peter Thiel Evaluation

**Dev Mandal’s Sarvam AI experience and doubt.ai’s 700 users give Markov credible credentialed-outsider founders; Markov’s lowest signal is structural moat, because 12,300 hours of recordings remain bits without a regulatory, capex, or rights barrier.** Markov is therefore an operator-led data supplier with evidence of unusual execution but no demonstrated mechanism for becoming a monopoly. Intelligence can assemble an asset. It cannot prevent imitation.

**Affirm’s high rung was Max Levchin’s verified history of shipping PayPal to scale; operator pedigree reduced execution uncertainty before Affirm’s financing thesis was proven.** Markov shares the adjacent-domain mechanism: Dev Mandal worked at Sarvam AI, and Harish Ashok previously built Zenith around hardware workflows. Markov diverges sharply because neither founder has Levchin’s shipped-to-scale outcome; doubt.ai’s 700 users in four weeks is a useful proxy, not a Mafia-grade credential. Markov sits above an untested YC pairing but below the pedigree that alone justified the Asana and Quora exceptions.

**Asana’s low rung on the atoms axis was pure workflow software with no regulatory or capex barrier; Dustin Moskovitz’s Facebook lineage supplied the exception.** Markov reproduces the structural weakness without reproducing the override. Markov collects workplace screen activity, but the dossier identifies no certification regime, proprietary hardware, regulated customer, or exclusive contributor-rights structure. The privacy problem could become a moat if Markov develops rights-cleared collection infrastructure others cannot replicate; today, the undisclosed consent and filtering process is liability, not monopoly.

**Scale AI’s ability to enter GUI data exposes the missing secret: Markov names competitors but does not name why Scale, Surge, or Turing structurally cannot win.** Over the next 18 months, Markov’s 150,000 Hugging Face downloads can attract researchers, projects, and more contributors, but open distribution also teaches competitors which workflows matter. Turing has already delivered more than 10,000 supervised GUI tasks. Markov’s thesis currently terminates at specialization—computer-use data instead of general annotation—which is a market position, not the Stripe-like secret that PayPal’s architecture could not answer.

**Markov’s 12,300-hour, 48,478-video corpus creates an early data advantage, but CC-BY-4.0 converts the flagship asset into common infrastructure.** Markov’s monopoly score would flip if the public corpus became an acquisition channel for an exclusive contributor network, proprietary task environments, and longitudinal customer feedback that improved quality faster than Scale or Turing could buy it. No customer retention, negotiated contract, or proprietary collection growth is disclosed. Without that flywheel, Markov sells inputs to powerful laboratories that can vertically integrate; the buyer has pricing power, not Markov.

**SpaceX’s definite plan committed to reusable launch through named technical milestones that could not be discovered by customer interviews.** Markov discloses recordings across Salesforce, AutoCAD, Blender, Photoshop, Excel, and VS Code, but Markov discloses no two-to-five-year architectural commitment: no target for interactive RL environments, workflow coverage, contributor density, or evaluation infrastructure. The dossier therefore triggers the absent-evidence default. A collection program is visible; a definite plan is not.

**Markov’s 150,000 downloads support a falsifiable forecast: by July 2029, Markov will be a $1 million–$3 million ARR bespoke GUI-agent data and evaluation vendor rather than an independent training-data platform with monopoly pricing.** Markov’s founders are capable enough to convert attention into contracts, but the low structural-moat signature channels that capability toward services and customized environments. One year of scarce data is a feature. Ten years of exclusive workflow supply would be a company.

**Markov’s 48,478 released videos form the strongest bull case: a two-person founder group assembled 12,300 hours before Summer 2026 and distributed it 150,000 times, evidence that Markov may be building the default computer-use data network before consensus sees the category.** The Asana and Quora exceptions warn against over-penalizing missing atoms when founder quality and network effects can compound. But Markov lacks their Facebook-scale pedigree, the public corpus is reusable, and no paying customer proves that downloads convert into a closed feedback loop. I pass unless Markov signs a verifiable three-year exclusive contract with a named frontier laboratory—such as Anthropic—worth at least $5 million in committed ARR while retaining proprietary rights to the resulting workflow data.
