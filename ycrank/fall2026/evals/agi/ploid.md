# Ploid -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path A — Scarce complements.** If cheaper intelligence drives more automated lookups, sales and recruiting agents will need a reliable way to tell which profiles belong to the same person and which details are current. Ploid has shipped search, profile research, and enrichment through an API, CLI, and MCP server. It reports a proprietary index of more than 2 billion people and a system that links professional and social identities. The inference is that buyers could still pay Ploid when models are equally capable if its index delivers materially better verified matches and Ploid controls the inputs behind that advantage. The memo provides no comparative results on accuracy, freshness, or coverage. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Risks)

The Fall 2026 launch and San Francisco listing suggest a plausible U.S. seed-stage fit. Hernandez’s retrieval and recommendation experience is relevant to the next technical test. Ploid reports thousands of companies using the product, and a founder reports closing clients. Payment, repeat usage, and contract values remain unverified. Path B remains unproven because the memo identifies an existing cleanup burden and provides no evidence that savings make a previously uneconomic buyer or use case viable. (Market & Traction, Founders & Team, Key Facts, The Idea)

**Scale scenario.** At full use of the included credits, Growth and Scale yield $0.20 and $0.15 per credit, respectively. Assuming customers pay list price and fully use their credits, variable costs per billed credit must stay below those figures for positive contribution. The relevant buyer metric is cost per verified result after retries and unresolved matches. The memo supplies no actual utilization, credits per verified result, or costs for vendor data, live retrieval, and exception handling. (The Idea, Key Risks, Key Facts)

### Countercase

With comparable models and ample capital, buyers could combine Apollo’s MCP people search and Crustdata’s live data API with their own identity matching. Ploid also relies on vendors for some contact data and acknowledges that vendor-supplied addresses can be stale or misclassified. These overlaps and dependencies outweigh the claimed index size as evidence of durable value capture. They leave the Path A outreach gate unmet. A like-for-like test has yet to establish that buyers can switch without losing quality, so the thesis override does not close the path. (Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Will an independent sales or recruiting platform commit to repeat paid volume after a same-model trial shows that Ploid delivers more correct, current matches per dollar than the buyer’s best available alternative? The trial should use representative lookups, count retries and human review, and trace any advantage to data or matching capabilities Ploid controls. A material advantage at viable margins, followed by a paid commitment, would support rescoring for Reach out if the score and confidence gates clear. Comparable results from the alternative would close Path A and move the action to Pass on the current Path B evidence.
