# Databuddy -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path B, new economies, is the strongest path.** Databuddy describes Databunny as combining traffic, funnel, error, and connected revenue data, exposing its queries for review, and sending scheduled investigations to Slack. The plausible new transaction is a recurring, decision-ready investigation for a startup without a data analyst. If automation cuts the time founders spend reconciling tools and checking findings, teams could afford to investigate conversion changes routinely. The memo gives no like-for-like comparison of investigation quality or human time, including review, retries, and rework after an incorrect diagnosis. (The Idea; Key Risks)

The memo records 38 active subscriptions and $3,345 in company-wide MRR. That establishes early paid demand for Databuddy, while the share attributable to recurring AI investigations by teams without analysts is unknown. Solo founder Issa Nassar has relevant analytics experience and has shipped the original analytics product and launched Databunny. The next execution test is delivering reliable investigations across customer setups. (Market & Traction; Key Facts; Founders & Team; The Idea)

Event-metered subscriptions provide a way to earn more as accounts grow. In an illustrative scenario, a $299 Business account running four investigations a month would have about $75 in subscription revenue per investigation if the entire fee were allocated to investigations. The plan also funds analytics, and the memo gives no costs for event processing, inference, or human support. Contribution per additional account remains unknown. (The Idea; Key Risks)

### Countercase

With the same model capabilities, buyers could investigate data already held in their analytics tools. Mixpanel already offers proactive metric monitoring, and PostHog makes analytics available to AI agents. Databuddy’s configured event schemas, funnels, and investigation history might make switching costly, but the memo gives no retention or buyer preference evidence under functional parity. The incumbent overlap weighs against durable value capture. It leaves full-job substitution unproven, so the Path B override stays open and the affirmative outreach gate remains unmet. Path A has no separate support because the memo identifies no scarce input or capability Databuddy controls. (Defensibility; The Idea; Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Will independent startups without analysts repeatedly pay for Databunny to run investigations they previously skipped when they can use an equally capable model on their existing analytics stack? A paid comparison should measure human time per equally useful investigation, including review and rework, then give teams a renewal choice at viable pricing. Material time savings and repeated paid preference would support **Reach out** if the updated score clears 65. If the savings disappear or buyers readily switch under parity, **Pass**.
