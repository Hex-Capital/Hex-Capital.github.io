## The Idea

- **Problem:** [Inferred]: Robotics and AI researchers who need to test spatial reasoning and team coordination must build or adapt tasks in simulators such as NVIDIA Isaac Lab whenever they need different mechanics, objectives, or agent roles ([NVIDIA Isaac Lab](https://developer.nvidia.com/isaac/lab), [Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/XT3-melty-gym-programmable-games-for-rl-and-evals)).
- **Approach:** Melty Gym uses an engine that decompiles and reworks existing games so researchers can bring their own models or robots and change the mechanics, objectives, and team dynamics ([Launch YC, Nov 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/XT3-melty-gym-programmable-games-for-rl-and-evals)).
- **Differentiation:** Gym exposes game mechanics for researchers to modify around a task, Good Start Labs builds training tasks around curated games with verifiable rewards, and Isaac Lab provides physics simulation designed for robot learning ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/XT3-melty-gym-programmable-games-for-rl-and-evals), [Good Start Labs](https://goodstartlabs.com/), [NVIDIA Isaac Lab](https://developer.nvidia.com/isaac/lab)).
- **Business Model:** The Windows launcher and consumer mashups are free ([Melty FAQ](https://melty.gg/#faq), [Melty Terms, Oct 2026](https://melty.gg/terms)); Gym pricing and company revenue: No public data found.
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** Gym invites research teams to contact Miller for custom environments, and the consumer launcher distributes multiplayer mashups through shareable invite links and public lobbies ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/XT3-melty-gym-programmable-games-for-rl-and-evals), [Melty website](https://melty.gg/)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Melty’s consumer terms give it exclusive marketplace distribution rights over creator-published mashups and reserve ownership of its prepared packages, catalog, and compatibility, testing, and audit data ([Melty Terms, Oct 2026](https://melty.gg/terms)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Reusable integrations across game engines could reduce the work of building each new Gym task, but the launch gives no public data on integration reuse or task setup time ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/XT3-melty-gym-programmable-games-for-rl-and-evals)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** [Inferred]: Game-based evaluation teams can reproduce some training tasks without matching Melty’s full engine, as Good Start Labs already builds game environments with verifiable rewards and publishes evaluations on them ([Good Start Labs](https://goodstartlabs.com/)).

## Key Risks

- **Publisher and platform dependency:** Consumer mashups require legitimate game copies and remain subject to publisher rules and anti-cheat restrictions, while Melty requires creators to hold distribution permissions and provides a takedown process ([Melty Terms, Oct 2026](https://melty.gg/terms)).
- **Simulation-to-real transfer:** [Inferred]: Policies trained in commercial games may lose accuracy on physical hardware because the launch shows simulated tasks and provides no physical-robot transfer results ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/XT3-melty-gym-programmable-games-for-rl-and-evals), [Nvdium](https://nvdium.com/en/articles/444878376)).
- **Third-party code execution:** The consumer launcher downloads tools and runs creator-supplied programs locally, and Melty says it audits each release and update and requires a successful run on the creator’s PC before publication ([Melty Terms, Oct 2026](https://melty.gg/terms)).
- **Name ambiguity:** [Inferred]: Search and market tracking can misattribute activity because Conductor previously launched a separate AI code editor named Melty under `melty.sh` ([YC Conductor profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/conductor), [YC Melty profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/melty-gg)).
