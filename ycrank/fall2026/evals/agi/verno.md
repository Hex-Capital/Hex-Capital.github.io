# Verno -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

New Economies is Verno’s strongest path. EPCs currently pay for drafting, manual reconciliation across documents, and engineering review. Verno documents a workflow that prepares native CAD edits and related file updates for engineer approval. Verno reports finding three conflicting design pressures for one tank in drawings already issued for construction. That finding illustrates the cost of missed reconciliation. The memo gives no baseline or measured reduction in cost per correctly reconciled, engineer-approved change. One plausible new use is routine cross-document checking for small plant changes that teams currently skip because manual reconciliation costs too much. Whether those teams would pay for that use remains open. (*The Idea*, *Market & Traction*, *Key Risks*)

Under model parity, customer-specific Excel templates, native CAD write-back, and approval histories could give buyers a reason to keep paying Verno. The memo shows workflow integration, while buyer preference under comparable functionality and network effects remain unproven. Verno reports an active pilot and YC reports live customer sites. Pricing, payment, and repeat use are undisclosed. (*Defensibility*, *Market & Traction*, *Key Facts*)

The team brings directly relevant engineering documentation, document extraction, and process engineering experience. The reported pilot shows early operating progress, while error rates across customer document sets remain unmeasured in the memo. A repeat project within an EPC is a plausible expansion unit if templates and integrations carry over. Contribution would depend on whether the license covers inference, deployment, customer-specific CAD validation, and support. The buyer’s savings would also have to survive engineer review and rework. (*Founders & Team*, *The Idea*, *Defensibility*, *Key Risks*)

### Countercase

Verno may save time on engineering work EPCs already buy without making any previously uneconomic work viable. Engineers still approve changes, and setup-specific validation and error correction could absorb much of the drafting labor saved. Autodesk already validates P&IDs against project models and could extend that workflow to downstream files. The memo establishes neither actual substitution nor a failed cost test, so the New Economies path stays open on Watch. There is no independently supported scarce-complement path. (*The Idea*, *Defensibility*, *Key Risks*)

### Decisive diligence question

Will an independent EPC pay to expand Verno to a class of changes it previously left unchecked, when a comparable model-based workflow is available? Move to Reach out if a paid expansion shows that Verno brings the cost per correctly reconciled, engineer-approved change below the buyer’s prior viability threshold, counting review, exceptions, and rework, and the buyer chooses Verno for its workflow capabilities at comparable model quality. Stay on Watch if the comparison is inconclusive. Move to Pass if the test shows the work remains uneconomic or buyers readily substitute at parity.
