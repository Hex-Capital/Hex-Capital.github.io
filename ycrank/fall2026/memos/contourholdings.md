## The Idea

- **Problem:** Data center, power plant, and factory developers rely on environmental consultants who prepare studies and applications manually and coordinate revisions with regulators, creating delays before construction ([Launch YC, Nov 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Xeo-contour-ai-native-enviromental-permitting-and-compliance-for-america-s-buildout)).
- **Approach:** Contour’s acquisition model puts Cortex inside environmental consultancies, where its agents research federal and state rules, calculate emissions, and draft permit applications and compliance reports while staff handle technical judgment and client work ([Launch YC, Nov 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Xeo-contour-ai-native-enviromental-permitting-and-compliance-for-america-s-buildout)).
- **Differentiation:** Contour’s model pairs permanent ownership with an internal AI platform, while True Environmental offers acquisition capital and management support, Blumen sells site and permitting intelligence, and PermitFlow automates construction permit filing and coordination ([Contour](https://contourholdings.ai/), [True Environmental](https://true-environmental.com/true-environmental-announces-acquisition-of-sikumiut-environmental-management-sem/), [Blumen](https://blumensystems.com/), [PermitFlow](https://www.permitflow.com/blog/permitflow-series-b)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Contour would earn service fees through acquired firms’ client engagements because Cortex is designed for internal deployment and the company solicits permitting work directly ([Contour](https://contourholdings.ai/), [Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Xeo-contour-ai-native-enviromental-permitting-and-compliance-for-america-s-buildout)).
- **TAM/SAM:** Stout estimated the broader U.S. environmental consulting market at about $27B in 2025 with 6% forecast annual near-term growth, with no public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment ([Stout, Sep 2025](https://www.stout.com/-/media/pdf/industry-update/environmental-sciences/stout-ib_september-2025-environmental-services-industry-update-vf.pdf)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Contour solicits environmental practice owners through its website, says it will hold acquired firms permanently and install Cortex with engineers on site, and invites infrastructure developers to contact the founders for permitting work ([Contour](https://contourholdings.ai/), [Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Xeo-contour-ai-native-enviromental-permitting-and-compliance-for-america-s-buildout)).

## Key Risks

- **Technical feasibility:** [Inferred]: Incorrect AI-generated emissions calculations could require rework before a responsible official can certify a federal Title V application, and Contour says staff retain responsibility for technical judgment ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Xeo-contour-ai-native-enviromental-permitting-and-compliance-for-america-s-buildout), [40 CFR § 71.5(d)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CFR-2025-title40-vol17/pdf/CFR-2025-title40-vol17-sec71-5.pdf)).
- **Acquisition competition:** [Inferred]: Contour competes for environmental consulting practices with buyers such as Halle Capital-backed True Environmental, and Contour offers permanent ownership and on-site engineering support to sellers ([True Environmental, Oct 2026](https://true-environmental.com/true-environmental-announces-acquisition-of-sikumiut-environmental-management-sem/), [Contour](https://contourholdings.ai/)).
- **Regulatory scope:** [Inferred]: Wider use of categorical exclusions could reduce demand for some federal environmental studies, while Cortex also addresses state permitting and ongoing compliance ([White House CEQ, Apr 2026](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/04/ceq-issues-guidance-on-categorical-exclusions/), [Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Xeo-contour-ai-native-enviromental-permitting-and-compliance-for-america-s-buildout)).
- **Name confusion:** YC lists two companies named Contour, creating a risk of misattribution between environmental permitting and healthcare voice automation ([YC Fall 2026 listing](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/contourholdings), [YC Summer 2023 listing](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/contour)).
