## The Idea

- **Problem:** Independent practices face delayed or denied insurance payments from authorization, documentation, and coding mismatches while staff work across disconnected eligibility, claims, and appeals systems ([Altair, accessed Oct 27, 2026](https://altair-health.com/)).
- **Approach:** Altair says its agents perform billing, a theorem prover checks proposed claim actions against formalized insurer policies, and human billers handle actions that fail verification ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/altair-health)).
- **Differentiation:** Altair positions itself as an outsourced billing operation using existing systems, compared with athenahealth’s integrated EHR/billing suite and Anomaly’s payer-intelligence platform ([Altair comparison](https://altair-health.com/docs/compare), [Anomaly](https://www.findanomaly.com/)).
- **Business Model:** Altair offers a free standalone EHR with optional billing, while Capterra separately lists a $395/year prior-authorization plan with conflicting per-user/per-provider descriptions; current managed-billing fees: No public data found ([Altair EHR](https://altair-health.com/ehr), [Capterra, Oct 2026, via search snippet](https://www.capterra.com/p/10048806/Altair-Health/)).
- **TAM/SAM:** The broader U.S. medical-billing-outsourcing market is estimated at $7.73B in 2026, with 12.56% CAGR through 2033, but this includes customers beyond independent practices and does not establish Altair’s SAM ([Grand View Research, Jan 2026](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/us-medical-billing-outsourcing-market)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Aouss reports acquiring an initial design partner through clinic visits in Texas, while the website solicits demos and offers a behavioral-health EHR waitlist ([Aouss LinkedIn post reproduced in activity feed, Oct 2026, via search snippet](https://hu.linkedin.com/in/yassine-laajil-11964221a), [Altair EHR](https://altair-health.com/ehr)).

- **Integration and deployment:** [Inferred]: Operational dependence on EHR and clearinghouse connections may complicate rollout; Jad reports a year between initial conversation and full deployment at the disclosed practice ([LinkedIn activity feed, Oct 2026, via search snippet](https://ma.linkedin.com/in/omar-laalej-6636925)).
- **Patient-data exposure:** [Inferred]: Handling billing and clinical information creates security obligations; Altair reports BAAs, encryption, role-based access, and action logging as controls ([Altair security FAQ](https://altair-health.com/)).
- **Name confusion:** An unrelated neurosurgical organization also uses Altair Health, creating attribution risk for funding, financial, and staffing searches ([unrelated organization profile, via search snippet](https://www.signalhire.com/companies/ans-altair-health)).
