# Theseus Robotics -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path B — New Economies.** Theseus could make reliable overnight replenishment economical for grocers that currently leave some shifts uncovered. The former delivery model depends on staff unloading cartons and stocking shelves by hand. The company says overnight shifts are hard to staff. The Zürich footage shows a robot operating in a store, and Theseus reports a peak of 400 units restocked per hour. The footage establishes in-store operation. The reported peak leaves full-shift throughput and human labor savings unresolved. *(The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)*

Under the investment scenario, one remote operator can cover enough robots that operator time and store-side exception handling per correctly stocked unit fall below the manual baseline. The retailer’s cost per accepted unit would include the monthly fee and residual store labor, measured over representative shifts with retries and downtime reflected in completed output. Theseus would also need the fee to cover hardware, installation, maintenance, and remote staffing at realized utilization. Daytime picking could spread fixed costs if stores use it consistently. The memo gives a monthly pricing structure and leaves the fee, intervention rates, and sustained utilization undisclosed. *(The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)*

Under model parity, a buyer could still pay Theseus to run replenishment in its existing aisles, including handling store-specific planograms and recovering from exceptions. Theseus has assembled that workflow with remote fallback. Whether it can deliver a consistently better service than an integrator with equivalent models and hardware remains untested. Two stores gave Theseus operating access, and the company reports pilots lined up for up to 10 additional robots in 2027. Buyer budgets and paid repeat use remain unverified. The founders’ learning-based controls work and prior live picking deployment support seed-stage execution. *(The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction, Founders & Team)*

### Countercase

Theseus may automate part of an existing labor budget while leaving the same overnight coverage uneconomic. If unfamiliar packaging and shelf layouts require frequent remote intervention or store-side cleanup, peak throughput could coexist with little shift-level labor savings. HIVE is already piloting overnight replenishment, and Theseus uses off-the-shelf robot bodies. A rival with equivalent models could plausibly match the service. The memo leaves both market opening and value capture under model parity unproven and provides no representative comparison that defeats either condition. That supports Watch. *(Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)*

Move to Reach out if shift logs show lower human labor per correctly stocked unit, the paid price supports both the grocer’s new coverage and positive contribution for Theseus, and the grocer chooses Theseus for a demonstrated service advantage.
