# Biomes -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

The strongest path is **scarce complements**. As private-equity sponsors spend more on AI, engineers who can integrate agents into legacy systems and keep them reliable in production could become a bottleneck. The memo reports that 80% of surveyed general partners increased portfolio AI spending over the prior year. Biomes says it embeds engineers with operating teams and uses failures on real tasks to build evaluations. That approach targets a specific deployment problem, but the memo documents no Biomes field test, control over scarce delivery capacity, or evidence that its evaluations transfer across acquisitions. The New Economies path would require evidence that lower cost per successful task opens a previously uneconomic use. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

The memo records the founders’ Cardinal Square launch and relevant data and ML infrastructure experience. That supports seed-stage execution credit, with production delivery for a portfolio company still unproven. The memo reports no buyer commitment or revenue for the current business. A second deployment of the same workflow is the natural unit of expansion. The advertised $400,000 engineering salary plus equity is a cost signal, while pricing, integration hours, and ongoing exception-handling costs remain unknown. (Founders & Team, Market & Traction, Key Facts, Key Risks)

### Countercase

If each acquisition requires substantial custom integration, Biomes may have to add engineering capacity with each rollout while competing for sponsor access. Topsail markets a similar embedded service, and the memo cites DeployCo working with a TPG portfolio company. Those are credible routes to substitution, but the memo contains no buyer switching or comparable delivery results that establish easy substitution. The path remains open for a specific field test. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

Will an independent sponsor that evaluated a credible alternative pay Biomes to deploy the same workflow at a second portfolio company?
