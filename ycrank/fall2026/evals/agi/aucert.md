# Aucert -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Pass  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Aucert targets mobile release verification. The memo documents a public launch and a sample scan report. Aucert says its agents explore builds, check them against customer-approved expectations, and return findings that humans can use in release decisions. Coding agents may increase the volume of changes that teams need to review, but the memo leaves unsubstantiated whether scarce verification capacity constrains any specific AI workload or buildout. Building blocks scores zero. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

The old delivery model relies on manual QA, design, accessibility, and policy review. Scriptless exploration could reduce the human effort per verified build. The memo leaves open which teams or release cadences were priced out of that review and whether Aucert can bring them within reach at acceptable coverage. Industry enabled scores zero, triggering the Pass gate. (The Idea, Key Risks)

The company-described app knowledge graph and approved expectations could create switching costs if teams rely on them across releases. Retention and migration costs are unverified. The private beta listing and free scan offer leave buyer participation and paid conversion unverified. The founders’ prior work running mobile QA pipelines and building automated payment tests supports seed-stage execution credit, while dependable coverage on customer builds remains unproven. Draft subscription terms leave revenue per verified build and the costs of devices, inference, and human review unknown. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, The Idea, Founders & Team, Key Risks)

### Countercase

Aucert could make verification across QA, design, accessibility, and policy practical on every build for a team that can afford those checks only on selected releases. Running the app, applying its approved expectations, and preserving evidence for release decisions could remain valuable when buyers have access to equally capable models. The memo supports the workflow and identifies competing QA tools and overlapping native checks. The assumed review gap and the cost of closing it remain unverified, so the Pass gate still applies. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Risks)
