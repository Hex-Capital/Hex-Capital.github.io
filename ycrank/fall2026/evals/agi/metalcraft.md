# Metalcraft -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path A — Scarce complements.** If frontier-quality models become cheap and widely available, independent evaluations could become more valuable to developers deciding which models to trust for research workflows. Metalcraft’s opportunity is to license tasksets that cover decisions buyers need to test and would spend substantial time or expertise reproducing. The memo documents six public tasksets with sample cases and a request channel. Metalcraft reports model results by taskset, which shows how it presents comparisons. Whether buyers face a binding evaluation bottleneck and whether Metalcraft controls capacity that relieves it remain open. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Defensibility)

At seed, shipping the catalog after the pivot is execution evidence, reinforced by Marshall Kiely’s computational biology and applied AI experience. Licensing is an inferred business model. Buyer commitments, realized prices, and the cost of creating, validating, and refreshing tasksets remain unknown. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Founders & Team, Key Facts)

### Countercase

OpenAI has already built an overlapping expert-authored benchmark in-house, and Benchling offers evaluations drawn from laboratory experiments. Buyers may be able to get sufficient coverage from those alternatives or build their own. The memo also raises a separate question about whether taskset scores predict performance in live research workflows. The evidence of competing supply weakens value capture, while equivalence on a buyer’s required workflow remains untested. That leaves a specific test for Watch. (Defensibility, Key Risks)

Will an independent model developer pay to license a Metalcraft taskset after comparing it with the best available alternative on a research workflow it needs to evaluate? Move to **Reach out** if a paid commitment follows a documented comparison showing that Metalcraft covers a decision that matters to the buyer and that equivalent coverage would take substantial time or effort to reproduce.
