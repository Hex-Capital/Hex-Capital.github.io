## The Idea

- **Problem:** Claims and special investigation unit teams at carriers, MGAs and TPAs face investigation backlogs because flagged files require manual document gathering, verification and reporting ([Hesper About](https://gethesperai.com/about/)).
- **Approach:** Agents inspect documents, query external records, reconstruct timelines, check coverage and assemble evidence-backed case files for human review ([Hesper technology](https://gethesperai.com/technology/)).
- **Differentiation:** Hesper emphasizes completed investigation files; FRISS also addresses underwriting risk, Sprout.ai spans health and life alongside P&C, and Shift offers overlapping coverage, fraud and subrogation agents ([Hesper](https://gethesperai.com/about/), [FRISS](https://www.friss.com/), [Sprout.ai](https://sprout.ai/), [Shift](https://www.shift-technology.com/)).
- **Business Model:** Custom usage-based pricing depends on claim volume and deployed agents, with eligible new MGAs/TPAs receiving their first 200 claims free and no platform fee for 90 days ([Hesper Launch, accessed October 27, 2026](https://gethesperai.com/launch/)).
- **TAM/SAM:** The adjacent global insurance fraud detection market is estimated at $8.5 billion in 2026, growing at 25.2% CAGR through 2033; this includes products beyond Hesper’s investigation segment, whose SAM is undisclosed ([Grand View Research, 2026](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/insurance-fraud-detection-market)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Founder-led demonstrations, retrospective claim evaluations, the Launch introductory offer and insurance conferences provide documented acquisition paths ([Hesper contact](https://gethesperai.com/contact/), [Badu on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/nitish-badu-529b28165_insurancefraud-claims-insuranceai-activity-7503120272028327936-aFWX), [company LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/hesperai/)).

## Key Risks

- **Technical reliability:** Hesper’s contract acknowledges hallucinations, missed fraud and false fraud indicators, requiring qualified human approval for adverse decisions and referrals ([Hesper MSA](https://gethesperai.com/legal/msa/)).
- **External-data access:** Customers must supply their own database licences, credentials and permissible-purpose determinations, making investigative coverage dependent on customer entitlements ([Hesper MSA](https://gethesperai.com/legal/msa/)).
- **Sensitive-data exposure:** Processing medical records, financial information and recorded statements creates privacy/security obligations; Hesper specifies customer-controlled retention, access controls and restrictions on shared model training ([privacy policy](https://gethesperai.com/privacy/)).
- **Incumbent distribution:** [Inferred]: Shift’s Guidewire strategic partnership can place overlapping decisioning products inside an existing customer channel ([Guidewire announcement, 2024](https://ir.guidewire.com/news-releases/news-release-details/guidewire-deepens-relationship-shift-technology-naming-shift-its)).
