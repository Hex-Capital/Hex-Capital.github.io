## The Idea

- **Problem:** First-time hardware builders must learn CAD, resolve component compatibility, source parts, and write firmware before completing projects ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/confetti)).
- **Approach:** Conversational agents help users edit designs, select components, order printed parts, and remix community projects ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/confetti)).
- **Differentiation:** Confetti advertises consumer project creation through delivery, whereas Adam emphasizes existing engineering software workflows and Flux emphasizes circuit-board design and manufacturing outputs ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/confetti); [Adam](https://adam.new/); [Flux, February 2026](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/02/27/3246498/0/en/flux-the-ai-hardware-engineer-announces-37m-in-new-investment.html)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Printed-part ordering could support transaction fees, but published pricing could not be verified; Website not accessible at time of research, returning only “Opening your workspace…” ([Confetti website, October 27, 2026](https://confetti.build)).
