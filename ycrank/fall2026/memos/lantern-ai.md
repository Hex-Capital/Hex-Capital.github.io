## The Idea

- **Problem:** Enterprise hiring managers lose their hiring criteria across handoffs between sourcing and screening, prompting clients of Lantern’s original interviewer to request upstream candidate discovery instead of relying on job descriptions and keyword searches ([founder’s LinkedIn announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/donovanso_two-more-hk-founders-big-congrats-to-thomas-activity-7493174365577064449-WhTB)).
- **Approach:** A World People Model describes candidates and career histories, while a Company Hiring Model shares hiring criteria, feedback and decisions across sourcing, application review, interviews, meeting notes and analytics ([Lantern](https://lantern.md/)).
- **Differentiation:** Lantern emphasizes company-specific hiring judgment and AI interviews, whereas Gem also supplies a native ATS and Juicebox emphasizes candidate discovery, evaluation and outreach with feedback-adaptive sourcing ([Lantern](https://lantern.md/), [Gem](https://www.gem.com/), [Juicebox](https://juicebox.ai/about)).
- **Business Model:** Employers purchase subscriptions and usage allowances through customer agreements, candidates participate without charge, and the separate Lantern People offering provides a three-day trial followed by paid plans and research-credit consumption; public dollar-denominated subscription prices were not found ([Terms](https://lantern.md/terms-of-service), [Lantern People](https://people.lantern.md/)).
- **TAM/SAM:** The broader recruitment-software market is estimated at $3.62B in 2026, reaching $6.87B by 2033 at 9.6% CAGR, but this is not a company-specific serviceable market estimate ([Coherent Market Insights, April 2026](https://www.coherentmarketinsights.com/industry-reports/recruitment-software-market)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Recruiting buyers enter through demo bookings, while Lantern People distributes through agent connections and a free trial ([Lantern](https://lantern.md/), [Lantern People](https://people.lantern.md/)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** The disclosed customer-specific data asset consists of hiring criteria, interview feedback and past decisions shared across recruiting agents ([Lantern](https://lantern.md/)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Accumulated hiring outcomes could improve customer-specific matching and increase switching costs, but public retention or comparative-performance evidence was not found.
- **Market structure:** [Inferred]: No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** [Inferred]: Gem and Juicebox could extend their existing shared recruiting data and feedback-adaptive agents into overlapping workflows; the difficulty of reproducing Lantern’s matching quality cannot be assessed from public evidence ([Gem](https://www.gem.com/), [Juicebox](https://juicebox.ai/about)).

## Key Risks

- **Assessment validity:** Lantern advertises completely consistent scoring, while its terms disclaim guarantees of accuracy or absence of bias and require human review of final decisions ([Interview page](https://lantern.md/interview), [Terms](https://lantern.md/terms-of-service)).
- **Data handling:** Stored communications and meeting records are not automatically deleted when Google access is disconnected; deletion requests and customer-specific retention controls are available ([Privacy policy, October 2026](https://lantern.md/privacy-policy)).
- **Competitive overlap:** Gem already offers agents sharing ATS and CRM history, overlapping Lantern’s shared-context positioning ([Gem](https://www.gem.com/), [Lantern](https://lantern.md/)).
- **Name collision:** A separate Lantern at asklantern.com sells AI-search marketing software under CEO Gideon Ruhara, creating research-attribution ambiguity ([other company’s About page](https://www.asklantern.com/about)).
