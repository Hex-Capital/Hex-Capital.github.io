# Touchy -- Elad Gil Evaluation

The market map is straightforward: inference costs fell roughly 5–10× annually from April 2024 through November 2025, enabling persistent consumer agents, but Apple controls the customer surface, and Touchy becomes large only if users repeatedly choose its cross-app execution over system-level Siri AI.

That controlling variable is not yet working in Touchy’s favor. The product promises lock-screen voice and camera input, memory, and access to more than 1,000 tools, but the founders acknowledge that current models cannot yet deliver the required filtering, personalization, or airtight action safety. Meanwhile, Apple announced personal context, visual understanding, and cross-app actions at the operating-system layer in June 2026. This is not simply competition. It compresses Touchy’s distribution, permissions, and feature differentiation simultaneously.

The technical discontinuity is real but its economics remain incomplete. Cheaper inference can make a $14.99 consumer subscription feasible, and deploying through existing iPhones avoids Rabbit-style hardware costs. Yet the dossier provides no inference cost per active user, gross margin, conversion rate, or evidence that consumers will pay separately for functionality bundled into their operating system. Falling model costs expand the feasible product surface; they do not establish Touchy’s value capture.

The application does own more workflow than a generic chatbot: it retains preferences, accepts multimodal context, connects to Slack, WhatsApp, Calendar, and Uber, and attempts actions rather than merely formatting answers. Stronger models should improve task completion. But the company has disclosed no proprietary evaluation loop, privileged integration, accumulated state at meaningful scale, or capability Apple could not reproduce. Its App Store listing has too few ratings for an overview, and 11,000 launch-post views with 63 likes show awareness rather than product-market pull.

The strongest bull case is the team. Rohit brings AI-alignment research, Andrew built educational software used by approximately 500,000 students, and Kevin has agent-infrastructure experience plus a 2,500-star repository. This resembles the rare-technical-talent exception behind Character.AI: a strong team can matter before a market crystallizes. The material difference is that Touchy does not appear to own a scarce underlying model or technical asset; it is assembling a consumer application on infrastructure available to Apple and several funded competitors. The pivot from Tokenless infrastructure to Touchy only two months before launch further weakens the claim that the team has found its durable market boundary.

I pass today. The product is credible, but the market currently amplifies the platform owner more than the startup, and neither demand nor technical ownership overrides that structure. The verifiable flip condition is 30% or better 90-day retention among users completing at least three cross-app actions weekly after Siri AI is broadly available; that would demonstrate that Touchy owns a recurring behavior rather than a temporary interface gap.
