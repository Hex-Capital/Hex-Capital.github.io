# Aviern -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Strategic acquisition** is Aviern’s strongest path. A parcel carrier could buy the purpose-built cargo aircraft and AviernOS to control feeder capacity into smaller airports. Ownership could justify a premium if the integrated aircraft and software deliver a durable cost or reliability advantage that the carrier cannot secure through a service contract. Aviern is seeking carrier introductions, and the memo records an adjacent aircraft partnership between Grid Aero and Oshkosh. Buyer willingness to acquire Aviern remains an inference. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Defensibility)

Aviern reports more than 150 autonomous miles on a scaled demonstrator. The 1,000 lb payload is a design target, and about $100 per flight hour is a company cost estimate. The announced retrofit flight in Q1 2027 and purpose-built flight in Q1 2028 provide observable milestones. The memo records YC backing, while the capital and runway needed to reach full-scale feeder operations remain unverified. The draft FAA rule’s 110 lb cap for certificated package delivery makes feeder-route classification material to reaching that milestone. The memo leaves asset title and transfer terms unverified and supplies no separately supported route to shareholder cash. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks, Defensibility, Key Facts)

**Proceeds bridge.** The illustrative $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation buys 5%, diluted to 3%, with no follow-on checks. A 5× gross return requires $2.5 million in cash within eight years, or about $83.3 million in distributable equity proceeds at 3%. The hurdle is $125 million at 2% and $62.5 million at 4%. The sale scenarios assume zero net debt, no other price adjustments, and a simple pro rata waterfall. Enterprise value therefore equals distributable equity consideration, all in cash. Stock, escrow, earnouts, rollover, and compensation are each modeled at $0. The memo supplies no usable sale valuation or buyer economics to anchor either price. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Facts)

| Scenario assumption | Timing | Gross cash to holder at 3% |
|---|---|---:|
| Modest sale at $50 million | Year 6 | $1.5 million, 3× |
| Strong sale at $100 million | Year 8 | $3 million, 6× |
| Growth plateaus, buyers stay away, public scale remains out of reach, and cash is reinvested | Through year 8 | $0, 0×, with shares retained |

Even the modeled strong sale returns only $2 million, or 4×, at 2% ownership.

### Countercase

A carrier could contract for autonomous feeder capacity or use a retrofit platform. Reliable Robotics is pursuing certification on the Caravan and was selected for an FAA pilot program that includes regional cargo operations. That gives carriers a concrete alternative and weakens Aviern’s ownership premium. The memo leaves the substitute’s adequacy and Aviern’s achievable sale price unresolved, so the structural Pass override is untriggered. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can an authorized, full-scale feeder pilot demonstrate enough incremental value from owning Aviern to support roughly $83.3 million in distributable equity proceeds? Carrier-validated route economics, measured against contracted or retrofit capacity, that credibly support that price within eight years would move the action to Reach out.
