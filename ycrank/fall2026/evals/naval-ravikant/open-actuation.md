# Open Actuation -- Naval Ravikant Evaluation

Jon Facer has crossed the useful boundary between knowing about manufacturing and making something customers can test. In July 2026, independent reporting described USA Drone Motors samples undergoing customer feedback; Open Actuation now links that business as its drone offering. That is stronger evidence of specific knowledge than his Gecko Robotics and 1Factory affiliations. It earns substantial founder credit, although samples and unnamed OEM builds establish less than repeated production deliveries ([AgFunderNews](https://agfundernews.com/usa-drone-motors-emerges-from-stealth-to-target-gap-in-domestic-drone-supply-chain)).

Copy Open Actuation’s code and you still need to wind motors, insert magnets, balance assemblies, and deliver consistent performance. Its LA manufacturing equipment supplies a real physical substrate. But equipment can also be purchased by a competitor. The investment question is whether its proprietary tooling makes the hundredth custom design substantially cheaper to deliver than the first. Four-week delivery is an attractive offer; Atlas Motion’s competing promise of prototypes within weeks prevents me from treating speed alone as a demonstrated advantage ([USA Drone Motors](https://usadronemotors.com/), [Atlas Motion](https://atlasmotion.com/)).

The strongest bull case is a factory that turns fragmented engineering demand into repeatable production. Drone OEMs provide the initial buyer; frameless motors and eventually rotary actuators extend the capability into robotics. The independently estimated $18.62 billion robotics-actuator market establishes adjacent spending, although it does not establish how much this team can serve. Reported domestic-sourcing pressure could accelerate entry. This fits my recent preference for physical bottlenecks, but imported magnets and silicon steel constrain how completely the company controls that bottleneck ([Global Market Insights](https://www.gminsights.com/industry-analysis/robotics-actuators-market), [AgFunderNews](https://agfundernews.com/usa-drone-motors-emerges-from-stealth-to-target-gap-in-domestic-drone-supply-chain)).

The distinction that matters is whether customization creates reusable knowledge or recurring engineering labor. Open Actuation could fit my pattern of turning individual expertise into scalable output. It does not yet demonstrate my pattern of participants creating value for one another: downloadable designs, engineering inquiries, and a storefront establish access, without evidence that one customer improves another customer’s experience. Nor does the dossier establish a network that organizes fragmented suppliers. Hardware survives copied code; attractive returns still require production to compound.

There is a direct route to collecting money. Pendulum lists a CubeMars actuator at $187, making the commercial offer more concrete than an unspecified future business model. That price establishes neither completed sales nor custom-motor economics. Imported catalog resale also offers limited exclusivity. I can invest before revenue or network effects when an exceptional prototype and efficient execution justify the exception; here, the samples support technical execution, while production efficiency remains unresolved. The conflicting thousands-per-week and thousands-per-month capacity claims need reconciliation. They are a disclosure concern, not sufficient evidence of dishonesty; the dossier supplies no evidence of unfair dealing, artificial financing pressure, or reassurance-dependent founders.

I am neutral at 56. Open Actuation has credible founder action and a tangible product, with a plausible route into substantial adjacent demand. My money waits on whether this becomes a compounding manufacturing system. One verifiable flip condition would change my decision: production records showing three successive paid custom-motor batches with falling engineering hours per design and positive contribution margins after scrap and rework. That would demonstrate that accumulated knowledge produces economic advantage.
