# Telamont -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Pass  
**Evidence confidence:** Medium

### Company case

Telamont applies AI to construction document review. Its product checks incoming revisions against drawings, specifications, contracts, and applicable codes, then assigns source-linked findings for resolution. The memo identifies no AI workload that depends on a scarce input Telamont supplies, so the building-block path earns zero. (*The Idea*)

The prior workflow relies on contractors reviewing documents individually, which can leave conflicts undiscovered until work starts. Telamont’s design could reduce the labor needed to cross-check revisions, but the memo gives no baseline review cadence or cost and identifies no project type or review frequency that contractors previously found uneconomic. The evidence supports an improvement to an existing workflow. The enabled-industry path also earns zero, triggering Pass. (*The Idea*, *Key Risks*)

The published Autodesk Forma integration and the founders’ construction, software, and industrial AI experience support seed-stage execution credit. Telamont reports monitoring more than $2 billion in project value, and its project examples suggest some contractors are using the product. Payment, repeat use, and buyer commitments remain unverified. The source-linked decision history could make switching harder, though retention is unverified and Trimble already offers overlapping review. The memo gives no price or delivery cost for the next project, so scale economics stays at its default. (*The Idea*, *Founders & Team*, *Market & Traction*, *Defensibility*, *Key Facts*)

### Countercase

The strongest case for outreach is that contractors could afford to check every revision as it arrives for the first time. Telamont reports that it helped avoid $1.1 million in foundation rework, which illustrates the potential value of an early catch. If comparable models become widely available, repository sync, issue ownership, and resolution tracking could still give contractors a reason to buy a dedicated product. The reported catch leaves the decisive question open because it gives no measure of review labor, missed conflicts, or whether the contractor could already afford comparable coverage. The Pass gate still applies. (*The Idea*, *Market & Traction*, *Defensibility*, *Key Risks*)
