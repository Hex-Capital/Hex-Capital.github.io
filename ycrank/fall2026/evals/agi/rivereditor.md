# River -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path B — New Economies.** The scenario to test is whether small consulting teams would pay to produce recurring client reports they previously skipped or prepared infrequently. The founder describes moving context and drafts between chatbots and dozens of files. River’s shared workspace lets its agent read across those files and make edits users can review. If that workflow saves enough preparation and review time at the same quality, it could make a higher reporting cadence economical. The memo describes the workflow but gives no measured comparison of labor or full cost per accepted report, including review, retries, and exceptions. (The Idea, Key Risks)

The desktop launch after the document preview shows the team can ship a broader cross-file product. River’s claim of 100,000+ creators is an early interest signal. Paid repeat use by teams producing newly viable reports remains unverified. Self-serve subscriptions and credit overages give River a plausible way to charge as usage grows. Contribution per recurring team remains unknown because the memo provides no realized revenue or delivery costs. (Market & Traction, The Idea)

### Countercase

River may make existing report work faster while leaving customers’ reporting cadence unchanged. Even if it opens a new use, Genspark already offers native office tools and agents, and Microsoft can execute cross-file work inside the tenant where customers already keep their files and permissions. River’s shared files and workspace memory could give customers a reason to stay, but the memo gives no retention, migration, or paid-choice evidence under functional parity. The overlap makes substitution credible. Whether buyers can get the same outcome and readily switch remains untested, so Path B stays on Watch. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Will independent small consulting teams repeatedly pay River to produce client reports they previously skipped, when a functionally equivalent alternative is available? A field trial should compare the former workflow, River, and that alternative at matched report quality, counting software charges and all human delivery time. Move to **Reach out** if the savings make the new cadence economical and the teams repeatedly choose to pay for River at a price that covers its delivery costs. Move to **Pass** if the trial shows the reports remain uneconomical or buyers readily switch while preserving the outcome.
