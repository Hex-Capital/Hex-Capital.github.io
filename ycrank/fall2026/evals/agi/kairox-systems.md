# Kairox Systems -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Pass  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Tempest has no supported building-blocks path. Kairox describes a vehicle for maritime operations, and the memo identifies no AI workload that depends on it or scarce input it could supply as AI deployment grows. (The Idea)

The strongest commercial case is selling one vehicle to buyers that currently use separate aerial and underwater fleets. Kairox says those fleets require separate operators and logistics. The memo identifies no buyer or operating cadence that the current arrangement makes unaffordable, and it provides no evidence that AI reduces labor per successful operation. The industry-enabled path therefore scores zero. With both paths at zero, the rubric requires Pass. (The Idea, Key Risks)

Buyer qualification could create switching costs after adoption, but the memo identifies no current advantage that protects pricing. Kairox reports Marine Corps engagement and end-user concept feedback. The memo records a third-party revenue classification with no amount or contract detail. The founders’ hardware and autonomy experience and Kairox’s reported lab component validation support partial execution credit. Integrated field performance, buyer commitments, and the costs of delivering the next qualified unit remain unverified. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Founders & Team, Key Risks, Key Facts)

### Countercase

If autonomy cuts supervision enough, buyers might be able to carry out combined air and underwater work at a frequency they could previously afford only occasionally. Buyers would still need a physical vehicle even with access to equivalent AI. The dual-mode design and reported end-user feedback make that scenario worth testing. The memo establishes neither the affordability threshold nor autonomy’s contribution to crossing it, so the scenario leaves the Pass gate in place. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a buyer-backed field trial show that autonomy makes a specific, previously unaffordable operating cadence viable? The trial would need a buyer-defined affordability threshold and a comparison with separate fleets that measures fully loaded cost per successful operation, including supervision, logistics, and failed attempts. Evidence that autonomy supplies the savings needed to cross that threshold would change the action to Reach out.
