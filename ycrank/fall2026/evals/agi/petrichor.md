# Petrichor -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path A — Scarce complements.** If cheap, widely available frontier models lead AI teams to propose more experiments, qualified wet-lab capacity that returns reproducible, training-ready records could become the limiting input. Frontier biology teams would bear the cost through slower research cycles. The memo documents a $300 million commitment to Biohub’s Virtual Biology Initiative, which supports the premise that model developers are allocating money to biological training data. Petrichor says in-house labs take years to build and outsourced results can arrive weeks after benchwork. It says its staff run experiments in parallel and return step-by-step records. Whether that service shortens the full cycle on representative customer work remains unverified. (Market & Traction, The Idea)

Petrichor’s staff-run model gives it a plausible way to control execution and turnaround. The team publicly launched the service and previously released a benchmark for AI on scientific measurements. Repeatable wet-lab delivery remains unverified. Ginkgo’s reported OpenAI cloud-lab use is an indirect signal of demand for outsourced data generation. Petrichor-specific buyer commitments and paid use remain unverified. (The Idea, Founders & Team, Market & Traction, Key Facts)

Under a per-batch pricing assumption, expansion depends on the price per accepted batch covering staff time, consumables, quality review, and reruns, with bench utilization determining the facility cost each batch must cover. Parallel execution could increase throughput if staffing and bench capacity keep pace and reproducibility holds. The memo provides no measured inputs for that bridge. (The Idea, Key Risks, Key Facts)

### Countercase

Emerald already captures structured experimental metadata. If it can deliver equivalent records with comparable turnaround and reproducibility, buyers could switch while retaining the core outcome. Petrichor’s potential switching costs depend on customers standardizing their workflows around its records, and repeat use remains unverified. Incumbent parity is credible and remains untested on Petrichor’s initial work, so the evidence supports Watch. (Defensibility, The Idea)

### Decisive diligence question

Will an independent frontier AI biology team pay for a repeat batch after comparing Petrichor with an established provider on representative work? A paid repeat order after Petrichor delivers materially faster end-to-end turnaround at comparable reproducibility and record completeness, using capacity it can reliably schedule, would move the action to Reach out. Equivalent incumbent delivery on comparable terms would close Path A and move the action to Pass.
