# Streamline Systems -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path A — Scarce complements** is the strongest fit. If cheap frontier intelligence expands autonomous drone fleets, positioning without GPS could become a more valuable input. The bottleneck is concrete for manufacturers whose aircraft lose autonomous operation when GPS is unavailable and may fall back to manual piloting. Streamline reports that its retrofit module demonstrated autonomous flight without satellite positioning. That supports prototype feasibility at seed. The memo records no independent performance validation. (The Idea, Key Risks)

Streamline reports more than $80,000 in contracted module revenue and deposits reserving more than $87,000 worth of modules. The deposit amount and overlap are unknown, so the figures cannot be added. These are meaningful early commitments, concentrated in one procurement program, with shipments and cash collection undisclosed. The founders bring relevant aerospace, software, and hands-on aircraft experience, and the memo records a founding engineer hire. The expansion unit would be a module sold into a qualified aircraft platform. Pricing, manufacturing cost, and integration cost are undisclosed, leaving contribution per deployment unresolved. (Market & Traction, Founders & Team, The Idea, Key Risks)

### Countercase

Manufacturers could qualify an incumbent navigation product and capture the same benefit. The memo identifies ANELLO Photonics and Advanced Navigation as existing suppliers, while Streamline’s potential switching costs depend on buyers qualifying its module and having to repeat that work to replace it. There is no comparative buyer result showing whether alternatives meet the same requirements. The disclosed commitments could reflect a single procurement cycle, and the link from cheaper AI to more demand for this module remains a scenario assumption. Scarcity and durable control remain unproven, with no direct evidence of equivalence that would close Path A. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Will an independent drone manufacturer place a paid production order after testing Streamline and a credible incumbent against the same qualification requirements? An order tied to planned autonomous fleet expansion, with the buyer documenting a material requirement that Streamline meets and the incumbent misses, would move the action to **Reach out**. If the comparison shows the incumbent meets the same requirements and the buyer can readily substitute it, move to **Pass**.
