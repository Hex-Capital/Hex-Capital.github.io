## The Idea

- **Problem:** People seeking assessment before memory symptoms have fewer clinical testing options because existing tests such as Roche’s Elecsys pTau217 are indicated for patients already showing signs, symptoms, or complaints of cognitive decline ([FDA review](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/reviews/K261686.pdf), [Saccade Research](https://saccade.health/membership)).
- **Approach:** Saccade Research records eye movements during five guided VR tasks and uses machine learning to analyze the patterns, and Saccade Acuity combines a headset cognitive exam with a brain-focused blood panel, physician consultation, and follow-up testing ([Launch YC, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Vff-saccade-world-s-earliest-test-for-alzheimer-s-biomarkers), [Saccade product page, Nov 2026](https://saccade.health/membership)).
- **Differentiation:** Saccade’s research design uses a VR headset attachment to capture eye movements, ViewMind Atlas AD uses headset-based visual memory tasks, and Roche Elecsys pTau217 measures phosphorylated tau in plasma ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Vff-saccade-world-s-earliest-test-for-alzheimer-s-biomarkers), [NIH SBIR](https://www.sbir.gov/awards/219642), [FDA review](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/reviews/K261686.pdf)).
- **Business Model:** Acuity lists $897 per test, billed every three months, for the headset exam, blood panel, and doctor consultation, and Saccade Research participation is free ([Saccade product page, Nov 2026](https://saccade.health/membership)).
- **TAM/SAM:** The broader global Alzheimer’s diagnostics market was estimated at $5.22 billion in 2026 with 8.58% projected CAGR through 2031, and no public estimate was found for the specific VR screening or Acuity serviceable segment ([Mordor Intelligence, 2026](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/alzheimers-disease-diagnostics-market)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** The October clinic launch solicited referrals to U.S. longevity practices, and the November site offers direct Acuity appointment inquiries for home or office visits and separate Research enrollment inquiries ([Launch YC, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Vff-saccade-world-s-earliest-test-for-alzheimer-s-biomarkers), [Saccade product page, Nov 2026](https://saccade.health/membership)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Saccade describes its headset attachment as patent-pending and its scoring model as proprietary, and UC Santa Barbara documents a working design with near-infrared cameras and custom circuit boards ([Launch YC, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Vff-saccade-world-s-earliest-test-for-alzheimer-s-biomarkers), [UCSB Engineering Capstone, 2026](https://capstone.engineering.ucsb.edu/index.php/projects/saccade)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Repeat eye-movement measurements linked to subsequent clinical outcomes could improve the model, and the company describes the study as ongoing and the assessment as investigational ([Saccade Research](https://saccade.health/membership), [Saccade terms](https://saccade.health/terms)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** ViewMind already combines VR, eye tracking, and machine learning and has NIH funding to evaluate its system against amyloid and tau burden in people with mild cognitive impairment ([NIH SBIR, 2025](https://www.sbir.gov/awards/219642)).

## Key Risks

- **Presymptomatic validation:** Saccade’s terms say its investigational assessment lacks validation for individual risk prediction, and the company reports an ongoing IRB-approved study with results intended for publication ([Saccade terms, Nov 2026](https://saccade.health/terms), [Saccade Research, Nov 2026](https://saccade.health/membership)).
- **Treatment eligibility:** The FDA label for Leqembi specifies initiation at mild cognitive impairment or mild dementia and requires confirmation of amyloid pathology, limiting the immediate treatment implications of an eye-movement result in an asymptomatic patient ([FDA Leqembi label, 2026](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/761269s011lbl.pdf)).
- **Regulatory pathway:** Saccade describes Research as an investigational Class II device in development and plans to publish study results ahead of an FDA submission ([Saccade, Nov 2026](https://saccade.health/)).
- **Service delivery:** [Inferred]: Acuity’s on-site headset testing and blood draws require local staffing and scheduling to expand into additional markets ([Saccade Acuity, Nov 2026](https://saccade.health/membership)).
- **Name ambiguity:** Saccade Vision’s industrial machine-vision records refer to a separate company with different founders and a different domain ([Startup Nation Central](https://finder.startupnationcentral.org/company_page/saccade-vision), [Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/saccade)).
