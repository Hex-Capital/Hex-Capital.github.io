## The Idea

- **Problem:** Institutional GPU buyers, sellers, lenders, and operators rely on fragmented OEM allocations, brokers, private quotes, stale lists, emails, group chats, and spreadsheets without shared clearing prices ([Stoa website](https://www.stoaexchange.com/), Jul 2026).
- **Approach:** Stoa converts hardware requirements or uploaded quote PDFs into structured RFQs, routes them to verified dealers, records firm quotes and terms, and tracks payment, shipment, inspection, and settlement ([Stoa website](https://www.stoaexchange.com/), Jul 2026).
- **Differentiation:** Compared with off-market brokers and dealer communications, Stoa provides KYB-verified institutional participants, firm RFQ quotes, recorded execution, payment release after confirmed delivery, and an audit trail ([Stoa FAQ](https://www.stoaexchange.com/faq), Jul 2026).
- **Business Model:** Stoa charges a platform fee on completed transactions, with the applicable fee disclosed during registration or by agreement, while eligible data partners receive a 0% platform fee ([Stoa FAQ](https://www.stoaexchange.com/faq), Jul 2026; [Stoa Data Partner Program](https://www.stoaexchange.com/data), Jul 2026).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** Stoa solicits institutional buyers, dealers, data-center operators, neoclouds, OEM channels, lenders, liquidators, and funds through access applications, direct market inquiries, and a data-partner program for organizations contributing active hardware flow ([Stoa marketplace](https://www.stoaexchange.com/marketplace), Jul 2026; [Stoa Data Partner Program](https://www.stoaexchange.com/data), Jul 2026).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** The data-partner program aggregates transaction-derived supply, demand, clearing-price, condition, regional, and counterparty-performance signals while withholding individual submissions ([Stoa Data Partner Program](https://www.stoaexchange.com/data), Jul 2026).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: More completed RFQs could improve pricing coverage and attract additional counterparties, but no transaction volume or longitudinal price dataset has been disclosed.
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** Compute Exchange already offers an institutional market for used, refurbished, and OEM-surplus NVIDIA GPUs with identity-verified counterparties and quotes within 24 hours, while cloud-compute marketplaces can extend into physical hardware sourcing ([Compute Exchange](https://compute.exchange/hardware-market), Jul 2026; [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/07/17/compute-exchange-opens-secondary-market-used-nvidia-h100-a100-gpus/), Jul 2026).

## Key Risks

- **Counterparty and hardware risk:** Stoa performs KYB checks but does not guarantee participants, inspect hardware, take title, or assume responsibility for counterparty disputes, leaving diligence and product-conformity risk with users ([Stoa FAQ](https://www.stoaexchange.com/faq), Jul 2026; [Terms](https://www.stoaexchange.com/terms), Jul 2026).
- **Liquidity and data-bootstrap risk:** Stoa’s pricing product depends on contributed supply, demand, transaction, and counterparty-performance signals, but no participant count, RFQ volume, or completed transaction count is publicly disclosed ([Stoa Data Partner Program](https://www.stoaexchange.com/data), Jul 2026).
- **Direct competition:** Compute Exchange launched a marketplace for used and refurbished H100 and A100 GPUs in July 2026 and already aggregates procurement across more than 100 compute providers ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/07/17/compute-exchange-opens-secondary-market-used-nvidia-h100-a100-gpus/), Jul 2026).
- **Geographic and compliance constraint:** Stoa limits access to U.S.-based institutional participants and requires users to ensure that hardware sales and exports comply with applicable law ([Stoa FAQ](https://www.stoaexchange.com/faq), Jul 2026; [Terms](https://www.stoaexchange.com/terms), Jul 2026).
- **Name ambiguity:** Search results contain unrelated companies and products named Stoa, including real-estate, savings, collaboration-software, and meditation businesses, increasing disambiguation risk in customer discovery and press tracking ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/better-with-stoa), Jul 2026; [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/stoa-4/built-with), Jul 2026).
