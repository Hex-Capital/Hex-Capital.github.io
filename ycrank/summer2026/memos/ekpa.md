## The Idea

- **Problem:** Retail investors with jobs and families lack the time and tools to continuously research their holdings, leaving passive index investing or self-directed research as their current alternatives ([Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ekpa), Aug 2026).
- **Approach:** Ekpa imports read-only positions through SnapTrade or CSV, then combines each position with news, analyst ratings, SEC filings, economic data, portfolio analysis, alerts, discovery, and strategy backtesting ([Ekpa website](https://goekpa.com/), Aug 2026).
- **Differentiation:** PortfolioPilot provides regulated advice across a user’s full net worth, Composer builds and executes automated strategies, and Autopilot mirrors third-party portfolios, while Ekpa currently focuses on research and backtesting tied to connected brokerage holdings without placing trades ([PortfolioPilot](https://www.prweb.com/releases/portfoliopilotcom-secures-2m-to-revolutionize-personal-financial-advising-with-ai-302209563.html); [SoFi](https://investors.sofi.com/news/news-details/2026/Introducing-Composer-by-SoFi-AI-Powered-Investing-From-Idea-to-Execution/default.aspx); [Autopilot App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/autopilot-automated-investing/id1613625799); [Ekpa](https://goekpa.com/)).
- **Business Model:** Ekpa Basic is free during early access, while the website discloses a paid Ekpa Plus/Strategy Lab subscription but does not publish its price ([Ekpa website](https://goekpa.com/), Aug 2026).
- **TAM/SAM:** The global AI-in-asset-management market was estimated at $6.8 billion in 2025 and projected to grow at a 23.4% CAGR through 2032, while no public SAM estimate was found for consumer portfolio-research agents ([Research and Markets](https://www.researchandmarkets.com/report/global-ai-in-asset-management-market), 2026).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Ekpa uses free self-service account creation, founder-led onboarding and introductions, brokerage connectivity, and an in-product referral link ([Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ekpa); [Ekpa website](https://goekpa.com/), Aug 2026).

## Key Risks

- **Regulatory classification:** Paid security-specific analysis may raise investment-adviser classification questions because the SEC definition includes compensated securities advice or reports, while Ekpa markets personalized portfolio suggestions but states it is a research-and-education product, not an adviser ([SEC](https://www.sec.gov/interps/legal/slbim11.htm); [Ekpa](https://goekpa.com/)).
- **Performance validation:** The public performance evidence located is a company-reported return above 1.5% over one week, while Ekpa states that backtests cannot predict future results; source-linked reasoning and non-custodial operation are the disclosed mitigations ([Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ekpa); [Ekpa](https://goekpa.com/)).
- **Brokerage-data dependency:** Connected-account ingestion depends on SnapTrade, so connector availability or policy changes could interrupt synchronization; Ekpa also supports CSV/manual holdings and restricts connections to read-only access ([Ekpa website](https://goekpa.com/), Aug 2026).
- **Broker and platform substitution:** Robinhood Cortex and SoFi’s Composer place overlapping AI analysis or strategy execution inside existing financial platforms, while Ekpa’s disclosed mitigation is aggregation across multiple brokerages ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2025/12/17/ai-robinhood-stock-market); [SoFi](https://investors.sofi.com/news/news-details/2026/Introducing-Composer-by-SoFi-AI-Powered-Investing-From-Idea-to-Execution/default.aspx); [Ekpa](https://goekpa.com/)).
- **Name collision:** “Ekpa” is also used by an unrelated Turkish fashion retailer and as an acronym for Greek institutions, creating search-result ambiguity, with no public mitigation found ([Ekpa Perakende LinkedIn](https://tr.linkedin.com/company/ekpaperakende)).
