# Repovive -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

The strongest path is a strategic acquisition by an AI lab that wants to own a recurring supply of private formal-verification tasks. Repovive says it packages non-public Lean tasks, machine-checked reference proofs, and reinforcement-learning environments. A lab could have a reason to acquire the asset if exclusive access materially improves post-training or lowers the cost of producing fresh tasks compared with licensing or building them. The memo gives no measured buyer benefit. (The Idea, Defensibility)

Repovive reports $100,000 in signed contracts across two formalization environments. That gives the team a starting point for testing whether a paying lab renews or expands after a measurable production benefit. The separate $200,000 letter of intent covers broader coding data. Renewal results, the cost of supplying fresh tasks, and available runway are undisclosed. Assignment terms for the task sets and any contributor rights also remain unverified. The memo supports no independent liquidity route. (Market & Traction, Defensibility)

Under the benchmark, a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation starts at 5% ownership and dilutes to 3% with no follow-ons. The holder needs $2.5 million in gross cash within eight years, requiring about $83.3 million in distributable equity at 3% pro rata. The sale scenarios below assume a close within eight years, $5 million in combined net debt and deal deductions, an eligible holder selling the full stake for cash, and a pro rata waterfall with no further preference or escrow deductions. The memo provides no price evidence for either sale scenario. (Market & Traction, Key Facts)

| Scenario | Enterprise value to distributable equity | Gross cash to holder |
|----------|-------------------------------------------|----------------------|
| Modest sale | $30M less $5M = $25M | $750K, or 1.5× |
| Strong sale | $100M less $5M = $95M | $2.85M, or 5.7× |
| Prolonged private | Continued operation with cash reinvested and no eligible share sale or payout | $0 realized |

Even in the strong scenario, selling only half the stake on the same terms would yield about $1.43 million. The bridge shows what the acquisition would need to deliver. The reported contracts establish some demand for the product, while a realizable acquisition price remains unevidenced.

### Countercase

Labs may keep licensing data or build verification infrastructure in-house. The memo identifies LeanDojo’s open training environment, Reinforce Labs’ checked proof data, and DeepMind’s internal formal-verification work. These alternatives weaken the case for paying a premium to own Repovive, especially while the benefit of its private tasks remains unmeasured. The evidence leaves open whether those substitutes match Repovive’s private environments and gives no supported sale price or holder restriction that triggers the Pass gate. (Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a paying lab show that owning Repovive’s private task pipeline would create enough incremental value over licensing or building it to support a sale above the holder’s proceeds hurdle? A paid production expansion with measured gains over the lab’s best alternative, backed by buyer-side economics consistent with at least $83.3 million in distributable equity under the benchmark, would move the action to Reach out. A renewal on its own would leave it at Watch.
