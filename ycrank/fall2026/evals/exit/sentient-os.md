# Sentient OS -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Strategic acquisition is the strongest path. A desktop productivity vendor could acquire Sentient to add overnight context processing and cross-app workflows on 8 GB Macs. The memo documents a customized local inference integration, an AGPL-licensed repository, and a commercial licensing option. The reason to buy would be control of the integration and its roadmap if proven enterprise deployments let a buyer ship materially faster or at lower cost than building or licensing. That ownership advantage remains an inference. (*The Idea*, *Defensibility*, *Key Facts*)

Sentient reports 10,000+ users at its October launch and usage at more than 30 enterprises. Contract status, paid conversion, and retention are undisclosed. The roughly $1 million pre-seed and reported usage give the two-person team a plausible starting point for a paid, privacy-approved deployment that measures sustained workflow savings on 8 GB Macs. Burn, remaining runway, and the cost of reaching that milestone are unknown. A company sale is a plausible way to convey the commercial licensing business, subject to verifying the rights Sentient controls and any required consents. The disclosed primary financing provides no evidence of demand for this holder’s shares, and the memo supplies no independently supported cash route. (*Market & Traction*, *The Idea*, *Key Risks*, *Key Facts*)

The proceeds bridge uses the illustrative $500,000 check at $10 million post-money, with 5% initial ownership diluted by 40% to 3% and no follow-on checks. The eight-year 5× target requires $2.5 million in gross holder cash, or $83.3 million in distributable equity proceeds at 3% ownership. The hurdle is $125 million at 2% and $62.5 million at 4%.

| Illustrative outcome | Timing | Gross holder cash |
|---|---|---:|
| Modest sale at $40 million enterprise value and $40 million distributable equity value | Year 5 | $1.2 million, 2.4× |
| Strong sale at $100 million enterprise value and $100 million distributable equity value | Year 7 | $3.0 million, 6.0× at 3% ownership, or $2.0 million, 4.0× at 2% |
| Prolonged private ownership with growth plateauing, buyers staying away, public scale out of reach, and cash reinvested | Through year 8 | $0, 0×, with all shares retained |

The sale scenarios assume zero net debt and other price adjustments, all cash paid at closing, and a simple pro rata waterfall with no preference deductions. Stock, escrow, earnouts, rollover, and compensation are each assumed to be zero. The prices and timing are scenario assumptions. The memo gives no usable transaction price or buyer economics to support either sale value. (*Market & Traction*, *Key Facts*)

### Countercase

A buyer may be able to build overlapping features using public local inference tooling or obtain sufficient capability under a commercial license. Apple already documents overlapping personal context, screen awareness, and app actions. Portable Markdown limits the value of owning accumulated context, while Full Disk Access and potential cloud transmission create approval hurdles for enterprise deployments. If building or licensing delivers comparable results, the price a buyer can justify could fall below the holder’s hurdle. The memo establishes credible substitutes, but their adequacy and effect on price remain unresolved. That warrants Watch. The modeled zero-cash outcome alone does not trigger the Pass override. (*Defensibility*, *The Idea*, *Key Risks*)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a paid, privacy-approved deployment demonstrate enough sustained workflow savings on 8 GB Macs for a strategic buyer to justify an acquisition price that clears the holder’s proceeds hurdle? Measured production results and a buyer’s quantified comparison of acquiring, building, and licensing would answer it. Evidence supporting at least $83.3 million in distributable equity value at 3% holder ownership, or the applicable hurdle under updated terms, would move the action to Reach out.
