## The Idea

- **Problem:** U.S. importers use brokers, consultants charging $250–500 per hour, and per-entry or per-user ABI software to navigate classification, tariff stacking, valuation, origin, and filing errors that Tarifflo says affect one in eight entries ([Tarifflo](https://www.tarifflo.com/), Aug 2026; [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/tarifflo-inc), Aug 2026).
- **Approach:** Tarifflo ingests entry packets, HTS codes, and ACE reports, checks each line against HTS, Chapter 99, PGA, and CBP rules, drafts corrections or protests, and routes filings through an in-house or partner licensed broker ([Tarifflo Audit](https://www.tarifflo.com/platform/audit), Aug 2026).
- **Differentiation:** Tarifflo combines free direct-CBP filing and contingency-based recovery, versus Zonos’s cross-border landed-cost and classification platform, KlearNow’s multi-country document-to-clearance system, and iCustoms’s AI customs-declaration software ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/tarifflo-inc), [Zonos](https://zonos.com/about-us), [KlearNow](https://klearnow.ai/), [iCustoms](https://www.icustoms.ai/blogs/raises-2-2-mil-to-revolutionise-trade-compliance/), Aug 2026).
- **Business Model:** Filing software is offered for $0, while audit engagements usually use contingency pricing with no upfront charge and unspecified program-specific terms ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/tarifflo-inc), [Tarifflo Audit](https://www.tarifflo.com/platform/audit), Aug 2026).
- **TAM/SAM:** Global customs-brokerage services were $23.78B in 2022 with a projected 7.7% CAGR through 2030, while the U.S. segment is projected at $5.48B in 2026 with a 2.88% CAGR through 2031 ([Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/pipeline/customs-brokerage-service-procurement-intelligence-report), 2023; [Mordor Intelligence](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/united-states-customs-brokerage-market), 2026).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Tarifflo offers free sample audits and works through importers, freight brokers, and customs-brokerage partners that can retain the filing relationship ([Tarifflo](https://www.tarifflo.com/), Aug 2026; [KSL](https://www.ksl.com/article/51283643), Mar 2025).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Tarifflo reports an ABI filer code and direct CBP connection, more than 1M products audited, and supplier intelligence derived from more than 10M linked shipment records ([Tarifflo](https://www.tarifflo.com/), [ABI Filing](https://www.tarifflo.com/platform/abi), [Suppliers](https://www.tarifflo.com/platform/suppliers), Aug 2026).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Reviewed classifications, correction outcomes, and linked entry histories could create a company-specific compliance dataset, but public sources do not establish its ownership, exclusivity, or measured learning effects.
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage; Zonos, KlearNow, iCustoms, and Altana already operate overlapping classification, filing, compliance, or supply-chain-data systems ([Zonos](https://zonos.com/about-us), [KlearNow](https://klearnow.ai/), [iCustoms](https://www.icustoms.ai/blogs/raises-2-2-mil-to-revolutionise-trade-compliance/), [Altana](https://altana.ai/resources/series-c-valuation)).
- **Commoditization risk:** Customs-data, AI-classification, and filing workflows are already offered by funded vendors including Zonos, KlearNow, iCustoms, and Altana, although direct ABI integration and licensed-broker review add regulatory and implementation work ([competitor sources](https://zonos.com/about-us), Aug 2026).

## Key Risks

- **Classification liability:** [Inferred]: Incorrect classifications or false-positive recovery findings could expose customers to rejected corrections or compliance errors; Tarifflo says every finding is linked to a governing rule and reviewed by licensed brokers ([Tarifflo Audit](https://www.tarifflo.com/platform/audit), Aug 2026).
- **CBP infrastructure dependency:** Direct filing depends on CBP’s ABI and Tarifflo’s filer-code access, while the ABI product remained in private beta in August 2026 ([Tarifflo ABI](https://www.tarifflo.com/platform/abi), Aug 2026).
- **Contingency timing:** Tarifflo is paid only after customer refunds, while Post-Summary Corrections generally have a 300-day entry window and protests follow liquidation deadlines ([Tarifflo Audit](https://www.tarifflo.com/platform/audit), Aug 2026).
- **Competitive overlap:** Zonos has been selected by CBP, KlearNow reports five direct customs connections, and Altana serves CBP and enterprise customers, creating overlap in regulatory data and government integrations ([Zonos](https://zonos.com/about-us); [KlearNow](https://klearnow.ai/); [Altana](https://altana.ai/resources/series-c-valuation)).
- **Policy-maintenance burden:** U.S. tariff authority and rates changed repeatedly during 2025–2026, including the IEEPA decision and replacement Section 122 and Section 232 measures, requiring continuing updates to codes and stacking rules ([U.S. Supreme Court](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/25pdf/24-1287_4gcj.pdf); [White House](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/04/strengthening-actions-taken-to-adjust-imports-of-aluminum-steel-and-copper-into-the-united-states/)).
