## The Idea

- **Problem:** Blinka says founders who incorporate in the U.S. and operate abroad face account rejections from U.S. banks and can lose 4–5% to FX when using approved accounts to pay overseas teams and vendors ([Launch YC, Nov 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Wkv-blinka-the-banking-platform-designed-for-global-companies)).
- **Approach:** Blinka opens USD business accounts remotely through Portage Bank, uses Mural Pay for stablecoin settlement on cross-border payouts, and offers local payment routes alongside cards, treasury, invoicing, and QuickBooks-compatible accounting ([Blinka website](https://blinka.co/), [Blinka Partner Disclosures](https://docs.blinka.co/legal/partner-disclosures)).
- **Differentiation:** Blinka emphasizes remote U.S. account opening and Latin American local payouts, while Mercury requires existing or planned U.S. operations, Slash offers eligible foreign entities a stablecoin-based Global USD Account, and Jeeves bundles local-currency credit with enterprise spend management ([Blinka website](https://blinka.co/), [Mercury eligibility](https://support.mercury.com/hc/en-us/articles/28770467511060-Eligibility-and-requirements-for-opening-a-Mercury-account), [Slash Global Account Terms](https://www.slash.com/legal/global-account-terms), [CoinFund, Sep 2026](https://www.coinfund.io/insights/jeeves-building-the-stablecoin-native-banking-platform-for-global-enterprises)).
- **Business Model:** Blinka discloses service fees before charging and makes its fee schedule available in-app, while public card terms disclose 0% FX and cross-border fees, late fees up to $40, and returned-payment fees up to $29 ([Blinka Terms of Service](https://docs.blinka.co/legal/terms-of-service), [Blinka Card Disclosures, Nov 2026](https://docs.blinka.co/legal/cards/overview)).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment. FXC Intelligence estimates broader global B2B cross-border payment volume at $31.6 trillion in 2024, growing at a forecast 5.9% CAGR through 2032 ([FXC Intelligence, 2025](https://www.fxcintel.com/research/press-releases/new-data-cross-border-payments-market-now-worth-over-194tn-and-is-forecast-to-reach-320tn-by-2032)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Blinka routes website leads to an application or team call, solicits referrals from accountants, lawyers, and incorporation services, and offers a visa-fee benefit through LegalOS ([Blinka website](https://blinka.co/), [Launch YC, Nov 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Wkv-blinka-the-banking-platform-designed-for-global-companies), [Blinka LinkedIn, 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/company/blinka-tech)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** No defensibility signals found in public sources.
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Stored vendor records, invoice collection, and accounting integrations could raise switching costs as customers move more workflows onto Blinka, with no public retention or multi-product adoption figures available ([Blinka website](https://blinka.co/)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** Slash and Jeeves already combine cards and stablecoin payments with business finance software, and Blinka uses third-party infrastructure for deposits, card issuance, and stablecoin settlement ([Slash, Apr 2026](https://www.slash.com/blog/series-c-fundraise-release), [Jeeves, Sep 2026](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/jeeves-raises-110-million-equity-round-to-scale-its-stablecoin-native-banking-platform-for-global-enterprises-across-35-countries-302892811.html), [Blinka Partner Disclosures](https://docs.blinka.co/legal/partner-disclosures)).

## Key Risks

- **Provider concentration:** Deposit accounts depend on Portage Bank, cards on Third National, and stablecoin settlement on Mural Pay, making each service dependent on its provider’s continued participation ([Blinka website](https://blinka.co/), [Blinka Partner Disclosures](https://docs.blinka.co/legal/partner-disclosures)).
- **Payout disruptions:** Stablecoin routes face network delays and settlement risks, and Blinka also offers ACH, wires, and local bank routes where available ([Blinka website](https://blinka.co/)).
- **Treasury exposure:** Blinka separates partner-bank deposits from Atomic-provided investment products, which carry principal-loss risk ([Blinka treasury disclosures](https://blinka.co/)).
- **Name ambiguity:** Adafruit uses Blinka for its CircuitPython compatibility library, which can surface unrelated GitHub and search results ([Adafruit GitHub](https://github.com/adafruit/Adafruit_Blinka)).
