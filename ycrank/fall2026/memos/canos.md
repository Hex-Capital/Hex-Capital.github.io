## The Idea

- **Problem:** Veterinary cancer monitoring relies on clinical assessment and complementary diagnostic tests, while Canos seeks to measure molecular changes between visits for dogs undergoing treatment ([Canos OncoTrack, accessed Oct 2026](https://www.canos.ai/relapse-monitoring)).
- **Approach:** Canos sequences plasma cell-free DNA and combines fragmentation, copy-number variation and native methylation signals, including 5mC and 5hmC, for cancer detection and longitudinal comparison ([YC, accessed Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/canos)).
- **Differentiation:** Origin combines cancer-associated signals with inherited genetics and a banked baseline, versus Oncotect’s urine-based nematode assay and ImpriMed’s live-cell drug-response testing ([Canos Origin](https://www.canos.ai/origin), [Oncotect](https://oncotect.co/blogs/resources/science-behind-oncotect), [ImpriMed](https://www.imprimedicine.com/veterinary)).
- **Business Model:** Origin costs $699 once, including the veterinary visit, while accepted OncoTrack trial participants receive free testing ([Canos homepage](https://www.canos.ai/), [OncoTrack](https://www.canos.ai/relapse-monitoring), accessed Oct 2026).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment; adjacent U.S. veterinary oncology diagnostics were estimated at $985.24 million in 2024, with 8.48% forecast CAGR during 2025–2030, covering more than canine liquid biopsy ([TechSci Research, 2025](https://www.techsciresearch.com/report/united-states-veterinary-oncology-diagnostics-market/28391.html)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Origin offers San Francisco group draws, Bay Area mobile veterinarians and collection kits through owners’ veterinarians nationwide, with samples processed in San Carlos; the website now describes Canos as San Francisco-based ([Canos Origin, accessed Oct 2026](https://www.canos.ai/origin)).

## Key Risks

- **Clinical validation:** [Inferred]: Research-dataset cross-validation does not establish prospective screening performance for the marketed assay; Canos labels Guardian as in development and OncoTrack as early access ([Science](https://www.canos.ai/science), [homepage](https://www.canos.ai/)).
- **Clinical actionability:** [Inferred]: Cancer signals may require additional investigations without identifying an immediately actionable treatment; Canos states a single result cannot confirm or exclude cancer and reserves decisions for the treating veterinarian ([Canos Terms, Sep 2026](https://www.canos.ai/terms)).
- **Sample-handling dependence:** [Inferred]: Distributed collection introduces pre-analytical variability that could affect molecular measurements; Canos acknowledges biological, pre-analytical and technical influences and provides veterinary collection kits ([Terms](https://www.canos.ai/terms), [For Vets](https://www.canos.ai/for-vets)).
