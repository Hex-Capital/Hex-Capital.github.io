## The Idea

- **Problem:** U.S. employers buying coverage through brokers, PEOs, and incumbent carriers face annual family premiums averaging $26,993 in 2025, while employees encounter deductibles, denied claims, and administrative work ([KFF, 2025](https://www.kff.org/series/employer-health-benefits-survey/); [YC launch, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Rjs-prescience-superintelligence-for-the-american-health-system)).
- **Approach:** Time Machine uses reinforcement learning trained on medical histories to search possible care trajectories, while Crystal handles navigation, appointments, questions, and bills and the platform automates plan filings and administration ([YC launch, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Rjs-prescience-superintelligence-for-the-american-health-system)).
- **Differentiation:** Prescience combines medical-history-based care routing and savings-linked fees, while Angle Health combines carrier, TPA, and underwriting functions, Sana offers level-funded plans with embedded primary care, and Collective Health provides TPA and navigation services for self-funded employers ([Prescience launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Rjs-prescience-superintelligence-for-the-american-health-system); [Angle Health](https://www.anglehealth.com/about-us); [Sana](https://www.sanabenefits.com/); [Collective Health](https://collectivehealth.com/faqs/)).
- **Business Model:** The company says it charges no administrative fees and is paid only when it saves the employer money, but no fee percentage or contract minimum is public ([YC launch, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Rjs-prescience-superintelligence-for-the-american-health-system); [company website, Aug 2026](https://www.getprescience.com/)).
- **TAM/SAM:** U.S. private-health-insurance spending was $1.6446 trillion in 2024, up 8.8% year over year, while no public SAM estimate was found for Prescience’s employer segment ([CMS, 2026](https://www.cms.gov/data-research/statistics-trends-and-reports/national-health-expenditure-data/nhe-fact-sheet)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** The launch solicits introductions to founders, CFOs, chief people and medical officers, benefits managers, HRIS platforms, and brokers, alongside quote requests and a YC-company offer ([YC launch, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Rjs-prescience-superintelligence-for-the-american-health-system)).

- **Moat today:** Prescience holds California Administrator License #6019366 and reports integrations or partnerships spanning banking, HSAs, compliance, provider networks, risk, and plan administration ([company website, Aug 2026](https://www.getprescience.com/); [YC launch, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Rjs-prescience-superintelligence-for-the-american-health-system)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: De-identified claims, care-navigation, pricing, and outcome data could improve its prediction models over time, but public customer-scale and model-performance evidence is unavailable ([privacy policy, Jul 2026](https://www.getprescience.com/privacy)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.

## Key Risks

- **Clinical-model validation:** Time Machine is described as predicting and routing patient trajectories, but no public peer-reviewed validation, sensitivity data, clinical outcomes, or customer-level savings study was found; Crystal’s use of human physicians for escalation provides a stated control ([YC launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Rjs-prescience-superintelligence-for-the-american-health-system); [company website](https://www.getprescience.com/)).
- **Regulatory scaling:** [Inferred]: National operation requires state-specific and partner compliance beyond the publicly disclosed California administrator license, and the company states that its services remain subject to federal and state regulations ([company website](https://www.getprescience.com/)).
- **PHI and security exposure:** Prescience processes health records, claims, prescriptions, financial information, and connected Gmail documents, while its privacy policy says controls are SOC 2-aligned but no Type I or Type II examination has been completed ([privacy policy, Jul 2026](https://www.getprescience.com/privacy)).
- **Competitive substitution:** Angle Health and Collective Health already sell overlapping AI-enabled benefits, navigation, and administration functions to employers ([Angle Health](https://www.anglehealth.com/about-us); [Collective Health](https://collectivehealth.com/)).
- **Name disambiguation:** Unrelated companies use the Prescience name in construction, intelligence recruiting, supply-chain software, and data services, which complicates search-result attribution ([LinkedIn search results](https://www.linkedin.com/company/prescienceinc)).
