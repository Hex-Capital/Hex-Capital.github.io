# Ascenix -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Medium

### Company case

**Path B — Repeatable sales or partner distribution with account expansion.** Ascenix sells to senior care operators whose staff repeatedly reconstruct fall incidents from EHR notes and spreadsheets. The first sale could replace that workflow, then spread to more homes under the same operator. Responsive Health Management deployed Ascenix across all its homes and reported 80% less time spent on manual fall analysis and 15% fewer non-compliance incidents over three months. That is a concrete signal of value in a recurring workflow. The rollout sequence, renewal, and incremental revenue from additional homes are unknown. With monthly platform invoicing, broader usage would grow revenue only if contract terms monetize additional sites or workflows. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Facts)

Ascenix reports 55 live facilities across Canada and the US, including facilities in Ontario, Massachusetts, Ohio, and Florida. That supports geographic reach for the initial workflow. The number of independent paying operators and their routes to activation are unknown. Ascenix solicits introductions and sells through demos. The growth scenario assumes those demos can repeatedly convert new operators and that existing EHR connectors and workflow templates can carry into a new market with bounded changes to local rules and data handling. A measured rollout would do more to resolve those assumptions than a conversation today. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Countercase

Each new operator could require a fresh sales effort and substantial implementation work. Ascenix must connect systems, operators must validate workflows against local policies, and scanned charts require a digital workaround. PointClickCare also controls access to live EHR records and offers native incident management. Reusable components could reduce setup effort, but the memo gives no measured reuse rates or costs for sales, setup, support, or compute. The Pass gate requires evidence that those costs scale proportionally or worse without a supported path to improvement. That remains unresolved. (Defensibility, Key Risks, Market & Traction)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a cohort of independent US senior care operators activate and keep paying for the falls workflow through the same sales and onboarding process? Move to **Reach out** if rollout records show recurring paid use, reuse of the connector and workflow template, and positive contribution after measured sales, setup, local validation, support, and compute costs. Move to **Pass** if the rollouts show that each operator adds proportional or worse cost with no supported path to reduce it.
