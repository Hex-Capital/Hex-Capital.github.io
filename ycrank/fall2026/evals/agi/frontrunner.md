# Frontrunner -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path B — New economies** is the strongest path. Frontrunner’s shared graph lets agents reuse CRM, communications, and product data across account research, lead routing, and outbound. The seed-stage hypothesis is that this cuts the work of configuring and running campaigns enough for lean teams to pursue account segments that previously cost too much to cover. The memo describes the workflow but gives no like-for-like labor comparison or example of a buyer entering a previously uneconomic segment. (The Idea)

Foresight Health’s CEO reported seven meetings in 48 hours after onboarding. That customer account supports early use. Frontrunner’s reported win-rate and conversion improvements leave payment, repeat use, and the prior cost of delivering those outcomes open. The founders’ experience leading growth and building internal GTM systems supports their ability to build and iterate on the product. (Market & Traction, Founders & Team)

The default in-service license for customer-specific playbooks and configurations gives Frontrunner a potential switching advantage. Whether buyers would still choose it under functional parity is untested. Organization-specific fees establish a billing model, while the memo gives no realized prices or marginal costs for onboarding, inference, and ongoing support. Scale economics take the absent-evidence default. (Defensibility, The Idea, Key Facts)

### Countercase

Frontrunner may improve throughput for existing sales teams while leaving the economics of smaller campaigns unchanged. Its simulations surfaced stale data and early stops, so review and recovery could absorb the labor savings. Unify already uses general-purpose models for prospect research and outreach, and Zoom bundles overlapping buyer intelligence and agents. Frontrunner also relies on third-party CRM and outreach APIs, leaving no separately supported scarce-complement case. The memo establishes neither equivalent-quality labor savings for newly served buyers nor preference under parity. It also leaves both conditions open to a seed-stage test, which supports Watch. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a paid pilot show that lean teams repeatedly run previously uneconomic campaigns with Frontrunner and still choose it at a viable price when offered a functionally comparable workflow using equally capable models? Compare human time per qualified meeting at the same quality bar, including setup, review, retries, and exceptions. Repeat paid use and buyer preference under those conditions would support rescoring for Reach out if the threshold and confidence gates are met. A credible trial showing that the labor savings disappear or that buyers readily switch at parity would change the action to Pass.
