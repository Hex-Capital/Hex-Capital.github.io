# Archangel Health -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path B — repeatable sales with account expansion.** The first buyer is a medical AI developer that needs to test whether its agent can complete clinical workflows, such as actually placing an order in an EHR. Archangel describes a sandbox with de-identified records and physician grading. The growth hypothesis is that a developer licenses an initial nephrology evaluation, reruns it as models change, and pays to add workflows or specialties. That could create recurring revenue from the same account if Archangel can reuse the environment and its underlying data rights. (The Idea; Defensibility)

The memo documents an Opendatabay listing. Archangel reports use by frontier labs and medical AI companies, with buyer identities, payment, and repeat use undisclosed. The listing establishes a route for discovery. Paid activation through that route, buyer commitment, and account expansion remain unverified. At this stage, one buyer-funded rollout could materially strengthen the case. (The Idea; Market & Traction; Key Facts)

**Second-market scenario.** An independent developer outside the U.S. buys the same evaluation. This assumes the license permits reuse and the existing records, EHR workflows, and grading criteria are relevant to that buyer. The next account adds sales, onboarding, support, and compute. A rollout that needs new records, EHR integration, or physician-authored tasks also adds licensing, de-identification, and specialist labor. The memo gives no rollout cost or pricing evidence to show how much contribution Archangel would retain. (The Idea; Defensibility; Key Risks; Key Facts)

### Countercase

A public benchmark already covers multi-step EHR workflows. Buyers could use it for routine testing and pay Archangel only for custom evaluations. Negotiated data licenses and custom physician labeling could then make each sale depend on fresh rights work and specialist time. The memo establishes those offerings and constraints, while the per-account effort and scope of reuse remain unknown. The structural Pass gate remains untriggered. (The Idea; Defensibility; Key Risks)
