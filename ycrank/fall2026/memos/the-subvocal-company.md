## The Idea

- **Problem:** Frequent voice-dictation users fall back to typing or whisper microphones in open offices, cafés, and transit where speaking prompts, emails, or messages aloud can distract others ([Launch YC, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Wf9-the-subvocal-company-talk-without-talking)).
- **Approach:** The glasses send low-power radio waves through the face and use a model to decode signal changes from silently mouthed words into text on a computer ([Subvocal](https://subvocal.company/), [Launch YC, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Wf9-the-subvocal-company-talk-without-talking)).
- **Differentiation:** Subvocal uses contactless RF sensing in glasses, AlterEgo senses neuromuscular signals with an ear-worn device, and Wispr Flow transcribes spoken audio through a microphone ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Wf9-the-subvocal-company-talk-without-talking), [MIT Media Lab](https://www.media.mit.edu/projects/alterego/press-kit/), [Wispr Flow](https://wisprflow.ai/post/series-b)).
- **Business Model:** The U.S. hardware preorder requires a refundable $199 deposit toward a $499 early-bird price, and Yadav outlined capped free dictation and a planned unlimited tier at about $20 per month ([Subvocal preorder page, via search snippet](https://subvocal.company/preorder), [Digg, Nov 2026](https://digg.com/ai/13qdcuyf), [Hacker News, Nov 2026](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50361712)).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** The company opened direct web reservations on October 30, 2026, using a founder-led X launch and a YC demo to reach existing dictation users ([Subvocal launch update, Nov 2026](https://subvocal.company/launch-update), [Launch YC, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Wf9-the-subvocal-company-talk-without-talking)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** The founders built custom sensing hardware and collected RF training data by paying participants to wear the glasses and mouth prompts, reporting lower errors as the dataset grew ([Show HN, Nov 2026](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50340088)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: A larger labeled RF dataset could shorten calibration for new wearers, although performance across users, sessions, and devices remains an active development objective ([Show HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50340088), [YC Head of ML posting](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/the-subvocal-company/jobs/RzUITdx-head-of-ml)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** [Inferred]: Apple could offer overlapping input through its own devices after acquiring Q.ai, which filed a patent application for detecting mouthed or spoken words from facial micromovements ([Reuters, Jan 2026](https://ca.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/apple-acquires-audio-ai-startup-qai-4427149)).

## Key Risks

- **Recognition across wearers:** Kumar reported 85–92% accuracy in the launch showcase, and the company is designing tests across users, sessions, and devices while working on calibration for new wearers ([Kumar on LinkedIn, Nov 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/arnav-kumar-tsc_the-subvocal-companys-launch-reached-122-activity-7525210538226712577-vaOB), [YC Head of ML posting](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/the-subvocal-company/jobs/RzUITdx-head-of-ml)).
- **Manufacturing and fulfillment:** Changes in sensor placement, enclosure, or power design can change the signal seen by the model, and the company is recruiting hardware leadership for production while targeting first shipments in Q1 2027 ([YC Head of Hardware posting](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/the-subvocal-company/jobs/XEN2HGf-head-of-hardware), [Show HN, Nov 2026](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50340088)).
- **Cloud dependence:** Cloud decoding creates privacy and connectivity concerns for dictation, and Yadav says a local model is planned after the glasses ship ([Show HN discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50340088), [Yadav on Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50361712)).
- **Name overlap:** An independently operated iPhone translation product also uses the SubVocal name, creating potential confusion in app searches ([SubVocal iPhone product](https://subvocal.app/business/), [The Subvocal Company](https://subvocal.company/)).
