# Collar -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Collar’s strongest path is a strategic sale to FactSet. Collar’s live prompts draw on issuer-approved materials and could extend Irwin’s investor-targeting workflow into the meeting itself. Collar says a representative use case cut earnings preparation from three weeks to about one day. That suggests a reason to buy if paying issuers consistently see similar savings. The memo documents no buyer interest. (The Idea; Market & Traction)

FactSet’s $120.2 million acquisition of Irwin shows that an adjacent IR asset can command a price above the benchmark. Collar’s realizable price remains unverified. Using the rubric assumptions of a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, 5% initial ownership diluted to 3%, and no follow-ons, the holder needs $2.5 million in cash within eight years. That requires about $83.3 million in distributable equity at 3% pro rata. (Market & Traction)

| Scenario | Assumed bridge to holder cash within eight years |
|---|---|
| Modest sale | $40M enterprise value less $5M in net debt and deal adjustments leaves $35M in distributable equity. The holder receives $1.05M, or 2.1×. |
| Strong sale | $100M enterprise value less $10M in net debt and deal adjustments leaves $90M in distributable equity. The holder receives $2.70M, or 5.4×. At 2.5% ownership, the same sale returns $2.25M, or 4.5×. |
| Prolonged private | Continued operation with cash reinvested and no eligible share purchase or distribution yields $0 in realized proceeds. |

Both sale scenarios assume the holder sells its entire stake, participates pro rata with no preference reducing its share, and receives cash at closing within eight years. Actual investor terms, contract assignability, and customer-data permissions remain unverified. The memo supplies no evidence of an independent route to holder liquidity.

The operating milestone is a cohort of compliance-cleared, paying issuers that renew after using Collar across multiple earnings cycles and document preparation savings. Collar reports $1.4 million in deals pending compliance, and the memo records $500,000 in seed funding. Those signals support testing conversion and repeat use. Conversion, runway, and the cost of serving the cohort remain unknown. (Market & Traction; Key Risks)

### Countercase

FactSet could build the workflow into Irwin. Q4’s AI-native CRM and Notified’s expansion into earnings preparation show that incumbents already ship adjacent capabilities. Collar’s compliance-pending pipeline and representative savings example leave open whether live prompts are differentiated enough to buy. That supports Watch while the acquisition economics remain unresolved. The memo establishes neither a structural closing block nor a credible ceiling below the holder’s proceeds hurdle. (Defensibility; Market & Traction; Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Would FactSet’s economics favor buying Collar over building the live workflow into Irwin at a price that leaves at least $83.3 million in distributable equity? Buyer-side feedback supporting that price range, grounded in measured savings and renewals from compliance-cleared paying issuers, would move the action to Reach out. A supported price ceiling below the hurdle across credible strategic buyers would move it to Pass.
