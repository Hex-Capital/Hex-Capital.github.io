## The Idea

- **Problem:** Satellite fleet operators use ground teams to send commands during scheduled contact windows, which can delay responses to faults when spacecraft are out of contact ([Orca Aerospace](https://orcaaerospace.com/)).
- **Approach:** An onboard language model proposes the next action, a deterministic planner translates it into commands, and a safety layer checks each command against vehicle and mission limits before execution ([Orca Aerospace](https://orcaaerospace.com/), [Payload, Nov 2026](https://payloadspace.com/exclusive-orbital-taps-orca-aerospace-for-onboard-autonomy-software/)).
- **Differentiation:** Orca proposes and checks commands onboard, Cognitive Space optimizes fleet schedules and ground-station contacts, and In Orbit Aerospace pairs onboard decisions with a ground application that recommends actions to operators ([Orca Aerospace](https://orcaaerospace.com/), [Cognitive Space](https://www.cognitivespace.com/cntient-optimize/), [In Orbit Aerospace](https://inorbitaerospace.com/)).
- **Business Model:** The Orbital letter of intent specifies an integration fee and a license fee per satellite running Orca’s software, with fee amounts undisclosed ([Payload, Nov 2026](https://payloadspace.com/exclusive-orbital-taps-orca-aerospace-for-onboard-autonomy-software/)).
- **TAM/SAM:** Stratistics MRC’s illustrative estimate for global autonomous spacecraft operations software is $1.3B in 2026, growing at 18.9% annually through 2034, with no public SAM estimate found for onboard AI spacecraft operators ([Stratistics MRC, 2026](https://www.strategymrc.com/report/autonomous-spacecraft-operations-software-market)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Orca solicits briefings directly from spacecraft operators and plans to reach Orbital’s fleet by integrating with its bus supplier Reflex Aerospace’s flight software ([Orca Aerospace](https://orcaaerospace.com/), [Payload, Nov 2026](https://payloadspace.com/exclusive-orbital-taps-orca-aerospace-for-onboard-autonomy-software/)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** The Orbital plan calls for integration once per flight-software stack and reuse across the fleet, with the Reflex integration still planned ([Payload, Nov 2026](https://payloadspace.com/exclusive-orbital-taps-orca-aerospace-for-onboard-autonomy-software/)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Flight validation and reusable integrations could raise switching costs if customers would need to repeat qualification with a replacement, and the planned Orbital deployment has yet to establish that effect ([Payload, Nov 2026](https://payloadspace.com/exclusive-orbital-taps-orca-aerospace-for-onboard-autonomy-software/)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** Shield AI and Sedaro have demonstrated onboard AI with an independent command-checking layer on a NOVI satellite, and AIKO has demonstrated onboard mission replanning on D-Orbit hardware ([Shield AI, Aug 2026](https://shield.ai/shield-ai-and-sedaro-demonstrate-trusted-autonomy-capabilities-on-novi-satellite/), [ESA InCubed](https://incubed.esa.int/portfolio/orbital_oliver/)).

## Key Risks

- **Flight validation:** Orca plans to review telemetry with humans before expanding command authority, with a possible Pathfinder test in July 2027 and Orbital-1 use planned for 2028 ([Payload, Nov 2026](https://payloadspace.com/exclusive-orbital-taps-orca-aerospace-for-onboard-autonomy-software/)).
- **Avionics integration:** Model memory and power draw can constrain which spacecraft can host the software, and Orca lists hardware-in-the-loop testing on flight-representative equipment as part of its integration work ([Lyra study on Orca’s LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/orcaaerospace), [Y Combinator Jobs](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/orca-aerospace/jobs/eMihWDs-founding-flight-software-engineer)).
- **Customer rollout:** The Orbital LOI’s per-satellite license opportunity depends on Orbital deploying its planned fleet ([Payload, Nov 2026](https://payloadspace.com/exclusive-orbital-taps-orca-aerospace-for-onboard-autonomy-software/)).
- **Name confusion:** A Hungarian company using the same name develops electric vertical-takeoff aircraft for medical transport under a separate domain ([Orca eVTOL](https://www.orca-evtol.com/), [YC company profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/orca-aerospace)).
