## The Idea

- **Problem:** P&C claims and special investigation teams manually chase records, verify providers, and reconcile evidence, with outsourced investigations costing $1,500–$2,500 per claim according to Hesper ([Launch YC, Nov 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WpY-hesper-ai-ai-agents-that-work-every-insurance-claim-end-to-end)).
- **Approach:** Hesper’s agents cross-check documents and coverage, call providers, query external databases, and compile a case file that links each flag to its underlying evidence for adjuster review ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WpY-hesper-ai-ai-agents-that-work-every-insurance-claim-end-to-end)).
- **Differentiation:** Hesper emphasizes gathering missing evidence and documenting findings, Shift emphasizes cross-insurer fraud networks, FRISS offers SIU referral and case management inside Guidewire, and Five Sigma offers a core claims system with embedded AI ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WpY-hesper-ai-ai-agents-that-work-every-insurance-claim-end-to-end), [Shift](https://www.shift-technology.com/claims-fraud-detection-solution), [FRISS](https://www.friss.com/press/updated-friss-accelerator-moves-flagged-claims-into-investigation-in-one-step), [Five Sigma](https://fivesigma.ai/claims-management-platform/)).
- **Business Model:** Hesper quotes usage-based pricing by insurance line, claim volume, and agents deployed, with 200 free claims and no platform fee for 90 days for eligible new MGAs and TPAs ([Hesper pricing FAQ](https://gethesperai.com/#faq), [Hesper Launch](https://gethesperai.com/launch/)).
- **TAM/SAM:** Next Move Strategy Consulting’s 2026 report estimates the broader global P&C claims processing segment at $19.72B in 2025 and $44.24B in 2035, implying about 8.4% annual growth ([Next Move Strategy Consulting, 2026](https://www.nextmsc.com/report/claims-processing-software-market-bf5967)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** The founders run demos and solicit introductions to claims and SIU leaders, and eligible MGAs and TPAs can apply directly for the launch program ([Hesper Contact](https://gethesperai.com/contact/), [Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WpY-hesper-ai-ai-agents-that-work-every-insurance-claim-end-to-end), [Hesper Launch](https://gethesperai.com/launch/)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Hesper lists API integrations with Guidewire, Duck Creek, and Majesco that let it take in claim files and return cited findings to the customer’s existing system ([Hesper Integrations](https://gethesperai.com/integrations/)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Carrier-specific policy rules and approval workflows could raise switching costs after deployment, although public retention data are unavailable ([Hesper Integrations](https://gethesperai.com/integrations/), [Hesper Launch](https://gethesperai.com/launch/)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** Duck Creek, a claims system Hesper integrates with, launched native infrastructure for deploying and governing insurance agents, giving existing Duck Creek customers another way to automate claims work ([Duck Creek, Apr 2026](https://www.duckcreek.com/resource/press-releases/launch-agentic-ai-platform-underwriting-claims/), [Hesper Integrations](https://gethesperai.com/integrations/)).

## Key Risks

- **Investigation accuracy:** [Inferred]: Incorrect coverage readings or fraud flags can delay payment or send legitimate claims to investigation, with source-linked findings and adjuster sign-off providing a review step ([Hesper on claims AI accuracy](https://gethesperai.com/blog/claims-ai-accuracy-over-time/), [Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WpY-hesper-ai-ai-agents-that-work-every-insurance-claim-end-to-end)).
- **Regulatory oversight:** Insurers face scrutiny of third-party AI and its inputs, and Hesper’s data-processing terms provide for audit rights and notice of subprocessor changes ([NAIC](https://content.naic.org/insurance-topics/artificial-intelligence), [Hesper DPA](https://gethesperai.com/legal/dpa/)).
- **Incumbent substitution:** [Inferred]: Buyers can adopt investigation workflows through their existing claims platform, as illustrated by FRISS’s direct Guidewire referral and case-status integration ([FRISS, Nov 2026](https://www.friss.com/press/updated-friss-accelerator-moves-flagged-claims-into-investigation-in-one-step)).
- **Name confusion:** An unrelated Hesper voice-journal app is published by Peralabs, and the insurance company’s official domain is gethesperai.com ([Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hesper-voice-journal-memory/id6828091015), [YC company profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/hesper-ai)).
