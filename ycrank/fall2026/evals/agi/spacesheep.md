# Spacesheep -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path B — New economies** is the strongest path for this seed-stage company. Spacesheep says coding agents can publish working dashboards and mockups to hosted links where teammates can comment on specific elements and request revisions. Its launch showed a lab dashboard and a live thermal-camera stream. Those examples illustrate what can be published. The memo provides no comparison of end-to-end review labor or evidence that teams started reviewing work they previously skipped. (The Idea, Market & Traction)

**Seed-stage scenario.** A team might review disposable internal mockups that previously went unreviewed because packaging and sharing each one took too long. The relevant comparison is labor per completed review at equivalent quality, including setup, retries, reviewer time, and permission exceptions. If frontier-quality models are cheap and widely available, a shared review history across coding agents could still give teams a reason to pay. Repeat use and preference under functional parity remain untested. The memo identifies no bottleneck Spacesheep controls that would support Path A. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Risks)

Observed extension adoption was 28 Chrome users and three Firefox users, an early demand signal with repeat use and payment undisclosed. Makarov’s prior work building the first Claude Code Artifacts and Spacesheep’s public launch support the team score, with delivery at repeat team usage still untested. The listed Team price of $10 per member per month gives a revenue basis for a scale test. Contribution would depend on realized seat revenue after inference, hosting, and support, and the memo supplies no measured inputs for that bridge. (Market & Traction, Founders & Team, Key Facts)

### Countercase

Spacesheep may make existing reviews more convenient without making skipped reviews economical. Claude Artifacts and ChatGPT Sites already handle parts of publishing inside the tools teams use to generate the work. Type.com offers a shared workspace across Claude and Codex, and Spacesheep’s export options reduce switching costs. This overlap creates a credible substitution risk, but the memo records no like-for-like buyer test that defeats either Path B condition. Both conditions remain unproven, which supports Watch. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

In a paid comparison with a functionally equivalent alternative using comparably capable models, do independent teams repeatedly complete reviews they previously skipped, at lower end-to-end labor and equivalent quality, and renew at the regular Team price because they value Spacesheep’s shared cross-agent review history? A positive result would establish both Path B conditions and support Reach out if the updated score reaches 65 with at least medium confidence. If the trial shows no newly viable reviews or buyers readily switch under parity, move to Pass.
