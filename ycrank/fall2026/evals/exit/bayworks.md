# Bayworks -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Medium

### Company case

The strongest route is a strategic acquisition by a dealership service software provider. Bayworks’ product description shows how technicians dictate against an assigned repair order, attach evidence, and review a manufacturer-formatted narrative before writeback. A buyer could bundle that workflow into its service platform to save technician time and improve warranty documentation. Snap-on’s $200 million acquisition of Dealer-FX, net of acquired cash, shows that a strategic buyer has paid for dealership service software. Dealer-FX spans a broader workflow, and Bayworks’ buyer economics remain unmeasured. (*The Idea*, *Market & Traction*)

Bayworks reports operations across six dealership groups and more than 80 locations. The memo also records a named customer endorsement and $125,000 in listed seed funding. A proposed 12-month milestone is to verify retained, paying deployments across multiple groups and measure technician time saved per repair order and the rate of warranty narrative rework. The reported footprint gives Bayworks a base for that test. The capital required and available runway remain unclear. (*Market & Traction*, *Key Risks*)

The benchmark assumes a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, 5% starting ownership diluted to 3%, and zero follow-ons. A 5× gross cash return within eight years requires $2.5 million to the holder, or about $83.3 million in distributable equity at 3%. Bayworks reports nearly seven figures in contracted annual recurring revenue after four months. Revenue from live, paying deployments remains undisclosed, so the revenue and multiples below are scenario assumptions. Both sale cases assume a full cash acquisition by year eight, holder eligibility for the full stake, and pro rata distribution after debt and deal adjustments, with zero additional preference leakage or deferred consideration. (*Market & Traction*, *Key Risks*)

| Scenario | Enterprise value to distributable equity | Gross cash to holder |
|-----------|------------------------------------------|----------------------|
| Modest sale | $8M in recurring revenue × 5 = $40M enterprise value, less $10M in assumed debt and deal adjustments = $30M | $900K (1.8×) |
| Strong sale | $15M in recurring revenue × 7 = $105M enterprise value, less $10M in assumed debt and deal adjustments = $95M | $2.85M (5.7×) |
| Prolonged private | Operating cash is reinvested, with zero eligible secondary sales or distributions during the eight-year horizon | $0 (0×) |

With $10 million in deductions, the sale needs about $93.3 million in enterprise value. At $15 million in recurring revenue, a 6× price yields $2.4 million to the holder, below the objective. The strong case therefore depends on both converting early contracts into durable paid revenue and earning a strategic premium.

### Countercase

Wrench Voice already offers technician dictation and dealership-system writeback, giving a buyer a plausible substitute. Bayworks also depends on continued access to dealership systems. Dealerships own the repair data and can request deletion, so an acquirer would need to verify which integration and data permissions carry over. The memo leaves both a closing barrier and an inadequate payout under the strongest route unestablished. It supplies no evidence of an independent liquidity route. Watch fits the unresolved question of what a buyer would pay for the workflow. (*Defensibility*, *Market & Traction*, *Key Risks*)

### Decisive diligence question

Can Bayworks’ results in retained, paying dealer deployments justify an acquisition price of roughly $93 million or more for a dealership service platform? Paid renewal records and measured time savings and rework rates across dealer groups, paired with a prospective buyer’s estimate of the economic benefit of owning the workflow, would test that price. Evidence supporting the hurdle would move the action to Reach out. Evidence that a buyer could capture comparable value through an available substitute and would pay materially below the hurdle would move it to Pass.
