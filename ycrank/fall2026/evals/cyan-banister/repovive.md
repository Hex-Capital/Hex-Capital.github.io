# Repovive -- Cyan Banister Evaluation

By 2032, I want an engineer to ask a machine to prove that a chip behaves as intended—and receive something independently checkable before fabrication. Repovive is working on one ingredient that world needs: environments where models learn formal reasoning. Its reported 8.3-percentage-point improvement on Leanstral 1.5 is a small, concrete leak from that future. But the benchmark remains unpublished, and proving the wrong specification perfectly would still leave us with a broken chip. The remaining transitions are substantial: reliable formalization, specifications that capture intent, and transfer into production engineering. Repovive has evidence of the first step, with little evidence yet about the distance between them.

What attracts me is the possibility that a programming community becomes a factory for difficult, machine-checkable training experiences. Repovive reports access to 12,000 IMO, IOI and ICPC medalists. That is an unusually relevant pool of human ingenuity beneath the visible AI wave. It fits my preference for concealed infrastructure and businesses discovered through community artifacts. However, access to talented people becomes an enabling asset only if the company can consistently turn their work into differentiated environments. Contributor exclusivity, ownership of the resulting material, and reproduction costs remain undocumented. I see a promising supply channel; durable control over the bottleneck is still unproven.

The founders have more substance than an accelerator badge. Shayan’s reported Lean-verified dataset work and NeurIPS/ICLR publications connect directly to the task, while Amir’s Google and YouTube post-training experience supplies a complementary skill. Those are actual pieces of the required machine. They earn meaningful founder credit without establishing the deeper obsession I look for: dated experiments before the company, repeated independent builds, and evidence that these two can recruit people into a difficult, unfashionable journey. Academic and employer credentials help explain capability. They do not finish that story.

The commercial evidence deserves respect at this stage. As of November 2026, Repovive reports $100,000 in contracts across two formalization RL environments, plus a separate $200,000 coding-data LOI. Contracts represent more customer commitment than a waitlist, although neither recognized revenue nor payment is established. The earlier extension’s 879 installs and 11 ratings show that the team has put a tool into people’s hands; they do not establish repeated use of today’s verification product. I can invest before revenue when a working artifact and unusually strong founder evidence carry the uncertainty. Here, the contracts make that exception worth considering, but the public product evidence does not yet carry it far enough.

The strongest objection is that Repovive could become a capable bespoke data supplier whose customers eventually reproduce its work internally. Open-source Lean makes participation possible for everyone, including competitors; Harmonic already markets software and hardware verification. The company’s community could support a valuable business without producing a scarce asset. There is also little evidence of the particular system repair that excites me: no quantified gatekeeper rent, artificial restriction, or transfer of occupational control. Scarce training material is a technical constraint, but the dossier does not establish an institutional toll Repovive removes. I find no documented leadership deception, severe privacy abuse, or dependence on one closed model supplier; those red flags do not explain my hesitation. The uncertainty is whether Repovive owns something increasingly necessary.

I would keep my personal checkbook closed today, with real interest in the next artifact. Repovive has credible technical founders, early contractual demand, and a plausible place beneath a much larger future. My verifiable flip condition is a named lab renewing a formalization-environment contract after independently reproducing a gain on a held-out benchmark that tests specification correctness. That would connect the interesting research to repeat customer dependence—the missing link in this particular bet.
