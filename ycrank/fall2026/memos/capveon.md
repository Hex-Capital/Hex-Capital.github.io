## The Idea

- **Problem:** Infrastructure contractors and manufacturers manually reconcile lead feeds, estimating systems, CRMs, ERPs, specifications, and email, leaving staff to research projects and transfer records between disconnected tools ([Capveon, accessed Oct 2026](https://capveon.ai)).
- **Approach:** Capveon connects existing systems, resolves differently named customers and projects into shared records, extracts document clauses, and proposes qualification, pricing, forecasting, and record updates requiring human approval ([Capveon, Oct 2026](https://capveon.ai/how-it-works)).
- **Differentiation:** Capveon emphasizes executing work across customers’ internal systems, while Citylitics emphasizes public infrastructure sales intelligence and Document Crunch emphasizes construction document compliance ([Capveon](https://capveon.ai/partners), [Citylitics](https://citylitics.com/), [Document Crunch](https://www.documentcrunch.com/news/series-b)).
- **Business Model:** The privacy policy references subscriptions, and the capabilities statement offers contracted integration and automation work, but public prices, billing units, and contract values were not found ([Capveon privacy](https://capveon.ai/privacy), [capabilities](https://capveon.ai/capabilities)).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** Capveon describes using published infrastructure research for inbound and outbound prospecting, founder-led workflow sessions, and expansion across customer workflows and private-equity portfolios ([Capveon investor page](https://capveon.ai/investors)).
