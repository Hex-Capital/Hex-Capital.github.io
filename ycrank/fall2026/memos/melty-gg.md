## The Idea

- **Problem:** [Inferred]: PC gamers seeking cross-game experiences face repeated mod installation and configuration work, with manual setup the alternative Melty’s automated launcher addresses ([Melty](https://melty.gg/)).
- **Approach:** The Windows app downloads mashups, installs dependencies, launches owned games, supplies multiplayer invitation links, and lets creators build and publish through external coding agents ([Melty](https://melty.gg/)).
- **Differentiation:** Melty emphasizes cross-game mashups and agent-assisted publishing, whereas Nexus Vortex emphasizes mod installation and collections, and mod.io supplies studio-integrated mod infrastructure ([Melty](https://melty.gg/), [Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/vortex), [OIF Ventures](https://www.oifventures.com.au/insights/our-investment-in-mod-io)).
- **Business Model:** Melty and all current mashups are free; its terms contemplate future paid mashups, tips, and creator payouts without announcing prices ([Melty Terms, October 18, 2026](https://melty.gg/terms)).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** Distribution uses a free Windows download, creator publishing, shareable multiplayer links, public lobbies, and an officially linked Discord community ([Melty](https://melty.gg/)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Creator terms require exclusive marketplace distribution while content remains listed and for twelve months after removal, providing contractual catalogue exclusivity rather than evidence of a measured network effect ([Melty Terms, October 18, 2026](https://melty.gg/terms)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Accumulated compatibility and failure reports could improve installation reliability, but their scale and resulting performance advantage are undisclosed ([Melty Privacy Policy](https://melty.gg/privacy)).
- **Market structure:** [Inferred]: No structural barrier identified at this stage, given existing mod-management and studio-integration infrastructure at Nexus Mods and Overwolf ([Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/vortex), [Overwolf](https://www.overwolf.com/about-overwolf/)).
- **Commoditization risk:** [Inferred]: Nexus Mods and Overwolf could extend existing distribution tools toward mashups, although public evidence does not establish the effort required ([Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/vortex), [Overwolf](https://www.overwolf.com/about-overwolf/)).

## Key Risks

- **Publisher permissions:** Game ownership does not supersede publisher or anti-cheat restrictions; Melty acknowledges that some games prohibit mods and requires legitimate copies ([Melty Terms](https://melty.gg/terms)).
- **Technical compatibility:** Melty disclaims universal PC compatibility and multiplayer connectivity; its release checks require successful execution on the creator’s PC ([Melty Terms](https://melty.gg/terms)).
- **Privacy and product-history scrutiny:** Dexerto reported unverified allegations concerning inherited LookLoot recording components, while a directory archive lists LookLoot and Deployed Telemetry as prior names; exact pivot timing: No public data found ([Dexerto, October 14, 2026](https://www.dexerto.com/gaming/melty-game-launcher-lets-you-play-ai-game-mashups-in-one-click-but-theres-a-catch-3419011/), [MentionFox](https://mentionfox.com/funding/yc-melty-gg)). The current policy describes selected launcher-window recordings while excluding games and hiding typed input ([Melty Privacy Policy, October 12, 2026](https://melty.gg/privacy)).
- **Creator participation:** [Inferred]: Exclusive distribution obligations could discourage creators who publish across marketplaces, despite retaining ownership ([Melty Terms](https://melty.gg/terms)).
- **Name confusion:** The unrelated melty.sh coding editor shares the Melty name, creating attribution ambiguity for searches, downloads, and historical metrics ([AI Merge Games](https://aimergegames.app/melty/)).
