# Telamont -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path B — New Economies.** General contractors can miss conflicts when they review drawings, specifications, and contracts individually. Telamont checks new files and revisions against the project record and assigns source-linked findings for resolution. The scenario to test is whether this makes cross-document review economical at every revision on projects where contractors previously reviewed selectively because of labor cost. Telamont reports $1.1M in avoided foundation rework. That example illustrates the value of catching a conflict early, while the cost and accuracy of reviewing a representative revision stream remain unknown. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

A project is the unit of expansion. Compare manual review labor per verified and resolved conflict with Telamont’s per-conflict cost, including setup, inference, retries, false-positive triage, manual uploads, and human approval for notices. Compare coverage and missed material conflicts at the same time. The memo describes paid offerings and displays contractor logos. Telamont reports monitoring over $2B in project value. These are early demand signals, with payment, repeat use, and contribution per project still unverified. The listed Autodesk Forma integration and the founders’ construction, software, and industrial AI experience support seed-stage execution credit. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Founders & Team, Key Risks)

### Countercase

Selective manual review may remain cheaper if incomplete file access, missed conflicts, and human follow-up limit the labor savings. Under model parity, Telamont’s source-linked record of owners, deadlines, and decisions could give contractors a reason to stay across projects. That switching cost remains an inference, and Trimble’s Document Crunch and Buildcheck already offer overlapping review capabilities. The memo leaves both market opening and buyer preference under parity unproven. A representative field test can address both, which supports Watch. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Will an independent general contractor that previously skipped routine cross-document checks because of labor cost pay to deploy Telamont on a second project after a representative trial shows that continuous review is economical and a rival with equivalent detection quality is available? A trial that verifies coverage and full cost per resolved conflict, followed by a repeat purchase at a price that covers delivery costs and a buyer-confirmed preference for Telamont’s resolution workflow, would move the action to Reach out. Representative evidence that review remains uneconomic or that the buyer readily switches while preserving the same outcome would close the path and move the action to Pass.
