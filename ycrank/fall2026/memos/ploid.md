## The Idea

- **Problem:** Developers building sales and recruiting products must reconcile stale contact exports and scattered web sources, which adds manual cleanup and can leave customers with outdated results ([Ploid homepage](https://ploid.com/), [Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Wak-ploid-people-and-company-data-for-ai-agents)).
- **Approach:** Ploid combines an index of 2B+ people with cross-site identity matching and live retrieval, exposing natural-language search, profile research, and contact enrichment through a REST API, CLI, and Model Context Protocol (MCP) server ([Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ploid), [Ploid Search API](https://ploid.com/documentation/api/search)).
- **Differentiation:** Ploid centers on profiles that link professional and social identities, while Apollo bundles contact search with sales execution, Clay orchestrates enrichment across providers, Exa offers broader web search, and Crustdata emphasizes change monitoring across people and companies ([Ploid](https://ploid.com/), [Apollo](https://docs.apollo.io/reference/apollo-api), [Clay](https://www.clay.com/series-c), [Exa](https://exa.ai/blog/announcing-series-c), [Crustdata](https://www.crustdata.com/blog/crustdata-closes-6m-seed-round)).
- **Business Model:** Growth costs $200 per workspace per month for 1,000 credits with $0.20 overages, Scale costs $600 for 4,000 credits with $0.15 overages, and Custom has negotiated terms ([Ploid Pricing, Nov 2026](https://ploid.com/pricing)).
- **TAM/SAM:** Grand View Research estimates the broader global sales intelligence market at $4.4B in 2026 with 10.1% CAGR through 2033, and no public TAM/SAM data was found for the narrower people-data API segment ([Grand View Research, 2026](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/sales-intelligence-market)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Ploid offers self-serve signup and developer documentation, takes demo bookings, and used Launch YC to request introductions to data and product leads at sales and recruiting platforms ([Ploid Pricing](https://ploid.com/pricing), [Ploid Documentation](https://ploid.com/documentation), [Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Wak-ploid-people-and-company-data-for-ai-agents)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Ploid reports a proprietary index of 2B+ people and an identity-matching system linking professional profiles, social accounts, and personal sites ([Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ploid), [Ploid homepage](https://ploid.com/)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Accumulated corrections to identity matches could improve retrieval over time, but Ploid’s public documentation does not establish a customer-feedback loop or measure its effect ([Ploid Person API](https://ploid.com/documentation/api/person)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage ([Apollo Anywhere](https://www.apollo.io/anywhere), [Crustdata API](https://www.crustdata.com/)).
- **Commoditization risk:** Apollo already offers people search through MCP, and Crustdata already sells live people data via API, so buyers can source overlapping functions through existing integrations ([Apollo MCP](https://docs.apollo.io/docs/apollo-mcp), [Crustdata API](https://www.crustdata.com/)).

## Key Risks

- **Privacy and deletion:** Ploid may use a public notice in place of individual notice where permitted and accepts deletion requests by email, while California’s DROP imposes separate deletion duties on covered data brokers ([Ploid Privacy](https://ploid.com/privacy), [Ploid Data Rights](https://ploid.com/data-rights), [CalPrivacy](https://privacy.ca.gov/drop-for-data-brokers/)).
- **Upstream data quality:** Ploid uses vendors including FullEnrich for contact information and says vendor-supplied addresses can be stale or misclassified, giving customers a reason to validate them before use ([Ploid Privacy](https://ploid.com/privacy)).
- **Identity resolution:** Ambiguous matches can associate data with the wrong person, so Ploid’s research API documents returning an unresolved result when the available evidence cannot distinguish candidates ([Ploid Person API](https://ploid.com/documentation/api/person)).
- **Brand ambiguity:** A Barcelona bioinformatics company uses the Ploid AI name at `ploid.ai`, making the domain and founder names necessary to attribute third-party listings ([Ploid AI legal notice](https://ploid.ai/legal-notice), [Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ploid)).
