## The Idea

- **Problem:** Households coordinate groceries and meals through memory, receipts, cupboards, and separate apps, leaving someone responsible for tracking consumption and avoiding duplicate purchases ([YC, via search snippet](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/trepo)).
- **Approach:** Receipt/fridge photos initialize inventory, while voice and camera interactions with a kitchen device update food records and support recipes, shared shopping lists, and nutrition estimates ([Trepo](https://hellotrepo.com/)).
- **Differentiation:** Trepo adds dedicated kitchen hardware to functions offered through Cooklist’s consumer app and retailer software, while its external device works with existing refrigerators rather than requiring Samsung’s integrated appliance ([Trepo](https://hellotrepo.com/), [Cooklist CEO interview, Aug 2026](https://www.retailgentic.com/p/700-stores-10-million-shoppers-3m), [Samsung, Jan 2025](https://news.samsung.com/global/samsung-joins-forces-with-instacart-to-enhance-kitchen-experiences-through-hallmark-innovation)).
- **Business Model:** The app is free, hardware is offered through preorder batches, and terms contemplate optional subscriptions without publishing prices ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/trepo/id6764135986), [Trepo](https://hellotrepo.com/), [Terms](https://hellotrepo.com/terms)).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment; the broader global smart-kitchen market is estimated at $24.22 billion in 2026, growing at 11.09% CAGR through 2031 ([Mordor Intelligence](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/smart-kitchen-market)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Distribution includes the App Store, founder TikTok content, a hardware waitlist, and grocery-partner pilots reported by Founders, Inc. ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/trepo/id6764135986), [Snowball](https://www.linkedin.com/company/trysnowballai), [Founders, Inc.](https://f.inc/portfolio/trepo/)).

## Key Risks

- **Inventory maintenance:** [Inferred]: Missed consumption or disposal updates could corrupt replenishment decisions because inventory requires ongoing household input, partly mitigated by receipt scanning and voice updates ([Trepo](https://hellotrepo.com/)).
- **Recognition and nutrition accuracy:** [Inferred]: Incorrect food identification or portion estimates could produce unsuitable guidance, while Trepo’s terms require users to verify AI outputs and caution against substituting them for professional advice ([Terms](https://hellotrepo.com/terms)).
- **Incumbent substitution:** Samsung announced overlapping inventory and replenishment functions, including updates to compatible existing appliances ([Samsung, Jan 2025](https://news.samsung.com/global/samsung-joins-forces-with-instacart-to-enhance-kitchen-experiences-through-hallmark-innovation)).
- **Household-data privacy:** [Inferred]: Processing kitchen images and voice inputs creates privacy exposure; the policy permits third-party audio processing and states that inadvertently captured facial images are deleted ([Privacy Policy](https://hellotrepo.com/privacy)).
- **Name ambiguity:** [Inferred]: An unrelated Android alarm app also named Trepo can confuse discovery and metric attribution, so its downloads and reviews must be excluded ([AppBrain, developer rimoworld](https://www.appbrain.com/app/trepo/tech.infosout.trepo)).
