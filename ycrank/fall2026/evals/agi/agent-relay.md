# Agent Relay -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path B — New Economies.** Engineers manually copy questions, decisions, and handoff summaries between coding-agent sessions and Slack. Agent Relay automates that routing across tools and teammates’ machines. If frontier-quality models become cheap and teams run more agents in parallel, the opportunity is to make frequent cross-tool handoffs economical for teams that previously skipped them or limited how many agents they ran. (The Idea)

Pomerado reported coordinating 80+ agents and improving its product at 2–4× its previous speed. The testimonial supports the feasibility of coordination at that scale. The memo gives no before-and-after cost per successful handoff, including engineer time, review, retries, exceptions, and security overhead. It also leaves open whether Pomerado could already afford the workflow. Separately, an unnamed prospect reportedly exchanged 7,000+ messages in a week and expressed willingness to pay. That shows active use and stated purchase intent, with paid repeat use still unverified. (Market & Traction)

Searchable decisions and handoff history could give buyers a reason to stay when rivals have equivalent models, if teams come to rely on that context and moving it disrupts their work. The memo offers no retention or migration evidence. The founders’ integrations and engineering experience, followed by the release of the cross-provider SDK and team messaging, support seed-stage execution. A team workspace is a plausible expansion unit given self-serve installation and paid collaboration features. Realized revenue per workspace and hosting, search, and support costs remain unknown. (Defensibility, Founders & Team, The Idea, Key Risks)

### Countercase

Agent Relay may speed up a workflow that engineering teams already find economical. Claude Code already handles messaging within its own agent teams, and the open A2A standard could make equivalent cross-tool routing easier for platforms to offer. The memo supplies neither a representative cross-tool substitution test nor evidence that review and exceptions erase the labor savings. Both Path B gates remain open, and the memo identifies no independently supported scarce complement for Path A. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Will a team that previously skipped frequent cross-tool handoffs pay to renew after a field trial shows that Agent Relay makes those handoffs economical and the team still prefers it to a credible substitute using equivalent models? Measure full cost per accepted handoff against the former manual workflow, then document the paid renewal and the buyer’s reason for choosing Agent Relay after trying the substitute. A positive result would move the action to **Reach out**. If the trial shows the workflow remains uneconomic or the buyer switches to the substitute without losing the outcome, move to **Pass**.
