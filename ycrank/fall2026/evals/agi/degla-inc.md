# Degla Inc -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path B — New Economies.** Search-and-rescue teams currently build flight plans and coordinate aircraft through operator workflows. Degla says its onboard model takes a spoken objective and replans locally during flight. The memo records a live flight demonstration at REACH’s Chula Vista test site and describes founder Aziz Hanafi’s prior work on autonomous-system coordination. These support seed-stage execution. The demo leaves operator time, miss rates, and repeatability unmeasured. (The Idea; Market & Traction; Founders & Team; Key Risks)

The market-opening scenario assumes that planning and coordination labor keeps an agency from flying some searches today. If Degla can reduce that labor while maintaining search reliability, those missions could become economical. Compare operator minutes and full cost per successful search across both workflows, counting supervision, failed attempts, retries, exceptions, aircraft support, and authorization. Pilot solicitations leave buyer commitment unverified, and the memo gives no pricing, utilization, or support costs for an agency rollout. (The Idea; Key Facts; Key Risks)

### Countercase

Degla could make existing drone missions easier to run while leaving the same missions uneconomic once supervision, retries, and authorization are counted. Under model parity, a rival could use commercially available Jetson hardware and comparable onboard intelligence. Degla reports training on expert-pilot decisions, and aircraft integration and flight validation could give it an advantage. The memo leaves data exclusivity, control of service execution, and buyer preference under parity unproven. Both required conditions remain open to a field test, which supports Watch. ### Decisive diligence question

Will an independent search-and-rescue agency place a paid repeat order for missions it previously left unflown because of staffing cost? A representative trial would move the action to Reach out if it shows comparable search reliability, lower operator time and full cost per successful mission, and a viable repeat price the agency chooses to pay for Degla’s flight execution after comparing an alternative with equivalent onboard intelligence. If the trial shows the missions remain uneconomic or the agency can switch and retain the full outcome, close Path B and move to Pass.
