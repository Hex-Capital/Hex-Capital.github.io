# Clean -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path B — repeatable sales with account expansion.** The clearest initial buyer is a sales team selling into manufacturing that needs to reach decision-makers before suppliers are selected. Clean describes a demo-led sale and a setup that uses the seller’s website and documents to surface buying windows from permits, shipments, and filings. If that setup can be reused across sellers in the same vertical, Clean could win accounts through targeted outbound and expand them as customers pay to monitor more accounts. That scenario assumes customers act on fresh signals repeatedly and will pay for broader coverage. The memo gives no billing unit or evidence of paid expansion. (*The Idea; Market & Traction*)

**Observed evidence and reported outcomes.** The sales product launched in November after a summer pivot, and its Chrome extension showed 43 users. The memo does not connect those users to paid activation. Clean reported a first paying customer and more than $200K in ARR in October, with no customer-level breakdown. Vivameda’s testimonial reports $60K in signed contracts in two weeks and a reply-rate increase from about 4% to 19%. Those are early signs that the buyer’s problem matters. The contract value belongs to Vivameda, and the memo contains no cohort evidence that Clean can reproduce the result or retain buyers at their normal buying cadence. (*Market & Traction; Key Facts*)

### Countercase

The sales workflow could travel while the data needed to make it valuable has to be rebuilt market by market. Clean’s aircraft example describes outreach after a supplier had already been selected, illustrating how a mistimed signal can erase the product’s advantage. The next geography may require new data licenses, work to normalize local records, and contact verification. Each new customer may still require founder-led sales and hands-on onboarding, while additional monitored accounts may add processing and data costs. The memo does not measure those costs or show an overseas deployment. That leaves portability and contribution untested, with insufficient evidence to trigger the structural Pass gate. (*The Idea; Defensibility; Key Risks*)
