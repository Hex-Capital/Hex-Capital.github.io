# EveryApp -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path A, product-led adoption.** The clearest initial buyer is a small team running a recurring SEO content workflow. EveryApp’s proposed route is discovery through GitHub or OpenSEO, an agent-guided Gateway install, and shared sign-in for teammates. If that first workflow becomes indispensable, the same team could add apps through the Gateway and pay for managed hosting. The platform’s 906 GitHub stars show discovery. The memo contains no record of independent teams completing that route to paid activation. (The Idea, Market & Traction)

Importivity reported building a content workflow on an OpenSEO fork and seeing about 7× organic clicks over six months. The founder separately reported hundreds of paying customers for standalone OpenSEO. That supports OpenSEO as an entry point and gives some evidence for the initial SEO job. Gateway conversion, retention, and adoption of a second app remain untested. (Market & Traction, Key Facts)

**Second-market scenario.** The same product could serve small teams elsewhere if they can provision the required Cloudflare account, domain, and service credentials and if managed billing and support work in that market. The memo provides no buyer evidence by geography. Self-hosted teams pay providers directly and generate no platform subscription revenue. At the announced $10 monthly managed entry price, the next paying team’s contribution would depend on acquisition effort, setup and support time, and any infrastructure costs EveryApp bears as usage grows. Those costs are unmeasured. (The Idea, Key Risks, Key Facts)

### Countercase

OpenSEO could succeed on its own while few customers adopt EveryApp. Teams can use hosted OpenSEO or deploy it independently. Teams that do try the Gateway face account, domain, and credential setup and can stay on the free tier. That gives users several ways to get the SEO benefit without paying EveryApp for the platform. The memo documents those alternatives and setup requirements. It does not establish a structural demand ceiling or show that delivery costs rise in proportion to adoption, so the Pass gate is unmet. (The Idea, Key Risks, Key Facts)

### Decisive diligence question

Can independent small teams in a second geography discover EveryApp through GitHub or OpenSEO, activate a shared SEO workflow through the Gateway without founder help, and pay for managed service while continuing to use it at their normal cadence? A first paid cohort that completes that sequence, with setup, support, and provider costs recorded against its revenue, would move the action to Reach out. Until then, Watch.
