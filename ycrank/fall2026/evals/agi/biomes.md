# Biomes -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Pass  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Biomes describes embedding engineers with private-equity portfolio companies to integrate agents into existing tools and turn workflow failures into evaluations. The memo documents legacy-system friction but identifies no scarce input Biomes could supply to relieve a specific AI buildout constraint. The new-industry path also scores zero. The proposed service automates existing manual work, and the memo identifies no specific buyer or use that becomes economically viable because of AI. (The Idea, Key Risks)

The memo reports rising portfolio AI spending, but provides no Biomes-specific buyer commitment, paid deployment, or revenue. Topsail already markets a similar embedded service, and Biomes’ proposed reuse of evaluations across companies remains unverified. The founders’ prior launch and ML infrastructure experience support early execution credit. The memo gives no deployment price or delivery-cost data to assess the economics of the next rollout. (Market & Traction, Defensibility, Founders & Team, Key Facts)

### Countercase

If legacy integration and ongoing supervision previously made a recurring workflow too expensive to automate, Biomes could open that use by reusing evaluations across acquisitions. Even with access to comparable models, a buyer could still need live-system integration and exception handling. That scenario depends on transfer across companies and a buyer-specific affordability threshold, neither of which the memo establishes. Both path scores remain zero, which triggers Pass. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a sponsor show that it paid to roll out the same recurring workflow at a second portfolio company after Biomes made a use previously ruled out on cost viable? A documented prior cost threshold, sustained use at the second company, and a measured cost per successful task below that threshold after integration, review, and failures would move the action to Reach out.
