# Shielded -- Sam Lessin Evaluation

I cannot underwrite the deal before knowing the post-money valuation, competing-term-sheet count, or whether YC and Susa Ventures have already turned this into an auction. Their participation is not itself adverse, but financing silence earns no presumption of scarcity. More importantly, Shielded has not named what this round uniquely proves. “Automate supply-chain risk management” is a company ambition; it is not a finite experiment with a metric, deadline, and dramatic value change.

The strongest evidence is operational. Shielded says it converted customer ERP data into live products within 48 hours, completed every deployment in under 72 hours, and handled defense suppliers, multinational CPG ingredients, manufacturing tariffs, and 200 restaurant SKUs. That is materially better than an AI demo searching public data. It suggests the product can enter a messy workflow and return something quickly enough for finance and procurement teams to use. It does not establish that anyone paid, returned, expanded, or acted profitably on a recommendation.

There is a real translation layer here: internal ERP and bill-of-materials records on one side, external commodity prices, tariffs, and events on the other. James Simon’s computer-science and economics training plus commodities work at Balyasny gives him direct access to the market-risk side, and the reported deployments show he can ship into enterprise data environments. But Shielded does not yet own the bridge. Customer exports remain customer property, no proprietary learning loop is demonstrated, and there is no evidence that its mappings caused a purchase or retained usage.

The competitive structure makes that omission decisive. Interos, Altana, Prewave, and Everstream have collectively raised hundreds of millions of dollars and already cover adjacent mapping, monitoring, tariff, and disruption functions. Shielded’s margin calculation and hedging guidance could be the narrow wedge, but no recommendation accuracy, back-testing, or realized customer outcome is public. If the output depends on incomplete ERP and supplier-origin data, fast deployment can become fast bespoke analysis rather than a defensible product.

The bull case stings: four distinct deployment contexts at pre-seed, each completed in under 72 hours, could mean Simon has found a much faster implementation model than incumbents. Tariff volatility supplies an urgent purchasing event, and the absence of hiring suggests a remarkably capital-efficient solo operation. But the known-exception standard for an AI-dependent application requires hard conversion, retention, or economic receipts—not deployment announcements. Shielded currently reports the activity surrounding value, not the cash or realized margin improvement proving value.

I pass today. Delete the next financing and the dossier supplies no price, revenue, gross margin, burn, runway, or controllable cash metric. One verifiable condition would flip me: three named deployments converting into paid annual contracts, remaining active for 90 days, and documenting realized margin savings attributable to Shielded’s recommendations. That would show an owned workflow causing a purchase and turn the current implementation speed into a real venture moment.
