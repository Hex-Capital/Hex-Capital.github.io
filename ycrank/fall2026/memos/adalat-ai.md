## The Idea

- **Problem:** Indian judges, stenographers, and litigants face hearing delays associated with handwritten records, paperwork, and stenographer shortages, with manual dictation and transcription remaining existing alternatives ([Harvard Kennedy School, September 2026](https://www.hks.harvard.edu/alumni/stories-of-impact/faster-justice-utkarsh-saxena)).
- **Approach:** The platform combines courtroom speech recognition, document processing, and case-flow dashboards that retrieve case details through court APIs, with company-claimed support for over 15 Indian languages ([Adalat products, accessed November 2026](https://www.adalat.ai/products)).
- **Differentiation:** Adalat provides Indian-language courtroom software free to Indian courts, while TERES delivers managed hearing services with human-reviewed transcription and jhana sells court workflow automation priced per case ([SSIR, Spring 2025](https://ssir.org/articles/entry/legal-tech-nonprofit-court-ai-injustices-india-court-system); [TERES](https://teres.ai/); [jhana](https://jhana.ai/public-sector)).
- **Business Model:** Adalat operates as a nonprofit supported by philanthropy and grants, with court agreements examined by India’s tax tribunal specifying pro bono delivery without commercial fees ([SSIR, Spring 2025](https://ssir.org/articles/entry/legal-tech-nonprofit-court-ai-injustices-india-court-system); [ITAT order, 2025](https://itat.gov.in/public/files/upload/1764138899-YafDGW-1-TO.pdf)).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment; the adjacent global court-case-management software market is estimated at $2.17 billion in 2026, growing at 10.84% CAGR through 2031 ([Mordor Intelligence, 2026](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/court-case-management-software-market)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Distribution proceeds through agreements with High Courts and District Courts, supported by training and workshops for court personnel ([ITAT order, 2025](https://itat.gov.in/public/files/upload/1764138899-YafDGW-1-TO.pdf)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** [Inferred]: Court integrations and institutional implementation experience provide operational know-how, supported by documented court agreements and company-reported deployment across 11 states ([ITAT, 2025](https://itat.gov.in/public/files/upload/1764138899-YafDGW-1-TO.pdf); [company LinkedIn, accessed November 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/company/adalat-in/)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Embedded workflows and staff familiarity could increase switching costs, although public sources do not quantify retention, replacement costs, or contractual exclusivity.
- **Market structure:** [Inferred]: Philanthropically financed, free delivery creates a pricing difference from commercial suppliers, but no structural barrier preventing incumbent replication was identified ([SSIR, Spring 2025](https://ssir.org/articles/entry/legal-tech-nonprofit-court-ai-injustices-india-court-system)).
- **Commoditization risk:** TERES already supplies court transcription, while jhana supplies filing scrutiny, document extraction, and order-generation workflows overlapping Adalat’s product layers ([TERES](https://teres.ai/); [jhana](https://jhana.ai/public-sector)).

## Key Risks

- **Production infrastructure compromise:** A September 8, 2026 investigation demonstrated credential-enabled access to code and production-cluster administration, creating service-disruption exposure; the company reports no confidential court-user data access and says it disabled the affected host, rotated credentials, and restricted access ([joint company–researcher incident report, September 2026](https://www.adalat.ai/blog/tech/designing-for-compromise)).
- **Transcription errors:** [Inferred]: Errors could enter evidentiary records despite the company’s claimed 90%+ recognition accuracy; Kerala’s procedure requires completed depositions to be endorsed and signed before upload ([product claims](https://www.adalat.ai/products); [court circular](https://dpo-india.com/Resources/RTBF_in_Indian_Courts/Mandatory-Recording-Witness-Depositions-Adalat.AI.tool.pdf)).
- **Institutional rollout dependency:** [Inferred]: Expansion depends on court agreements, training, and administrative authorization, while Kerala permits approved alternative platforms during technical difficulties ([ITAT order](https://itat.gov.in/public/files/upload/1764138899-YafDGW-1-TO.pdf); [Kerala circular](https://dpo-india.com/Resources/RTBF_in_Indian_Courts/Mandatory-Recording-Witness-Depositions-Adalat.AI.tool.pdf)).
