# Sentient OS -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Path B — New Economies. The strongest case is that small Mac-based teams could reliably complete routine follow-ups they previously skipped because reconstructing context across email, messages, notes, and files took too much staff time. The memo documents a released Mac app, and Sentient says its overnight processing on 8 GB Macs prepares editable context for reply drafts and actions queued for approval. The increase in completed follow-ups is an investment hypothesis. The memo provides no measured comparison with the manual workflow. (The Idea, Defensibility)

The relevant unit is a correctly completed follow-up. Manual delivery includes gathering context, drafting, and acting. Assisted delivery includes reviewing drafts, correcting errors, handling exceptions, and bearing any device, cloud, and privacy approval costs. Local processing offers a plausible way to keep per-user inference costs low for supported workflows. Enterprise contribution still depends on license revenue covering integration, support, and any cloud costs Sentient bears. The memo provides no realized cost or pricing data for that bridge. (The Idea, Key Risks, Key Facts)

Sentient reports more than 10,000 users at launch and use by more than 30 enterprises. Those claims support early interest, with payment, repeat use, and evidence that teams are completing previously skipped work still unverified. Shipping the Mac alpha and Tarun’s prior desktop assistant support the team’s ability to deliver and run the next test. (Market & Traction, Founders & Team)

### Countercase

Sentient may mainly speed up follow-ups teams already do, with review and exceptions absorbing enough savings to keep skipped work uneconomic. Under the scenario of cheap, widely available frontier-quality intelligence, a rival with equivalent local execution and privacy could compete for the same workflow. Sentient’s customized runtime shows a current implementation advantage, while its portable Markdown and documented overlap with native macOS features weaken the case for durable buyer preference. The memo contains neither a labor comparison nor an observed switch to an equivalent product. Both required conditions remain testable, which supports Watch. (Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Will an independent Mac-based team that previously skipped routine follow-ups renew a paid Sentient deployment after testing it against its manual workflow and a functionally equivalent local assistant? Move to Reach out if the trial shows more correctly completed follow-ups at lower full cost per outcome and the buyer renews at a price that covers delivery, citing a specific reason it prefers Sentient under parity. Move to Pass if a representative trial shows that review and exceptions erase the market-opening savings or that the buyer readily switches while retaining the outcome.
