## The Idea

- **Problem:** Health-system revenue-cycle teams manually reconcile changing payer contracts against payments, often discovering underpayments months afterward through spreadsheets and retrospective reviews ([YC launch, October 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WBc-cesara-ai-agents-that-make-sure-every-healthcare-transaction-is-paid-per-contract)).
- **Approach:** Cesara extracts contractual reimbursement terms, calculates expected payment at claim submission, compares incoming remittances, and files appeals containing the governing clause and payment evidence ([YC launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WBc-cesara-ai-agents-that-make-sure-every-healthcare-transaction-is-paid-per-contract)).
- **Differentiation:** Cesara emphasizes contract-linked checking and automated appeals, whereas MD Clarity additionally offers patient estimates and deposits, and Tenix markets broader financial analysis and board reporting ([Cesara](https://cesara.com/), [MD Clarity](https://www.mdclarity.com/), [Tenix](https://tenixhealth.com/)).
- **Business Model:** Rahman describes outcome-based pilots in which Cesara earns nothing unless agreed dollar-identification or recovery targets are met; published fee percentages and subscription tiers: No public data found ([DGTL Voices interview transcript, October 8, 2026](https://podscripts.co/podcasts/dgtl-voices-with-ed-marx/what-you-should-have-been-paid-ft-xinan-rahman)).
- **TAM/SAM:** The adjacent global healthcare contract-management software market is estimated at $1.83B in 2026, reaching $3.63B by 2031 at 14.66% CAGR; Cesara-specific TAM/SAM: No public data found ([Mordor Intelligence, January 2026 estimates](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/healthcare-contract-management-software-market)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Cesara solicits health-system buyers through demo bookings and complimentary audits of their contracts and claims ([Cesara contact page, accessed October 27, 2026](https://cesara.com/contact)).

## Key Risks

- **Calculation and action accuracy:** [Inferred]: Misinterpreted amendments or reimbursement clauses could produce incorrect expected payments and appeals; Cesara describes validation against customer data, approval thresholds, and clause-linked audit trails as controls ([Cesara trust page](https://cesara.com/trust)).
- **Integration and sensitive-data exposure:** [Inferred]: Processing contracts and claims across hospital systems creates deployment and data-handling dependencies; Cesara states it offers encryption, role-based access, BAAs, and zero-retention AI providers ([Cesara trust page](https://cesara.com/trust)).
- **Competitive overlap:** MD Clarity already offers contract intelligence and recovery services and expanded agentic analysis in September 2026, overlapping Cesara’s advertised workflows ([MD Clarity release](https://www.mdclarity.com/newsroom/md-clarity-unveils-major-expansion-of-revfind-with-agentic-ai-to-optimize-the-revenue-of-provider-organizations)).
