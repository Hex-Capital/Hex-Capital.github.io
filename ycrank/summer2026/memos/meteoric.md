## The Idea

- **Problem:** Low- and mid-level overcast clouds reduce incoming sunlight by 73–82% while overhead for utility-scale solar operators, while adding capacity faces a U.S. interconnection queue containing 773 GW of proposed generation ([YC launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/meteoric), Aug 2026).
- **Approach:** Fleets of tens to hundreds of autonomous drones fly into clouds at 1–5 km, alter droplets without chemicals, reduce reflectivity, and allow more sunlight to reach existing panels ([YC launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/meteoric), Aug 2026).
- **Differentiation:** Meteoric targets solar output through chemical-free droplet alteration, versus Rainmaker’s aerosol-dispersing precipitation drones, North American Weather Consultants’ conventional precipitation and fog-seeding operations, and Reflect Orbital’s satellite-reflected sunlight ([Meteoric](https://meteoric.earth/); [Rainmaker](https://www.rainmaker.com/technology); [NAWC](https://www.northamericanweatherconsultants.com/cloud-seeding); [Reflect Orbital](https://www.reflectorbital.com/press/announcing-our-6-5-million-seed-round)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Meteoric could sell cloud treatment as a service priced by treated capacity, flight time, or recovered generation because it solicits utility-scale solar owners and quantifies modeled value per MW ([YC launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/meteoric), Aug 2026).
- **TAM/SAM:** One commercial estimate values the global cloud-seeding and weather-modification market at $1.42B in 2025 with an 8.8% CAGR through 2032, but no public SAM was found for chemical-free solar-farm cloud clearing ([Market Research Reports](https://www.marketresearchreports.com/reports/global-cloud-seeding-weather-modification-market-strategic-research-report), 2026).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Meteoric is directly soliciting utility-scale solar owners and operators in Texas, the northern U.S., the U.K., and northern Europe through its YC launch and website contact form ([YC launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/meteoric), Aug 2026).

## Key Risks

- **Field-effect validation:** The 13% result comes from an artificial-cloud chamber, with no published chamber dimensions, repeated trials, peer review, natural-cloud result, or measured solar-output increase ([TechSpot](https://www.techspot.com/news/113586-startup-wants-use-autonomous-drone-swarms-clear-clouds.html), Aug 2026).
- **Hurricane feasibility:** Meteoric targets a first storm operation in late 2028, while the World Meteorological Organization states there is no generally accepted evidence that tropical cyclones can be modified ([YC launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/meteoric); [WMO](https://wmo.int/content/wmo-statement-weather-modification)).
- **Airspace authorization:** Planned 1–5 km-altitude, multi-drone operations conflict with baseline U.S. Part 107 requirements for visual line of sight, one drone per pilot or observer, and waivers above 400 feet or beyond visual line of sight ([Meteoric launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/meteoric); [FAA](https://www.faa.gov/newsroom/small-unmanned-aircraft-systems-uas-regulations-part-107)).
- **Unit economics:** Meteoric cites $30–60 hourly electric-drone operating costs and fleets of tens to hundreds, but publishes no required drone-hours per MW, fleet capital cost, maintenance cost, customer price, or field-measured recovered MWh ([YC launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/meteoric), Aug 2026).
- **Name disambiguation:** Search results share “Meteoric” with unrelated companies including Australian miner Meteoric Resources and Meteoric Engineering, while the startup uses meteoric.earth and “Meteoric Technologies Inc.” for identification ([Meteoric Resources](https://www.linkedin.com/company/meteoric-resources/); [Meteoric Engineering](https://in.linkedin.com/company/meteoricengineering)).
