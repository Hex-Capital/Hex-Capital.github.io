# Verdict Machine -- Naval Ravikant Evaluation

The founders have earned the right to work on this problem. Dan Danay and Isaac Grossman built keyTango together beginning in 2019, then Danay and Liran Kogan led blockchain-security work at Check Point. This is specific knowledge acquired through shipping, not credentials arranged around a fashionable market. The former-employer overlap does require clean IP and customer-reference diligence, but the dossier shows no evidence of unfair dealing, artificial deadlines, or other integrity red flags.

Verdict Machine is attacking a consequential control point: constructing an institution-specific model, translating it into policy, and stopping a violating transaction before execution. POCs started in the company’s first weeks with organizations moving billions of dollars weekly, alongside evaluations by large-bank digital-asset teams. For a Summer 2026 company, that is meaningful evidence of access and urgency. It is not yet evidence that the model is accurate enough to control irreversible transactions or survive enterprise procurement.

The decisive weakness is what remains after the code is copied. Verdict Machine discloses no proprietary dataset, exclusive integration, performance benchmark, or deployed feedback loop. Blockaid already processes more than 180 million transactions monthly; Hexagate combines prevention across more than 75 chains with a decade of Chainalysis intelligence; Check Point previously shipped an overlapping AI-assisted blockchain firewall. The proposed compounding asset—institution-specific policy decisions and outcomes—is still hypothetical. Today, copying the product leaves competitors with more data and distribution than Verdict Machine.

The market has room: blockchain security was estimated at $6.37 billion in 2025 and is projected to grow 22.4% annually through 2033. Regulatory changes also reopened bank custody, stablecoin, and distributed-ledger activity. The shared capability could expand from transaction enforcement into inventory, posture, diligence, and screening. But a growing parent market is not the same as a demonstrated path across adjacent buyers, and no segment-level SAM, contract, price, or revenue has been disclosed.

The strongest bull case is unusually credible: an intact team that has built together, operated the exact product category, and reached serious institutions almost immediately. Founder-specific knowledge can override an immature market story. It cannot yet override an explicitly weak substrate. This is also a conventional enterprise sales system, not a permissionless network: each institution appears to require another sale and implementation, while no customer presently creates value or distribution for the next.

I would not write the check yet. My 36-month base case is that Fireblocks acquires Verdict Machine as a policy-security feature after it reaches roughly $1 million to $3 million in ARR, rather than the company becoming an independent platform. One verifiable condition flips me to Invest: three named paid production deployments whose proprietary policy-and-outcome data measurably improves false-positive and false-negative performance as deployment volume grows.
