## The Idea

- **Problem:** Sales, recruiting, and research teams must reconcile fragmented people information and stale contact records, with existing workflows involving database filters, CSV exports, and manual cleaning ([Ploid](https://ploid.com/)).
- **Approach:** Ploid combines a claimed index of over two billion people with identity resolution, live retrieval, contact enrichment, and evidence-backed person reports delivered through its app, API, CLI, and MCP server ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ploid), [documentation](https://ploid.com/documentation)).
- **Differentiation:** Ploid emphasizes cross-site identity resolution and person-level research, versus Apollo’s integrated sales execution platform, People Data Labs’ datasets and enrichment infrastructure, and Exa’s broader web-search infrastructure ([Ploid](https://ploid.com/), [Apollo](https://www.apollo.io/), [PDL](https://www.peopledatalabs.com/), [Exa](https://exa.ai/blog/announcing-series-c)).
- **Business Model:** Workspace subscriptions cost $200/month for 1,000 Growth credits or $600/month for 4,000 Scale credits, with additional credits priced at $0.20 and $0.15 respectively and negotiated Custom plans ([pricing, accessed Oct 27, 2026](https://ploid.com/pricing)).
- **TAM/SAM:** The adjacent global sales-intelligence market was $4.0 billion in 2025, with projected 10.1% CAGR during 2026–2033; no public TAM/SAM data found for Ploid’s specific people-data infrastructure segment ([Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/sales-intelligence-market)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Public signup, demo requests, developer documentation, and agent integrations provide acquisition paths, while Ploid announced an API integration with Monid ([website](https://ploid.com/), [documentation](https://ploid.com/documentation), [company post, Sep 4, 2026, mirrored by Teapawt](https://teapawt.org/ploid_ai)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Ploid claims a proprietary cross-site identity-linking system and an index exceeding two billion people, although public sources do not establish exclusive ownership of the underlying inputs ([Ploid](https://ploid.com/)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Accumulated identity corrections and refreshed evidence could improve matching over time, but no public longitudinal accuracy or customer-retention data establishes this feedback loop.
- **Market structure:** [Inferred]: No structural barrier identified at this stage, because Apollo already offers MCP access and Exa maintains people-search indexes ([Apollo](https://www.apollo.io/), [Exa](https://exa.ai/blog/announcing-series-c)).
- **Commoditization risk:** [Inferred]: Existing enrichment providers and search engines can assemble overlapping workflows, as Monid already combines Ploid, Apollo, People Data Labs, and other vendors behind one connection ([Monid announcement, Oct 20, 2026, mirrored by Teapawt](https://teapawt.org/ploid_ai)).

## Key Risks

- **Contact-data dependency:** Third-party vendors supply some emails and phone numbers, and Ploid says it does not control their sourcing, classification, or refresh practices ([privacy policy](https://ploid.com/privacy)).
- **Technical accuracy:** Search matches are not verified facts until person research returns evidence, creating a verification requirement for downstream agents; source URLs and field-level provenance provide mitigation ([documentation](https://ploid.com/documentation)).
- **Privacy and permitted-use constraints:** Ploid’s terms restrict use to lawful professional workflows and prohibit personal surveillance and unlawful sensitive-data processing, limiting permissible applications of its people index ([terms](https://ploid.com/terms)).
- **Competitive substitution:** Apollo offers agent access and sales execution, while Exa supplies people-search infrastructure, overlapping Ploid’s distribution and retrieval functions ([Apollo](https://www.apollo.io/), [Exa](https://exa.ai/blog/announcing-series-c)).
- **Name confusion:** Barcelona-based Ploid AI Biotech operates at ploid.ai and serves bioinformatics, so its funding and hiring records must be excluded from this company’s dossier ([Ploid AI newsroom](https://ploid.ai/press)).
