### Company case

The strongest path is a strategic acquisition by a manufacturing software vendor. Owning horizontal. could give a buyer a way to coordinate scheduling, quality holds, and batch release across the plant systems its customers already use. The memo records an October 2026 launch and $125,000 in listed seed financing, with total funding unverified. The company reports tests in operating plants and advertises 50% shorter release times. The memo provides no named customers, measurement baselines, or confirmed paid deployments. A paid rollout of the same workflow at a second plant, with customer-verified savings and measured integration effort, would make the buyer rationale more concrete. The reported plant tests give horizontal. a starting point, while the capital and time needed to replicate a deployment remain unclear. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Facts)

The benchmark assumes a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, 5% starting ownership diluted to 3%, and no follow-ons. The holder needs $2.5 million in gross cash within eight years, requiring about $83.3 million in distributable equity value at 3%. The sale scenarios assume $5 million in combined debt and deal adjustments, full participation, and a pro rata cash waterfall with no further senior claims.

- **Modest sale.** $50 million in enterprise value leaves $45 million for equity and pays the holder $1.35 million in cash, or 2.7×.
- **Strong sale.** $100 million in enterprise value leaves $95 million for equity and pays the holder $2.85 million in cash, or 5.7×. If only 80% of the holder’s allocation is paid in cash within eight years, realized proceeds fall to $2.28 million, or 4.56×.
- **Prolonged private operation.** If available cash is reinvested and the holder has no eligible share sale within eight years, realized proceeds are zero.

The sale prices are scenario assumptions. The memo supplies no buyer economics or transaction pricing to support either price. Transfer rights, required consents, and the actual waterfall remain unverified. The memo provides no evidence of an independent liquidity route. (Market & Traction, Key Facts)

### Countercase

SAP has announced overlapping shortage detection and rescheduling features, and NVIDIA’s factory-agent blueprint gives vendors a starting point for building related workflows. If each horizontal. deployment still requires substantial site-specific work, a buyer could cover the highest-value functions internally and have little reason to pay for the broader product. The overlap weakens the acquisition case. A substitute for the full planning-to-release workflow and a ceiling on realizable sale proceeds both remain unestablished, leaving the Pass gate untriggered. (Defensibility, Key Risks)
