# Axiomat -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Pass  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Axiomat targets enterprise teams that rely on dashboards, manual checks, or restricted AI assistants for consequential pricing and reporting questions. The public launch and product demo establish a seed-stage product milestone. Axiomat says it translates questions and business rules into formal definitions, shows users its interpretation, and uses Lean to verify that each generated query matches the definition before execution. A valid proof still depends on the definition capturing the business intent. (The Idea, Key Facts, Key Risks)

**Building blocks of superintelligence earns zero.** The proof gate addresses reliability in enterprise analytics. The memo establishes neither a scarce input that constrains AI deployment nor evidence that substitutes would be slow to supply as demand grows. **Industry enabled by superintelligence also earns zero.** The memo identifies existing workflows and their safeguards, while leaving open which analyses customers currently forgo because of cost or turnaround and whether Axiomat would make them viable. Both zeros trigger Pass. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

The proof gate gives Axiomat a specific product distinction, and customer-approved rules could create switching costs if they become embedded in recurring workflows. The founders’ formal verification and real-time control experience supports execution credit alongside the demo. The memo supplies launch activity and demo booking, with buyer trials, paid use, and delivery costs still unverified. (Defensibility, Founders & Team, Market & Traction, Key Facts)

### Countercase

A finance team might use Axiomat to answer ad hoc pricing questions at a cadence that manual review makes uneconomic. The reported reliance on manual checks and restricted assistants makes this plausible. Under comparable model capability, buyers could still need rule approval, proof-gated execution, and exception handling, though warehouse-native tools may already meet their assurance requirements. The newly viable cadence remains a scenario assumption, so the Pass gate holds. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Risks)
