# Telamont -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path B — New economies** is the strongest path. Telamont’s software checks each new drawing or specification against the project record and routes source-linked findings to the people who can resolve them. The seed-stage hypothesis is that general contractors who manually review selected revisions could afford to check every revision. An equivalent-quality comparison would need to count reviewer time, false alarms, missed conflicts, checks on files outside connected repositories, and human approval of notices. The memo describes the workflow. It leaves the labor savings and the previously uneconomic review frequency unproven. (*The Idea*, *Key Risks*)

Telamont reports monitoring more than $2B in project value and avoiding $1.1M in foundation rework on one project. The memo also records displayed customer logos and published billing terms. These signals suggest potential buyer value, with paid use and repeat deployments unverified. For each additional project, subscription revenue would need to cover onboarding, inference, support, and any review labor Telamont provides. The memo gives no project-level pricing or costs to test that bridge. (*Market & Traction*, *Key Facts*)

Goulet’s construction and software experience and Gagnon’s industrial AI work support seed-stage execution. The listed Autodesk Forma integration is a concrete delivery milestone. The memo lists San Francisco and also reports a Canadian base, leaving fit with the primarily U.S. mandate uncertain. (*Founders & Team*, *The Idea*, opening company table)

### Countercase

Telamont may make existing document review faster while leaving its frequency unchanged. Under model parity, Trimble’s overlapping review capabilities could make it easy for contractors to switch. Telamont’s source-linked history of owners, deadlines, and decisions could give buyers a reason to stay, but the memo provides no evidence that buyers prefer or pay for that record when detection quality is equivalent. Telamont also shows no control of a separate scarce input for Path A. Both Path B conditions remain open to a field test, so the outreach gate stays closed and Watch applies. (*The Idea*, *Defensibility*, *Key Risks*)

### Decisive diligence question

In a paid field trial, will contractors who previously reviewed only selected revisions adopt every-revision review and choose Telamont again on their next project over an alternative with comparable detection quality? Measure all-in labor per revision at matched quality, including exceptions and approvals, and test repeat purchase at a price that covers delivery costs. Evidence of both outcomes would support Reach out if the revised score and mandate fit clear the gates. Evidence that full review costs still put every-revision checks out of reach, or that buyers readily switch at parity, would move the action to Pass.
