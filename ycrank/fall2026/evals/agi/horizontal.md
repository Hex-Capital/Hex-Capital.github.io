# horizontal. -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Pass  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Horizontal’s agents target factory teams that already reconcile records and coordinate responses to shortages, order changes, and quality holds. The company says its agents work across existing plant systems and retain human sign-off for critical actions. This supports a case for improving existing factory workflows. The memo identifies no AI workload whose deployment depends on a scarce input Horizontal supplies, and no factory buyer or use that becomes economically viable because of the agents. Both AGI paths score zero, which triggers Pass. (*The Idea*, *Key Risks*)

For the existing business, plant-system integrations, on-premises deployment, and company-reported ISO 27001 certification support some differentiation. Site-specific approvals could create switching costs, while SAP’s overlapping features and NVIDIA’s reference blueprint give buyers and rivals alternatives. Horizontal has publicly launched and reports tests in operating plants. Those reports and the described manual workarounds provide early demand evidence. The memo identifies no buyer commitment or paid deployment. The founders’ prior industrial enterprise sales and modeling work support seed-stage execution credit. Without contract pricing or measured integration, support, and review costs, the economics of adding the next workflow remain unverified. (*Defensibility*, *Market & Traction*, *Founders & Team*, *Key Facts*)

### Countercase

Horizontal could make continuous cross-system exception review affordable at a plant that currently handles only the highest-priority cases manually. That scenario assumes the agents save enough coordination time to cover integration, human approvals, and error handling. Horizontal’s advertised 50% reduction in release time makes the scenario worth testing, but the claim has no named customer or measurement baseline. The memo provides no evidence that a plant adopted a previously unaffordable review cadence, so the Pass gate applies. (*The Idea*, *Market & Traction*, *Key Risks*)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a paying plant use Horizontal to review production exceptions at a cadence it previously could not afford? A buyer-verified deployment would change the action to Reach out if it showed sustained use at that cadence, a lower fully loaded cost per correctly resolved exception after integration, approvals, and error handling, and a need for Horizontal’s cross-system workflow even with access to a comparably capable model.
