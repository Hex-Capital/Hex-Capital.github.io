# Composal -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path B — New economies.** Verify could make browser QA on every pull request economical for teams that can only afford to check key user journeys before release. The memo documents parallel browser agents, reusable scenarios, and recordings with reproduction steps. Composal reports finding more than 1,500 bugs in customer apps, an early usage signal with buyer count, paid conversion, and repeat use unknown. At a seed-stage standard, this supports a delivery trial that measures engineer time at equivalent coverage and defect accuracy. **The Idea**, **Market & Traction**

The founders’ experience in agent infrastructure, product engineering, and enterprise AI sales supports their ability to run that trial. Verify’s launch and the Recorder rollout show early delivery. Composal is based in London, so fit with the primarily U.S. mandate would need confirmation before outreach. **Founders & Team**, **Market & Traction**, **Key Facts**

At the documented $500 monthly price, a team using all 3,000 included browser minutes leaves about $0.17 per minute to cover direct costs at breakeven before setup and support. This scenario assumes full usage and zero add-on revenue. Actual contribution per account depends on inference, browser compute, retries, and the human work needed to onboard and support each additional team. **The Idea**, **Key Risks**

### Countercase

Verify may make existing QA cheaper without making more frequent testing viable. Composal says half of 10 setup calls stalled on authentication or network controls, and human review could absorb the time saved on manual testing. It also reports that nine teams had built internal QA agents. With comparable models and ample capital, a buyer or competitor could reproduce much of the workflow. Retained scenarios and test history might give buyers a reason to stay, but their effect on coverage and retention is unmeasured. Buyers control test-environment access, leaving Path A without a separately supported bottleneck Composal controls. The evidence leaves both Path B conditions open to a feasible test, which supports Watch. **Key Risks**, **Defensibility**

### Decisive diligence question

Will teams that previously rationed browser QA renew at the stated price for per-pull-request coverage after trying an equivalent workflow built with the same model capabilities? A paid cohort trial should compare coverage, confirmed defects, and total human time, including setup, review, retries, and exceptions. Renewals after a meaningful labor reduction at comparable quality would support rescoring for Reach out if the score and mandate gates clear. A trial showing that the labor savings disappear or buyers consistently choose the equivalent workflow would move the action to Pass.
