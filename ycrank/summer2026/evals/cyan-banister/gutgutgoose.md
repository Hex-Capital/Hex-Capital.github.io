# Gutgutgoose -- Cyan Banister Evaluation

Seven paid sign-ups on opening night make Gutgutgoose a tangible, unsexy-regulated health bet whose weakest organ is founder authority. Gutgutgoose can sequence the stool, compound the capsule, and test colonization after 90 days, but Anis Mihrshahi and Leon Mojarrabi are both merely “Founder”; no CEO or tiebreaker is named. That pairing is the signature: magically weird biotechnology attached to a governance conversation that appears not to have happened.

**Mindbloom** is the high rung because an aesthetically transgressive treatment became investable through regulatory difficulty rather than despite it. Gutgutgoose shares that mechanism: personalized microorganisms, health claims, physical compounding, and an Australian patent application covering metabolic modeling and iterative verification. Gutgutgoose diverges where Mindbloom had a legible therapeutic protocol—the reported colonization and persistence results remain unpublished, and FDA rules could turn Gutgutgoose’s disease-prediction ambition from supplement commerce into a drug-development burden.

**HQ Trivia** is the low-rung warning: tactile user love temporarily hid a co-founder structure that later fractured. Gutgutgoose has childhood friends of roughly ten years, but longevity is not authority; Gutgutgoose names neither a CEO nor an operating tiebreaker between Mihrshahi’s algorithm work and Mojarrabi’s pharmacy-and-GTM domain. Two founders can be a duet until the first clinical setback, financing crisis, or regulatory disagreement. Then somebody must conduct.

Leon Mojarrabi built a pharmacy reportedly producing $4 million annually, while Anis Mihrshahi co-built the mechanistic algorithm; that is real Biz and Tiz, but it is not the **Anduril** origin. Palmer Luckey was of the artifact before the market existed. Gutgutgoose’s founders are adjacent to pharmacy and AI, yet Gutgutgoose provides no evidence that either founder personally lived IBS, GLP-1 side effects, or failed probiotic treatment. Domain adjacency earns credit; the missing autobiographical moment keeps me from falling in love.

Floré already sells sequencing-based custom probiotics, so Gutgutgoose is weird in method but not lonely in category. Gutgutgoose would move into my top rung if sophisticated investors rejected the 90-day verification loop as too regulated or grotesque while repeat customers proved those investors wrong. Instead, Viome has raised $175 million, Pendulum more than $150 million, and Gutgutgoose says it won the YC product showcase. That looks less like Crusoe in an oil field and more like an interesting entrant walking into an already financed room.

The $200-per-month probiotic and scheduled 90-day retest make Gutgutgoose physically observable: a customer can collect the sample, swallow the formula, and inspect the follow-up report. Over 18 months, Gutgutgoose’s artifact compounds only if customers complete retesting, because every baseline-intervention-outcome pair improves personalization and makes the 50,000-microbiome corpus more proprietary. If customers abandon the unpleasant second stool collection or reject the subscription price, Gutgutgoose becomes an expensive supplement brand wearing an AI lab coat.

Gutgutgoose’s unpublished beta results point to my 36-month forecast: Gutgutgoose will pivot from a $200-per-month direct-to-consumer probiotic subscription into clinic-facing microbiome testing and compounding infrastructure, remaining below $2 million ARR. Clinics can enforce the 90-day loop and absorb regulatory workflow better than fickle consumers, while the patent and longitudinal data survive the pivot. I’m never playing the game I seem to be playing; the probiotic may be the wrapper, and the intervention dataset may be the product.

Five waitlist referrals, seven paid sign-ups, a completed alpha cohort, Mojarrabi’s $4 million pharmacy, and the 50,000-microbiome corpus form the strongest bull case: Gutgutgoose may already possess the early physical pull that allowed **HQ Trivia** to override my governance rule. Gutgutgoose has not shown HQ-level compulsion, however; seven buyers cannot yet outweigh unpublished efficacy, Floré’s direct overlap, and unnamed founder authority. A preregistered 80-person study showing a clinically meaningful symptom improvement with strong 90-day retest completion would flip my verdict from Neutral to Invest. Until then, this is a rebuttal, not a hard no.
