# Reconero -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

A strategic sale to a law-enforcement evidence or records platform is Reconero’s strongest potential liquidity route. The company describes turning timestamped video events into source-linked narratives and completed department forms. A platform could have a reason to buy that capability if it measurably reduces the work required to produce an approved report across existing agency systems. That buyer benefit is an inference. The memo records an October 2026 launch and YC backing. The company reports consulting more than 100 officers, and the founder reports beta feedback from several police units on an earlier product. Those signals support pursuing a paid production test. The memo gives no measured time savings, paid deployment, or confirmed budget for that test. (*The Idea*, *Market & Traction*, *Key Risks*)

Using the shared benchmark, assume a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, 5% starting ownership diluted to 3%, and no follow-ons. A 5× gross cash return requires $2.5 million within eight years, or about $83.3 million in distributable equity value at 3%. The following are illustrative sensitivities. Both sale cases assume an eligible holder sells the full stake for cash within eight years, with $5 million in combined net debt and deal deductions and a pro rata waterfall with no senior claims.

- **Modest sale.** $40 million in enterprise value leaves $35 million in distributable equity and $1.05 million in holder cash, or 2.1×.
- **Strong sale.** $100 million in enterprise value leaves $95 million in distributable equity and $2.85 million in holder cash, or 5.7×.
- **Prolonged private.** Continued operation with cash reinvested yields zero realized proceeds over the eight-year window.

The memo supplies no revenue or buyer pricing evidence to support either sale value. Cap-table terms, contract assignability, and any agency consents remain unverified. The memo identifies no independent liquidity route for this holder. (*The Idea*, *Market & Traction*, *Key Facts*)

### Countercase

Axon already bundles report drafting into its evidence platform, and Code Four offers source-linked drafting across camera vendors and integrations with records and dispatch systems. Reconero’s form automation could give a buyer a reason to acquire it, but the memo contains no measured improvement in the full reporting workflow. A trial of a competing tool found no speed gain, which makes that benefit worth testing directly. The substitutes weaken the acquisition rationale. The memo establishes neither a structural barrier to a sale nor a supported price ceiling below the holder’s hurdle, so neither mandatory Pass gate is triggered. (*Defensibility*, *Market & Traction*, *Key Risks*)

### Decisive diligence question

Can Reconero demonstrate enough incremental value over bundled drafting to justify an acquisition that clears the holder’s proceeds hurdle? Paid production deployments showing repeatable reductions in officer time per approved report while maintaining accuracy and required audit records, together with credible buyer economics supporting at least $83.3 million in distributable equity, would move the action to Reach out. Results that establish workflow savings alone would keep it at Watch.
