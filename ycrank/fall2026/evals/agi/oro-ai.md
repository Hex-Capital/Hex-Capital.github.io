# ORO AI -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Medium

### Company case

**Scarce complements** is the strongest path. As AI teams test agents on longer business workflows, a wholesaler-calibrated simulator could become valuable if it reveals failures that simpler benchmarks miss. ORO’s SMB Bench gives agents a simulated 90-day produce-buying assignment and compares matched scenarios with and without delayed customer and supplier effects. ORO reports that the best model averaged $7,749 in simulated profit versus $10,795 for the scripted expert. That result shows the benchmark can measure a performance gap within the simulation. Its value for real deployment decisions remains unproven. (The Idea, Key Risks)

GetASAP and other wholesalers helped calibrate the simulation, and ORO controls access to its grader. The memo records 670 dataset downloads and ORO reports 859 runs across 16 models. Those signals show research interest, while independent paid adoption and repeat use remain unverified. The founders’ evaluation and distributed-systems experience, together with the shipped benchmark, support execution at this stage. A hosted license is a plausible business model. Scale would depend on reuse across customers and the incremental cost of calibration, compute, and support, for which the memo supplies no unit economics. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Founders & Team, The Idea, Key Facts)

### Countercase

Veris can recreate customer-specific tools and workflows, and the memo identifies public methods and datasets as a route to building comparable tests. ORO has demonstrated calibration in one workflow, with no documented exclusive data rights. Grader access establishes control over ORO’s test, while the scarcity of equivalent tests remains open. The memo also supplies no cost per successful purchasing outcome or evidence of a previously excluded buyer, leaving the New Economies path unsupported. Substitution is credible but unobserved, so the decision is Watch. (Defensibility, Key Risks, The Idea)
