# Simantic -- Peter Thiel Evaluation

Simantic’s possible secret is that virtual hardware can become reliable enough for AI agents to validate firmware before physical prototypes exist. What would prove that wrong? Firmware that repeatedly passes simulation and fails on silicon. The founders’ reported waits of weeks or months establish a real bottleneck, but the dossier identifies neither a prevailing belief that named peers reject nor a completed experiment overturning it. The [validation standard](https://simantic.com/validation) proposes the right comparison. A proposed test is not its result.

The investment question turns on who owns the solution. Simantic’s [Python SDK](https://github.com/simantic-dev/pippy) defaults to Renode, which already supports peripheral modeling, multi-node simulation, and CI. Wokwi also offers editor and CI integration. Hosting these capabilities and connecting them to agents could create a useful business; it does not yet establish a monopoly. Privately developed engines and a Rust backend are substantive development efforts, but the dossier supplies no comparative fidelity result, exclusive model coverage, or bounded customer segment in which those assets confer control. Open-source foundations are compatible with proprietary businesses. Here, the proprietary advantage remains unproved against documented substitutes.

The founding relationship is more encouraging. Hong began the venture in September 2025, and Shahriar joined in early 2026 after they already knew each other through Waterloo’s engineering community. That is affirmative evidence against a team assembled solely to apply to YC. Their Tesla, robotics, and Neuralink experience also makes the strongest opposing case credible: these founders have encountered the physical constraints that generic agent developers may misunderstand. But employment history cannot establish present economic alignment. Full-time status, ownership, and prior joint execution remain undocumented; Shahriar’s degree being in progress does not itself establish part-time commitment.

Simantic fits my less-public pattern of interest in the complete AI production stack: faster code generation makes an unresolved physical validation constraint more consequential. The bull case is that model fidelity becomes the scarce asset, and that solving difficult peripheral behavior produces a library competitors cannot cheaply reproduce. The announced Embedder, BoardRepo, and WATFlight relationships offer places to test that proposition. However, the dossier describes planned testing and a recent Cloud Sim beta, while the SDK explicitly asks users to defer production pipelines. My known exceptions depend on demonstrated technical discontinuity, persistent transaction demand, or a concrete deployment path. None is established here strongly enough to override the competitive concern.

The commercial route is visible but immature. Documentation, API clients, and partner integrations provide actual acquisition surfaces; they earn more credit than an unexplained promise of developer-led growth. Yet the [$1,000 annual Developer plan](https://simantic.com/pricing) would require 100,000 annual subscribers to generate $100 million in subscription revenue. Custom Enterprise hosting might change that arithmetic substantially, but no contract values, delivery margins, or expansion behavior establish how. Approximately $600,000 raised finances the attempt; it does not validate customer economics.

I would pass on writing a personal check today. Simantic has credible founders addressing a consequential constraint, but its present product sits above an established simulation substrate without demonstrated ownership of the scarce capability. The red flag is competitive substitutability, not the absence of pre-seed revenue. My verifiable flip condition is an independently reproduced, customer-run silicon comparison showing that Simantic’s proprietary models reliably catch consequential firmware failures missed by both Renode and Wokwi on a defined MCU family. That would establish a technical reason for this company to exist beyond packaging—and identify a beachhead worth investigating.
