# Sentient OS -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Path A, product-led adoption, has the strongest evidence. Mac-based knowledge workers repeatedly pull context from messages, meeting notes, and files to draft replies and handle follow-ups. Sentient’s free app is available through direct download and Homebrew. The company reports 4,000+ first-week alpha installs on $0 in marketing and 2,264 computer-use tasks in the first 48 hours after its r/macapps launch. Those figures show early distribution and use, with activation per install and repeatability across cohorts still unknown. (The Idea, Market & Traction)

The growth scenario assumes individual users get enough recurring value from reply drafts and follow-ups to bring Sentient into their teams, where employers would pay for commercial licenses or support. The same workflow could recur among Apple silicon users in a second market. Portability depends on whether the existing app handles local languages and app setups and meets employer privacy requirements. The memo gives no buyer demand by geography. Running the built-in model on users’ Macs could limit Sentient’s incremental compute costs for overnight processing. Each team could still add approval, integration, and support work, and the memo gives no rollout costs or cost breakdown for cloud-assisted features. (The Idea, Key Risks, Key Facts)

### Countercase

Free launch interest may never turn into a recurring workflow that employers will pay for. The company reports use at more than 30 enterprises, with payment status and usage cadence undisclosed. Full Disk Access and possible transmission of screenshots or personal context could push team adoption into account-by-account reviews and founder-led setup. The memo provides too little evidence to establish the structural ceiling required for Pass. A paid cohort would resolve more of the uncertainty than a conversation today. (Market & Traction, The Idea, Key Risks, Key Facts)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a first cohort of Mac-heavy teams outside the US discover Sentient through its existing self-serve channel, pay for it, and keep using it for replies or follow-ups through several normal work cycles? Paid invoices, cohort usage records, and onboarding logs showing a shared rollout with limited founder involvement would change the action to Reach out.
