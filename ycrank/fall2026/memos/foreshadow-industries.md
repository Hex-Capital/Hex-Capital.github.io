## The Idea

- **Problem:** Naval operators seeking longer missions with less crew exposure can keep using crewed vessels or procure purpose-built autonomous vessels (supplied company profile, undated; [Global Market Insights](https://www.gminsights.com/industry-analysis/autonomous-naval-vessel-market), 2026).
- **Approach:** Foreshadow retrofits existing military vehicles with Zulu, a model the company says uses spatiotemporal graphs and factor graphs to reason across space and time during extended operations (supplied company profile, undated).
- **Differentiation:** Foreshadow describes one model for vessels, aircraft, ground vehicles, and satellites, while Sea Machines sells marine-specific retrofit systems, Havoc emphasizes coordination across platforms, and Saronic designs and builds autonomous vessels (supplied company profile, undated; [Sea Machines](https://sea-machines.com/sea-machines-expands-product-line-for-defense-customers/), Sep 2025; [Havoc](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/havoc-raises-100m-series-a-to-power-the-future-of-all-domain-collaborative-autonomy-302769697.html), May 2026; [Saronic](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/saronic-closes-1-75b-series-d-at-9-25b-valuation-to-accelerate-a-new-era-of-maritime-autonomy-302729298.html), Mar 2026).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: The retrofit offering could support software licensing and paid integration.
- **TAM/SAM:** Global Market Insights estimates the broader autonomous naval vessel market at $2.22B in 2026 and projects 15.6% CAGR through 2035, with no public sizing found for Foreshadow’s specific retrofit segment ([Global Market Insights](https://www.gminsights.com/industry-analysis/autonomous-naval-vessel-market), 2026).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Mo Akbar presented Foreshadow at YC’s defense and industrial-base event in Washington, D.C. ([YC event recap](https://businessdatasolutions.github.io/ai-wiki/sources/2026-09-30-yc-root-access-startup-industrial-base-dc), Sep 2026 event).
