# Talos -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

A strategic acquisition by a grid-equipment or monitoring vendor is Talos’s strongest supported route. Siemens Energy announced an agreement to acquire Camlin Energy at an undisclosed price. Talos describes a retrofit system that learns equipment-specific baselines from external sensors across multiple asset types. An incumbent could use it to extend monitoring beyond oil-based transformer diagnostics if field results show that Talos catches actionable faults earlier than the incumbent’s existing products. The memo leaves that incremental benefit unmeasured. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

Talos reported deployments across 40+ assets and partnerships with utilities, plant owners, and national laboratories. That footprint gives Talos a plausible starting point for a paid fleet trial measuring confirmed faults, warning lead time, and false alarms. Trial funding, deployment costs, and fleet procurement commitments remain unverified. Any acquisition would also depend on diligence into field-data reuse rights and contract consents. The memo provides no evidence of an eligible secondary or another independent payout route. (Market & Traction, Defensibility, Key Risks, Key Facts)

The benchmark assumes a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, 5% ownership diluted to 3%, and no follow-ons. A 5× gross cash return within eight years requires $2.5 million to the holder, or about $83.3 million in distributable equity at 3%. The sale cases below assume a full cash sale within eight years, holder eligibility for the full stake, $10 million in combined debt and deal adjustments, and a pro rata waterfall with no further senior claims. All sale values and adjustments are illustrative. The memo provides neither a disclosed price for the adjacent deal nor Talos revenue to anchor a valuation. (Market & Traction, Key Facts)

| Scenario | Proceeds bridge | Holder cash |
|---|---|---|
| Modest sale | $50M enterprise value less $10M yields $40M distributable equity | $1.2M (2.4×) |
| Strong sale | $100M enterprise value less $10M yields $90M distributable equity | $2.7M (5.4×) |
| Prolonged private | Cash is reinvested, with no distribution or eligible share purchase within eight years | $0 (0×) |

The strong case clears the hurdle by $200,000. At 2.5% ownership, the same sale yields $2.25 million, below the objective.

### Countercase

An incumbent could add external sensors to its existing platform and sell through its established channel. Siemens Energy’s agreement to acquire Camlin gives that possibility weight. Talos’s reported deployments leave open whether its alerts improve on incumbent monitoring enough to justify buying the company or whether utilities will pay for fleet-wide use. This countercase supports Watch and falls short of establishing either a structural block or materially inadequate proceeds under the strongest credible route. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)
