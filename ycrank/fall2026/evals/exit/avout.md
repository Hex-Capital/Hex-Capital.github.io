# Avout -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Strategic acquisition is the strongest path.** An equipment lender could buy Avout to gain control of a borrower origination channel and an underwriting workflow that uses hardware companies’ operating data. The memo documents that Trinity Capital and GPU Capital already serve parts of this market. Avout says it uses borrower operating data to underwrite loans. The acquisition rationale is an inference. If Avout can link that data to repayment and collateral recoveries across repeat financings, ownership could give a lender better loan selection or access to borrowers it would otherwise miss. The memo provides no measured advantage or evidence of buyer interest. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction)

Avout reports helping arrange a $1.5 million term sheet for a data-center company. Closing and disbursement are undisclosed. The founders’ experience in real-asset investing and regulated financial infrastructure supports a plausible path to a funded loan cohort with measurable underwriting results. Reaching that milestone depends on repeat commitments from third-party lenders, and the memo gives no operating budget or runway. Rights to transfer the underwriting workflow, borrower data, and contracts are unverified. The memo supplies no independently supported route to cash for this holder through a sponsor buyout, secondary sale, IPO, or company-funded payout. (Market & Traction, Founders & Team, Key Risks, Key Facts)

**Illustrative proceeds bridge.** Assume a single $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, with 5% initial ownership diluted to 3% and no follow-on checks. The eight-year objective is $2.5 million in gross cash. In the sale scenarios below, assume zero applicable net debt, transaction costs, or other equity adjustments, and a simple pro rata waterfall with no senior preferences. Enterprise value therefore equals distributable equity value. All consideration is paid in cash at closing, with zero stock, escrow, earnouts, rollover, or compensation. These prices are scenario inputs. The memo offers no usable valuation evidence, and the reported $1.5 million term sheet concerns financing for Avout’s borrower. (Market & Traction, Key Facts)

| Scenario | Timing and assumed outcome | Gross holder cash | Gross multiple |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Modest sale | Year 6 at $30 million enterprise value | $900,000 | 1.8× |
| Strong sale | Year 8 at $100 million enterprise value | $3 million | 6.0× |
| Prolonged private | Through year 8, growth plateaus, buyers stay away, public scale remains out of reach, and cash is reinvested. The holder retains the shares. | $0 | 0.0× |

The hurdle is $83.3 million in distributable equity at 3% ownership, $125 million at 2%, and $62.5 million at 4%. The strong scenario clears the objective at 3% and falls short at 2%, where it returns $2 million.

### Countercase

Equipment lenders already underwrite hardware against appraisals and customer contracts. They could keep underwriting in-house and use Avout as a referral partner. That could cap what a buyer would pay unless Avout demonstrates that ownership secures borrower access or underwriting gains that a partnership cannot deliver. The memo establishes that those alternatives exist and leaves their relative economics untested. This blocks outreach today. The Pass override remains untriggered because the evidence establishes neither a binding price ceiling nor a probable eight-year outcome with no credible route to holder cash. (Defensibility, Market & Traction)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a repeat cohort of funded Avout loans show that owning its origination and underwriting capability creates enough incremental value for an equipment lender to support the required purchase price? Move to Reach out if lender-verified origination, repayment, and recovery results against the lender’s existing channels show gains that depend on ownership and could support roughly $83.3 million in distributable equity under the base case. Stay on Watch while those economics remain unmeasured. If lenders can capture the same gains through referrals at a cost that caps the acquisition price materially below the hurdle, move to Pass.
