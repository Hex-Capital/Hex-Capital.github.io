# FlashDrive -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

The strongest path is a potential scarce complement for AI agents: reliable shared files across laptops, cloud sandboxes, and CI when multiple agents write at once. FlashDrive says teams stitch together Git, S3, and sync scripts, and that its macOS and Linux mounts and MCP access can merge concurrent Markdown and CSV edits or preserve both versions. If more parallel agents make conflicting writes a recurring source of rework, reliable cross-environment handoffs could become more valuable. The memo describes the mechanism. Its reliability under representative workloads and its scarcity remain unverified. (The Idea; Market & Traction; Key Risks)

The launch drew 28,040 post views in two days. That measures attention, and buyer commitments remain unverified. The founders previously built the Master iOS app together and launched other products at Evokoa, supporting early-stage execution credit. The next execution gap is production correctness during interrupted writes and across additional file formats. (Market & Traction; Founders & Team; Key Risks)

A plausible expansion unit is one paid workspace per month. In that scenario, subscription revenue needs to cover storage, request processing, egress, and support. The company’s reported on-demand streaming could reduce bytes transferred, while its announced free egress leaves FlashDrive exposed to heavy usage. Paid pricing and costs at representative usage remain unmeasured. (The Idea; Key Facts)

### Countercase

AWS and Daytona already offer shared storage inside their agent environments, and Layven offers shared MCP storage with attributed versions and rollback. Those alternatives defeat the scarcity case for basic shared storage. FlashDrive’s reported merge behavior is a specific point of differentiation, but the proposed switching costs from shared paths and file history remain an inference. Whether bundled storage and version recovery suffice for mixed-environment teams remains testable, which supports Watch. The memo also leaves a separate New Economies path unsupported because it identifies neither a previously excluded buyer nor a measured reduction in labor per successful outcome. (Defensibility; Market & Traction; Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Will independent teams running concurrent agent writes across local and cloud environments renew paid FlashDrive deployments after comparing them with their best available storage alternative? Reach out if multiple teams renew at a price that covers measured workspace costs and their trials show fewer failed handoffs or less manual recovery. Move to Pass if the alternatives perform comparably and teams decline to pay. (The Idea; Defensibility; Key Risks)
