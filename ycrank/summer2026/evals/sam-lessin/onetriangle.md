# OneTriangle -- Sam Lessin Evaluation

I write down three blanks: post-money valuation, competing-term-sheet count, and whether this is genuinely the first scarce check. YC affiliation is not evidence that the round is auctioned, but it is not evidence that the belief remains cheap either. With no financing terms or investor-competition data, I cannot underwrite the deal before I even reach the technology.

The company does have a real technical question: can a 1,048,576-parameter correction transfer KV state from a smaller model into a larger model cheaply enough to matter? OneTriangle has moved that question beyond a slide. At 8,192 tokens it reports 82.52% top-token agreement and a 1.25× end-to-end speedup when source prefill is counted. But a research result is not yet a capital-funded venture moment. The dossier names no use of proceeds, dated milestone, or pass/fail result that this round buys; “hire and commercialize” would merely finance motion.

The bull case is sharper than the traction suggests. Long-context agents consume expensive prefill, customers can supposedly switch with one configuration line, and inference infrastructure is forecast to grow rapidly. If OneTriangle can preserve task quality while making heterogeneous model caches portable, it becomes a useful translation layer rather than another model host. Medha Venkatapathy’s LLM-optimization research and the team’s working handoff mechanism are meaningful pre-seed technical receipts.

The problem is that the published boundary contains the bear case. The 7.91× headline assumes a resident source cache; the cold-context advantage falls to 1.25×, native prefill wins at short contexts, and the study did not test open-ended generation, task accuracy, or human preference. Ordinary-prefill fallback protects customers from bad outputs while giving back the cost advantage. That is an engineering tradeoff, not yet a business with a controllable cash metric.

I also cannot find the dark pool. Five Launch YC votes, 488 company followers, and founder reach have not demonstrably caused a purchase, pilot, or retained workload. Upstreaming into vLLM may accelerate adoption, but it also teaches Fireworks, Together AI, Baseten, and open-source contributors how to collapse the differentiation. Pricing as low as $0.15 per million DeepSeek V4 Flash input tokens proves that a SKU exists; without paid volume, gross margin, GPU cost, burn, or runway, it does not prove that OneTriangle can survive without the next round.

The founders have shipped a technical prototype and previously launched TrustAI’s governance product, but the company pivoted from governance to inference within roughly one month. That could be fast learning; it could also mean the team is chasing successive AI surfaces before owning either workflow. Their academic and advisory access is credible, yet the dossier does not show that they have operated production inference infrastructure or purchased it at scale. The known-exception path would require overwhelming external validation, and there are no enterprise customers, retention data, acquisition offers, or production deployments here to provide it.

I pass today. My verifiable flip condition is three paying production customers sustaining at least a 1.2× end-to-end improvement at 95% or better task-quality parity, with positive contribution margin, for 90 days. That would show that the cache-transfer mechanism causes purchases and survives contact with real workloads; until then, I am being asked to finance an open technical result inside a brutally capitalized market without knowing the strike price.
