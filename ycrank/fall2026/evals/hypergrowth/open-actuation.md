# Open Actuation -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Medium

### Company case

The strongest path is **repeatable direct sales** to drone OEMs that need custom motors for ongoing production. The memo describes a shaft modification for which the incumbent quoted a $50,000 minimum order and a 13-week lead time. Open Actuation says it built the modified motor in five weeks and that the OEM found it outperformed the alternatives it tested. That reported comparison supports early fit. The company also reports fulfilling volume orders after the build, which shows a move from testing toward production. Payment, reorder cadence, and sustained expansion remain unverified. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction)

The recurring job could travel to drone OEMs in other markets if the same design and production process meets their sourcing and qualification requirements. The memo identifies a specification form as an intake route, but gives no customer record tracing that route through payment and production. Even with strong buyer fit, the first link likely to break is the handoff from a new specification to a qualified production run. The five-week build measures elapsed time. Engineering and qualification hours, yield, and contribution at the custom order price remain unknown. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Countercase

The shaft build may have been a one-off win. If each new design requires fresh engineering and qualification, those costs could rise in proportion to sales even when an existing customer orders more units. The memo covers one timed build and one reported production ramp, and it identifies domestic sourcing constraints for magnets and silicon steel. The structural downside gate does not trigger because proportional costs remain a risk rather than an observed pattern. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a second independent drone OEM go from specification intake to a paid, qualified production run through a process another operator can execute? An order and a production record showing reuse of tooling or test procedures, bounded engineering and qualification hours, and positive contribution after those costs would move the action to Reach out.
