## The Idea

- **Problem:** Enterprise engineering teams need to identify exploitable vulnerabilities before releases, architecture changes, or breaches; NebuSec offers continuous monitoring alongside the alternative of a fixed-scope security audit ([NebuSec audits](https://nebusec.ai/security-audit/)).
- **Approach:** Nebu builds threat models, scans repositories and pull requests, verifies findings, and generates patches, with researcher-led investigation available for audits ([product](https://nebusec.ai/product/code-security/), [audits](https://nebusec.ai/security-audit/)).
- **Differentiation:** NebuSec reports maximum coverage of 88%, versus Codex Security’s 80% and Claude Security’s 74%, in its own benchmark of 76 known vulnerabilities; these are vendor-reported results ([NebuSec benchmark](https://nebusec.ai/research/nebu-benchmark/), September 2026).
- **Business Model:** Documentation lists Researcher pay-as-you-go, Developer, Business, and Enterprise plans, with a fixed quote approved before each scan; public dollar prices: No public data found ([onboarding](https://nebusec.ai/help/getting-started/onboarding/), [scan billing](https://nebusec.ai/help/code-security/start-a-scan/), September 2026).
- **TAM/SAM:** The broader application-security market is forecast to reach $42.09B by 2033, growing at 18.8% CAGR during 2026–2033; NebuSec-specific SAM: No public data found ([Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/press-release/global-application-security-market), January 2026).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Public acquisition channels include demo booking, technical research publications, GitHub exploit releases, and conference outreach ([website](https://nebusec.ai/), [GitHub](https://github.com/NebuSec/CyberMeowfia), [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/nebula-security)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** The team reports $400K+ in Google vulnerability rewards and publishes working exploit code, documenting vulnerability-research expertise without establishing exclusive IP ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/nebusec), [GitHub](https://github.com/NebuSec/CyberMeowfia)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Accumulated exploit-validation cases could improve detection workflows, but public evidence does not establish an exclusive training dataset or customer-data rights.
- **Market structure:** [Inferred]: No structural barrier identified at this stage, given Semgrep’s existing combination of static analysis, LLM reasoning, and automated remediation ([Semgrep](https://semgrep.dev/blog/2025/series-d-announcement/), February 2025).
- **Commoditization risk:** [Inferred]: Aikido can compete across overlapping detection, exploit-validation, and remediation workflows through its existing code, cloud, runtime, and pentesting products ([Aikido](https://www.aikido.dev/blog/aikido-funding-series-b), January 2026).

## Key Risks

- **Technical generalization:** [Inferred]: Results on 76 known bugs do not establish performance on unseen customer repositories; dynamic exploit verification is available but disabled by default ([benchmark](https://nebusec.ai/research/nebu-benchmark/), [onboarding](https://nebusec.ai/help/getting-started/onboarding/)).
- **Product availability:** Cloud and supply-chain security remain marked “coming soon,” limiting currently documented coverage relative to the broader platform positioning ([NebuSec](https://nebusec.ai/), October 2026).
- **Enterprise assurance:** [Inferred]: Source-code access can trigger procurement requirements that delay deployment; NebuSec states SOC 2 readiness is underway without a completed report and offers architecture and controls reviews ([security disclosure](https://nebusec.ai/security/), September 2026).
