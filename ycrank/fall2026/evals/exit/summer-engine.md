# Summer Engine -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Summer Engine’s strongest route is a strategic sale to a game-creation or distribution platform. The memo documents a Godot-compatible editor that connects creation to hosted multiplayer and publishing. Summer reports more than 240,000 reusable community assets. A buyer could use that combination to bring in creators and increase publishing on its platform. That benefit is an inference. Repeat-publishing and marketplace retention data are unavailable. (The Idea, Defensibility)

The milestone is to show that successive creator cohorts publish again and remain paying customers. The memo records 2,966 weekly MCP and CLI downloads, while Summer reports more than 60,000 organically acquired users and 40% revenue growth over 30 days. The live product and paid hosted-AI tiers give Summer a way to measure those cohorts. A 12-month test is a planning assumption, conditional on runway and the cost of serving paid users, both unquantified in the memo. (Market & Traction, The Idea)

The shared benchmark assumes a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, with 5% ownership diluted to 3% and no follow-ons. The eight-year objective is $2.5 million in gross cash, which requires about $83.3 million in distributable equity at 3%. The sale cases are illustrative sensitivities. Both assume closing within eight years, $5 million in net debt and deal deductions, full holder eligibility, and an all-cash sale with the remaining equity proceeds distributed pro rata at closing. Summer’s debt, preference stack, and transfer terms are unknown.

- **Modest sale.** A $50 million enterprise value leaves $45 million to distribute, yielding $1.35 million to the holder, or 2.7×.
- **Strong sale.** A $100 million enterprise value leaves $95 million to distribute, yielding $2.85 million, or 5.7×. At 2% ownership after further dilution, the same sale yields $1.9 million, or 3.8×.
- **Prolonged private.** If Summer keeps operating and reinvests available cash, the holder realizes $0 within eight years. The memo provides no evidence of a funded payout or eligible secondary.

### Countercase

Unity and Roblox already connect coding agents to their editors, and Summer’s MCP and CLI repository is MIT-licensed. A strategic buyer could reproduce much of the authoring workflow or improve its own tools. The acquisition case therefore depends on whether Summer’s creator community and publishing activity are valuable enough to buy. The memo supports feature substitution, while a structural block to a sale and a price ceiling below the holder hurdle both remain unestablished. The Pass gate does not apply. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

What would a game platform pay to acquire Summer’s creator and publishing business once repeat publishing and paid retention are demonstrated? Verified cohort data paired with a buyer’s build-versus-buy analysis supporting a price range that clears the $2.5 million holder hurdle after dilution and deal deductions would move the action from Watch to Reach out.
