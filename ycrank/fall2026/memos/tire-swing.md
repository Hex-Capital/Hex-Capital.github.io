## The Idea

- **Problem:** Affordable housing operators coordinate income verification, eligibility checks, forms and signatures through calls, emails, spreadsheets and office visits, and delays can leave units vacant while households wait ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/XTS-tire-swing-the-ai-platform-for-affordable-housing)).
- **Approach:** Tire Swing extracts income and asset data from tenant documents, applies property and program rules, flags missing or conflicting evidence, and assembles certifications for staff approval with links to the supporting documents ([Tire Swing platform](https://www.gettireswing.com/)).
- **Differentiation:** Tire Swing works with operators’ existing forms and review workflows, Yardi Voyager bundles certification with property accounting, and Roost pairs AI certification with its own renter marketplace ([Tire Swing](https://www.gettireswing.com/), [Yardi Voyager](https://www.yardi.com/resources/voyager-affordable-housing-brochure/pdf/), [Roost](https://partnerships.roostaffordable.com/)).
- **Business Model:** Tire Swing sells auto-renewing subscriptions and sets fees and billing cycles in customer order forms or invoices ([Terms of Service, Oct 2026](https://www.gettireswing.com/terms_of_service)).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** The founders solicit introductions to property operators and housing authorities, announced sponsorship of AHF Live, and offer demos using an anonymized file from a prospect’s portfolio ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/XTS-tire-swing-the-ai-platform-for-affordable-housing), [Paul Witten, LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/paul-witten_thrilled-to-be-sponsoring-and-attending-ahf-activity-7395231036256989184-LuqX), [Tire Swing Contact](https://www.gettireswing.com/contact)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Tire Swing reports a library of thousands of deterministic and AI-assisted checks, with support for property policies and state-specific requirements ([Launch YC, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SBA-tire-swing-ai-compliance-for-affordable-housing), [Tire Swing](https://www.gettireswing.com/)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Customer-specific rules, forms and review histories could raise switching costs as portfolios accumulate records, with no public retention data to test that effect ([Tire Swing platform](https://www.gettireswing.com/)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** Roost already markets AI income calculations linked to source documents, and ExactEstate offers document tampering checks inside its property management suite ([Roost](https://partnerships.roostaffordable.com/), [ExactEstate](https://www.exactestate.com/product)).

## Key Risks

- **Certification accuracy:** Incorrect extraction of income or asset data can affect eligibility or subsidy calculations, and Tire Swing requires staff to review the source evidence and approve each certification ([Tire Swing](https://www.gettireswing.com/), [HUD HOTMA FAQ](https://www.hud.gov/sites/default/files/PIH/documents/HOTMA-Implementation-FAQs-for-PHAs.pdf)).
- **Tenant data exposure:** Third-party AI and OCR providers process files that can contain Social Security numbers and bank statements, and Tire Swing cites contractual safeguards, encryption, access controls and SOC 2 Type II certification ([Privacy Policy](https://www.gettireswing.com/privacy_policy), [Security](https://www.gettireswing.com/security)).
- **Rule maintenance:** Different HOTMA deadlines for most housing authorities and multifamily PBRA owners require separate rule configurations, and Tire Swing says it supports program- and property-specific rules ([HUD housing authority FAQ](https://www.hud.gov/sites/default/files/PIH/documents/HOTMA-Implementation-FAQs-for-PHAs.pdf), [HUD multifamily extension](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USHUDFHA/bulletins/4316657), [Tire Swing](https://www.gettireswing.com/)).
- **Record attribution:** The YC directory lists Fall 2026 while Witten’s acceptance announcement cites Winter 2025, and a funding database uses the domain of Tire Swing Collective, a separate foster-care nonprofit ([YC directory](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/tire-swing), [Witten on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/paul-witten_big-professional-update-ahead-tire-swing-activity-7298080856584146944-YQCs), [Who Raised What](https://whoraisedwhat.com/c/tireswingcollective-org), [Tire Swing Collective](https://www.tireswingcollective.org/who-we-are)).
