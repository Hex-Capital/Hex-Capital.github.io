# AnyAlpha -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Assuming strong product-market fit with retail prediction-market traders, AnyAlpha’s strongest path is to turn a recurring job into a self-serve mobile workflow. Traders can express a thesis in plain language, backtest it, and link an existing Kalshi account for automated execution. The memo documents website and app-store distribution and paper trading for supported strategies. The strategy and backtesting workflow appears reusable across eligible venues. The work needed to support another venue and the path from a free download to paid live use remain unclear. (The Idea, Key Risks)

The two-founder team launched the trading product on October 26. AnyAlpha reports more than 1,000 active traders and over $1 million in weekly trading volume, with weekly volume doubling through the November 11 update. That is a fast reported adoption signal. Revenue and billable volume for the current product remain unknown, so initial traction takes the absent-evidence default. Saved strategies, backtests, and execution histories give traders a reason to return. The disclosed subscriptions belong to the earlier BetAlpha product, leaving paid retention and same-account expansion unverified. (Founders & Team, Market & Traction, Defensibility, Key Facts)

If AnyAlpha charges a recurring fee, contribution per added paid trader would be that fee minus attributable acquisition, onboarding, support, backtesting, and continuous monitoring costs. Trade-light accounts could still consume monitoring resources. The memo provides neither current-product pricing nor operating inputs sufficient to estimate contribution, so cost economics takes the absent-evidence default. (The Idea, Key Facts)

### Countercase

Live execution depends on exchange permissions and available contracts. The memo documents Kalshi account linking and describes listing restrictions that can narrow what traders can automate. If each new market requires bespoke venue integrations and regulatory work, the reusable strategy workflow may have limited reach. The structural gate remains open because the memo leaves the degree of required adaptation unresolved and gives no basis to conclude that serving additional traders is economically infeasible. (Key Risks, The Idea)

### Decisive diligence question

Can AnyAlpha grow a cohort of retail traders in a second market who independently activate, pay, and renew on the same app? A live cohort on a second venue, supported by a bounded integration and showing positive contribution after attributable acquisition and service costs, would move the action to Reach out. Until then, Watch.
