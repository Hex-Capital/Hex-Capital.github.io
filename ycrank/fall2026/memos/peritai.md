## The Idea

- **Problem:** AI labs’ speech models misinterpret transaction-critical details and interruptions that clean speech corpora and general crowd raters miss, according to [Perit About, accessed Oct 2026](https://perit.ai/about).
- **Approach:** Perit recruits sector practitioners to collect, transcribe, align, and grade recordings through its Foundry workspace, and offers live caller simulations for voice-model evaluation ([Perit Annotation](https://perit.ai/services/data-annotation), [RL Environments](https://perit.ai/rl-environments)).
- **Differentiation:** Perit emphasizes operator-authored speech data and live voice environments, versus Mercor’s cross-domain expert matching and Turing’s engineering/STEM data and enterprise AI programs ([Perit](https://perit.ai/rl-environments), [Mercor](https://www.mercor.com/blog/series-c/), [Turing](https://www.turing.com/blog/turing-raises-111m-to-accelerate-the-future-of-agi)).
- **Business Model:** Custom program quotes depend on locale, annotation depth, turnaround, exclusivity, and recording equipment, with contributor compensation separately itemized and non-exclusive licensing the default ([Perit Pricing, Oct 2026](https://perit.ai/pricing)).
- **TAM/SAM:** The broader data-labeling solutions/services market is estimated at $27.6B in 2026, with 20.3% CAGR during 2025–2030; no public estimate isolates Perit’s expert-data/RL segment ([Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/data-labeling-solution-services-market-report)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Buyers submit model failures for scoping, pilot delivery, and continuing data deliveries, while public benchmarks and downloadable datasets expose the product to researchers ([Perit Request Data](https://perit.ai/request-data), [Datasets](https://perit.ai/datasets)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Operational assets include a company-reported bench of 650 transcribers/aligners and project calibration gates, without public evidence of exclusive contributor access ([Perit Annotation, Oct 2026](https://perit.ai/services/data-annotation)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Accumulated sector rubrics and reviewer histories could increase replication effort, although customer switching costs remain undocumented ([Perit Method](https://perit.ai/research/method)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** [Inferred]: Mercor and Turing could extend their existing expert networks and evaluation programs into Perit’s speech workflows, requiring recruitment and calibration rather than a publicly identified exclusive technology ([Mercor](https://www.mercor.com/blog/series-c/), [Turing](https://www.turing.com/blog/turing-raises-111m-to-accelerate-the-future-of-agi)).

- **Customer concentration:** [Inferred]: Reliance on six disclosed-but-unnamed lab customers could expose revenue to individual procurement changes; contract concentration and renewal terms are unavailable ([CEO announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kshitijdixit_i-am-excited-to-share-that-perit-ai-yc-f26-activity-7502597870538366976-3u6g)).
