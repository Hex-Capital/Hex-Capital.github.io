## The Idea

- **Problem:** Prolonged screen work can cause eye fatigue, irritation, and blurred vision, which users manage with measures such as screen breaks ([Experimental Eye Research, 2025](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40466853/), [Scientific Reports, 2026](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-22510-4)).
- **Approach:** Inward-facing sensors track blinks, squints, and gaze to estimate fatigue and cognitive load, and the glasses can tint automatically, prompt breaks, and adjust connected screens ([founder launch post, Nov 2026](https://xitter.gq/sochak03/status/2119838587512574443), [STASIS checkout, via search snippet](https://www.wearstasis.com/checkout)).
- **Differentiation:** STASIS combines eye-fatigue feedback with automatic tint and screen adjustments, while Luchs focuses on cognitive-state tracking and Alibaba’s Qwen N1 Pro uses gaze for object interaction and iris payments ([STASIS checkout, via search snippet](https://www.wearstasis.com/checkout), [Luchs Eyewear](https://luchseyewear.com/), [Alibaba Cloud, Sep 2026](https://www.alibabacloud.com/blog/alibaba-unveils-agentic-computer-ai-wearables-and-more-at-2026-apsara-conference_603599)).
- **Business Model:** The checkout lists a refundable $99 deposit toward the $299 first-batch price, a $399 regular price, prescription lenses included, a free app tier, and optional Premium at $10/month after a free first month ([STASIS checkout, via search snippet](https://www.wearstasis.com/checkout)).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment, with the broader global smart-glasses market estimated at $3.2 billion in 2026 and projected to grow at 24.2% CAGR through 2033 ([Grand View Research, 2026](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/smart-glasses-market-report)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** The founder’s launch post directs consumers to a branded reservation checkout that offers HSA/FSA payment, and Chakraborty reported discussions with Kaiser Optometry and eyewear brands ([founder launch post, Nov 2026](https://xitter.gq/sochak03/status/2119838587512574443), [STASIS checkout, via search snippet](https://www.wearstasis.com/checkout), [founder LinkedIn, via search snippet](https://www.linkedin.com/in/soham-ch)). ## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** STASIS reports training its models on 550 billion eye-data points, with data ownership and exclusivity undisclosed ([El Nacional, Nov 2026](https://www.elnacional.cat/ca/tecnologia/ni-cameres-ni-fotos-stasis-son-ulleres-intelligents-analitzen-teus-ulls-mesurar-fatiga-concentracio_1723517_102.html)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Longitudinal wear data paired with labeled fatigue outcomes could improve personalization, with dataset rights and incremental model performance still unverified ([El Nacional, Nov 2026](https://www.elnacional.cat/ca/tecnologia/ni-cameres-ni-fotos-stasis-son-ulleres-intelligents-analitzen-teus-ulls-mesurar-fatiga-concentracio_1723517_102.html)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** [Inferred]: Eyewear makers can source sensing components and prototype individual features using published designs, as Himax supplies a processor with inward-tracking support and Penn’s BlinkWise project publishes blink-tracking code and data ([Himax, Nov 2026](https://www.himax.com.tw/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/3Q26-EC-Transcript-Himax-Final.pdf), [University of Pennsylvania, 2025](https://waves.seas.upenn.edu/projects/blinkwise/)).

## Key Risks

- **Measurement validity:** STASIS reports over 90% accuracy for fatigue and cognitive load without publishing its reference measure or validation protocol, and field research on other optical trackers found sunlight and movement degraded blink detection ([El Nacional, Nov 2026](https://www.elnacional.cat/ca/tecnologia/ni-cameres-ni-fotos-stasis-son-ulleres-intelligents-analitzen-teus-ulls-mesurar-fatiga-concentracio_1723517_102.html), [Military Medicine, 2022](https://academic.oup.com/milmed/article/187/3-4/e404/6132015)).
- **Reservation conversion:** Customers can cancel and recover the deposit before shipment, and the balance comes due just before the scheduled March 2027 shipment ([STASIS checkout, via search snippet](https://www.wearstasis.com/checkout)).
- **Substitution:** A controlled study reported improvements in dry-eye symptoms from smartphone-based blink training, giving screen users a software-only option for one of the problems STASIS targets ([npj Digital Medicine, 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-025-02053-8), [STASIS checkout, via search snippet](https://www.wearstasis.com/checkout)).
- **Name confusion:** FDA clearance K183634 belongs to the unrelated Stasis Labs hospital monitoring system, while STASIS markets its eyewear for wellness ([FDA 510(k) database](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpmn/pmn.cfm?ID=K183634), [STASIS checkout, via search snippet](https://www.wearstasis.com/checkout)).
