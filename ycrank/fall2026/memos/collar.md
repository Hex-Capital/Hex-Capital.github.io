## The Idea

- **Problem:** Collar says large public companies spend two-thirds of fundraising time compiling figures and preparing for investor meetings, with some outsourcing that work to investor-relations consultants charging $15,000 per month ([Collar description in YC directory, accessed Nov 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/industry/generative-ai/san-francisco)).
- **Approach:** Collar matches investor history against fund mandates and holdings, flags conflicting figures across company documents, and surfaces citation-backed talking points from approved materials during live calls ([Collar product page](https://www.usecollarai.com/product.html), [YC launch announcement on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/collarai)).
- **Differentiation:** Collar centers on in-call prompts from approved sources, while Q4 combines IR CRM with websites and events, Irwin emphasizes shareholder monitoring and targeting, and Notified combines earnings preparation with webcasting and disclosure distribution ([Collar](https://www.usecollarai.com/product.html), [Q4](https://www.q4inc.com/platform/platform-features/investor-relations), [Irwin](https://www.getirwin.com/), [Notified](https://www.notified.com/blog/ir-hub-what-it-is-how-it-works)).
- **Business Model:** Collar advertises a pilot with a refund guarantee for public-company IR and publishes no current rate, while its prior private-market deal platform was priced at US$1,000 per month after a free month ([Collar software case study](https://www.usecollarai.com/case-studies/software), [The Straits Times, Aug 2026](https://www.straitstimes.com/world/united-states/from-little-red-dot-to-silicon-valley-singapore-start-up-by-poly-students-makes-it-to-y-combinator)).
- **TAM/SAM:** The Insight Partners estimates the broader global investor relationship management software market at US$1.24 billion in 2025, with 9.65% forecast CAGR for 2026–2034, and no public SAM estimate was found for Collar’s public-company workflow ([The Insight Partners, Apr 2026](https://www.theinsightpartners.com/reports/investor-relationship-management-software-market)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Collar offers prospects a ticker-based investor-targeting preview and a bookable demo ([YC launch announcement on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/collarai), [Collar website](https://www.usecollarai.com/)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Collar reports mapping 12,000+ portfolio managers and matching them against each issuer’s own investor history ([Collar About](https://www.usecollarai.com/about.html), [Collar product page](https://www.usecollarai.com/product.html)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: A customer’s accumulated meeting history and approved Q&A could raise switching costs if teams rely on them each earnings cycle, but public retention or migration data were unavailable ([Collar product page](https://www.usecollarai.com/product.html)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** Q4 launched an AI-native IR CRM with automated interaction capture, and Notified added market intelligence to its earnings-preparation workflow ([Q4, Apr 2026](https://www.q4inc.com/resource-center/newsroom/q4-launches-ai-native-crm-transforming-how-investor-relationships-are-managed), [Notified, Mar 2026](https://newsroom.notified.com/news/402780/notified-adds-market-intelligence-to-ir-hub-m)).

## Key Risks

- **Pipeline conversion:** Collar describes $1.4M of deals as pending compliance, with no disclosed conversion rate ([Collar description in YC directory, accessed Nov 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/industry/generative-ai/san-francisco)).
- **Selective disclosure:** [Inferred]: Live prompts during private meetings could surface material nonpublic information covered by Regulation FD, and Collar says its teleprompter draws on approved materials ([SEC Regulation FD rule](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2000/08/selective-disclosure-insider-trading), [Collar product page](https://www.usecollarai.com/product.html)).
- **Live-answer accuracy:** [Inferred]: A wrong or delayed prompt can reach an executive mid-call, and Collar describes source citations and rehearsal checks without publishing live accuracy or latency benchmarks ([Collar product page](https://www.usecollarai.com/product.html)).
- **Incumbent substitution:** [Inferred]: Q4’s AI CRM and Notified’s earnings tools may cover enough of an existing customer’s workflow to limit demand for a separate purchase ([Q4, Apr 2026](https://www.q4inc.com/resource-center/newsroom/q4-launches-ai-native-crm-transforming-how-investor-relationships-are-managed), [Notified, Mar 2026](https://newsroom.notified.com/news/402780/notified-adds-market-intelligence-to-ir-hub-m)).
- **Name collision:** [Inferred]: Searches for Collar can surface the unrelated Collar Ltd. pet app, complicating app-store attribution ([Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/collar/id1535869072)).
