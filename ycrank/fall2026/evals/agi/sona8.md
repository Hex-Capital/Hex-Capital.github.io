# Sona8 -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Medium

### Company case

Sona8 has an early industry-enabled path in recurring workflow mapping across sites. Consulting and transformation teams use sampled interviews and existing documents, which can miss local workarounds and leave maps stale. Sona8’s adaptive multilingual interviews, task demonstrations, and automated synthesis could reduce the interviewer and analyst time needed per validated map. The seed-stage assumption is that those savings make repeat mapping viable at sites that manual discovery would cover only once or skip. The customer testimonial describes s.Oliver’s CEO office using Sona8 to surface differences in teams’ planning requirements. Sona8 separately reports more than 10,000 interviews. These signals support early use and collection volume. The memo gives no comparable cost per validated map or evidence of paid repeat use at a newly viable cadence. (The Idea; Market & Traction)

Under model parity, customers would still need to recruit employees, secure participation, capture task demonstrations, and validate exceptions before using the maps. That work could sustain demand for a delivered product. A growing process history could make switching costly if customers reuse it across projects, but the memo provides no evidence of that behavior, and Sona8’s data policy restricts interview content to serving the customer that supplied it. The building-block path scores zero because the memo identifies no scarce input that Sona8 supplies to a specific AI workload. Its employee interviews support enterprise transformation, and the memo gives no evidence that a shortage of this context constrains AI deployment. (The Idea; Defensibility; Key Risks)

The s.Oliver testimonial and Sona8’s announcement of a signed consultancy show early buyer use, with payment and repeat demand unverified. The founders’ experience conducting manual discovery and delivering enterprise AI, together with the workflow-mapping launch, supports seed-stage execution. Dependable exception handling remains unverified. The proposed platform and usage fees establish a pricing structure. The memo leaves the costs of interviews, review, and rollout open, so scale economics stays at the default. (The Idea; Market & Traction; Founders & Team; Key Risks)

### Countercase

Sona8 may make existing consulting engagements faster while leaving the buyer, coverage, and refresh cadence unchanged. The named customer use fits an existing process-mapping workflow. The memo also identifies a competitor offering interview-generated maps and Model Context Protocol access. Employee participation and review of rare exceptions could absorb enough of the labor savings to keep broader coverage uneconomic. That is the decisive, testable gap behind Watch. Evidence that customers use Sona8 only to save time on discovery they already fund would put both paths at zero and trigger Pass. (Market & Traction; Defensibility; Key Risks)
