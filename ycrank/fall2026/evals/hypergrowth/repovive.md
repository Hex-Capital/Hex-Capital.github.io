# Repovive -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Repeatable direct sales** is Repovive’s strongest path. The memo documents a route to AI lab post-training teams through introductions and an offer of sample Lean tasks. The recurring job is training and evaluating models on fresh formal-reasoning tasks. The packaged environment gives labs private tasks and machine-checked reference proofs in a common format that could carry across markets. That portability is an inference from the product. Buyer locations and integration effort remain unknown. (The Idea)

Repovive reports $100K in signed contracts covering two formalization environments. That is meaningful early budget commitment, while the separate $200K coding-data letter of intent leaves commitment to the core product unproven beyond those contracts. The memo does not establish whether the two environments went to independent labs or reached paid use. Even assuming strong fit, the first link likely to break is the handoff from sample to live deployment. The memo gives no account of a buyer completing that route, how much work the rollout took, or whether buyers returned for additional paid environments. (The Idea, Market & Traction)

### Countercase

Labs could buy a bespoke environment once and then build subsequent environments internally or use open alternatives. The memo identifies both in-house lab verification work and open Lean training resources. If each sale also requires fresh task authoring, proof checking, and lab-specific integration, delivery costs could rise in proportion to revenue. Repovive reports a competition community that could help replenish tasks, but the memo leaves contributor participation and delivery costs unmeasured. The structural downside gate is unmet because proportional delivery effort remains a hypothesis. (Defensibility, Key Risks, Market & Traction)
