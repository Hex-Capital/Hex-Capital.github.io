# Light Anchor -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Medium

### Company case

Light Anchor has an early industry-enabled path in managed US growth for brands that leave local creative, acquisition, and storefront work unstaffed. The memo records that Light Anchor took on this work for Anuko. The company describes using agents for review research, creative production, and shopper simulation, with live campaign results guiding decisions. The seed-stage hypothesis is that automating enough recurring work lets Light Anchor serve brands that could not justify a local hire or agency retainer. Comparable models could generate creative, while the delivered service still requires running campaigns in client accounts and responding to actual purchases. The memo leaves open whether staffing cost kept Anuko from doing this work earlier and how much labor the agents saved. (The Idea, Key Risks)

The memo lists seven brands on Light Anchor’s case-study page. Anuko’s testimonial reports daily Amazon sales reaching 54× its June baseline in one week and sales of 3.07× ad spend over October 1–7. That supports actual buyer use. Incremental sales, fees collected, and repeat paid demand remain unverified. The founders’ prior agent deployments at Sendbird support execution at seed. Light Anchor claims its agents reuse operating records across brands, a potential advantage whose effect on results and retention remains unverified. (Market & Traction, Founders & Team, Defensibility)

At the quoted rate, each $100 in tag-attributed sales would yield $5–$15 in fees, assuming the client pays as quoted. Clients pay ad spend directly. The memo gives no per-account costs for human oversight, model usage, or exception handling, so contribution on the next account remains unknown. The building-blocks path scores zero because the business uses AI to deliver commerce services and supplies no scarce input to AI deployment. (The Idea, Key Facts, Key Risks)

### Countercase

Light Anchor may be a performance agency using increasingly common automation. Amazon and Meta already automate parts of campaign management, and the memo gives no comparative cost evidence showing that conventional managed growth was unaffordable for Anuko. Anuko’s short-window sales figures also leave open how much growth the service caused. A live deployment in a previously unstaffed workflow supports a concrete mechanism at seed. The unresolved affordability threshold keeps the action at Watch. (Defensibility, The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a brand that previously left US growth unstaffed because of cost renew a paid Light Anchor engagement at terms that work for both sides? A renewal backed by a comparable staffed quote above the buyer’s budget, a credible estimate of incremental gross profit after ad spend and fees, and positive contribution for Light Anchor after account-level delivery costs would move the action to Reach out. Evidence that the brand could already afford equivalent managed growth and simply switched providers would move it to Pass.
