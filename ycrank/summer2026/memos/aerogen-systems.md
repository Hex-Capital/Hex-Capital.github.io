## The Idea

- **Problem:** Semiconductor manufacturers use facility-wide cleanrooms, consumable AMC filters, and ultra-high-purity nitrogen purges, yet parts-per-billion molecular contamination during wafer movement and staging can cause corrosion, residue, and yield loss ([company site, Aug 2026](https://www.aerogen-systems.com/)).
- **Approach:** Talos recirculates air inside semiconductor equipment and chemically destroys contaminants around the wafer, while the planned CleanPipe would maintain sealed, purified environments between tools ([YC, Aug 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/aerogen-systems)).
- **Differentiation:** Unlike Entegris and Camfil AMC filters or Purafil’s replaceable chemical media, Aerogen says Talos destroys contaminants without consumables and provides onboard monitoring at the tool ([Aerogen Systems](https://www.aerogen-systems.com/about), [Entegris](https://www.entegris.com/en/home/products/gas-filtration-and-purification/amc-filters.html), [Camfil](https://www.camfil.com/en-us/industries/electronics-optics/semiconductor), [Purafil](https://www.purafil.com/products/molecular-filtration/molecular-media/)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: The disclosed discussion, on-site evaluation, integration, and multi-system deployment sequence indicates custom hardware and integration contracts, but no pricing is public.
- **TAM/SAM:** Semiconductor contamination-control solutions were estimated at $6.163 billion in 2025 and projected to reach $11.103 billion in 2032 at an 8.8% CAGR, while no public SAM was found for tool-integrated AMC destruction ([LP Information, 2026](https://www.marketresearch.com/LP-Information-Inc-v4134/Global-Semiconductor-Contamination-Control-Solutions-43293672/)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Aerogen is recruiting semiconductor fabs, equipment OEMs, and manufacturing teams into pilots that begin with an on-site contamination assessment and can expand to multi-system deployment ([YC, Aug 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/aerogen-systems), [company site, Aug 2026](https://www.aerogen-systems.com/)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Aerogen reports patent-pending IP, multiple Talos hardware generations, a two-year NASA research origin, more than $500,000 of R&D, and a self-reported test reaching below 1 ppb after achieving 7N purity in 16 minutes ([YC, Aug 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/aerogen-systems), [company site, Aug 2026](https://www.aerogen-systems.com/about)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Tool-specific adapters, qualification records, and contamination-performance data could create switching costs, but named commercial deployments and accumulated field data are not public.
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage because Entegris already supplies point-of-use and tool-level AMC purification, while Camfil supplies qualified AMC tool filters ([Entegris](https://www.entegris.com/en/home/our-science/by-solution-area/contamination-control.html), [Camfil](https://www.camfil.com/damdocuments/25474/54507/brochure-microelectronics-eng.pdf)).
- **Commoditization risk:** Entegris, Camfil, and Purafil already manufacture AMC-removal media, tool filters, monitoring systems, or catalytic adsorbents and could develop competing non-consumable or recirculating configurations ([Entegris](https://blog.entegris.com/understanding-amc-the-invisible-threat-to-semiconductor-yield-and-reliability), [Camfil](https://www.camfil.com/en-us/industries/electronics-optics/semiconductor), [Purafil](https://www.purafil.com/markets/electronics/semiconductor-manufacturing-facility/)).

## Key Risks

- **Semiconductor validation:** Public performance evidence is a company-reported single 30-minute PID run, while tool integrations, pilots, and commercial deployments remain in progress; multiple hardware generations and patent-pending IP are the disclosed mitigation ([company site](https://www.aerogen-systems.com/), [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/aerogen-systems)).
- **Integration and qualification:** Each engagement requires site evaluation and equipment integration, and no semiconductor manufacturer or equipment OEM has been identified publicly as a customer ([company site, Aug 2026](https://www.aerogen-systems.com/)).
- **Product pivot:** Public positioning moved from spacecraft air purification to industrial ducted systems and then semiconductor-tool contamination control by Summer 2026, with the photocatalytic VOC-destruction reactor retained across applications ([U-M CFE, Jan–Jun 2026](https://cfe.umich.edu/a-breath-of-fresh-air-aerogen-systems-wins-big-with-fuel-grant/), [YC, Aug 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/aerogen-systems)).
- **Incumbent substitution:** Entegris and Camfil already offer tool-level AMC products, and Purafil’s parent has approximately 85% aftermarket sales across validated filtration products ([Entegris](https://www.entegris.com/en/home/products/gas-filtration-and-purification/amc-filters.html), [Camfil](https://www.camfil.com/en-us/industries/electronics-optics/semiconductor), [Parker Hannifin](https://investors.parker.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/496/parker-to-acquire-filtration-group-corporation)).
- **Domain and name ambiguity:** Website not accessible at time of research at the supplied non-hyphenated domain, while the live site uses aerogen-systems.com and an unrelated Irish medical-device manufacturer operates as Aerogen ([live company site](https://www.aerogen-systems.com/), [unrelated Aerogen](https://www.aerogen.com/our-technology)).
