## The Idea

- **Problem:** Utilities must identify vegetation encroachment and asset hazards across power networks, using manual inspections or specialized surveys that Kanopy describes as requiring expensive hardware and weeks of processing ([Kanopy website, retrieved Nov 2026, via search snippet](https://kanopy-ai.com/)).
- **Approach:** Kanopy converts ordinary drone, vehicle, or handheld video into geospatial 3D reconstructions, clearance measurements, hazard flags, and exportable work records ([Kanopy website, via search snippet](https://kanopy-ai.com/)).
- **Differentiation:** Kanopy uses customer-owned cameras without LiDAR, whereas AiDASH and Overstory emphasize satellite imagery for network monitoring and vegetation intelligence ([Kanopy, via search snippet](https://kanopy-ai.com/); [AiDASH](https://www.aidash.com/about/); [Overstory investor announcement](https://blumeequity.com/overstory-closes-43m-series-b-to-scale-ai-driven-wildfire-prevention-and-grid-resilience/)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Enterprise software contracts with utilities and inspection contractors are a plausible monetization path given documented MSAs and LOIs, but public prices and billing units were not found ([Antler, Apr 2026](https://www.antler.co/blog/announcing-the-spring-2026-u-s-portfolio-showcase)).
- **TAM/SAM:** The broader global utility vegetation management market is estimated at $31.95 billion in 2026, growing 7.10% annually through 2034, but includes physical treatment rather than solely inspection software; Kanopy-specific SAM: No public data found ([Fortune Business Insights, Oct 2026](https://www.fortunebusinessinsights.com/utility-vegetation-management-market-107247)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Founders demonstrated the product at Trees & Utilities and publicly solicited utility, infrastructure, vegetation-management, and inspection contacts ([Nicolas Pfitzer, LinkedIn, retrieved Nov 2026, via search snippet](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nicolas-pfitzer)).
