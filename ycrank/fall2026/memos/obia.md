## The Idea

- **Problem:** Businesses seeking industrial or office space rely on brokers to reconcile listings, brochures, zoning codes, maps, and broker conversations, a process Obia says can take hours per property ([YC company profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/obia)).
- **Approach:** Obia’s AI compares rents, checks permitted uses, and screens for constraints such as loading and power before a local broker leads tours and negotiates terms ([Obia website](https://obia.io/), [Obia CoreNet announcement](https://obia.io/news/corenet-global-summit-2026), Oct 2026).
- **Differentiation:** Obia emphasizes pre-tour zoning and operational screening, while TenantBase matches leads to partner brokers, Cresa offers occupier-only representation and lease administration, Raise offers portfolio analytics and lease management, and CBRE integrates leasing into a global advisory platform ([Obia](https://obia.io/), [TenantBase](https://www.tenantbase.com/broker/), [Cresa](https://www.cresa.com/AboutUs), [JLL](https://www.jll.com/en-us/newsroom/jll-elevates-client-experience-with-leasing-tech-and-brokerage-acquisition), [CBRE 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1138118/000113811826000005/cbre-20251231.htm)).
- **Business Model:** Obia says tenants and buyers use the service free of charge and landlords pay its fee on the tenant-leasing side ([Obia website](https://obia.io/), [Obia LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/obia-cre/)).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** Obia takes search and listing inquiries through its website, and Cho publicly offered referral fees for introductions to businesses seeking industrial or office space ([Obia contact page](https://obia.io/contact), [Cho’s X announcement, mirrored](https://www.teapawt.org/SamuelMCho/status/2110842930546270399), Oct 2026).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Obia says it collects location, size, and budget requirements from tenant searches and uses them to match properties with prospective occupants ([Obia website](https://obia.io/)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Linking search requirements to verified zoning decisions and completed deals could improve future matching, although Obia’s public materials describe the inputs without quantifying the dataset or any matching gains ([Obia website](https://obia.io/)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** CBRE’s Ellis AI already extracts lease data and searches research documents, and TenantBase already uses AI to match tenants with brokers ([CBRE Ellis AI](https://www.cbre.com/about-us/technology/artificial-intelligence), [TenantBase partner-broker page](https://www.tenantbase.com/broker/)).

## Key Risks

- **Property and zoning accuracy:** Obia’s terms say third-party property data and comparables may be incomplete, stale, or inaccurate, and its workflow flags uncertain findings for broker review ([Obia Terms](https://obia.io/terms-and-conditions), [Obia website](https://obia.io/)).
- **Interstate brokerage:** Obia plans national expansion from California, and Nevada, for example, requires an out-of-state broker working with a Nevada broker to obtain a cooperative certificate ([YC company profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/obia), [Nevada Real Estate Division](https://red.nv.gov/uploadedFiles/rednvgov/Content/Forms/542A.pdf)).
- **Contracting entity:** Obia’s October terms identify Jason Henkel as a sole proprietor doing business as Obia, while the website footer identifies Obia Inc., creating ambiguity about the named counterparty ([Obia Terms](https://obia.io/terms-and-conditions), [Obia website](https://obia.io/)).
- **Name confusion:** A separate French AI software business uses OBIA at obia.fr, creating a risk of misattributed search results and company records ([OBIA Suite legal notice](https://www.obia.fr/mentions-legales), [YC company profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/obia)).
