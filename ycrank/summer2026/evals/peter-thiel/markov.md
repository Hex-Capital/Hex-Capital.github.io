# Markov -- Peter Thiel Evaluation

Markov’s implied contested truth is that computer-use models will remain constrained by scarce, auditable human workflows even as synthetic trajectories improve. The falsification test is straightforward: if models trained primarily on synthetic actions match or exceed those trained with Markov’s recordings across unseen professional applications, the company’s foundational claim disappears. Markov has built the experiment—48,478 videos, roughly 12,300 hours, synchronized inputs, and an AutoCAD environment retaining artifacts and model turns—but it has not published the decisive training comparison. This is a plausible secret, not yet a demonstrated one.

The beachhead is unusually concrete: professional-software workflows, beginning with CAD, browser, coding, design, and spreadsheets. AutoCAD Bench’s retained files and input events are more substantive than a generic annotation service, while 150,000 cumulative Hugging Face downloads and 17,814 in the preceding month show real developer attention. But attention is not monopoly. The flagship corpus is public, its recordings are internet-sourced, the benchmark contains only 50 tasks, and its evaluator can confuse visually similar with semantically different geometry. No exclusive data, measured performance advantage, network effect, or customer feedback loop presently prevents Scale AI, Invisible, a model laboratory, or a synthetic-data system from reproducing the supply.

The strongest bull case is that two founders have already produced infrastructure at a scale normally associated with a much larger organization. Mandal’s work at Sarvam AI, DevRev, and Telekinesis and Ashok’s robotics-assistant experience are relevant; the public corpus and benchmark are shipped artifacts, not presentation slides. Markov also fits the emerging AI production stack better than another application-layer copilot. Yet the known exception for a crowded AI market requires a verified zero-to-one technical event, as occurred with OpenAI; Markov has developer downloads but no reported model improvement, paid deployment, or customer-qualified result. The shared mechanism is technical infrastructure for a model transition. The material difference is proof that the infrastructure changes model capability.

The founding structure is unresolved rather than adverse. Both founders publicly identify with Markov, and the core assets appear to have been built internally, but Mandal remains listed as an IIT Madras student through 2027, no prior co-founder history is documented, and there is no evidence about ownership, salaries, or full-time status. None of the classic red flags—outsourced core engineering, investor-majority ownership, or convenience matching—is established. Neither is the long alignment that would clear the foundation gate. Missing evidence is not refutation, but it cannot become conviction.

Distribution is the clearest commercial hint and the weakest economic proof. Hugging Face provides a named route to AI developers, benchmarks create technical credibility, and the website converts specialized-data inquiries into sales calls. Markov nevertheless discloses no buyer, price, conversion rate, sales cycle, revenue, or renewal. The $3.2 billion training-dataset market growing toward $16.3 billion is a category forecast, not an explanation of how this company captures value. Dataset projects may become a large proprietary environment business, or they may remain bespoke work exposed to well-capitalized vendors and synthetic substitution.

I would investigate, not invest. Markov has found a narrow and timely technical problem and demonstrated exceptional supply-side execution, but it has not yet shown that its data produces a result buyers cannot generate elsewhere—or that one revenue engine can compound into a last-mover position. The verifiable flip condition is a named frontier-model customer paying and renewing a seven-figure annual contract for application-specific environments after a controlled evaluation shows Markov’s data materially outperforming a synthetic-only baseline. Until then: useful dataset or proprietary infrastructure remains the unanswered binary.
