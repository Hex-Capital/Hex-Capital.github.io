# Reific -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path A — Scarce complements.** Reific is a San Francisco seed company selling reinforcement-learning environments to AI labs. The potential bottleneck is a recurring supply of operating records that labs have permission to train on and can replay to grade whether an agent acted at the right time. Static benchmarks expose the full record before grading. Reific’s clocked replay makes timing part of the task. Reific reports that one open model’s full-solve rate on unseen tasks rose from 25.6% to 39.5%. That supports a seed-stage technical test, while transfer to an independent lab’s workflow remains open. (Reific, The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

Reific publicly launched and offers environment licenses. The memo records first-author research on rare-event time-series forecasting by founder Antanas Žilinskas and attributes prior hyperscaler deployments to his own account. The launch and research support technical execution. Buyer qualification, paid use, and repeat orders remain undocumented. The scale scenario assumes several labs license the same environment and that the combined fees cover record rights, privacy checks, practitioner review, and refreshes. The memo leaves pricing and those delivery costs unknown. Path B remains unproven because the memo supplies neither a delivery-labor comparison nor evidence of a newly viable transaction. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Founders & Team, Defensibility, Key Risks, Key Facts)

### Countercase

A well-funded lab could use its own records with open tooling or HUD’s SDK. Reific’s record-checked grading and reported practitioner review provide some differentiation, but the memo shows only that Reific asks suppliers about exclusivity. It documents no rights that would keep comparable records from rivals. The company’s updated leaderboard also shows a 60.2% industry score, weakening the launch claim that all task families were below 50% and leaving open how long existing tasks remain useful as models improve. Substitution is credible and still untested. That leaves Path A open and the affirmative gate for outreach unmet. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Will an independent frontier lab renew a paid license for a refreshed environment whose underlying records Reific has enforceable rights to supply and the lab cannot readily source elsewhere? A renewal after a matched comparison against the lab’s best in-house alternative, showing a material gain on held-out timed tasks, would support **Reach out** if the updated case clears the score and confidence gates. If readily available records and tooling match the result and the lab declines to renew, **Pass** on the current case.
