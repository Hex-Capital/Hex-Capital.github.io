# Wingman Law -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path B — New economies.** Wingman says its agents handle evidence gathering and prepare documents and communications for attorney approval, reducing the manual follow-up that conventional case managers perform. The memo documents free online intake and a contingency-fee offer for California accident victims. The seed-stage opportunity is to represent clients with smaller, meritorious claims that conventional firms decline on cost grounds. That segment is a hypothesis. The memo contains no like-for-like comparison of labor per resolved case after attorney review, retries, exceptions, and confidentiality safeguards. (The Idea, Key Risks)

Under model parity, clients would still need a firm to take responsibility for the case and carry it through to recovery. Wingman offers that service directly. Whether clients would choose Wingman over another firm with comparable AI and terms remains untested. The memo identifies no scarce capability Wingman controls that would support Path A independently. (The Idea, Defensibility)

The memo reports multiple six-figure cases in progress, with no documented collections or evidence that those clients were previously underserved. Lee’s litigation and legal-tech experience and Le’s reported work on more than 700 injury cases support the team’s ability to run a representative trial. A California seed investment is plausible if there is an eligible entity in which the fund can acquire equity. (Market & Traction, Founders & Team)

**Scenario assumption.** Wingman can accept smaller claims at comparable service quality and contingency terms. For each additional cohort, expected fee proceeds equal the share of cases that recover money multiplied by the average fee collected on those recoveries. Contribution per accepted case subtracts acquisition, inference, staff and attorney time, and unreimbursed case costs across all accepted cases, including those with zero recovery. Cash required before collection depends on time to recovery. The memo gives no cohort figures to establish either positive contribution or the volume Wingman can acquire through referrals and inbound intake. (The Idea, Key Risks)

### Countercase

Wingman may be making conventional injury work more efficient for the same clients. The memo identifies overlapping tools that firms can buy and an incumbent with its own AI platform and an established referral network. Attorney rework and the delegation and confidentiality limits taking effect in January 2027 could erase the labor savings on smaller cases. These facts leave both Path B conditions unproven. They establish neither failed delivery economics nor easy substitution for Wingman’s full service, so the case misses the outreach gate and remains on Watch. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can Wingman take a representative cohort of smaller claims that conventional firms declined on cost grounds through to recovery, with comparable quality, lower fully loaded human labor per successful case, and clients choosing Wingman when offered comparable AI-enabled representation on similar terms? A completed cohort that demonstrates all three and shows positive contribution after acquisition and case costs would justify rescoring for Reach out, subject to mandate fit. If the review burden erases the savings or clients readily switch under parity, Pass on the current thesis.
