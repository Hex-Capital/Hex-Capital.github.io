## The Idea

- **Problem:** Data-center operators and manufacturers rely on scarce technicians for repetitive wiring, assembly, and testing because conventional automation struggles with variation across products and sites ([YC launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WJb-shepherd-robotics-robots-for-data-centers-and-advanced-manufacturing), Oct 2026).
- **Approach:** Robot foundation models trained on real-world and simulation data control modular hardware, initially performing commissioning, inspection, wire routing, and terminal insertion under human supervision ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/shepherd-robotics)).
- **Differentiation:** [Inferred]: Compared with Micropsi’s robot-control software, Shepherd bundles hardware, models, integration, and support; compared with Dyna’s cross-industry robots, it emphasizes data-center and electrical workflows ([Shepherd website](https://www.shepherdrobotics.ai/), via search snippet; [Micropsi](https://www.micropsi-industries.com/blog/micropsi-industries-secures-30m-in-series-b), via search snippet; [Dyna](https://www.dyna.co/news/series-a)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Hardware delivery, integration, and ongoing support suggest enterprise equipment-and-service contracts; public pricing: No public data found ([company website](https://www.shepherdrobotics.ai/), via search snippet).
- **TAM/SAM:** The broader industrial-robotics market is estimated at $41.9B in 2026, with 9.5% CAGR through 2033; No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment ([Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/industrial-robotics-market), 2026).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Founders request introductions to data-center operations, construction, and manufacturing leaders experiencing manual-work bottlenecks ([YC launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WJb-shepherd-robotics-robots-for-data-centers-and-advanced-manufacturing), Oct 2026).
