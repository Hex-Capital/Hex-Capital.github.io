## The Idea

- **Problem:** Plumbing and HVAC dispatchers balance skills, travel, and revenue manually; Fleety describes customers handling 100+ jobs daily and a $500,000 annual revenue gap between their best and average technicians ([YC, Oct 2026, via search snippet](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/fleety)).
- **Approach:** Fleety checks call transcripts against equipment records and notes, matches technicians by skill, location, and revenue potential, and adjusts schedules as conditions change ([Fleety website, Oct 2026](https://usefleety.com/)).
- **Differentiation:** Fleety advertises compatibility with ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, and Jobber, whereas ServiceTitan Dispatch Pro operates within ServiceTitan; Avoca emphasizes call answering, booking, follow-up, and coaching ([Fleety](https://usefleety.com/), [ServiceTitan documentation](https://help.servicetitan.com/commercial/docs/dispatch-pro-home), [Avoca](https://www.avoca.ai/blog/avoca-raises-125m-series-b-1b-valuation)).
- **Business Model:** Fees are specified in an order form or subscription agreement and generally billed in advance; public dollar pricing: No public data found ([Fleety terms, May 2026](https://usefleety.com/terms-of-service.html)).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment; broader global service dispatch software was estimated at $3.4 billion in 2025, with 4.45% CAGR during 2026–2034 ([The Insight Partners, Apr 2026](https://www.theinsightpartners.com/reports/service-dispatch-software-market)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** The website solicits demos and describes onsite configuration with operators, including weeks spent working in customer shops ([Fleety website, Oct 2026](https://usefleety.com/)).

## Key Risks

- **Incumbent substitution:** [Inferred]: ServiceTitan customers can obtain overlapping revenue-based assignments through Dispatch Pro, reducing the need for an additional dispatch vendor ([ServiceTitan documentation](https://help.servicetitan.com/commercial/docs/dispatch-pro-home)).
- **Integration dependency:** [Inferred]: Changes to CRM APIs or authorization could interrupt workflows; Fleety’s terms explicitly exclude responsibility for third-party API changes affecting integration functionality ([Fleety terms, May 2026](https://usefleety.com/terms-of-service.html)).
- **Dispatch accuracy:** [Inferred]: Incorrect call extraction or technician matching could misroute jobs; Fleety provides dispatcher review and adjustment as a mitigation, while public accuracy benchmarks: No public data found ([Fleety website](https://usefleety.com/)).
- **Name confusion:** Unrelated car-sharing and shopping products also use Fleety, creating attribution risk for funding, followers, and downloads; their different domains and publishers distinguish them ([car-sharing LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/company/fleety), [shopping app listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.getfleety.app)).
