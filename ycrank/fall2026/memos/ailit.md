## The Idea

- **Problem:** North American freight operators need an alternative to diesel that preserves locomotive power, range and continuous availability, according to Ailit’s founders ([YC, via search snippet](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ailit)).
- **Approach:** Superwagon carries interchangeable batteries behind an existing diesel-electric locomotive, supplies its electric drive, and exchanges depleted packs for off-board charging while retaining diesel backup ([Ailit website, Nov 2026](https://www.ailitrail.com/)).
- **Differentiation:** Ailit proposes pack exchanges, whereas [Voltify](https://www.voltify.com/) develops on-route charging and microgrids, [Intramotev](https://www.intramotev.com/) retrofits railcars with propulsion, and [Dayton-Phoenix](https://www.dayton-phoenix.com/white-paper-hybridizing-existing-freight-locomotives-using-a-modular-battery-tender/) uses a tender for supplemental hybrid power.
- **Business Model:** Railroads pay for energy without purchasing equipment upfront, with Ailit owning batteries and swapping sites and advertising pricing 20% below diesel cost ([YC, via search snippet](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ailit)).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** A previously indexed commercial hiring page describes developing pilots and relationships with Class I/II railroads, regulators and infrastructure partners, although the page now returns 404 ([Ailit careers, via search snippet](https://www.ailitrail.com/careers/rail-executive)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Ailit describes integrated battery hardware and AilitOS coordination of packs, charging and departures, but its website presents these as systems under development rather than demonstrated operating advantages ([Ailit website, Nov 2026](https://www.ailitrail.com/)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: A deployed swapping network could create route coverage advantages and operational switching costs, but network operation remains unverified publicly ([Ailit system description](https://www.ailitrail.com/)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** An existing supplier already reports locomotive integration testing of a modular battery tender, demonstrating competing development of the underlying architecture ([Dayton-Phoenix white paper, Mar 2026](https://www.dayton-phoenix.com/white-paper-hybridizing-existing-freight-locomotives-using-a-modular-battery-tender/)).

## Key Risks

- **Technical feasibility:** Ailit says its first full-scale prototype is being built and targets deployment in summer 2027; operating performance data were not found, while diesel backup is part of the proposed design ([YC, via search snippet](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ailit), [Ailit](https://www.ailitrail.com/)).
- **Network availability:** [Inferred]: Continuous battery operation depends on charged packs being available at exchange sites when trains arrive, making charging, inventory and departure coordination interdependent ([Ailit system description](https://www.ailitrail.com/)).
- **Cost exposure:** [Inferred]: Owning the batteries and charging operation exposes Ailit to equipment and electricity costs while it promises diesel-relative pricing, and BloombergNEF reports North American pack prices above the global average ([YC, via search snippet](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ailit), [BloombergNEF, Dec 2025](https://about.bnef.com/insights/clean-transport/lithium-ion-battery-pack-prices-fall-to-108-per-kilowatt-hour-despite-rising-metal-prices-bloombergnef/)).
- **Name ambiguity:** An unrelated South Korean software company also appears as “Ailit,” using ailiit.com; its funding and operating records were excluded ([Dealroom](https://spacetech.dealroom.co/companies/ailit)).
