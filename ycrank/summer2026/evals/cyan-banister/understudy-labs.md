# Understudy Labs -- Cyan Banister Evaluation

Luis Manrique sold $1.9 million in ARR at Gumloop, while Aamir Poonawalla spent ten years building Instacart’s ads, auction, experimentation, and logistics infrastructure. Understudy Labs is a domain-native pair building from the inside of production ML, but Understudy Labs has chosen the least tactile version of that advantage: a software control layer for replacing one model API with another. The signature is strong founder proximity paired with almost no frontier-hardware, regulatory, or physical-world friction. Biz and Tiz are visible; Rizz remains unobservable.

**Anduril** is the high rung because Palmer Luckey carried a prior artifact, technical obsession, and autobiographical trajectory directly into defense. Understudy Labs shares the important mechanism: Poonawalla built production ML infrastructure for a decade, Manrique worked across GTM, FDE, and data platforms, and both co-invented an advertising-campaign pacing patent at Instacart. Understudy Labs diverges because neither founder is shown repeatedly suffering from frontier-model replacement as an operator, and neither has already shipped an adjacent model-optimization product at scale. They are of the neighborhood, but the dossier does not yet show the moment the problem grabbed them by the collar.

The **consensus YC AI-agent darling** fails because pure software abstraction inherits its importance from someone else’s model and can be rebuilt by the next capable polymath. That low-rung mechanism appears inside Understudy Labs: Fireworks, Baseten, Together AI, Braintrust, and internal ML teams can assemble overlapping capture, evaluation, tuning, and routing systems, while Understudy Labs has no public moat signal. Understudy Labs is not capital-intensive, regulated, aesthetically transgressive, or ugly enough to repel conventional money. It is infrastructure in the hottest neighborhood in San Francisco, which is nearly the inverse of the SpaceX and Crusoe setup.

Understudy Labs could become magically weird over the next 18 months if customer-owned traces, failure maps, routing rules, and tuned weights compound into a trusted migration workflow. Understudy Labs weakens that compounding loop by prohibiting cross-customer payload training and giving customers ownership of the artifacts, while Baseten and Together AI already control adjacent training and inference surfaces. Nine GitHub stars, five forks, and unnamed private-preview design partners do not prove consensus, but they do not reveal sophisticated investors passing because the idea looks ridiculous either. Today this feels sensible and early, not magically weird.

Luis Manrique is explicitly CEO and Aamir Poonawalla is explicitly CTO, which clears the first governance gate for Understudy Labs. Understudy Labs would move into the top governance tier if Manrique held documented operating control, founder vesting, and a written deadlock protocol covering the longtime partnership. The dossier supplies none of those facts, so the title split is encouraging but not the tiebreaker evidence I learned to demand after HQ Trivia and GameCrush. “Longtime partner” is warm language until the first irreconcilable decision.

**Flock Safety** could be encountered as a Wi-Fi network and then observed as cameras installed in the world; its physical artifact made founder claims testable without permission. Understudy Labs offers an MIT-licensed agent toolkit that an engineer can install, which is more verifiable than a slide deck, but Understudy Labs remains invisible to a normal human and valuable only through benchmark interpretation. Ten-run task-specific comparisons and held-out tests are useful evidence, yet they are still evidence about an abstraction. I cannot ride it, photograph it, or watch a delivery arrive.

Fireworks reports more than $1 billion in annualized revenue while Understudy Labs remains in private preview with no public revenue. My 36-month forecast is that by July 2029 Understudy Labs reaches roughly $3 million to $8 million in ARR, shifts its emphasis from open-weight replacement to eval-driven routing, and is acquired by Braintrust rather than becoming an independent inference platform. Understudy Labs has the operator quality to build something useful, but the low-capital software layer sits naturally inside a larger observability product whose distribution already exists.

Aamir Poonawalla’s ten years at Instacart and Luis Manrique’s $1.9 million Gumloop sales record make the bull case sting: Understudy Labs may be the control plane rather than another inference vendor, and customer-owned weights could turn portability into the product instead of a moat weakness. Understudy Labs could even resemble the DeepMind exception, where cross-domain technical compounding overrode my usual founder-origin filter. The dossier still gives the deciding evidence to the bear: no public defensibility, nine GitHub stars, unnamed design partners, and four heavily financed adjacent competitors. HQ Trivia’s exception does not apply because there is no tactile viral pull. I would flip to Invest if three named production customers publicly showed Understudy Labs routing at least 30% of their workloads to open models while preserving measured quality and generating at least $1 million in aggregate ARR.
