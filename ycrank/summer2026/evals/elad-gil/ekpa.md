# Ekpa -- Elad Gil Evaluation

$3.4M+ in reported managed assets sits on top of a technically straightforward, single-miracle product and a structurally weak distribution position. Ekpa can assemble portfolio data through SnapTrade and apply already-validated LLM capabilities; Ekpa does not need new science. The problem is that Public, Composer, and PortfolioPilot can bundle the same analysis into products where customers already hold or manage money. The analytical signature is a buildable product without an index-like position on the category it serves.

**Color Genomics** is the high rung for single-miracle architecture: commodity sequencing was the hard thing, while Color owned the workflow and data layer above it. Ekpa shares that mechanism because Ekpa can rely on SnapTrade connectivity, public market data, and existing models rather than inventing financial intelligence from first principles. Ekpa diverges because accurate recommendations are not sufficient: the product must also earn investor trust, stay outside or comply with investment-adviser regulation, and acquire consumers despite bundled substitutes. That is more than one execution dependency, even if none requires new physics.

**Lyft** exposed the failure mechanism of entering a networked market without a credible path to becoming the default: #2 economics remain #2 economics. Ekpa shows an analogous weakness before scale. Public already combines brokerage, portfolio agents, research, indexes, and execution; SoFi acquired Composer in June 2026; PortfolioPilot supplies registered advice. Ekpa’s read-only, cross-brokerage position is useful, but Ekpa currently looks like a feature that must win share against distribution owners rather than an index on AI-mediated investing.

Public’s agentic brokerage and Robinhood’s support for user-deployed trading agents provide a real 2025–2026 catalyst, but they also define how the mechanism compounds over the next 18 months. As portfolio agents become normal, Ekpa gains category demand while Public and Robinhood gain cheaper access to the same models and substantially lower customer-acquisition costs. Ekpa needs cross-broker neutrality to become the control plane every brokerage customer touches; otherwise the catalyst compounds for the brokerages faster than it compounds for Ekpa.

2,400 analyses and $3.4M+ in reported managed assets are evidence that Ekpa has shipped and attracted some portfolio connectivity, but neither figure establishes a usage or revenue curve. Ekpa does not disclose users, retention, subscription revenue, analysis growth, or the time interval behind either number, so I cannot distinguish “just working” from launch-cohort tourism. The score flips into invest territory if Ekpa shows paid revenue compounding above 20% month over month with connected accounts retaining through multiple market cycles; until then, the paid Strategy Lab with undisclosed pricing is a revenue hypothesis.

Anant Asthana built agentic drug-discovery pipelines, while Yuga Patel brings MIT computer science, economics, investment exposure, and automation work at AbbVie. The relevant parallel is **Perplexity**: Aravind repeatedly returned with the discussed product built a week later. Ekpa has produced a functioning multi-broker product and appears to have moved from an S&P 500 trend-following strategy to portfolio research, which is some evidence of iteration; Ekpa has not shown named prior products, independent shipping history, or a documented build-feedback-build cadence comparable to Perplexity.

Composer’s June 2026 acquisition by SoFi points to my 36-month forecast: Ekpa will abandon the broad consumer research-agent positioning and pivot toward adviser-facing portfolio research and compliance workflow, reaching $1M–$3M ARR by July 2029 rather than becoming an independent consumer brokerage platform. Ekpa’s strongest asset is the non-custodial analysis workflow across 38 brokerages; Ekpa’s weakest asset is direct consumer distribution. Moving closer to RIAs or financial institutions converts regulatory ambiguity and cross-broker connectivity into product requirements a business buyer may pay for.

Fourteen Launch YC votes make the strongest opposing case sting: Ekpa may simply be very early, while $3.4M+ connected assets and 2,400 analyses show more usage than most pre-seed companies disclose. Public’s launch and SoFi’s Composer acquisition validate the catalyst, and Ekpa’s read-only neutrality could become a “Switzerland” advantage that bundled brokerages cannot credibly offer. I still pass on that bull case because Ekpa discloses no retention or revenue velocity, has no defensibility signal, and gives incumbents a direct bundling path. One verifiable condition would flip my verdict: Ekpa reaching $25,000 MRR while sustaining greater than 20% month-over-month growth for four consecutive months.
