# Native -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Medium

### Company case

Product-led adoption is Native’s strongest path. Paid ads bring small businesses without a marketing team to a free preview that turns their website into a draft post. The preview lets an owner see brand-specific output before paying. A subscription gives the owner a reason to return each time they need to choose topics, create content, and publish. Native reports more than 2,200 paying customers across 20+ countries. That supports portability, with the amount of localization and the mix of buyers by country still unknown. The memo documents the preview and publishing workflow, while the share of ad-acquired customers who reach paid publishing without help remains unknown. (The Idea, Market & Traction)

The HAVN Storage testimonial reports 9,495 social impressions after adoption versus 2,322 in the preceding period. It gives a concrete example of a buyer adopting the workflow and seeing more reach, with the effect on sales unmeasured. The CEO reports that acquisition cost fell from roughly NOK 1,800 to NOK 800 per new customer. That supports some improvement in acquisition efficiency, while content generation and support costs leave marginal contribution unresolved. (Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Countercase

The first link likely to break is repeat paid use. A personalized preview can win an initial subscription even if the owner stops publishing after the first campaign. The reported customer count and ARR establish the scale Native claims, but the memo gives no usable record of renewals, recurring publishing, or paid upgrades. Several monthly renewal cycles have elapsed since the March launch, so repeat behavior is already testable. That gap warrants Watch. The memo does not establish structural failure or trigger the downside gate. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Do small businesses acquired through paid ads and activated through the website preview still publish regularly by their third paid renewal? Account-level billing and publishing records showing that pattern across multiple cohorts that onboarded without hands-on help would move Native to Reach out. Consistent early cancellation after a first campaign across those cohorts would move it to Pass.
