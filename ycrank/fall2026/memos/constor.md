## The Idea

- **Problem:** Insulation and HVAC contractors manually measure drawings to prepare bids, consuming hours and weekends according to customer testimonials ([Constor website, accessed Nov 10, 2026](https://constor.ai/landing/en)).
- **Approach:** Computer vision extracts piping, ducts, surfaces, and equipment quantities from uploaded PDFs, supports review and 3D visualization, and exports structured tables ([Constor website](https://constor.ai/landing/en)).
- **Differentiation:** Constor emphasizes HVAC and insulation workflows with 3D checking, whereas Togal advertises broader architectural takeoffs and Drawer AI specializes in electrical quantities and routing ([Constor](https://constor.ai/landing/en), [Togal](https://www.togal.ai/), [Drawer AI](https://about.drawer.ai/)).
- **Business Model:** Terms permit paid subscriptions and credits, with prices disclosed during subscription rather than in a public price table ([Constor terms, June 2026](https://constor.ai/conditions-generales)).
- **TAM/SAM:** The broader construction-estimating-software market is estimated at $1.7B in 2026, growing at 12.3% CAGR through 2033; no public SAM for Constor’s specific trades was found ([Grand View Research, 2026](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/construction-estimating-software-market-report)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Acquisition includes booked demonstrations using prospects’ drawings and search-oriented articles about takeoffs and ERP exports ([Constor website](https://constor.ai/landing/en), [company blog, Nov 2026](https://constor.ai/landing/en/blog)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** A founder reports obtaining historical data and feedback from paying design partners, establishing a data-access signal without demonstrating exclusive ownership or measured model advantage ([Dany Delacour, LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/danydelacour_just-got-into-yc-heres-the-playbook-that-activity-7505506028554412033-c2Tg)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Customer drawings and corrections could improve trade-specific detection, but public evidence does not establish dataset scale or an accuracy advantage.
- **Market structure:** [Inferred]: No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** Competing vendors already automate drawing measurements, including Beam AI’s HVAC and plumbing takeoffs, so the core workflow has competing implementations ([Beam AI](https://www.ibeam.ai/)).

## Key Risks

- **Technical accuracy:** Results depend on drawing quality and completeness, and customers must validate quantities before bidding or execution; mandatory human checking is the published mitigation ([Constor terms](https://constor.ai/conditions-generales)).
- **Confidential drawing use:** [Inferred]: Using uploaded plans for model training could impede adoption by customers with confidentiality restrictions; Constor states that technical providers operate under confidentiality commitments ([privacy policy](https://constor.ai/politique-de-confidentialite)).
- **Workflow competition:** [Inferred]: Beam AI’s overlapping HVAC/plumbing capabilities and integrated estimating/bid management could reduce Constor’s differentiation ([Beam AI](https://www.ibeam.ai/)).
