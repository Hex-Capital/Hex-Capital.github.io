## The Idea

- **Problem:** Frontier labs need agents that act correctly as records arrive, whereas conventional evaluations present completed histories and miss failures caused by late decisions, incomplete information, and delayed human responses ([Reific, November 2026](https://reific.co/research/timing-is-part-of-the-answer)).
- **Approach:** Reific releases real records on a clock inside tool-enabled environments, grades answers against recorded outcomes, and supplies personal-agent, industrial, and scientific/compute task families ([Reific methodology](https://reific.co/environments/methodology), [product site](https://reific.co/)).
- **Differentiation:** Reific emphasizes recorded temporal systems, whereas Prime Intellect supplies a broader compute-to-training stack and Patronus generates digital workflow simulations using world models ([Reific](https://reific.co/research/why-we-build-from-real-records), [Prime Intellect announcement](https://www.intelcapital.com/prime-intellect-raises-130m-series-a-to-build-the-open-superintelligence-stack/), [Patronus](https://www.patronus.ai/news/announcing-our-50m-series-b)).
- **Business Model:** Environment licensing for reinforcement-learning training and custom environment construction are offered; public prices, billing units, and contract minimums: No public data found ([Reific training page](https://reific.co/research/a-curriculum-for-reasoning-over-time)).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** The website collects work emails for environment samples and offers researcher calls, while a separate partner funnel solicits datasets for licensing ([Reific](https://reific.co/), [data-partner page](https://reific.co/data-partners)).

## Key Risks

- **Training-transfer evidence:** [Inferred]: Low evaluation scores establish task difficulty but do not establish generalizable training gains; Reific claims improvement without publishing its magnitude, training compute, or held-out transfer results on the training page ([Reific, November 2026](https://reific.co/research/a-curriculum-for-reasoning-over-time)).
- **Grading dependence:** [Inferred]: Written-answer rewards depend partly on model judges, allowing grading errors to affect training; Reific mitigates this with judges from different labs, record-checked keys, and judge-free numeric checks ([methodology](https://reific.co/environments/methodology)).
- **Data exclusivity:** [Inferred]: Nonexclusive licensing permits suppliers to license overlapping records elsewhere; Reific offers higher payments for exclusivity but discloses no executed exclusive agreements ([data-partner page](https://reific.co/data-partners)).
- **Supplier substitution:** [Inferred]: Buyers can source environments within broader training platforms or competing simulation systems, potentially reducing standalone procurement; Prime Intellect and Patronus already offer overlapping infrastructure ([Prime Intellect](https://www.intelcapital.com/prime-intellect-raises-130m-series-a-to-build-the-open-superintelligence-stack/), [Patronus](https://www.patronus.ai/news/announcing-our-50m-series-b)).
