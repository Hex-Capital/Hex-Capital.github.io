# Simulithic -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

A strategic acquisition by a testing platform such as BrowserStack is the strongest supported route. Simulithic documents a visitor-behavior SDK, workspace journey maps, and per-device comparisons of pull-request previews against a baseline. A device-cloud buyer could use those capabilities to prioritize tests around the journeys customers actually take. That rationale is an inference. The memo contains no measured coverage gains, buyer savings, or acquisition interest. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction)

The founders report several live deployments and more than 2× weekly growth on an unspecified metric. The customer testimonial indicates willingness to try the product, while realized revenue remains unknown. A plausible seed milestone is to show repeatable gains in regression detection or QA time in paid production pilots against customers’ existing tests. The reported $500,000 in YC funding supports an initial test, with runway and the capital needed to reach the milestone still unknown. Customer permissions for a buyer to inherit or use captured visitor data also remain unverified. The memo offers no evidence of a separate liquidity route. (Market & Traction, Key Facts, Key Risks)

The shared benchmark assumes a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, ownership diluted from 5% to 3%, and no follow-ons. A 5× gross cash return requires $2.5 million within eight years, or about $83.3 million in distributable equity at 3%. For illustration, assume a cash sale within eight years, $5 million in net debt and deal adjustments, and an eligible holder who sells the entire stake and receives 3% pro rata with no additional senior claims or holdbacks.

| Scenario | Enterprise-to-equity bridge | Cash to holder |
|----------|-----------------------------|----------------|
| Modest sale | $40M enterprise value less $5M leaves $35M | $1.05M (2.1×) |
| Strong sale | $100M enterprise value less $5M leaves $95M | $2.85M (5.7×) |
| Prolonged private | Continued operation with cash reinvested and no share purchase or distribution | $0 realized |

Under those assumptions, the hurdle is about $88.3 million in enterprise value. Even in the strong case, selling only 75% of the stake at the same per-share price yields about $2.14 million. The sale values are sensitivities. The memo gives no realized revenue, buyer valuation, or deal terms to anchor them. (Key Facts, Market & Traction)

### Countercase

BrowserStack already combines device infrastructure with testing agents, and TestSprite already discovers and tests application flows. An acquirer could extend its existing product if Simulithic’s visitor-derived journeys produce little incremental value. Visitor-data permissions could further narrow what a buyer can use. Those risks warrant Watch. The memo establishes neither a structural barrier to closing nor a payout ceiling below the hurdle for the strongest credible route. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

What acquisition price could a testing platform justify for Simulithic’s visitor-derived journey testing after comparing it with extending its own tools? Repeatable results from paid production deployments and a buyer-side build-versus-buy estimate that supports clearing the holder’s cash hurdle would move the action to Reach out. A supported price ceiling materially below the hurdle for the strongest credible route would trigger Pass.
