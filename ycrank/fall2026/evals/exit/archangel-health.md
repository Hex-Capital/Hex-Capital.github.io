# Archangel Health -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

A strategic acquisition by a medical AI developer is Archangel Health’s strongest exit route as of Fall 2026. The memo documents a public listing for the data offering, and Archangel reports use by frontier labs and medical AI companies without identifying customers, payment, or repeat usage. Its physician-graded simulations could give a developer a reason to buy if they consistently uncover consequential errors across patient visits and ownership secures access to the underlying cases and specialist feedback. The memo leaves that incremental benefit and the value of ownership unmeasured. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Defensibility, Key Facts)

A plausible seed milestone is a paid repeat evaluation in which a developer measures how many clinically consequential workflow failures Archangel catches beyond its existing tests. The listed offering and reported use give Archangel a starting point. The memo reports YC backing, with differing funding figures, and leaves the cost and funding of additional data access and specialist grading unresolved. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Defensibility)

The benchmark assumes a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, 5% ownership diluted to 3%, and no follow-ons. A 5× gross cash return within eight years requires $2.5 million to the holder, or about $83.3 million in distributable equity value. The sale cases below assume an eligible holder can sell the full stake by year eight, with cash at closing and pro rata distribution after the stated deductions. They assume no additional senior preferences or deferred payments. The memo supplies no buyer valuation, comparable exit, or holder terms to substantiate either sale price. ### Countercase

A developer could use the public PhysicianBench for multi-step EHR testing and license specialized cases when needed. Archangel’s sandbox grades can also miss failures in live care, weakening the case for paying an acquisition premium. Reported limits on onward sharing could impair an acquirer’s ability to reuse longitudinal records, and the memo leaves change-of-control permissions unresolved. The evidence establishes neither a structural closing block nor materially inadequate proceeds under the strongest credible route. Watch fits the unresolved question of whether the product delivers enough incremental value to justify a purchase. (Defensibility, The Idea, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

What would a current medical AI developer pay to own Archangel’s evaluation environment and the data rights it needs, compared with licensing the service or using PhysicianBench? A paid repeat evaluation showing material gains over the developer’s existing tests, paired with a buyer-backed valuation range and license terms permitting the intended use after acquisition, would move the action to Reach out if the range could clear the holder’s cash hurdle after plausible deductions.
