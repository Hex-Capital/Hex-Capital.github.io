## The Idea

- **Problem:** AI data center developers relying on utility power and piped cooling water face multiyear grid connection waits and water constraints at some sites ([Launch YC, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WTh-viraj-aero-the-gas-turbine-that-powers-and-cools-data-centers), [Cleanview, Feb 2026](https://newsletter.cleanview.co/p/bypassing-the-grid-how-data-centers)).
- **Approach:** GENA condenses water from natural-gas exhaust, sends some to data center cooling and reinjects the rest as steam, with published specifications of 10 MW, 45% electrical efficiency and 2 ppm NOx ([Viraj Aero GENA specifications, Nov 2026 snapshot](https://www.viraj-aero.com/service/gena)).
- **Differentiation:** GENA combines power and recovered cooling water in modular gensets, while Boom’s Superpower supplies 42 MW per turbine, VoltaGrid packages Jenbacher reciprocating engines, and Capstone Energy+ supplies microturbines with heat-recovery options ([Viraj Aero](https://www.viraj-aero.com/service/gena), [Boom Supersonic](https://boomsupersonic.com/superpower), [VoltaGrid](https://voltagrid.com/2026), [Capstone FY2026 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1009759/000110465926077817/cgeh-20260331x10k.htm)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Project-specific equipment sales are a possible model given Viraj Aero’s sales calls and invitation to reserve GENA production slots ([Viraj Aero GENA launch, Oct 2026](https://www.viraj-aero.com/news-insights/discover-gena)).
- **TAM/SAM:** DC Market Insights estimates the broader global data center gas turbine market at $5.84B in 2026 with 16.39% CAGR from 2025 to 2035, including equipment, installation and service ([DC Market Insights, Nov 2026 snapshot](https://www.dcmarketinsights.com/report/data-center-gas-turbine-market)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** The founders solicit data center developers, hyperscalers and neoclouds directly for GENA and target turbine packagers and operators for a retrofit water-recovery system ([Launch YC, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WTh-viraj-aero-the-gas-turbine-that-powers-and-cools-data-centers), [Viraj Aero Genset Upgrades](https://www.viraj-aero.com/service/water-recovery-system)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Viraj Aero reports a patent on the compact exhaust-water condenser used to close the steam-injection loop ([Viraj Aero i-Lab announcement, Sep 2026](https://www.viraj-aero.com/news-insights/i-lab-2026-france-2030-backing-for-viraj-aeros-steam-injection-technology)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Field operating data and site-specific integration could create switching costs once GENA is installed, with the first power-and-water turbine still being built in November ([Joseph Risson on X, Nov 2026](https://www.xitter.gq/joseph_risson)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** [Inferred]: Turbine manufacturers can compete for on-site power contracts without replicating water recovery, and steam injection itself has a history in stationary turbines ([IRDI Capital Investissement, Sep 2026](https://www.irdi.fr/viraj-aero-levee-de-fonds-aeronautique-toulouse/), [Boom Supersonic, Dec 2025](https://boomsupersonic.com/press-release/boom-supersonic-to-power-ai-data-centers)).

## Key Risks

- **Continuous-duty performance:** [Inferred]: The aircraft ground tests leave GENA’s continuous-duty efficiency, emissions and cooling-water yield to be demonstrated together at a data center site, with the first power-and-water turbine reported under construction ([Launch YC, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WTh-viraj-aero-the-gas-turbine-that-powers-and-cools-data-centers), [Joseph Risson on X, Nov 2026](https://www.xitter.gq/joseph_risson)).
- **Order conversion:** [Inferred]: GENA’s disclosed demand depends on converting letters of intent into purchase contracts and deliveries, with production slots open for reservation ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WTh-viraj-aero-the-gas-turbine-that-powers-and-cools-data-centers), [Viraj Aero GENA launch](https://www.viraj-aero.com/news-insights/discover-gena), Oct 2026).
- **Fuel and permits:** [Inferred]: GENA still requires site gas supply and applicable air approvals, so the company’s low-NOx design addresses one component of the siting timeline ([Viraj Aero GENA launch, Oct 2026](https://www.viraj-aero.com/news-insights/discover-gena), [EPA data center air permitting resources](https://www.epa.gov/stationary-sources-air-pollution/clean-air-act-resources-data-centers)).
- **Aviation certification:** EYJA’s targeted 2029 market entry depends on certification, with Ailefroide flight testing planned for 2027 ([IRDI Capital Investissement, Sep 2026](https://www.irdi.fr/viraj-aero-levee-de-fonds-aeronautique-toulouse/)).
