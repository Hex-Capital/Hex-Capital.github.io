## The Idea

- **Problem** — Kairox says military maritime teams use separate aerial and underwater drones, each with its own operators and logistics ([Launch YC, Nov 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/XFg-kairox-systems-drones-that-can-fly-and-swim)).
- **Approach** — Tempest is a single autonomous vehicle designed for flight and underwater operation ([Launch YC, Nov 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/XFg-kairox-systems-drones-that-can-fly-and-swim)).
- **Differentiation** — Tempest spans air and underwater operation, while Bedrock Ocean Exploration maps the seafloor, Open Ocean Robotics monitors from the surface, and Voliro inspects industrial infrastructure from the air ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/XFg-kairox-systems-drones-that-can-fly-and-swim), [Bedrock](https://www.bedrockocean.com/), [Open Ocean Robotics](https://www.openoceanrobotics.com/our-story), [Voliro](https://voliro.com/blog/voliro-secures-23m-via-series-a-extension-to-modernize-infrastructure-with-aerial-robotics/)).
- **Business Model** — [Inferred]: Hardware sales through government procurement are a possible revenue path given the company’s stated procurement objective ([Launch YC, Nov 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/XFg-kairox-systems-drones-that-can-fly-and-swim)).
- **TAM/SAM** — No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution** — Kairox offers direct briefings for government and industry and reports engagement with the U.S. Marine Corps ([Kairox Contact](https://www.kairoxsys.com/contact), [Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/kairox-systems)).

- **Field qualification** — [Inferred]: Kairox reports technology readiness level 4, which documents laboratory component validation and leaves integrated-system field performance unverified in the cited materials ([Kairox Tempest](https://www.kairoxsys.com/tempest), [GAO Technology Readiness Assessment Guide](https://www.gao.gov/assets/gao-16-410g.pdf)).
- **Procurement conversion** — [Inferred]: The reported end-user engagement reflects concept feedback, and Kairox describes procurement as a future milestone ([Launch YC, Nov 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/XFg-kairox-systems-drones-that-can-fly-and-swim)).
- **Company-record discrepancies** — PitchBook lists Cambridge, Massachusetts and four employees, while YC lists San Francisco and three ([PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/1566252-64), [Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/kairox-systems), accessed Nov 2026).
