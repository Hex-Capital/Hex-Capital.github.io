# Mnemonic Labs -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

New Economies is the strongest candidate path. Mnemonic aims to learn and execute trading strategies directly from tokenized market activity. The company reports building its training pipeline and model architecture in under six weeks and using over 25 TB of tokens in its launch training run. The memo describes a separate model that reproduced features of order flow and generalized to held-out markets. That result supports the modeling approach, while Mnemonic’s own cost per successful strategy remains unmeasured. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

The scenario is that a quant desk could pursue strategies it previously skipped because analyst research, validation, and execution oversight cost more than the expected trading profit. The test is whether Mnemonic lowers the fully loaded cost per strategy that clears a preset live performance hurdle, including the cost of failed attempts. An independent desk would also need a reason to pay Mnemonic when a comparable model is available. The memo infers proprietary trading as the likely business model. Under that scenario, the unit of expansion is another live strategy or more capital allocated to it, with contribution measured after execution costs, market impact, data, compute, and oversight. The memo reports neither live results nor buyer commitments. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks, Key Facts)

### Countercase

Mnemonic could accelerate strategy research within the existing quant market while competitors capture the same gains. The memo describes a published route to market-event pretraining, competitors already trading live, and a potential advantage from proprietary fill and outcome data that depends on Mnemonic first trading live. It identifies no controlled scarce complement. This leaves market opening and durability under model parity unproven, with no direct evidence that comparable models erase Mnemonic’s edge. The decision gate supports Watch. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

Both founders have relevant quant experience, and the reported pipeline build shows early execution progress. Mnemonic also attributes $20M in annual trading P&L to Kalki’s prior work at Tower. The memo gives no live field test for this product. Launch attention does not establish buyer demand, and the memo does not quantify live contribution or capacity. (Founders & Team, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Will an independent quant desk pay for a repeat deployment after a live pilot shows that Mnemonic can make a previously uneconomic strategy viable? Compare fully loaded cost per successful strategy with the desk’s analyst-led workflow and include a functionally comparable model as an alternative. A paid repeat deployment with a documented reason for choosing Mnemonic after that comparison would move the action to Reach out. If the comparison shows that buyers switch readily to the comparable alternative, move to Pass.
