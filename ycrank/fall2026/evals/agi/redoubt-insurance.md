# Redoubt Insurance -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path B — New economies** is the stronger case at seed. Redoubt says small trucking fleets can lose loads while waiting for certificates or policy changes. Its shared account record could reduce the human work per request enough to make routine, time-sensitive servicing economical for fleets that brokers underserve. The memo documents that applicable insurance filings affect operating authority. Redoubt describes using agents to prepare submissions and handle servicing, with human approval before workflows move to autonomous execution. The labor savings at equivalent filing accuracy and turnaround remain unmeasured. *(The Idea, Key Risks)*

Redoubt operates a licensed brokerage and says it builds first-party account histories through servicing. If those histories let it resolve exceptions faster and maintain continuity through renewal, fleets could have a reason to stay when competing brokers have equally capable models. That advantage remains untested. The sole founder’s prior medical-coding and legal AI work and the brokerage launch support seed-stage execution credit. The founder reports 19 accounts and a testimonial citing $56,000 in premium savings within two days. Payment, repeat use, and demand from previously underserved fleets remain unverified. *(Defensibility, Founders & Team, Market & Traction)*

**Scale scenario.** If the reported $37,600 in expected annual net commissions covers those 19 accounts, it implies about $1,980 per account per year before acquisition and delivery costs. The next account’s contribution depends on realized commissions after acquisition, inference, human review, compliance, and exception handling. The memo gives neither measured costs nor renewal rates. *(Key Facts, Key Risks)*

### Countercase

Redoubt may be speeding up an existing brokerage service without making a new class of transactions viable. Human review and filing errors could consume the labor savings, and incumbents can adopt similar automation. Carriers and wholesalers control placement capacity, with no evidenced exclusive access, leaving Path A unsupported. These risks leave both Path B conditions unproven, so the outreach gate stays closed. The memo contains no like-for-like delivery or switching test that would trigger a thesis override. *(Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)*

### Decisive diligence question

Can a matched trial show that previously underserved small fleets repeatedly use Redoubt for time-sensitive servicing on commission-generating policies, at positive contribution after review and exceptions, and choose to stay when an equally capable broker offers comparable coverage? Measure fully loaded labor, turnaround, and filing accuracy against the conventional workflow, then test renewal choice against the comparable offer. Evidence on both conditions would move the case toward Reach out if it clears the score and confidence gates. Evidence that the service remains uneconomic or fleets readily switch under parity would move it to Pass.
