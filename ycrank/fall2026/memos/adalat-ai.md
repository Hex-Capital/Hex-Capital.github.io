## The Idea

- **Problem:** Indian trial judges and court staff rely on handwritten records, stenographers and paper files amid a backlog exceeding 50 million cases ( [Atal Innovation Mission, 2026](https://aim.gov.in/pdf/AI-Prenuers-Coffee-Table-Book-final.pdf), [Adalat AI, Aug 2026](https://www.adalat.ai/blog/legal/the-paper-court-what-50-million-pending-cases-look-like-from-the-ground-part-1)).
- **Approach:** Adalat AI turns courtroom speech into editable transcripts using models tuned for Indian languages and combines that workflow with digital case files, scheduling and WhatsApp case updates ([Adalat AI Products](https://www.adalat.ai/products), [WhatsApp Helpline](https://www.adalat.ai/whatsapp-helpline)).
- **Differentiation:** Adalat AI integrates vernacular dictation into district-court workflows, TERES pairs transcription with human review and arbitration e-bundles, and Jhana focuses on verifying citations and documents for courts and claims teams ([Adalat AI Products](https://www.adalat.ai/products), [TERES](https://teres.ai/), [Jhana](https://jhana.ai/)).
- **Business Model:** Legsys AI Technology Foundation operates as a grant-funded nonprofit, with court agreements reviewed by India’s tax tribunal specifying pro bono deployments ([Mumbai Income Tax Appellate Tribunal, Nov 2025](https://itat.gov.in/public/files/upload/1764138899-YafDGW-1-TO.pdf), [DAKSH and UNDP, 2026](https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-02/daksh_report_final_25.01_single_page_view.pdf)).
- **TAM/SAM:** Mordor Intelligence estimates the broader global court case management software market at $2.17 billion in 2026, with 10.84% forecast CAGR through 2031 ([Mordor Intelligence, 2026](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/court-case-management-software-market)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Adalat AI works through High Court pilots and agreements, then trains judges and staff during phased or statewide rollouts ([Adalat AI, Aug 2026](https://www.adalat.ai/blog/legal/andhra-pradesh-becomes-third-indian-state-to-mandate-statewide-adoption-of-adalat-ai)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Kerala’s High Court made Adalat AI the primary tool for recording trial-court witness depositions and requires approval for substitutes ([Kerala High Court memorandum, Sep 2025](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3ec01dc6a7e655d7e5840e66733e9ee67/uploads/2025/10/2025102791.pdf)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Court-specific templates, staff training and case-system integrations could increase switching costs as daily use deepens, with durability still dependent on continued court adoption ([Adalat AI Kerala retrospective, Nov 2026](https://www.adalat.ai/blog/legal/one-year-of-adalat-ai-mandate-in-kerala)).
- **Market structure:** Court-level approvals constrain substitution because Kerala requires its High Court IT directorate to approve an alternate transcription tool ([Kerala High Court memorandum, Sep 2025](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3ec01dc6a7e655d7e5840e66733e9ee67/uploads/2025/10/2025102791.pdf)).
- **Commoditization risk:** TERES already offers court-approved speech-to-text with human review, and Adalat AI has released open Koyal models for Indian-language dictation ([TERES](https://teres.ai/), [Adalat AI, Oct 2026](https://www.adalat.ai/blog/tech/koyal-model-release)).

## Key Risks

- **Infrastructure security:** A September 2026 investigation found production infrastructure and source-code exposure, and Adalat AI reports revoking credentials and restricting access, with confidential court documents and production audio outside the confirmed exposure ([Joint incident report, Sep 2026](https://www.adalat.ai/blog/tech/designing-for-compromise)).
- **Recording continuity:** Unreliable courtroom internet can interrupt evidence capture, and Adalat AI reports that offline buffering covers up to five minutes if connectivity returns within 30 minutes ([Adalat AI Kerala retrospective, Nov 2026](https://www.adalat.ai/blog/legal/one-year-of-adalat-ai-mandate-in-kerala)).
- **Transcription accuracy:** Adalat AI reports lower accuracy for live dictation than batch dictation, and Kerala requires recorded depositions to be endorsed and signed before upload ([Adalat AI Kerala retrospective](https://www.adalat.ai/blog/legal/one-year-of-adalat-ai-mandate-in-kerala), [Kerala High Court memorandum](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3ec01dc6a7e655d7e5840e66733e9ee67/uploads/2025/10/2025102791.pdf)).
- **Rollout dependence:** Court mandates still require staff adoption, and Adalat AI supplemented Kerala’s mandate with in-person training, refresher sessions and workflow changes ([Adalat AI Kerala retrospective, Nov 2026](https://www.adalat.ai/blog/legal/one-year-of-adalat-ai-mandate-in-kerala)).
- **Name ambiguity:** A separate GitHub project uses the Adalat AI name for a bilingual legal research tool, creating an attribution risk for repository metrics ([Independent GitHub project](https://github.com/hamzawithpython/Adalat-AI), [Company GitHub](https://github.com/adalat-ai-tech)).
