## The Idea

- **Problem:** PCB engineers using traditional design tools can spend weeks reading datasheets, selecting parts, drafting schematics, and routing boards, and errors found after fabrication can require another design and manufacturing cycle ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/VG7-resin-codex-for-electronics)).
- **Approach:** Resin turns requirements into editable schematics using purchasable components, generates missing symbols and footprints from datasheets, and places and routes boards against project rules before running design-rule checks ([Resin EDA](https://resineda.com/)).
- **Differentiation:** Resin combines chat-driven schematic drafting with PCB editing and routing, while Quilter specializes in physics-driven layout and JITX compiles designs from code ([Resin EDA](https://resineda.com/), [Quilter](https://www.quilter.ai/product), [JITX](https://www.jitx.com/blog/series-a-announcement)).
- **Business Model:** Resin advertises a free tier with 100 monthly AI credits, Lite at $39/month with AI layout, Pro at $149/month for the first user plus $99/month per additional user, and custom pricing, while its October terms list a different plan and price schedule ([Resin pricing, Nov 2026](https://resineda.com/), [Resin Terms, Oct 2026](https://resineda.com/terms)).
- **TAM/SAM:** Grand View Research estimates the global PCB design software market at $5.4B in 2025 and forecasts 12.8% annual growth from 2026 to 2033 ([Grand View Research, 2026](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/pcb-design-software-market-report)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Resin offers self-serve browser signup and used its YC launch to offer signup credits and onboarding and request introductions to electrical engineers and hardware leads ([Resin EDA](https://resineda.com/), [Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/VG7-resin-codex-for-electronics)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Resin describes its routing engine as proprietary and says approved symbols and footprints can be saved to a workspace library for reuse ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/VG7-resin-codex-for-electronics), [Resin EDA](https://resineda.com/)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Shared component libraries and revision histories could increase switching costs as teams accumulate projects, though retention tied to those features is unverified ([Resin EDA](https://resineda.com/)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** [Inferred]: Existing PCB vendors can reproduce parts of Resin’s automation within tools engineers already use, as Cadence offers automated placement and constraint-driven PCB routing in Allegro AI Studio ([Cadence](https://www.cadence.com/en_US/home/tools/pcb-design-and-analysis/allegro-ai-studio.html)).

## Key Risks

- **Technical feasibility:** Resin labels its autorouter beta and warns that generated designs can contain wiring, component, or clearance errors, with editable outputs, design-rule checks, and required engineering review before fabrication ([Resin EDA](https://resineda.com/), [Resin Terms](https://resineda.com/terms)).
- **Enterprise security review:** [Inferred]: Procurement may take longer at customers that require independent assurance, as Resin reports SOC 2 readiness in progress and offers security questionnaires and contractual data-handling terms ([Resin Security](https://resineda.com/security)).
- **Product substitution:** [Inferred]: Teams can obtain automated layout within Cadence or use Quilter with existing ECAD files, while Resin lowers migration friction through editable KiCad and Altium imports and exports ([Cadence](https://www.cadence.com/en_US/home/tools/pcb-design-and-analysis/allegro-ai-studio.html), [Quilter](https://www.quilter.ai/product), [Resin EDA](https://resineda.com/)).
- **Name ambiguity:** A Fort Worth plastics manufacturer is also listed as Resin Technologies Inc., while YC identifies the San Francisco PCB software company by `resineda.com` ([BizStanding](https://bizstanding.com/p/resin%2Btechnologies%2Binc-188635214), [Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/resin-technologies)).
