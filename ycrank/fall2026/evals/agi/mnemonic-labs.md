# Mnemonic Labs -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path A, scarce complements, is the strongest candidate.** The potentially scarce capability is a repeatable trading edge that survives live execution. Mnemonic says it trains directly on tokenized market activity to discover and execute strategies. The memo describes research showing that a market-event model can reproduce order-flow patterns in held-out markets. That supports the modeling premise. Mnemonic’s own live returns and execution costs remain unverified. (The Idea; Market & Traction; Key Risks)

**Scenario assumption.** Cheaper training could let Mnemonic refresh strategies faster, while a proprietary record of orders, fills, and outcomes could improve subsequent models. If Mnemonic trades its own capital, the equity case depends on the company retaining trading gains after fees, slippage, market impact, data, and compute costs. The scale test is whether incremental capital still earns attractive returns as order sizes rise. A San Francisco seed-stage technology company plausibly fits the mandate if its equity captures those economics. Path B has no supported route because the memo identifies neither a newly viable buyer transaction nor a reason for buyers to pay Mnemonic under model parity. (The Idea; Defensibility; Key Risks; Key Facts)

### Countercase

Well-funded rivals can already train on market events using published methods, and several competitors report trading live. They can build their own execution feedback while Mnemonic’s proposed feedback advantage remains hypothetical. A model that learns historical order flow could also lose its edge once its own orders move prices. This leaves Path A’s control gate open at seed, with a live test still capable of establishing or defeating the case. (Defensibility; Market & Traction; Key Risks)

Both founders’ quant experience and the company-reported build of its training pipeline in under six weeks support execution credit. The next execution gap is operating the system live. ### Decisive diligence question

Can Mnemonic complete an independently verified live pilot with a quant desk committing capital on commercial terms, in which its system repeatedly beats an equally resourced model trained on accessible market data at agreed risk and position sizes, net of trading and operating costs? A sustained advantage tied to execution feedback Mnemonic has durable rights to use, with terms that let it retain a share of the gains, would warrant rescoring for **Reach out**. If the matched model replicates the edge or realistic execution costs erase it, move to **Pass**.
