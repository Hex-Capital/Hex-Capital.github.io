# Synara -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Assuming strong product-market fit with security integrators, Synara’s best growth path is to let dealers resell monitoring across their installed camera base. The company describes a service that analyzes existing camera feeds, routes flagged incidents to operators, and lets dealers sell the service under their own brand. Existing recorder support makes the same monitoring job plausible abroad, though local dispatch requirements could narrow where the full service transfers. The public partner inquiry form documents a route into the program. Independent activation and payment remain unverified. (The Idea, Key Risks)

Synara describes monthly billing from dealers to site customers. A recurring wholesale fee to Synara and higher spend as a dealer adds sites are plausible assumptions. Site-specific rules, escalation contacts, and incident history could support continued use, while actual renewals and expansion remain unverified. The observed October 20 YC launch provides a dated public milestone. The memo gives no usable evidence of paid adoption, so commercial traction receives the absent-evidence default. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Facts)

If Synara bills dealers per monitored site, incremental contribution would be the wholesale fee less video processing, operator time for verification and response, and attributable onboarding and support. AI filtering could let each operator cover more sites if it reduces reviews while preserving response performance. The fee, review load, and staffing needed at peak times remain uncertain. (The Idea, Key Risks)

### Countercase

Even with strong fit, adding sites could require proportionally more operator coverage, while differences in police dispatch rules could make verified response a market-by-market rollout. Human verification is part of the advertised workflow, and the memo identifies a concrete example of local alarm verification rules. The company’s advertised response time and illustrative footage leave performance under load unverified. The structural gate stays open because the current evidence leaves room for bounded dispatch changes and for AI filtering to reduce operator work per site. (The Idea, Key Risks)
