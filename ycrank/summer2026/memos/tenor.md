## The Idea

- **Problem:** Companies deploying copilots, agents, and automations across disconnected systems can measure consumption but lack ownership, durable context, and visibility into the business outcomes produced by that AI spend ([Tenor website, Aug 2026](https://heytenor.com/); [YC, Aug 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/tenor)).
- **Approach:** Tenor assigns each AI worker a responsibility, success definition, budget, system access, escalation path, and persistent context, then links resources spent to results and reallocates tokens toward work producing value ([Tenor website, Aug 2026](https://heytenor.com/)).
- **Differentiation:** Relevance AI lets businesses construct teams of agents, Artisan supplies a sales-focused AI BDR, and Glean grounds agents in an enterprise knowledge graph, while Tenor centers its product on worker-level budgets, outcome attribution, and subsequent token allocation ([TechCrunch, May 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/05/06/relevance-ai-raises-24m-series-b-to-help-anyone-build-teams-of-ai-agents/); [Artisan, Apr 2025](https://www.artisan.co/blog/artisan-series-a); [Glean, Jun 2025](https://www.glean.com/blog/glean-series-f-announcement); [Tenor website, Aug 2026](https://heytenor.com/)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: The request-access and booked-demo flow indicates a sales-led enterprise product, while worker budgets and token attribution could support subscription or usage-linked pricing, but no public pricing was found ([Tenor website, Aug 2026](https://heytenor.com/)).
- **TAM/SAM:** The enterprise AI-agents market was estimated at $5.23B in 2025 and projected to reach $165.58B by 2035 at a 41.27% CAGR, while no public SAM estimate was found for outcome-attributed AI labor ([Kaiso Research, Jul 2026](https://www.marketresearch.com/Kaiso-Research-and-Consulting-v4350/Global-Enterprise-AI-Agents-Size-45605033/)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Tenor requests introductions to COOs, CIOs, and AI leaders and directs prospects to a founder-led demo calendar and hamze@heytenor.com ([YC launch, Aug 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/tenor)).

## Key Risks

- **Attribution integrity:** Tenor must connect work across multiple systems to business outcomes and token costs, but no public attribution benchmark, audit method, or customer validation was found ([Tenor website, Aug 2026](https://heytenor.com/)).
- **Enterprise permissions and data exposure:** The product requests Slack scopes and processes workspace data; documented mitigations include administrator approval, configurable access, security reporting, and deletion requests ([Tenor Support, Aug 2026](https://heytenor.com/support/)).
- **Autonomous-output error:** Tenor states that its AI can produce inaccurate output and should not be relied on without review for consequential decisions, while its worker design includes guardrails and human escalation paths ([Tenor Support, Aug 2026](https://heytenor.com/support/); [Tenor website, Aug 2026](https://heytenor.com/)).
- **Funded competitive set:** Relevance AI, Artisan, and Glean have raised $37M, $46.88M, and $765.3M respectively, while Tenor’s mitigation is its narrower emphasis on economic identity, outcome attribution, and budget reallocation ([TechCrunch, May 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/05/06/relevance-ai-raises-24m-series-b-to-help-anyone-build-teams-of-ai-agents/); [CB Insights, Aug 2026](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/artisan-ai/financials); [CB Insights, Aug 2026](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/glean-2/financials); [Tenor website, Aug 2026](https://heytenor.com/)).
