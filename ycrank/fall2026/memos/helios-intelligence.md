## The Idea

- **Problem:** Helios targets consumers seeking earlier warning of health changes between conventional blood tests, framing symptom-triggered testing as a barrier to tracking those changes over time ([Helios job posting on YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/helios-intelligence/jobs/IxOJ4s1-founding-engineer), Nov 2026).
- **Approach:** Helios says its at-home test is designed to measure more than 1,000 biomarkers from one drop of blood and combine repeat results with labs, wearables, health records, and outcomes in its Helios Foundation Model ([Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/helios-intelligence), Nov 2026).
- **Differentiation:** Helios proposes a model for tracking physiology and predicting disease risk, while Generation Lab estimates biological age from DNA methylation, SiPhox offers repeat conventional biomarker panels, Function uses Quest or clinician-collected draws, and VO Health estimates cardiorespiratory fitness from proteins ([Helios on YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/helios-intelligence/jobs/IxOJ4s1-founding-engineer), [Generation Lab](https://www.generationlab.com/science), [SiPhox](https://answers.siphoxhealth.com/about/what-is-siphox), [Function](https://www.functionhealth.com/), [Nomic Bio](https://nomic.bio/resources/nomics-proteomics-platform-to-power-vo-healths-molecular-fitness-insights)).
- **Business Model:** No public data found for Helios Test pricing. Roon, its separate consumer pouch product, advertises a cancel-anytime subscription from $9.16 per tin ([Roon storefront](https://www.takeroon.com/), Nov 2026).
- **TAM/SAM:** PW Consulting estimates the broader global home blood testing services market at $2.45B in 2025 with 11.5% projected CAGR through 2032, including wellness and chronic disease testing by finger prick or mobile phlebotomy ([PW Consulting](https://pmarketresearch.com/hc/home-blood-testing-services-market/), Jun 2026).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Founderland reported a consumer waitlist on myhelios.com on October 31, and Helios describes planned direct online kit ordering and web and mobile results ([Founderland](https://www.founderland.ai/articles/helios-intelligence-unveils-at-home-test-for-1000-biomarkers-mvx01g58), Oct 2026; [Helios job posting on YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/helios-intelligence/jobs/IxOJ4s1-founding-engineer), Nov 2026). Website not accessible at time of research.

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Helios reports using repeat draws and linked health records to train its model, with dataset size and exclusivity undisclosed ([Helios job posting on YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/helios-intelligence/jobs/IxOJ4s1-founding-engineer), Nov 2026).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Paired repeat tests and outcome data could improve person-specific predictions if members retest and share outcomes, with public retention and model calibration data unavailable ([Helios job posting on YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/helios-intelligence/jobs/IxOJ4s1-founding-engineer), Nov 2026).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** VO Health already uses a third-party platform to measure more than 1,000 proteins from a small self-collected blood sample, giving other developers a commercial route to broad protein profiling ([Nomic Bio](https://nomic.bio/resources/nomics-proteomics-platform-to-power-vo-healths-molecular-fitness-insights), Oct 2026).

## Key Risks

- **Analytical validation:** Published self-collection research found that delayed processing preserved agreement with venous draws for only some analytes, and Helios’s biomarker list and assay validation remain undisclosed ([Journal of Applied Laboratory Medicine](https://academic.oup.com/jalm/article/11/6/1546/8697258), Nov 2026; [Founderland](https://www.founderland.ai/articles/helios-intelligence-unveils-at-home-test-for-1000-biomarkers-mvx01g58), Oct 2026).
- **Clinical prediction:** Disease-risk estimates can be miscalibrated when training data leak into evaluation, a risk Helios flags alongside plans for model validation and checks on generated reports ([Helios job posting on YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/helios-intelligence/jobs/IxOJ4s1-founding-engineer), Nov 2026).
- **Health data exposure:** Linking lab results, wearables, and records concentrates sensitive data in one member profile, and Helios specifies encryption, access controls, and audit logging in its engineering plan ([Helios job posting on YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/helios-intelligence/jobs/IxOJ4s1-founding-engineer), Nov 2026).
- **Name ambiguity:** Elligint Health markets a separate Helios Intelligence Platform for payers, making company-name matches alone insufficient to attribute health-sector partnerships ([Elligint Health announcement](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/938779145/elligint-health-laguna-health-announce-strategic-partnership-to-further-advance-ai-driven-care-mgmt-for-payers-members), Oct 2026).
