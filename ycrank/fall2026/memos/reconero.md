## The Idea

- **Problem:** Patrol officers manually review body-camera footage, re-enter incident details across forms, and consult supervisors or policy manuals mid-shift, and Reconero cites estimates that officers spend roughly 40% of their work time behind a desk ([Launch YC, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WdX-reconero-ai-that-does-police-paperwork-so-officers-can-keep-their-communities-safe)).
- **Approach:** Reconero reads existing records and dispatch systems, identifies timestamped events across bodycam, CCTV, dashcam, and interview footage, drafts narratives linked to those timestamps, and selects and fills forms for officer review and signature ([Launch YC, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WdX-reconero-ai-that-does-police-paperwork-so-officers-can-keep-their-communities-safe)).
- **Differentiation:** Reconero combines visual event detection, form completion, and policy answers that cite department rules and state law, while Axon Draft One drafts narratives from evidence audio transcripts and Code Four also links drafts to video but offers built-in video redaction ([Launch YC, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/reconero), [Axon AI Era Plan](https://www.axon.com/products/axon-ai-era-plan), [Code Four YC profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/code-four)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: A department-level software subscription is a possible model given Reconero’s agency demos and data onboarding, with no public pricing or billing terms found ([Reconero Contact](https://reconero.com/contact/)).
- **TAM/SAM:** Grand View Research estimates the broader global law-enforcement software market at $18.26B in 2025 and 10.4% CAGR for 2026–2033, with no public estimate found for Reconero’s specific serviceable segment ([Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/law-enforcement-software-market-report)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** The founders solicit introductions to police chiefs and agency decision-makers through Launch YC and offer demonstrations using sample data or an agency-supplied export ([Launch YC, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/reconero), [Reconero Contact](https://reconero.com/contact/)).

## Key Risks

- **Report accuracy:** A blinded study found lower accuracy ratings for AI-assisted police reports in another system, and Reconero’s stated safeguards are links to the original footage and officer review and signature ([CrimRxiv, May 2026](https://www.crimrxiv.com/pub/u7azgqzd/release/1), [Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/reconero)).
- **Draft retention and disclosure:** California’s police-report AI rules took effect January 1, 2026, and require disclosure, first-draft retention, and an audit trail, with Reconero advertising logged AI actions and open-format export ([San José Police Department, Apr 2026](https://info.sjpd.org/records/pc-13650_library/Department_Orders/2026-015%20Use%20of%20Generative%20AI%20in%20Police%20Report%20Writing.pdf), [Reconero](https://reconero.com/)).
- **Time savings:** A randomized trial of a competing drafting tool found no improvement in report-writing speed, and Reconero’s launch disclosed no measured reduction in paperwork time ([Journal of Experimental Criminology](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11292-024-09644-7), [Fondo, Nov 2026](https://fondo.com/blog/reconero-links-ai-written-police-reports-to-the-footage-behind-them)).
- **Incumbent bundling:** Agencies already buying Axon Evidence can obtain report drafting through Axon’s AI Era Plan, while Reconero offers demonstrations on agencies’ existing data ([Axon AI Era Plan](https://www.axon.com/products/axon-ai-era-plan), [Reconero Contact](https://reconero.com/contact/)).
