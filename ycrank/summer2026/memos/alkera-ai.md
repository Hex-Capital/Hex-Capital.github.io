## The Idea

- **Problem:** Data engineers, analysts, and scientists using general coding agents risk incorrect transformations propagating through dbt models, dashboards, and business decisions, while remote computation is often managed through SSH command chains ([YC launch, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Rcy-alkera-the-data-agent-that-you-can-trust)).
- **Approach:** Alkera combines cross-platform column-level lineage, graph-based planning, a human-and-agent-verified knowledge base, fine-grained SQL and shell permissions, and IDE, CLI, and browser interfaces ([YC launch, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Rcy-alkera-the-data-agent-that-you-can-trust)).
- **Differentiation:** Against PromptQL, Altimate Code, Spacedock, SCRIBE, and general coding agents, Alkera adds whole-stack column lineage, downstream impact mapping, persistent team knowledge, and approval controls for destructive actions ([Alkera website, Aug 2026](https://www.alkera.ai/)).
- **Business Model:** Free provides limited usage, Plus costs $50 per month, Pro costs $250 per month, and Enterprise uses custom pricing for pooled usage, VPC or on-premises deployment, audit logs, SSO/SAML, SCIM, and SLAs ([Alkera pricing, Aug 2026](https://www.alkera.ai/pricing)).
- **TAM/SAM:** The global data-integration market was $17.9 billion in 2025 and is projected to reach $47.0 billion in 2033 at a 12.7% CAGR, while no public SAM estimate isolates agentic data engineering ([Grand View Research, 2026](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/data-integration-market-report)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Alkera uses a free self-service download for IDE and terminal users alongside booked calls and custom integrations for enterprise deployments ([Alkera website, Aug 2026](https://www.alkera.ai/)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Alkera recorded 83.28% Pass@1 on DataAgentBench as of July 16, 2026, compared with 81.85% for SCRIBE and 74.33% for Spacedock, but no public retention, patent, or proprietary-data evidence was found ([Alkera website, Jul 2026](https://www.alkera.ai/)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Customer-specific lineage graphs and accumulated human-verified organizational knowledge could create switching costs, but no deployment duration or retention data is public.
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage, as PromptQL, Altimate, Prophecy, and data-platform incumbents can combine agents with metadata, permissions, and data connectors.
- **Commoditization risk:** PromptQL already offers persistent shared context and deterministic data-layer permissions, while Altimate sells AI data-engineering teammates with enterprise deployment options ([PromptQL, Aug 2026](https://www.promptql.io/about); [Altimate pricing, Aug 2026](https://altimate.ai/pricing)).

## Key Risks

- **Benchmark generalizability:** DataAgentBench contains 54 queries across 12 datasets, nine domains, and four database systems, so the reported 83.28% Pass@1 does not establish performance across arbitrary production stacks ([UC Berkeley DataAgentBench, Aug 2026](https://ucbepic.github.io/DataAgentBench/)).
- **Enterprise compliance:** Alkera states that SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, GDPR, and HIPAA work is pending; VPC/on-premises deployment, zero-data-retention plans, SSO, SCIM, audit logs, and approval controls are offered as mitigations ([Alkera website, Aug 2026](https://www.alkera.ai/)).
- **Integration coverage:** Looker, Tableau, Power BI, Metabase, Sigma, and Hex integrations are marked “coming soon,” limiting presently documented lineage coverage into BI outputs; the company offers custom integrations ([Alkera integrations, Aug 2026](https://www.alkera.ai/integrations)).
- **Competitive overlap:** PromptQL has raised more than $135 million, Prophecy $113.5 million, and Altimate $2 million while offering overlapping AI-data workflows ([PromptQL, Aug 2026](https://www.promptql.io/about); [Forge, Aug 2026](https://forgeglobal.com/prophecy_stock/); [CB Insights, Aug 2026](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/altimate-ai/financials)).
- **Name disambiguation:** Search results also surface a French risk-and-claims management group named Alkera, while this dossier concerns Alkera AI, Inc. at alkera.ai ([French Alkera Group, Aug 2026](https://www.alkera.fr/le-groupe/); [YC profile, Aug 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/alkera-ai)).
