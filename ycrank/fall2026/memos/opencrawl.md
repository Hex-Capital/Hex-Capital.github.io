## The Idea

- **Problem:** [Inferred]: Developers building agents need searchable, current information from fragmented multimodal sources, otherwise requiring separate collection, processing and retrieval infrastructure ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/opencrawl)).
- **Approach:** Agents describe required data, and OpenCrawl discovers sources, builds and hosts crawlers, processes content, maintains search indexes and exposes retrieval through MCP, CLI or API ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/opencrawl)).
- **Differentiation:** [Inferred]: OpenCrawl emphasizes bespoke data pipelines, versus Exa’s shared search indexes and Firecrawl Alexandria’s provider/index library, although their capabilities overlap ([OpenCrawl](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/opencrawl), [Exa](https://exa.ai/blog/announcing-series-c), [Firecrawl](https://www.firecrawl.dev/changelog/introducing-alexandria-series-b)).
- **Business Model:** Advertised consumption pricing includes a $25 starter grant, $10 monthly allowance, $10 minimum top-ups and custom annual enterprise agreements, but paid usage awaits a published rate card ([company pricing](https://getopencrawl.com/), accessed October 27, 2026).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** Free agent-guided onboarding targets developers, while founder calls offer custom company-search deployments ([company website](https://getopencrawl.com/), October 27, 2026).

- **Competitive convergence:** [Inferred]: Firecrawl’s combination of source discovery, connectors and indexes could substitute for parts of OpenCrawl’s offering ([Firecrawl](https://www.firecrawl.dev/changelog/introducing-alexandria-series-b), September 2026).
- **Technical performance:** [Inferred]: OCR, transcription and scheduled crawling introduce extraction and freshness failure modes; source-linked results provide traceability, while performance benchmarks: No public data found ([company website](https://getopencrawl.com/)).
- **Permission enforcement:** [Inferred]: Internal search requires synchronized source permissions to prevent unauthorized retrieval; OpenCrawl states it checks workspace and source access ([company website](https://getopencrawl.com/)).
