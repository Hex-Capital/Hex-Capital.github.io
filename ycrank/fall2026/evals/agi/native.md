# Native -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path B — New economies** is the strongest fit. Native ranks campaign ideas, generates creative, and publishes organic posts and Meta ads with optional approval. The opportunity is to make recurring campaigns viable for small businesses that previously lacked the owner time or agency budget to run them. That remains an inference. The memo gives no comparison of owner or agency labor at equivalent quality, including approvals, revisions, and publishing exceptions. (The Idea)

Native reports more than $2M ARR and roughly 2,200 paying customers. Those claims suggest willingness to pay, but the memo leaves cohort retention and buyers’ prior marketing activity unknown. The three founders previously built Wepost together and shipped Native publicly in March 2026, with relevant experience in content, applied ML, and agency sales. The seed-led mandate is a plausible fit given the Fall 2026 YC batch and listed San Francisco presence. (Market & Traction, Founders & Team, Key Facts)

The listed $79 single-brand plan gives Native a repeatable unit of expansion, and the CEO reports that acquisition cost fell from about NOK 1,800 to NOK 800 per customer. The economics of adding another account remain unclear without retention and per-account inference, support, and publishing costs. (The Idea, Key Risks)

### Countercase

Native may mainly save time for businesses that already market regularly. Under model parity, they could switch to a comparable workflow or use tools bundled into the channels they already buy from. Google already generates brand profiles and campaign assets from websites, and Meta offers overlapping creative tools while providing Native’s current paid publishing channel. Native’s approval, edit, and performance history could make its rankings more valuable, but its observational research has yet to establish repeatable lift or buyer preference under parity. Neither Path B condition is established or defeated, so the outreach gate stays closed and Watch applies. The memo identifies no separately supported scarce complement for Path A. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Will small businesses that previously could not sustain recurring campaigns pay to renew Native after trying a comparable workflow with equally capable models and the same publishing channels? A paid trial should verify that prior constraint, measure owner and service labor including approvals, rework, and exceptions at equivalent campaign quality, and record renewal after buyers try both options. Meaningful labor savings and paid renewals favoring Native would support Reach out if the updated score clears 65. Evidence that neither workflow makes recurring campaigns viable, or that buyers readily substitute the comparable workflow, would move the action to Pass.
