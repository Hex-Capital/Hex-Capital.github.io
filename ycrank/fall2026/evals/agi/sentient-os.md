# Sentient OS -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Medium

### Company case

**New Economies** is the strongest path. Sentient shipped a Mac app that builds editable context overnight and uses it to draft replies and queue actions for approval. The current workflow requires workers to reconstruct context across messages, notes, and files. If reviewing and correcting a queued draft takes materially less time per successful follow-up, teams could handle routine follow-ups they currently skip. That is a scenario assumption. The memo gives no baseline labor time, measured savings, or evidence that teams now complete previously skipped work. (*The Idea*)

Configured workflows could give teams a reason to keep paying for Sentient when models are equally capable. The memo documents portable Markdown context and provides no evidence of retention or preference under functional parity. Sentient reports 10,000+ users at launch and use by 30+ enterprises. Those figures show early adoption, with payment and repeat usage still unverified. (*Defensibility*, *Market & Traction*, *Key Facts*)

The founders’ prior desktop software and model compression work, followed by the July alpha and October launch, supports execution at pre-seed. A compatible Mac seat is a plausible unit of expansion. Overnight inference uses the user’s device, while cloud-assisted actions, human review, integration, and support can still add costs. Enterprise pricing and contribution per deployment are unknown. (*Founders & Team*, *Market & Traction*, *The Idea*, *Key Risks*, *Key Facts*)

### Countercase

Sentient may save time on follow-ups that teams already do while Apple or another desktop assistant offers equivalent personal context and app actions. The memo documents native feature overlap, public inference tooling, and portable context. It establishes neither functional parity nor buyer switching, so the substitution risk leaves the path open for testing. The memo identifies no independently supported scarce complement. (*Defensibility*, *Key Risks*)

### Decisive diligence question

Will independent teams renew paid deployments for follow-ups they previously skipped when an equally capable alternative is available? A comparative pilot that records previously skipped work, shows materially lower labor per successful follow-up including review and correction, and ends in paid renewals would move the action to Reach out. If the pilot shows that savings apply only to existing work or that buyers readily switch at parity, move to Pass.
