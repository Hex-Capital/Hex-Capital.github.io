## The Idea

- **Problem:** Smartphone users handle routine tasks manually across apps, such as finding a contract in Drive, changing its permissions, sending the link in WhatsApp and placing a call ([Launch YC, Nov 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WkD-cyclon-the-ai-phone-to-replace-your-iphone)).
- **Approach:** Cyclon puts an agent in the OS to work across signed-in Android apps and notifications, run tasks in the background, and write and test requested apps in a cloud sandbox before installing them ([Launch YC, Nov 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WkD-cyclon-the-ai-phone-to-replace-your-iphone), [Cyclon Preorder FAQ](https://cyclon.ai/preorder)).
- **Differentiation:** Cyclon combines background task execution and app generation on an OS it plans to ship with its own handset, while Google Gemini automates supported tasks on existing Android devices, Apple integrates Siri AI into iOS, and Nothing generates Essential Apps for its phones ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WkD-cyclon-the-ai-phone-to-replace-your-iphone), [Google](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/android/gemini-intelligence-november-features-devices/), [Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/06/apple-unveils-next-generation-of-apple-intelligence-siri-ai-and-more/), [Nothing](https://nothing.community/d/60310/1)).
- **Business Model:** Direct handset preorders require a refundable $199 deposit toward an estimated $699 price, with AI usage included during the closed beta and no beta subscription required ([Cyclon Preorder Terms, Nov 2026](https://cyclon.ai/preorder-terms)).
- **TAM/SAM:** IDC forecasts a $613B global smartphone market in 2026, up 6.3% year over year, as a broad category proxy ([IDC Worldwide Quarterly Mobile Phone Tracker, 2026](https://www.idc.com/promo/smartphone-market-share/market-share/)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Cyclon collects waitlist signups and direct preorders on its website and offers first-batch buyers a refurbished Pixel 8a running Cyclon OS for closed-beta feedback ([Cyclon](https://cyclon.ai/), [Cyclon Preorder Terms, Nov 2026](https://cyclon.ai/preorder-terms)).

- **Moat today:** No defensibility signals found in public sources.

## Key Risks

- **Hardware authorization and delivery:** Custom handset delivery depends on FCC authorization, and Cyclon’s homepage gives Q2 2027 while its preorder terms give Q1 2027 and provide for deposit refunds if authorization fails ([Cyclon homepage](https://cyclon.ai/), [Cyclon Preorder Terms, Nov 2026](https://cyclon.ai/preorder-terms)).
- **App compatibility:** Banking, payment and work apps that require a Google-certified phone may fail or limit automation, and some identity checks still require user action ([Cyclon Preorder FAQ](https://cyclon.ai/preorder)).
- **Technical reliability:** The assistant can send messages or change app state after a single request, and Cyclon acknowledges mistakes are possible and lets users disable access by app ([Cyclon Preorder FAQ](https://cyclon.ai/preorder)).
- **Cloud data exposure:** Request-relevant messages, screen content or location can reach cloud AI providers that may retain data temporarily, and Cyclon describes controls by data type and app ([Cyclon Preorder FAQ](https://cyclon.ai/preorder)).
- **Name collision:** App-store searches also surface a separate productivity app called Cyclon by Denis Mikaya, creating room for confusion when attributing downloads or reviews ([AppBrain store archive](https://www.appbrain.com/appstore/cyclon/ios-1563704615)).
