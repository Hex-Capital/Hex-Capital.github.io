## The Idea

- **Problem:** Technical teams distribute decisions across Slack, email, meetings, research documents, schematics, code repositories, and project-management tools, leaving people and agents without a shared, traceable account of why cross-disciplinary changes occurred ([Shepherd website](https://askshepherd.ai/), Jul 2026).
- **Approach:** Shepherd connects authorized sources, indexes their content into memory and wiki artifacts, and exposes retrieved context through Slack, web, CLI, API, and MCP surfaces for agents such as Codex, Claude Code, and Cursor ([Shepherd website](https://askshepherd.ai/), Jul 2026; [Privacy Policy](https://askshepherd.ai/privacy), Jun 2026).
- **Differentiation:** Unlike Glean’s general work-AI platform and Dust’s configurable enterprise assistants, Shepherd currently positions around tracing decisions across research, hardware, firmware, software, and operations ([Shepherd website](https://askshepherd.ai/), Jul 2026; [Glean](https://www.glean.com/press/glean-raises-150m-series-f-at-7-2b-valuation-to-accelerate-enterprise-ai-agent-innovation-globally), Jun 2025; [Dust](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/18/3296726/0/en/dust-raises-40m-to-make-ai-multiplayer-inside-the-enterprise.html), May 2026).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Negotiated enterprise subscriptions or paid pilots, because the terms reference paid features, pilots, enterprise deployments, order forms, and separate agreements while publishing no price ([Terms of Service](https://askshepherd.ai/terms), Jun 2026).
- **TAM/SAM:** The global enterprise-search market was $4.87B in 2023 and is projected to reach $8.85B by 2030 at an 8.9% CAGR, while no public SAM estimate was found for cross-domain technical teams ([Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/enterprise-search-market), 2024).
- **GTM / Distribution:** [Inferred]: Founder-led outbound and scheduled demonstrations, based on the “Talk to the founders” CTA and Ishan Ramrakhiani’s statement that an outbound tool increased Shepherd’s calls ([Shepherd website](https://askshepherd.ai/), Jul 2026; [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/sebonomics_the-next-generation-of-software-wont-have-activity-7463272457689280513-DAb1), Jun 2026).

## Key Risks

- **Sensitive-corpus exposure:** Shepherd processes messages, documents, meetings, code-session metadata, audio-derived transcripts, embeddings, and source credentials, and its terms state ordinary production controls do not guarantee that employees can never access plaintext; scoped access, audit paths, and separately configured no-employee-plaintext deployments are listed mitigations ([Privacy Policy](https://askshepherd.ai/privacy), Jun 2026; [Terms](https://askshepherd.ai/terms), Jun 2026).
- **Retrieval and generation reliability:** Shepherd states that automated outputs can be incomplete, stale, or incorrect and requires human review before consequential use ([Terms](https://askshepherd.ai/terms), Jun 2026).
- **Connector dependency:** Source-service outages, permission changes, rate limits, security incidents, and API-policy changes can interrupt ingestion, while customers can limit or disconnect optional sources ([Terms](https://askshepherd.ai/terms), Jun 2026; [Privacy Policy](https://askshepherd.ai/privacy), Jun 2026).
- **Incumbent overlap:** Glean, Dust, Notion, and Guru already sell overlapping enterprise-search, knowledge, and agent products with larger disclosed financing bases, and no public Shepherd patents or exclusive data agreements were found ([Glean](https://sacra.com/c/glean/), 2026; [Dust](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/18/3296726/0/en/dust-raises-40m-to-make-ai-multiplayer-inside-the-enterprise.html), May 2026; [Notion](https://sacra.com/c/notion/), 2026; [Guru](https://forgeglobal.com/guru_stock/), 2025).
- **Name collision:** Two other active YC companies use the Shepherd name—commercial-insurance Shepherd from Winter 2021 and education Shepherd from Winter 2024—creating search-result and company-record ambiguity, partially mitigated by the askshepherd.ai domain and “YC S26” branding ([YC insurance Shepherd](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/shepherd), Jul 2026; [YC education Shepherd](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/shepherd-2), Jul 2026).
