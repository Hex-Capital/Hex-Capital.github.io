## The Idea

- **Problem:** In-person sales managers observe only a fraction of field meetings and otherwise rely on physical ride-alongs and outcome dashboards, leaving limited evidence for individualized coaching ([Nicolas Sebag, LinkedIn, Jul 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/nicolas-sebag-504747118_would-you-trust-didier-deschamps-to-coach-activity-7477345164689125376-_Ofz)).
- **Approach:** Reps record meetings, after which COACH compares their behavior with the team’s best closers, generates personalized feedback, flags where reps are stuck, and aggregates meetings into a leadership knowledge base ([YC company page, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ai-coach)).
- **Differentiation:** [Inferred]: Unlike Gong’s broad revenue platform, SalesRabbit’s field-sales workflow suite, and Second Nature’s simulated role-play, COACH centers on post-meeting analysis of face-to-face sales and team-specific closer behavior.
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: The reported ARR, forward-deployed pilots, and customer-success function indicate a recurring B2B subscription sold through pilots, but no public pricing structure was found.
- **TAM/SAM:** The adjacent global conversation-intelligence market was estimated at $2.96 billion in 2025 and projected to grow at a 20.8% CAGR through 2034 ([Growth Market Reports, 2026](https://growthmarketreports.com/report/conversation-intelligence-market)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** COACH reports a forward-deployed model with 100% pilot conversion and 0% churn, while its founders initially worked alongside Réseau ACEH representatives in door-to-door and in-home meetings ([YC company page, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ai-coach); [Mathieu Perez, LinkedIn, Jul 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mathieu-perez-719019201)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** COACH says every recorded meeting contributes to a shared sales knowledge base and that its AI learns the behavioral differences of each team’s best closers, creating a customer-specific conversation dataset ([YC company page, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ai-coach)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Accumulated, outcome-linked field-meeting recordings could improve vertical-specific coaching and create switching costs, but no dataset size, retention evidence, or proprietary-model documentation is public.
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** Rilla already records, transcribes, and analyzes in-person sales conversations for coaching, while Gong has $584 million in funding and a broader revenue-intelligence platform ([Sacra, Jul 2026](https://sacra.com/c/rilla/); [Gong, Jun 2021](https://www.gong.io/press/gong-raises-250-million-in-series-e-funding-at-7-25-billion-valuation)).

## Market & Traction

- **Traction signals:**
  - $500,000 ARR after five months and 120% month-over-month growth were reported by co-founder Mathieu Perez ([Mathieu Perez, LinkedIn, Jul 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mathieu-perez-719019201)).
  - A 32% average conversion-rate lift is reported on the company’s YC profile, without sample size or measurement methodology ([YC company page, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ai-coach)).
  - 100% pilot conversion and 0% churn are reported in co-founder Thomas Perez’s YC biography, without cohort size or observation period ([YC company page, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ai-coach)).
  - One Brand/Product Designer opening offered $100,000–$200,000 and 0.5%–1.5% equity ([YC company page, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ai-coach)).
  - Mathieu Perez had approximately 6,000 LinkedIn followers, Thomas Perez 8,000, and Nicolas Sebag 3,000 ([LinkedIn profiles, Jul 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mathieu-perez-719019201)).
- **Competitors (minimum 3, up to 5):**
  - Rilla ($78.9M raised, $70M estimated ARR): directly records and analyzes in-person field-sales conversations and offers upfront annual subscriptions with approximately five-seat minimums ([Sacra, Jul 2026](https://sacra.com/c/rilla/)).
  - Gong ($584M raised, more than $500M ARR): covers recorded conversations, forecasting, engagement, and enablement across a broader revenue operating system ([Gong, May 2026](https://www.gong.io/press/gong-growth-accelerates-past-55-yoy-arr-tops-500m)).
  - SalesRabbit ($4.27M raised, revenue unknown): provides field-sales canvassing and operational software rather than COACH’s stated team-specific post-meeting coaching mechanism ([CB Insights, Jul 2026](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/sales-rabbit/financials)).
  - Second Nature ($12.5M raised, revenue unknown): trains representatives through AI role-play simulations rather than analysis of completed field meetings ([Second Nature funding report, via search result, 2026](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Nature_%28AI_Company%29)).
- **Why now:** [Inferred]: Improvements in speech transcription and large-language-model analysis now permit automated evaluation of unstructured field conversations, while COACH’s founders state that 2026-era models and agent harnesses crossed the capability threshold needed for previously infeasible products ([Mathieu Perez, LinkedIn, Jul 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mathieu-perez-719019201)).

## Key Risks

- **Direct product overlap:** Rilla already markets recording, transcription, analytics, and coaching for in-person sales teams and had an estimated $70 million ARR in April 2026 ([Sacra, Jul 2026](https://sacra.com/c/rilla/)).
- **Metric verification:** The reported 32% conversion lift, 100% pilot conversion, 0% churn, $500,000 ARR, and 120% monthly growth lack disclosed customer counts, cohort definitions, and measurement periods ([YC company page, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ai-coach); [Mathieu Perez, LinkedIn, Jul 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mathieu-perez-719019201)).
- **Recording-data procurement:** The product requires recording customer-facing meetings, but no public security, consent-management, retention, or compliance documentation was found and the website was inaccessible at research time.
- **Pivot continuity:** The founders report six pivots and say COACH followed the termination of an earlier product called Lucy, so prior venture activity must be separated from COACH’s current-product metrics ([Thomas Perez, LinkedIn, Jul 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/thomas-perez-13011_8-rejections-3-failed-interviews-6-pivots-activity-7477353910245068800-xo3d); [Mathieu Perez, LinkedIn, Jul 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mathieu-perez-719019201)).
- **Name disambiguation:** “COACH” is shared with unrelated companies and consumer products, limiting reliable discovery of company-specific social, traffic, review, and press signals.
