# Sentient OS -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path B — New economies** is the strongest path. Sentient OS ships a Mac app that builds personal context overnight on 8 GB Apple silicon Macs and uses it to draft replies and queue cross-app actions for approval. The seed-stage hypothesis is that this makes routine follow-ups economical for workers who currently skip them because reconstructing context takes too long. The memo documents the workflow, while labor savings and newly viable use remain unmeasured. The reported pre-seed stage and San Francisco base fit the mandate. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Facts)

The company reports over 10,000 users at its October launch and use at more than 30 enterprises. Payment, buyer commitments, and repeat use are undisclosed. The founders have shipped a Mac alpha and a public release, and Tarun’s prior desktop-assistant and model-compression work is relevant. Enterprise deployment under customer privacy requirements remains unproven. (Market & Traction, Founders & Team, Key Risks)

Under the scenario, the labor test compares manual and Sentient-assisted follow-ups at equivalent quality, counting context gathering, approvals, retries, and exception handling per successful job. The expansion unit would be a paying enterprise account. Contribution would be license and support revenue less inference where borne by Sentient, integration, privacy onboarding, and ongoing support. Local processing could reduce hosted inference costs, while approval and exception work still affect buyer economics. The memo supplies no pricing, paid conversion, or delivery-cost measurements. (The Idea, Key Risks, Key Facts)

### Countercase

Sentient may speed up existing work without making previously skipped follow-ups worth doing. Even if it opens that use, Apple documents overlapping personal context and app actions, the underlying model and runtime are publicly available, and Sentient’s editable Markdown makes accumulated context portable. With abundant capital and equivalent models, competitors could reproduce enough of the workflow to erase willingness to pay. The memo identifies no separate bottleneck Sentient controls for Path A. These facts weaken value capture, while actual substitution and failure to open new use remain unproven. That leaves a testable Watch case under the thesis gates. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Will independent Mac-based teams repeatedly pay a price that covers Sentient’s delivery costs to complete follow-ups they previously skipped when they can choose an alternative with equivalent model capability, data access, and permissions? A paid comparative pilot should measure completed follow-ups at matched quality, all human labor per successful job, and repeat paid choice. Verified labor savings that unlock previously skipped work, favorable account contribution, and repeat preference under parity would support Reach out if the updated score and confidence clear the outreach gates. If teams still skip the work or switch to the equivalent alternative, move to Pass.
