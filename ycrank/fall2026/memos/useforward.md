## The Idea

- **Problem:** Commercial debt advisors manually contact lenders through existing relationships, while funds screen incoming deals against mandates that can change before an advisor learns of them ([Launch YC, Nov 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/XBO-forward-the-private-credit-network-for-leading-debt-advisors)).
- **Approach:** Forward packages advisor-supplied documents into a financing brief, matches the deal against current lender mandates, and coordinates submissions, document requests, and indicative terms ([Forward for Advisors](https://www.useforward.co/advisors), [Forward for Funds](https://www.useforward.co/funds)).
- **Differentiation:** Forward sources deals through debt advisors and coordinates lender outreach, while Lev sells commercial real estate workflow software, Leverest offers sponsor-led debt execution tools, and GetLiquid adds settlement and reporting for originators and capital providers ([Forward](https://www.useforward.co/advisors), [Lev](https://www.lev.com/about), [Leverest](https://leverest.net/platform-overview/), [GetLiquid](https://www.getliquid.io/)).
- **Business Model:** Lenders pay 25 basis points on funded loans that Forward introduces, and advisors use the service for free and keep their own fees ([Forward lender pricing](https://www.useforward.co/funds), [Forward advisor pricing](https://www.useforward.co/advisors)).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment, with broader global private credit AUM at US$4.1 trillion at year-end 2025, up 17% year over year ([Alternative Credit Council, Oct 2026](https://acc.aima.org/article/press-release-global-private-credit-market-hit-us-4-1tn-aum-in-2025-acc-data-reveals.html)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Forward used cold calls and LinkedIn outreach, then began a controlled rollout with an exclusive debt-advisor partner in Southern California commercial real estate ([Startmate, Oct 2026](https://www.startmate.com/writing/forward-founder-story), [Launch YC, Nov 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/XBO-forward-the-private-credit-network-for-leading-debt-advisors)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Forward reports that its initial Southern California debt-advisor partner went exclusive after the first transaction ([Launch YC, Nov 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/XBO-forward-the-private-credit-network-for-leading-debt-advisors)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Lender responses could improve matching across advisors’ deals if Forward sees enough transactions to keep mandate data current, and public sources provide no measured lift in match performance ([Fondo, Nov 2026](https://fondo.com/blog/forward-routes-private-credit-deals-without-replacing-debt-advisors)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** Lev and Leverest already offer lender discovery and financing workflows, giving advisors alternatives for those tasks ([Lev](https://www.lev.com/), [Leverest Marketplace](https://leverest.net/product/marketplace/)).

## Key Risks

- **Closing dependency:** Forward collects its fee only when a lender funds a loan, making collections dependent on deals progressing beyond indicative terms ([Forward lender pricing](https://www.useforward.co/funds), [Fondo](https://fondo.com/blog/forward-routes-private-credit-deals-without-replacing-debt-advisors)).
- **Advisor concentration:** The controlled rollout began with one exclusive Southern California CRE advisor after Forward qualified 63 advisory firms nationwide ([Launch YC, Nov 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/XBO-forward-the-private-credit-network-for-leading-debt-advisors)).
- **Future allocation conflict:** Forward’s planned credit fund could compete with platform lenders for deals, and the company says the fund would follow a published allocation policy ([Fondo, Nov 2026](https://fondo.com/blog/forward-routes-private-credit-deals-without-replacing-debt-advisors)).
- **Name collision:** An unrelated API-integration startup also launched as Forward, creating a risk of misattributing Product Hunt activity ([ProvenRadar, Jun 2026 launch record](https://provenradar.com/product/forward)).
