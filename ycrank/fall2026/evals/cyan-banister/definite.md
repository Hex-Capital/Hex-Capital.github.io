# Definite -- Cyan Banister Evaluation

In 2032, I can imagine asking my books a question and getting an answer that survives an accountant asking, “Where did that number come from?” Definite’s separation of model-proposed mappings from deterministic calculations makes that future more believable. But getting there requires customer definitions to become reusable and additional agents to operate without another round of bespoke implementation. The dossier does not establish those transitions. I can imagine the destination; I cannot yet underwrite Definite’s path to it.

The interesting object here is the financial model underneath the agents. Customer-specific mappings, source-to-cell lineage, retained rules and historical snapshots could become the memory that every subsequent workflow needs ([Security](https://www.usedefinite.com/security)). This fits my pattern of looking for concealed infrastructure inside mundane workflow repair. Why reconstruct the same financial relationships every month? If Definite preserves them reliably, each new task should become cheaper to build. That is the strongest argument against passing. Workato already offers semantic context and orchestration, however, and Definite’s scoped connector work leaves open whether its accumulated knowledge compounds across deployments or stays trapped inside individual implementations.

There is a real operating wound: finance teams repeatedly assemble relationships across ledgers, operational systems and spreadsheets. Giving that time back matters. But the dossier identifies neither a quantified recurring burden nor a gatekeeper extracting a specific rent. Definite could give finance teams more control over their own definitions; it could also make them dependent on its engineers whenever those definitions change. I give credit for the concrete workflow mechanism, while leaving the larger agency claim unearned.

The three founders have useful pieces of the problem between them: BitGo approval controls, Optiver trading systems, Boosted.ai financial agents and Meta recommendations. Gurshabd also has a credited 2023 Excelerate repository artifact, which is more informative than a degree alone ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/definite), [GitHub](https://github.com/LuffyAI/HackDearborn2023Work)). These establish relevant building experience, but little evidence of a sustained obsession predating Definite. Mazin’s September 2026 account of failed demos and compliance LOIs, followed by contract-first selling, shows the team learning an important business lesson. It does not establish that customers now repeatedly rely on the broader product.

I will back a company before revenue when a functioning artifact and unusually deep founder commitment make the missing commercial proof tolerable. That exception does not carry this dossier: the earlier regulatory-reporting benchmark is not current-product adoption, and monthly contract terms do not reveal completed purchases or repeated use. The 297 LinkedIn followers add nothing to that question. The other exceptions offer no reason to waive operating gates here. Gurshabd is identified as CEO; there is no documented leadership shell game. Read-only access and human approvals are sensible controls, although read-only access still leaves financial-data custody to examine. I see no established fatal red flag, and I would not invent one from missing information.

I pass with my own money today. Definite has a credible place in the stack, but insufficient evidence that customers will make its model the persistent foundation of their finance work. My single flip condition is a verifiable deployment where a paying finance team adds a second recurring workflow using the existing mappings and lineage, without bespoke connector engineering. That would show the infrastructure beginning to compound—the particular behavior this bet needs.
