## The Idea

- **Problem:** Frontier AI labs, enterprise AI teams, and agent startups need training data for multi-step work across documents, communications, and software tools because static, text-only benchmarks do not reproduce these workflows ([Ooak Data, Aug 2026](https://ooakdata.com/)).
- **Approach:** Ooak sources multimodal operational data from companies, transforms it through an automated anonymization pipeline into structurally preserved digital twins, and creates expert-level RL tasks within those environments ([Ooak Data, Aug 2026](https://ooakdata.com/)).
- **Differentiation:** Ooak uses licensed operational company data rather than AfterQuery’s real-API and expert-curated tasks, Mercor’s expert network plus reconstructed enterprise applications, or Scale AI’s broader annotation and RL-environment platform ([Ooak Data](https://ooakdata.com/), [AfterQuery](https://www.afterquery.com/products), [Mercor](https://www.mercor.com/blog/mercor-to-acquire-deeptune/), [Scale AI](https://sacra.com/c/scale-ai/)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Ooak sells bespoke dataset and RL-environment contracts to AI labs while acquiring source-company data through negotiated payments of up to $300,000, with no public buyer pricing page ([YC job posting](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ooak-data/jobs/TqbBKm2-technical-ops-manager), [partner page](https://ooakdata.com/partner)).
- **TAM/SAM:** The global AI-training-dataset market was estimated at $4.44 billion in 2026 and projected to reach $23.18 billion by 2034 at a 22.9% CAGR, while no public SAM estimate isolates real-company workflow RL environments ([Fortune Business Insights, 2026](https://www.fortunebusinessinsights.com/ai-training-dataset-market-109241)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Ooak uses direct sales to AI labs, partnerships with companies supplying operational data, and six YC-listed sales, marketing, operations, and engineering openings, including a US GTM role ([YC jobs, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ooak-data/jobs)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Ooak reports seven-figure contracts with the two largest AI labs and additional smaller-lab partnerships, alongside a contracted pipeline that converts signed data deals into RL-ready datasets ([YC job posting, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ooak-data/jobs/TqbBKm2-technical-ops-manager)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: A growing library of contractually acquired, cross-tool company workflows could become difficult to reproduce, but the stated goal of acquiring and anonymizing 300-plus company ecosystems remained forward-looking in July 2026 ([YC jobs](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ooak-data/jobs)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** Mercor, AfterQuery, and Scale AI already supply RL environments or adjacent training data and have larger expert networks, funding bases, or customer footprints ([Mercor](https://www.mercor.com/research/), [AfterQuery](https://www.afterquery.com/products), [Scale AI](https://sacra.com/c/scale-ai/)).

- **Competitors:**
  - Mercor ($483.6M raised, $2B annualized gross revenue): combines a domain-expert network with hundreds of reconstructed enterprise applications, whereas Ooak emphasizes source-company operational data ([Contrary Research](https://research.contrary.com/company/mercor), [Mercor, Jul 2026](https://www.mercor.com/blog/mercor-to-acquire-deeptune/)).
  - Scale AI ($1.6B venture funding before Meta’s $14.3B investment, $870M 2024 revenue): supplies broad training-data, annotation, evaluation, and RL-environment services rather than focusing only on licensed company-workflow twins ([Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/scale-ai/), [AP, Jun 2025](https://apnews.com/article/4b55aabf7ea018e38ffdccb66e37cf26)).
  - AfterQuery ($30.5M raised, $100M annualized revenue): builds expert datasets and tool-calling environments over real APIs and MCP servers rather than Ooak’s anonymized enterprise datasets ([Sacra, Apr 2026](https://sacra.com/c/afterquery/)).
  - Datacurve ($17.7M raised, revenue unknown): concentrates on software-development training data, while Ooak targets cross-functional business workflows ([TechCrunch, Oct 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/09/datacurve-raises-15-million-to-take-on-scaleai/), [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/datacurve/)).
- **Why now:** [Inferred]: Tool-using agents exposed a training gap when recent research found under-50% task success and roughly 25% eight-run consistency, while Mercor’s July 2026 Deeptune acquisition demonstrated buyer demand for enterprise-software RL environments ([Ooak Data Research, Feb 2026](https://ooakdata.com/research/the-data-problem-in-agentic-ai), [Mercor, Jul 2026](https://www.mercor.com/blog/mercor-to-acquire-deeptune/)).

## Key Risks

- **Customer concentration:** Seven-figure contracts are attributed to two AI labs, creating renewal and pricing exposure if either customer internalizes environment production; additional smaller-lab partnerships provide some diversification ([YC job posting, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ooak-data/jobs/TqbBKm2-technical-ops-manager)).
- **Data privacy and provenance:** The product processes communications, documents, financial figures, and tool data, creating consent, licensing, GDPR, and re-identification exposure; Ooak states that it uses NDAs, regional storage, automated anonymization, written usage terms, and deletion rights ([partner page](https://ooakdata.com/partner)).
- **Competitive substitution:** Mercor acquired Deeptune after it had reconstructed hundreds of enterprise applications, while Scale and AfterQuery also offer RL environments, allowing buyers to consolidate with larger vendors ([Mercor, Jul 2026](https://www.mercor.com/blog/mercor-to-acquire-deeptune/), [AfterQuery](https://www.afterquery.com/products), [Scale AI](https://sacra.com/c/scale-ai/)).
- **Pivot and scaling execution:** The founders report one pivot during the company’s first year, while the current plan calls for acquiring and anonymizing more than 300 company ecosystems within six months and filling six roles ([founder post, Jul 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/lamyg_one-year-in-one-pivot-later-ooak-data-activity-7472594412141977600-iKHY), [YC jobs](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ooak-data/jobs)).
