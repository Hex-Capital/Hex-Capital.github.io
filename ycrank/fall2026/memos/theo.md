## The Idea

- **Problem:** Small businesses need continuing search optimization after website launch, currently handled through DIY tools, freelancers, or agencies; Theo’s self-selected audit sample found estimated zero monthly Google visitors for 47% of sites with search data ([Theo Research, October 2026](https://theo.site/research/state-of-small-business-websites)).
- **Approach:** Theo builds and hosts websites from conversational instructions, connects Google Analytics and Search Console, researches keywords and competitors, and drafts weekly pages, posts, and technical fixes for approval or optional automatic publishing ([Theo About, October 2026](https://theo.site/about)).
- **Differentiation:** Theo bundles ongoing publishing and optimization, whereas Wix provides AI creation plus editing tools and B12 offers AI creation with optional human specialists ([Theo](https://theo.site/about), [Wix](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-harmony-editor-the-difference-between-wix-editor-and-wix-harmony), [B12](https://www.b12.io/about)).
- **Business Model:** Monthly subscriptions per website cost $29.99 Basic, $69.99 Starter, or $129.99 Pro; Starter includes a 30-day trial, ongoing SEO starts at Starter, requested edits consume credits, and Enterprise pricing is customized ([Theo Pricing, accessed October 27, 2026](https://theo.site/pricing)).
- **TAM/SAM:** The broader global website-builder market is estimated at $5.3 billion in 2026, with 12.7% CAGR through 2035; Theo-specific SAM: No public data found ([Grand View Research, 2026](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/website-builder-market-report)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Theo offers free audits and website drafts, publishes industry-specific landing pages, and requests introductions to accountants, agencies, chambers of commerce, franchise groups, and business banks ([Theo](https://theo.site/), [YC Launch, October 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/W36-theo-the-site-builder-and-engine-that-gets-your-business-found)).

## Key Risks

- **Search-platform dependency:** Growth work requires an active domain plus Google Analytics and Search Console connections; it remains inactive until those prerequisites are completed ([Theo FAQ](https://theo.site/faq)).
- **Incumbent overlap:** Durable already markets SEO/GEO alongside website creation, while Wix released Harmony in January 2026, overlapping Theo’s creation and discovery functions ([Durable](https://durable.com/), [Wix Help](https://support.wix.com/en/article/wix-harmony-editor-the-difference-between-wix-editor-and-wix-harmony)).
- **Migration and functionality constraints:** Theo hosts replacement sites rather than modifying externally hosted sites and lacks native payment processing, bookings, and member databases; integrations and external checkout links address some requirements ([Theo About](https://theo.site/about)).
- **Traction measurement limits:** Published sites-built figures do not identify live or paying sites, and the reported average traffic increase lacks cohort and measurement details ([Theo Press Kit](https://theo.site/press), [YC Launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/W36-theo-the-site-builder-and-engine-that-gets-your-business-found)).
