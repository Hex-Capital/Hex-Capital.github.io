# Pomerado -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Medium

### Company case

Conditional on strong product-market fit, Pomerado can scale by turning recurring work in API-less portals into reusable MCP and REST tools. Agent developers can use the same interface across markets, and the public catalog grew from 46 tools across 23 sites on October 17 to 584 tools across 447 sites on November 2. That breadth supports reuse of the underlying product. Each new portal can still bring its own authentication and authorization requirements. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

The open-source core, npm distribution, and self-serve signup provide a credible bottom-up path. The memo establishes a way to get started and a published $50 monthly Premium plan. It leaves independent checkout and production setup for the typical buyer unverified. Recurring portal tasks and a growing library of configured integrations could keep customers subscribed. Expansion beyond Premium depends on custom outcome-based Business pricing, and the memo gives no account-level evidence that growing workloads raise recurring bills. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Defensibility)

For a Premium account, incremental contribution is $50 per month less browser, proxy, sandbox, and model costs for executions and repairs, plus attributable onboarding, support, and manual maintenance costs. Reusing integrations and automating repairs could spread maintenance costs across more runs. The founders report a 2.2% run error rate and automatic resolution of 82% of errors. Representative costs at production usage remain unknown. Launch activity and founder-reported use show developer interest, while the memo leaves realized revenue and pre-revenue status unknown, so initial traction receives the default. (The Idea, Key Risks, Market & Traction, Key Facts)

### Countercase

The strongest opposing case is that the open-source funnel attracts experimentation while valuable enterprise workflows require recurring, portal-specific intervention. Portal operators can change interfaces, authentication, or access policies, and customers must maintain authorization. Sensitive-data processing can complicate enterprise rollout. The company benchmark used prebuilt integrations and one attempt per task, so it leaves the burden of setup and ongoing maintenance unresolved. The structural gate stays open because the memo does not establish that substantial rebuilding by market or uneconomic human work per buyer is unavoidable. (The Idea, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can successive cohorts of developers reach paid production independently and remain contribution-positive? A rising count of paying production accounts across comparable periods, with account records showing self-guided setup, recurring workloads, and positive contribution after execution, repairs, and support, would move the action to Reach out.
