## The Idea

- **Problem:** Frontier labs testing voice agents against interruptions, accents and noisy calls can use static test sets, which Perit says miss failures that surface during live interaction ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/XT2-perit-expert-led-datasets-for-frontier-labs), Nov 2026).
- **Approach:** Perit recruits practitioners by sector and locale to record, annotate and grade speech, robotics demonstrations and agent tasks, then delivers data with QA reports, consent references and reviewer histories ([Perit](https://perit.ai/), [Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/XT2-perit-expert-led-datasets-for-frontier-labs)).
- **Differentiation:** Perit packages live speech evaluation and egocentric capture with its annotation service, while Mercor markets enterprise workflow evaluation, micro1 operates a dedicated robotics data lab, and Turing publishes a software-engineering benchmark drawn from real repositories ([Perit](https://perit.ai/), [Mercor](https://www.mercor.com/blog/series-d/), [micro1](https://www.micro1.ai/team/why-i-joined-micro1-by-mazen), [Turing Research](https://research.turing.com/swebench)).
- **Business Model:** Perit quotes by program and delivery unit, such as an audio hour or video episode, itemizes contributor pay, and prices exclusive rights separately from its default non-exclusive license ([Perit Pricing](https://perit.ai/pricing)).
- **TAM/SAM:** Grand View Research estimates the broader global AI training dataset market at $3.9B in 2026 with 22.6% CAGR through 2033, while no public SAM estimate was found for Perit’s service mix ([Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/ai-training-dataset-market), 2026).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Buyers can submit a model failure for a scoped quote and pilot or request catalog data, while contributors can submit datasets or pitch expert-authored tasks through Perit ([Perit](https://perit.ai/), [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/perit-marketplace), Nov 2026).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Perit reports 650 transcribers and aligners on its roster and uses its own Foundry workspace for annotation, reviewer calibration and batch QA ([Perit About](https://perit.ai/about), [Perit Data Annotation](https://perit.ai/services/data-annotation), accessed Nov 2026).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Repeat programs could build a roster of specialists whose work has already passed customer-specific QA, though public sources give no retention or exclusivity figures for that roster ([Perit Data Annotation](https://perit.ai/services/data-annotation), [Perit Pricing](https://perit.ai/pricing)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** The default non-exclusive license limits buyer exclusivity, and competing vendors already offer overlapping robotics capture and reinforcement-learning environments ([Perit Pricing](https://perit.ai/pricing), [micro1 Robotics](https://www.micro1.ai/team/why-i-joined-micro1-by-mazen), [Turing RL Environments](https://www.turing.com/frontier-ai/rl-environments)).

## Key Risks

- **Buyer concentration:** [Inferred]: A small group of lab customers can make contract renewals material to revenue, given the CEO’s report of six lab customers and the launch disclosure of multimillion-dollar contracts ([CEO LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kshitijdixit_i-am-excited-to-share-that-perit-ai-yc-f26-activity-7502597870538366976-3u6g), [Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/XT2-perit-expert-led-datasets-for-frontier-labs)).
- **Consent and licensing:** Voice and first-person video can capture identifiable people, and Perit says it records consent on file, rejects footage with unconsented identifiable bystanders and cannot recall copies already licensed and delivered ([Perit Privacy](https://perit.ai/privacy), Sep 2026).
- **Quality at higher throughput:** [Inferred]: Expanding from 100 to the stated capacity of 2,000 RL tasks per week could strain expert review, with task calibration and pilot QA serving as published controls ([YC company profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/peritai), [Perit Expert Tasks](https://perit.ai/expert-tasks), [Perit Pricing](https://perit.ai/pricing)).
- **Reported scale:** Perit’s Launch YC post reports 16,000+ trained experts, while YC’s LinkedIn announcement cites 1,600+ experts and gives no reconciliation of the two counts ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/XT2-perit-expert-led-datasets-for-frontier-labs), [YC LinkedIn announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/y-combinator_perit-ai-yc-f26-is-the-human-data-partner-ugcPost-7527399616774905857-1Pq9), Nov 2026).
- **Name ambiguity:** PERIT Consulting also operates in technology recruiting, and the training-data startup identifies its legal operator as Zyno AI Inc ([PERIT Consulting](https://www.perit.com/en/about-us.html), [Perit Terms](https://perit.ai/terms)).
