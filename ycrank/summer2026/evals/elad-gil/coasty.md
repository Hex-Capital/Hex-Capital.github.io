# Coasty -- Elad Gil Evaluation

82.81% on OSWorld Verified makes Coasty a shipping-led technical bet with a weak structural distribution position: Coasty’s highest signal is how quickly Prateek Jannu and Nitish Kovuru turn models into working systems, while Coasty’s lowest is whether that system becomes an index on computer use rather than another agent fighting UiPath, Browserbase, and foundation-model vendors for share. The March-to-July 2026 shift from an “AI employee” VM to developer-controlled legacy-software automation shows iteration, but it does not yet establish durable control of the workflow path.

**Perplexity** is the high rung for founder shipping: Aravind repeatedly returned a week later with the discussed product built. Coasty shares that mechanism through Prateek’s open-cowork repository, Nitish’s prior agentic-sales automation, a published benchmark repository, and the rapid product repositioning between March and July 2026. Coasty diverges because the dossier documents artifacts across months, not repeated customer-driven releases between conversations, and the earlier product’s 700-person waitlist is not confirmed as usage of Coasty’s current API. The shipping evidence is materially better than credentials alone, but it has not reached the Perplexity cadence.

**Lyft** is the low rung’s failure mechanism: occupying the weaker position in a market where scale and distribution consolidate around someone else. Coasty risks the same structural problem because UiPath already produces $1.43 billion in annual revenue, Adept has raised $415 million, Browserbase has raised $67.5 million, and no barrier prevents those players or foundation-model providers from adding screenshot grounding and recovery. Coasty could become infrastructure across many back-office categories, but Coasty currently sells metered actions and VM hours without owning the customer system, model, or exclusive data layer. That is fighting for share, not yet becoming an index on computer use.

Summer 2025 model inadequacy followed by Coasty’s 82.81% verified result against Claude’s 62.9% is a credible recent catalyst, even though “why now” remains partly inferred. Over the next 18 months, that threshold can compound for Coasty if legacy workflows previously below the automation reliability line become economically deployable; every newly viable portal expands the addressable workflow set. The mechanism fails if 82.81% is benchmark-local and regulated customers require near-perfect execution, because Coasty’s mock healthcare demonstrations and permissive session-data terms would then leave the market technically reopened but commercially closed.

Thousands of active users and paying customers across four continents would move Coasty above a normal pre-seed default, but Coasty discloses neither ARR nor month-over-month growth, and the claim spans a product pivot. For the score to flip into the Harvey range, the current developer product would need a clean cohort showing more than 20% monthly paid-usage growth, repeat production workflows, and named customers buying after the proof of concept. Without that separation, “thousands” may mix current buying behavior with March’s consumer/SMB launch—a false signal rather than evidence that Coasty is just working.

**Color Genomics** is the relevant single-anchor parallel: workflow infrastructure can capture value from a hard technical layer without underwriting the hard layer itself. Coasty uses commercially available multimodal models and concentrates its work in grounding, state, verification, recovery, and replay, so Coasty does not require a new foundation-model breakthrough. The architecture is not entirely single-miracle, however: moving from 82.81% benchmark success to safe execution in payments, patient records, and regulatory filings also requires reliability, security posture, and enterprise adoption to converge. That is still product-and-GTM risk rather than new science, which keeps Coasty inside my investable boundary.

**UiPath’s $1.43 billion FY2025 revenue** makes my 36-month forecast specific: by July 2029, Coasty will reach $5 million–$10 million in ARR and be acquired by UiPath for $100 million–$200 million as a computer-use recovery layer. Coasty’s founders can ship fast enough to create technical scarcity, while Coasty’s weak index position makes acquisition more probable than an independent category-defining outcome. UiPath would be buying the benchmark lead, recovery trajectories, and people rather than conceding the automation control plane.

**Adept’s $415 million war chest** is the strongest bear case: Coasty’s 82.81% benchmark advantage could disappear with one model release, the sensitive-data terms can block regulated deployment, and the prior-product waitlist may say nothing about current retention. Coasty could therefore be a technically impressive benchmark wrapper whose pricing—$0.03 to $0.10 per action—never supports durable margins. The evidence keeping me on the invest side is that Coasty already combines verified performance, paying users, replayable audit trails, and founders who executed a substantial repositioning in four months; most wrappers have one of those, not all four. If Coasty’s current-product six-month gross revenue retention is below 80% by July 2027, I would flip to Pass because the “paying customers” signal would have resolved into pilot tourism.
