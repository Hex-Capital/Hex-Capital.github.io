## The Idea

- **Problem:** [Inferred]: Deployment teams must translate customer requirements into working implementations while coordinating fragmented information and manual handoffs, currently using meetings, spreadsheets, documents, and institutional knowledge ([Auctor’s description of implementation workflows](https://www.getauctor.com/series-a-announcement)).
- **Approach:** OpenScaffold describes generating a deployment-specific agent scaffold from customer discovery, business objectives, and previous deployments, then autonomously operating that scaffold ([YC, accessed Oct 27, 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/openscaffold)).
- **Differentiation:** [Inferred]: Its stated focus is a custom scaffold per deployment, compared with Auctor’s implementation artifacts, Tandem’s account-context workspace, and June’s enterprise-system implementation platform; public comparative performance data were not found ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/openscaffold), [Auctor](https://www.getauctor.com/), [Tandem](https://usetandem.ai/), [June](https://www.june.ai/)).
