## The Idea

- **Problem:** Finance, procurement, and supply-chain teams manually combine ERP exports, spreadsheets, market data, and news, causing supplier-price and tariff analysis to take weeks and sometimes arrive after action is possible ([Shielded LinkedIn, Aug 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/company/shieldedglobal)).
- **Approach:** Shielded connects ERP, API, MCP, bill-of-materials, and spreadsheet data to external prices and events, calculates company-specific margin effects, and recommends operational or hedging responses ([YC launch, Aug 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SPp-shielded-the-company-brain-for-supply-chain-risk)).
- **Differentiation:** Shielded emphasizes margin calculations and operational or hedging guidance, while Interos spans six supplier-risk domains, Altana maps multi-tier global value chains, Everstream predicts physical disruptions, and Prewave emphasizes sustainability and compliance monitoring ([YC launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SPp-shielded-the-company-brain-for-supply-chain-risk); [Interos](https://www.interos.ai/blog/press/interos-secures-40m-investment-from-blue-owl-capital); [Altana](https://altana.ai/resources/series-c-valuation); [Everstream](https://www.everstream.ai/media/everstream-raises-24m-to-conquer-supply-chain-complexity-and-disruption/); [Prewave](https://www.gruendungsfonds.at/en/news-en/detail/news-prewave-series-b-en)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Demo-led enterprise SaaS is the likely monetization path because Shielded solicits demonstrations by email but publishes no pricing, and its public website was not accessible at research time due an authentication redirect ([Shielded LinkedIn, Aug 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/company/shieldedglobal)).
- **TAM/SAM:** The global supply-chain risk-management market was valued at $3.1 billion in 2024 and forecast to reach $5.2 billion by 2030 at a 9.2% CAGR, while no public SAM was found for Shielded’s target segments ([Research and Markets, 2026](https://www.researchandmarkets.com/report/supply-chain-risk-management)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Shielded uses founder-led LinkedIn and email outreach, demo calls prepared around prospect data, and a $1,000 successful-referral offer ([Shielded LinkedIn, Aug 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/company/shieldedglobal)).

## Key Risks

- **Competitive feature overlap:** Four funded platforms already offer supplier mapping, risk monitoring, tariff analysis, or predictive disruption functions, including Altana with $322 million and Interos with nearly $200 million raised ([Altana](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/altana-trade/financials); [Washington Business Journal](https://www.bizjournals.com/washington/news/2026/01/07/interos-funding-ai-blue-owl-structural-capital.html)).
- **Data dependency:** [Inferred]: Output quality depends on the completeness of customer ERP exports, supplier origins, BOMs, volumes, and price data because those inputs drive Shielded’s company-specific calculations ([YC launch, Aug 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SPp-shielded-the-company-brain-for-supply-chain-risk)).
- **Recommendation validation:** Shielded recommends operational and hedging responses, but no public accuracy, back-testing, or realized-outcome measurements were found ([YC launch, Aug 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SPp-shielded-the-company-brain-for-supply-chain-risk)).
- **Website accessibility:** Website not accessible at time of research because the homepage redirected to a Clerk developer-authentication handshake, although an indexed application login exists ([Shielded app, Aug 2026](https://app.shieldedglobal.com/login)).
- **Name disambiguation:** “Shielded” is also used by unrelated data-security and LinkedIn-analytics products, increasing search-result ambiguity ([Shielded.fyi LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/shielded-fyi); [Shield LinkedIn analytics](https://www.linkedin.com/company/shieldapp/)).
