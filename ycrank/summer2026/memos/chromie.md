## The Idea

- **Problem:** Government contractors spend hours searching procurement portals, reviewing solicitations, analyzing competitors, pricing bids, and coordinating through spreadsheets and email before deciding whether to pursue an opportunity ([Akshay Mistry on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/akshaymistry-_not-too-long-ago-i-was-building-one-of-the-activity-7482086371109797888-zXkC), Aug 2026).
- **Approach:** Chromie builds a “Company Brain” from capabilities, documents, past performance, and team context, then monitors federal and SLED sources, scores opportunities, predicts recompetes, extracts solicitation requirements, and tracks pursuits ([Chromie website](https://www.chromie.dev/), Aug 2026).
- **Differentiation:** Chromie emphasizes one reusable company profile, source-traced requirements, and pre-publication recompete signals, while Govly centers procurement discovery and partner coordination, and GovDash and Awarded AI extend further into post-award contract delivery ([Chromie](https://www.chromie.dev/), [Govly](https://www.govly.com/about), [GovDash](https://www.govdash.com/blog/press-govdash-raises-30m-series-b-to-help-companies-win-and-manage-government-contracts-with-ai), [Procurement Sciences](https://www.procurementsciences.com/blog/series-b), 2026).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Demo-led B2B subscription, because the website directs prospects to book a demonstration and displays team workflows but publishes no pricing ([Chromie website](https://www.chromie.dev/), Aug 2026).
- **TAM/SAM:** The global government-contracting software market was estimated at $8.1B in 2025 with a projected 9.2% CAGR through 2033 ([Data Insights Market](https://www.datainsightsmarket.com/reports/government-contracting-software-526706), 2026); no public SAM data found.
- **GTM / Distribution:** Founder-led outbound includes manual prospecting, booking a one-way trip to Washington, D.C., and visiting 50 GovCon offices, supplemented by website demo booking ([Ananth Sankaralingam on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ananth-s), Aug 2026).

- **Moat today:** No defensibility signals found in public sources.

## Key Risks

- **Product-pivot cadence:** Chromie moved during 2026 from an AI Chrome-extension builder to general browser-agent infrastructure and insurance workflows before relaunching around government contracting ([PeerPush](https://peerpush.com/p/chromiedev), Mar 2026; [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ananth-s), Aug 2026).
- **Competitive overlap:** GovDash reports nearly 200 customers and at least $40M in disclosed Series A and B funding, while Procurement Sciences reports more than 300 organizations and $40M+ raised ([GovDash](https://www.govdash.com/blog/press-govdash-raises-30m-series-b-to-help-companies-win-and-manage-government-contracts-with-ai), Jan 2026; [Procurement Sciences](https://www.procurementsciences.com/blog/series-b), Nov 2025).
- **Source and extraction dependency:** Opportunity discovery depends on continuous ingestion from SAM.gov and SLED portals, while qualification depends on model-generated scores and extracted requirements; Chromie’s disclosed mitigation is source tracing and replayable audit logs ([Chromie website](https://www.chromie.dev/), Aug 2026).
- **Security posture:** Chromie ingests past-performance documents and internal company context, but no public security certification was found, while GovDash and GovSignals disclose federal-security controls ([Chromie](https://www.chromie.dev/), [GovDash](https://www.govdash.com/blog/press-govdash-raises-30m-series-b-to-help-companies-win-and-manage-government-contracts-with-ai), [GovSignals](https://www.govsignals.ai/blog/as-department-of-government-efficiency-becomes-a-reality-govsignals-raises-seed-round-to-transform-government-contracting-with-ai/), 2026).
- **Name ambiguity:** Search results also surface the unrelated Chromie Health, which has 2,843 LinkedIn followers and raised a separate $2M pre-seed round, creating potential search and brand confusion ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/chromiehealth), Aug 2026).
