# AnyAlpha -- Peter Thiel Evaluation

AnyAlpha’s candidate secret is that prediction-market traders possess useful theses but cannot express and execute them reliably enough to capture their value. That is testable: give traders comparable capital and information, then measure whether thesis-driven agents improve results after fees, slippage, and failed execution. But this is my reconstruction. The dossier identifies neither a prevailing belief the founders reject nor evidence that their implementation overturns it. Automating a tedious workflow establishes usefulness; it does not yet establish a secret.

The founders are substantially more convincing than the monopoly. Shirley Peng and Jerry Miao report three years building together, with prior apps reaching $500K ARR. Meeting five years ago at a Microsoft poker tournament is quite different from assembling a team to apply to YC. Their shared shipping history directly answers part of the founding-structure question. Full-time status, ownership, and economic exposure remain unverified, but there is no documented part-time founder or outsourced engineering. I would credit the relationship without treating their previous company revenue as AnyAlpha traction.

What will AnyAlpha own if its product works? Prediction-market traders are an identifiable starting customer, but Olas already offers autonomous Polymarket trading; Composer and Capitalise.ai establish that strategy creation, backtesting, and execution are available elsewhere. These are not necessarily identical products. They do establish that the workflow itself is insufficient differentiation. The dossier provides no exclusive information, demonstrated technical advantage, or network mechanism through which each additional trader strengthens AnyAlpha’s position. Successful automation could therefore create value principally for traders and exchanges while leaving the intermediary replaceable. That is the decisive adverse evidence.

The strongest objection to passing is that this team has already combined trading infrastructure experience with an ability to build consumer products people purchase. Jerry’s reported six-year trading history could conceal a genuinely proprietary execution insight. Consumer finance and tools for making contested information actionable also fit my revealed investing patterns. **Polymarket** supplies the relevant exception: persistent transactions can reveal demand that conventional assessments miss. The material difference is that AnyAlpha’s evidence is a founder-reported $53 gain on $100 during a demo, not sustained customer transactions or willingness to pay for this product. Its 42 iOS ratings and 100+ Android downloads span an earlier positioning. Neither establishes the behavior needed for that override. Nor does the dossier document an independent technical breakthrough or validated deployment path supporting the other exceptions.

AnyAlpha has shipped distribution surfaces, but their economics remain unresolved. Mobile listings and direct outreach to power traders are concrete routes to customers; conversion, acquisition cost, and current-product retention are unknown. Separately, purchases labeled BetAlpha range from $4.99 to $79.99 without disclosed billing periods or agent entitlements, leaving no usable subscription arithmetic for a power-law outcome. The sports-information terms alongside agent execution deserve clarification, but they do not establish an enforcement order or prohibited activity. I would not manufacture a regulatory red flag from inconsistent disclosures.

I pass on this evidence. AnyAlpha has a credible founding relationship and a product entering an already contested workflow; the founders’ competence does not resolve who captures the value. My verifiable flip condition is an independently audited, 90-day paying cohort in one defined prediction-market niche showing that an exclusive AnyAlpha data or execution mechanism produces superior net results against available substitutes and customers renew to retain that advantage. That would establish something competitors cannot obtain merely by implementing the same interface.
