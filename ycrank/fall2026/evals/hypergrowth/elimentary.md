# Elimentary -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Medium

### Company case

**Path B. Repeatable sales or partner distribution with account expansion.** Private credit fund CFOs and operations leads face recurring deadlines for reconciling records, testing covenants, and preparing reports. Elimentary’s agents work over existing systems and prepare outputs for human sign-off. The multi-year TresVista agreement has initial agents live in fund administration and financial planning. In one reported filing reconciliation, a listed business development company cut roughly 20 hours of work to under 30 minutes of agent runtime plus about an hour of human review. That is a concrete early signal of workflow value. Pricing and revenue remain unverified. (*The Idea*, *Market & Traction*, *Key Facts*)

The growth mechanism would be for TresVista to bring the same initial workflow to multiple fund managers and for each manager to add deals or workloads over time. Elimentary reports that one direct lender expanded covenant testing to more than 150 portfolio companies. That supports workload expansion within one account, while paid expansion and retention across reporting cycles remain unverified. A second-market rollout would test whether the same buyer can use the same core workflow with changes limited to data mapping, local agreement and reporting rules, and vendor approval. The memo establishes one live partner deployment. Repeat acquisition through the partner and demand in a second geography remain open. (*The Idea*, *Market & Traction*, *Key Risks*)

### Countercase

Elimentary could save analysts substantial time while still needing specialist labor for each rollout. Each new manager may require vendor diligence and integration work. Each distinct agreement may require rule configuration and validation, and each reporting cycle adds processing and exception support alongside the customer’s sign-off. Client-specific calculation tools and the risk of misreading amendments make this a credible constraint. The buyer’s time savings leave Elimentary’s own delivery costs unmeasured. The Pass gate remains open pending evidence that those costs scale proportionally and that reusable configurations cannot reduce them. (*The Idea*, *Market & Traction*, *Key Risks*)

### Decisive diligence question

Can TresVista bring an independent fund manager in a second geography onto the same paid reconciliation workflow with materially less Elimentary setup effort than the first deployment? A paid rollout that repeats across month-end closes, with documented reuse of the core configuration and positive contribution after partner, implementation, processing, and support costs, would change the action to **Reach out**.
