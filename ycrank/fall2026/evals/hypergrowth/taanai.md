# Taan.ai -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path A. Product-led adoption.** The first buyer is a video-tool developer whose users repeatedly need soundtracks to follow edits and revise individual cues. Music for Video AI identifies Taan as its scoring provider, giving Taan one concrete signal of fit for that job. The integration’s acquisition route, payment terms, and production usage are unknown. (The Idea, Market & Traction)

Taan advertises self-serve API access. If developers can discover it and ship cut-aligned scoring through the same integration, each new app could bring recurring scoring workloads. The same workflow could serve developers abroad if the model handles the music styles their users need and the output rights cover their use. The memo gives no buyer evidence from a second geography. Posted plans establish an offer, while paid conversion, repeat usage, and expansion remain unmeasured. Contribution would depend on compute per usable score, including revisions and retries, plus the integration and support time each new account requires. Those costs are unmeasured. (The Idea, Key Facts, Key Risks)

### Countercase

Precise timing may matter too rarely to pull editors out of their existing tools or persuade developers to choose Taan over another scoring API. Premiere already offers scoring within the timeline, and competing video-to-music APIs give developers alternatives. Taan reports on-time event rates of 91.3% in its research and 73.2% at launch, and the benchmark uses 30-second instrumental requests. The practical advantage on longer production edits remains uncertain. This supports Watch. The memo establishes neither a structural demand ceiling nor proportional delivery costs. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can an independent video-tool developer outside the US activate through the standard API, pay, and use Taan on successive production projects at its normal editing cadence? A deployment with paid usage records, measured compute and support costs, and no custom build would move the action to Reach out. A one-off trial would leave it at Watch.
