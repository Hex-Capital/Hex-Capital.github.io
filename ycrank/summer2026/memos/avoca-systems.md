## The Idea

- **Problem:** Radiology networks lose patient demand because up to 30% of calls go unanswered while staff manually reconcile clinical requirements, referrals, equipment, rosters, and booking rules across legacy systems and spreadsheets ([Y Combinator, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/avoca-systems)).
- **Approach:** Avoca interprets calls, uploaded referrals, faxes, and e-referrals; applies network-specific clinical and operational rules; writes confirmed appointments back to the RIS; and automates preparation, reminders, results, and follow-up communications ([Avoca booking engine, Jul 2026](https://www.avocasystems.com/booking-engine)).
- **Differentiation:** Unlike generic scheduling tools, Avoca encodes radiology-specific modality, contrast, preparation, equipment, staffing, and referrer constraints, while Medmo emphasizes referral orchestration and Clearwave spans general healthcare scheduling, registration, eligibility, and payments ([Y Combinator, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/avoca-systems); [Medmo, Oct 2025](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/10/14/3166303/0/en/medmo-raises-15-million-series-a-funding-round.html); [Clearwave](https://www.clearwaveinc.com/patient-engagement-platform/)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Enterprise software sold directly to radiology networks through negotiated deployments, since the company requests introductions to network executives and publishes no self-service pricing ([Y Combinator, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/avoca-systems)).
- **TAM/SAM:** The global radiology-scheduling-software market was valued at $780 million in 2025, with its cloud segment projected to grow at an 18.96% CAGR through 2034 ([Fortune Business Insights, Jul 2026](https://www.fortunebusinessinsights.com/radiology-scheduling-software-market-115914)); no public SAM estimate was found.
- **GTM / Distribution:** The founders target radiology-network CEOs, CIOs, COOs, CDOs, CROs, and CAIOs, concentrating sales on consolidated networks with many locations and expanding from Australia into the United States ([Y Combinator, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/avoca-systems)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Avoca has encoded customer-specific booking rules, protocols, rosters, staff capabilities, and site-level knowledge, integrated with Visage, Karisma, Comrad, Epic, Oracle Health, and eClinicalWorks, and obtained SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, and HIPAA compliance ([Y Combinator, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/avoca-systems); [Avoca security](https://www.avocasystems.com/security-and-governance)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Accumulated radiology workflow knowledge and deployment-specific integrations could increase switching costs, but public sources do not establish proprietary data ownership, exclusive access, or measurable performance improvement over time.
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier prevents radiology-software incumbents or broader patient-access vendors from adding overlapping voice, referral, and scheduling automation, although reproducing integrations and site-specific clinical rules entails implementation work ([AbbaDox](https://www.abbadox.com/about-us); [OpenDoctor](https://opendr.com/)).
- **Commoditization risk:** AbbaDox already offers radiology-specific VoiceAI, FaxAI, scheduling, check-in, results sharing, and referrer communications, while OpenDoctor offers a 24/7 AI self-scheduling agent with exam-mapping logic ([AbbaDox](https://www.abbadox.com/about-us); [OpenDoctor](https://opendr.com/)).

## Key Risks

- **Clinical-booking error:** Radiology appointments depend on modality, body region, contrast, patient history, preparation, equipment, and staff qualifications, so an incorrect automated decision could disrupt or delay care; Avoca states that out-of-parameter interactions escalate to humans ([Y Combinator, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/avoca-systems); [Avoca security](https://www.avocasystems.com/security-and-governance)).
- **Protected-health-data exposure:** Avoca processes calls, referral letters, medical records, clinical notes, and other health information, creating privacy and security exposure; the company reports encryption, audit logging, Australian data residency, SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, and HIPAA compliance ([Avoca privacy policy](https://www.avocasystems.com/privacy-policy); [Avoca security](https://www.avocasystems.com/security-and-governance)).
- **Integration dependency:** Confirmed bookings rely on real-time write-back to RIS products and integrations with multiple healthcare systems, making deployment and service continuity dependent on third-party interfaces ([Avoca booking engine](https://www.avocasystems.com/booking-engine); [Y Combinator, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/avoca-systems)).
- **Competitive overlap:** AbbaDox and OpenDoctor already market radiology-specific voice, fax, patient-engagement, and AI-scheduling functions, while Clearwave offers overlapping patient-access automation across healthcare ([AbbaDox](https://www.abbadox.com/about-us); [OpenDoctor](https://opendr.com/); [Clearwave](https://www.clearwaveinc.com/patient-engagement-platform/)).
- **Name ambiguity:** Search results for “Avoca” frequently refer to the unrelated YC W23 home-services AI company at avoca.ai or the UK healthcare-data firm Avoca Systems Ltd, increasing the risk of misattributed funding and traction data ([TechCrunch, Jul 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/05/almost-40-new-unicorns-have-been-minted-so-far-this-year-here-they-are/); [Avoca Systems Ltd LinkedIn](https://uk.linkedin.com/company/avoca-systems-ltd)).
