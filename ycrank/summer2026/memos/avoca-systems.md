## The Idea

- **Problem:** More than 30% of patient calls to radiology clinics are abandoned while bookings require coordination among clinical protocols, site rules, referrals, preparation instructions, RIS workflows, and staff knowledge, with manual staff and generic scheduling tools as the stated alternatives ([Avoca](https://www.avocasystems.com/about), Aug 2026).
- **Approach:** Avoca combines voice AI, online booking, e-referral intake, patient communications, clinic-defined rules, human escalation, and real-time write-back to RIS/EHR systems ([booking engine](https://www.avocasystems.com/booking-engine), Aug 2026; [integrations](https://www.avocasystems.com/integrations), Aug 2026).
- **Differentiation:** Avoca adds voice, fax, e-referral, longitudinal communications, and clinic-specific operational knowledge to the radiology self-scheduling offered by openDoctor, while Assort Health and Prosper AI apply patient-access agents across healthcare specialties rather than radiology alone ([Avoca](https://www.avocasystems.com/), Aug 2026; [openDoctor](https://opendr.com/solutions/enable-self-scheduling/), Aug 2026; [Assort Health](https://www.assorthealth.com/blog/assort-health-raises-120-million-series-c-to-scale-largest-deployment-of-ai-agents-for-the-patient-journey), Jun 2026; [Prosper AI](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/22/3315343/0/en/prosper-ai-raises-30m-from-andreessen-horowitz-to-scale-the-first-ai-platform-to-run-the-entire-patient-journey.html), Jun 2026).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: The partner-led sales form, network-level deployments, and absence of published self-service pricing indicate an enterprise software contract structured around each radiology network’s deployment or usage ([Avoca](https://www.avocasystems.com/), Aug 2026).
- **TAM/SAM:** The global radiology-scheduling-software market was valued at $780 million in 2025 and is projected by its source to grow at a 10.32% CAGR through 2034, while no company-specific SAM was published ([Fortune Business Insights](https://www.fortunebusinessinsights.com/radiology-scheduling-software-market-115914), Jul 2026).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Avoca is pursuing radiology executives, private-equity groups, and technology vendors while expanding from Australia into the U.S. ([Avoca Systems LinkedIn](https://au.linkedin.com/company/avocasystems), Jul 2026).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Current signals include SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, HIPAA compliance, clinic-specific knowledge bases, and integrations with Epic, Oracle Health, MEDITECH, Karisma, Aura, and Visage ([security](https://www.avocasystems.com/security-and-governance), Aug 2026; [integrations](https://www.avocasystems.com/integrations), Aug 2026).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Accumulated clinic rules, exception histories, integration coverage, and booking-outcome data could increase switching costs, but public sources do not disclose proprietary datasets, patents, or retention.
- **Market structure:** [Inferred]: No structural barrier is identified because openDoctor already supports complex radiology scheduling and RIS integration, while AbbaDox automates referral intake, scheduling, and follow-up within a radiology operations platform ([openDoctor](https://opendr.com/solutions/enable-self-scheduling/), Aug 2026; [AbbaDox](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/beyond-the-image-abbadox-operational-ai-helps-imaging-centers-recover-thousands-of-staff-hours-lost-to-manual-workflows-302718461.html), Mar 2026).
- **Commoditization risk:** Existing RIS vendors, radiology workflow platforms, and healthcare voice-agent companies can build overlapping scheduling and communication functions using their installed integrations and customer relationships ([Relatient](https://www.relatient.com/about/), Aug 2026; [openDoctor](https://opendr.com/), Aug 2026).

## Key Risks

- **Competitive overlap:** openDoctor and AbbaDox already provide radiology-specific scheduling, referral, patient-engagement, and integration workflows, while Assort and Prosper offer healthcare voice agents ([openDoctor](https://opendr.com/), Aug 2026; [AbbaDox](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/beyond-the-image-abbadox-operational-ai-helps-imaging-centers-recover-thousands-of-staff-hours-lost-to-manual-workflows-302718461.html), Mar 2026).
- **U.S. data-residency mismatch:** Avoca states that all infrastructure is hosted in Australia and patient data never leaves Australia while the company is beginning U.S. expansion, potentially requiring a separate U.S. hosting architecture despite stated HIPAA compliance ([Avoca Security](https://www.avocasystems.com/security-and-governance), Aug 2026; [LinkedIn](https://au.linkedin.com/company/avocasystems), Jul 2026).
- **Workflow-safety exposure:** Radiology bookings involve clinical protocols and preparation requirements, creating incorrect-booking risk; Avoca mitigates this with approved knowledge bases, defined escalation paths, human exception handling, and continuous monitoring ([Avoca Security](https://www.avocasystems.com/security-and-governance), Aug 2026).
- **Integration dependency:** Product functionality depends on read/write access to RIS and EHR systems, while athenahealth and eClinicalWorks integrations are still marked “coming soon” ([Avoca Integrations](https://www.avocasystems.com/integrations), Aug 2026).
- **Name ambiguity:** An unrelated U.K. healthcare-data company has operated as Avoca Systems Ltd since 1995, and an unrelated YC W23 company named Avoca sells AI agents to home-services businesses ([Avoca Systems Ltd LinkedIn](https://uk.linkedin.com/company/avoca-systems-ltd), Jul 2026; [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/05/almost-40-new-unicorns-have-been-minted-so-far-this-year-here-they-are/), Jul 2026).
