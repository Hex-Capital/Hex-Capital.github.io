# Dydema -- Peter Thiel Evaluation

Dydema’s potential secret is that consumer brain interfaces are constrained principally by missing recordings, and that collecting the right data will unlock useful control. What would prove that wrong? If additional recordings fail to improve performance on unseen users enough to support a useful task, the bottleneck lies elsewhere. The dossier establishes neither a named opposing consensus nor a successful test of this proposition. Wizard’s reported 52% zero-shot left-versus-right accuracy against 50% chance establishes the difficulty; it does not establish that Dydema knows how to overcome it. I give the secret the missing-evidence default, rather than treating an honestly disclosed limitation as a disproved thesis.

The more immediate investment problem is ownership. What small market can Dydema actually dominate? “Map to anything” supplies no bounded customer segment. Wizard fits licensed pretrained models and classical classifiers, while EMOTIV already combines hardware, APIs, and EEG data infrastructure. Neurosity and Neurable address different portions of the stack, so their existence alone would not settle the question. The direct platform overlap matters more: Dydema has not demonstrated exclusive recordings, proprietary performance, or a mechanism that makes each customer strengthen its position. Commissioned data could eventually supply that mechanism, but rights to reuse those recordings and their measurable advantage remain hypotheses.

The founding structure is substantially better than the market structure. Weaver and Oosthuizen have been friends since high school, conducted a neurotechnology experiment together, and subsequently worked together at Syntra. That is affirmative evidence against the convenience partnership red flag. Their research and engineering backgrounds also make an internally developed product credible, although they do not establish ownership, full-time status, or economic exposure. I would investigate those particulars without inventing an adverse answer. This is a team with a shared past; the unresolved question is whether it possesses a proprietary future.

The strongest objection to passing is that Dydema has already built the machinery through which its missing advantage could accumulate. A live classification API, commissioned recordings, and demonstrations of 2048 and TikTok control create a plausible experimental loop. The founders can ship together and understand the underlying research. This fits the revealed attraction to consumer businesses and neglected physical frontiers. But the known exceptions to my monopoly requirement depend on a demonstrated technical discontinuity, persistent customer transactions, or a concrete deployment path resolving the coordination risk. Dydema’s demonstrations establish functionality without establishing any of those overrides. There is also no evidence here of a regulatory shutdown or an impossible factory stack; the objection is narrower and more fundamental.

Wizard has one intelligible revenue engine: $1 to create a model, $1 per model per month, and $0.003 per prediction window. A million prediction windows would generate $3,000 before costs. That supplies usable arithmetic, but no observed workload, margin, or customer expansion connects it to a power-law outcome. Self-service keys and documentation provide a real delivery route for developers, earning some distribution credit. They do not yet identify a repeatable acquisition channel or demonstrate that support and calibration fit contract value. Neither the absence of revenue nor 82 LinkedIn followers decides this investment.

I pass on the present evidence. The decisive weakness is the gap between collecting useful brain data and owning an indispensable market position. One verifiable event would reopen the decision: an independently measured, paid deployment in a named developer niche where Dydema’s exclusively reusable recordings reduce calibration time tenfold at matched accuracy against the strongest available alternative. That would connect the proposed secret to a proprietary advantage and an actual buyer. Today, the durable founding relationship is demonstrated; the durable business advantage is not.
