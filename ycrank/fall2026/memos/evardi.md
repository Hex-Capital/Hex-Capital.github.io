## The Idea

- **Problem:** Distribution utilities lack visibility below substations, where Evardi says 94% of outage minutes originate, leaving SCADA without transformer-level visibility ([Evardi, Nov 2026](https://evardi.ai/)).
- **Approach:** Evardi combines GIS, SCADA, billing and meter data into a power-flow model, estimates transformer loads through virtual meters, and checks agent recommendations before operator approval ([Evardi, Nov 2026](https://evardi.ai/how-it-works)).
- **Differentiation:** Evardi emphasizes transformer virtual meters and switching-order checks, while Neara emphasizes infrastructure modelling and resilience, and GridCARE emphasizes activating capacity for data centres ([Evardi](https://evardi.ai/how-it-works), [Neara](https://www.neara.com/resources/press/neara-raises-90-million-to-solve-the-global-infrastructure-crisis-with-ai/), [Future Energy Ventures](https://fev.vc/fev-participates-in-gridcares-64m-series-a-led-by-sutter-hill-ventures-and-john-doerr/)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Negotiated utility software licensing is the likely monetization path because platform use requires an organizational written agreement; public pricing: No public data found ([Evardi terms, Nov 2026](https://evardi.ai/terms)).
- **TAM/SAM:** The broader grid-digital-twin market is estimated at $1.2 billion in 2026, reaching $7.8 billion by 2034 at 26.3% CAGR; Evardi-specific SAM: No public data found ([Stratistics MRC, 2026](https://www.strategymrc.com/report/grid-digital-twin-market)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Evardi solicits utility demos and asks prospects for a feeder map and a year of meter data to demonstrate findings on their network ([Evardi, Nov 2026](https://evardi.ai/how-it-works)).

## Key Risks

- **Field-performance validation:** Published case-study results are simulations and estimates, limiting evidence of live operational outcomes; Evardi requires engineering approval rather than autonomous execution ([Evardi terms](https://evardi.ai/terms), [security](https://evardi.ai/security)).
- **Input-data dependence:** [Inferred]: Incorrect connectivity or incomplete historical data could impair recommendations because the model depends on utility GIS and meter records; SCADA checks provide a documented safeguard ([Evardi](https://evardi.ai/how-it-works)).
- **Security qualification:** [Inferred]: Procurement may depend on assurance requirements while Evardi’s SOC 2 Type II audit remains underway; encryption, role-based access and isolated workspaces are documented mitigations ([Evardi security](https://evardi.ai/security)).
- **Name ambiguity:** An unrelated Belgian management consultancy also operates as Evardi, requiring financial-record disambiguation ([Companyweb](https://www.companyweb.be/en/0687517786/evardi)).
