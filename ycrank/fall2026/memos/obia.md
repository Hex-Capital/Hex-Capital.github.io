## The Idea

- **Problem:** Businesses seeking industrial or office space must reconcile operational requirements, zoning, and prices across listings, brochures, municipal codes, maps, and broker conversations, requiring hours of research per property according to Obia ([YC profile, accessed Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/obia)).
- **Approach:** AI researches permitted uses, operating requirements, availability, and potential approvals, while brokers conduct tours and negotiate transactions ([Obia announcement, Oct 2026](https://obia.io/news/corenet-global-summit-2026); [website](https://obia.io/)).
- **Differentiation:** Obia combines property research with representation across industrial and other asset classes, versus Tandem’s office focus and Crexi’s marketplace and broker software ([Obia](https://obia.io/); [Tandem](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/tandem-2); [Crexi](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/crexi-expands-crexi-ai-capabilities-to-help-cre-professionals-work-faster-and-close-deals-more-efficiently-302770467.html)).
- **Business Model:** Tenant representation is free to tenants, with landlords paying Obia’s fee; commission rates are not published ([Obia website, accessed Oct 2026](https://obia.io/)).
- **TAM/SAM:** A broader proxy including property management is $292.6 billion globally in 2025, forecast to grow 5.69% annually during 2026–2034; no California industrial-and-office SAM was found ([IMARC, 2026](https://www.imarcgroup.com/commercial-real-estate-brokerage-management-market)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Obia collects buyer, tenant, and property-owner inquiries online and announced sponsorship and exhibition at CoreNet Global Summit ([contact page](https://obia.io/contact); [announcement, Oct 2026](https://obia.io/news/corenet-global-summit-2026)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Obia reports accumulating tenant and buyer search requirements and using that information to match properties, but publishes no dataset size or exclusivity evidence ([Obia website](https://obia.io/)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Search requirements and completed-deal feedback could improve matching, although a durable advantage from these records remains unverified.
- **Market structure:** [Inferred]: No structural barrier to incumbent replication was identified, given Crexi’s existing integration of AI research with proprietary transaction data ([Crexi, May 2026](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/crexi-expands-crexi-ai-capabilities-to-help-cre-professionals-work-faster-and-close-deals-more-efficiently-302770467.html)).
- **Commoditization risk:** Crexi already offers document extraction and market reporting, while Tandem operates an AI office brokerage, establishing overlap in research automation and representation ([Crexi](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/crexi-expands-crexi-ai-capabilities-to-help-cre-professionals-work-faster-and-close-deals-more-efficiently-302770467.html); [Tandem](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/tandem-2)).

## Key Risks

- **Property-data reliability:** [Inferred]: Incomplete zoning or availability information could produce unsuitable recommendations; Obia’s terms acknowledge potentially inaccurate third-party data and require independent verification ([Obia terms](https://obia.io/terms-and-conditions)).
- **Service-delivery scaling:** [Inferred]: Expansion requires local execution capacity because brokers remain responsible for tours and negotiations ([Obia website](https://obia.io/)).
- **Overlapping incumbent tools:** Crexi’s embedded research and document-processing capabilities give existing brokers access to overlapping automation ([Crexi, May 2026](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/crexi-expands-crexi-ai-capabilities-to-help-cre-professionals-work-faster-and-close-deals-more-efficiently-302770467.html)).
- **Contracting-entity ambiguity:** Public branding identifies Obia Inc., while October 2026 terms identify Jason Henkel as a sole proprietor doing business as Obia ([website](https://obia.io/); [terms](https://obia.io/terms-and-conditions)).
- **Name confusion:** [Inferred]: Discovery and data attribution can be confused with the separate French OBIA marketing platform at obia.fr, whose metrics are excluded here ([OBIA France, accessed Oct 2026, via search snippet](https://www.obia.fr/)).
