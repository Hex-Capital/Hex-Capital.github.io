# Audora -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Audora’s strongest exit path is a strategic sale to an audiobook or reading platform. The asset would be its workflow for turning a story link into an account-free, multi-voice preview, along with any reader demand that converts into paid listening. The memo records a public launch and 23,573 views of the founder’s announcement. The founder reports a 10,000-person waitlist of readers and authors. A platform could infer value from bringing more stories into paid audio, but the memo provides no verified paying customers, realized revenue, or measured benefit to a buyer. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Facts)

A paid pilot using stories cleared for commercial use is a plausible seed-stage milestone. Audora has launched the preview, and the founder has built tools for the same fanfiction audience. Repeat purchases and contribution margin after generation costs would show whether the preview can support a business a platform would want to own. The funding reports leave available runway and the capital needed for the pilot uncertain. (The Idea, Founders & Team, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

Using the shared benchmark, a $500,000 check at $10 million post-money starts at 5% ownership and dilutes to 3% with no follow-ons. The eight-year objective is $2.5 million in gross cash, requiring about $83.3 million in distributable equity value. The following are illustrative assumptions. Both sales assume a close within eight years, cash consideration, an eligible holder selling the full stake, and a pro rata waterfall after the stated deductions. The memo leaves actual deal terms and holder rights open.

| Scenario | Enterprise value to distributable equity | Gross cash to holder |
|---|---|---|
| Modest sale | $40M EV with zero assumed net debt or deal adjustments gives $40M | $1.2M (2.4×) |
| Strong sale | $100M EV less $10M in assumed net debt and deal adjustments gives $90M | $2.7M (5.4×) |
| Prolonged private | Continued operation with cash reinvested and no share sale or distribution | $0 (0×) |

The strong case clears the benchmark arithmetically. The memo supplies no transaction pricing or buyer economics to support that sale value and no evidence of an independent route to holder cash. (Market & Traction, Key Facts)

### Countercase

ElevenLabs already offers character casting and direct publishing to ElevenReader, giving a prospective buyer a ready substitute for Audora’s core production capability. The interim terms require authorized access and personal use, so a buyer would need to establish which stories it could use commercially before underwriting the current reader funnel. Those constraints weaken the rationale to buy and threaten closing feasibility. The memo leaves open a sale built around commercially cleared content and gives no supported sale price that would trigger the inadequate-proceeds gate. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a paid pilot on commercially cleared stories show a prospective platform buyer enough incremental contribution profit over its existing workflow to justify a price that clears the holder’s hurdle? Repeat paid conversion, actual generation costs, and a buyer’s valuation rationale would answer it. Evidence supporting roughly $83.3 million or more in distributable equity within eight years would move the action to Reach out. A supported price materially below that hurdle on the strongest route would move it to Pass.
