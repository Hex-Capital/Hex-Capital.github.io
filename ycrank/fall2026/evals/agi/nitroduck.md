# Nitroduck -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**New Economies** is Nitroduck’s strongest path. The company proposes a pollen-editing service that fits breeders’ existing crosses and could remove a separate plant-regeneration step. Nitroduck estimates that conventional development takes 8–16 years for a new variety and costs $3 million for a new trait. Those figures describe the broader breeding process. The cost of the step Nitroduck aims to remove, the savings per successful edited seed, and the role of AI in those savings remain unestablished. A plausible scenario is that lower delivery costs make a specialty-crop trait viable for a breeder that previously shelved it. The memo identifies no such breeder or project. (The Idea, Key Risks)

Under model parity, breeders could still pay for physical execution. A reason to choose Nitroduck specifically could emerge if experience with a breeder’s crops makes subsequent projects more reliable. That advantage is inferred. The memo documents a public launch and relevant prior team delivery through the award-winning duckweed project and crop-engineering work. It records no paid breeder commitment, and edited seed in a commercially relevant specialty crop remains a stated milestone. The proposed flat fee defines the unit of expansion as one breeder project. Price, success rate, and the cost of failed attempts remain unknown, so contribution per successful project is unproven. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Founders & Team, The Idea, Key Risks)

### Countercase

Nitroduck could improve an existing breeding workflow while serving the same buyers and budgets. Pairwise already licenses editing tools to a vegetable breeder, Hudson River Biotechnology offers specialty-crop trait development, and Plantik’s published pollen-editing patent application raises potential claim overlap. These are credible competitive pressures. The memo leaves open whether those alternatives can serve the specific projects Nitroduck aims to unlock and whether breeders would switch readily. The New Economies gate remains unproven, which supports Watch. There is no independently supported scarce-complement path. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can Nitroduck complete a paid specialty-crop project that an independent breeder previously shelved on cost grounds? A verified edited-seed outcome, a lower all-in cost per successful outcome at a commercially viable fee, and a documented reason the breeder would choose Nitroduck again under model parity would move the action to Reach out. An inconclusive result keeps it on Watch. Evidence that the service opens no new use or that buyers can readily switch to comparable providers would close the only supported path and move the action to Pass.
