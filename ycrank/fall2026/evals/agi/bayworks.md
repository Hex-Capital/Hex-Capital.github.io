# Bayworks -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Path B, **New economies**, is the strongest path. Bayworks turns technician dictation and repair evidence into a manufacturer-formatted warranty narrative that the technician reviews before saving it to the dealership system. The potential new transaction is a low-value warranty claim that a dealer would otherwise skip because documenting it takes too much billable time. That is a scenario assumption. The memo provides no like-for-like labor comparison that includes evidence capture, review, corrections, and administrator follow-up, and it identifies no claims newly made viable. (The Idea, Key Risks)

The memo records a named endorsement from Harvey Auto Group. Bayworks reports nearly seven figures in cARR within four months, six dealership groups, and more than 50,000 repairs. These are early demand signals, with the share of cARR attributable to live, paying deployments and the rate of repeat paid use still unknown. The shipped Android app and PBS extension, alongside the founders’ prior voice AI and mobile product work, support seed-stage execution. The dealership location is a plausible expansion unit, with per-site pricing, onboarding labor, and ongoing integration and support costs still unmeasured. (Market & Traction, Founders & Team, Key Risks)

### Countercase

Bayworks may save time on claims dealers already submit, while dealers with access to equally capable models can buy comparable dictation and repair-order writeback from Wrench Voice. Bayworks’ use of prior narratives may improve its drafts, but the memo leaves cross-dealer reuse rights and measurable quality gains open. It also identifies no Bayworks-controlled supply of technician labor or privileged access to dealer systems that would support a separate Path A case. Both Path B conditions remain unproven. The competing workflow makes substitution credible, while actual switching at equivalent quality remains untested. That leaves a specific basis for Watch under the thesis gate. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Will dealerships repeatedly pay Bayworks to document warranty claims they previously skipped when an equally capable, integrated alternative is available? A paid side-by-side trial should establish which eligible claims dealers skipped under the manual workflow, compare labor per accepted claim through review and rework, and test whether dealers keep paying for Bayworks at a price that covers its delivery costs. A trial that establishes both newly viable claims and sustained preference at comparable quality would justify rescoring for Reach out, subject to the score and confidence gates. A trial showing only faster processing of existing claims or ready substitution would move the action to Pass.
