## The Idea

- **Problem:** Electric distribution utilities rely on feeder-level SCADA and manual switching checks despite limited transformer-level visibility, and a Berkeley Lab study attributed at least 94% of U.S. customer interruption minutes to the distribution system ([Evardi switching case study](https://evardi.ai/case-studies/switching-orders), [Berkeley Lab study, 2019](https://ietresearch.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1049/iet-gtd.2018.6452)).
- **Approach:** Evardi combines SCADA, meter, billing and GIS data into a power-flow model, maps meters to transformers, estimates hourly transformer loads and simulates switching steps before operators approve them ([Evardi platform](https://evardi.ai/platform), [Evardi website](https://evardi.ai/)).
- **Differentiation:** Evardi emphasizes transformer-level estimates and switching-order checks, while Gridsight emphasizes dynamic solar export limits, Camus Energy dispatches flexible loads and distributed energy resources, and Neara models physical assets and clearances from LiDAR and imagery ([Evardi platform](https://evardi.ai/platform), [Gridsight announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/gridsight-raises-26m-series-b-to-unlock-electric-grid-capacity-and-affordability-302866161.html), [Camus Grid DERMS](https://www.camus.energy/grid-derms), [Neara](https://neara.com/)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Evardi could charge utilities through individually negotiated software contracts, given its demo-led intake and terms requiring a separate written platform agreement ([Evardi website](https://evardi.ai/), [Evardi terms](https://evardi.ai/terms)).
- **TAM/SAM:** As a broader market proxy, Mordor Intelligence estimates global advanced distribution management systems at US$4.31B in 2026 and 19.60% CAGR through 2031 ([Mordor Intelligence, 2026](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/advanced-distribution-management-system-market)). No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** Evardi solicits utility operations, outage-response and planning teams through CEO outreach and website demos, and an earlier program offered to model part of a utility’s network for free ([Launch YC, Nov 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Wwy-evardi-agents-that-engineer-the-grid-edge), [Evardi website](https://evardi.ai/), [Evardi Free Network Model Program, via search snippet](https://evardi.ai/free-model)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Evardi drafts switching orders from each utility’s topology and prior orders, then checks each step against that utility’s operating limits ([Evardi switching case study](https://evardi.ai/case-studies/switching-orders)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Validated meter-to-transformer mappings and approved switching histories could raise switching costs once embedded in utility workflows, with the published studies providing simulation evidence ([Evardi virtual-meter case study](https://evardi.ai/case-studies/virtual-meters), [Evardi terms](https://evardi.ai/terms)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** Sacramento Municipal Utility District reports deploying meter-based transformer mapping and load monitoring and integrating AspenTech outage management for coordinated switching and restoration ([SMUD board packet, Oct 2026](https://www.smud.org/vi/-/media/Documents/Corporate/About-Us/Board-Meetings-and-Agendas/2026/Oct/Policy-Info-Packet--Oct-7-2026.ashx)).

## Key Risks

- **Field validation:** Evardi says its published case studies use simulations on public data, leaving field performance during live switching and storm restoration unverified in those studies ([Evardi terms](https://evardi.ai/terms)).
- **Model accuracy:** In a blind feeder simulation, median transformer peak-load estimation error was 6.2% and Evardi corrected 57% of planted mapping errors. The platform shows uncertainty bands and requires operator review and supervisor sign-off ([Evardi virtual-meter case study](https://evardi.ai/case-studies/virtual-meters), [Evardi website](https://evardi.ai/), [Evardi security](https://evardi.ai/security)).
- **Security review:** The platform uses operational and metering data, and Evardi reports that its SOC 2 Type II audit is underway, with encryption, separate utility workspaces and access logging in place ([Evardi security](https://evardi.ai/security)).
- **Incumbent substitution:** SMUD’s deployment combines meter-based transformer visibility with an AspenTech outage management integration for coordinated switching and restoration, covering workflows marketed by Evardi ([SMUD board packet, Oct 2026](https://www.smud.org/vi/-/media/Documents/Corporate/About-Us/Board-Meetings-and-Agendas/2026/Oct/Policy-Info-Packet--Oct-7-2026.ashx), [Evardi platform](https://evardi.ai/platform)).
