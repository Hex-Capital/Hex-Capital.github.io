# Grep AI -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Medium

### Company case

**Path B — New Economies** is the strongest path. The scenario to test is whether Grep can make individual underwriting reviews economical for low-value accounts that fintechs currently skip because manual evidence gathering takes too much analyst time. IG reported that automated screening-alert review rose from 10% to 30% and that flagged-customer activation fell from 2–3 days to under 10 minutes. Those operating results support the automation mechanism. The memo leaves open whether it reduces the full cost of an acceptable decision enough to unlock previously skipped reviews. (The Idea, Market & Traction)

Under the assumption that rivals have equally capable models, a buyer could still pay Grep to maintain validated workflows as screening rules change. Grep encoded IG’s criteria and secured governance approval after shadow testing. That work suggests a potential switching cost, while buyer preference under functional parity remains untested. Shopmonkey’s 64 Deep Research jobs in 30 days show actual use, with payment and renewal unknown. The founders’ prior risk and compliance automation work at Brex and the team’s managed-agent launch support seed-stage execution. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Founders & Team)

The expansion unit is a completed decision that meets the buyer’s quality standard. Grep claims a 91% model-cost reduction in an internal screening benchmark. IG retained manual oversight for high-risk cases, so scale economics still depend on reviewer time, retries, exceptions, and deployment costs per accepted decision. (Key Risks)

### Countercase

Grep may speed up work that existing buyers already fund while leaving previously skipped reviews uneconomical. Hosted agent infrastructure overlaps with Grep’s offering, and its open-source AgentRun runtime gives buyers and rivals building blocks for comparable workflows. The memo shows neither a full-cost comparison for previously skipped reviews nor a buyer switching to a functionally equivalent substitute. Both required conditions remain open, which supports Watch. (Market & Traction, Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Will an independent fintech buyer place a paid repeat order for previously skipped small-account underwriting reviews after testing Grep against a functionally equivalent alternative using the same model? Reach out if the trial meets the buyer’s quality standard, brings fully loaded cost below the threshold that previously ruled out those reviews, and the buyer renews at a price that supports positive contribution for Grep. Pass if a representative trial shows the reviews still cost too much or the buyer switches and retains the same outcome and economics.
