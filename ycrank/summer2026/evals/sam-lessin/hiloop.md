# hiloop -- Sam Lessin Evaluation

The first three numbers are missing: post-money valuation, competing-term-sheet count, and whether this is genuinely the first scarce check. Silence does not establish non-consensus pricing. YC affiliation and a fashionable autoresearch category do not prove an auction either, but I cannot underwrite cheap convexity without knowing what the check buys.

The technical result is real, but it is not yet a capital plan. hiloop ran 4,188 agent-directed experiments on 50 B200 GPUs in two days, confirmed the result across 25 paired runs, and beat the cited validation benchmark. That proves the machinery can operate at scale. It does not name the next hypothesis, a deadline, or the pass/fail result this round finances. “Run more campaigns across data, prompts, tools, and systems” is a platform ambition, not one truth-revealing venture moment.

The missing bridge is from experiments to purchases. Erdos Miller appears under “Trusted by,” but the dossier provides no paid engagement, retained usage, or customer outcome. The 12.54% NanoGPT runtime improvement is technically legible; it is not evidence that hiloop owns distribution or that its experiment lineage already causes buying behavior. There may eventually be a proprietary dark pool in failed paths, verified outcomes, and researcher decisions, but one self-run campaign does not create it.

The bull case is the team. Brar worked in ML infrastructure at DynamoAI and on ML and post-training systems at Reducto; Boser brings ML and platform experience from Reducto, Discord, Sentropy, and SoFi. They have built functioning infrastructure rather than publishing an architectural diagram. The strongest known-exception route would be hard external validation sufficient to override missing deal evidence. hiloop does not have that yet: no reported revenue, paid pilots, renewal, acquisition offer, or customer-controlled deployment result accompanies the benchmark.

The bear case remains harsher because Prime Intellect already reports more than 10,000 beta training jobs, while Runloop and OpenPipe/CoreWeave overlap with the orchestration, sandbox, evaluation, and post-training layers. hiloop’s published result also depends on third-party frontier models and 50 B200 GPUs, and its request-time secret-delivery design remains unfinished. If model vendors, compute providers, or internal ML-platform teams absorb the experiment loop, the product becomes a founder-operated optimization service rather than durable infrastructure. My 36-month base case is exactly that: a narrow managed-research business producing $1–3 million ARR by August 2029, not a horizontal self-improvement platform.

I am passing today. Delete the next financing and the dossier supplies no gross margin, burn, pricing, or controllable cash metric showing that customer-funded campaigns can support the company. One condition flips me: three unrelated enterprises each sign and renew contracts worth at least $100,000 annually after paid campaigns delivered at greater than 50% gross margin. That would simultaneously prove purchase-causing workflow ownership and a business capable of surviving without another capital-factory round.
