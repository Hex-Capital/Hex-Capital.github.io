# Adside.ai -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Medium

### Company case

Assuming strong product-market fit with startups and agencies, Adside’s most scalable path is its self-serve software for running paid campaigns across channels. The documented workflow connects ad accounts, turns briefs into campaign drafts and creative variants, and monitors performance after launch. The same workflow should transfer across markets where buyers use the supported platforms and can grant account access. Local language and creative conventions may still affect how much adaptation buyers need. (The Idea, Key Risks)

The free ad library gives buyers a way to discover Adside, and the company reports inbound interest from its own paid campaigns. Account connections and campaign drafting provide reusable onboarding steps. The memo leaves open whether ordinary buyers can go from discovery through checkout to a live campaign without staff help. Adside reports 30 companies live at its October launch, but their payment status is unknown. The advertised $50 demo price establishes an offer, while realized revenue and paid growth remain unknown. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Facts)

Ongoing campaign monitoring and account-specific history give customers a reason to keep using the product. At $990 per brand per month, or $490 per month with annual prepayment, an agency can expand its bill by adding brands. Unlimited users limit expansion within a single brand, and whether added brands can offset churn remains uncertain. At the annual plan’s $490 monthly equivalent, contribution depends on actual creative and inference costs, plus attributable acquisition, setup and support. Automated drafting and monitoring could reduce staff time per brand, though the memo gives no measured cost per account. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Facts)

### Countercase

Google and Meta offer native AI capabilities that could satisfy single-channel advertisers, leaving Adside reliant on more complex cross-channel accounts. If those accounts routinely need hands-on setup or oversight, growth could lean on the managed service, which charges 10% of ad spend with a $2,500 monthly minimum. The structural downside gate stays open because the memo documents reusable account connections and automated workflows, leaving a credible path to scale through self-service. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a cohort acquired through the free ad library or Adside’s own ads pay, connect its accounts and launch campaigns independently, then renew at positive contribution? A dated cohort showing those steps across multiple ordinary buyers, with recurring fees covering attributable acquisition and servicing costs, would move the action to Reach out.
