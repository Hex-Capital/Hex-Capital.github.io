## The Idea

- **Problem:** Small businesses without a marketing department must regularly choose topics, produce creative and publish across channels, work owners otherwise do themselves or assign to an agency. ([Native About](https://native.no/about-us), [Launch YC, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Vdd-native-artificial-intuition-for-marketing))
- **Approach:** Native reads a business’s website, ranks ideas with Norn using brand context, trends, approval history and post performance, then generates creative and publishes organic posts and Meta ads with optional approval. ([Launch YC, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Vdd-native-artificial-intuition-for-marketing), [Native Pricing](https://native.no/pricing))
- **Differentiation:** Native leads with performance-based idea ranking and publishing, while Blaze also runs Google Search ads and publishes to WordPress, Holo packages ads with email and SEO assets, and Predis.ai specializes in turning product listings into ad creative. ([Native Norn](https://native.no/norn), [Blaze Integrations](https://www.blaze.ai/integrations/), [Holo](https://tryholo.ai/), [Predis.ai](https://predis.ai/ecommerce-ad-maker/))
- **Business Model:** Monthly subscriptions list Pro at $79 for one brand, Max at $249 for three brands, Agency at $499 for ten client workspaces, and Enterprise at a custom price, with ad spend billed separately. ([Native Pricing, Nov 2026](https://native.no/pricing))
- **TAM/SAM:** The broader global social media management market was estimated at $30B in 2025 with a projected 24.8% CAGR for 2026–2033, and no public SAM estimate was found for Native’s specific segment. ([Grand View Research, 2026](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/social-media-management-market-report))
- **GTM / Distribution:** Native offers a free website-to-post preview, the CEO reported scaling through paid ads, and the company is recruiting developers and agencies for the planned Norn API. ([Native homepage](https://native.no/), [Impressions CEO interview, Nov 2026](https://podcastportalen.no/podcast/okonomi/impressions/episode/native-fra-10-betalende-kunder-til-y-combinator-og-2m-arr-benjamin-bekken-ep-337/), [Native Norn](https://native.no/norn))

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Native trains Norn on anonymized, aggregated approval, edit, rejection and performance signals from its publishing workflow, with a customer opt-out. ([Native Privacy Policy, Nov 2026](https://native.no/privacy-policy), [Native Terms](https://native.no/terms-and-conditions))
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: A longer history of brand approvals and outcomes could improve ranking and increase switching costs if those signals produce repeatable lift, which Native’s public observational research does not establish. ([Native Norn](https://native.no/norn), [Native Research](https://native.no/fr/research/the-taste-layer))
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** Google already derives brand profiles and campaign assets from a website through Pomelli, and Meta offers AI-generated ad creative through its own platform. ([Google Labs, Oct 2025](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-labs/pomelli/), [Meta Q3 2026 earnings call](https://s21.q4cdn.com/399680738/files/content_files/META-Q3-2026-Earnings-Call-Transcript.pdf))

## Key Risks

- **Platform dependency:** Paid automation currently runs on Meta, which offers overlapping AI creative tools, and Native lists Google Ads as rolling out. ([Native Pricing](https://native.no/pricing), [Meta Q3 2026 earnings call](https://s21.q4cdn.com/399680738/files/content_files/META-Q3-2026-Earnings-Call-Transcript.pdf))
- **Prediction validation:** Native’s published observational research measures reach and engagement without systematic access to customer sales, leaving conversion lift unmeasured. ([Native Research](https://native.no/fr/research/the-taste-layer))
- **Paid acquisition exposure:** The CEO described scaling through paid ads and reported bringing acquisition cost down from roughly NOK 1,800 to NOK 800 per new customer. ([Impressions, Nov 2026](https://podcastportalen.no/podcast/okonomi/impressions/episode/native-fra-10-betalende-kunder-til-y-combinator-og-2m-arr-benjamin-bekken-ep-337/))
- **Brand-name ambiguity:** Trustpilot surfaces separate businesses at nativecos.com and native.io alongside native.no, creating potential for review and search attribution errors. ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/native.no))
