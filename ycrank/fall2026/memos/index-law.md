## The Idea

- **Problem:** Businesses buying legal services must locate counsel, repeatedly explain context, coordinate specialists, and navigate hourly billing, with the founder reporting routine answers taking weeks ([Index Law, Jun 2026](https://indexlaw.com/blog/why-im-starting-a-legal-platform)).
- **Approach:** Mike combines document understanding, persistent business context, contract preparation, negotiation workflows, and referrals to independent licensed lawyers when professional judgment is required ([Index Law](https://indexlaw.com/)).
- **Differentiation:** Mike targets business users directly, whereas Harvey and Legora serve legal professionals, while Crosby delivers work through an integrated law firm rather than independent counsel ([Mike](https://usemike.com/), [Crosby, Mar 2026](https://crosby.ai/blog/series-b-planting-our-flag)).
- **Business Model:** The free research preview advertises forthcoming Pro at $149/month for one user and 150 credits, Teams at $499/month for five users and 600 shared credits, custom Enterprise pricing, and separately approved lawyer fees ([Mike pricing, accessed Nov 15, 2026](https://usemike.com/)).
- **TAM/SAM:** Grand View Research estimates global legal AI spending at $2.1B in 2026, reaching $3.9B by 2030 at 17.3% CAGR during 2025–2030; this broader category is not a measured SAM for startup legal agents ([Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/legal-ai-market-report)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Distribution includes founder-led outreach through YC, setup calls, lawyer-network recruitment, and a free contract-template library promoted through LinkedIn ([YC launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/XP1-meet-mike-1-your-company-s-ai-lawyer), [LinkedIn, Nov 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/company/indexlaw)).

## Key Risks

- **Legal reasoning reliability:** Index identifies incorrect governing law, commercially unsuitable answers, and missed escalation requirements as failure modes, with evaluations and specialist supervision described as mitigations ([Index Law, Sep 2026](https://indexlaw.com/blog/dominic-raab-joins-index-law)).
- **Jurisdiction-specific oversight:** Index states that licensing, insurance, supervision, and accountability must be incorporated into delivery, while licensed independent professionals provide regulated advice ([Index Law, Sep 2026](https://indexlaw.com/blog/dominic-raab-joins-index-law)).
- **Confidential-data processing:** Documents and matter context pass through external infrastructure and AI providers, including OpenRouter; disclosed safeguards include contractual use restrictions, access controls, and encryption in transit ([Mike privacy policy](https://usemike.com/privacy)).
- **Execution limits:** Real document delivery requires enabled sending, a configured provider, and user confirmation, while Mike does not sign for users and labels simulated actions ([Mike FAQ, accessed Nov 15, 2026](https://usemike.com/)).
