# Hesper AI -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path B — Repeatable sales with account expansion.** The clearest initial buyer is an MGA claims leader whose adjusters repeatedly chase records, verify providers, and reconcile evidence. Hesper’s agents gather that evidence and return a source-linked case file for adjuster review. The growth hypothesis is that Hesper can sell the same workflow to more MGAs, reuse its claims-system integrations, and earn more usage revenue as customers route additional claims or insurance lines through it. The memo documents founder-led demos, a direct application route, a free trial for eligible MGAs and TPAs, and listed integrations with Guidewire, Duck Creek, and Majesco. It does not trace a paying customer through a repeatable acquisition and onboarding process. (The Idea, Defensibility)

Hesper reports paying production customers and pilots across carriers, MGAs, and TPAs. A founder reported 82% less average adjuster time per claim over two weeks at one MGA. That is a concrete early signal for the investigation workflow. The memo does not identify which buyer segments converted to paid production or show retention and expansion after the initial test. The reported $5M measures claim value processed. (Market & Traction, Key Facts)

**Second-market scenario.** If a non-U.S. MGA uses a supported claims system, Hesper could potentially reuse claim intake and delivery of cited findings. It would still need to validate local policy rules, access to provider records and databases, and insurer audit requirements. The memo provides a broad global market estimate but no buyer-level evidence of portability. At pre-seed, one paid rollout could materially improve the case. Confidence is low because the decisive steps of the growth mechanism remain untested in the memo. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Countercase

The connector may be reusable while the investigation workflow remains bespoke. Each new customer could require founder-led sales, security review, and custom policy configuration. Each additional claim could incur model, phone, database, and exception-handling costs, while each new geography could require fresh data-access and compliance work. The reported adjuster time savings measure customer value. Hesper’s own paid per-claim contribution and implementation effort are unknown. Existing claims platforms also offer competing automation, which could make it harder for Hesper to win distribution. These risks do not trigger the Pass gate because the memo establishes neither a structural limit on global demand nor proportional delivery costs. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can an independently sourced MGA in a second geography move from pilot to recurring paid investigations through an existing connector without a custom rebuild? A rollout record showing paid use at the buyer’s normal claims cadence, bounded setup and localization effort, and positive per-claim contribution after compute, calls, data fees, and exception handling would move the action to Reach out. Until that result is available, Watch.
