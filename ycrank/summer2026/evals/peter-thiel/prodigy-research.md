# Prodigy Research -- Peter Thiel Evaluation

Prodigy Research’s proposed secret is precise: a finance-specific foundation model can discover and execute durable delta-neutral alpha better than frontier general-purpose models and elite human traders. It would be falsified if independently marked-to-market results—after financing, slippage, fees, and increased capital—failed to preserve that advantage. The reported 108% live return over two months with no down week is an unusually strong pre-seed experiment, but it remains a company assertion without an audit, daily return series, drawdown, volatility, or capital assumptions. The comparisons with GPT-5.6 Sol, Claude Fable, and a 90th-percentile Jane Street trader are similarly unpublished. This is a falsifiable secret, but not yet a verified one.

The model, agent harness, and live-trading data could form a compounding proprietary loop: better trades create data that improves the model, which produces better trades. But alpha is not automatically a monopoly. Standard Signal already operates an end-to-end AI hedge fund, EquiLibre trades billions of dollars daily using reinforcement learning, and Numerai has $550 million in AUM. Prodigy has disclosed neither exclusive data nor a structural barrier preventing these better-capitalized competitors from reproducing its methods. A two-month performance lead may be proprietary technology—or merely temporary alpha decaying in public markets.

The founders make the technical claim credible enough to investigate. Prodigy reports that Michael Wang trained Gemini 3.1 Pro at DeepMind and generated billions in P&L as a Jane Street quant, while Yuhua Wang built AI infrastructure at Apple and Salesforce. That is an unusually coherent combination of modeling, trading, and production infrastructure. Yet résumé fit is not founding alignment: there is no public evidence about whether both founders are full-time, how long they have worked together, their ownership, compensation, or whether core research remains entirely in-house. There is no affirmative evidence of the part-time-founder, outsourced-engineering, or investor-heavy-cap-table red flags—but missing evidence cannot establish the opposite.

The apparent revenue engine is proprietary trading, which has the virtue of being singular: the company need not persuade customers if its model can compound its own capital. The difficulty is that Prodigy publishes no deployed capital, strategy capacity, leverage, gross-margin structure, fund terms, or evidence that returns survive scale. A system earning 108% on a small account may be extraordinary science and still fail power-law arithmetic once market impact appears. Nor is there a named alternative distribution route: no software pricing, institutional buyer, sales cycle, or capital-acquisition channel. My 36-month base case is therefore a pivot from balance-sheet trading toward licensing research or agent infrastructure to quantitative funds, with less than $5 million in ARR.

The strongest opposing case is serious. These are exactly the founders who might turn an adversarial information system into an autonomous financial institution, matching my unstated preference for technologies that make contested reality legible. If the live result is genuine, trading itself supplies both technical proof and distribution; customers become unnecessary. A verified zero-to-one technical event can also override uncertain monopoly structure. But “verified” is doing all the work. Prodigy currently asks me to infer the breakthrough from internal benchmarks and an unaudited eight-week record, while three funded competitors attack adjacent versions of the same proposition.

I pass today. This is not because quantitative AI is too ambitious; it is because the dossier cannot yet distinguish a durable secret from a transient trading result, or a monopoly from a well-staffed competitor. One condition would flip the decision: an independent audit showing at least 12 months of live trading at ten times the current disclosed capital base, with greater than 20% net returns and less than 10% maximum drawdown after all costs. That would transform the foundational claim from slogan into technical fact.
