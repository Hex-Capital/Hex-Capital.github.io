# GatherHQ -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Medium

### Company case

Strategic acquisition is GatherHQ’s strongest path to investor cash. An enterprise marketing or customer-insights platform could buy Gather to integrate fresh buyer interviews, simulations, and campaign production into its own products. The Cover Genius report documents a survey of 1,392 consumers across seven countries, showing Gather’s ability to deliver research at scale. The reported Salesforce deal for Listen Labs shows strategic interest in an overlapping workflow. Gather says its models draw on more than 300,000 interviews. The ownership case depends on whether continued access to fresh interviews produces a measurable prediction advantage that a buyer can retain after closing. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Defensibility, Key Risks)

The seed milestone is a production test that shows repeatable prediction lift over a buyer’s licensed or in-house alternative. Gather’s reported use by 65+ enterprise marketing teams gives it a plausible base for that test. The $5 million in reported pre-seed financing predates the shift to market research, so current runway and the capital available for the test remain unknown. Gather’s privacy policy makes data use subject to customer agreements. The memo leaves post-close usage rights and required consents unverified. No independently supported fallback shows how this investor would receive cash if a strategic sale fails. (Market & Traction, Key Risks, Key Facts)

**Proceeds bridge.** With investment terms unspecified, assume a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, 5% initial ownership diluted to 3%, and no follow-on checks. The eight-year objective is $2.5 million in gross cash. That requires $83.3 million in distributable equity proceeds at 3% ownership, $125 million at 2%, or $62.5 million at 4%. The memo supplies no usable acquisition price or buyer economics, so the sale prices below are scenario assumptions.

| Scenario | Timing and transaction assumptions | Gross cash to investor |
|---|---|---:|
| Modest sale | Year 5. $50 million enterprise value equals $50 million in distributable equity under the assumptions below. | $1.50 million (3.0×) |
| Strong sale | Year 7. $100 million enterprise value equals $100 million in distributable equity under the same assumptions. At 2% ownership, the investor receives $2 million (4.0×). | $3.00 million (6.0×) |
| Prolonged private hold | Through year 8, growth plateaus, buyers stay away, public-listing scale remains out of reach, and the company reinvests its cash. The investor retains the shares. | $0 (0.0×) |

Both sale scenarios assume zero net debt, no other price adjustments, and no preference claims that change the pro rata allocation. All equity consideration is cash at closing. Stock, escrow, earnouts, rollover, and compensation are each assumed to be zero. Proceeds are before investor taxes.

### Countercase

A platform could license Gather’s API while sourcing AI-moderated interviews and analysis from other vendors. Salesforce’s reported Listen Labs deal strengthens that countercase because it gives a potential buyer an overlapping product it could bundle. If licensing or bundling delivers comparable results, the buyer’s reason to pay an acquisition premium weakens. The memo establishes credible substitutes for parts of Gather’s workflow and leaves their performance against its combined workflow untested. It also leaves any acquisition price ceiling unverified. The structural Pass gates are therefore untriggered, and the ownership gap warrants Watch. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a production pilot with a strategic buyer show that owning Gather, with documented post-close rights to the required data, creates enough incremental value over licensing or building to support at least $83.3 million in distributable equity proceeds at 3% ownership? A repeatable lift in predicting actual buyer responses, paired with buyer-validated economics that clear the hurdle at a realistic diluted stake, would move the action to Reach out. Comparable results from a readily available substitute would keep it at Watch unless the evidence also establishes a below-hurdle price ceiling across credible buyers, which would trigger Pass.
