# Roma -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

A strategic acquisition by a productivity suite is Roma’s strongest path. Roma describes a shared task layer that hands work across Claude, ChatGPT, and Muse while preserving decisions, edits, and attribution. A buyer could pay to bring teams that use several agents into its workspace and earn more through retention or paid expansion. Ownership would be worth more than building a connector or licensing the integration if the buyer could retain those teams and their accumulated workflows after closing. The memo establishes product fit with this buyer class, while the economic benefit remains an inference. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction)

The memo records the Roma 2.0 launch, 35 U.S. iPhone ratings, and app updates addressing missed agent pickups. Roma reports that some users connected at least 10 agents and that some heavy users exceeded 100,000 tool calls before 2.0. These signals support testing sustained cross-agent use in a paid team cohort. Paid retention, burn, and the capital needed to reach that milestone are unverified. A buyer would also need continuity of third-party integrations and permission to carry over customer task histories. The memo identifies platform dependencies but establishes no closing barrier. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

Using illustrative terms, a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation buys 5%, diluted to 3% with no follow-ons. A 5× gross cash return within eight years requires $2.5 million to the holder, or $83.3 million of distributable equity at 3%. The equity hurdle rises to $125 million at 2% ownership and falls to $62.5 million at 4%. With $5 million of illustrative net debt, the 3% case requires $88.3 million of enterprise value. Roma’s actual financing terms, debt, fees, and senior claims are unverified.

- A **$25 million sale** in year eight leaves $20 million after illustrative net debt and yields **$600,000 gross, or 1.2×**, at 3%.
- A **$100 million sale** on the same terms leaves $95 million and yields **$2.85 million gross, or 5.7×**. The memo supplies no buyer economics or transaction pricing that supports this valuation for Roma.
- In a **prolonged-private scenario**, assume growth plateaus below public-market scale, buyers stay away, and Roma reinvests all available cash. With no eligible secondary or funded payout, the holder realizes **$0** over eight years and keeps the shares. Any wind-down recovery would depend on the residual after obligations and preferences, which the memo leaves unquantified.

The other routes lack separate support. Free access and the available usage signals leave recurring revenue and post-reinvestment cash flow unverified for a sponsor buyout, dividend, or buyback. The memo also leaves demand for the holder’s shares, tender eligibility, and a path to public-market scale and resale unverified. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Facts)

### Countercase

ClickUp already offers assignable agents, Notion runs agents across connected tools, and Asana embeds agents in its Work Graph. A suite could cover enough of the workflow through native features or integrations to have little reason to acquire Roma. The current usage signals leave open whether shared histories drive durable retention or whether customers would stay after a sale. This weakens the willingness-to-pay case and supports Watch. The Pass gate remains unmet because the memo establishes neither a structural block nor a supported sale price that leaves the holder materially short. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a 90-day paid team cohort show that recurring cross-provider handoffs improve retention or paid expansion enough for a productivity suite to justify a purchase near the required price? Move to **Reach out** if cohort data links completed handoffs to that benefit and a buyer-side estimate supports at least $83.3 million in distributable equity at 3% ownership, or the equivalent hurdle under actual terms. Until then, **Watch**.
