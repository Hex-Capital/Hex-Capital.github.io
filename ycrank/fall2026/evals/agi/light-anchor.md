# Light Anchor -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path B — New economies** is the stronger seed-stage case. The memo records that Anuko had left its U.S. growth work unstaffed before engaging Light Anchor to run it. Light Anchor combines review research, AI-generated creative, simulated shoppers, and live campaign data. The investment hypothesis is that this workflow cuts the labor needed to serve brands that would otherwise leave U.S. growth unstaffed. Anuko’s prior staffing decision makes it a relevant test case, though the memo gives neither its prior budget nor a like-for-like labor comparison. (The Idea)

Anuko reports that daily Amazon sales reached 54× its June baseline in the week ending October 6 and that sales were 3.07× ad spend on October 1–7. That is evidence of a live engagement and a customer-reported outcome. Seven brands appear on the case-study page, with payment and renewal terms undisclosed. The founders’ prior AI product and deployment roles at Sendbird support their ability to ship agent workflows. Repeat delivery at a measured staffing level remains untested. (Market & Traction, Founders & Team)

Light Anchor quotes a 5–15% fee on tag-attributed sales, with no retainer and ad spend billed to the client. **Illustrative scenario:** If the tags record $1,000 in sales and the fee is collected as quoted, Light Anchor earns $50–$150 before inference, account labor, reviews, retries, and exceptions. Actual contribution depends on those costs and how much tagged revenue survives attribution disputes. The owned-brand portfolio also carries physical fulfillment work, including founder and family packing reported in the memo. (The Idea, Key Risks)

### Countercase

The strongest countercase is that Light Anchor provides existing agency services more efficiently while merchants and competing agencies gain access to the same tools. Amazon and Meta already automate parts of campaign delivery, and clients own the accounts Light Anchor operates. Light Anchor says its agents reuse operating records across brands, but the memo gives no measured cross-brand lift or evidence that customers prefer its service to an equally capable alternative. The portfolio also offers no separately evidenced scarce complement for Path A. Platform tools establish partial substitution. Full-service substitution and labor savings sufficient to open a new segment remain testable, which supports Watch under the thesis override. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Will brands that previously left U.S. growth unstaffed renew at Light Anchor’s quoted fee after comparing it with a managed alternative that has access to equally capable models? A paid pilot across several such brands would answer this if it measures all human hours at comparable service quality, shows that those savings make the previously unstaffed work viable at positive contribution, and records why buyers renew after trying the alternative. That result would support Reach out if the updated score clears the outreach threshold. Comparable delivery economics without the labor savings, or buyers readily switching to the equivalent service, would trigger Pass.
