# OpenTrade -- Elad Gil Evaluation

Ojas Kandhare shipped CruiseAI, maintains 26 public GitHub repositories, and helped launch both web and iOS products before OpenTrade repositioned during July 2026. OpenTrade’s signature is high founder shipping velocity paired with an almost nonexistent index-like position: OpenTrade looks like a fast consumer-product laboratory, not yet infrastructure on a compounding category. The reported 5x user growth in five days supports the first half; the lack of monetization, moat, or structural distribution supports the second.

**Perplexity** is the high rung for founder iteration because Aravind Srinivas repeatedly built the discussed product between meetings. OpenTrade shares the artifact-producing mechanism: Kandhare shipped CruiseAI, Tario You co-authored an LLM portfolio-optimization paper, and OpenTrade rapidly moved from AI briefs toward market drills and friend challenges. OpenTrade diverges because rapid repositioning is not yet the same as a tight user-feedback loop—no retained cohort, repeat-usage figure, or feature-by-feature iteration history connects those artifacts to demand. I see builders, but not yet the Perplexity pattern of every build making the curve steeper.

**Lyft** exposed the low-rung failure mechanism: occupying a subordinate position in a market where scale advantages accrue elsewhere. OpenTrade has the analogous structural problem without Lyft’s underlying transaction network. Invstr already combines fantasy investing with brokerage, StockGro has raised $112.01 million around social education and advice, and Alinea connects education to managed portfolios. OpenTrade is not an index on retail investing; OpenTrade is currently a feature surface that must repeatedly win consumer attention against funded products controlling more valuable financial workflows.

**$5.4 trillion** of US retail stock-and-ETF activity in 2025, up 47% year over year, can increase the audience OpenTrade addresses, but it does not itself reopen the market. Over the next 18 months, higher trading volume compounds for OpenTrade only if market games convert into retained behavior, proprietary prediction data, or transaction economics. OpenTrade currently executes no trades, disclaims broker and adviser status, and offers no public pricing. That makes the cited growth a demand tailwind rather than a Stripe-style catalyst that changed who can build, distribute, or monetize the product.

**5x user growth in five days** and 3x WAU growth in three days are potentially what “just working” looks like at pre-seed, but OpenTrade provides neither absolute baselines nor cohort retention. OpenTrade also changed positioning during the measurement window, making the earlier AI-brief engagement incomparable with the current fantasy and drill product. The counterfactual that flips this dimension is straightforward: disclose six consecutive months above 20% monthly retained-user growth, with conversion into actual revenue. Until then, these ratios are closer to launch volatility and a 100K-view video—a false signal risk—than to Harvey’s revenue curve.

**Color Genomics** isolated one miracle by building workflow and data infrastructure on top of commodity sequencing rather than betting on new chemistry. OpenTrade similarly needs no foundational-model breakthrough to generate briefs or historical scenarios, which keeps OpenTrade out of the science-project category I explicitly avoid. The business architecture is less clean: durable consumer retention, a monetization model, and either regulatory approval or a brokerage partner must all emerge, while the published historical scorecard shows only a 50% hit rate and a nine-point calibration gap. Those are multiple market miracles, even if none is new physics.

**Invstr’s** existing fantasy-investing-plus-brokerage product makes my 36-month forecast specific: by July 2029, OpenTrade will have pivoted from a direct-to-consumer stock game into white-label gamified investor education for brokerages and will remain below $1 million ARR. OpenTrade’s shipping strength makes a pivot more likely than immediate death, while OpenTrade’s weak consumer index position makes sustained standalone scale unlikely. Selling engagement software to institutions would move OpenTrade closer to the workflow path and away from an expensive fight for fickle retail attention.

**100K+ views** on one OpenTrade video, 1,941 LinkedIn followers, 5x user growth, and Tario You’s 5,876-follower reach form the strongest bull case: OpenTrade may have found a native social distribution loop before the absolute numbers became public. The favorable Known Exception is Color Genomics—OpenTrade could turn commodity AI and market data into a workflow layer incumbents prefer to buy rather than build. The dossier presently loses that argument because OpenTrade has no named brokerage partner, revenue, scaled proprietary dataset, or retained cohort, while three funded competitors already span games, advice, and execution. One verifiable condition would flip my verdict: a brokerage integration delivering 100,000 retained monthly users to OpenTrade.
