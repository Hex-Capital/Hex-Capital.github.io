## The Idea

- **Problem:** Manufacturers face production interruptions from missing materials, equipment downtime, and staffing mismatches, while existing ERP workflows require manual coordination ([YC, via search snippet](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/organizely)).
- **Approach:** Organizely connects inventory, purchasing, forecasting, and work orders, with its Oppy assistant querying operational records and drafting purchase orders from supplier documents ([Organizely documentation](https://docs.organizely.io/p/eRGpOTr1FyjGTR/Oppy-AI-assistant)).
- **Differentiation:** [Inferred]: Organizely’s advertised equipment-maintenance and labor-planning scope extends beyond Katana’s inventory emphasis and Fulfil’s DTC operations emphasis, although their inventory and production capabilities overlap ([Organizely](https://organizely.io/), [Katana](https://katanamrp.com/newsroom/), [Fulfil](https://www.fulfil.io/)).
- **Business Model:** The Shopify inventory app lists Stocky at $26/month, Pro at $99/month, and Scale at $250/month, with 14-day trials, advertised introductory discounts, and custom enterprise pricing; separate factory ERP pricing: No public data found ([Shopify App Store, accessed Nov 5, 2026](https://apps.shopify.com/organizely)).
- **TAM/SAM:** Global manufacturing ERP spending is estimated at $6.36 billion in 2026, reaching $10.17 billion by 2031 at 9.84% CAGR, providing a broader category reference rather than an American-manufacturer SAM ([Mordor Intelligence, 2026](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/manufacturing-enterprise-resource-planning-market)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Distribution includes Shopify installation, Stocky migration tooling, website demo bookings, and a Shopifreaks partnership that the publisher says generated customers from an earlier pitch ([migration guide](https://docs.organizely.io/p/4B2eQfTYaaEe9h/Migrating-Data-from-Stocky), [Shopifreaks, Aug 24, 2026](https://www.shopifreaks.com/amazon-drones-tiktok-payments-canadian-tariffs/)).

## Key Risks

- **AI execution accuracy:** [Inferred]: Incorrect supplier, quantity, or pricing interpretation could affect purchasing, and Organizely explicitly instructs users to verify answers and review purchase-order drafts before sending ([Oppy documentation](https://docs.organizely.io/p/eRGpOTr1FyjGTR/Oppy-AI-assistant)).
- **Platform substitution:** [Inferred]: Shopify’s built-in purchasing, stock-counting, and inventory workflows overlap with Organizely’s entry product, giving merchants a native alternative after Stocky’s shutdown ([Shopify migration guidance](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/inventory-and-locations/transitioning-from-stocky)).
- **Affiliated outcome evidence:** [Inferred]: The quantified homepage case study comes from Kinetic Labs, which the founders operated, limiting its independence as evidence of outcomes across unrelated factories ([Organizely](https://organizely.io/), [YC, via search snippet](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/organizely)).
- **Name ambiguity:** A separate Organizely uses getorganizely.com and describes workplace productivity software, so its company profiles and metrics must be excluded ([unrelated company’s LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/organizely)).
