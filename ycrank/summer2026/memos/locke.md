## The Idea

- **Problem:** Early-stage companies need to track government activity, communicate with policymakers, and pursue public contracts but may otherwise rely on separate monitoring platforms, lobbyists, law firms, and contracting consultants ([Locke](https://locke.inc/government-affairs)).
- **Approach:** Locke combines human lobbyists with AI agents that monitor legislation, regulations, hearings, RFPs, and other signals; deliver updates through Slack; produce citation-backed compliance briefs; map stakeholders; draft proposals; and support lobbying campaigns ([Locke](https://locke.inc/government-affairs)).
- **Differentiation:** Unlike software platforms Quorum, FiscalNote PolicyNote, and Plural, Locke presents itself as a full-service government-affairs firm combining monitoring software with lobbying and government-contracting execution ([Locke](https://locke.inc/government-affairs); [FiscalNote comparison](https://fiscalnote.com/blog/fiscalnote-vs-quorum); [Plural](https://pluralpolicy.com/blog/plural-secures-10m-in-series-a-funding-led-by-s3-ventures/)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Locke likely charges service retainers or project fees because it supplies lobbyists, compliance briefs, proposal drafting, and contracting support, but no public pricing was found.
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** Locke directs prospective customers to schedule a consultation, has “first few partners” and a growing pipeline, and is hiring policy and technical staff, indicating founder-led sales supported by relationship-based delivery ([Locke](https://locke.inc/government-affairs); [Parth Badhwar on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/parth-badhwar); [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/locke)).

## Key Risks

- **Incumbent overlap:** Quorum, FiscalNote, Plural, Polintel, and Sporos already offer AI-enabled monitoring or analysis, while Quorum serves more than 2,000 organizations and announced an investment to expand agentic AI in July 2026 ([Quorum](https://www.quorum.us/company-news/quorum-receives-strategic-investment-from-enlightenment-capital-to-accelerate-ai-powered-public-affairs-platform/); [Polintel](https://polintel.ai/); [Sporos](https://www.sporos.ai/)).
- **AI-output accuracy:** Locke promises citation-backed regulatory briefs and proposal drafting, while documented legal uses of generative AI have produced nonexistent or misleading citations; Locke says its briefs are citation-backed but does not disclose its verification process ([Locke](https://locke.inc/government-affairs); [American Bar Association](https://www.americanbar.org/groups/litigation/resources/litigation-news/2026/fake-cases-real-sanctions-dangers-ai/)).
- **Lobbying compliance:** Locke’s service includes lobbying policymakers, activities that may trigger federal registration and reporting obligations under the Lobbying Disclosure Act depending on personnel, time, and revenue thresholds ([Locke](https://locke.inc/government-affairs); [U.S. Senate](https://www.senate.gov/legislative/Public_Disclosure/new_thresholds.htm)).
- **Brand disambiguation:** “Locke” is shared by unrelated businesses, and company pages still reference the legal or prior name Justinian AI, Inc., which can fragment search visibility and external records ([Locke](https://locke.inc/); [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/lockeinc)).
