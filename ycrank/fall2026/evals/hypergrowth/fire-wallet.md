# Fire Wallet -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Assuming strong product-market fit, Fire’s most plausible growth path is through developers and API providers that need to make or accept recurring micropayments. Agent spending limits, revocation, and documented e402 payment verification are reusable across markets where buyers can fund and settle in eUSD on MobileCoin. MoonPay eligibility and 0x routing leave the extent of market-specific adaptation uncertain. (*The Idea*, *Key Risks*)

Launch YC gives Fire a concrete route to developers. The documented merchant integration and Fire’s reported sub-two-second mainnet test support a path to first value. Independent production onboarding remains unverified. The WordPress integration is in beta, and the observed Ethereum-only App Store listing leaves availability of the advertised agent-payment product unclear. Funded agent accounts and spending policies could anchor repeat use. Recurring Fire revenue is a scenario that assumes customers would pay for those controls or private settlement as usage grows. (*The Idea*, *Defensibility*, *Key Risks*)

Fire reports a signed LOI and an enterprise customer in onboarding. The reported $1.5 million per month is the customer’s existing volume, and the estimated $100 million per year represents potential GMV from introductions. The memo leaves realized commercial traction and pre-revenue status unresolved, so the traction default applies. (*Market & Traction*)

### Countercase

Fire could process growing payment volume and still capture little value. At advertised pricing, contribution per e402 payment is $0 in Fire revenue minus any incremental costs Fire bears. The $0.00256 network fee goes to MobileCoin, and Fire’s share of onboarding, infrastructure, and support costs is unquantified, so the cost default applies. The structural downside gate stays open because the documented integration offers a credible reuse path and the memo establishes neither unavoidable high-touch delivery nor persistent subsidies. (*The Idea*, *Key Risks*)
