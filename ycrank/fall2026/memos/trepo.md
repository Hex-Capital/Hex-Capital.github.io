## The Idea

- **Problem** — Households rely on memory, receipts and cupboard checks to decide what to cook and replenish, often leaving one person to track what is running low and what needs using ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WhS-trepo-the-agentic-grocery-shopper-that-knows-what-you-have-and-need)).
- **Approach** — A shared kitchen device and app turn receipt scans, photos and voice updates into a household inventory used for recipes, grocery lists and draft orders ([Trepo](https://hellotrepo.com/), [Trepo Food Club](https://hellotrepo.com/food-club/)).
- **Differentiation** — Trepo captures food use on a device that attaches to an existing fridge, whereas Samsung builds recognition into its refrigerators, Cooklist builds carts through retailer websites, and Instacart’s Clementine uses purchase history to suggest replenishment ([Trepo](https://hellotrepo.com/product/), [Samsung](https://news.samsung.com/global/samsung-joins-forces-with-instacart-to-enhance-kitchen-experiences-through-hallmark-innovation), [Cooklist](https://cooklist.com/), [Instacart](https://investors.instacart.com/node/10746/pdf)).
- **Business Model** — The device costs $99 on preorder against a listed $129 regular price, with free core features and optional Food Club at $9.99 per month after three months included from device activation ([Trepo product page](https://hellotrepo.com/product/)).
- **TAM/SAM** — No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution** — Trepo takes U.S. hardware preorders directly, distributes its free iPhone and iPad app through Apple, and collects signups for Android and Food Club early access ([Trepo product page](https://hellotrepo.com/product/), [Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/trepo/id6764135986), [Trepo Android](https://hellotrepo.com/android), [Trepo Food Club](https://hellotrepo.com/food-club/)).

## Key Risks

- **Inventory accuracy** — [Inferred]: Missed check-ins or mistaken scans can leave the agent with stale stock data, while receipt imports, photos and voice entry give households several ways to update it ([Trepo](https://hellotrepo.com/)).
- **Retailer coverage** — [Inferred]: Cross-store pricing and checkout depend on local store coverage and product matching, while Food Club is advertised through early access and its investor describes retailer pilots ([Trepo Food Club](https://hellotrepo.com/food-club/), [Founders, Inc.](https://f.inc/portfolio/trepo/)).
- **Hardware fulfillment** — U.S. preorders have a March 2027 ship target, with cancellation before shipment, a 30-day return window after delivery and a one-year warranty ([Trepo product page](https://hellotrepo.com/product/)).
- **Household privacy** — The kitchen device collects user-triggered images and audio, and third parties may process voice inputs, with a downward-facing camera and push-to-talk microphone limiting capture ([Trepo privacy policy](https://hellotrepo.com/privacy/), [Trepo privacy design](https://hellotrepo.com/privacy-design/)).
- **Name collision** — Tampere University’s public repository also uses Trepo, creating ambiguity in unqualified searches ([Tampere University](https://www.tuni.fi/en/disruptions-trepo-repository)).
