# Sona8 -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Medium

### Company case

Sona8’s strongest path is **channel-led sales through management consultancies**. Transformation teams need to map how employees actually work before changing a process and can repeat that job across sites and client engagements. Sona8’s multilingual phone and browser interviews, task capture, and workflow maps make the same product credible across markets. Employee consultation, participation, and security approval can still limit where it goes live. The memo gives no evidence of a paid rollout across markets. (*The Idea*, *Key Risks*, *Market & Traction*)

In a customer testimonial, s.Oliver Group’s CEO office described using Sona8 to map processes and identify differences in teams’ planning requirements. Sona8 separately reports that a signed, unnamed top-three consultancy is using it to map client processes. That is meaningful early evidence of buyer commitment and a specific workflow benefit. The consultancy could become a repeatable channel if its delivery teams can launch the same workflow on successive client engagements. That scenario assumes a handoff the memo has yet to document. The earliest unproven link is getting a consultancy outside the founders’ network from discovery to a paid, live deployment through a standard onboarding process. (*Market & Traction*, *The Idea*)

### Countercase

The early wins may reflect founder relationships and demand for individual consulting projects. The first customers came through the founders’ network. Sona8 reports more than 10,000 completed interviews and describes platform and usage fees, but the memo gives no account-level evidence of repeat paid use at the cadence of client engagements. If each engagement requires fresh employee consultation and substantial manual setup, delivery effort could rise with revenue. The memo provides no rollout-effort or marginal-cost measurements to establish that pattern, so the structural downside gate does not apply. (*The Idea*, *Market & Traction*, *Key Risks*)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a consultancy sourced outside the founders’ network reach a paid, live client deployment through an onboarding process its delivery team can run? A record of the sale and go-live showing which steps the consultancy handled and how much Sona8 staff time the rollout took would answer it. A successful handoff with limited Sona8 effort would move the action to Reach out.
