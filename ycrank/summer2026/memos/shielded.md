## The Idea

- **Problem:** Finance and procurement teams manually combine ERP exports, spreadsheets, market data, and regulatory updates, causing tariff, commodity-price, and supplier-impact analysis to arrive after conditions have changed ([Shielded LinkedIn, Jul 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/company/shieldedglobal)).
- **Approach:** Shielded connects internal ERP data with external information, maps how prices and events affect product margins, and continuously delivers alerts, bridge charts, and response options ([YC, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/shielded); [Shielded LinkedIn, Jul 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/company/shieldedglobal)).
- **Differentiation:** [Inferred]: Shielded emphasizes company-specific margin effects and hedging or procurement actions, while Prewave emphasizes supplier-risk and sustainability monitoring, Altana maps multi-tier global value chains, and Interos covers supplier risk, tariffs, costs, and revenue impacts ([Prewave](https://www.gruendungsfonds.at/en/news-en/detail/news-prewave-series-b-en); [Altana](https://altana.ai/resources/series-c-valuation); [Interos](https://www.interos.ai/solutions)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: The demo-led onboarding, ERP integration, and promised live deployment within 48 hours indicate enterprise software sold through negotiated contracts, but no public pricing was found ([Shielded LinkedIn, Jul 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/company/shieldedglobal)).
- **TAM/SAM:** The global supply-chain risk-management market is estimated at $5.85 billion in 2026 and projected to reach $16.93 billion by 2034, a 14.21% CAGR ([Fortune Business Insights, Jul 2026](https://www.fortunebusinessinsights.com/supply-chain-risk-management-market-103757)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** [Inferred]: Founder-led outreach to finance and procurement teams is supplemented by a $1,000 referral payment and customized demos using prospect data ([Shielded LinkedIn, Jul 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/company/shieldedglobal)).

## Key Risks

- **Competitive overlap:** Interos already markets tariff-cost, product, revenue, and compliance impact tools, while three other funded platforms cover adjacent supply-chain monitoring and analysis ([Interos](https://www.interos.ai/solutions)).
- **Input and interpretation dependency:** [Inferred]: Incorrect ERP exports, supplier origins, volumes, external prices, or regulatory interpretations could propagate into margin calculations, although Shielded says its agents monitor White House, USTR, Federal Register, and CBP sources ([Shielded LinkedIn, Jul 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/company/shieldedglobal)).
- **Customer-data security:** [Inferred]: Deployment requires commercially sensitive supplier, volume, origin, product, and margin data, while no public security certification or data-governance documentation was found ([Shielded LinkedIn, Jul 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/company/shieldedglobal)).
- **Public verification and discoverability:** The website was not accessible at the time of research, customer examples are unnamed, and unrelated organizations use the Shielded name, including Shielded Technologies and Shielded Labs ([Shielded website, Jul 2026](https://www.shieldedglobal.com/); [Shielded Technologies](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/martaszluinska_today-we-launched-shielded-the-engineering-activity-7328075756662583296-WSqq); [Shielded Labs](https://blockonomi.com/shielded-labs-introduces-advisory-services-to-support-teams-building-on-zcash-network/)).
