# Reason Machines -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

The strongest route is a strategic sale to a developer-tools platform that could use Reason’s harness to lower the cost of running coding agents across cloud and local workflows. The memo records a shipped product and SpaceX’s acquisition of Cursor, an adjacent transaction that shows strategic interest in the category. Reason claims 22% lower cost per solved task than Codex on the same model in its own benchmark. The acquisition rationale depends on whether those savings persist in customers’ production workflows and are worth more to a buyer than building similar capabilities internally. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction)

The milestone is repeat paid use by engineering teams that sustain a lower cost per merged pull request at comparable quality. Reason has self-serve onboarding and published paid plans, and it reports 20,000 users in its first four weeks. Those are grounds for a production test. Paid conversion, retention, the capital required to reach the milestone, and whether the seed round closed are unverified. The memo supplies no transaction terms or evidence of an independent liquidity route. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Facts)

Under the shared benchmark, a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation starts at 5% and dilutes to 3% with no follow-ons. Receiving $2.5 million gross within eight years requires about $83.3 million in distributable equity value at full pro rata participation. The following prices are scenario assumptions. The memo gives no basis for a Reason-specific sale price. (Market & Traction, Key Facts)

| Scenario | Holder cash under stated assumptions |
|---|---|
| Modest strategic sale | $50 million enterprise value yields $1.5 million, or 3×, assuming an all-cash close within eight years, full holder eligibility and sale of the entire stake, zero net debt or deal deductions, and a pro rata waterfall. |
| Strong strategic sale | $100 million enterprise value yields $3 million, or 6×, under the same assumptions. Net debt and deal deductions totaling $10 million would leave $90 million of equity value and $2.7 million for the holder before any preference diversion. If the holder could sell only 80% of the stake at that price, cash received would fall to $2.16 million. |
| Prolonged private operation | $0 realized within eight years if cash is reinvested and no eligible share purchase or funded payout occurs. |

### Countercase

A strategic buyer could build around the same public Pi harness or extend capabilities it already has. Cursor already runs cloud agents, Cognition has its own cost-optimized harness, and Reason’s reported benchmark savings may shrink on production work measured per merged pull request. That could remove the reason to buy or keep the price below the holder’s hurdle. The memo establishes neither a structural closing barrier nor materially inadequate proceeds across the strongest credible route. Watch fits the unresolved buyer economics. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Would production savings demonstrated by repeat paying teams justify a strategic buyer paying enough to leave at least $83.3 million in distributable equity value under the benchmark terms? Customer data showing sustained savings at comparable pull-request quality, paired with a prospective buyer’s build-versus-buy economics supporting that price after deal adjustments, would move the action to Reach out.
