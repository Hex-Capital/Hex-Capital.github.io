# Applied Kinetics -- Naval Ravikant Evaluation

Applied Kinetics’ decisive product is a transformer arriving when promised. Rohit’s GPU telemetry work at Crusoe and Adhik’s AI-agent development at IBM give them relevant technical experience. Their shared Cal Hacks project establishes that they have built together. None of those artifacts yet demonstrates the specific knowledge required to qualify electrical equipment, secure factory capacity, and deliver through customs and acceptance testing. I give the founders credit for adjacent experience. The hardest part of this business remains unproven.

The strongest bull case is that Applied Kinetics is organizing fragmented physical supply around an expensive bottleneck. Its reported access to 40+ partner plants fits my preference for bringing offline capacity into a more useful network. The proposed seven-week transformer turnaround, against up to 65 weeks on the open market, would matter enormously if delivered. But the manufacturing base’s 10+ GW of equipped projects belongs to that base; it does not establish this team’s execution. A supplier directory becomes an asset when it gives customers access or reliability they cannot obtain elsewhere.

Copy the agents tomorrow. What does Applied Kinetics retain? Supplier relationships would still take work to reproduce, which earns some defensibility credit. Public evidence establishes neither exclusive capacity nor a proprietary performance dataset. Managing approvals, testing, freight, and service also creates substantial operating obligations. There is no demonstrated mechanism through which one customer improves another customer’s outcome without comparable additional company work. Forty factories establish potential supply breadth; they do not establish network effects.

The market offers genuine room to maneuver. Data center developers provide an initial buyer, while EPC firms, utilities, and energy developers offer adjacent demand for the same specification and production-coordination capabilities. The independently estimated $25.8 billion data center power market supports the existence of substantial spending, although its inclusion of services prevents treating it as the company’s addressable market. Project quotations identify a plausible payer and transaction model. Without a disclosed price, completed transaction, or gross margin, I cannot yet judge how much value Applied Kinetics captures after carrying its delivery obligations.

I would pass today. My exceptions allow investing before monetization or a visible user loop when unusually strong founder behavior or a distinctive working product supplies the missing conviction. This dossier provides relevant employment and an ambitious operating proposition, without comparable evidence of mastery. There is no documented integrity or financing-pressure red flag; uncertainty alone is not misconduct. The strongest objection to passing is that supplier execution may be the scarce asset while investors fixate on the software. My verifiable flip condition is a customer-confirmed repeat transformer delivery within the advertised seven weeks, fulfilled through enforceable capacity rights unavailable to competing brokers. That would demonstrate both learned execution and something durable behind the quotation.
