# Quippy -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path B, New economies**, is Quippy’s strongest fit. Quippy describes voice and text rehearsals with line-by-line feedback and drills tailored to recurring mistakes. The seed-stage hypothesis is that automated feedback could make daily practice affordable for adults who would otherwise go without regular coaching. Delivery labor per successful session and the transfer of practice to real conversations remain unmeasured. (The Idea; Key Risks)

Quippy had 92 U.S. iOS ratings averaging 4.7/5. One reviewer reported forming a friend group after using it to practice. These are early signs of use, with paid conversion and repeat demand among newly served users still unknown. Sole founder Jonathan Li’s prior work on conversational practice at Duolingo is relevant, and Quippy’s iOS launch shows he has shipped the product. (Market & Traction; Founders & Team)

For a subscriber on a listed annual plan, Quippy would gross about $4.17–$5.83 per month. In a scenario where subscribers rehearse several times weekly, contribution depends on usage after accounting for platform fees, speech and model costs, retries, and any human review or support. The memo gives prices, but actual usage costs and subscriber retention remain unknown. (The Idea; Key Risks)

### Countercase

A cheap general-purpose voice model could provide equivalent rehearsal and feedback. Several consumer apps already offer conversation simulations, and Quippy relies on external providers for core capabilities. Its saved history could make later drills more useful, but the memo gives no measured improvement or evidence that users keep paying when offered equivalent practice elsewhere. The substitution gate remains unresolved, and the memo identifies no separately controlled bottleneck that would support Path A. (Defensibility; Market & Traction; Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can Quippy earn repeat payments from adults who previously went without regular coaching when they can choose a cheap voice model with comparable scenarios and access to their practice history? Run one paid cohort trial that measures renewals at the listed monthly price and compares human delivery time, including retries and review, with human-led practice at comparable real-world outcomes. Repeat payment under parity plus materially lower labor would support Reach out if the updated score clears the outreach threshold. A clear failure on either condition would move the action to Pass.
