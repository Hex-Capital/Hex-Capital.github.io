# Dydema -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

A strategic acquisition by a gaming-peripheral maker is the strongest path. The buyer would pay to own a decoder and integration stack that could give its devices reliable hands-free commands with brief setup. Ownership could make sense if it secured an exclusive performance advantage and let the buyer improve the decoder across its product line. That rationale is an inference. The memo documents a research-cap demo using three imagined movements. Dydema separately reports standardizing about 128,000 hours of EEG recordings and says its pretrained decoder beat user-specific models in internal benchmarks. The memo gives no buyer-side evidence of what that advantage is worth. (Market & Traction, The Idea, Defensibility)

The operating milestone is a buyer-backed pilot on consumer-priced sensors that measures calibration time, command reliability, and false triggers with new users against available alternatives. The research-cap demo supports attempting the pilot. The reported $500,000 convertible note is a financing signal, with terms and available runway unspecified. The memo gives no pilot budget or evidence that it is funded. The rights to transfer the decoder and any data or licenses essential to maintaining it are also unverified. The disclosed API terms address model-improvement use of data, and the memo identifies neural data as sensitive. (Market & Traction, Key Risks, Defensibility)

The note lacks terms for modeling this holder’s proceeds, so the bridge uses the thesis’s illustrative $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, 5% initial ownership diluted to 3%, and no follow-on. The eight-year goal is $2.5 million gross cash, or 5×. That requires $83.3 million of distributable equity proceeds at 3% ownership, $125 million at 2%, or $62.5 million at 4%. In both sale scenarios, assume zero net debt and no other price adjustments, so enterprise value equals equity consideration. Assume a simple pro rata waterfall with no senior claims and all consideration paid in cash at close. Stock, escrow, earnouts, rollover, and compensation are each $0. Prices and timing are scenario assumptions. (Market & Traction, Key Facts)

| Scenario | Timing and outcome | Holder cash | Gross multiple |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Modest sale | Year 5 at $50 million enterprise value | $1.5 million | 3× |
| Strong sale | Year 8 at $100 million enterprise value | $3.0 million | 6× |
| Prolonged private | Through year 8, growth plateaus, buyers stay away, public scale remains out of reach, and cash is reinvested. The holder keeps the shares. | $0 | 0× |

The strong sale clears the hurdle at 3% ownership and falls short at 2%, where it pays $2 million. The memo supplies no transaction prices or buyer economics to support the modeled sale values. Its posted API prices establish a way to charge for usage. The memo supplies no evidence of the cash generation or share demand needed to support a separate liquidity route. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Facts)

### Countercase

Emotiv and Neurosity already offer programmable EEG commands, and Dydema itself offers an on-premises licensing option for labs. A buyer could license a decoder, build on existing components, or keep conventional inputs if brief calibration and three commands offer too little benefit on cheaper sensors. If a license delivers comparable performance, the rationale for paying an acquisition premium weakens. The memo establishes that substitutes exist, while leaving their relative performance and cost for the target use case unresolved. The structural Pass override therefore does not apply. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a gaming-peripheral maker’s pilot on consumer-priced sensors show a repeatable performance advantage and enough incremental value from ownership over licensing or building to support a sale above the holder’s proceeds hurdle? A buyer-validated comparison showing that result would move the action to Reach out. Comparable results from a cheaper substitute would move it to Pass if they cap the price below the hurdle.
