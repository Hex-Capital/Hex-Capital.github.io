# Tire Swing -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Pass  
**Evidence confidence:** Medium

### Company case

Assuming strong product-market fit with U.S. affordable housing operators, Tire Swing’s strongest growth path is to win multi-property operators, then reuse its certification workflow across their portfolios. The product extracts income and asset data, applies program and property rules, and assembles evidence-linked certifications for staff approval. The founders report dozens of live properties across eight states and several U.S. housing programs. That suggests the workflow can carry across domestic portfolios. Payment status, the number of distinct paying operators, and the date of first paid availability are unknown, so the reported footprint leaves the commercial growth rate unresolved. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

Founder introductions and demos using a prospect’s own file provide a repeatable sales motion that still relies on direct selling. Auto-renewing subscriptions and recurring certification work support renewal potential. More properties could increase usage, while the billing terms leave unclear whether that would increase the same customer’s bill. Under a portfolio rollout scenario, the next operator’s subscription must cover document processing, onboarding, support, and any rule maintenance attributable to that deployment. Reusing checks across properties could lower setup cost. Staff approval is operator labor, and Tire Swing’s contribution per deployment remains unknown. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Countercase

The domestic rollout spans multiple rule sets, which gives the configurable product a credible portability argument. The structural downside gate still applies. The certification output depends on local eligibility rules, forms, and approval workflows. Even within the U.S., different deadlines require separate rule configurations. Document extraction and evidence review look reusable, but serving a materially different housing system would likely require substantial work to rebuild and validate the rules and certification outputs. The memo leaves open how much of that work the existing configuration tools can absorb. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a paying operator in a materially different housing system complete and approve a live certification using the current product with bounded rule and form configuration? Accepted production certifications and a record of the vendor effort required would answer it. A deployment with limited vendor work would clear the portability gate and move the action to Watch.
