## The Idea

- **Problem:** [Inferred]: Online-poker players who want an audience must move hands into external sharing tools, as PokerStars supports social links and GGPoker exports Hand Moments outside the game ([PokerStars](https://www.pokerstars.uk/poker/learn/news/pokerstars-hand-replayer-new-tool-allows-players-to-replay-share-and-analyze-poker-hands/); [GGPoker](https://legal.ggpoker.com/poker-games/table-social-features/)).
- **Approach:** Snap Poker combines real-money poker with an in-app feed where players publish hands as interactive reels with voiceover and build followings ([YC, Aug 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/snap-poker)).
- **Differentiation:** GGPoker offers table-level video and external hand sharing, PokerStars offers a hand replayer with external social links, and ClubGG organizes private clubs, while Snap Poker describes a native public feed and follower graph ([GGPoker](https://legal.ggpoker.com/poker-games/table-social-features/); [PokerStars](https://www.pokerstars.uk/poker/learn/news/pokerstars-hand-replayer-new-tool-allows-players-to-replay-share-and-analyze-poker-hands/); [ClubGG](https://www.clubgg.com/)).
- **Business Model:** Snap Poker says it takes zero rake and “monetize[s] like a game rather than a casino,” but no product-level pricing or monetization items are publicly disclosed ([YC, Aug 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/snap-poker)).
- **TAM/SAM:** Global online poker was estimated at $4.7B in 2026 with a 10.2% 2025–2030 CAGR, while the North American segment was estimated at $1.7B in 2025 with a 10.2% 2026–2035 CAGR ([Grand View Research, Jul 2026](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/online-poker-market-report); [Custom Market Insights, Jul 2026](https://www.custommarketinsights.com/report/online-poker-market/)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** [Inferred]: Distribution could use player-created hand reels to attract followers into the poker network, but no public launch, creator-acquisition, referral, or paid-marketing data were found.

## Key Risks

- **State-by-state regulation:** Snap Poker describes real-money wagering, while regulated U.S. poker requires jurisdiction-specific approval and eligible-location controls; no Snap Poker licenses are publicly identified ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/snap-poker); [PGCB](https://gamingcontrolboard.pa.gov/pa-licensed-online-gaming-operators)).
- **Player liquidity:** A public poker operator states that online-poker attractiveness and competitive strength depend on high simultaneous player liquidity, while Snap Poker discloses no user metrics ([Rush Street Interactive 10-K](https://s26.q4cdn.com/794539746/files/doc_financials/2024/q4/7acf26b3-e07b-4a31-809d-ac3c225ec11a.pdf)).
- **Feature overlap:** GGPoker already combines real-money poker with video, voice, reactions, and hand sharing, reducing the functional distance from Snap Poker’s described product ([GGPoker](https://legal.ggpoker.com/poker-games/table-social-features/)).
- **Name and trademark collision:** 888poker has used “Snap Poker” since 2013–2014, and a SNAP POKER trademark application was previously the subject of a Snap Inc. opposition proceeding; no current-company mitigation was found ([PokerNews, Apr 2014](https://www.pokernews.com/news/promotions/2014/04/888poker-snap-poker-jet-flight-promo-18114.htm); [USPTO TTAB filing](https://ttabvue.uspto.gov/ttabvue/ttabvue-91240685-OPP-4.pdf)).
- **Content moderation:** Voiceover reels create harassment and offensive-content controls alongside gambling operations, a burden reflected in GGPoker’s microphone restrictions and reporting rules ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/snap-poker); [GGPoker](https://legal.ggpoker.com/poker-games/table-social-features/)).
