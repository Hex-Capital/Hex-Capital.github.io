## The Idea

- **Problem:** Employees and customers relying on AI-generated data answers must manually check queries because errors in business definitions can produce incorrect revenue or customer counts ([Axiomat](https://www.axiomat.com), [YC directory](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/industry/b2b/san-francisco-bay-area)).
- **Approach:** Axiomat says it converts documentation and database schemas into a logical business model and formally verifies agents’ queries before answers are used, while leaving underlying data in place ([website](https://www.axiomat.com), [YC directory](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/industry/b2b/san-francisco-bay-area)).
- **Differentiation:** Axiomat emphasizes mathematical query verification, whereas Defog emphasizes fine-tuned SQLCoder models and PromptQL emphasizes shared business context and deterministic permissions ([Axiomat](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/industry/b2b/san-francisco-bay-area), [Defog](https://defog.ai/), [PromptQL](https://promptql.io/about)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Enterprise software contracts are a plausible monetization path given the demo-led website, but public subscription tiers, usage charges, and contract prices were not found ([website](https://www.axiomat.com)).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** The website solicits company data use cases through demo bookings and email, while its privacy policy explicitly describes outreach through email and LinkedIn ([website](https://www.axiomat.com), [privacy policy, Oct. 25, 2026](https://www.axiomat.com/privacy)).

## Key Risks

- **Verification scope:** [Inferred]: The website’s “100%” correctness claim requires evidence about supported queries, assumptions, and failure handling; public independent benchmarks were not found ([company claim](https://www.axiomat.com)).
- **Business-model specification errors:** [Inferred]: A proof against an incorrectly encoded business definition could validate an unintended answer, making documentation-to-model translation a separate technical dependency ([stated architecture](https://www.axiomat.com)).
- **Platform substitution:** [Inferred]: AWS’s existing formal-verification service could reduce demand for standalone verification in overlapping workloads, although equivalence to Axiomat’s database-query coverage is unestablished ([AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/machine-learning/automated-reasoning-policy-refinement-in-amazon-bedrock/)).
