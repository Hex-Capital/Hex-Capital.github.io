## The Idea

- **Problem:** STASIS targets daily eye strain among screen users who currently rely on static filtering glasses, break reminders, and manual brightness adjustments ([STASIS, retrieved Oct 2026, via search snippet](https://www.get-stasis.com/)).
- **Approach:** The company describes inward-facing sensors, personalized ocular analytics, automatic lens tinting, and Bluetooth-connected screen adjustments ([STASIS, via search snippet](https://www.get-stasis.com/)).
- **Differentiation:** [Inferred]: STASIS combines ocular monitoring with environmental adjustments, whereas IXI changes optical focus and Emteq measures facial activity and behavior ([founder description, via search snippet](https://www.linkedin.com/in/soham-ch), [IXI](https://ixieyewear.com/), [Emteq documentation](https://support.emteqlabs.com/01_ocosense/02_ocosense-glasses/)).
- **Business Model:** Indexed preorder copy conflicts between $240 and $95, with optional $15/month tracking after the first year ([STASIS, retrieved Oct 2026, via search snippet](https://www.get-stasis.com/)).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** Website preorders and founder LinkedIn demonstrations are visible acquisition channels ([STASIS](https://www.get-stasis.com/), [founder LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/soham-ch), both via search snippets).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** STASIS claims patented sensors, but patent identifiers and claim scope were not located ([STASIS, via search snippet](https://www.get-stasis.com/)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Longitudinal ocular measurements could support personalized baselines, but public dataset size and evidence of improved predictions are unavailable.
- **Market structure:** [Inferred]: No structural barrier identified at this stage, given existing eye-tracking adaptive lenses and glasses-based physiological sensing ([IXI](https://ixieyewear.com/), [JINS research report](https://jinsholdings.com/jp/common/file/2021_sustainability_report_en.pdf)).
- **Commoditization risk:** [Inferred]: Adaptive-eyewear and sensing companies could build overlapping features, although their existing products do not establish how easily STASIS’s complete system could be replicated ([IXI](https://ixieyewear.com/), [Emteq documentation](https://support.emteqlabs.com/01_ocosense/02_ocosense-glasses/)).

## Market & Traction

- **Traction signals:**
  - Fall 2026 YC participation; no active YC job postings at research time ([YC, Oct 27, 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/stasis/jobs)).
  - Founder-reported 30-day personal wear test; this is self-testing rather than an independently measured customer outcome ([Soham Chakraborty LinkedIn, retrieved Oct 2026, via search snippet](https://www.linkedin.com/in/soham-ch)).
  - Founder reports conversations with Kaiser Optometry leadership, eyewear brands, and testing laboratories; signed STASIS partnerships were not verified ([founder LinkedIn, via search snippet](https://www.linkedin.com/in/soham-ch)).
  - **Separate product—GlaucoGlasses:** GlaucoNova reported intent forms from 348 physicians and LOIs from seven distributors totaling $168,000 of pilot intent; these are glaucoma-product claims, not STASIS revenue or orders ([GlaucoNova LinkedIn, retrieved Oct 2026, via search snippet](https://www.linkedin.com/company/glauconova-labs-inc)).
- **Competitors (including adjacent and historical alternatives):**
  - **IXI** ($36.5M raised by April 2025; revenue unknown): Eye-tracking autofocus lenses address optical correction rather than STASIS’s proposed ocular-wellness monitoring, with Plural leading the disclosed Series A and its standalone round size not consistently separated from cumulative funding ([funding chronology, Oct 2026](https://www.eiceu.com/report/pixieray-eic-accelerator-autofocus-glasses), [IXI product](https://ixieyewear.com/)).
  - **Emteq Labs** ($7M prior capital and $4M grants reported; revenue unknown for the combined business): OCOsense tracks facial activity, attention, and eating behavior; its US crowdfunding campaign recorded $117,500 committed at its December 2025 close, with no lead investor identified ([KingsCrowd](https://kingscrowd.com/emteq-labs-on-dealmaker-securities-2025/), [Emteq documentation](https://support.emteqlabs.com/01_ocosense/02_ocosense-glasses/)).
  - **JINS MEME** (product-specific funding unavailable; product revenue unknown): Historical ocular-potential and motion-sensing glasses overlapped with self-care monitoring, but consumer sales ended March 27, 2024, and app support was scheduled through August 2025 ([JINS technology report](https://jinsholdings.com/jp/common/file/2021_sustainability_report_en.pdf), [JINS discontinuation announcement](https://www.jins.com/jp/topics_detail.html?info_id=680)).
- **Why now:** [Inferred]: Recent consumer adoption of sensor-equipped eyewear may reduce unfamiliarity with wearing electronics in glasses, with NIQ reporting Ray-Ban Meta sales-value growth of 158% year-to-date in 2025 across its tracked European markets ([NIQ, 2026](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/a-new-era-for-vision-correction/)).

## Key Risks

- **Technical validation:** [Inferred]: Measuring ocular activity does not itself validate derived fatigue or cognitive-performance scores, and the located demonstration is founder self-testing rather than independent validation ([founder LinkedIn, via search snippet](https://www.linkedin.com/in/soham-ch)).
- **Delivery and integration:** [Inferred]: Shipping depends on integrating sensing, tint control, and connected-device adjustments; indexed shipping guidance is end-2026 to early-2027 ([STASIS, via search snippet](https://www.get-stasis.com/)).
- **Commercial-information ambiguity:** Website not accessible at time of research, following a redirect to wearstasis.com; indexed pricing conflicts, preventing verification of current checkout terms ([STASIS indexed page, via search snippet](https://www.get-stasis.com/)).
- **Consumer retention:** [Inferred]: JINS MEME’s discontinued consumer sales and app support show that an ocular-sensing product can lose ongoing consumer availability, although JINS did not attribute discontinuation to a disclosed retention metric ([JINS](https://www.jins.com/jp/topics_detail.html?info_id=680)).
- **Identity and product attribution:** [Inferred]: Unrelated STASIS businesses and the founders’ separate GlaucoNova product create attribution risk; no public transition timeline linking GlaucoGlasses to STASIS was verified ([unrelated STASIS directory entry](https://www.eu-startups.com/directory/stasis/), [GlaucoNova, via search snippet](https://www.linkedin.com/company/glauconova-labs-inc)).
