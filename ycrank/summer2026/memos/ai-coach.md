## The Idea

- **Problem:** Field-sales managers rely on physical ride-alongs, including company-reported 100-kilometer trips to observe one meeting, leaving most rep conversations unseen and coaching intermittent ([YC launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ai-coach), Aug 2026).
- **Approach:** Reps record in-person meetings on a mobile app; AI transcribes and analyzes them, compares behavior with top-performing reps, assigns one skill at a time, and gives managers team-level patterns ([COACH website](https://www.getcoach.com/), Aug 2026; [YC launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ai-coach), Aug 2026).
- **Differentiation:** [Inferred]: Rilla and Siro offer similar mobile recording and analysis, while COACH publicly emphasizes one-skill-at-a-time coaching and a shared “sales brain”; Gong covers a broader revenue workflow spanning calls, emails, meetings, and forecasting ([COACH website](https://www.getcoach.com/), Aug 2026; [Siro](https://www.siro.ai/insights/series-b), May 2025; [Gong](https://www.gong.io/press/gong-growth-accelerates-past-55-yoy-arr-tops-500m), May 2026).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Demo-led B2B subscription contracts for field-sales teams, supported by published ARR claims and the absence of public self-serve pricing ([COACH website](https://www.getcoach.com/), Aug 2026; [COACH job listing](https://fr.linkedin.com/jobs/view/founding-customer-success-intern-at-coach-4405261223), 2026).
- **TAM/SAM:** The broader sales-enablement-platform market was estimated at $6.9 billion in 2026 and projected to reach $21.2 billion by 2033 at a 17.3% CAGR, but no public SAM was found for in-person conversation coaching specifically ([Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/sales-enablement-platform-market-report), Jun 2026).
- **GTM / Distribution:** COACH targets organizations with at least five field reps through booked demos, forward-deployed pilots, customer-success work, and expansion from France into the United States ([YC launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ai-coach), Aug 2026).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** COACH reports analyzing 74,473 field meetings across 200 companies, providing a corpus of sales behaviors and outcomes for its coaching system ([COACH LinkedIn](https://fr.linkedin.com/company/my-aicoach), Jun 2026).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: A larger customer-specific conversation corpus could improve benchmarking and personalized coaching, but no public validation methodology or model-performance evidence was found.
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage, because Rilla and Siro already offer mobile capture, transcription, analysis, and coaching for face-to-face sales ([Rilla](https://sacra.com/c/rilla/), 2026; [Siro](https://www.siro.ai/insights/series-b), May 2025).
- **Commoditization risk:** Speech-to-text and LLM analysis are available through external providers, and COACH itself names Anthropic as its AI and transcription processor ([COACH privacy policy](https://www.ai-coach.fr/privacy-policy), Feb 2026).

## Key Risks

- **Direct competition:** Rilla and Siro already implement substantially the same mobile recording and AI-coaching workflow and have raised approximately $72–79 million and $75 million respectively ([Rilla](https://sacra.com/c/rilla/), 2026; [Siro](https://www.siro.ai/insights/series-b), May 2025).
- **Recording consent:** The product records customer conversations, while California law requires all-party consent for confidential communications; COACH’s policy requires prior explicit consent and offers deletion following withdrawal ([California Penal Code §632](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=PEN&sectionNum=632.); [COACH privacy policy](https://www.ai-coach.fr/privacy-policy), Feb 2026).
- **Processor dependency:** Anthropic handles AI and transcription, creating operational exposure to an external provider; COACH states that EU data transfers use contractual clauses, encryption, and impact assessments ([COACH privacy policy](https://www.ai-coach.fr/privacy-policy), Feb 2026).
- **Metric consistency:** Public conversion-lift claims differ between 27% on the company LinkedIn page and 32% on the YC page, and neither source publishes cohort definitions or methodology ([COACH LinkedIn](https://fr.linkedin.com/company/my-aicoach), Jul 2026; [YC company page](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ai-coach), Aug 2026).
- **Name and brand attribution:** “COACH” is a common name, while public assets span getcoach.com, ai-coach.fr, the package `com.lucy.coach`, and developer MY LUCY CORP, which can complicate search-result disambiguation; the YC page now links getcoach.com ([YC company page](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ai-coach), Aug 2026; [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lucy.coach), Jul 2026).
