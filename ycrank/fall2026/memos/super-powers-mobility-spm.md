## The Idea

- **Problem:** Military operators powering radios, sensors, and electronic warfare equipment use generators or idling engines, creating acoustic and thermal signatures and fuel-resupply requirements ([SPM PEK page, accessed Oct 2026](https://spm.us/pek)).
- **Approach:** SPM combines battery packs with power-export electronics, advertising a standard PEK with 15 kWh modules, 12 kW continuous AC output, and 14 kW DC output ([SPM specifications, accessed Oct 2026](https://spm.us/pek)).
- **Differentiation:** SPM combines custom packs, power export, and vehicle engineering, while Inventus supplies cross-industry battery systems and Electric Power Systems emphasizes aviation modules and charging infrastructure ([SPM](https://spm.us/custom-development), [Inventus](https://inventuspower.com/h-i-g-capital-invests-in-inventus-power/), [EPS](https://epsenergy.com/united-grows-its-electric-battery-portfolio-with-investment-in-eps/)).
- **Business Model:** Industrial systems use quotation-based hardware and engineering sales, while the consumer LB 60/60 battery lists a $1,799 first-batch purchase or $100 refundable reservation toward later production ([SPM purchasing](https://spm.us/how-to-buy), [battery listing, accessed Oct 2026](https://shop.spm.fun/products/lb-60-60)).
- **TAM/SAM:** Broader military-battery market context is $1.6 billion in 2026 with 4.3% forecast CAGR through 2033; SPM-specific TAM/SAM: No public data found ([Grand View Research, 2026](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/military-battery-market-report)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** SPM solicits direct government and industrial inquiries, offers evaluation units, and markets subcontracting through defense primes alongside commercial engineering agreements ([SPM purchasing page, accessed Oct 2026](https://spm.us/how-to-buy)).

- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Platform-specific integration and qualification work could create replacement costs, but public evidence of retained production programs or contractual exclusivity was not found.
- **Market structure:** [Inferred]: No structural barrier identified at this stage, given Inventus already develops custom military battery systems and EPS supplies integrated energy-storage and power-electronics technology ([Inventus, Apr 2026](https://inventuspower.com/h-i-g-capital-invests-in-inventus-power/), [EPS](https://epsenergy.com/united-grows-its-electric-battery-portfolio-with-investment-in-eps/)).
