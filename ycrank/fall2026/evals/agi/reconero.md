# Reconero -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Path B — New economies is the strongest path. Reconero says it can identify events across video sources, draft narratives linked to footage, and fill department forms for officer review. The seed-stage hypothesis is that cheaper video understanding could make footage-backed reporting practical for routine incidents where reviewing footage manually would take too much officer time. That use case is an assumption. The memo describes the workflow and documents a public launch, while the labor savings and added reporting coverage remain unmeasured. Path A lacks evidence of a bottleneck Reconero controls. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Defensibility)

The founders built the initial demo together. The company reports consulting more than 100 officers, and Azeroual says several police units tested an earlier product and provided feedback. That supports early buyer engagement and gives the team a starting point for a field trial. The memo identifies no paid or repeat use. A department subscription is a scenario assumption. Scaling it would require the price per agency to cover inference, onboarding, and ongoing integration and support. The memo provides no pricing or cost inputs for that calculation. (Founders & Team, Market & Traction, The Idea, Key Facts)

### Countercase

Reconero may help agencies do existing paperwork without making any new use viable. A randomized trial of a competing drafting tool found no speed gain, and a separate study of another system found lower accuracy ratings for AI-assisted reports. Officer review, corrections, and audit requirements could absorb the time saved. Even if Reconero clears that hurdle, Axon bundles drafting into its evidence platform and Code Four already offers source-linked drafting and records integrations. Agency-specific form mappings could give buyers a reason to stay, but the memo contains no evidence that agencies prefer Reconero when competing tools offer comparable functionality. Both Path B conditions remain unproven. The evidence on competing tools leaves room for a representative trial of Reconero itself. (Key Risks, Defensibility)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a paid field trial show that a department repeatedly uses and pays for Reconero to produce footage-backed reports it previously skipped because of officer time, even when it can choose a functionally comparable tool? Compare manual work and both tools at equivalent report quality, counting footage review, corrections, exceptions, sign-off, and audit work. Repeat paid use with lower all-in labor and a demonstrated preference for Reconero would support Reach out if the updated score clears 65. If a representative trial shows that review erases the savings or that the department readily substitutes the comparable tool, move to Pass. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Risks)
