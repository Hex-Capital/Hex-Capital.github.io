## The Idea

- **Problem:** iPhone users seeking hands-free assistance must often interpret search results and manually open separate apps because existing phone assistants do not reliably complete cross-app tasks ([YC launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/touchy), Aug 2026).
- **Approach:** Touchy launches from the lock screen or an iPhone action button, receives voice and camera input, maintains a continuous conversation with memories and preferences, and connects to more than 1,000 tools including Slack, WhatsApp, Calendar, and Uber ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/touchy); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/touchy-ai-at-your-fingertips/id6798889392), Aug 2026).
- **Differentiation:** Touchy provides a native voice-and-image iPhone interface, while Poke operates through messaging without an app, Rabbit uses dedicated hardware, and TwinMind emphasizes ambient listening and a personal knowledge graph ([Touchy YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/touchy); [Poke](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/08/poke-makes-using-ai-agents-as-easy-as-sending-a-text/); [Rabbit](https://www.rabbit.tech/newsroom/rabbit-raises-additional-10m); [TwinMind](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/10/ex-google-x-trio-wants-their-ai-to-be-your-second-brain-and-they-just-raised-6m-to-make-it-happen/)).
- **Business Model:** The iPhone app is free with in-app purchases, and Apple lists “Touchy Plus” at $14.99 without displaying a billing interval ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/touchy-ai-at-your-fingertips/id6798889392), Aug 2026).
- **TAM/SAM:** The global AI-assistant market was estimated at $18.60 billion in 2025 with an 18.8% CAGR through 2033, while U.S. AI-assistant software generated $2.363 billion in 2025 with a projected 16.11% CAGR through 2033 ([Grand View Research global](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/ai-assistant-market-report), May 2026; [U.S. segment](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/horizon/outlook/ai-assistant-software-market/united-states), Jul 2026).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Touchy is distributed through Apple’s App Store using free trial access and an August 2026 YC launch directed at iPhone users ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/touchy); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/touchy-ai-at-your-fingertips/id6798889392)).

## Key Risks

- **Privileged-data exposure:** Touchy may handle location, contacts, messages, photos, and audio and shares relevant input, output, and personal information with OpenAI, Google Cloud AI, and Anthropic; its policy provides permission controls and states that account data is not required beyond three months after termination ([privacy policy](https://touchyapp.com/privacy/), Aug 2026).
- **Action reliability:** The company states that current models are not yet sufficient for its intended filtering and personalization and that “airtight safety guarantees” are needed before agents receive access to every surface ([Touchy](https://touchyapp.com/), Aug 2026).
- **Platform substitution:** Touchy is iPhone-only and requires iOS 18 or later, while Apple’s forthcoming Siri AI provides personal context, visual understanding, and system-level cross-app actions ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/touchy-ai-at-your-fingertips/id6798889392); [Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/06/apple-introduces-siri-ai-a-profoundly-more-capable-and-personal-assistant/), Jun 2026).
- **Recent product pivot:** The founders publicly described Tokenless as model-selection infrastructure for agentic workflows approximately two months before launching Touchy as a consumer assistant, creating continuity risk across customer, product, and distribution assumptions ([founder announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kevinzhiwang_im-founding-a-company-tokenless-with-rohit-activity-7472776281541996544-i9yy); [current YC page](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/touchy)).
- **Name disambiguation:** Search results for “Touchy” surface unrelated applications and companies, while the App Store identifies Touchy’s seller as Tokenless, Inc.; the `touchyapp.com` domain and YC profile provide disambiguation ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/touchy-ai-at-your-fingertips/id6798889392); [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/touchy)).
