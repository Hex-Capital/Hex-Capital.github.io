## The Idea

- **Problem:** Developers building AI agents need current, structured web information but otherwise assemble crawlers, scrapers, parsers, and multiple data vendors while relying on model knowledge frozen at its cutoff ([Context.dev](https://www.context.dev/), Jul 2026).
- **Approach:** One API scrapes URLs, crawls sites, converts pages to markdown or HTML, extracts data into developer-defined JSON schemas, captures screenshots, and returns brand, company, product, and transaction data ([Context.dev](https://www.context.dev/), Jul 2026).
- **Differentiation:** Context.dev combines scraping, structured extraction, brand intelligence, and transaction identification under one API, whereas Firecrawl centers on web extraction, Tavily on agent search, and Browserbase on hosted browser automation ([Context.dev](https://www.context.dev/); [Firecrawl](https://www.firecrawl.dev/blog/firecrawl-v2-series-a-announcement); [Tavily](https://www.tavily.com/blog/big-news-from-tavily-announcing-25m-series-a-to-power-the-internet-of-agents); [Browserbase](https://www.browserbase.com/blog/series-b-and-beyond), Jul 2026).
- **Business Model:** Consumption-metered subscriptions include Free, Developer at $25/month, Pro at $149/month, Scale at $499/month, paid overages, and custom Enterprise pricing above 2 million monthly credits ([Context.dev pricing](https://www.context.dev/pricing), Jul 2026).
- **TAM/SAM:** The web-scraping market was $1.34 billion in 2025 and is forecast to reach $3.49 billion by 2031 at a 17.39% CAGR ([Mordor Intelligence](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/web-scraping-market), Jul 2026).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Self-service API keys, a free tier, SDKs in five languages, agent-driven signup instructions, customer case studies, startup discounts, and an affiliate program support developer-led acquisition ([Context.dev](https://www.context.dev/), Jul 2026).

## Key Risks

- **Competitive substitution:** Firecrawl, Tavily, and Browserbase have each funded overlapping agent-web infrastructure, while Firecrawl’s open-source project had surpassed 48,000 stars ([Firecrawl](https://www.firecrawl.dev/blog/firecrawl-v2-series-a-announcement), Aug 2025).
- **Access and compliance dependency:** Product reliability depends on continued access to third-party websites, and a Product Hunt reviewer reported unclear behavior when target sites change robots.txt or terms of service ([Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/context-dev?launch=context-dev-2), Jul 2026).
- **Freshness limits:** Context.dev describes live retrieval, but cached brand data refreshes quarterly by default and data older than three months is refetched upon request ([Context.dev](https://www.context.dev/), Jul 2026).
- **Latency variability:** First-time domain lookups can require 10–30 seconds, requiring prefetching, longer client timeouts, or asynchronous polling for time-sensitive applications ([Context.dev](https://www.context.dev/), Jul 2026).
- **Name and domain ambiguity:** Searches for “context.dev” can return unrelated technical phrases and datasets, requiring verification against the context.dev domain, YC S26 batch, or Yahia Bakour.
