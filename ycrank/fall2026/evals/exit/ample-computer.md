# Ample Computer -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

A strategic sale to a hosting platform such as Railway or Render is Ample’s strongest plausible route. Both already serve external coding agents. Ample’s potential value to them is turning an app’s source code into a live deployment by identifying and provisioning the services it needs. Ample’s salon-booking test reportedly required nearly ten services, and the memo records 2,144 published deployment recipes. A buyer could gain faster onboarding for complex apps if Ample’s workflow measurably improves deployment success or paid conversion. That buyer benefit remains an inference. (*The Idea*, *Defensibility*)

The memo records a verified Claude connector and reports that Ample crossed 5,000 users at public launch. Two customer testimonials describe live apps taking checkout or registration submissions. Those signals make it plausible to test whether launch users convert into paying customers and keep production apps running. Paid retention, deployment costs, and runway remain unknown. The listed $125,000 seed entry leaves the capital needed to reach that milestone unclear. (*Market & Traction*, *Key Facts*)

The shared benchmark assumes a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, 5% starting ownership diluted to 3%, and no follow-ons. The holder needs $2.5 million in gross cash within eight years, which requires about $83.3 million in distributable equity at 3%. The sale cases below assume $5 million in combined net debt and deal deductions, zero senior claims, and an eligible holder selling the full stake for cash at closing within eight years. The memo leaves buyer pricing and the actual waterfall unverified. (*Market & Traction*, *Key Facts*, *Key Risks*)

| Scenario | Cash to the holder |
|-----------|--------------------|
| Modest sale | $40 million enterprise value leaves $35 million in distributable equity and pays $1.05 million, or 2.1×. |
| Strong sale | $100 million enterprise value leaves $95 million in distributable equity and pays $2.85 million, or 5.7×. Selling only 80% of the stake would reduce cash received to $2.28 million, or 4.56×. |
| Prolonged private | Continued operation with cash reinvested yields $0 realized within eight years. The memo provides no evidence of an independent liquidity route for this holder. |

### Countercase

Railway and Render already expose infrastructure tools to external agents and could build comparable automation themselves. Ample’s recipe count shows breadth, while comparative deployment performance and paid retention remain unverified. The conflicting operator names in Ample’s legal and company pages also leave open which entity owns the product and can transfer customer contracts. These facts support Watch. The memo leaves both a structural closing block and inadequate proceeds under the strongest credible route unproven. (*Defensibility*, *Key Facts*, *Key Risks*)

### Decisive diligence question

Would a hosting platform’s economics justify buying Ample at a price that clears the holder’s cash hurdle? A buyer-side build-versus-buy analysis grounded in a head-to-head production test of deployment success and paid conversion could answer that. A defensible price range above roughly $88.3 million in enterprise value under the modeled deductions and full-sale assumptions would move the action to Reach out.
