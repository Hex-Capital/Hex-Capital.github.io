## The Idea

- **Problem:** Consumer-protection investigators rely on incoming complaints and manual investigations, leaving violations on changing websites undetected, according to the founders ([YC launch, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Vq1-particularity-ai-investigators-for-government-regulators)).
- **Approach:** Agents navigate commercial websites, test checkout prices and cancellation flows, capture timestamped and hashed evidence, and map findings to statutory elements ([YC launch, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Vq1-particularity-ai-investigators-for-government-regulators)).
- **Differentiation:** [Inferred]: Particularity emphasizes regulator-directed case sourcing, whereas MirrorWeb archives communications, PerformLine monitors enterprise marketing compliance, and IntelligenceBank/Red Marker reviews marketing materials ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/particularity), [Mainsail](https://mainsailpartners.com/company/red-oak/), [PerformLine](https://performline.com/press-release/performline-attracts-strategic-investment-from-m33-growth/), [Startup Daily](https://www.startupdaily.net/topic/business/marketing-startup-intelligencebank-acquires-compliance-rival-red-marker/)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Agency software subscriptions are a possible monetization path given the identified buyers; pricing: No public data found; “Website not accessible at time of research” applies to both access attempts on October 27, 2026.
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** The founders solicit introductions to state attorneys general, city consumer-affairs departments, state agencies, and former federal consumer-protection staff ([YC launch, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Vq1-particularity-ai-investigators-for-government-regulators)).

## Key Risks

- **Detection reliability:** [Inferred]: Incorrectly interpreting checkout or cancellation flows could generate false allegations; the launch describes autonomous investigations without publishing accuracy benchmarks ([YC launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Vq1-particularity-ai-investigators-for-government-regulators)).
- **Evidence suitability:** [Inferred]: Timestamping and hashing address record integrity but do not establish that a captured interaction proves a violation; Particularity describes these safeguards and statutory mapping without publishing judicial validation ([YC launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Vq1-particularity-ai-investigators-for-government-regulators)).
- **Agency-built substitution:** [Inferred]: Regulators could combine existing tools instead of buying a separate case-sourcing platform; UNCTAD documents the EU eLab’s mystery-shopping, recording, and entity-risk tools ([UNCTAD, 2025](https://unctad.org/system/files/information-document/ccpb_artificial_intelligence_consumer_protection_en.pdf)).
- **Name ambiguity:** An unrelated directory record identifies Particularity with particularity.be; its company data were excluded using Particularity’s YC-linked domain and founders ([directory record](https://podobne-firmy.pl/particularity), [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/particularity)).
