# Tire Swing -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Strategic acquisition by an affordable-housing property software vendor** is Tire Swing’s strongest path. The memo documents that suites such as Yardi Voyager and MRI already serve the certification workflow. Tire Swing says its software applies program and property rules, flags conflicting evidence, and assembles certifications using operators’ existing forms. It also reports thousands of checks. A suite vendor could pay to own that rules library and control how it is integrated and updated across its customer base. That rationale depends on Tire Swing proving that ownership delivers a material advantage over building or licensing the capability. (*The Idea*, *Defensibility*, *Market & Traction*)

The founders report dozens of live properties across eight states. The operating milestone is a production benchmark across multiple operators showing materially fewer staff review hours while maintaining certification accuracy as rules change. The reported footprint gives Tire Swing a place to run that test. The memo supplies no measured results, test budget, or runway. It describes safeguards for sensitive tenant records and leaves IP ownership, contract assignment, and data-transfer permissions unverified. (*Market & Traction*, *Key Risks*)

The memo supplies no investment terms or usable acquisition pricing evidence. Under the illustrative seed terms, a $500,000 check buys 5% initially and dilutes to 3%, with no follow-on checks. The eight-year objective is $2.5 million in gross cash. That requires $83.3 million in distributable equity proceeds at 3%, $125 million at 2%, or $62.5 million at 4%.

The sale figures below are scenario assumptions. They assume zero net debt and transaction costs, a simple pro rata waterfall with no preference adjustment, and all consideration paid in cash at closing. Stock, escrow, earnouts, rollover, and compensation are each zero. Under those assumptions, enterprise value equals distributable equity proceeds.

| Scenario | Timing and outcome | Gross investor cash |
|---|---|---:|
| Modest sale | Year 5, $40 million enterprise value | $1.2 million (2.4×) |
| Strong sale | Year 7, $100 million enterprise value | $3.0 million (6.0×) at 3% ownership, or $2.0 million (4.0×) at 2% |
| Prolonged private | Through year 8, growth plateaus, buyers stay away, public scale remains out of reach, and cash is reinvested | $0 (0×), with shares retained |

The memo’s subscription terms describe how customers are billed. There is no usable evidence of sustainable cash flow for a sponsor buyout or shareholder payout, demand for this holder’s shares, or a path to public resale within eight years. No independent fallback is supported. (*The Idea*, *Key Facts*, *Market & Traction*)

### Countercase

An incumbent could get enough of the benefit from its own suite or an ordinary license. Yardi already bundles certification with accounting and subsidy administration, ExactEstate offers document tampering checks, and Roost markets income calculations linked to source documents. That overlap weakens the ownership premium. The memo leaves open whether those substitutes match Tire Swing’s performance and what price ceiling they impose. No structural Pass override is established. The decisive gap is evidence that a buyer could justify a price that clears the holder’s hurdle. (*The Idea*, *Defensibility*, *Market & Traction*)

### Decisive diligence question

Would independently measured production results across multiple operators give a suite vendor an economic reason to buy Tire Swing at a price that clears the investor’s hurdle? A buyer-side comparison of acquiring, building, and licensing that uses those results and supports at least $83.3 million in distributable equity proceeds at 3% ownership, with a credible path to transferring the required assets, would move the action to Reach out. Evidence that equivalent capabilities are available at a cost that puts acquisition value materially below the hurdle would move it to Pass.
