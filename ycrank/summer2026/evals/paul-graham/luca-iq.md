# Luca IQ -- Paul Graham Evaluation

Angelo Policicchio is a CPA who lived through tax-season drudgery, while Akash Sadashivapeth’s public hacker trail amounts to 15 GitHub repositories, three followers, and a pinned fork. Luca IQ is therefore a high-schlep, low-hacker-genealogy company: Luca IQ understands the ugly work unusually well, but has not yet shown that its founders can turn that understanding into an exceptional technical artifact. I like the first fact more than most investors will. I distrust the second more than most investors will.

**Coinbase** is the high rung on the schlep ladder because regulatory grind became the product rather than an obstacle around it. Luca IQ has the same promising shape: IRS-authorized e-filing, deterministic computation, document-level traceability, migrations from entrenched systems, and human review of every line. Luca IQ diverges where it matters most: Coinbase had Armstrong’s working wallet, while Luca IQ’s 1040 engine is still “coming soon” and in pilot. The schlep is real, but the decisive artifact is not finished.

**Loopt** is the low rung on hacker genealogy because force of personality overrode weak product timing; that was a known exception, not the pattern I usually want. Luca IQ has engineering credentials in Akash’s Penn master’s degree and former head-of-data-science role, but Luca IQ has no comparable Dropbox demo, Stripe-like developer reputation, competitive-programming result, or meaningful open-source adoption. Fifteen repositories with three followers are tracks, but not animal tracks. The two Harvard MBAs make this gap more dangerous because polished founders can look technical without being hackers.

More than 300 firm-owner interviews and a completed customer tax season give Luca IQ a resourcefulness mechanism that could compound over the next 18 months. Each ugly migration teaches Luca IQ another workflow; each reviewer correction can improve document mappings; each seasonal deployment can produce referrals. The reported 1,100 hours saved and 57% reduction in administrative preparation time show that Luca IQ shipped something people used. But interviews, an outbound-sales internship, and hundreds of pilot applications are organized execution, not an Airbnb cereal-box stunt. I see persistence, not yet relentless resourcefulness.

Angelo Policicchio and Akash Sadashivapeth form the right numerical configuration and cover CPA operations plus data engineering, but Luca IQ’s dossier establishes only that they attended Harvard Business School together and presented at HBS Demo Day. Luca IQ’s cofounder score would flip upward if Angelo and Akash had built together for years, survived a failed product, or could name a pre-MBA artifact they shipped jointly. It would flip downward if they met principally to enter a promising market. At present, the pair looks complementary but recently assembled.

**Dropbox** began with Drew Houston repeatedly suffering the problem himself; Luca IQ has the same organic-idea mechanism through Angelo’s first-hand tax-season experience. Luca IQ is not merely applying AI to a large accounting category: the product attacks document chasing, transcription, workpaper assembly, and the three hours of manual entry Angelo’s profession actually endures. The difference is that Dropbox’s founder could build the decisive artifact himself. Luca IQ splits the pain and implementation across two founders, so the organic origin is strong even though the hacker expression remains incomplete.

Luca IQ’s deterministic 1040 engine is still in pilot, so my 36-month forecast is that Luca IQ reaches $4 million to $9 million in annual recurring revenue from regional CPA firms while retaining human review and failing to establish the tax engine as a widely adopted standalone API. Luca IQ’s high-schlep advantage should win workflow customers because 30 minutes from intake to review-ready is valuable. Luca IQ’s weak visible hacker genealogy makes the more ambitious infrastructure outcome less likely.

Thomson Reuters paid $600 million for SafeSend, and that is the bear case that stings: Luca IQ could be two MBAs wrapping commodity extraction around a tax engine that never escapes pilot while an incumbent bundles equivalent workflow into existing distribution. Luca IQ also has no disclosed revenue, no public engine-scale evidence, and no founder artifact comparable to Armstrong’s wallet; those are precisely my unstated-pattern and manager-mode alarms. The opposing facts win narrowly: Angelo is a real CPA, Luca IQ completed a tax season with customers, and one customer reports 1,100 hours saved rather than a demo-day promise. I would invest, but fewer than ten CPA firms renewing after the 2027 tax season would flip me to Pass.
