### Company case

The renewal analysis could travel. Delivering the full brokerage service abroad would likely require substantial work to establish carrier access and adapt placement, enrollment, and compliance to each market. The memo establishes no near-term route to make those changes with limited effort, so the structural downside gate applies. Ignition reports that it obtained competing quotes for AWM using workforce profiling when claims history was unavailable, and Light Labs kept Gusto for payroll after switching plans. Those cases show flexibility within the current market and suggest a reusable software layer. Required broker work alone does not trigger the cost gate.
