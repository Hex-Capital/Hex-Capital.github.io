# Parasma -- Vinod Khosla Evaluation

The consequence could be enormous: replace energy-intensive silicon with adaptive biological compute and the AI cost curve changes globally. But Parasma has not quantified that consequence. There is no baseline for GPU energy, hardware cost, throughput, or population served, and no claim that 200,000 neurons can produce a 10x—let alone 100x—resource advantage. A dramatic substrate is not yet a dramatic outcome.

The technical evidence is more substantial. Sean Cole connected Cortical Labs’ neurons to Doom, and the hybrid system reportedly outperformed the learning algorithm alone ([Scientific American](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/how-human-neurons-on-a-chip-learned-to-play-doom/), Mar 2026). That proves a real closed-loop experiment, not a presentation. Yet Parasma discloses no accuracy, latency, energy, lifetime, or cost benchmark against GPUs or even Cortical Labs’ software baseline. The central question is not whether neurons can learn. It is whether they provide a 5–10x useful-compute advantage after accounting for stimulation hardware, biological variability, and cell degradation.

The founder has unusually direct founder-market fit for a solo pre-seed company: an AI MSc with distinction, spiking-neural-network research, and the code behind the 200,000-neuron demonstration. The 255 GitHub stars, 29 forks, and coverage in Scientific American and The Guardian show technical curiosity around the work. They do not show belief revision or magnet recruiting. Advertising a founding-scientist role at a $1 million salary and 1%–3% equity is ambition, not evidence that a complementary scientist has joined to own the biological kill risks.

Capital must now attack substrate reliability before product breadth. Parasma already names noise, non-stationarity, limited observability, and degradation under sustained stimulation, but it supplies no cheapest decisive experiment, numerical threshold, cost, or stop condition. A token-prediction demonstration should not precede proof that learning persists reproducibly across cultures and stimulation cycles. Otherwise money will purchase increasingly impressive demos while leaving the existential risk untouched.

The bull case is that this resembles the kind of compute option for which conventional economics are initially unknowable. The OpenAI exception shows that a missing scale model can be tolerated when exponential technical progress and world-scale consequence are already visible. Parasma has neither demonstrated exponential scaling nor separated itself quantitatively from Cortical Labs, The Biological Computing Company, and Intactis Bio. Its public Doom implementation is GPL-3.0, the competitors control funded hardware or cloud platforms, and no proprietary dataset, commercial deployment, or structural barrier is disclosed. The exception does not yet apply.

I pass today, despite wanting the experiment to work. The verifiable flip condition is a third-party-replicated benchmark showing at least a 5x improvement over a named silicon baseline in joules per useful learning update, sustained for 30 days across multiple neuron cultures. That single result would turn biological novelty into a technical discontinuity and justify buying the option before the economics are fully known.
