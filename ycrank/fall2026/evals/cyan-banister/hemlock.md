# Hemlock -- Cyan Banister Evaluation

By 2032, I can imagine a robot installing a lightbulb without crushing it because somebody once recorded what tightening one actually feels like. Video shows the movement; it leaves out the pressure. Hemlock is trying to capture that missing channel through a wrist-worn EMG Bracelet. The path requires reliable muscle-to-force inference, transfer between human demonstrations and robot hands, and useful generalization across people and tasks. The dossier identifies a plausible beginning, but it does not establish those transitions. That distinction matters: I like the future considerably more than I can yet underwrite the route.

Julian Olschwang gives me a reason to keep looking. His Talk To The Hand sign-language glove earned a $25,000 Davidson Fellowship in 2024, before this company, and his UCLA work involved generating military robotics data with GANs. Wearable hand sensing and robot-training data are threads he was already pulling. That is substantially more convincing than a founder discovering physical AI during accelerator applications. There is real independent obsession here, supported by artifacts and institutional recognition. Evidence of recruiting other believers or turning that technical fluency into customer commitments remains limited.

The Bracelet also fits my preference for the concealed machinery beneath a fashionable market. Hemlock could supply a missing training input to multiple manipulation applications rather than compete to make the most impressive robot video. But proprietary hardware is only the beginning of control over that layer. Four claimed wearable patents without identifiers do not establish company-owned protection, and the proposed cross-worker corpus remains hypothetical. Nor does easier collection automatically mean greater worker agency: the dossier identifies glove occlusion and collection constraints, but no quantified gatekeeper rent, payment arrangement, or transfer of control to the people producing the demonstrations.

The reported results deserve attention: 10× improvement in egg pick-and-place and 2.7× in lightbulb installation suggest a testable technical proposition. They do not establish that customers repeatedly spend money or reorganize their work around Hemlock. The dossier supplies no independent lab adoption or repeated collection behavior. My willingness to back pre-revenue robotics at **CosmicBrain AI** rested on a shared combination of founder obsession and a functioning prototype; the material difference was signed industrial proofs of concept. Hemlock has enough evidence to investigate that exception, but not enough to invoke it fully. Revenue itself is not the missing ingredient. An outside party making a consequential commitment is.

The strongest objection cuts deeper than early sales: perhaps Hemlock produces useful demonstrations without becoming a necessary supplier. MANUS already sells collection hardware, while FACT introduces the EgoForce dataset and force-aware pretraining. If buyers can obtain adequate force supervision elsewhere, better sensing may support commissioned projects without creating a durable data advantage. The infrastructure pattern attracts me, but Hemlock has not yet demonstrated the scarce asset that makes it work. Worker EMG paired with egocentric video also raises my specific consent and data-custody questions; silence does not establish a privacy violation, but the architecture remains unresolved. Julian is identified as founder and CEO, so I see no affirmative evidence of the leadership shell game or equal-founder deadlock that would independently stop me.

I would stay close and keep my personal check unwritten today. Hemlock has a credible inventor and a concrete missing signal; it has not yet shown that the signal becomes a business customers depend on. My verifiable flip condition is one independent robotics lab renewing a paid dataset contract after reproducing the gains across previously unseen workers. That would connect the technical artifact to repeat demand and directly test the generalization risk. Until then, this is a compelling experiment with an investable possibility still inside it.
