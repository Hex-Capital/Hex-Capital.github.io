# Raven -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Pass  
**Evidence confidence:** Medium

### Company case

Raven’s handset uses agents to handle tasks across email, calendars, calls, and research. The memo describes a prototype demonstrating flight search and calendar changes. The handset consumes AI capabilities, and the memo identifies no scarce input Raven supplies to AI deployments. The current use case saves time on work users already do themselves. The memo identifies no buyer or use previously excluded by cost and provides no measured cost per successfully completed task. Both AGI paths score zero, which triggers Pass. (The Idea, Market & Traction)

Raven reports more than 1,000 inbound contacts with feedback or requests and says early adopters received test handsets. The memo gives no preorder count or evidence of recurring paid use. The prototype and founder Isa Usmanov’s prior launch of the Liam email and calendar assistant support execution credit. Final hardware specifications and a commercial ship date remain open. (Market & Traction, Founders & Team, Key Risks)

Galaxy S26 can already delegate some multi-step tasks, and Phone Harness brings agent control to existing devices. Saved workflows could make Raven harder to switch away from, but retention is unreported. The projected $99–$199 monthly price leaves unit economics unresolved because the memo gives no handset, cellular, inference, or support costs. (Defensibility, Key Risks, The Idea)

### Countercase

A plausible seed-stage scenario is that small teams without dedicated administrative support could delegate recurring cross-app work they currently skip because staff time is too expensive. Raven’s handset, integrations, and approval steps could make that service easier to use than assembling it on an existing phone. The memo shows the ingredients for that workflow. It does not establish that these buyers were previously excluded, that they would pay for recurring use, or that a comparable agent on an existing phone falls short. The scenario does not clear the AGI fit gate. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a paid pilot at Raven’s projected price show that small teams routinely complete a workflow they previously skipped because of labor cost? Move to Reach out if pilot users keep paying and Raven’s cost per successful completion, including approvals and rework, is low enough to sustain use while a comparable agent on their existing phones falls short.
