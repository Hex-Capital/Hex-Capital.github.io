# Grep AI -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Medium

### Company case

**Path B — Repeatable sales or partner distribution with account expansion.** Grep’s clearest initial buyer is a bank or fintech operations team that repeatedly gathers evidence and reviews flagged applicants. IG reports that automated screening-alert review rose from 10% to 30% and flagged-customer activation fell from 2–3 days to under 10 minutes. That production workflow change is an early signal of fit for the October managed-agent launch. Grep names other financial-services customers, but their deployment geographies, workflows, and payment status are unknown. (The Idea, Market & Traction)

**Growth scenario.** If Grep can sell the same screening workflow to another regulated buyer, it could reuse the evidence-gathering and review steps, configure the buyer’s rules and integrations, then expand into more queues. In a second geography, the test is whether local screening requirements and governance can be handled through configuration and validation with the same core workflow. Shopmonkey’s 64 Deep Research jobs in its first 30 days show early repeat use in an adjacent product. Retention and paid expansion for managed screening remain unverified. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction)

### Countercase

IG’s launch required customer-specific rules, months of shadow testing, governance approval, and ongoing manual oversight for high-risk cases. If each new buyer requires comparable Grep engineering and validation effort, delivery could grow roughly in step with customer count. At the workload level, Grep’s reported 91% model-cost reduction comes from an internal screening benchmark. It leaves open what production exception handling, monitoring, and support cost relative to realized revenue. One rollout establishes the approval burden. The structural Pass gate would apply if subsequent rollouts show proportional delivery effort with no supported way to reduce it. (Defensibility, Key Risks, Key Facts)

### Decisive diligence question

Can an independent regulated buyer in a second geography pay for and sustain production screening using the same core workflow with materially less Grep implementation effort than IG required? Move to **Reach out** if a rollout shows paid recurring use after approval and records sales, setup, validation, support, and per-run costs that support positive contribution.
