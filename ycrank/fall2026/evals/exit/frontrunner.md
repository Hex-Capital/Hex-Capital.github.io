# Frontrunner -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

A strategic sale to a revenue software platform is Frontrunner’s strongest liquidity route. Zoom’s acquisition of Common Room shows that a platform has bought adjacent buyer intelligence and agent capabilities. Frontrunner’s potential value to a buyer is its shared graph, which feeds sales outcomes back into account research, lead routing and outbound. A buyer could use that feedback to improve workflows across its installed base. The memo gives no acquisition price for Common Room or evidence of buyer interest in Frontrunner. (The Idea, Market & Traction)

Foresight Health’s CEO reported seven meetings in 48 hours after onboarding. Frontrunner separately reported a 15% lift in new-business win rate. These signals make a paid production test plausible. The milestone is to show sustained incremental wins or labor savings across paying customers against their existing workflows. The memo leaves the test budget and runway open. It also leaves change-of-control permissions, continued API access and independent liquidity routes unverified or unevidenced. (Market & Traction, The Idea, Key Risks)

Using the rubric’s $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, with 5% ownership diluted to 3% and no follow-ons, the eight-year objective is $2.5 million in cash. That requires about $83.3 million in distributable equity. The sale scenarios assume an eligible holder sells its full stake for cash within eight years and receives 3% of equity proceeds, with no preference reducing its payout. The prices and deductions are illustrative assumptions.

| Scenario | Proceeds bridge | Holder cash |
|---|---|---:|
| Modest sale | $40M enterprise value less $5M in net debt and closing adjustments leaves $35M in distributable equity | $1.05M (2.1×) |
| Strong sale | $100M enterprise value less the same $5M leaves $95M in distributable equity | $2.85M (5.7×) |
| Prolonged private | Cash is reinvested and no eligible share sale or payout occurs within eight years | $0 (0×) |

### Countercase

Zoom’s Common Room integration already overlaps with Frontrunner’s account research and prioritization, while Unify already offers intent-triggered outbound and account research. A platform could use capabilities it already has or build the remaining features. Frontrunner’s own simulations found stale data and incomplete agent runs, which weakens the case that early customer outcomes would persist at scale. The memo establishes neither a structural closing barrier nor a supported sale-price ceiling below the holder’s hurdle. The Pass gate remains untriggered. (Market & Traction, Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can Frontrunner’s feedback loop produce enough incremental value over a plausible buyer’s existing workflow to justify a price that leaves at least $83.3 million in distributable equity? Repeatable gains in paid deployments and a buyer-specific comparison of acquisition cost with internal build cost and expected gains would move the action to Reach out if they support that price. If that evidence puts the strongest credible route materially below the hurdle, the action would move to Pass.
