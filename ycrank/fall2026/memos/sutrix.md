## The Idea

- **Problem:** Hospital researchers spend weeks extracting disconnected EHR, REDCap, and wearable records, reviewing charts manually, and reconciling spreadsheets before studies can begin ([YC, accessed Oct 27, 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/sutrix)).
- **Approach:** Sutrix links source records, applies study definitions, records reconciliation decisions, and routes unresolved cases to staff, retaining provenance alongside the prepared dataset ([Sutrix](https://www.sutrix.ai/)).
- **Differentiation:** Against manual notebooks and spreadsheets, Sutrix records approved joins, mappings, and exclusions; against chart-abstraction vendors such as Layer Health, its positioning centers on study-specific preparation workflows ([Sutrix solution](https://www.sutrix.ai/solutions/multi-source-clinical-data-reconciliation), [Layer Health](https://www.layerhealth.com/press-and-articles/layer-health-raises-series-a)).
- **Business Model:** Paid engagements have individually agreed scope, pricing, timelines, and deliverables; numerical prices and software subscription tiers: No public data found ([Sutrix terms](https://www.sutrix.ai/terms)).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.

## Key Risks

- **Production integration:** [Inferred]: Hospital deployment can require additional integration work because current FHIR connections are EHR sandboxes, while institution-cloud deployment is a scoped option; file uploads provide an alternative intake route ([Sutrix](https://www.sutrix.ai/)).
- **Scientific correctness:** Sutrix requires customers to verify results before publication, regulatory submission, or clinical use, while its workflow includes independent validation and approval gates ([terms](https://www.sutrix.ai/terms), [team page](https://www.sutrix.ai/who-we-are)).
