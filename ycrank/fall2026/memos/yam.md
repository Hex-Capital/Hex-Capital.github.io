## The Idea

- **Problem:** Yam targets women who read fanfiction, play dating sims, or watch romance shows and want to participate in the story and change its outcome ([Yam About](https://www.hiyam.ai/about), [Launch YC, Nov 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Wsv-yam-ai-powered-romance-simulation-games-for-women)).
- **Approach:** A player describes a world and a role, then Yam generates a cast with persistent memories and relationships and advances the story in response to player choices ([Launch YC, Nov 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Wsv-yam-ai-powered-romance-simulation-games-for-women)).
- **Differentiation:** Yam generates romance plots around an interacting cast, CHAI centers on conversations with user-created characters, and Dorian gives creators a visual editor for authoring branching stories ([Yam FAQ](https://www.hiyam.ai/faq), [CHAI Company Facts](https://www.chai-research.com/company-facts/), [Dorian](https://dorian.live/news)).
- **Business Model:** Free play includes 100 daily moves for new accounts, with optional virtual currency packs at $0.99–$19.99 and subscriptions at $9.99 or $19.99 per month ([Yam Pricing, Nov 2026](https://www.hiyam.ai/pricing)).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** Creators publish games through shareable links and earn virtual currency as players progress, while referrals unlock free play perks ([Yam FAQ, Nov 2026](https://www.hiyam.ai/faq)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Yam’s public creator catalog spans 112 fandoms, and its most-played listing shows 2.4 million plays ([Yam Explore, Nov 17, 2026](https://www.hiyam.ai/)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Creator publishing could produce a network effect if popular games attract players who then publish or share more games, but Yam has disclosed the reward mechanics without publishing how many players become creators ([Yam FAQ](https://www.hiyam.ai/faq)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** [Inferred]: AI roleplay providers can add romance scenarios using commercially available models and existing tools for persistent worlds and characters ([Yam FAQ](https://www.hiyam.ai/faq), [Latitude Voyage press kit](https://voyage.io/press)).

## Key Risks

- **Third-party IP:** Rights-holder requests can remove fan games from Yam’s catalog, and Yam maintains a takedown process and has announced plans to develop original IP ([Yam Terms](https://www.hiyam.ai/terms), [Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Wsv-yam-ai-powered-romance-simulation-games-for-women)).
- **Model dependence:** [Inferred]: Vendor pricing or behavior changes can affect per-turn costs or character consistency because Yam sends inputs to Anthropic, Google, and OpenAI ([Yam FAQ](https://www.hiyam.ai/faq)).
- **Generated-content safety:** Yam says generated dialogue can be objectionable despite safeguards and uses adult eligibility checks, content filters, and player reporting ([Yam Terms](https://www.hiyam.ai/terms), [Yam Safety](https://www.hiyam.ai/safety)).
- **Name collision:** [Inferred]: App-store searches can conflate Yamstudio’s game with Ben Savadogo’s education app titled *YAM* ([Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/yam-dating-sim-games/id6826968526), [AppBrain](https://www.appbrain.com/appstore/yam/ios-6788182918)).
