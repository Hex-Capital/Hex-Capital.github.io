## The Idea

- **Problem:** Data-center developers face constrained electricity access, with grid bottlenecks potentially delaying approximately 20% of planned global capacity through 2030, while alternatives include grid-connected infrastructure and diesel or gas generators ([IEA, 2025](https://www.iea.org/reports/energy-and-ai/ai-and-energy-security); [Grand View Research, 2026](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/data-center-generators-market)).
- **Approach:** A condenser recovers combustion water from turbine exhaust, exhaust heat converts recovered water into steam, and steam injection increases turbine mass flow while moderating combustion temperatures, with recovered water also intended for cooling ([Viraj technology](https://www.viraj-aero.com/technology); [GENA](https://www.viraj-aero.com/service/gena)).
- **Differentiation:** Viraj combines steam injection and recovered cooling water, whereas Boom’s Superpower advertises waterless generation and Turbotech uses exhaust-heat recuperation for aircraft propulsion ([GENA](https://www.viraj-aero.com/service/gena); [Boom, December 2025](https://boomsupersonic.com/press-release/boom-supersonic-to-power-ai-data-centers); [Innovacom, June 2025](https://reindustrialisation.iledefrance.fr/presse/turbotechannonce-une-levee-de-fonds-de-85-millions-deuros-pour-accelerer-son-developpement-industriel/)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Equipment sales and turbine-upgrade contracts are potential monetization paths given the standalone GENA and retrofit offerings; public pricing: No public data found ([company products](https://www.viraj-aero.com/service/gena)).
- **TAM/SAM:** The broader data-center generator market is estimated at $9.7 billion in 2026, growing at 6.3% CAGR through 2033, but this includes equipment outside Viraj’s segment and does not establish its SAM ([Grand View Research, 2026](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/data-center-generators-market)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** The website solicits direct sales and partnership conversations and offers steam-injection upgrades to existing turbine-genset builders ([contact page](https://www.viraj-aero.com/contact/contact); [upgrade offering](https://www.viraj-aero.com/service/water-recovery-system)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Viraj identifies its onboard condenser as the subject of a company patent, although a patent publication number and independently verified claim scope were not found ([company i-Lab announcement, September 2026](https://www.viraj-aero.com/news-insights/i-lab-2026-france-2030-backing-for-viraj-aeros-steam-injection-technology)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Operating data and customer-specific integration knowledge could create switching costs, but public evidence of deployed GENA systems is unavailable.
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** [Inferred]: Turbine manufacturers could pursue alternative water-recovery architectures because steam injection itself already has decades of terrestrial use, although copying Viraj’s particular implementation would depend on engineering requirements and patent scope ([company funding announcement, August 2026](https://www.viraj-aero.com/news-insights/viraj-aero-raised-4-5meu-in-seed-funding)).

## Key Risks

- **Technical feasibility:** [Inferred]: Sustaining closed-loop water recovery during continuous hot-site operation requires validation beyond published GENA development specifications of 45% electrical efficiency and 2 ppm NOx ([GENA](https://www.viraj-aero.com/service/gena)).
- **Aviation certification:** Market entry depends on certification, with the company targeting a 2027 demonstrator flight and 2029 EYJA commercialization ([Viraj, August 2026](https://www.viraj-aero.com/news-insights/viraj-aero-raised-4-5meu-in-seed-funding)).
- **Customer-segment transfer:** [Inferred]: The disclosed €13 million aviation LOIs do not establish demand for data-center gensets, which require separate customer qualification ([AD’OCC, March 2026](https://www.agence-adocc.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/DP_OccitanieInvest_2026.pdf)).
- **Performance comparability:** YC advertises up to 40% additional power while the technology page states up to 30%, without explaining the different baselines ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/viraj-aero); [company technology](https://www.viraj-aero.com/technology)).
