# Prodigy Research -- Elad Gil Evaluation

The market map is simple: finance-specialized models can now move from analysis into live execution, proprietary trading capital is the initial buyer, and the controlling variable is whether Prodigy Research can preserve risk-adjusted alpha as capital and market impact increase. Everything else—including benchmark wins—is secondary.

Prodigy reports a 108% delta-neutral return over two months with no down week. That is an unusually strong pre-seed result, but it does not yet test the controlling variable. There is no audited return series, volatility, drawdown, capital base, transaction-cost attribution, or capacity estimate. A strategy can be spectacular at small scale and disappear when exposed to costs, crowding, or another $20 million. Prodigy therefore presents evidence of possible alpha, not evidence that the market mechanism works at venture scale.

The technology is more substantive than a thin AI trading interface. A proprietary finance model, agent harness, and live execution loop together perform the workflow rather than merely summarize research. Michael Wang reportedly combines Gemini 3.1 Pro training experience with Jane Street trading, while Yuhua Wang built AI infrastructure at Apple and Salesforce. This is a credible team for training and operating the system. Better models should improve the workflow if Prodigy retains execution data and outcome feedback, although the claimed data advantage remains unproven because two months of trading history may be cheap for a well-capitalized competitor to reproduce.

The strongest bull case is a talent-and-technology override: rare model-training ability and quantitative-market experience have produced live results before the company has polished its business model. The problem is that the market is already crystallizing. EquiLibre reports systems trading billions of dollars daily, Numerai has $550 million in AUM, and Standard Signal also operates an end-to-end AI hedge fund. Prodigy has not identified a structural opening, differentiated data source, or economic discontinuity explaining why its model compounds faster than those better-capitalized systems.

There is also no demonstrated customer pull because the apparent user is Prodigy’s own capital. That can become an excellent business, but it leaves venture value capture unresolved: proprietary returns, fund-management fees, and software revenue have different capacity limits, margins, and distribution paths. No pricing, fund structure, commercial deployment, or adjacent product channel is disclosed. The founders’ credentials are strong, but the dossier documents neither prior startup learning nor the costly commitment that would support a founder-motivation premium.

I pass today. The single flip condition is independent administrator verification of 12 consecutive months of net returns on at least $25 million of deployed capital with maximum drawdown below 10%. That would show that the reported alpha survives time, costs, and scale—the one variable capable of turning this from an impressive technical result into a large financial company.
