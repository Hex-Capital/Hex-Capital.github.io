## The Idea

- **Problem:** Law firms, healthcare clinics, and real-estate companies have administrative and operational workflows that remain uneconomical to staff manually, while off-the-shelf software often requires users to operate another tool rather than complete the work ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/withasync), Jul 2026; [Async](https://www.withasync.com/), Jul 2026).
- **Approach:** Async audits a customer’s workflows and technology stack, scopes one or two workflows, and deploys agents using company memory, approval logic, scheduled tasks, and integrations with documents, email, CRM, calendars, and databases ([Async](https://www.withasync.com/our-process), Jul 2026).
- **Differentiation:** [Inferred]: Unlike Relevance AI and Gumloop’s customer-built agent platforms or Glean’s enterprise context layer, Async supplies hands-on workflow auditing, deployment, maintenance, and outcome ownership inside each customer’s existing systems ([Async](https://www.withasync.com/our-process), Jul 2026; [Relevance AI](https://techcrunch.com/2025/05/06/relevance-ai-raises-24m-series-b-to-help-anyone-build-teams-of-ai-agents/), May 2025; [Gumloop](https://www.gumloop.com/blog/series-b), Mar 2026).
- **Business Model:** Async prices one or two initial workflows against the outcome rather than seats or tokens, with each expansion covered by a new agreement and no public price figure ([Async](https://www.withasync.com/our-process), Jul 2026).
- **TAM/SAM:** The global AI-agent market was $7.6B in 2025 and is forecast to grow 49.6% annually through 2033, while the U.S. segment generated $2.23B in 2025 and is forecast to grow 46.9% annually ([Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/ai-agents-market-report), 2026; [U.S. outlook](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/horizon/outlook/ai-agents-market/united-states), 2026).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Async uses a direct consultative process that begins with a contact form and workflow audit, proceeds to a scoped deployment, and seeks follow-on agreements after the first deployment produces an agreed outcome ([Async](https://www.withasync.com/contact), Jul 2026; [process](https://www.withasync.com/our-process), Jul 2026).

## Key Risks

- **Platform substitution:** Async’s product includes Claude and other external models, while Anthropic now sells a direct small-business package covering overlapping workflows; Async’s mitigation is customer-specific auditing and deployment ([Async](https://www.withasync.com/), Jul 2026; [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-for-small-business), May 2026).
- **Compliance-status inconsistency:** Async’s homepage states SOC 2 Type I certification, while its security page says certification is in progress; the site also states data isolation, encryption, and zero-data-retention controls ([homepage](https://www.withasync.com/), Jul 2026; [security](https://www.withasync.com/security), Jul 2026).
- **Delivery economics:** [Inferred]: Auditing, custom deployment, ongoing maintenance, and outcome pricing can create customer-specific labor and model costs that may not decline proportionally as deployments expand ([Async process](https://www.withasync.com/our-process), Jul 2026).
- **Product-pivot continuity:** The founders previously built nonprofit fundraising agents, launched Skope as AI billing software, repositioned Skope around legal agents, and now market Async across multiple service industries ([Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=44973758), 2025; [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/skope-2), 2025; [YC legal-agent announcement](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/y-combinator_skope-builds-ai-agents-for-local-law-firms-activity-7419770837463060480-hjlw), 2026; [Async](https://www.withasync.com/), 2026).
- **Name disambiguation:** Unrelated companies use “Async,” including an AI-video platform at async.com, creating search and diligence ambiguity mitigated by the company’s withasync.com domain and YC profile ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/async-platform1), Jul 2026; [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/withasync), Jul 2026).
