# Particularity -- Elad Gil Evaluation

Particularity is betting that browser agents let consumer-protection agencies buy investigation capacity that previously required additional staff. The controlling variable is investigator hours per legally actionable case, including human review and false positives. More website scans matter only if that number falls enough to justify an agency purchasing the product. The dossier describes how investigations could be automated, but supplies no baseline, measured improvement, or agency purchasing threshold. I can formulate the market hypothesis; I cannot yet underwrite it.

The most substantive evidence is the product’s described sequence: navigate commercial websites, test checkout and cancellation flows, capture timestamped and hashed records, and map findings to statutory elements ([October 2026 launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Vq1-particularity-ai-investigators-for-government-regulators)). Those are actions across an investigation, giving Particularity more substance than a document summarizer. Better browser models could improve task completion while the evidence record remains useful. But hashing establishes record integrity, not legal sufficiency. No deployed case-management integration, agency feedback loop, or persistent workflow ownership is demonstrated. That earns partial architectural credit, without establishing durability.

The market boundary matters more than the adjacent vendors’ financing. PerformLine’s reported revenue exceeded $10 million ARR in December 2021, but its enterprise compliance buyers do not establish regulator demand. UNCTAD’s 2025 account of agency mystery-shopping and recording tools identifies a concrete substitute. Particularity must improve the economics of that existing workflow enough to displace internal tools or investigator time. The inferred availability of better agent frameworks supplies a plausible why-now, but no quantified economic change. Its October 15 launch and roundup coverage establish emergence, not product-market pull; absent revenue and usage receive the specified defaults.

The strongest bull case is the combination of Kumaravel’s Senate Banking Committee consumer-protection experience and Bannon’s AWS security and compliance engineering. They have relevant context for connecting technical observations to an investigator’s actual work. A successful first deployment could also create distribution for adjacent investigation products within the same agency. That fits my pattern of funding software around institutional transitions. However, distribution reuse remains hypothetical, and these biographies document neither costly founder commitment nor prior startup learning. The exception for rare frontier research talent does not apply on this record, and there is no documented incumbent withdrawal creating a strategic vacuum. Neither exception resolves the missing market evidence.

I would pass with my own money today. Particularity has a more concrete product mechanism than commercial mechanism. I would not label it a disposable wrapper, nor infer fundraising or proprietary-data red flags the dossier does not document. The unresolved issue is whether autonomous investigation creates economically useful enforcement capacity after review. My flip condition is one agency renewing a paid deployment after documenting at least a 50% reduction in investigator hours per counsel-accepted case, with positive contribution margin for Particularity. That single deployment would test the controlling belief and establish a reason for another agency to buy.
