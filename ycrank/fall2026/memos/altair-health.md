## The Idea

- **Problem:** Independent practices reconcile eligibility, authorizations, claims, and denials across separate systems using spreadsheets and phone calls, and missed payer requirements can delay or prevent reimbursement ([Altair](https://altair-health.com/), [Altair About](https://altair-health.com/about)).
- **Approach:** Altair connects to the practice’s EHR and clearinghouse, uses a theorem prover to check proposed claim actions against formalized payer policies, and routes exceptions to in-house billers ([Altair](https://altair-health.com/), [Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/altair-health)).
- **Differentiation:** Altair takes over billing on the practice’s existing systems with payer-policy checks and human exception handling, while Tebra bundles billing with its EHR, Candid Health offers configurable rules and agents for enterprise providers, and Adonis automates workflows for provider groups and health systems ([Altair](https://altair-health.com/), [Tebra](https://www.tebra.com/press-release/private-practice-platform-tebra-secures-250m-to-accelerate-ai-innovation), [Sixth Street](https://sixthstreet.com/newsroom/candid-health-raises-120m-led-by-sixth-street-growth-to-fuel-autonomous-revenue-cycle-management-in-healthcare), [Adonis](https://adonis.io/resources/adonis-raises-40m-series-c-to-equip-healthcare-providers-with-ai-driven-revenue-cycle-operations)).
- **Business Model:** Capterra lists a prior authorization plan starting at $395 annually with a free trial and gives conflicting billing units, while SourceForge lists a 5% starting rate with no stated fee basis or plan ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/10048806/Altair-Health/), [SourceForge](https://sourceforge.net/software/product/Altair-Health/), Nov 2026).
- **TAM/SAM:** Grand View Research estimates the broader U.S. medical billing market it tracks at $7.73 billion in 2026 and projects 12.56% CAGR through 2033 across in-house and outsourced billing for hospitals and physician offices ([Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/us-medical-billing-outsourcing-market), Jan 2026).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Altair solicits demos and targets new practices and practices switching billers through its website, and its previously indexed free behavioral-health EHR offer now redirects to the billing homepage ([Altair](https://altair-health.com/), [New Practice](https://altair-health.com/docs/new-practice), [Switch Billing](https://altair-health.com/docs/switch), [Altair EHR](https://altair-health.com/ehr), Nov 2026).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Altair reports tracking policies across 3,400+ payers and retaining payer-specific resolution history for reuse on subsequent claims ([Altair](https://altair-health.com/), Nov 2026).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Accumulated payer outcomes could improve denial and payment forecasts if patterns transfer across practices, with no public validation of that improvement found ([Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/altair-health)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** Tebra has announced investment in AI billing and payments within its existing practice platform, and Candid Health already markets a configurable rules engine and AI agents for billing ([Tebra](https://www.tebra.com/press-release/private-practice-platform-tebra-secures-250m-to-accelerate-ai-innovation), [Sixth Street](https://sixthstreet.com/newsroom/candid-health-raises-120m-led-by-sixth-street-growth-to-fuel-autonomous-revenue-cycle-management-in-healthcare)).

## Key Risks

- **Payer-policy fidelity:** [Inferred]: An outdated or incomplete encoded rule can let a claim action pass despite a payer requirement, and Altair says it tracks published policy updates and routes failed checks to billers ([Altair](https://altair-health.com/), [Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/altair-health)).
- **Manual exception capacity:** [Inferred]: Exception-heavy claim volumes can increase biller workload and delay follow-up because Altair assigns failed checks to in-house staff ([Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/altair-health)).
- **Patient-data exposure:** EHR and claims access brings protected health information into the workflow, and Altair reports signing business associate agreements and using access controls, logging, and encryption ([Altair](https://altair-health.com/), [S7 Lab Trust Center](https://trust.altair-health.com/)).
- **Name collision:** A New Jersey neurosurgery and spine-care provider uses the same name at `altairhealth.com`, creating potential confusion in searches and referrals ([New Jersey provider’s LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/brain-spine-care), [YC company profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/altair-health)).
