# The Agentic Data Co. -- Sam Lessin Evaluation

The Agentic Data Co. has built a real asset, but I cannot yet underwrite the investment. Post-money valuation: undisclosed. Competing term sheets: undisclosed. What this check specifically proves: undisclosed. None of that establishes an auction or a bad capital plan. It does mean I cannot establish the two things I care about most: whether I am buying uncertainty cheaply and whether my money resolves a particular uncertainty.

The interesting asset is the machinery for collecting natural conversation. Open Yap contains 1,000.245 hours from 1,602 conversations and 239 speakers, with acquaintances recording separate speaker tracks. The commercial question is whether that collection system gives the founders access buyers cannot readily reproduce. The free corpus can demonstrate quality and attract demand, but it also makes the released material available to others. The 3,022 monthly sample downloads establish interest in a free resource. A founder’s acknowledgment of existing customers is encouraging, but without knowing what they bought or why, I cannot connect proprietary access to a purchase.

The strongest objection to passing is that the team has already crossed an important technical boundary. Its October 2026 research reports training a turn-taking model on 1,984 hours across 2,500 conversations, placing first of 24 entries for end-of-turn prediction and second of 23 for interruption prediction. Christian’s prior Postbuddy venture and reported sale add operating evidence beyond a résumé; Matias brings growth and operations experience from Female Invest. There is a plausible translation layer here between recruiting ordinary people and supplying demanding speech-model developers. That fits my unstated preference for businesses connecting systems that struggle to transact directly.

But the leaderboard result measures technical utility, while the investment depends on capturing its economic value. The research does not establish performance in noisy or mixed-channel environments, and the supplied transcripts are not human-verified. David AI and Appen already offer overlapping collection services. The founders could still win through better specifications, contributor relationships, or delivery economics, but those advantages need receipts. My exceptions to the usual price and AI gates rely on demonstrated conversion, retention, cash economics, or unusually strong external validation. This dossier supplies a credible technical result without the commercial evidence needed for that override. Nor does it establish the red flags of an auctioned round, failed conversion, or compulsory serial financing; those remain unknown.

Delete the next financing from the spreadsheet. Private licenses could finance further collection, but there are no disclosed prices, collection costs, margins, burn, or runway to show that loop works. Two founders do not establish low operating costs when the product requires participant recruitment and human review. I would track cash contribution per accepted, licensed hour. Downloads and benchmark rankings cannot tell me whether another collection cycle pays for itself.

I pass today. My verifiable condition for reopening is a signed private-data contract whose committed gross profit covers the next collection cycle without another equity raise. That would establish a functioning commercial loop and materially strengthen the case for an exception. Until then, The Agentic Data Co. has demonstrated that it can produce useful speech data, while the scarce access, financing experiment, and self-funding business remain unproven.
