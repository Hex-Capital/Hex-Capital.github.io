## The Idea

- **Problem:** Back-office teams in accounting, tax, insurance, healthcare, compliance, and data privacy rely on manual work or selector-based RPA inside legacy software and third-party portals where pop-ups, focus changes, and interface updates can disrupt workflows ([Y Combinator, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/coasty)).
- **Approach:** Coasty reads screenshots, returns grounded coordinates and structured actions, maintains state across multi-step sessions, verifies outcomes, attempts mid-task recovery, and records timestamped replayable trails ([Coasty product page, Jul 2026](https://coasty.ai/)).
- **Differentiation:** Coasty contrasts its screen-reading and recovery framework with selector-based RPA and reports an 82.81% OSWorld Verified score versus 62.9% for Claude, 69.9% for GPT agents, and 67.1% for UiPath in its Product Hunt launch materials ([Y Combinator, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/coasty); [Hunted.Space/Product Hunt data, Mar 2026](https://www.hunted.space/dashboard/coasty-2/launches/coasty-2)).
- **Business Model:** Prepaid consumption pricing ranges from $0.03 per grounding request and $0.04–$0.10 per session action to $0.05–$0.09 per running VM-hour, while BYOK model calls incur zero Coasty platform credits ([Coasty pricing, Jul 2026](https://coasty.ai/)).
- **TAM/SAM:** The computer-using-agent market was estimated at $0.5 billion in 2025 and projected to reach $54.8 billion by 2034 at a 60.0% CAGR ([Market Intelo, Jul 2026](https://marketintelo.com/report/computer-using-agents-market)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** The founders request workflow submissions and offer to build proofs of concept on customers’ existing software, targeting operators and executives in accounting, tax, insurance, healthcare, compliance, and data privacy ([Y Combinator launch page, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/coasty)).

## Key Risks

- **Production reliability:** Coasty’s 82.81% OSWorld score leaves benchmark tasks incomplete, while its target workflows include payments, patient records, and regulatory filings where incorrect actions can create operational consequences; the product mitigations described are output verification, recovery, escalation, and replayable audit trails ([Y Combinator, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/coasty)).
- **Benchmark-to-workflow transfer:** OSWorld measures open-ended computer tasks, but Coasty’s cited healthcare and payer-portal launch demonstration used mock portals and data, leaving public evidence for regulated production workflows limited ([Y Combinator, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/coasty)).
- **Sensitive-data handling:** Coasty’s terms permit hosting, processing, analyzing, and using customer content and session data through Coasty’s AI and infrastructure providers, which may affect adoption in regulated workflows ([Coasty Terms, Jun 2026](https://coasty.ai/terms)).
- **Interface and model competition:** UiPath generated $1.43 billion in FY2025 revenue and is directing R&D toward AI, while Adept has $415 million in disclosed funding for agent technology, models, data, and infrastructure ([UiPath FY2025 filing](https://ir.uipath.com/financials/sec-filings/content/0001734722-25-000007/path-20250131.htm); [Forbes, Apr 2024](https://www.forbes.com/companies/adept/)).
- **Product-pivot comparability:** The March 2026 launch emphasized isolated “AI employee” VMs, 10+ departments, and consumer/SMB use cases, whereas the July 2026 YC launch emphasizes developer APIs and legacy back-office workflows, so earlier waitlist and Product Hunt metrics are not confirmed as current-product usage ([Hunted.Space/Product Hunt data, Mar 2026](https://www.hunted.space/dashboard/coasty-2/launches/coasty-2); [Y Combinator, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/coasty)).
