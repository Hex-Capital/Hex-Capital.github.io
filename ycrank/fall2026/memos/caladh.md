## The Idea

- **Problem:** Port, offshore energy and subsea infrastructure operators face drone incursions that Caladh says existing detection systems may flag without stopping, while physical barriers such as nets are difficult to scale ([Caladh](https://caladh.com/), [U.S. Coast Guard market-research notice, Jul 2026](https://samclerk.com/opportunity/dept-of-homeland-security/70Z02326RFI0005)).
- **Approach:** Eclipse is designed to disrupt hostile maritime drones electronically, while Cloak is designed to mask subsea assets from drone-mounted sensors ([Caladh](https://caladh.com/#products)).
- **Differentiation:** Caladh offers maritime drone disruption and asset concealment, while Ultra Maritime’s Sea Spear detects and classifies underwater vehicles, Triton Depth and Optics11 monitor subsea activity, and D-Fend’s maritime product targets aerial drones ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Wny-caladh-anti-drone-maritime-defense), [Ultra Maritime](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/ultra-maritime-successfully-demonstrates-counter-unmanned-undersea-vehicle-capability-at-us-navy-lanternfish-2026-exercise-302833379.html), [EIFO](https://via.ritzau.dk/pressemeddelelse/15041762/triton-depth-rejser-75-mio-kr-til-at-styrke-europas-sikkerhed-under-vandet?publisherId=13561323), [Optics11](https://optics11.com/pressrelease/as-europe-rebuilds-security-infrastructure-structural-investors-commit-another-e50-million-to-optics11-surpassing-e100-million-in-just-15-months/), [D-Fend Solutions](https://d-fendsolutions.com/enforceair2-maritime-version/)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Site-specific hardware contracts are a possible monetization path given Caladh’s offer of site assessments and pilots, with no public pricing found ([Caladh](https://caladh.com/)).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment, with the broader global underwater security systems market estimated at $741M in 2025 and projected to grow at 5.5% annually through 2032 ([Global Info Research, 2026](https://www.marketresearch.com/GlobalInfoResearch-v4117/Global-Underwater-Security-System-Company-45403014/)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Caladh solicits introductions to port, energy and subsea security leads and U.S. naval operators, and invites government and industry buyers to request briefings, assessments or pilots ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Wny-caladh-anti-drone-maritime-defense), [Caladh](https://caladh.com/)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** The documented technical capability is Kita’s underwater acoustics research and reported leadership of DARPA Sync work, with no public evidence of exclusive Caladh IP ([MIT–WHOI](https://mit.whoi.edu/academics/fields/aope/aope-recent-dissertations-and-theses/advances-in-passive-acoustic-detection-localization-and-tracking-applied-to-unmanned-underwater-vehicles/), [Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Wny-caladh-anti-drone-maritime-defense)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Completed pilots could build site-validation records and customer integrations that take time to replicate, with Caladh publicly soliciting pilot requests ([Caladh](https://caladh.com/)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** [Inferred]: The detection layer faces substitution from existing sonar suppliers, including Ultra Maritime, which demonstrated underwater drone detection and classification in a U.S. Navy port-protection exercise ([Ultra Maritime, Jul 2026](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/ultra-maritime-successfully-demonstrates-counter-unmanned-undersea-vehicle-capability-at-us-navy-lanternfish-2026-exercise-302833379.html)).
