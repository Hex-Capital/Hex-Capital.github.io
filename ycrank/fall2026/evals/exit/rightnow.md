# RightNow -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

The strongest route is a strategic acquisition by a GPU vendor. RightNow has documented a kernel-tuning workflow and launched a hosted API that combines kernel optimization with serving settings. RightNow reports kernel-tool use at NVIDIA and AMD. A GPU vendor could have a reason to buy if owning the serving stack and team would materially improve inference performance on its hardware. The reported use establishes a diligence lead. Buyer savings and acquisition interest remain unverified. (Defensibility, Market & Traction)

A plausible seed-stage milestone is repeatable cost and latency gains on paying production workloads. The live API and workload benchmarking offer provide a way to run those tests. The memo gives no realized revenue, funded production test, or budget for the GPU capacity needed to demonstrate the gains at scale. Transfer rights and holder eligibility remain unverified, and the memo identifies no independent route to cash. (The Idea, Key Facts, Key Risks)

The benchmark is a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, with 5% ownership diluted to 3%, no follow-ons, and a $2.5 million gross cash objective within eight years. That requires about $83.3 million in distributable equity value. Both sale scenarios assume a full cash acquisition within eight years, an eligible holder selling the entire stake, and pro rata payment at closing with no further preference deductions or holdbacks. The prices and deductions are illustrative assumptions.

- **Modest sale.** $30 million enterprise value less $5 million in net debt and deal costs leaves $25 million in distributable equity. The holder receives $750,000, or 1.5×.
- **Strong sale.** $100 million enterprise value less $10 million in net debt and deal costs leaves $90 million in distributable equity. The holder receives $2.7 million, or 5.4×. At 2% ownership, proceeds fall to $1.8 million.
- **Prolonged private.** Continued operation with cash reinvested and no eligible share purchase yields zero realized proceeds. The memo supplies no pricing evidence for either sale scenario and no evidence of an available private payout. (Market & Traction, Key Facts)

### Countercase

A buyer could reproduce enough of the benefit in-house. AutoKernel is Apache 2.0 licensed, Together already uses agents to tune inference, and RightNow’s gains may vary with prompts, cache reuse, and concurrency. That weakens the case for paying a premium to own the stack. The memo establishes neither a structural closing block nor a supported sale-price ceiling below the holder’s hurdle, so the Pass gate does not trigger. (Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Do RightNow’s repeatable savings on paying production workloads give a GPU vendor enough reason to buy the stack at a price that leaves at least $83.3 million in distributable equity? Matched benchmarks against the vendor’s own stack, paired with buyer-side economics that support that price after integration costs, would move the action to Reach out.
