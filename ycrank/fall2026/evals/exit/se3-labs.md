# SE3 Labs -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

The strongest path is a strategic acquisition by a robot-model developer. Owning SE3’s standardized physical workcells and correction-data workflow could lower the cost of improving policies for real deployments. SE3 reports access to industrial, processing, and hospitality sites, with partner-site training sandboxes still planned. The memo records 500 released teleoperation episodes across 10 tasks and 1,596 monthly dataset downloads. That release shows SE3 can produce benchmark data. Paid demand, buyer savings, and acquisition pricing remain unverified. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Facts)

The next milestone is a paid repeat evaluation that shows lower cost to reach a fixed task-success rate on held-out workflows at a second site. The benchmark release supports an initial test. On-site hardware and staffing needs remain unquantified, and total financing is unverified. The workcell and evaluation operation is a plausible acquisition asset. Continued site access and authorized reuse of recordings after a sale depend on unverified contract terms. The memo supplies no evidence of an independent liquidity route. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

The benchmark assumes a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, ownership diluted from 5% to 3%, no follow-ons, and a $2.5 million gross cash objective within eight years. That requires about $83.3 million in distributable equity. The sale scenarios assume an all-cash close within eight years, an eligible holder selling the entire stake, and pro rata distribution after debt and deal adjustments with no senior equity claims. Sale prices and deductions are illustrative assumptions.

| Scenario | Distributable equity | Gross cash to holder |
|---|---:|---:|
| Modest sale | $45M enterprise value less $5M of debt and deal adjustments = $40M | $1.2M (2.4×) |
| Strong sale | $110M enterprise value less $10M of debt and deal adjustments = $100M | $3.0M (6.0×) |
| Prolonged private operation | Cash reinvested, with no payout or share purchase within eight years | $0 (0×) |

In the strong sale, selling only half the stake would yield $1.5 million. The memo gives no basis for estimating a realizable sale price, so proceeds receive the absent-evidence default.

### Countercase

A model developer could build physical benchmarks with RoboDojo or contract with Enact for rollouts and failure-recovery data. SE3’s reported zero full-task completions across 100 zero-shot Dream-1 episodes identifies a testing problem. Whether SE3’s corrections improve performance on new workflows is still open. The substitutes weaken the case for buying SE3, but the evidence establishes neither a structural closing block nor a ceiling on holder proceeds. The Pass gate is unmet. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)
