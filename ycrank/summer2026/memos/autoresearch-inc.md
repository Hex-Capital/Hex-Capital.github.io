## The Idea

- **Problem:** Application-layer AI teams need task-specific model performance but must otherwise coordinate specialized data generation, training, experimentation, and evaluation work themselves ([Peak XV](https://surge.peakxv.com/companies/hiloop)).
- **Approach:** Customers supply a task, baseline model or agent, and evaluation; Autoresearch searches across data, post-training, prompts, tools, and systems using persistent memory, experiment lineage, compute orchestration, and statistical verification ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/autoresearch-inc)).
- **Differentiation:** Against [Karpathy’s single-GPU research loop](https://github.com/karpathy/autoresearch) and [Predibase’s fine-tuning/serving platform](https://www.w4nderlu.st/projects/predibase), Autoresearch offers execution of broader research campaigns, hosted or inside customer infrastructure ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/autoresearch-inc)).
- **Business Model:** Self-serve paid plans are billed at signup, while enterprise customers use separate master services agreements; public prices and revenue figures: No public data found ([Terms, October 23, 2026](https://autoresearch.inc/terms/)).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** Founder-led campaign engagements originate through early-access email outreach and a YC launch inviting teams to submit their baseline and evaluation ([YC launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SBN-hiloop-we-run-thousands-of-experiments-to-improve-your-models)).

## Key Risks

- **Evaluation generalization:** [Inferred]: Gains against a supplied evaluation may fail to transfer to production workloads; published evidence concerns a hardware-sensitive training benchmark, with repeated interleaved testing addressing measurement noise ([company GitHub](https://github.com/hiloopai/search-is-enough)).
- **Confidential-data handling:** Self-serve inputs and outputs may train company models by default, with prospective opt-out available; enterprise customer data is excluded by default ([Privacy Policy, October 23, 2026](https://autoresearch.inc/privacy/)).
- **Customer-code isolation:** [Inferred]: Executing customer code alongside sensitive datasets creates isolation requirements; the company documents sandbox pods and scoped credentials while listing SOC 2 as in progress ([Security, October 23, 2026](https://autoresearch.inc/security/)).
- **Name/source confusion:** [Inferred]: The company’s name overlaps Karpathy’s autoresearch project, creating attribution risk; its earlier hiloop launch describes the same research service, without establishing a separate product pivot ([YC launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SBN-hiloop-we-run-thousands-of-experiments-to-improve-your-models), [Karpathy GitHub](https://github.com/karpathy/autoresearch)).
