## The Idea

- **Problem:** Solar, storage, charging, and data-center developers submit utility applications before learning whether capacity exists, exposing them to months of waiting, rejection, or upgrade costs ([YC launch, Nov 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Wkq-ryhla-faster-power-grid-connection-for-your-project)).
- **Approach:** Ryhla models distribution-grid behavior and utility approval decisions to estimate capacity, project sizing, timing, and upgrade costs, then manages interconnection ([YC LinkedIn announcement, Nov 2026, via search snippet](https://eg.linkedin.com/school/y-combinator/)).
- **Differentiation:** Ryhla targets distribution-grid projects, while Pearl Street emphasizes utility-scale interconnection studies and GridCARE targets AI data-center power acceleration; Paces also combines site selection and interconnection diligence ([YC directory](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/ryhla), [Pearl Street](https://pearlstreettechnologies.com/), [GridCARE](https://www.gridcare.ai/), [Paces](https://www.paces.com/post/interconnection-pathways-what-developers-need-to-know-before-filing)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Project fees could monetize its interconnection service; pricing: No public data found; Website not accessible at time of research, November 3, 2026, after an initial attempt and one retry.
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** Ryhla requests developer introductions and project submissions through its founders’ email ([YC launch, Nov 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Wkq-ryhla-faster-power-grid-connection-for-your-project)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** YC describes proprietary distribution-grid data and models, but does not disclose dataset scope or exclusive access rights ([YC LinkedIn announcement, Nov 2026, via search snippet](https://eg.linkedin.com/school/y-combinator/)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Accumulated application outcomes could improve utility-specific predictions, although exclusive collection rights and measurable learning effects remain unverified.
- **Market structure:** [Inferred]: No structural barrier preventing incumbent entry is established, given Enverus’s existing combination of energy data and acquired interconnection simulation software ([Enverus, March 2025](https://www.enverus.com/newsroom/undo-the-queue-enverus-acquires-pearl-street-technologies-to-solve-for-a-more-reliable-resilient-grid/)).
- **Commoditization risk:** Paces already offers interconnection-pathway reports and power-flow studies, demonstrating overlapping functionality, although replication cost for Ryhla’s models is undisclosed ([Paces, September 2026](https://www.paces.com/post/interconnection-pathways-what-developers-need-to-know-before-filing)).
