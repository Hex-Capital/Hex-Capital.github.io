## The Idea

- **Problem:** Construction teams discover concealed pipes, cables, structure, and defects after work begins, contributing to a company-cited $20B+ in annual avoidable rework, while thermal cameras and LiDAR do not penetrate surfaces ([Launch YC, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SAN-applied-electrodynamics-a-new-kind-of-camera-that-can-see-through-walls); [company website, Jul 2026](https://www.ae-dyn.com/)).
- **Approach:** WaveSight transmits radio waves through building materials, reconstructs a scale-native point cloud claimed accurate to 1 mm, and is planned to fuse radio imaging with RGB and thermal cameras ([Launch YC, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SAN-applied-electrodynamics-a-new-kind-of-camera-that-can-see-through-walls)).
- **Differentiation:** Applied Electrodynamics claims across-room scans in under one minute and 1-mm-scale output, versus Walabot’s phone-connected RF scanner rated for four inches into drywall and Exodigo’s outdoor underground mapping service ([company LinkedIn, Jul 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/company/applied-electrodynamics); [Walabot, Feb 2026](https://walabot.com/blogs/guides/wall-scanner-technology); [Exodigo](https://www.exodigo.com/technology)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: An upfront hardware sale with possible mapping-software or service revenue, because the company is registering preorders for launch pricing but publishes no price or commercial terms ([company website, Jul 2026](https://www.ae-dyn.com/)).
- **TAM/SAM:** The global ground-penetrating-radar market is a $457.32M 2025 proxy, forecast to grow 7.58% annually from 2026–2034, while no public SAM estimate was found for in-wall construction imaging ([Fortune Business Insights, Jul 2026](https://www.fortunebusinessinsights.com/ground-penetrating-radar-market-102600)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** The company is collecting direct preorders, attending construction conferences, and requesting introductions to general contractors, industrial-inspection teams, and U.S. defense organizations ([company website, Jul 2026](https://www.ae-dyn.com/); [Launch YC, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SAN-applied-electrodynamics-a-new-kind-of-camera-that-can-see-through-walls); [company LinkedIn, Jul 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/company/applied-electrodynamics)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** The team reports 250+ granted patents across its members and says it has built both the radio hardware and imaging algorithms, but public sources do not establish that those patents are assigned to Applied Electrodynamics ([company website and Launch YC, Jul 2026](https://www.ae-dyn.com/)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Labeled scan data across different materials and verified as-built conditions could improve reconstruction algorithms, but no deployment volume, proprietary dataset size, or customer validation is public.
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage; Vayyar already commercializes a $199.95 RF wall scanner, while Exodigo and Lumafield sell adjacent subsurface and industrial-inspection systems ([Walabot, Feb 2026](https://walabot.com/pages/walabot-diy-2); [Exodigo](https://www.exodigo.com/); [Lumafield](https://www.lumafield.com/products/neptune-industrial-x-ray-ct-scanner)).
- **Commoditization risk:** Vayyar already supplies RF wall imaging, and Qorvo introduced a fully integrated low-power UWB system-on-chip supporting radar sensing in March 2025, widening access to component-level technology ([Walabot, Feb 2026](https://walabot.com/blogs/guides/wall-scanner-technology); [Qorvo, Mar 2025](https://www.qorvo.com/newsroom/news/2025/qorvo-expands-ultra-wideband-portfolio-with-first-fully-integrated-low-power-soc)).

## Key Risks

- **Material-dependent performance:** The company’s across-room and 1-mm claims are self-published, while a 2025 technical review states that penetration depth and image resolution depend on antenna and material-related parameters; no independent product validation was found ([Launch YC, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SAN-applied-electrodynamics-a-new-kind-of-camera-that-can-see-through-walls); [Radio Science, May 2025](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1029/2024RS008094)).
- **Spectrum classification and coordination:** U.S. wall-imaging operation for construction requires FCC coordination, while equipment classified as through-wall imaging is restricted to public-safety operators, making device classification and application-specific compliance material ([47 CFR §§15.509–15.510, Jul 2026](https://ecfr.io/Title-47/Section-15.510); [47 CFR §15.525, Jul 2026](https://ecfr.io/Title-47/Section-15.525)).
- **Direct product substitution:** Vayyar has raised more than $300M and sells Walabot’s RF wall scanner for $199.95, creating an existing lower-depth alternative for drywall use cases ([Vayyar, Jun 2022](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/vayyar-imaging-secures-108m-in-series-e-funding-led-by-koch-disruptive-technologies-301559574.html); [Walabot](https://walabot.com/pages/walabot-diy-2)).
- **Procurement fragmentation:** The company names construction, industrial inspection, security, cargo, and defense applications, which involve different buyers and compliance requirements, although its stated initial focus is construction ([YC profile and Launch YC, Jul 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/applied-electrodynamics-inc)).
