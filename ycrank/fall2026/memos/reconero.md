## The Idea

- **Problem:** Police officers manually document incidents in legacy systems, with Reconero claiming paperwork consumes over 40% of their day ([YC profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/reconero); [company LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/reconero), accessed Oct 2026).
- **Approach:** Reconero’s Alyx assistant searches departmental records and evidence through voice or text, extracts narratives and entities from body-camera footage, and prefills reports and forms for officer review ([Reconero Intelligence](https://reconero.com/intelligence/), accessed Oct 2026).
- **Differentiation:** Reconero combines records, dispatch, and an evidence assistant, versus Abel’s advertised AI toolkit layered into existing workflows, Peregrine’s data integration platform, and Mark43’s integrated CAD/RMS suite, with no independently established performance advantage found ([Reconero](https://www.linkedin.com/company/reconero), [Abel](https://abelpolice.com/), [Peregrine](https://peregrine.io/resources/peregrine-technologies-raises-250-million-series-d-at-68-billion-valuation), [Mark43](https://mark43.com/platform/)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Agency software subscriptions are a possible monetization path given the departmental demo process, but public prices, billing units, and contract terms were not found ([Reconero contact page](https://reconero.com/contact/)).
- **TAM/SAM:** Grand View Research estimates global law-enforcement software at $20.4B in 2026, growing at 10.4% CAGR through 2033, a broader category than Reconero’s addressable segment; no company-specific SAM was found ([Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/law-enforcement-software-market-report)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** The company solicits police-department demos using agency data and offers migration from legacy RMS, CAD exports, spreadsheets, and scanned documents ([Reconero contact page](https://reconero.com/contact/); [Reconero website](https://reconero.com/)).

## Key Risks

- **Report accuracy:** [Inferred]: Automated evidence-to-report generation can introduce factual errors, as documented in a comparable Axon trial; Reconero requires officer review and signature, but public accuracy measurements were not found ([ABC, Oct 23, 2026](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-10-23/ai-assisted-police-report-writing-dv-domestic-violence/107297138); [Reconero Intelligence](https://reconero.com/intelligence/)).
- **Migration and procurement burden:** [Inferred]: Replacing departmental systems requires implementation and integration work, illustrated by a municipal RMS procurement evaluating technical compatibility and implementation costs; Reconero offers legacy-data migration ([Darien council packet, Apr 2026](https://www.darien.il.us/media/q4ubqiar/p260420.pdf); [Reconero](https://reconero.com/)).
- **Incumbent substitution:** [Inferred]: Agencies can buy overlapping records, dispatch, or AI drafting capabilities from established suppliers, reducing the need for a separate Reconero purchase ([Mark43](https://mark43.com/platform/); [Axon](https://www.axon.com/products/draft-one)).
