## The Idea

- **Problem** Consumer protection investigators start with complaints and manually examine changing websites, leaving hidden checkout fees and subscription traps undiscovered when consumers never report them ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Vq1-particularity-ai-investigators-for-government-regulators), Oct 2026).
- **Approach** Browser agents test live checkout and cancellation flows, timestamp and hash what they capture, match complaints to saved evidence, and link findings to statutory elements for staff review ([Particularity](https://particularity.io/), [Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Vq1-particularity-ai-investigators-for-government-regulators)).
- **Differentiation** Particularity combines live-site checks with complaint matching and statutory evidence mapping, while EU eLab supports EU enforcement sweeps, Pagefreezer’s WebPreserver captures online evidence, and Case IQ manages agency intake and investigations ([Particularity](https://particularity.io/), [European Commission](https://commission.europa.eu/topics/consumers/consumer-rights-and-complaints/enforcement-consumer-protection/sweeps_en), [Pagefreezer](https://blog.pagefreezer.com/what-can-you-capture-with-webpreserver), [Case IQ](https://www.caseiq.com/industries/government)).
- **Business Model** [Inferred]: Agency software contracts are a possible monetization path given the company’s government focus and New York State’s solicitation for consumer complaint software ([Particularity](https://particularity.io/), [New York Department of State RFP](https://dos.ny.gov/system/files/documents/2024/02/revised_dcp_software_rfp.pdf)).
- **TAM/SAM** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution** The YC launch solicits conversations with state attorneys general’s offices, city consumer affairs departments, and state agencies, and the website offers call booking and a shared inbox ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Vq1-particularity-ai-investigators-for-government-regulators), [Particularity](https://particularity.io/)).

## Key Risks

- **Browser workflow reliability** [Inferred]: Incomplete sessions could hide violations, as a separate dark-pattern audit verified completion on 79% of deployment workflows, and Particularity provides staff review of captured findings ([Sun et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03881), Mar 2026, [Particularity](https://particularity.io/)).
- **Evidence authentication** [Inferred]: Agencies may need additional provenance or corroboration for dynamic content, even when Particularity timestamps and hashes its captures ([SWGDE acquisition guidance](https://www.swgde.org/documents/published-complete-listing/21-f-001-best-practices-for-acquiring-online-content/), [Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Vq1-particularity-ai-investigators-for-government-regulators)).
- **Public procurement** [Inferred]: Agency deployments may require security and data residency reviews, as New York’s consumer complaint software solicitation required security controls and continental U.S. data storage ([New York Department of State RFP](https://dos.ny.gov/system/files/documents/2024/02/revised_dcp_software_rfp.pdf)).
- **Name ambiguity** Public records also surface a Los Angeles entity named Particularity LLC, while the YC startup identifies itself as a Delaware public benefit corporation ([BizProfile registry extract](https://www.bizprofile.net/ca/los-angeles/particularity-llc), [Particularity](https://particularity.io/)).
