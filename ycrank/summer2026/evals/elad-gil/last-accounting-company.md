# Last Accounting Company -- Elad Gil Evaluation

The market map is straightforward: computer-use accuracy improved from roughly 12% in 2024 to 85% by July 2026, Nordic SMBs can now buy accounting as an agent-operated service, and the controlling variable is whether automation reduces accountant-review labor faster than accuracy and liability costs increase. Last Accounting Company has identified the mechanism but not the threshold. It discloses neither review minutes per customer nor gross margin, so $80 million processed cannot yet establish that this is a structurally better accounting business rather than a technology-enabled firm with familiar service economics.

The technical shift is real. Björn collects documents, reconciles transactions, prepares filings, and maintains books before human review. Yet OSWorld performance is only a proxy for production accounting, where one consequential error can erase savings across many successful transactions. Fixed pricing could turn improving automation into expanding margins, but without pricing, cost-to-serve, or exception-rate data, the dossier does not quantify that step change. This is the central underwriting gap.

The strongest evidence is that the product owns the workflow rather than formatting model output. Last Accounting Company controls the ledger, evidence links, permissions, approval gates, accounting controls, and customer-separated databases; its API and MCP server expose reviewed records after the work is completed. Better models should therefore increase task completion inside an enduring system of record. Human corrections and daily evaluations could compound that advantage, although the company has not disclosed evaluation results or shown that competitors could not reproduce the training data.

Dozens of Finnish companies have already sent more than $80 million in transaction value through the system for accounting, analysis, and decisions. That is meaningful pre-seed usage, particularly for a product entrusted with bank, payroll, tax, Gmail, and Drive data. It remains company-reported volume rather than demonstrated product-market fit: revenue, retention, customer expansion, filing accuracy, and the concentration of that transaction value are undisclosed. Transaction throughput can be large even when customer willingness to pay or contribution margin is small.

The bear case is sharp. Pilot, Truewind, and Zeni already combine AI with accounting workflows or human expertise, each has raised at least $17 million, and no structural barrier prevents an incumbent firm from adding the same review layer. This is also precisely where an agent-native narrative can conceal a service-heavy business with commodity margins. The proprietary ledger makes Last Accounting Company more durable than a wrapper, but it does not by itself prove differentiated economics or distribution. The founders bring financial-data infrastructure, finance, law, and investment experience, yet the dossier documents neither prior founder learning nor the costly personal commitment that would establish desperation.

I would make a small pre-seed investment because the combination of live financial throughput and owned workflow clears the market and technology gates, but conviction is narrow. The verifiable flip condition is a cohort showing at least 70% gross margin while closing books with under 30 minutes of human review per customer per month and retaining at least 90% of customers for twelve months. Failure on that test would make this an accounting firm with good internal software, not a market-ending software company.
