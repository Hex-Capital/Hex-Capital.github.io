# Collar -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Collar’s strongest path is repeatable direct sales to public-company investor relations teams. The memo documents a ticker-based investor-targeting preview, a bookable demo, and a pilot offer. The recurring job is preparing for earnings and investor meetings, reconciling figures across documents, and giving executives citation-backed talking points from approved materials. That workflow could carry across markets. Investor coverage and local disclosure review would need to be checked market by market, and the memo gives no evidence of a cross-market rollout. (The Idea, Key Risks)

Collar reports $1.4M in deals pending compliance over two months and 4× month-over-month revenue growth from an undisclosed base and period. It also describes a representative use case in which earnings preparation fell from three weeks to about one day. These claims suggest buyer interest and a concrete workflow benefit. The memo provides too little evidence to establish meaningful budget or workflow commitments by identifiable buyers, a repeatable route from demo to paid go-live, repeat paid use across earnings cycles, or the effort each rollout adds. Even assuming strong fit, the first unresolved link is whether Collar can repeatedly turn compliance-gated interest into paid deployments. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Countercase

Compliance review could stall deals or require issuer-specific work on approved materials and live-call controls for every deployment. The reported pipeline is already pending compliance, and the memo identifies selective disclosure and live-answer accuracy as risks. If each rollout requires substantial manual review and setup, sales and delivery effort could rise in proportion to revenue. The memo establishes a compliance bottleneck, while conversion and implementation effort remain unknown. The structural downside gate remains untriggered. (Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can two independent public-company IR teams go from the ticker demo to paid use in a live earnings cycle through the same compliance and onboarding process? Dated records of approval, payment, and live use, with comparable setup hours across both accounts, would move Collar to Reach out. If that pattern remains unproven, keep Collar on Watch.
