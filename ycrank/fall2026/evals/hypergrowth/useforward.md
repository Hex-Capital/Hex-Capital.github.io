# Forward -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Marketplace liquidity** is Forward’s strongest path. Commercial real estate debt advisors repeatedly need to find lenders whose current mandates fit a deal. Private-credit funds, the paying side, need suitable deals to screen. Forward packages advisor documents into a financing brief, matches the deal to lender mandates, and coordinates submissions. The workflow could carry into another market if Forward can build local lender coverage and keep mandate data current. The memo gives evidence of a controlled Southern California rollout. (The Idea, Key Risks)

Capital Partners’ CEO says Forward introduced unfamiliar lenders and helped secure term sheets. Forward reports that its first advisor partner went exclusive after the first transaction and that 100 U.S. funds signed fee agreements. Those are early signs of value and commitment. Cold calls and LinkedIn outreach established a route into the market, but the record traces only one advisor rollout. The growth hypothesis is that lender responses improve matching for the next advisor’s deals. The memo provides no measured improvement in matching or evidence of repeat paid closings at an advisor’s normal deal cadence. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Countercase

The exclusive advisor could supply enough deal flow to make the network look active while Forward still has to assemble lender coverage and coordinate closings deal by deal. Signed fee agreements, reported pipeline volume, and term sheets leave collections uncertain. If the two reported transactions fund at their stated amounts of $6 million and $10.25 million and both qualify for the 25-basis-point fee, they would generate $40,625 in gross fees before costs. The memo gives no usable measure of staff time or delivery cost per closing. Dependence on bespoke work is a testable risk, and the evidence falls short of triggering the structural downside gate. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks, Key Facts)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a second, independent CRE debt advisor use Forward’s standard workflow and existing lender network to close multiple loans at its normal deal cadence? Deal records showing funded loans, collected fees, and comparable staff time per closing across the two advisors would move the action to Reach out if they show repeat paid use without rising coordination effort per deal.
