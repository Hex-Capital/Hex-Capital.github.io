# Metalcraft -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Scarce complements** is Metalcraft’s strongest path. As life-science models improve, developers may pay for independently maintained evaluations that reveal meaningful differences in research performance. Metalcraft has published six tasksets with samples, and the company reports model performance by taskset. That establishes a working evaluation product. Whether its scores predict performance in live research, and whether developers face a shortage of credible alternatives, remain open. The memo leaves the New Economies path unsupported because it gives no comparative labor cost per successful evaluation or evidence of newly viable buyers. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

Marshall Kiely’s computational biology and applied AI experience, together with the catalog shipped after the pivot, support execution at this stage. The booking flow shows how buyers can request access. Buyer commitments and payment remain undocumented. Under a licensing scenario, each refreshed taskset would need enough paid reuse to cover expert authoring, review, maintenance, and delivery. The memo supplies neither the costs nor paid usage needed to estimate contribution. (Founders & Team, Market & Traction, The Idea, Key Facts)

### Countercase

Model developers can build overlapping evaluations internally, as OpenAI did with LifeSciBench, and Benchling offers evaluations drawn from lab experiments. Metalcraft’s catalog establishes that it can produce tasksets. Exclusive rights, buyer preference, and switching costs remain unverified. The overlap makes substitution credible, but the memo does not establish that buyers find those alternatives equivalent for the decisions Metalcraft targets. The scarcity gate remains unproven, which supports Watch. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Will an independent model developer pay to renew access after comparing a Metalcraft taskset with its best in-house or third-party alternative? Move to Reach out if the buyer renews at a price that covers delivery and explains which consequential model decision depended on evidence it could not readily obtain elsewhere. Move to Pass if the comparison shows a readily available alternative supports the same decision at comparable cost.
