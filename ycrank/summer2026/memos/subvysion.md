## The Idea

- **Problem:** Construction contractors rely on incomplete records and surface markings for buried infrastructure, while utility strikes cause an estimated $30 billion in annual U.S. damage and 20 deaths, and one-third are attributed to locator errors or inaccurate maps ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/subvysion), Jul 2026; [Wenson Tang](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/wenson-tang_four-months-ago-i-quit-my-job-to-solve-a-activity-7481415342800551936-OyGE), Jul 2026).
- **Approach:** An autonomous rover collects multi-sensor GPR and 3D surface data, which SubVysion aligns, validates, and converts into georeferenced maps displayed through a mobile AR application ([SubVysion](https://subvysion.com/), Jul 2026; [Penn Engineering](https://pa.linkedin.com/school/penn-engineering/), Jul 2026).
- **Differentiation:** SubVysion combines site data collection, GPR processing, centimeter-accurate mapping, and phone-based AR, whereas 4M Analytics primarily compiles existing public records and vGIS visualizes customers’ existing GIS, BIM, and scan data ([SubVysion](https://subvysion.com/), [4M Analytics](https://www.4manalytics.com/), [vGIS](https://www.vgis.io/why-vgis-utilities-high-accuracy-survey-grade-augmented-reality-ar-bim-gis-2/), Jul 2026).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: The tailored-demo and project-details intake suggests project- or site-based commercial contracts rather than self-service software pricing.
- **TAM/SAM:** The global underground-utility-mapping market was estimated at $1.45 billion in 2025 and forecast to reach $2.29 billion by 2030 at greater than 9.61% CAGR ([Mordor Intelligence via Research and Markets](https://www.researchandmarkets.com/reports/5119880/underground-utility-mapping-market-share), May 2025).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Founders report cold-calling contractors, converting one recipient into the first customer and investor, and operating on construction sites across the United States ([Wenson Tang](https://www.linkedin.com/in/wenson-tang), Jul 2026).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** The product integrates autonomous field capture, multi-sensor GPR, surface-positioning data, quality control, 3D reconstruction, and mobile AR, but no patents or proprietary-data scale were publicly identified ([SubVysion](https://subvysion.com/), Jul 2026).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Repeated scans could create a proprietary library linking raw sensor signatures to verified utilities, although the company publishes no dataset size or validation record.
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** Exodigo already combines multi-sensor scanning, AI, and geolocated 3D underground maps, while Groundhawk offers centimeter-level 3D utility documentation and vGIS supplies field AR visualization ([Exodigo](https://www.exodigo.com/), [Groundhawk](https://www.groundhawk.io/blog/groundhawk-raises-2-million-to-scale-ai-powered-3d-underground-infrastructure-mapping-across-europe), [vGIS](https://www.vgis.io/2018/01/14/meemim-launches-vgis-comprehensive-ar-mr-vr-platform-gis/)).

## Key Risks

- **Detection completeness:** The website claims “100% visibility,” while published GPR research describes 3D reconstruction of underground objects as an ongoing technical challenge, creating risk around performance across soil, depth, and material conditions ([SubVysion](https://subvysion.com/); [GPR mapping research](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.16446), Sep 2024).
- **Accuracy-chain dependency:** AR placement depends on the accuracy of survey positioning, terrain models, and utility interpretation; vGIS documents that imperfect elevation models can visibly misalign underground objects ([vGIS Support](https://support.vgis.io/hc/en-us/articles/360023886274-How-Do-Augmented-Reality-Visuals-Work), Jun 2025).
- **Capitalized competition:** Exodigo reports more than $200 million in funding, 100+ customers, and 86.1 million square feet mapped, giving it field data and deployment resources in the same subsurface-mapping category ([Exodigo](https://www.exodigo.com/), Jul 2026).
- **Workflow liability:** Contractors may rely on the maps during excavation, while the company has not publicly disclosed accuracy-validation standards, insurance arrangements, or responsibility for missed utilities ([SubVysion](https://subvysion.com/), Jul 2026).
