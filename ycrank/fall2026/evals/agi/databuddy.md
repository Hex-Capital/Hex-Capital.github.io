# Databuddy -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Medium

### Company case

Databuddy’s strongest AGI path is New Economies. The company describes a workflow in which founders without an analyst reconcile traffic, product events, errors, and payments by hand. It says Databunny can query those signals together, expose the underlying query for review, and deliver scheduled investigations in Slack. The plausible new use is routine conversion and revenue investigations that small teams previously skipped because the work took too much founder time. The memo gives no measured baseline for manual investigations or evidence of how often Databunny produces a correct, actionable result (The Idea; Key Risks).

For an illustrative scenario, assume a team buys the $299 monthly plan and gets four correct, actionable investigations per month. The subscription alone costs $74.75 per successful investigation, before allocated setup and human review time. The former delivery cost would include founder time spent reconciling and validating the same data, plus any incremental tool costs. Event-metered plans and overages give Databuddy a way to charge as data volume rises. Contribution per account still depends on event processing, inference, and support costs, which the memo leaves unquantified (The Idea; Key Risks).

The memo records 38 active subscriptions and $3,345 in company-wide MRR. These show early willingness to pay for Databuddy, while paid adoption and renewal for the newly viable investigation workflow remain unverified. Issa Nassar’s prior analytics work and delivery of both the original tracker and Databunny support seed-stage execution. Repeatable investigation accuracy in customer use is the next execution gap (Market & Traction; Key Facts; Founders & Team; The Idea; Key Risks).

### Countercase

Databunny may make existing analytics work faster while giving customers little reason to keep paying once comparable models are widely available. Mixpanel already offers proactive metric monitoring, and PostHog exposes analytics to AI agents on data customers already collect. Configured funnels and investigation history could make Databuddy harder to replace, but the memo provides no retention evidence for that inference. Autumn’s reported switch from Framer Analytics offers a limited signal of preference, with functional parity untested. The incumbent overlap keeps both market opening and durable value capture unproven. It is insufficient to close the path because equivalent investigation outcomes and ready substitution remain untested (Defensibility; Market & Traction; Key Risks).

### Decisive diligence question

Will independent teams without analysts repeatedly pay for Databunny to run investigations they previously skipped when a functionally comparable alternative is available? A paid cohort using its own data should document which investigations it previously skipped, compare the full cost per correct, actionable result across workflows, and show whether buyers renew after trying the alternative. Move to **Reach out** if the cohort shows materially lower costs that enable recurring use, renewal at viable prices under functional parity, and positive contribution after delivery costs. Move to **Pass** if the trial shows that the workflow opens no new use or that buyers readily switch at parity. Keep **Watch** while the results remain inconclusive.
