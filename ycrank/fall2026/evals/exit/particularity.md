# Particularity -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

The strongest path is a strategic acquisition by an investigations software vendor such as Case IQ. The memo describes Case IQ’s agency complaint intake and routing business. Particularity says its browser agents test live checkout and cancellation flows, preserve evidence, and map findings to statutory elements. Owning that capability could let Case IQ identify violations before complaints arrive and bring the resulting evidence into its existing case workflow. The memo documents Particularity’s public launch and new cancellation enforcement authority in New York City. Agency adoption, measured buyer benefit, and acquisition interest remain unverified. (The Idea, Market & Traction)

A paid agency deployment that demonstrates reliable flow completion, evidence accepted by investigators, and reduced review time would make the acquisition rationale more concrete. The reported $500,000 in YC financing and the founders’ relevant experience make an initial test plausible. Pilot funding, procurement timing, and the capital needed to repeat the deployment remain uncertain. Transaction feasibility and independent alternatives stay at their defaults because the memo supplies no deal-specific transfer or participation evidence and no separate route to holder cash. (Market & Traction, Founders & Team, Key Risks, Key Facts)

The benchmark assumes a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, 5% starting ownership diluted to 3%, and no follow-ons. The holder needs $2.5 million in gross cash within eight years, which requires about $83.3 million in distributable equity at 3%. The sale cases assume an all-cash acquisition within eight years, full participation by the holder, and a pro rata waterfall without senior claims. The prices are illustrative assumptions. The memo supplies no valuation evidence to anchor them. (Key Facts)

### Countercase

Case IQ could build browser screening itself, while tools such as WebPreserver and EU eLab already cover parts of the workflow. Particularity’s advantage depends on making the combined workflow reliable enough for investigators to use and accept its evidence. Authentication, security, and data residency requirements could slow the deployments needed to prove that advantage. The memo leaves a ready substitute, a structural closing block, and a credible sale-price ceiling unresolved, so the Pass gate is untriggered. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Would an investigations software vendor pay enough to clear the holder’s proceeds hurdle once a paid agency deployment demonstrates reliable evidence capture and measurable review-time savings? A buyer-side build-versus-buy analysis tied to those results, with a credible conditional price range that clears the hurdle after deal deductions and the waterfall, would move the action from Watch to Reach out.
