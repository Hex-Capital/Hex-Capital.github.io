## The Idea

- **Problem:** Hospital and academic research staff can spend weeks or months reviewing charts and reconciling EHR, REDCap, and wearable records in spreadsheets before a study can proceed ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WbF-sutrix-ai-that-turns-patient-data-into-study-ready-datasets), Nov 2026).
- **Approach:** Sutrix links patient records, standardizes codes and units, applies protocol rules to build cohorts and analysis tables, and routes unresolved records to staff with source evidence ([Sutrix](https://www.sutrix.ai/)).
- **Differentiation:** Sutrix prepares multi-source datasets using each investigator’s study rules, while Dyania Health emphasizes EHR chart review and trial screening, Layer Health emphasizes chart abstraction for clinical registries, and PatientIQ emphasizes collecting and benchmarking patient-reported outcomes ([Sutrix](https://www.sutrix.ai/), [Dyania Health](https://dyaniahealth.com/press-release/dyania-series-a/), [Layer Health](https://www.layerhealth.com/), [PatientIQ](https://www.patientiq.io/news/patientiq-raises-30-million-series-c-to-power-the-next-generation-of-patient-reported-outcomes-intelligence)).
- **Business Model:** Sutrix sets scope, pricing, and delivery timelines in individual paid engagement agreements and publishes no rates in its terms ([Sutrix Terms of Service](https://www.sutrix.ai/terms), Sep 2026).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** The founders solicit introductions to research teams through Launch YC and offer biostatistics cores direct access through the CTO’s software release post ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WbF-sutrix-ai-that-turns-patient-data-into-study-ready-datasets), Nov 2026, [Supratik Panuganti on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/supratik-panuganti-4732532a4_after-working-with-15-labs-running-50-activity-7508551437677932544-4akH), Sep 2026).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Sutrix saves versioned study rules, field-level source provenance, and reviewer decisions so teams can reuse the preparation workflow as data and protocols change ([Sutrix](https://www.sutrix.ai/)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Accumulated, approved study rules and review histories could raise switching costs, pending evidence on renewals and workflow migration ([Sutrix](https://www.sutrix.ai/)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** Dyania and Layer already automate EHR abstraction, and academic researchers have demonstrated open-source models for extracting structured data from clinical notes in a single disease area ([Dyania Health](https://dyaniahealth.com/press-release/dyania-series-a/), [Layer Health](https://www.layerhealth.com/), [npj Digital Medicine, via search snippet](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-026-03179-z), 2026).

## Key Risks

- **EHR integration:** Sutrix lists direct FHIR connectivity for EHR sandboxes, so production deployments may depend on approved endpoints or file exports, with institution-cloud deployment available by arrangement ([Sutrix](https://www.sutrix.ai/)).
- **Research data accuracy:** Record-linking or protocol interpretation errors could change study cohorts or endpoints, and Sutrix mitigates this with source-level evidence, exception review, and investigator verification ([Sutrix](https://www.sutrix.ai/), [Sutrix Terms](https://www.sutrix.ai/terms)).
- **Software revenue attribution:** The reported revenue predates the software release to biostatistics cores, leaving the contribution from recurring software subscriptions undisclosed ([Supratik Panuganti on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/supratik-panuganti-4732532a4_after-working-with-15-labs-running-50-activity-7508551437677932544-4akH), Sep 2026).
- **Name collision:** Sutrix Group also uses the name in technology services, so public results require matching to `sutrix.ai` or the Panuganti founders ([Sutrix Group](https://sutrixgroup.com/en/), [Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/sutrix)).
