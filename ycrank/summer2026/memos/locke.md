## The Idea

- **Problem:** Early-stage companies seeking regulatory influence or government contracts face legacy firms charging incumbent-oriented prices and fragmented coverage across Congress, agencies, procurement, and proposals ([YC Launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SSp-locke-the-first-ai-government-affairs-firm), Aug 2026).
- **Approach:** Locke combines in-house lobbyists with AI agents that analyze legislation, track stakeholder engagement, monitor government signals through Slack, and draft contract proposals and compliance briefs ([YC Launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SSp-locke-the-first-ai-government-affairs-firm), Aug 2026; [Locke Government Affairs](https://locke.inc/government-affairs), Aug 2026).
- **Differentiation:** Quorum, Plural, and Legible provide policy-tracking and government-affairs software, while NationGraph supplies government-purchasing intelligence; Locke says its lobbyists and agents execute the path from research and policymaker meetings through funded contracts ([Quorum](https://www.quorum.us/company-news/quorum-receives-strategic-investment-from-enlightenment-capital-to-accelerate-ai-powered-public-affairs-platform/), Jul 2026; [Plural](https://pluralpolicy.com/blog/letter-from-the-ceo-plural-raises-10m-to-make-understanding-public-policy-better-for-organizations/); [Legible](https://legiblepolicy.com/); [YC Launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SSp-locke-the-first-ai-government-affairs-firm), Aug 2026).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Client-specific retainers or project fees for hybrid software and professional services are the likely monetization path because Locke employs lobbyists, reports an annual revenue run-rate, and publishes no standard pricing.
- **TAM/SAM:** Emergen Research estimates the adjacent global advocacy-software market at $1.10B in 2025 with a 9.8% CAGR during 2026–2035, while no public SAM estimate was found for Locke’s hybrid U.S. service ([Emergen Research](https://www.emergenresearch.com/industry-report/advocacy-software-market), Jul 2026).
- **GTM / Distribution:** [Inferred]: Founder-led sales through YC and Badhwar’s government-policy network are the initial path because Locke targets early-stage companies and Badhwar previously led GTM at Clipbook after policy roles ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/locke); [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/parth-badhwar), Jul 2026).

## Key Risks

- **Incumbent feature overlap:** Quorum serves more than 2,000 organizations with legislative tracking, stakeholder management, AI intelligence, and professional services, overlapping several Locke functions ([Quorum](https://www.quorum.us/company-news/quorum-receives-strategic-investment-from-enlightenment-capital-to-accelerate-ai-powered-public-affairs-platform/), Jul 2026).
- **AI-output accuracy:** Locke’s agents produce citation-backed regulatory briefs and contract proposals, while federal AI guidance requires accuracy evaluation, risk management, monitoring, and lifecycle accountability; Locke publishes no accuracy results ([Locke Government Affairs](https://locke.inc/government-affairs); [GSA](https://www.gsa.gov/directives-library/accelerating-responsible-use-of-artificial-intelligence-at-gsa), Mar 2026).
- **Lobbying compliance:** Locke directly lobbies policymakers, and U.S. lobbying firms exceeding $3,500 in quarterly client-related lobbying income face registration and recurring reporting obligations ([Locke Government Affairs](https://locke.inc/government-affairs); [U.S. House Clerk](https://lobbyingdisclosure.house.gov/), 2026).
- **Service throughput:** [Inferred]: Owning the process through in-house lobbyists ties delivery capacity partly to policy headcount, consistent with Locke simultaneously recruiting founding policy and technical staff ([YC Launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SSp-locke-the-first-ai-government-affairs-firm); [YC Jobs](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/locke/jobs), Aug 2026).
- **Name and entity ambiguity:** Search results surface unrelated Locke businesses, while the company website identifies Justinian AI, Inc. in its footer and YC’s former Justinian URL redirects to Locke, creating entity-disambiguation work ([Locke](https://locke.inc/); [YC redirect](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/justinian); [unrelated Locke Identity Networks](https://www.linkedin.com/company/locke-id)).
