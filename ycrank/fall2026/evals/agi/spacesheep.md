# Spacesheep -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path B — New Economies.** Spacesheep could make routine team review economical for agent-generated dashboards, mockups, and other outputs that teams currently leave unshared. The former workflow requires someone to extract work from chats or local files, reformat it, share it, and coordinate revisions. Spacesheep says agents can publish directly to a shared link where teammates comment on specific elements and request revisions. Its launch examples show the kinds of outputs it can host. The market-opening hypothesis is that direct publishing saves enough labor per approved artifact for teams to review work they previously skipped. That requires a comparison that includes reviewer time, retries, access setup, and exceptions. (*The Idea*, *Market & Traction*, *Key Risks*)

Under the scenario that frontier-quality models are cheap and widely available, a team might still pay for one searchable review history across agents. That preference remains untested. The memo records 28 Chrome and three Firefox extension users, with paid or repeat use undisclosed. At the listed Team price of $10 per member per month, a paid team is a plausible expansion unit if seat revenue covers hosting, revision inference, and support as review volume grows. The memo provides no realized cost or utilization figures. The public launch and Makarov’s prior delivery of the first Claude Code Artifacts support the team’s ability to run a seed-stage trial. (*Defensibility*, *Market & Traction*, *Key Facts*, *The Idea*, *Founders & Team*)

### Countercase

Spacesheep may save time on reviews teams already do while remaining easy to bypass. Claude Artifacts and ChatGPT Sites let teams publish inside their existing agent tools, Type.com already connects Claude and Codex to a shared workspace, and Spacesheep’s exports could lower switching costs. These alternatives make substitution credible. The memo has no head-to-head buyer evidence showing whether they match Spacesheep for recurring cross-agent reviews, so the model-parity gate remains open. (*Defensibility*, *Key Risks*)

### Decisive diligence question

Will an independent team using multiple agents pay for a second review cycle at the listed Team price after trying a comparable alternative with equivalent models? Reach out if the trial shows that lower full cost per approved artifact enabled recurring reviews the team previously skipped and that the team renewed for Spacesheep’s cross-agent review workflow. Pass if the trial shows that review frequency stays the same or that the team readily switches while preserving the outcome.
