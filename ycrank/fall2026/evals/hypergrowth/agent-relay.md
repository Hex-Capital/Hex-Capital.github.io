# Agent Relay -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Medium

### Company case

Conditional on strong product-market fit, Agent Relay can serve engineering teams in many markets with the same product. Its cross-provider SDK and desktop relay address a recurring job across existing coding tools and teammates’ machines. Self-hosting gives teams an alternative to hosted search, which sends credential-redacted session text to OpenAI. Pending security attestations could delay procurement for buyers that require them. (The Idea, Key Risks)

The open-source repository, npm package, and desktop downloads give developers a practical path to discover and try the product independently. The memo describes a paid desktop upgrade for shared sessions and more connected agents, while the purchase flow and paid activation remain unverified. Searchable handoff history gives teams a reason to keep using the relay, and connecting more agents could increase demand for paid capacity. Billing cadence and same-account spending remain unknown. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Facts)

Pomerado reported that 80+ agents exchanged more than 38,000 messages. The founder separately reported a prospect’s willingness to pay after a week of heavy use. Those reports support workflow adoption around the October team-messaging launch. Realized revenue, paid production use, and pre-revenue status remain unverified, so Initial traction uses the default. (The Idea, Market & Traction)

For a hosted team, incremental contribution would depend on upgrade revenue after relay compute, history storage, search processing, and support. Self-hosting could shift infrastructure costs to buyers, depending on the support required. The memo gives too little price and cost evidence to estimate contribution, so Cost economics uses the default. (The Idea, Key Risks)

### Countercase

Native messaging already serves teams using Claude Code, and an open standard could make cross-provider messaging easier for coding platforms to add. Teams may use Agent Relay’s free or self-hosted core while paid demand concentrates among enterprises with more demanding procurement. The structural gate stays open because the memo documents self-serve deployment and gives no evidence that bespoke work or subsidies are unavoidable. (Defensibility, The Idea, Key Risks)
