## The Idea

- **Problem:** Transformer operators face failures whose replacements can take years, while maintenance alternatives include periodic manual inspections and dissolved-gas analysis ([Wood Mackenzie, October 2025](https://www.woodmac.com/news/opinion/tariffs-and-supply-chain-dislocation-hamper-us-power-projects/); [Elewit, July 2026](https://www.elewit.ventures/en/news/danish-startup-oktogrid-raises-eur-4-million-to-bring-power-transformer-intelligence-to-critical-electrical-infrastructure); [Camlin](https://camlingroup.com/energy/solutions/transformer-monitoring/)).
- **Approach:** Magnetically attached sensors collect vibration, magnetic-field, temperature, and humidity data, establish equipment-specific baselines, and generate anomaly alerts with investigation guidance ([Talos](https://www.talos-us.com/)).
- **Differentiation:** Talos emphasizes external multimodal sensing and spatial fault localization, whereas Camlin offers dissolved-gas, bushing, and partial-discharge monitoring and Oktogrid emphasizes transformer acoustic signatures ([Talos FieldSense](https://www.talos-us.com/fieldsense); [Camlin](https://camlingroup.com/energy/solutions/transformer-monitoring/); [Elewit, July 2026](https://www.elewit.ventures/en/news/danish-startup-oktogrid-raises-eur-4-million-to-bring-power-transformer-intelligence-to-critical-electrical-infrastructure)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Sensor sales or leases combined with recurring monitoring payments are a possible monetization path for the advertised hardware-and-dashboard product; published pricing: No public data found.
- **TAM/SAM:** The broader global transformer-monitoring market is estimated at $3.11 billion in 2026, reaching $4.98 billion in 2031 at 9.9% CAGR; Talos-specific SAM: No public data found ([MarketsandMarkets, August 2026](https://www.marketsandmarkets.com/Market-Reports/transformer-monitoring-system-market-197443553.html)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** The website solicits company details through a demo form routed directly to the founders ([Talos](https://www.talos-us.com/)).
