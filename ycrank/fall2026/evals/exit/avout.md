# Avout -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Avout’s strongest path is a strategic acquisition by an equipment or asset-based lender. Avout says it uses borrower operating data to underwrite frontier hardware companies and packages the results for third-party lenders. A buyer could acquire the channel to source borrowers it would otherwise miss and reduce underwriting work. Those benefits are inferred. Avout reports arranging a $1.5 million term sheet for a data-center borrower, with closing and disbursement undisclosed. The next operating milestone is repeat funded originations with repayment data. That requires committed third-party lending capital and enough operating runway to build a track record. The memo quantifies neither. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

Using the rubric’s benchmark, a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation starts at 5% ownership and dilutes to 3% with no follow-ons. The eight-year 5× objective requires $2.5 million in gross cash, or about $83.3 million in distributable equity value at 3%. The acquisition cases below assume an all-cash sale by year eight, an eligible holder selling their entire stake, and a pro rata waterfall with no preference payments ahead of the holder. The prices and deductions are illustrative assumptions. The memo provides no buyer pricing or Avout revenue to substantiate them. (Market & Traction, Key Facts)

| Scenario | Enterprise value to distributable equity | Gross cash to holder |
|---|---|---|
| Modest sale | $40M less $5M in assumed net debt and deal adjustments = $35M | $1.05M (2.1×) |
| Strong sale | $100M less $10M in assumed net debt and deal adjustments = $90M | $2.70M (5.4×) |
| Prolonged private | Continued operation with cash reinvested and no share purchase or distribution through year eight | $0 realized |

The memo leaves transfer rights for borrower data and lender relationships, required consents, and holder participation unverified. It provides no evidence of a separate route to holder liquidity. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Risks, Key Facts)

### Countercase

Trinity Capital already offers equipment and asset-based financing, and GPU Capital already sizes loans using server appraisals and customer contracts. A lender could serve the same borrowers through its existing underwriting process. Avout’s reported term sheet leaves repeat funded volume and any underwriting advantage unverified, so a buyer has little demonstrated reason to pay a premium today. The Pass gate remains open because the memo establishes neither a structural block nor a supported sale-price ceiling below the holder’s objective. (Defensibility, Market & Traction)

### Decisive diligence question

What would an equipment lender pay to acquire Avout’s origination channel based on repeat funded loans and their repayment performance? Buyer-backed pricing tied to that record would change the action to Reach out if the range could clear about $83.3 million in distributable equity by year eight under the benchmark holder terms.
