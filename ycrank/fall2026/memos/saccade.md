## The Idea

- **Problem:** Saccade targets patients seeking assessment before memory symptoms prompt specialist evaluation, whose existing diagnostic options include amyloid PET imaging, cerebrospinal-fluid testing, and blood biomarkers ([Saccade](https://saccade.health/), [FDA, May 2025](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-clears-first-blood-test-used-diagnosing-alzheimers-disease)).
- **Approach:** The company describes a 15-minute assessment combining neurologist-designed visual tasks, a VR-headset eye-tracking attachment, and machine-learning analysis of oculomotor responses ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/saccade)).
- **Differentiation:** Saccade records eye movements during VR tasks, whereas RetiSpec analyzes retinal images for amyloid burden and Altoida captures motion, speech, and touch during tablet-based cognitive tasks; ViewMind also measures eye movements during cognitive tasks ([UCSB](https://capstone.engineering.ucsb.edu/projects/saccade), [RetiSpec](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/retispec-closes-10m-usd-to-advance-commercialization-of-its-ai-driven-eye-test-for-detection-of-alzheimers-disease-302207203.html), [Altoida](https://altoida.com/), [ViewMind](https://www.viewmind.com/)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Clinic equipment provision and assessment fees are possible monetization paths given the clinic-based workflow, but public pricing and commercial terms: No public data found ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/saccade)).
- **TAM/SAM:** Grand View Research estimates the broader global Alzheimer’s diagnostics market at $10.2 billion in 2026, growing at 11.2% annually through 2033; this includes imaging, genetic testing, biomarkers, and cognitive assessments rather than Saccade’s specific segment, whose SAM has no public estimate ([Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/alzheimers-disease-diagnostics-market-report)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** The launch solicits introductions to US longevity clinics, while the current homepage recruits people with Alzheimer’s or family history for future research ([YC launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Vff-saccade-world-s-earliest-test-for-alzheimer-s-biomarkers), [Saccade, accessed Oct 27, 2026](https://saccade.health/)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Saccade describes its attachment as patent-pending, and UCSB documents custom camera, illumination, chassis, and thermal-management engineering; patent identifiers and granted claims: No public data found ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/saccade), [UCSB](https://capstone.engineering.ucsb.edu/projects/saccade)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Longitudinal eye recordings linked to confirmed pathology could support model differentiation, but an exclusive dataset and demonstrated predictive advantage remain undocumented, and Saccade currently discloses unvalidated diagnostic performance ([Saccade](https://saccade.health/)).
- **Market structure:** [Inferred]: No structural barrier identified at this stage, given ViewMind’s overlapping eye-tracking technology and preventive-clinic offering ([ViewMind](https://www.viewmind.com/about)).
- **Commoditization risk:** [Inferred]: Existing eye-tracking assessment vendors could compete for the same clinic workflow, although Saccade’s disease-specific prediction claims require separate validation ([ViewMind](https://www.viewmind.com/), [Saccade](https://saccade.health/)).

## Key Risks

- **Clinical validation:** The launch claims biomarker detection up to 15 years before symptoms, while the current homepage discloses that the technology lacks validation for diagnosis, individual risk prediction, and treatment decisions; product-specific longitudinal validation: No public data found ([YC launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Vff-saccade-world-s-earliest-test-for-alzheimer-s-biomarkers), [Saccade](https://saccade.health/)).
- **Treatment eligibility:** [Inferred]: An asymptomatic risk result would not itself establish eligibility for Kisunla, whose prescribing information requires confirmed amyloid pathology and initiation at mild cognitive impairment or mild dementia ([FDA prescribing information, 2025](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2025/761248s004lbl.pdf)).
- **Availability disclosures:** The YC launch advertises clinic testing, whereas the current homepage seeks interest in future research and the privacy notice describes appointment requests requiring staff confirmation ([YC launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Vff-saccade-world-s-earliest-test-for-alzheimer-s-biomarkers), [Saccade](https://saccade.health/), [privacy notice, Oct 2026](https://saccade.health/privacy.html)).
- **Hardware generalization:** The launch claims compatibility with any VR headset, while the published engineering implementation specifically mounts to Apple Vision Pro; cross-headset validation: No public data found ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/saccade), [UCSB](https://capstone.engineering.ucsb.edu/projects/saccade)).
