# Preload -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path A — Scarce complements.** Preload’s best case is that robotics teams training dexterous manipulation policies need demonstrations that capture effort as well as visible motion and contact. If cheaper, more capable models make human demonstrations more useful for training robots, synchronized physical recordings could become a bottleneck for tasks such as gripping and knot tying. Preload demonstrated a rig that synchronizes video, glove contact readings, and forearm EMG. The company reported preliminary separation of light and heavy handling effort. Whether that signal improves task success on a physical robot remains open. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

At a seed-stage standard, the team’s prior work in neural recording hardware, biosignal analysis, and EMG motor control supports its ability to run that test. Buyer commitment remains unknown. The ProHand demo shows an application of EMG control, while the memo documents no buyer allocating budget or committing to qualify Preload’s training data. Under a custom collection scenario, the next unit of expansion is a buyer-qualified task dataset. The memo gives no pricing, usable recordings per operator-hour, or recollection and integration costs to assess that unit’s economics. (Founders & Team, Market & Traction, Key Facts, The Idea, Key Risks)

### Countercase

With ample capital, competitors could build comparable capture rigs. Mecka already synchronizes its own multisensor hardware, and Human Archive publishes aligned video, depth, touch, and motion data. Under cheap, broadly available intelligence, a buyer could use video and tactile data alone if those inputs achieve comparable robot performance. The memo establishes neither that substitution works on an effort-sensitive task nor that Preload controls recordings buyers would struggle to replace. Path A remains open for qualification, with too little affirmative evidence for outreach. Path B has no supported case for a newly viable buyer or transaction. (Defensibility, The Idea, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can Preload win a paid qualification from an independent robotics team by showing that adding its EMG data to an otherwise matched video-and-tactile dataset materially improves physical-robot task success across held-out operators?

A buyer-verified gain that converts into a paid follow-on order on terms that let Preload retain and reuse the recordings would support rescoring for Reach out under the thesis’s score and confidence gates. Comparable performance without EMG on representative effort-sensitive tasks would move the action to Pass unless another bottleneck is evidenced.
