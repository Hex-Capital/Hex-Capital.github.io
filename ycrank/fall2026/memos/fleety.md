## The Idea

- **Problem:** Plumbing and HVAC shops dispatch manually as jobs cancel and technicians run late, and Fleety says a typical customer handles 100+ jobs daily and a shop’s top technician can generate $500K more annually than an average technician ([YC company profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/fleety), [YC launch, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WhC-fleety-the-ai-dispatcher-for-plumbing-and-hvac-companies)).
- **Approach:** Fleety checks call transcripts against equipment records and past notes, assigns technicians using skills, location, availability and close rates, and revises schedules as jobs change ([Fleety website](https://usefleety.com/), [YC launch, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WhC-fleety-the-ai-dispatcher-for-plumbing-and-hvac-companies)).
- **Differentiation:** Fleety sells call validation and revenue-based dispatch as an add-on for existing systems, while ServiceTitan runs revenue-based assignments natively, Housecall Pro combines GPS dispatch with invoicing and payments, and Jobber optimizes routes across its own calendar ([Fleety](https://usefleety.com/), [ServiceTitan](https://marketplace.servicetitan.com/partner/servicetitan-dispatch-pro), [Housecall Pro](https://www.housecallpro.com/features/), [Jobber](https://www.getjobber.com/features/route-optimization/)).
- **Business Model:** Fleety offers demos and sets fees in order forms or subscription agreements, with advance billing by default and no public rates or tiers found ([Fleety website](https://usefleety.com/), [Fleety terms, May 2026](https://usefleety.com/terms-of-service.html)).
- **TAM/SAM:** The broader global field service management market is estimated at $6.26B in 2026 with 9.54% CAGR through 2031, and no public estimate was found for dispatch automation specifically for plumbing and HVAC shops ([Mordor Intelligence, 2026](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/field-service-management-market)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Fleety solicits demos through its CEO’s business email and says its team goes on-site for every deployment ([Fleety website](https://usefleety.com/), [Fleety LinkedIn, Nov 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/company/usefleety/)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Fleety configures service zones and dispatch priorities for individual shops and writes assignments directly to the ServiceTitan dispatch board ([Fleety website](https://usefleety.com/), [YC launch, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WhC-fleety-the-ai-dispatcher-for-plumbing-and-hvac-companies)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Job outcomes and dispatcher overrides could improve technician matching over time, but public materials give no evidence that learning transfers between shops ([Fleety website](https://usefleety.com/)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** ServiceTitan already offers native assignment by sales potential and skills, and Housecall Pro already offers AI call answering and booking ([ServiceTitan Dispatch Pro](https://marketplace.servicetitan.com/partner/servicetitan-dispatch-pro), [Housecall Pro CSR AI, Jul 2026](https://help.housecallpro.com/en/articles/9740104-csr-ai-overview)).

## Key Risks

- **Platform dependence:** [Inferred]: ServiceTitan API or permission changes could disrupt dispatch-board access, while Fleety also lists Housecall Pro and Jobber integrations ([Fleety privacy policy](https://usefleety.com/privacy), [Fleety website](https://usefleety.com/)).
- **Native substitution:** [Inferred]: ServiceTitan customers can buy revenue-based technician matching from their existing platform, while Fleety also offers upstream booking checks against transcripts and equipment records ([ServiceTitan Dispatch Pro](https://marketplace.servicetitan.com/partner/servicetitan-dispatch-pro), [Fleety website](https://usefleety.com/)).
- **Dispatch reliability:** [Inferred]: Incorrect job records or technician scores can send the wrong technician, and Fleety gives dispatchers the ability to approve or adjust recommendations ([YC launch, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WhC-fleety-the-ai-dispatcher-for-plumbing-and-hvac-companies), [Fleety website](https://usefleety.com/)).
- **Brand confusion:** [Inferred]: App-store searches can surface unrelated products named Fleety, including a vehicle-management app from CarInfo ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.cuvora.carinfodealers), [Fleety privacy policy](https://usefleety.com/privacy)).
