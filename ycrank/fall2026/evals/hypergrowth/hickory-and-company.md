# Hickory & Company -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Channel-led or repeatable sales**, through demo-led direct sales, is the strongest path. The proposed first buyer is a bank BSA compliance team that repeatedly gathers evidence and documents investigation decisions. Hickory says its agents retrieve cited evidence and draft case notes using institution-specific policies, with human approval. The memo records demo booking and a bank compliance conference sponsorship and speaker slot. It also records prior use of an internal AI compliance tool at Seis by Hickory’s compliance lead. That experience supports the workflow premise, while external buyer commitment and the route from demo to paid go-live remain unverified. (The Idea, Founders & Team, Key Facts)

If the product achieves fit, the same evidence and review workflow could carry into another market with changes to policy, data access, and hosting. Within an account, additional investigation queues could deepen recurring use. Both are conditional. The memo leaves repeat paid use, expansion, and marginal delivery costs unmeasured. The first link to test is whether a second bank can reach paid production on the same implementation process with bounded effort. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Risks, Key Facts)

### Countercase

Each bank could require custom data access and policy setup, making implementation labor rise with every sale. The terms specify no payment-rail integrations, say the software is unsuitable for submitting consumer financial-account information, and allow hosted model processing outside the customer’s jurisdiction. These constraints could narrow the set of banks that can use Hickory for live investigations, especially where buyers require in-jurisdiction processing. The structural downside gate remains open because the memo contains no rollout evidence showing that eligible banks consistently require a rebuild or proportional delivery effort. (The Idea, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a second independent bank acquired through the demo-led process reach paid production and use Hickory through recurring investigation cycles with the same onboarding playbook as the first, within its data restrictions? Verified payment, case activity, and comparable rollout hours across both banks, with the second rollout led by a non-founder, would move the action to **Reach out**. Evidence that eligible banks consistently require custom integrations and policy work that cannot be reused would move it to **Pass**.
