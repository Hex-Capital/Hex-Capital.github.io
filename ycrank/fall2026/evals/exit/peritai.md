# Perit.AI -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

The strongest route is a strategic sale to a training-data vendor such as Mercor. Perit’s live speech evaluation could extend Mercor’s enterprise workflow evaluation offering. The memo records 29 marketplace listings, and Perit reports delivering more than 6,000 speech hours across nine locales. Those signals make the capability concrete. The economic reason to buy it remains an inference, with buyer savings and willingness to pay unmeasured. (The Idea, Market & Traction)

The proceeds case starts with two unreconciled revenue disclosures covering 4.5 months. Perit reported $11 million or more, and YC cited $9 million or more. A measurable seed-stage milestone is two lab customers renewing paid voice programs at positive contribution margins while maintaining speech QA. Reported delivery volumes and 95% or better accuracy on speech QA samples support a test at the next renewal cycle. The capital required to serve renewals at that quality remains unknown. (Key Facts, Market & Traction)

The shared benchmark assumes a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, 5% ownership diluted to 3%, and no follow-ons. A 5× gross cash return within eight years requires $2.5 million to the holder, or about $83.3 million in distributable equity. The illustrative sale cases assume $10 million in combined net debt and deal deductions, an eligible holder selling the entire stake for cash at closing by year eight, and pro rata distribution with no additional senior preference claims.

| Scenario | Enterprise value to distributable equity | Cash to holder |
|-----------|------------------------------------------|----------------|
| Modest sale | $50M less $10M = $40M | $1.20M |
| Strong sale | $100M less $10M = $90M | $2.70M |
| Prolonged private | Cash reinvested, with no eligible share sale or payout | $0 |

The sale prices are scenario assumptions. Under those deductions, the enterprise value hurdle is about $93.3 million. In the strong case, receiving only 90% of the holder’s proceeds in cash within eight years would reduce realized cash to $2.43 million. Perit’s separate pricing for exclusive rights and its documented consent process make an asset transfer plausible. Customer assignment terms, acquisition rights to contributor data, and holder eligibility remain unverified. The memo supplies no evidence of an independent liquidity route. (The Idea, Key Risks)

### Countercase

A buyer could license the data and source overlapping work from competing vendors, leaving little reason to pay an acquisition premium. Default non-exclusive licensing weakens the case for ownership, and reliance on a small reported lab customer base exposes the value of the programs to renewal risk. The modest sale misses the return hurdle. The evidence leaves both a hurdle-clearing price and a structural block unestablished, so the Pass gate does not trigger. (Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

How much recurring contribution profit can an acquirer retain from Perit’s renewed live-speech programs? Signed renewals, program-level costs including contributor pay and QA, and contract terms showing the programs can survive a change of control would answer it. Move to Reach out if that profit and a defensible buyer valuation support clearing roughly $93.3 million in enterprise value under the scenario assumptions. Pass if the evidence shows the strongest credible sale would leave the holder materially short.
