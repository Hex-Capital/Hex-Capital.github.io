# Kairox Systems -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Assuming strong product-market fit with the initial military maritime buyer, Tempest could address the same recurring job for allied buyers that operate separate aerial and underwater systems. Kairox reports concept validation with U.S. and allied military end users and laboratory component validation. The vehicle design is a plausible basis for reuse, with integrated field performance and the adaptations needed for each buyer still uncertain. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

Kairox offers direct briefings and describes procurement as a future milestone. That gives it an identifiable, high-touch route to buyers. The strongest scaling scenario assumes a standard vehicle and qualification work that later buyers can reuse. The memo records a November 12 public launch and an unquantified “Generating Revenue” classification. Tempest’s realized sales, paid deployments, and commercial growth remain unverified, so initial traction uses the default. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks, Key Facts)

Recurring missions could sustain use. The memo leaves recurring billing and paid expansion within existing accounts open, so retention economics uses the default. Under the assumed hardware-sale model, incremental contribution would be the realized sale price less assembly, testing, buyer-specific sales and onboarding labor, support, and any added production capacity. The memo leaves those inputs unquantified, so cost economics also uses the default. (The Idea, Key Risks, Key Facts)

### Countercase

Even with strong fit for the first buyer, each additional buyer could require separate qualification and hands-on deployment. The reported lab-stage validation and procurement status make that a credible risk. The structural gate stays open because the extent of buyer-specific redesign and the cost of repeat deployments remain unresolved. Required redesign across markets or unavoidable buyer-specific work that makes scaling economically infeasible would trigger the gate. (The Idea, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a second buyer in an allied market pay for and field the same Tempest configuration on economically repeatable terms? A completed paid deployment with records of qualification work reused, buyer-specific changes, and actual incremental costs would answer it. Reuse at positive contribution would support continued Watch while commercial growth develops. Evidence that each market requires substantial redesign, or that unavoidable deployment work makes growth uneconomic without a credible improvement path, would change the action to Pass.
