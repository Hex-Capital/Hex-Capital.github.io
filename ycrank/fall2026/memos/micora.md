## The Idea

- **Problem:** Remote businesses and industrial facilities need electricity where grid infrastructure is unavailable, while diesel requires fuel deliveries and conventional solar requires installation labor ([Micora](https://www.micora.com/), [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/micoraenergy/)).
- **Approach:** Stackable, forklift-placeable solar modules ship alongside a container housing batteries and power electronics, with Micora claiming autonomous installation of 100 kWp daily ([Micora](https://www.micora.com/), [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/micora)).
- **Differentiation:** Micora emphasizes autonomous array deployment, whereas BoxPower describes prefabricated microgrids, Sesame Solar combines mobile solar with hydrogen storage, and OffGridBox integrates electricity with water purification ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/micora), [BoxPower](https://boxpower.io/technology), [Sesame](https://sesame.solar/), [OffGridBox](https://netcapital.com/companies/offgridbox)).
- **Business Model:** The configurator offers purchase and 12- or 24-month leases including engineering, delivery, assembly, and commissioning, with final pricing following site review; a monetary quote was not retrievable ([Micora Order, accessed October 27, 2026](https://www.micora.com/order)).
- **TAM/SAM:** The broader global microgrid market—not Micora’s specific serviceable segment—is estimated at $43.47 billion in 2025, reaching $95.16 billion in 2030 at 17% CAGR; segment-specific SAM: No public data found ([MarketsandMarkets](https://www.marketsandmarkets.com/Market-Reports/micro-grid-electronics-market-917.html)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Micora accepts direct configuration inquiries, and Akemoto’s launch announcement solicits introductions to diesel users, remote operators, and customers lacking sufficient power ([Order](https://www.micora.com/order), [founder announcement reproduced on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/sejunpark_ashten-is-a-real-force-of-nature-extremely-activity-7508692989964247040-W1r9)).
