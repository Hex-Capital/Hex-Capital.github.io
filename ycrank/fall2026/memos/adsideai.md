## The Idea

- **Problem:** Startups and agencies managing paid ads across platforms manually set up tracking, audiences and campaigns, resize creative for each placement, and assemble reports across separate ad managers and specialist tools ([Adside](https://www.adside.ai/), [Launch YC, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/VrT-adside-ai-ai-native-paid-ads-agency)).
- **Approach:** Specialized agents pull data from connected ad accounts and public ad libraries, turn briefs into campaign drafts and creative variants, and monitor performance after launch, with a confirmation step for campaign changes through the agent connector ([Adside](https://www.adside.ai/agents), [Launch YC, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/VrT-adside-ai-ai-native-paid-ads-agency)).
- **Differentiation:** Adside combines competitor research, creative production and cross-channel campaign management with optional human oversight, while Runneth focuses on creative research and briefs and Soku pairs campaign management with SEO and ChatGPT Ads ([Adside comparison](https://www.adside.ai/blog/adside-vs-runneth), [Adside Managed Service](https://www.adside.ai/managed-service), [Nexad](https://nex.ad/)).
- **Business Model:** Self-serve AI Agency costs $990 per brand per month or $490 per month with annual prepayment, includes $100 in monthly creative credits and unlimited users, and the managed service charges 10% of ad spend with a $2,500 monthly minimum ([Adside Pricing, Nov 2026](https://www.adside.ai/pricing)).
- **TAM/SAM:** PW Consulting estimates the broader global online ad management software market at $15.2B in 2025, with 11.6% annualized growth through 2032; no public TAM/SAM data found for the specific AI-managed paid ads segment ([PW Consulting, Jun 2026](https://pmarketresearch.com/worldwide-online-advertising-management-software-market-research/)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Adside reports inbound demand from its own paid campaigns and also distributes a free startup ad library and co-hosts paid-ads workshops for YC companies ([Launch YC, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/VrT-adside-ai-ai-native-paid-ads-agency), [Adside Startup Ad Library](https://www.adside.ai/tools/startup-ad-library), [Luma](https://luma.com/lw0wityu)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Account-specific campaign history, creative assets, performance metrics and preferences personalize outputs, and Adside uses that data within each customer’s account ([Adside Privacy Policy, Oct 2026](https://www.adside.ai/privacy)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Brand-specific campaign history and recurring workflows could raise switching costs if they improve results over time, which Adside’s public product materials have yet to quantify ([Adside agent documentation](https://www.adside.ai/agents), [Adside Privacy Policy](https://www.adside.ai/privacy)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** Google provides an official connector that reduces the custom code needed to access ad data and make supported changes, and Meta offers direct agent access to ad accounts ([Google Ads developer documentation](https://developers.google.com/google-ads/api/docs/developer-toolkit/mcp-server), [Meta Q1 2026 prepared remarks](https://s21.q4cdn.com/399680738/files/doc_financials/2026/q1/META-Q1-2026-Prepared-Remarks.pdf)).

## Key Risks

- **Platform access:** [Inferred]: A platform permission or API change could interrupt campaign operations on that channel because Adside depends on delegated account access and the platform’s terms ([Adside Privacy Policy](https://www.adside.ai/privacy)).
- **Native substitution:** [Inferred]: Meta’s direct agent connector and Google’s native AI campaign creation could reduce demand for standalone tools among single-channel advertisers, while Adside offers consolidated multi-channel operations ([Meta Q1 2026 prepared remarks](https://s21.q4cdn.com/399680738/files/doc_financials/2026/q1/META-Q1-2026-Prepared-Remarks.pdf), [Google Ads announcements, May 2026](https://business.google.com/us/accelerate/announcements/), [Adside](https://www.adside.ai/)).
- **Campaign execution:** [Inferred]: Incorrect agent-generated campaign settings can affect live spend after approval; Adside requires confirmation for changes made through its agent connector ([Adside agent documentation](https://www.adside.ai/agents)).
- **Name confusion:** [Inferred]: AdSide Media’s overlapping name and paid-media services can lead to misattributed coverage; the YC company is identified by adside.ai and its two listed founders ([AdSide Media LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/adsidemedia), [Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/adsideai)).
