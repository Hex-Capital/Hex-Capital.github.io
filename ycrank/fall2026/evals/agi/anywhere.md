# Anywhere -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

New Economies is the strongest path. Anywhere shipped a free iPhone app, and its launch materials describe agents that can retrieve saved places and add pins to a shared map. Today’s workflow can require someone to search chat threads and map lists, then coordinate with friends. The market-opening hypothesis is that agent access cuts human minutes per completed outing enough for groups to make plans they otherwise abandon. The memo contains no before-and-after timing or evidence of newly viable outings. (The Idea, Market & Traction)

Under model parity, the most plausible reason to pay Anywhere is that a group’s accumulated place history and friends’ contributions make each outing easier to plan. The memo documents account-level history. A benefit from each additional friend remains an inference, and retention and willingness to pay are untested. YC reported thousands of first-week signups from one post. The observed App Store rating was 2.8/5 across nine ratings, a small sample. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Facts)

Delaroche’s prior map delivery at Zenly and Life360 and the iPhone launch support execution at this stage. The memo gives no test plan for worldwide 3D performance. An active friend group is a plausible expansion unit, but Anywhere is free and the memo gives no per-group cost data for inference or map services, leaving contribution economics untested. (Founders & Team, The Idea, Key Risks)

### Countercase

Anywhere may make existing planning more convenient without enabling outings that would otherwise be abandoned. Google Maps already handles conversational place discovery, Bump covers shared locations and visits, and standard agent connectors make read/write access easier for competitors to offer. Anywhere also depends on third-party models and map data. The overlap is concrete, while substitution for the full workflow and user switching remain untested. The New Economies path stays open at Watch, and the memo establishes no independently controlled scarce complement. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Will friend groups that previously abandoned outings because of planning effort complete more of them and keep paying for Anywhere when they have access to an alternative with comparably capable AI? A cohort comparison that records human minutes per completed outing, additional outings completed, and repeat payment at a price that covers measured service costs would answer it. Evidence of all three would move the action to Reach out.
