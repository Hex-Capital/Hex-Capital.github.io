# antimattr -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

The strongest route is a strategic sale to an industrial workflow platform that could use Mnemosyne to reduce failed voice commands during hands-free troubleshooting. The IntelliPro integration with Geocarbonite documents a concrete use case. antimattr reports below 5% word error versus 30% on AirPods in its own comparison, with the test protocol undisclosed. A repeatable improvement in command completion on factory floors would give a buyer a reason to own the technology. The integration establishes product fit, while the economic benefit and a buyer’s willingness to pay remain unverified. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

A paid factory rollout that measures failed commands and technician time across shifts is a plausible next milestone. The company reports two enterprise pilots and a first hardware batch that shipped and sold out. Reported investment and grants support an initial test, while pilot payment terms, rollout costs, and the capital required to scale production remain open. The memo leaves IP assignment, supplier obligations, and data permissions unresolved and provides no evidence of an independent liquidity route. (Market & Traction, Key Risks)

The proceeds bridge uses the shared $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, with ownership diluted from 5% to 3% and no follow-ons. The eight-year target is $2.5 million in gross cash, which requires about $83.3 million in distributable equity at 3%. Both sale scenarios assume $5 million in combined net debt and other deal deductions, no further senior claims or preference leakage, and an eligible holder selling their entire stake for pro rata cash within eight years.

| Scenario | Proceeds bridge | Holder cash |
|----------|-----------------|-------------|
| Modest sale | $50M enterprise value less $5M leaves $45M distributable | $1.35M (2.7×) |
| Strong sale | $100M enterprise value less $5M leaves $95M distributable | $2.85M (5.7×) |
| Prolonged private | Cash is reinvested, with no eligible share sale or distribution assumed | $0 realized |

The sale prices are illustrative assumptions. The memo supplies no transaction price or buyer economics to support them. Even in the strong scenario, receiving only 80% of the holder’s consideration in cash within eight years would reduce realized proceeds to $2.28 million, below the target.

### Countercase

A workflow vendor could retain an integration, use another device, or build the controls itself. Apple already offers voice isolation for calls, Subtle applies it to dictation, and Aina offers ring-based agent approvals. antimattr’s reported accuracy advantage lacks a disclosed field protocol, and its reported preorders are a weak indicator of paid demand given the refundable deposit. These facts weaken the case for paying an acquisition premium. The Pass gate remains untriggered because the memo establishes neither a structural closing barrier nor a credible upper bound on holder proceeds. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Would the savings measured in a paid factory rollout justify an acquisition price that clears the holder’s cash hurdle? A buyer-side comparison of acquiring, partnering, and building, grounded in measured reductions in failed commands and technician time, would answer it. A supported price that clears the proceeds bridge would move the action to Reach out. A credible price ceiling below the hurdle for the strongest route would move it to Pass.
