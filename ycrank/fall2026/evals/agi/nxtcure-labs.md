# Nxtcure Labs -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Pass

### Company case

**Building blocks.** Nxtcure applies AI to oncology trial operations. The memo identifies no AI workload whose deployment depends on a scarce input Nxtcure could supply. (*The Idea*)

**Industry enabled.** Conventional CRO work is labor-intensive, and late protocol issues can delay trials. Nxtcure says GalenOps assigns routine tasks to 13 agents and routes consequential decisions to qualified reviewers. The memo records a synthetic-study demo, and the company describes its digital twins as subject to validation. A plausible seed-stage scenario is that automation cuts the fully loaded cost of running a study enough for a budget-constrained oncology sponsor to proceed. That assumes savings survive reviewer time, validation, and rework. The memo identifies no specific study or buyer excluded by the economics of conventional CRO delivery, so the proposed savings support an efficiency case. Both AGI paths score zero, triggering Pass. (*The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks*)

**Shared diligence.** Buyers can already source trial execution and pre-enrollment simulation from separate vendors, and any advantage from combining them remains unproven. The memo records no sponsor commitment for GalenOps. The shipped trial-matching app and synthetic GalenOps demo support early execution credit, with live trial delivery still unverified. Planned per-program pricing supplies neither a realized price nor the cost of delivering the next study. These gaps keep evidence confidence low. (*Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks, Key Facts*)

### Countercase

The strongest case for outreach is that combining protocol simulation with trial execution could make a previously shelved study affordable. The proposed workflow targets two sources of cost: routine manual work and late protocol changes. Even with comparable models, a sponsor could still value a provider that supplies qualified review and takes responsibility for trial operations. The synthetic demo establishes an early test setting. The Pass gate still applies because the memo offers no sponsor-level evidence of an excluded study or the affordability threshold GalenOps would need to cross. (*The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks*)
