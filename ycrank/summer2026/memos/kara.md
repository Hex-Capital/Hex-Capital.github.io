## The Idea

- **Problem:** High-power computing, RF, power-electronics, aerospace, and quantum hardware must remove concentrated heat—NVIDIA cites requirements of up to 120 kW per Blackwell rack—while existing material alternatives include copper, silicon, SiC, and GaN ([NVIDIA, 2025](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/building-the-modular-foundation-for-ai-factories-with-nvidia-mgx/); [Kara](https://www.karalabs.ai/)).
- **Approach:** Kara grows single-crystal diamond in its own reactors and performs growth, processing, and characterization in-house to supply substrates, engineered layers, membranes, heat spreaders, submounts, and custom fabrication ([Kara](https://www.karalabs.ai/)).
- **Differentiation:** Kara reports diamond thermal conductivity of 2,400 W/m·K versus 150 for silicon, 55 for GaAs, 490 for 4H-SiC, and 130 for GaN, alongside a 10 MV/cm breakdown field versus 0.3–3.3 MV/cm for those alternatives ([Kara](https://www.karalabs.ai/)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Specification-based sales of diamond materials plus custom-fabrication services, because Kara asks buyers to begin with a specification and makes product sheets available only under NDA.
- **TAM/SAM:** The broader synthetic single-crystal diamond market was estimated at $2.12 billion in 2025 with an 8.3% forecast CAGR, while the single-crystal-diamond substrate segment was estimated at $10.2 million in 2025 with a 26.6% CAGR through 2031 ([Fortune Business Insights, 2026](https://www.fortunebusinessinsights.com/synthetic-single-crystal-diamond-market-104244); [LPI, 2025](https://www.marketresearchreports.com/lpi/global-single-crystal-diamond-scd-substrates-market-growth-2025-2031)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** [Inferred]: Direct technical sales to semiconductor, quantum, aerospace, and research customers through specification discussions, sampling, qualification, and custom fabrication, based on Kara’s contact-led website and NDA-gated specifications.

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Kara’s public technical-complexity signal is its claim that diamond growth, processing, and characterization occur in-house, but no patents, yield data, or production-capacity figures are disclosed ([Kara](https://www.karalabs.ai/)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Reactor recipes, impurity and isotope control, processing yield, characterization records, and customer-qualified specifications could accumulate into process knowledge, but public sources do not establish that advantage today.
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage, because established suppliers already sell overlapping single-crystal wafers, heat spreaders, and semiconductor-oriented diamond products ([Element Six](https://www.e6.com/en/products/semiconductors); [Diamond Foundry](https://www.df.com/)).
- **Commoditization risk:** More than a dozen firms are developing single-crystal diamond semiconductor wafers, although scaling wafer size and quality remains a documented manufacturing constraint ([Chemical & Engineering News, Jul 2026](https://cen.acs.org/materials/electronic-materials/forget-silicon-diamond-next-semiconductor/104/web/2026/07); [MPACVD review, 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.00223)).

## Key Risks

- **Manufacturing scale and yield:** Large, high-quality single-crystal wafers remain an industry manufacturing constraint, while Kara discloses neither wafer dimensions nor throughput, defect density, yield, or capacity ([MPACVD review, 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.00223); [Kara](https://www.karalabs.ai/)).
- **Qualification and customer validation:** Kara’s specification sheets are under NDA and no public customers, pilots, qualification results, or production shipments were found, while Diamfab describes industrial-size wafers and demonstrator components as prerequisites for adoption ([Kara](https://www.karalabs.ai/); [Bpifrance, Mar 2024](https://presse.bpifrance.fr/diamfab-annonce-une-levee-de-fonds-de-87meur-aupres-de-asterion-ventures-bpifrance-pour-letat-et-du-fonds-regional-avenir-industrie-auvergne-rhone-alpes/?lang=fra)).
