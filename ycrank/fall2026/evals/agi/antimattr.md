# antimattr -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

The strongest AGI path is **scarce complements**. In noisy factories, the ability to capture intelligible speech could become more valuable as technicians use agents for hands-free troubleshooting. The memo documents an IntelliPro integration partnership for that use. Antimattr reports below 5% word error for Mnemosyne versus 30% for AirPods in its own comparison. The test conditions and effect on completed troubleshooting tasks remain unknown. *(The Idea, Defensibility, Key Risks)*

Antimattr’s proprietary voice isolation and dedicated ring give it a specific product design to defend. Its CEO reports more than 2,000 preorders and two enterprise pilots, and the company reports that its first batch sold out. The listed reservation deposit is refundable, and pilot payment terms and repeat orders are unknown. The reported first shipment, alongside the founders’ voice-agent and robotics experience, supports seed-stage execution. For industrial rollout, the expansion unit would be a device per technician. Listed prices provide a revenue reference, while production yields, landed unit costs, and support costs remain unknown. *(The Idea, Market & Traction, Founders & Team, Key Risks)*

### Countercase

Subtle already offers voice-isolating dictation earbuds, and Aina offers ring-based agent controls. If readily available alternatives deliver comparable troubleshooting outcomes with the same agent backend, antimattr’s design may command little pricing power. The memo leaves field parity and switching costs unresolved, so the substitution evidence supports Watch. A New Economies case would require evidence that lower labor cost per successful repair makes a previously uneconomic use viable. The memo leaves both the prior cost and the change in cost per repair unmeasured. *(The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)*

### Decisive diligence question

Will an independent factory customer place a paid expansion order after a head-to-head pilot? Hold the agent backend constant and compare Mnemosyne with the best available substitute on successful hands-free troubleshooting across representative shifts. A material, repeatable advantage followed by a paid expansion would move the action to Reach out. Comparable outcomes from a readily available substitute at comparable or lower cost would move it to Pass.
