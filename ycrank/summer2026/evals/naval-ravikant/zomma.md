# Zomma -- Naval Ravikant Evaluation

Jihyun Kim helped launch a $1B multi-strategy hedge fund, giving Zomma unusually strong specific knowledge but no network effects. That high-low pairing defines Zomma: domain-native automation sold account by account, not a platform whose users compound one another. Zomma can become valuable by replacing financial-operations labor, but the present geometry points toward a good vertical enterprise business rather than a 10,000x network.

**Stack Overflow** is the high rung for specific knowledge: developers built for developers after living the problem. Zomma shares that mechanism because Kim operated inside Goldman Sachs, Endowus, and Arrowpoint, while Varun Arumugam built verification infrastructure at Apple and Cashel Fitzgerald built vision systems at Modern Intelligence. Zomma diverges because no founder has disclosed a public repository, long-running technical artifact, or prior computer-use product adopted at scale. The knowledge is real; its uniqueness is not yet proven.

**Perplexity** is the low rung for compounding user-added value: each additional user does not directly improve the product for prior users, so exceptional technical knowledge and permissionless distribution had to override the missing network. Zomma has the same structural absence without the override. Unnamed hedge funds and asset managers use Zomma independently, while no shared marketplace, developer ecosystem, cross-customer dataset, or user-generated workflow network is established. Customer-specific workflow maps may create switching costs for Zomma, but captivity is not compounding.

Zomma distributes through direct sales calls, forward deployment, workshops, partnerships, and negotiated contracts. Over 18 months, Zomma’s code may execute more work inside each account, but every new regulated institution still requires security review, workflow mapping, credential integration, and human approval design. That is code leverage trapped inside labor distribution. Anthropic and OpenAI also supply the underlying computer-use capability, so Zomma cannot treat model access as proprietary leverage.

The $7.78B financial-services agent market gives Zomma room beyond reconciliation into onboarding, KYC, investor reporting, wealth operations, and broader fintech administration. Zomma would move into a higher tier if one common workflow product could cross these categories without bespoke deployment—for example, a self-configuring approval and audit layer adopted across custodians. Without that proof, the narrower $1.97B BFSI RPA segment and investment-operations positioning provide several pivots, but not an obviously platform-shaped market.

**Twitter** paired shipping ability with a legible history among founders who could play a long-term game. Zomma shows intelligence and energy through Kim’s Arrowpoint launch, Arumugam’s founding-ML role at Bronco AI, and Fitzgerald’s work on systems supporting $20B in annual transaction volume. Zomma has no disclosed integrity concern, but no public source explains how the three founders met or demonstrates that they have endured a difficult cycle together. Silence on integrity is neutral; silence on partnership durability caps the score.

The $7.78B market and Kim’s operating history lead to a specific forecast: by July 2029, Zomma will reach $4M–$8M ARR while remaining an independent, forward-deployed vendor concentrated in investment operations rather than becoming a horizontal computer-use platform. Zomma’s domain knowledge should win complex accounts, but its missing network and labor-heavy distribution should make growth linear—customer by customer, workflow by workflow.

Anthropic’s general agents and Zalos’s $3.6M financing create the strongest bull case: finance-specific reliability, approval gates, correction histories, and audit records could turn regulation into a moat, as permission became a moat for **Anchorage Digital**. Zomma could then own the trusted execution layer while frontier laboratories commoditize clicking. The case fails today because Zomma’s SOC 2 remains in progress, no exclusive dataset or retention evidence exists, and Zalos, Eloquent AI, and Stacks already pursue overlapping workflows. I would flip to Invest if Zomma verifies at least $2M ARR, deployment under two weeks, and more than half of new customers arriving without founder-led outbound sales.
