# Terse -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

The strongest path is a strategic acquisition by a developer infrastructure platform seeking to add durable shared state for AI applications. Terse documents self-hosting and atomic commits of persisted fields and SQLite writes. A buyer could use those capabilities to win workloads that require deployment control and consistent shared state. Terse’s founder reported a 60.1% reduction in cold-start latency on a stateful read benchmark. The economic benefit to a buyer remains unmeasured. (*The Idea*, *Market & Traction*)

The next milestone is sustained paid production usage with measured reliability and operating costs. Doop’s creator reported a migration in progress, and the memo records $500,000 in YC financing. Those are early signals that Terse can test the milestone. Paid conversion, retention, burn, and any additional capital needed remain unknown. The launched cloud service makes a sale of the hosted business plausible, with IP ownership, contract assignment, and approvals still to verify. (*The Idea*, *Market & Traction*)

The benchmark assumes a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, 5% starting ownership diluted to 3%, and no follow-ons. The 5× objective is $2.5 million in cash within eight years, requiring about $83.3 million in distributable equity value at 3%. The sale cases below are illustrative. Both assume $5 million in combined net debt and deal deductions, a sale of 100% of the holder’s eligible shares, a pro rata waterfall with no preference diversion, and full cash payment at closing within eight years.

| Scenario | Distributable equity value | Cash to holder |
|---|---:|---:|
| Modest sale at $30M enterprise value | $25M | $750,000 (1.5×) |
| Strong sale at $100M enterprise value | $95M | $2.85M (5.7×) |
| Prolonged private operation with cash reinvested | No distribution or eligible share sale assumed | $0 |

At 2.5% ownership, even the strong sale yields about $2.38 million, short of the objective. The memo documents usage-based pricing and early activity. Achievable sale value and an independent liquidity route remain unevidenced. (*The Idea*, *Market & Traction*, *Key Facts*)

### Countercase

A platform can use the MIT-licensed runtime or integrate a substitute. Rivet already offers self-hosted actors with SQLite and WebSockets, and Cloudflare bundles persistent state and WebSockets into Workers. Terse’s reported coordination of 32,752 VM launches came from a demo, leaving repeat paid demand unverified. These facts weaken the case for a strategic premium. The Pass gate remains untriggered because the memo establishes neither a structural barrier to a sale nor a supported ceiling on holder proceeds. (*The Idea*, *Defensibility*, *Market & Traction*, *Key Risks*)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a specific infrastructure platform quantify enough incremental value from owning Terse’s hosted business to support at least $83.3 million in distributable equity value? A buyer-backed estimate grounded in retained paid workloads and measured operating savings would move the action to Reach out if it supports that hurdle. A supported valuation materially below the hurdle even under the strongest credible sale case would move the action to Pass.
