# Verdun -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Verdun’s strongest path is channel-led sales through security dealers and integrators serving multi-site dealership groups. These buyers have a recurring job: review alarms from existing cameras and intervene when needed. Verdun offers white-label monitoring billed monthly per camera. If a dealer can reuse the same setup across sites, one sale could expand to more cameras and locations. Existing-camera compatibility makes the workflow plausible across markets. Camera access, video retention, and local response arrangements still need to be tested in a second market. (The Idea, Key Risks)

The memo records an anonymized screenshot showing alarm activity across customer sites. Verdun reports that AI closed about 85% of alerts in a 14-day sample. That suggests a way to limit operator workload, while operator hours, inference costs, and threat recall at higher volumes remain unmeasured in the memo. The screenshot shows activity, while buyer commitment, dealer-led paid conversion, and subsequent paid expansion remain unverified. For a Fall 2026 launch, the earliest test is whether a dealer can turn a parallel trial into paid monitoring at the incumbent contract’s expiry using a repeatable setup. (Market & Traction, The Idea, Key Risks)

### Countercase

Verdun could win customers who value faster response and still grow like a labor-intensive monitoring service. Each site needs its own response protocols and camera access, and uncertain alerts still require staffed review. A high automated closure rate could coexist with substantial setup effort or staffing needs during spikes. The structural downside gate remains open because the memo provides no rollout comparison showing that those costs rise in proportion to paid camera volume. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a dealer take two independent dealership groups from parallel trial to paid go-live at incumbent contract expiry using the same onboarding steps? Two paid conversions with documented setup hours and a dealer-led handoff would move Verdun to Reach out.
