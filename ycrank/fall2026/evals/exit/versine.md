# Versine -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

The strongest path is a strategic acquisition by a consumer payments platform such as PayPal or Block. Versine says its product lets assistants initiate person-to-person transfers under passkey approval or standing rules and send bank claim links to recipients without accounts. A wallet operator could buy that workflow if it brings in repeat payment volume the operator would otherwise miss. The memo records 182 Product Hunt upvotes and 5+ Android downloads. Those observations establish launch visibility. Repeat transfers, incremental volume, and buyer interest remain unverified. (*The Idea*; *Defensibility*; *Market & Traction*)

A live product makes a partner pilot plausible at seed. The relevant milestone is repeat, completed assistant-initiated transfers after the $100 launch credit ends. The memo’s $125,000 seed entry dates to the earlier sign-in product, leaving the payments pilot’s budget and runway unclear. (*The Idea*; *Market & Traction*)

**Proceeds scenarios.** Assume a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, 5% initial ownership diluted to 3%, and zero follow-ons. The 5× gross cash objective requires $2.5 million within eight years, or about $83.3 million in distributable equity. Both sale scenarios assume the holder is eligible, sells its entire stake by year eight, and receives its pro rata share in cash at closing. Sale prices, deductions, and waterfall terms are illustrative.

| Scenario | Enterprise value less assumed debt and deal adjustments | Holder cash |
|---|---:|---:|
| Modest sale | $30M − $5M = $25M distributable equity | $750K (1.5×) |
| Strong sale | $100M − $10M = $90M distributable equity | $2.7M (5.4×) |
| Prolonged private operation | Cash is reinvested through year eight | $0 (0×) |

At the strong-case price, $20 million in deductions would reduce holder cash to $2.4 million (4.8×). The memo’s free standard transfers and $1 instant-payout fee leave buyer economics and achievable sale prices unverified. The memo also leaves Bridge continuity after a sale, holder participation terms, and an independent liquidity route unevidenced. (*The Idea*; *Key Risks*)

### Countercase

A wallet incumbent could build assistant initiation on top of its existing transfer and approval systems. Cash App already offers request links, and Stripe Link shows that a payments platform can add assistant authorization in merchant checkout. Bridge dependence also limits the value of buying the workflow if identity verification and bank payouts cannot continue after closing. These are credible reasons a buyer might decline to pay, but the memo establishes neither a closing block nor a supported sale-price range that falls materially short. The Pass gate remains open, and the unverified buy-versus-build economics warrant Watch. (*Market & Traction*; *Defensibility*; *Key Risks*)
