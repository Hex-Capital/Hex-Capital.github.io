# machine0 -- Sam Lessin Evaluation

Barnaby Malet published cloud-resource-allocation research, built VM tooling, and previously took Upflow through a $15M Series A—machine0 is a missionary infrastructure operator trapped inside a consensus infrastructure trade. **Drop.io** is the high rung on founder conviction: I kept turning the crank against free incumbents because the underlying problem had become personal. Malet’s years in cloud systems and his late-2025 observation that coding sessions were stretching to 6–8 hours give machine0 the same operator-before-pitch mechanism. The divergence is that machine0’s current thesis followed a recent pivot from Director, while the machine0 NixOS repository has only 13 stars and no disclosed usage proving that Malet will persist through Type 2 fun rather than execute another intelligent tack.

**Robinhood** is the low rung on sleeve fit: generic fintech was outside my revealed crypto, DTC, creator, and Wizard-of-Oz sleeves, and only an early enough entry price made the exception work. machine0 sits even closer to the anti-pattern because “cloud computers for AI agents” is almost a perfect 2026 consensus sentence. machine0 resells DigitalOcean compute into a field containing E2B, Daytona, Modal, and RunPod; persistence and NixOS are product choices, not yet an asset-class discontinuity like Solana. AI infrastructure is no longer investible—fine, that is provocative overstatement, but the operational point is that machine0 offers commodity exposure without a sleeve-specific reason for me to own it.

**E2B’s approximately $32M and Daytona’s approximately $31M of funding** make the round-dynamics mechanism unfavorable even before machine0’s price is known. Over the next 18 months, those balance sheets can copy suspendable VMs, static IPs, snapshots, and agent images while machine0 spends each financing tack defending gross margin on rented DigitalOcean capacity. machine0 provides no round valuation, visible tier-one pass, or unusual instrument, so I apply the 9/30 absent-evidence default: a Summer 2026 AI-infrastructure SAFE is presumed consensus until the cap table proves otherwise. The Show HN launch—96 points and 37 comments—shows curiosity, not mispricing.

**$0.013 per CPU-hour and $0.836 per GPU-hour** could support a capital-efficient machine0, but only if recurring suspended workloads generate enough contribution margin to make machine0 default-alive before another institutional round. The tier flips if machine0 can finance capacity from customer prepayments or structure the round around compute revenue rather than a generic SaaS SAFE. Today machine0 has no disclosed revenue, customer count, utilization, or gross margin, while DigitalOcean controls upstream pricing and availability. That makes the regatta legible: multiple infrastructure and demand-validation tacks remain before Series A, with no downside cushion yet visible.

**John Fish / Bookshelved** had distribution that was inseparable from the asset being financed; machine0 has founder reach, but not founder-owned demand. Barnaby Malet’s roughly 2,000 LinkedIn followers produced 299 reactions and 112 comments, yet machine0 converted that attention into only 13 GitHub stars on its principal image repository. machine0’s 96-point Show HN launch is rented event distribution rather than a community that compounds every week. Malet is credible enough to attract an initial audience, but machine0 is not a creator dressed as a startup and the audience is not presently on the cap table.

**DigitalOcean supplies machine0’s underlying VMs**, so my falsifiable 36-month forecast is that by July 2029 machine0 will have pivoted up-stack into agent-workspace governance and orchestration—closer to the former Director product—and will report $1M–$3M ARR rather than remain a standalone persistent-compute cloud. machine0’s strongest asset is Malet’s domain conviction; machine0’s weakest is category ownership. Raw compute will compress toward a feature, while snapshots, credentials, policy, and long-running-agent controls offer Malet a more legible enterprise asset to finance.

**The 96-point Show HN launch and Malet’s prior $15M-Series-A operating record** make the strongest bull case sting: machine0 may be a **Pinterest**-style known exception where missionary temperament overrides an unappealing category, and persistent 6–8-hour agent sessions may become a workload boundary incumbents misread. machine0 could also turn DigitalOcean into an asset-light Wizard-of-Oz layer, learning from manually supported workloads before automating orchestration. The dossier still loses that argument because machine0 discloses no customer, revenue, repeat usage, or moat while four funded substitutes already occupy the story. I would flip to Invest if machine0 reaches $25K MRR from persistent-agent workloads without raising another institutional round.
