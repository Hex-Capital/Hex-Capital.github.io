# Forward -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path B — New economies** is Forward’s strongest path. It packages deal documents, matches them against current lender mandates, and coordinates submissions and follow-up. Forward claims AI can handle lender discovery and routine coordination. If that materially reduces labor per funded loan, advisors could place smaller commercial real estate loans they currently decline to broker. That is a scenario assumption. The memo contains no like-for-like labor comparison or evidence of funded loans from that newly served segment. Forward’s San Francisco base and reported pre-seed raise fit the strategy’s geography and stage. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Facts)

The strongest direct customer evidence is Capital Partners’ account of reaching unfamiliar lenders and securing term sheets. Forward reports that 100 U.S. funds signed agreements to pay a fee on funded loans. Two reported deals were still progressing toward close. Processed and originated volumes leave funded volume unknown, and the reported $180,000 annualized and $545,000 contracted revenue figures have different bases, with currency and cash collections unspecified. The founders bring private-credit legal, fund diligence, and AI workflow experience. The controlled rollout shows initial execution, with repeat delivery across advisors still unproven. (Market & Traction, Key Facts, Founders & Team, Key Risks)

At the stated 25 basis points, each $1 million funded generates $2,500 in gross fees, assuming collection. The unit to test is funded volume per additional advisor. Contribution depends on whether those fees cover inference, credit review, exceptions, and coordination across all submissions, including deals that never close. Funded conversion and delivery cost remain unmeasured in the memo. (The Idea, Key Risks, Key Facts)

### Countercase

Forward may make existing brokerage work faster while opening few new transactions. With equally capable models, competitors can replicate document packaging and routine outreach, and existing products already offer lender discovery and financing workflows. Forward’s reported exclusivity with its first Southern California advisor gives it a specific distribution foothold. The scope and duration of that relationship and any improvement in match quality as the network grows remain unknown. Even with abundant capital, current lender mandates and lender attention could still be scarce, but the memo provides no separate evidence that Forward controls either, leaving Path A unsupported. The evidence leaves both Path B conditions unproven. That blocks Reach out, while a comparative delivery trial makes Watch appropriate. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can Forward repeatedly fund smaller loans that multiple independent advisors previously declined to broker, at positive contribution and with lenders still choosing Forward at its 25-basis-point fee when comparable AI-assisted alternatives are available? A matched trial should track funded outcomes at comparable quality, all human time including exceptions and failed deals, and fees actually collected. Repeat incremental closings with positive contribution and demonstrated preference under model parity would support Reach out if the updated score reaches 65 with at least medium confidence. Evidence that the workflow opens no new transactions or that buyers readily switch under parity would move the action to Pass on the current record.
