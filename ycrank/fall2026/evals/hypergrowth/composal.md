# Composal -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path A, product-led adoption,** is strongest for Verify. The initial buyer is an engineering team that needs to check critical user journeys before each release. Verify documents a discovery scan before checkout, a $500 monthly Team plan, and reusable browser scenarios. If the scan finds a failure worth fixing, the team has a reason to subscribe, rerun those scenarios on later releases, and buy more browser minutes as coverage grows. Composal reports finding more than 1,500 bugs in customer apps. That is an early signal of possible value, with paid conversion, customer-level outcomes, and repeat use still unknown. Sparks is on a beta waitlist and provides weaker evidence for a growth path. (The Idea, Market & Traction)

The same release-checking workflow could serve engineering teams outside the UK. The first portability test is whether those teams can give Verify access to their apps through its existing test-account and private-network options. Each new account may require setup and support time, while each additional run adds browser and model compute. At full use of the 3,000 included minutes, the listed subscription price works out to about $0.17 in revenue per browser minute before costs. Purchased add-ons are listed at $1 per minute. Actual utilization, add-on purchases, and delivery costs are unknown, so contribution at higher volume remains untested. (The Idea, Key Risks, Key Facts)

### Countercase

Composal says five of ten setup calls stalled on authentication or network controls. It also reports that nine teams it spoke with had built QA agents in-house. The strongest countercase is that the teams with the most urgent need already have a workable substitute, while winning the rest requires hands-on access work for each account. That supports Watch. The setup results expose a concrete bottleneck, and the existing access options leave open whether a standard rollout can resolve it. The memo does not establish the structural ceiling required for Pass. (Defensibility, Key Risks)

Can a small cohort of independent engineering teams outside the UK move from the discovery scan to paid Verify use across successive releases through the existing access setup? Reach out once account records show that sequence with measured setup time, support time, and compute costs consistent with positive contribution at the price paid. (The Idea, Key Risks, Key Facts)
