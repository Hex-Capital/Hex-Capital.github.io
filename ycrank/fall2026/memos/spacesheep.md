## The Idea

- **Problem:** Teams using AI coding agents receive long chat responses and locally generated files that colleagues must read, reformat, or share separately to review the work ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WK8-spacesheep-documents-of-the-future), [HackrLife, Oct 2026](https://www.hackrlife.com/p/spacesheep-share-agent-output)).
- **Approach:** Agents publish documents, mockups, live dashboards, or apps through an MCP connector or CLI to a hosted link where teammates can comment on specific elements, request live revisions from the built-in agent, and search past work by meaning ([Spacesheep setup guide](https://spacesheep.dev/start), [Spacesheep](https://spacesheep.dev/)).
- **Differentiation:** Spacesheep gives Claude Code, Codex, and Cursor a common publishing destination, while Claude Artifacts and ChatGPT Sites publish through their respective product ecosystems, Type.com centers on shared skills and automations, and Gamma centers on editable presentations and branded templates ([Spacesheep](https://spacesheep.dev/), [Anthropic](https://claude.com/features/artifacts), [OpenAI](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001339-creating-and-using-chatgpt-sites), [Type.com](https://type.com/blog/launch), [Gamma product coverage](https://siliconangle.com/2026/10/06/exclusive-gamma-revamps-presentation-platform-to-make-output-look-more-human/)).
- **Business Model:** Free tier, Pro at $4.50/month, Max at $9/month, Team at $10/member/month, and contact sales for Enterprise, with higher tiers adding model allowances, larger deployments, or shared team controls ([Spacesheep Plans, Nov 2026](https://spacesheep.dev/plans)).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** Spacesheep offers self-serve signup and browser extensions, asks users to share their first space with teammates, solicits design-partner introductions, and offers YC founders a free year of Team for up to 15 members ([Spacesheep Extensions](https://spacesheep.dev/extension), [Launch YC, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WK8-spacesheep-documents-of-the-future)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** No defensibility signals found in public sources.

## Key Risks

- **Native substitution:** Claude Artifacts and ChatGPT Sites let teams generate and publish inside existing agent products, while Spacesheep supports publishing from multiple competing agents ([Anthropic](https://claude.com/features/artifacts), [OpenAI](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001339-creating-and-using-chatgpt-sites), [Spacesheep](https://spacesheep.dev/)).
- **Connector access:** Claude Code on the web restricts access to Spacesheep’s domains by default, with a Claude connector or environment allowlisting documented as setup paths ([Spacesheep setup guide](https://spacesheep.dev/start)).
- **Data governance:** Connected agents and coding-session hooks can send conversation content to Spacesheep, which documents revocable connections, scoped agent access, and Enterprise controls for public links and integrations ([Spacesheep Privacy](https://spacesheep.dev/privacy), [Spacesheep Security](https://spacesheep.dev/security), [Spacesheep Plans](https://spacesheep.dev/plans)).
- **Name confusion:** An AI chatbot vendor at `spacesheep.ai` and a game on Steam share the name, while YC identifies `spacesheep.dev` as the startup’s domain ([SpaceSheep.ai](https://spacesheep.ai/about), [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3528930/SPACESHEEP/), [Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/spacesheep)).
