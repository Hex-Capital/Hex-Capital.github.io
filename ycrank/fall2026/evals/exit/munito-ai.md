# Munito AI -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Strategic acquisition is Munito’s strongest supported route. Opper AI’s live EU inference integration and RouterPlus’s launch of Aleph Alpha’s Kolibri 1 through Munito show that gateways can distribute its serving layer. Munito says its EU endpoint keeps inference and explicitly stored content in the EU and defaults to zero retention for transient requests. A gateway or inference platform could buy Munito to bring that capability in-house and capture the margin on recurring workloads. The memo leaves paid volumes, buyer savings, and the economics of ownership unquantified. (*The Idea*, *Market & Traction*)

A seed-stage milestone is 12 months of repeat paid EU production usage with positive gross profit after GPU rental. The 12-month window is an assumption. The launched service and the founder’s prior inference experience support running that test. The reported $500,000 convertible note leaves available runway and incremental capital needs unclear. Munito’s customer-hosted option suggests the serving layer can run outside its rented setup. IP ownership, model rights, and contract consents would still need verification for a sale. (*Market & Traction*, *Founders & Team*, *Key Risks*)

Under the shared benchmark, a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation starts at 5% ownership and dilutes to 3% with no follow-ons. The eight-year objective is $2.5 million in gross cash, requiring about $83.3 million in distributable equity value at 3%. The memo gives no conversion or waterfall terms for the reported note. The sale scenarios below assume $5 million in combined net debt and deal deductions, no further senior claims on the remaining equity, and an eligible holder selling its entire stake for cash within eight years. The prices and deductions are illustrative. (*Market & Traction*)

| Scenario | Enterprise value to distributable equity | Gross cash to holder |
|---|---|---|
| Modest sale | $50M less $5M gives $45M | $1.35M (2.7×) |
| Strong sale | $100M less $5M gives $95M | $2.85M (5.7×) |
| Prolonged private | Cash is reinvested and no shares are sold | $0 realized |

The strong case clears the hurdle under full cash participation. If only 75% of the holder’s stake is cashed out within eight years at that same price, realized proceeds fall to about $2.14 million. The memo supplies no supported acquisition price range or independent route to holder liquidity. (*Market & Traction*, *Key Facts*)

### Countercase

A buyer can access regional inference through a commercial partnership or use an existing substitute. Together AI offers dedicated EU inference, Mistral offers regional inference for its own models, and Nebius serves an overlapping set of open models. Standard SDK interfaces lower switching costs. Munito reports internal serving benchmarks, with no measured savings on customer workloads in the memo. The live integrations therefore establish distribution while leaving the economic case for buying Munito open. The evidence establishes neither a structural closing block nor materially inadequate proceeds under the strongest credible route, so the Pass gate is not triggered. (*The Idea*, *Defensibility*, *Market & Traction*)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a funded inference platform justify paying enough to acquire Munito for the holder to receive $2.5 million in cash within eight years? Evidence of retained paid gateway workloads and gross profit after GPU rental, combined with a buyer’s estimate of the cost and time to build an equivalent service, could support a price range. A supported range that clears the holder’s hurdle after deal deductions and the waterfall would move the action to **Reach out**.
