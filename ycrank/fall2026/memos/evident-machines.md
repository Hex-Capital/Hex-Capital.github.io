## The Idea

- **Problem:** [Inferred]: Frontier AI labs need to measure electrical-engineering research judgment beyond written answers, with simulation benchmarks such as EEBench providing an existing alternative; the frequency and cost of inadequate evaluation are undisclosed ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/evident-machines); [EEBench methodology](https://www.eebench.org/methodology.html)).
- **Approach:** Evident Machines converts research and engineering projects into evaluations grounded in computation, simulation, and physical experimentation ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/evident-machines)).
- **Differentiation:** Evident explicitly includes physical experimentation, whereas EEBench’s current version concentrates on simulation and METR’s RE-Bench evaluates machine-learning research engineering ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/evident-machines); [EEBench](https://www.eebench.org/methodology.html); [METR paper](https://metr.org/AI_R_D_Evaluation_Report.pdf)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Custom evaluation engagements or benchmark licensing to frontier labs are possible monetization paths given the stated customer segment; no public pricing was found ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/evident-machines)).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** [Inferred]: Founder-led outreach to frontier-lab evaluation teams is a possible distribution path given the stated audience and Jain’s previous Gemini for Science role ([YC founder profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/founders/trishala-jain)).

## Key Risks

- **Technical validity:** [Inferred]: Evaluation scores may fail to predict physical outcomes unless experiments validate them; Evident states physical experimentation is part of its approach, but no public validation results were found ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/evident-machines)).
- **Subsidized substitution:** [Inferred]: Buyers can consult benchmarks financed through adjacent businesses, because atopile funds EEBench itself and says it does not sell benchmark scores ([EEBench methodology](https://www.eebench.org/methodology.html)).
- **Benchmark contamination:** [Inferred]: Leaked tasks or rapid model improvement could reduce benchmark usefulness, a mechanism Vals describes and addresses through privately held test sets; Evident’s holdout policy is undisclosed ([Vals, August 2026](https://www.vals.ai/blogs/series-a)).
- **Product verification:** Website not accessible at time of research on October 27, 2026, preventing verification of pricing, demonstrations, and published results ([company website](https://evidentmachines.com/)).
