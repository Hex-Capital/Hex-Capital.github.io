## The Idea

- **Problem:** Affordable-housing operators must verify income and eligibility for move-ins and recertifications, with manual reviews and external consultants contributing to delays and vacancy costs ([YC launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SBA-tire-swing-ai-compliance-for-affordable-housing)).
- **Approach:** Tire Swing classifies household documents, extracts income and asset information, applies program rules, requests missing information, and presents source-linked findings for staff approval ([Platform](https://www.gettireswing.com/platform)).
- **Differentiation:** Tire Swing emphasizes document-based certification review across existing workflows, while Yardi Verification Services retrieves income and asset information directly through payroll and banking networks ([Tire Swing](https://www.gettireswing.com/), [Yardi](https://www.yardi.com/news/press-releases/yardi-partners-novacredit/)).
- **Business Model:** Subscription fees and billing cycles are specified in individual order forms or invoices; public prices, tiers, and charging units: No public data found ([Terms](https://www.gettireswing.com/terms_of_service)).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** Founder-led demonstrations use an anonymized prospect file, and Paul Witten publicly described sponsoring and attending AHF Live to meet affordable-housing operators ([Website](https://www.gettireswing.com/), [LinkedIn, via search snippet](https://www.linkedin.com/in/paul-witten)).

## Key Risks

- **Certification errors:** The terms disclaim error-free extraction and compliance determinations and require qualified human approval, making reviewer oversight a documented operating requirement ([Terms](https://www.gettireswing.com/terms_of_service)).
- **Regulatory maintenance:** [Inferred]: Changes to income, asset, and reporting requirements require continued rule maintenance; property-specific configuration provides a mitigation ([HUD](https://www.hud.gov/sites/default/files/hudclips/documents/PIH-2026-15.pdf), [Website](https://www.gettireswing.com/)).
- **Sensitive-data exposure:** [Inferred]: Processing tenant identity and financial documents creates breach exposure; the company reports encryption, role-based access, and SOC 2 Type II certification ([Privacy](https://www.gettireswing.com/privacy_policy), [Security](https://www.gettireswing.com/security)).
- **Incumbent substitution:** [Inferred]: Yardi’s integrated income/asset verification can substitute for part of the workflow within an existing vendor relationship ([Yardi](https://www.yardi.com/news/press-releases/yardi-partners-novacredit/)).
