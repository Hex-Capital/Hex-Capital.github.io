# Topos -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path B — New Economies.** Topos’s strongest case is making early hypothesis assessment affordable for research teams that currently skip it when literature review and expert judgment take too much time. That buyer segment and its unmet demand are scenario assumptions. Topos says Egregore coordinates thousands of agents and emphasizes formalizing and checking their reasoning. The memo records a beta access-request form. Buyer commitments, measured labor savings, and repeat use remain unverified. (The Idea, Market & Traction)

The relevant outcome is an assessment a researcher can use to decide whether to pursue a hypothesis. Compare Egregore with manual review on similar work, measuring expert hours and full cost per accepted assessment, including compute, retries, exception handling, and any empirical validation needed for acceptance. If the savings make previously skipped assessments economical, the remaining gate is whether buyers still pay Topos when alternatives have access to equally capable models. The founders’ joint Lean formalization and prior applied ML prototype support their ability to run a seed-stage test. Cost per accepted assessment at repeat volume remains unknown. (The Idea, Founders & Team, Key Risks)

### Countercase

Egregore could speed up initial review while leaving enough work with experts and experiments that teams still skip the same assessments. The memo identifies coordination overhead as a risk for sequential tasks. It also documents competing orchestration and research tools, which makes orchestration alone a weak basis for durable value capture. Buyer preference under comparable functionality remains untested. The evidence leaves both Path B gates open and supports Watch. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Will an independent research team pay for repeat Egregore assessments it previously skipped on cost grounds after trying a functionally comparable alternative with access to equally capable models? Move to Reach out if a representative trial shows that Egregore reduces expert hours and full cost per accepted assessment enough to make the work economical, and the buyer reorders at a viable price for a specific benefit Topos delivers. Stay on Watch if the result is inconclusive. Move to Pass if the trial shows the work remains uneconomical or buyers can switch and retain the outcome.
