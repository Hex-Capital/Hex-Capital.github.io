## The Idea

- **Problem:** Operations, support, and sales teams lose undocumented procedures when employees leave, relying on handover documents and shadowing; the founders describe a departure that disrupted their previous employer for months ([YC launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Vn7-godhands-agents-that-learn-by-watching-your-best-people)).
- **Approach:** A desktop application observes work across applications, converts steps and exceptions into sourced playbooks, and supplies that knowledge to people and agents without requiring application integrations ([GodHands, via search snippet](https://godhands.ai/)).
- **Differentiation:** GodHands emphasizes learning decisions and exceptions for playbooks and agents, versus [Tango’s](https://www.tango.ai/) screenshot guides and [Mimica’s](https://www.mimica.ai/articles/mimica-secures-26-2-million-in-series-b-funding-to-train-agentic-ai-on-real-enterprise-workflows) process maps, while [Scribe](https://scribe.com/library/100-million-arr) already offers overlapping workflow context and agents ([GodHands positioning](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/godhands)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Enterprise subscriptions or deployment contracts are plausible given the demo-led sales process and private deployment options; published prices: No public data found ([GodHands, via search snippet](https://godhands.ai/)).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** The launch solicits demos and introductions to operations leaders, support executives, and private-equity operating partners ([YC launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Vn7-godhands-agents-that-learn-by-watching-your-best-people)).

## Key Risks

- **Execution-scope ambiguity:** Marketing describes agents performing work, while indexed security text limits agents to reading playbooks and asking questions; autonomous execution availability is therefore unclear ([GodHands, via search snippet](https://godhands.ai/)).
- **Employee trust and sensitive-data exposure:** The CEO identified replacement anxiety as an adoption obstacle; the company advertises device-side redaction, application controls, and configurable retention as safeguards ([Entrepreneur India](https://india.entrepreneur.com/business-news/the-iit-founder-building-ais-missing-memory-layer); [GodHands, via search snippet](https://godhands.ai/)).
- **Competitive substitution:** [Inferred]: Buyers could obtain overlapping workflow knowledge and agent features from Scribe or Mimica rather than adopting another platform ([Scribe](https://scribe.com/library/100-million-arr); [Mimica](https://www.mimica.ai/articles/mimica-secures-26-2-million-in-series-b-funding-to-train-agentic-ai-on-real-enterprise-workflows)).
