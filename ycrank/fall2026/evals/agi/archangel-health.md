# Archangel Health -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path A — Scarce complements.** Clinical AI developers need to verify that an agent actually completes a workflow. The memo describes a concrete failure that static tests can miss. An agent can document an intended order without placing it in the electronic health record. Archangel describes sandboxed records, physician grading, and multi-visit tasks that use subsequent outcomes. If cheap frontier intelligence leads more developers to test clinical agents, access to realistic, specialist-graded evaluations could become a bottleneck. The memo gives no buyer-side measurement of evaluation delays or capacity limits. (*The Idea*, *Key Risks*)

Archangel reports that frontier labs and medical AI companies use its product, but the memo gives no independent buyer commitment or payment evidence. The dated marketplace listing shows that the team brought the current offering to market. Patel’s clinical-agent work and Bhatia’s training-data experience are relevant to delivering it. Archangel reports specialist grading and provenance tracking, while the scope of its data rights remains undisclosed. A plausible expansion unit is a specialty-specific evaluation package licensed to multiple labs. That scenario depends on reusable rights and on how much physician review and customization each deployment requires. The memo gives no pricing or delivery-cost data to test it. (*Market & Traction*, *Founders & Team*, *Defensibility*, *The Idea*, *Key Facts*)

### Countercase

Stanford’s public PhysicianBench already tests multi-step EHR workflows. If it meets a lab’s requirements, the lab can bypass Archangel for benchmark testing. Archangel’s longitudinal and custom evaluations could still fill a gap, but the memo shows neither a buyer comparison demonstrating that gap nor enforceable rights that would make it hard for rivals to fill. The public alternative raises a concrete substitution risk. The memo leaves equivalence unresolved, so the path remains open on Watch. (*Defensibility*, *The Idea*)
