# Shire Intelligence -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Channel-led or repeatable sales** is the strongest path. Full-service restaurant operators have a recurring job on busy shifts: spotting tables that need service or clearing and seating guests according to server workload. Shire uses existing CCTV feeds to turn dining-room activity into staff alerts and seating updates. If operators will pay for those improvements, a standard installation could carry the same workflow to other restaurants. The job plausibly travels across markets, with camera coverage, POS compatibility, and footage governance still to be resolved. (*The Idea*, *Key Risks*)

The memo documents a direct demo route and a reseller inquiry option. Shire reports 13 live restaurants at launch and a 12% average reduction in table-turn time, with the comparison baseline and measurement window unspecified. The reported deployments indicate early willingness to try the product in a live workflow. Mathews Restaurant’s testimonial concerns payment-processing savings. For floor intelligence, the memo gives no account-level record connecting a sales channel to paid activation, sustained use, or expansion, and no measures of installation effort or ongoing delivery cost. The first unproven link is whether Shire can repeat a paid installation with bounded effort. (*The Idea*, *Market & Traction*, *Key Risks*)

### Countercase

Camera-based floor management could require site-by-site calibration and ongoing troubleshooting. Shire’s prototype report documented inconsistent classifications, and the memo identifies camera blind spots as a risk. If missed or mistimed alerts force staff to keep checking tables manually, buyers may get enough of the seating benefit from their existing POS while Shire incurs recurring support costs. The structural downside gate remains open because the memo establishes a classification risk but leaves the production rollout and support burden unmeasured. (*Key Risks*, *Market & Traction*)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a non-founder take two independent full-service restaurant operators from the same demo channel to paid, reliable floor-intelligence use through a standard installation? Account records showing payment for floor intelligence, staff reliance on alerts across several busy shifts, and measured setup and support effort within what the subscription can cover would move Shire to Reach out.
