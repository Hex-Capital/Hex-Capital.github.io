## The Idea

- **Problem** — Robotics and autonomy companies buying through generalist brokers can face denials or AI exclusions that leave them without the coverage customers require before a pilot or deployment ([Launch HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49451495)).
- **Approach** — Risklytics drafts an application from the applicant’s site, filings and product pages, then a licensed broker shops it to carriers and reviews proposed exclusions before binding ([Risklytics homepage](https://www.risklytics.ai/), [Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SfN-risklytics-insurance-for-the-frontier)).
- **Differentiation** — Risklytics compares carrier terms and AI exclusions across commercial lines, while Alton Risk separately sizes product-liability limits for hardware and Indem is piloting submissions built from operating data ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SfN-risklytics-insurance-for-the-frontier), [Alton Risk](https://insurephysicalai.com/), [Indem](https://indeminsure.com/physical-ai/)).
- **Business Model** — Insurers set premiums and pay Risklytics a commission that the founders say is usually 10–20% of premium, with free applications, no separate broker fee and no published rates ([Launch HN, Aug 2026](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49451495), [Risklytics pricing](https://www.risklytics.ai/pricing/)).
- **TAM/SAM** — Meticulous Research estimates $300–600 million in 2026 gross written premiums for humanoid and industrial robot liability insurance, with a projected 30% CAGR through 2036 ([Meticulous Research, Nov 2026](https://www.meticulousresearch.com/meticulous-next/humanoid-and-industrial-robot-liability-insurance-outlook-2026-2036)); no public SAM data found for Risklytics’ licensed footprint.
- **GTM / Distribution** — The CEO describes outreach through YC and Harvard, partnerships with wholesale brokers and managing general agents, and founder-led cold email and LinkedIn outreach ([Center for Data Innovation, Jul 2026](https://datainnovation.org/2026/07/5-qs-with-samuel-gold-founder-of-risklytics/), [Samuel Gold on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/samuelggold)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today** — Risklytics has compiled a public tracker covering 736 AI-exclusion filings across 49 states and 33 large insurers, alongside a placement map of which carriers will remove exclusions or affirmatively cover AI ([Risklytics tracker, Sep 2026](https://www.risklytics.ai/ai-exclusions/), [Launch HN, Aug 2026](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49451495)).
- **Future moat** — [Inferred]: Renewal and claims histories could improve carrier matching over time, but the public tracker documents policy filings and the founders describe loss-data collection as a future goal ([tracker methodology](https://www.risklytics.ai/ai-exclusions/about-the-data/), [Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SfN-risklytics-insurance-for-the-frontier)).
- **Market structure** — No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk** — Licensed specialist brokers can combine public filings with their own carrier relationships, and Alton Risk already places robotics product liability and technology E&O through specialist markets ([Risklytics tracker methodology](https://www.risklytics.ai/ai-exclusions/about-the-data/), [Alton Risk](https://insurephysicalai.com/)).

## Key Risks

- **Carrier capacity and policy wording** — Insurers control underwriting and may interpret AI exclusions differently, so Risklytics reviews policy forms and seeks carriers that explicitly cover the applicant’s use ([Launch HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49451495), [Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SfN-risklytics-insurance-for-the-frontier)).
- **State licensing** — Placement availability depends on state producer licensing, and Risklytics says it adds licenses as clients require them ([Risklytics licensing register](https://www.risklytics.ai/about/)).
- **Application accuracy** — A launch-day tester reported that the intake tool identified general liability for a hypothetical robotics applicant but left it unselected, while the current workflow calls for applicant review and licensed-broker placement ([Launch HN, Aug 2026](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49451495), [Risklytics homepage](https://www.risklytics.ai/)).
- **Name collision** — An unrelated German business operates as risklytics GmbH under risklytics.de, creating a risk of misattributed financial or company records ([German company imprint](https://www.risklytics.de/about/), [YC directory](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/risklytics)).
