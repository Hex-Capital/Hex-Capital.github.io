# Kanopy -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Medium

### Company case

Kanopy’s strongest path is channel-led sales through ACRT. Electric utilities repeatedly assess vegetation clearances, plan trimming, and verify completion. Kanopy turns ordinary video into 3D clearance measurements, and ACRT has packaged that analysis with its vegetation management services as Arborcision. The same capture and analysis workflow could serve utilities in other markets if local field validation can be handled without rebuilding it. Kanopy reports mapping more than 300 miles across four states in two weeks, which supports technical portability. Paid adoption across markets remains unverified. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

The Arborcision launch documents an operating partner integration. The six reported LOIs or master service agreements show early buyer interest, while the 270,000 miles represent potential scope. Together these signals support early fit, with buyer value and budget commitment still unverified. The first link likely to break is the handoff from ACRT’s sales process to a paid utility go-live. The memo identifies ACRT’s route to buyers and Kanopy’s footage upload and results workflow, but documents no utility completing that route to payment. The cumulative mileage also gives no account-level evidence of repeat surveys or paid expansion. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Facts)

### Countercase

Utilities may require enough field checks to erase the labor savings. The remote-sensing plan cited in the memo calls for field validation and retains ground inspection where remote methods fall short. If ACRT and Kanopy have to repeat substantial fieldwork and custom setup for each utility, delivery costs could rise in proportion to revenue. The memo gives no rollout labor, processing costs, or revenue per deployment. That leaves a testable cost and handoff question, so the structural downside gate remains untriggered. (Market & Traction, Key Risks, Key Facts)

### Decisive diligence question

Can ACRT bring two independent utilities into paid production using the same capture and validation workflow? Paid work orders and rollout records showing ACRT handled onboarding with bounded field-validation and Kanopy support effort would move the action to Reach out. Evidence that each rollout requires substantial custom fieldwork that rises in proportion to paid scope, with no credible way to reduce it, would move the action to Pass.
