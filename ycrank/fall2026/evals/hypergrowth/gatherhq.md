# GatherHQ -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Reach out  
**Evidence confidence:** Medium

### Company case

Conditional on strong product-market fit, GatherHQ’s strongest growth path is usage-based buyer research for enterprise marketing teams through Slack and the Resonance API. A team can query its existing calls, surveys, and research, run buyer simulations, and commission fresh interviews when the available evidence is thin. Those interfaces support the same recurring job across markets. Cover Genius’s completed survey of 1,392 consumers in seven countries demonstrates cross-border fieldwork. How much localization and recruiting work a new market requires remains uncertain. (*The Idea*, *Market & Traction*, *Key Risks*)

Emma’s free Slack install gives teams a bottom-up way to start, while published per-use pricing gives retained accounts room to spend more as research activity grows. The memo documents the install and prices, while independent purchase and production setup for a typical enterprise buyer remain unverified. Gather reports customer upsells, which support the expansion mechanism. Whether that expansion can offset churn remains an inference. (*The Idea*, *Defensibility*, *Key Facts*)

Gather reported 50+ customers in September, 60 enterprise customers in October, and 65+ enterprise marketing teams by November, with a listed team of nine. The April report of more than doubled quarterly revenue is a commercial signal, with unclear attribution to the current offering around the BeHeard transition. The customer counts use different definitions, and the reported 10× growth has no specified metric. Together, the signals support early commercial uptake with an uncertain growth slope. (*GatherHQ*, *Market & Traction*, *Key Facts*)

At the listed prices, a $10 simulation must cover incremental inference, data processing, and attributable onboarding and support. The $50 consumer interview and $100 B2B interview must also cover recruitment, participant costs, and any delivery labor. If customers can reuse evidence across many simulations and refresh it selectively, incremental revenue could grow faster than delivery costs. The memo establishes that mechanism, with contribution at a representative workload still unmeasured. (*The Idea*, *Defensibility*, *Key Risks*)

### Countercase

If buyer evidence goes stale quickly or customers restrict data reuse, Gather could need frequent fresh interviews and hands-on setup to deliver credible results. Enterprise demos, data permissions, and the need to recruit buyers when evidence is sparse support that concern. The structural downside gate clears because the documented workflow can reuse existing evidence and automate interviews, and the memo leaves the amount of required human work unresolved. (*The Idea*, *Key Risks*)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a typical enterprise team reach first paid research value through Slack or the API at positive incremental contribution? A recent cohort showing the steps to first paid use, staff time, and delivery costs including any required fresh interviews would answer it. Repeatable, profitable activation would sustain Reach out. Routine hands-on activation or weak contribution with a credible improvement path would move the action to Watch.
