## The Idea

- **Problem:** AI labs and agent developers rely on fixed benchmarks such as OSWorld whose tasks can enter training data and do not measure failures on changing, real-world computer workflows ([CoArena Mission](https://coarena.ai/mission), Aug 2026).
- **Approach:** Users submit a computer task, two unidentified agents execute it in parallel with the same action space and sandbox, and a blind human vote produces a preference label and paired trajectories ([CoArena Governance](https://coarena.ai/governance), Aug 2026).
- **Differentiation:** CoArena records computer actions, observations, reasoning and workflow outcomes, versus OSWorld’s fixed task set and LMArena’s primarily response-oriented, multi-modality comparisons ([CoArena Dataset](https://coarena.ai/data), [LMArena](https://oldblog.lmarena.ai/blog/2025/two-year-celebration/), Aug 2026).
- **Business Model:** The arena is free for users, while preference data, trajectories and eval access are licensed to AI labs through consumption-priced, contract-signed agreements with no public dollar pricing ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/coarena), [CoArena Dataset](https://coarena.ai/data), Aug 2026).
- **TAM/SAM:** AI-agent evaluation and simulation platforms were estimated at $980M in 2026 with 27.4% projected CAGR through 2036, while evaluation and benchmarking represented 31%, or approximately $304M, of the 2026 market ([Fact.MR](https://www.factmr.com/report/ai-agent-evaluation-and-simulation-platforms-market), 2026).
- **GTM / Distribution:** CoArena distributes free access to frontier and preview models through its arena, Product Hunt launch and student hackathon, then solicits data and eval contracts from AI labs and robotics companies ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/coarena), [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/coarena-by-coasty), [Hackathon Radar](https://www.hackathonradar.com/database/hackathon/622e7258-dfa3-410d-a494-2d91159d7018), Aug 2026).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Each battle can create a consented real-work task, two full trajectories and a blind preference label, while the public product currently reports 57 measures across 14 ranked agents ([CoArena Dataset](https://coarena.ai/data), [benchmark](https://coarena.ai/benchmark), Aug 2026).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Repeated arena usage could produce a proprietary corpus of unseen computer-use tasks, failures and human judgments, but the dataset page currently displays no task-category data and says no annotation fields have been collected ([CoArena Dataset](https://coarena.ai/data), Aug 2026).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** LMArena already operates crowdsourced model comparisons, while Patronus AI and Braintrust provide agent evaluation, simulation or trace-based testing that could extend further into computer-use workflows ([LMArena](https://oldblog.lmarena.ai/blog/2025/two-year-celebration/), [Patronus AI](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/patronus-ai-raises-50-million-series-b-and-unveils-first-digital-world-models-for-ai-agent-training-and-simulation-302811248.html), [Braintrust](https://www.braintrust.dev/blog/announcing-series-b)).

## Key Risks

- **Operator conflict:** Coasty Systems owns CoArena while developing its own computer-use agent, although that agent is excluded from new matches and public ranking ([CoArena Governance](https://coarena.ai/governance), Aug 2026).
- **Sensitive-data exposure:** Raw task prompts and unmasked screenshots are transmitted to model providers during execution, while CoArena has no SOC 2, ISO 27001 or third-party security audit ([CoArena Security](https://coarena.ai/security), Aug 2026).
- **Dataset maturity:** The dataset page displays no task-category data and says no annotation fields have yet been collected, limiting public evidence for the licensed corpus ([CoArena Dataset](https://coarena.ai/data), Aug 2026).
- **Competitor scale:** LMArena reported more than 3M votes and 400 evaluated public models before CoArena’s launch and has raised $250M ([LMArena](https://oldblog.lmarena.ai/blog/2025/two-year-celebration/), [funding announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lmarena-raises-150-million-to-build-the-worlds-most-trusted-ai-evaluation-platform-302653012.html)).
- **Name ambiguity:** “COARENA” also appears in unrelated Brazilian municipal records, requiring the coarena.ai domain, YC S26 batch and founder names for disambiguation ([Santos official record](https://diariooficial.santos.sp.gov.br/edicoes/inicio/download/2026-05-05), [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/coarena)).
