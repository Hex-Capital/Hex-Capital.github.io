## The Idea

- **Problem:** AI companies need experimental biology data but face laboratory construction timelines measured in years, leaving internal laboratory development as an alternative to outsourcing ([YC, accessed Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/petrichor)).
- **Approach:** Models submit experiment instructions, scientists execute them in parallel, and photographs, raw instrument files, timestamped actions and failures stream back during execution ([Petrichor, How](https://petrichorbio.com/how/)).
- **Differentiation:** Petrichor emphasizes scientist-executed experiments and intermediate records for customer models, whereas Emerald Cloud Lab emphasizes software-controlled laboratory automation and Strateos offers remote laboratories plus automation for customers’ facilities ([Petrichor](https://petrichorbio.com/how/), [ECL](https://www.emeraldcloudlab.com/), [Strateos](https://strateos.com/)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Charging for commissioned experiments and associated data delivery follows the described service; public prices, billing units and contract terms: No public data found.
