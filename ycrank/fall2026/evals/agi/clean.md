# Clean -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path B — New economies** is the strongest path. Clean says it turns dated permits, shipments, and filings into buying-window alerts with a decision-maker and supporting records. The scenario to test is whether a small industrial supplier could afford to monitor accounts it currently skips because manual research costs more than the expected value of the opportunities. The memo documents a manual workflow and describes Clean’s alternative. It provides no like-for-like measure of labor per timely, verified opportunity after contact checks, missed windows, and retries. (The Idea, Key Risks)

Clean launched the current sales product on November 2 after a summer pivot. The memo records a reported first paying customer and company-reported ARR of over $200K. In a published testimonial, Vivameda reported signing $60K in contracts in two weeks. That is a reported customer outcome. The memo does not establish whether customers were previously priced out of account monitoring or whether they renew. The launch and the founders’ prior work together on WashedMCP, alongside relevant enterprise sales and ML experience, support seed-stage execution credit. Pricing, data costs, and the labor required to add and serve another account are unknown, so scale economics receives the absent-evidence default. (Market & Traction, Key Facts, Founders & Team, The Idea)

### Countercase

Clean may improve prospecting for teams that already pay for it while incumbents bundle comparable research into their existing workflows. Salesmotion already aggregates overlapping signals, and Apollo offers AI account research and outreach. Clean claims some datasets are its own and also relies on licensed data. The memo shows neither a controlled data bottleneck for Path A nor sustained buyer preference at functional parity for Path B. One aircraft example also surfaced a lead after the supplier had been selected, showing why a sourced alert can still miss the commercially useful window. The evidence leaves both Path B conditions unproven and supports a seed-stage test. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Will industrial suppliers that previously skipped account monitoring pay and renew for Clean when offered a functionally comparable alternative using the same class of model? Run a paid pilot that compares all-in delivery cost per timely, verified opportunity against manual research at equivalent quality, including data, inference, verification, and retries. If the savings open that buyer segment and suppliers renew with Clean under parity, rescore for Reach out against the outreach threshold. If the savings disappear or buyers readily switch at parity, Pass.
