## The Idea

- **Problem:** AI agents can research and plan but cannot safely complete purchases without receiving an unrestricted human card or handing checkout back to the user ([Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/agentcard), Jul 2026).
- **Approach:** Agentcard funds a wallet, applies budgets and approval thresholds, and issues one-time virtual cards that close after one authorized payment through an API, CLI, or MCP server ([Agentcard](https://www.agentcard.sh/); [Agentcard terms](https://www.agentcard.sh/terms), Jul 2026).
- **Differentiation:** Agentcard uses single-use Visa cards for purchases at existing online merchants, while Skyfire emphasizes agent identity and micropayments, Nekuda uses secure wallets and agentic mandates, and Catena Labs is building a multi-rail AI-native financial institution ([Agentcard](https://www.agentcard.sh/); [Skyfire](https://skyfire.xyz/overview-ai-agents-and-payments/); [Nekuda](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250514808097/en/Nekuda-Raises-%245M-Led-by-Madrona-Together-with-Amex-Ventures-and-Visa-Ventures-to-Power-Agentic-Payments); [Catena Labs](https://catenalabs.com/blog/announcing-catena-labs)).
- **Business Model:** A July 2026 launch promotion priced company access at $500 per month instead of a stated $5,000-per-month standard price, while organization subscriptions are processed through Stripe ([Product Hunt launch coverage](https://www.hunted.space/product/agent-card), Jul 2026; [Agentcard terms](https://www.agentcard.sh/terms), Jul 2026).
- **TAM/SAM:** The global agentic-commerce market was estimated at $5.7 billion in 2025 and forecast to reach $65.5 billion by 2033, a 35.7% CAGR from 2026–2033 ([Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/agentic-commerce-market-report), Jun 2026).
- **GTM / Distribution:** The product offers self-service setup through an npm CLI and API, uses an MCP integration, schedules sales calls for companies, and launched on Product Hunt ([Agentcard](https://www.agentcard.sh/); [Product Hunt launch coverage](https://www.hunted.space/product/agent-card), Jul 2026).

## Key Risks

- **Product-description inconsistency:** Marketing calls the product a debit card, but the legal terms describe each card as a fully collateralized credit account rather than a prepaid or debit card ([Agentcard terms](https://www.agentcard.sh/terms), Jul 2026).
- **Issuer and provider dependency:** Issuance depends on Third National and Visa, while funding, identity, and subscriptions depend on MoonPay, Crossmint, Coinbase, Sumsub, and Stripe ([Agentcard terms](https://www.agentcard.sh/terms), Jul 2026).
- **Geographic limitation:** Wallet funding is currently available only in the United States, with issuance also subject to supported jurisdictions and prohibited-use rules ([Agentcard terms](https://www.agentcard.sh/terms), Jul 2026).
- **Compliance readiness:** The website labels SOC 2 and PCI DSS as pending while the API can expose decrypted PAN and CVV for checkout workflows ([Agentcard](https://www.agentcard.sh/); [Agentcard API](https://www.agentcard.sh/api), Jul 2026).
- **Platform competition:** Visa-backed Nekuda, the $18 million-funded Catena Labs, and Skyfire are building overlapping agent-payment infrastructure ([Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250514808097/en/Nekuda-Raises-%245M-Led-by-Madrona-Together-with-Amex-Ventures-and-Visa-Ventures-to-Power-Agentic-Payments), May 2025; [Fortune](https://fortune.com/crypto/2025/05/20/sean-neville-catena-labs-18-million-a16z-breyer-circle-coinbase-tom-brady/), May 2025).
