# Streamline Systems -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

The strongest potential route is a strategic acquisition by a drone manufacturer. Streamline reports that its retrofit module enabled autonomous flight without satellite positioning and works with existing drone platforms. If an OEM qualifies the module for production and sees a measurable improvement over available alternatives, owning it could give the OEM control over a capability it would otherwise source. The memo supports a reason to evaluate the product. Whether an OEM would pay to own it remains open. (The Idea, Defensibility)

The next milestone is customer-validated production qualification followed by repeat paid shipments. Streamline reports more than $80,000 in contracted revenue and deposits against more than $87,000 in reserved modules. The deposit amount and overlap are undisclosed, so the figures cannot be added. The memo lists $500,000 in financing and a Q1 2027 availability target. Those signals support a path to initial delivery, while shipment volumes, time to qualification, and capital needed to scale remain unknown. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

The benchmark assumes a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, with 5% ownership diluted to 3% and no follow-ons. A 5× gross cash return requires $2.5 million within eight years, or about $83.3 million in distributable equity at 3%. The sale cases below are illustrative. Both assume an all-cash close within eight years, an eligible holder selling its full stake, and pro rata distribution after the stated deductions, with no further senior claims.

| Scenario | Proceeds bridge | Cash to holder |
|---|---|---|
| Modest sale | $40M enterprise value less $5M in assumed debt and deal adjustments leaves $35M in distributable equity | $1.05M (2.1×) |
| Strong sale | $100M enterprise value less $10M in assumed debt and deal adjustments leaves $90M in distributable equity | $2.70M (5.4×). Selling 90% of the stake yields $2.43M (4.86×). |
| Prolonged private | The company continues operating and reinvests available cash, with no eligible share purchase or payout | $0 realized within eight years |

The memo supplies no buyer valuation evidence, holder terms, or transfer permissions. It also supplies no evidence of an independent liquidity route. The scenarios show what would clear the hurdle, while realizable proceeds and closing feasibility remain unverified. (Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Countercase

An OEM could source navigation hardware from an established supplier and have little reason to acquire Streamline. The memo identifies competing suppliers, provides no independent comparison showing that Streamline outperforms them, and ties disclosed demand to manufacturers competing in one procurement program. Even if reservations convert, module sales alone may leave the buyer’s case for an acquisition weak. The evidence establishes neither a structural barrier to a sale nor a credible price ceiling below the holder’s hurdle, so the Pass gate is not triggered. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Would a drone OEM’s production qualification show enough economic benefit from owning Streamline’s module to support a price that could leave at least $83.3 million in distributable equity? Move to Reach out if a customer-validated comparison with available alternatives and a quantified buy-versus-source analysis support that price range. Until then, Watch.
