## The Idea

- **Problem:** Soldiers face drone operators, jammers, and command posts hidden behind terrain or fortifications, while existing counter-UAS systems primarily intercept individual aircraft and soldiers lack a reliable way to geolocate their operators ([Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/greypoint-industries), Aug 2026).
- **Approach:** LEGION passively detects radio emitters, fuses sensor data into a shared battlefield picture, geolocates signals from drones and vehicles, and cues autonomous ARROW interceptors ([company website](https://greypointindustries.com/), accessed Aug 2026).
- **Differentiation:** [Inferred]: Greypoint combines emitter geolocation and interception, versus Anduril’s Lattice-cued kinetic Anvil, Fortem’s radar-guided net-capture DroneHunter, and Epirus’s high-power-microwave Leonidas ([Anduril](https://www.anduril.com/anvil/), [Fortem](https://fortemtech.com/products/dronehunter-f700/), [Epirus](https://www.epirusinc.com/electronic-warfare)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Direct government sales of LEGION sensing systems, ARROW interceptors, integration, and support, based on its Canadian-government contract and briefing-led sales process ([Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/greypoint-industries), Aug 2026; [company website](https://greypointindustries.com/), accessed Aug 2026).
- **TAM/SAM:** The global counter-UAS market is projected to grow from $14.41 billion in 2026 to $55.25 billion in 2034 at a 22.4% CAGR, while no public SAM estimate was found ([Fortune Business Insights](https://www.fortunebusinessinsights.com/counter-uas-market-111906), 2026).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Greypoint is pursuing government and military procurement through an initial Canadian-government contract, a Canadian Armed Forces demonstration, technical briefings, and relationships listed with DND, CAF, IDEaS, TerraSense, NVIDIA, Innovation UBC, and YC ([Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/greypoint-industries), Aug 2026; [company website](https://greypointindustries.com/), accessed Aug 2026).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Public signals consist of integrated LEGION-to-ARROW architecture, a Canadian-government contract, a Canadian Armed Forces demonstration, and one successful aerial-platform geolocation test ([Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/greypoint-industries), Aug 2026).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Deployment-derived emitter signatures, geolocation data, C2 integrations, interceptor feedback, and manufacturing learning could accumulate, but no proprietary dataset, patent, or production-volume evidence is public.
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** Anduril and Fortem already market autonomous interceptors, while Epirus offers a non-kinetic counter-swarm system with U.S. military validation ([Anduril](https://www.anduril.com/anvil/), [Fortem](https://fortemtech.com/press-releases/2026-01-20-fortem-technologies-begins-deliveries-of-next-generation-dronehunter-5-0-advancing-counter-swarm-defense/), [Epirus](https://www.epirusinc.com/electronic-warfare)).

## Key Risks

- **RF-silent threats:** LEGION depends on emitter detection, while waypoint-controlled or otherwise RF-silent drones may not be visible to passive RF antennas; Greypoint’s listed mitigation is sensor fusion plus ARROW interception ([company website](https://greypointindustries.com/); [Fortem](https://fortemtech.com/products/dronehunter-f700/)).
- **Technical validation:** Public evidence covers one geolocation test and a military demonstration, but no range, accuracy, contested-environment, or swarm-scale interception results are disclosed ([Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/greypoint-industries), Aug 2026).
- **Procurement and export controls:** Canadian military and dual-use goods can require export permits, while the Defence Drone Initiative provides a domestic testing and procurement channel ([Global Affairs Canada](https://www.international.gc.ca/controls-controles/military-militaires/index.aspx?lang=eng); [DND](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/news/2026/07/government-of-canada-launches-defence-drone-initiative-to-strengthen-canadas-sovereign-defence-capabilities.html), Jul 2026).
- **Operating-location disclosure:** YC lists San Francisco, while LinkedIn lists Vancouver as headquarters, creating a diligence question around manufacturing and contracting jurisdiction ([Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/greypoint-industries); [LinkedIn](https://ca.linkedin.com/company/greypoint-industries), Aug 2026).
- **Name disambiguation:** Searches also return unrelated Greypoint Capital and Greypoint Inc. records; this dossier covers the YC S26 company founded by Darcy Zhang, Nic Chu, Jordan Khoo, and Steven Xu ([Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/greypoint-industries), Aug 2026).
