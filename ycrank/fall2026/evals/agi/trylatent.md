# Latent -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path B, new economies, is the strongest path.** Latent could make it economical for teams running self-hosted agents to check every consequential action before release. The memo records a self-serve launch and Python SDK distribution. Latent reports that its activation probe stopped 99% of rule-breaking agents versus 69% for a frontier judge in a gpt-oss-120b sandbox. That result supports testing the mechanism at seed. The production question is whether teams that can afford only sampled checks today could afford continuous checks at comparable safety and latency once false holds, escalations, and human review are counted. The memo documents no buyer commitment to that newly viable use. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Facts, Key Risks)

At full use of the published Team allowance, assuming no overages, Latent would collect about **$1.50 per 1,000 scored answers** before delivery costs. That gives a concrete benchmark for the economics of adding traffic. Actual compute, calibration, review, and support costs per additional deployment remain unknown, as does the share of traffic that can go unscored when spending limits are reached. Gaur’s relevant research and the shipped SDK support seed-stage execution credit. Production deployment and iteration with buyers remain unverified. (The Idea, Founders & Team, Key Risks, Key Facts)

### Countercase

Goodfire already offers activation-based monitoring for open models through Baseten, and cheaper judges could make continuous checking affordable on their own. Traffic-specific calibration may give customers a reason to stay, but Latent lets them export labeled examples and the memo shows no buyer preference under functional parity. The competing offering also weakens Path A because Latent has no evidenced control over scarce access to model activations. The record leaves both required Path B conditions unproven, so the affirmative outreach gate remains unmet. A production comparison can still resolve them. (Defensibility, The Idea, Market & Traction)

### Decisive diligence question

Will an independent self-hosted agent team that currently relies on sampled checks pay to keep Latent after a production trial against the best available low-cost substitute? A paid renewal after the trial would support Reach out if the trial shows that Latent makes continuous coverage viable at comparable safety and latency, with lower fully loaded delivery cost, and the updated case clears the score and confidence gates. If the trial shows that exceptions erase the savings or that the buyer switches to an equivalent, cheaper option, close Path B and move to Pass.
