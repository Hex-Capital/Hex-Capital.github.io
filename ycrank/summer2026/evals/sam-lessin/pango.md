# Pango -- Sam Lessin Evaluation

I start with three blanks: proposed post-money valuation, competing-term-sheet count, and whether my check would be genuinely scarce. The disclosed 5 million SEK financing ahead of Pango 1.0 proves the company has raised capital; it does not tell me its price, investor competition, or whether this YC round is already being auctioned. I will not infer favorable terms from silence. For my money, that leaves the first gate at its default.

The capital also lacks a named venture moment. Pango simultaneously spans transportation management, pick-and-pack, tracking, returns, refunds, claims, support, and warehouse workflows across 100-plus integrations and 40 countries. That may describe a large product, but it does not identify the one novel hypothesis this round will test, the metric that resolves it, or the deadline. “Launch 1.0” is an event, not a truth-revealing experiment. Without a finite test, new capital risks financing breadth rather than collapsing uncertainty.

The strongest evidence is inside customer operations. Thirty brands have signed; Understatement, Eytys, Kukinos, GLAS, and Switch Nails are named; and the Shopify app has five five-star reviews after operating since October 2024. If Pango truly automates 99% of returns and shipping work while reducing operating costs 20%, it is not merely generating text—it is exercising sensitive write access, applying merchant policies, and causing operational actions. That is the beginning of a proprietary workflow footprint connecting merchants, carriers, warehouses, ERPs, and customer-service systems.

The objection is that these are precisely the kinds of numbers that become metric theater. “100 integrations” is not a moat, “30 signed” does not establish 30 paying or retained customers, and the automation and savings claims are company-reported without methodology. Narvar and parcelLab already sell autonomous post-purchase agents, while Loop and AfterShip overlap heavily with the workflow. The dark pool would be retained merchant-specific exception data and embedded policy control; the dossier only infers that compounding advantage. ISO 27001 remains in progress even though the app can edit orders, discounts, gift cards, store credit, and policies, so one serious failure could reverse the apparent workflow advantage.

The founders are materially better than generic AI-wrapper founders. Steve Rahimi has seven years of e-commerce operating experience and researched reverse logistics directly. Lukasz Reszczynski shipped a patented configurator reportedly used for more than $1.5 billion in quotes and Accu-Tech’s $300 million sales process. That is credible evidence of a team able to translate between commerce operations and configurable software. CEO and CTO titles establish functional division, but the dossier does not show the co-founder history or who has final authority when product, security, and commercial priorities conflict.

I can believe Pango becomes a useful business; I cannot yet underwrite it as a cheap, one-and-done angel investment. There is no disclosed revenue, gross margin, burn, runway, or controllable cash metric, while the free Checkout+ tier and negotiated pricing reveal packaging rather than economics. My verifiable flip condition is a cohort showing that at least 20 of the 30 signed brands are paying, remain retained for 12 months, and generate greater than 60% gross margin. That would turn the current claims into the kind of conversion and cash evidence capable of overriding missing round information. Until then, I pass.
