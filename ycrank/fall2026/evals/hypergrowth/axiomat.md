# Axiomat -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

### Company case

**Path B. Repeatable sales with account expansion.** The memo records a November 2026 launch, demo booking, and email and LinkedIn outreach. Axiomat says it turns business rules into user-approved definitions and uses Lean to verify that generated queries match those definitions before execution. The memo gives no evidence of buyer activation, payment, or repeat use. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Facts)

A plausible first buyer is an enterprise finance team that repeatedly checks figures for board reporting. If demo-led sales can win that buyer and a standard onboarding process can get one reporting workflow into production, the same account could add pricing or other reporting workloads. A rollout outside the US would test whether the same product and onboarding process work when local reporting definitions and data-access requirements change. The growth case assumes teams can reuse setup across customers and approved rules across workloads. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Countercase

A proof can certify a query against a mistaken business definition. If each new customer needs extensive help translating and validating its rules, implementation and support labor could grow with every account. Each new workload could add more rule-validation work and compute from proof retries, while abstentions could leave recurring questions unanswered. The memo describes the workflow and its risks but provides no rollout or cost data that establish a structural ceiling. The Pass gate remains unmet. (The Idea, Key Risks)
