# Particularity -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path B — New economies.** Particularity could make recurring sweeps for hidden checkout fees and hard-to-cancel subscriptions economical for state and city consumer protection agencies. The memo describes a complaint-led workflow that leaves unreported conduct undiscovered and records new enforcement authority over cancellation flows in New York City. Particularity says its browser agents test live checkout and cancellation flows, timestamp and hash captures, match complaints to saved evidence, and map findings to statutory elements for staff review. The newly viable use would be screening sites routinely before complaints arrive. The memo establishes a plausible use and a public launch. Agency adoption and labor savings remain unverified. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Facts)

**Scenario assumption.** At equivalent coverage and evidentiary quality, agent-led sweeps could open this use if they reduce staff time per agency-accepted evidence package after retries, missed-flow checks, manual exceptions, review, and authentication. Particularity could retain agency spend under model parity if staff demonstrably prefer its integrated evidence and case workflow. The memo describes that integration and hypothesizes switching costs from accumulated case history. Buyer preference, paid demand, and a per-agency margin after inference, implementation, and support remain unverified. The founders’ consumer protection and security infrastructure experience supports a credible seed-stage trial. (The Idea, Defensibility, Founders & Team, Key Risks, Key Facts)

### Countercase

Browser failures and evidence authentication could leave investigators repeating enough work to erase the savings. Existing tools already cover sweeps, evidence preservation, or complaint intake, so a vendor with equally capable models could offer an equivalent workflow inside an agency’s existing system. The memo documents incomplete browser workflows in a separate setting and provides no Particularity-specific completion benchmark or buyer comparison. Both Path B gates remain open and unproven, and the memo identifies no separate bottleneck Particularity controls under Path A. (Market & Traction, Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Will a target agency commit budget to recurring proactive sweeps after comparing Particularity with both a manual baseline and a credible alternative using equally capable models? A representative pilot should show equivalent coverage and agency-accepted evidence, materially lower all-in staff time per accepted package, and a paid repeat commitment grounded in a preference for Particularity’s workflow. That result could move the action to Reach out if it clears the score and confidence gates. A representative result showing that manual rework erases the savings or that the agency readily switches under parity would move the action to Pass.
