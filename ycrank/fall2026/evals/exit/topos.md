# Topos -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Strategic acquisition is the strongest route. Topos says Egregore can coordinate thousands of agents and emphasizes formalizing and checking their reasoning. A scientific research platform could have a reason to buy Egregore if those checks reliably lowered the cost per expert-accepted result in its existing workflow. That buyer benefit is an inference. The memo provides no measured savings, buyer interest, or acquisition pricing. (The Idea, Market & Traction)

The observed beta intake and the founders’ documented work in machine-checked mathematics support a pilot that compares Egregore with an existing workflow across repeated research tasks. The pilot would measure total compute and expert review cost per accepted result. The memo leaves pilot participation, test funding, and runway unverified. It also leaves IP ownership, transfer consents, and holder rights unverified and provides no evidence of an independent liquidity route. (Market & Traction, Founders & Team, Key Facts)

Under the shared benchmark, a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation buys 5%, diluted to 3% with no follow-ons. The holder needs $2.5 million in cash within eight years, or about $83.3 million in distributable equity at a full pro rata payout. The sale cases below are illustrative. Both assume an eligible holder sells the entire stake for cash within eight years and receives 3% of distributable equity, with no preferential claims ahead of the holder after debt and deal adjustments.

| Scenario | Proceeds bridge | Cash to holder |
|-----------|-----------------|----------------|
| Modest sale | $30M enterprise value less $5M in debt and deal adjustments yields $25M in distributable equity | $750,000 (1.5×) |
| Strong sale | $100M enterprise value less $10M in debt and deal adjustments yields $90M in distributable equity | $2.7M (5.4×) |
| Prolonged private | Cash is reinvested throughout the eight-year window, with no eligible share sale or funded payout | $0 (0×) |

Even in the strong case, selling only 80% of the stake at the same per-share price would deliver $2.16 million (4.32×). The memo leaves the strong sale price and full cash participation unsupported. (Market & Traction, Key Facts)

### Countercase

A prospective buyer may already have enough capability to build or partner. DeepMind has demonstrated multi-agent scientific reasoning, Acyclic offers general-purpose orchestration, and Edison announced a Pfizer deployment in the same research workflow. Expert validation can still account for substantial work, and coordination overhead could erode any savings. These alternatives weaken the case for paying an acquisition premium. The memo establishes neither a structural closing barrier nor a credible price ceiling below the holder’s hurdle, so the Pass gate remains untriggered. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Can a prospective strategic buyer use results from a paid, head-to-head pilot to justify a purchase price that clears the holder’s $2.5 million cash objective? Repeated, expert-verified savings against the buyer’s current workflow, paired with a buyer-supported price range that clears the hurdle after estimated deal deductions and holder terms, would move the action to Reach out.
