## The Idea

- **Problem:** Post-sales teams at usage-priced B2B software companies manually reconcile calls, tickets, Slack threads and spreadsheets to track onboarding blockers and account health ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/VN0-quivly-ai-agents-that-run-everything-after-the-sale-from-onboarding-to-renewal), Oct 2026).
- **Approach:** Quivly turns kickoff transcripts, emails and Slack threads into shared launch plans with assigned tasks, then combines usage, support and external company signals to flag risk and expansion and prepare renewal briefs ([Quivly](https://www.quivly.ai/), Nov 2026).
- **Differentiation:** Quivly combines kickoff-to-launch automation and external expansion signals for usage-priced vendors, while Gainsight’s Atlas pairs agents with renewal managers, ChurnZero embeds agents in configurable Plays, and Vitally emphasizes CSM workspaces with project templates and a copilot ([Quivly](https://www.quivly.ai/), [Gainsight](https://www.gainsight.com/press/gainsight-proves-ai-native-services-model-for-retention-as-atlas-manages-over-75m-in-customer-arr/), [ChurnZero](https://churnzero.com/blog/churnzero-product-release-notes-june-2026), [Vitally](https://www.vitally.io/solution/csm)).
- **Business Model:** Unlimited-seat subscriptions cost $500/month for 5 agents and up to 50 accounts or $1,000/month for 10 agents and up to 100 accounts, with custom annual Enterprise pricing and a 20% annual billing discount on listed tiers ([Quivly Pricing](https://www.quivly.ai/pricing), Nov 2026).
- **TAM/SAM:** The broader customer-success platforms market was estimated at $760M in 2025 with 19.5% forecast CAGR through 2031, and no public TAM/SAM data was found for the usage-priced segment ([Ken Research](https://www.kenresearch.com/industry-reports/global-customer-success-management-platforms-market), Aug 2026).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Quivly offers CEO-led demos, publishes a post-sales report, runs workshops and offers a 30-day paid Enterprise pilot creditable toward the first year ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/VN0-quivly-ai-agents-that-run-everything-after-the-sale-from-onboarding-to-renewal), [Quivly Report](https://www.quivly.ai/state-of-post-sales), [July Recap](https://www.quivly.ai/blog/july-2026-updates), [Pricing](https://www.quivly.ai/pricing)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Mycroft runs security implementations as Quivly projects with milestones and billable-hour tracking, providing evidence of workflow embedding at one customer ([Mycroft case study](https://www.quivly.ai/customers/mycroft), Jun 2026).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Accumulated account history and configured workflows could raise migration costs, with no public data found on retention or migration costs ([Quivly](https://www.quivly.ai/)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage ([Gainsight Atlas](https://www.gainsight.com/press/gainsight-proves-ai-native-services-model-for-retention-as-atlas-manages-over-75m-in-customer-arr/), [ChurnZero release notes](https://churnzero.com/blog/churnzero-product-release-notes-june-2026)).
- **Commoditization risk:** ChurnZero already runs agents inside existing Plays, and Vitally’s MCP can write tasks and notes, giving both vendors a path to automate workflows from account data they already hold ([ChurnZero](https://churnzero.com/blog/churnzero-product-release-notes-june-2026), [Vitally MCP docs](https://docs.vitally.io/en/articles/15862100-vitally-mcp-server)).

## Key Risks

- **Native substitution:** Gainsight embeds AI in its retention suite and sells a managed renewal service, overlapping with Quivly’s renewal workflows ([Gainsight](https://www.gainsight.com/press/gainsight-proves-ai-native-services-model-for-retention-as-atlas-manages-over-75m-in-customer-arr/), Oct 2026).
- **Connector dependency:** CRM, billing or messaging providers can change or block access, interrupting the data flows and actions Quivly’s agents use ([Quivly Product Terms](https://www.quivly.ai/product-terms)).
- **Agent reliability:** Inaccurate outputs can inform external messages or CRM changes, and Quivly offers human approvals and Enterprise approval policies ([Quivly Product Terms](https://www.quivly.ai/product-terms), [Pricing](https://www.quivly.ai/pricing)).
- **Data approval:** The terms permit de-identified customer data to be used for Quivly model improvement, with customer opt-out and contractual exclusion available ([Quivly Product Terms](https://www.quivly.ai/product-terms)).
