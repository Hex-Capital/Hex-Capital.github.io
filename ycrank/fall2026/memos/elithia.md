## The Idea

- **Problem:** In malpractice and serious injury cases, physicians, nurses, and paralegals may spend days manually reconstructing a patient's history from thousands of unordered or duplicate pages ([Launch YC, Nov 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/X7B-elithia-agents-that-review-medical-records-for-malpractice-and-injury-cases)).
- **Approach:** Elithia removes blank and duplicate pages, sorts and dates documents, reconciles conflicting evidence, and builds an editable chronology with links to source pages for expert sign-off and Word or PDF export ([Elithia Product](https://elithia.com/products), [Launch YC, Nov 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/X7B-elithia-agents-that-review-medical-records-for-malpractice-and-injury-cases)).
- **Differentiation:** Elithia centers physician-expert review and sign-off, EvenUp bundles chronologies with plaintiff demand drafting, and Medrecords AI serves physician reviewers with an on-premises option ([Elithia Product](https://elithia.com/products), [EvenUp](https://www.evenuplaw.com/about/), [Medrecords AI](https://medrecords.ai/pricing/)).
- **Business Model:** The first 1,000 pages are free for physician and nurse experts, and Elithia reports sales to physician experts and medico-legal firms without disclosing paid rates or billing terms ([Launch YC, Nov 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/X7B-elithia-agents-that-review-medical-records-for-malpractice-and-injury-cases), [YC company profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/elithia)).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment ($64.1 billion in U.S. workers’ compensation benefits paid in 2023 measures claim payouts, per the [National Academy of Social Insurance, Aug 2026](https://www.nasi.org/2026/08/11/workers-compensation-benefits-costs-and-coverage-2023-data/)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Elithia used the Minerva brand at medico-legal conferences before its November 2026 YC launch and now offers self-serve trials for experts and demos for law firms, with third-party administrators and insurers listed as expansion targets ([Minerva Our Story](https://minervalabs.ai/our-story), [Elithia LinkedIn](https://uk.linkedin.com/company/elithia), [Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/X7B-elithia-agents-that-review-medical-records-for-malpractice-and-injury-cases), [YC company profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/elithia)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Elithia lets experts edit cited findings and configure organization-specific report layouts and branding, with customer templates isolated by organization ([Elithia Product](https://elithia.com/products)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Physician corrections could improve specialty-specific relevance ranking, but Elithia publishes no measured improvement from that feedback and says customer records are excluded from model training ([Launch YC, Nov 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/X7B-elithia-agents-that-review-medical-records-for-malpractice-and-injury-cases), [Elithia security statement](https://elithia.com/)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** Medrecords AI and Wisedocs already offer source-linked medical chronologies, and Medrecords AI also targets physician reviewers ([Medrecords AI](https://medrecords.ai/), [Wisedocs Product](https://www.wisedocs.ai/product/medical-record-review)).

## Key Risks

- **Technical accuracy:** A cited chronology can still omit a material event or misread contradictory notes, and Elithia’s published controls are source-page links, editable entries, and expert sign-off ([Elithia Product](https://elithia.com/products), [Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/X7B-elithia-agents-that-review-medical-records-for-malpractice-and-injury-cases)).
- **U.S. health-data procurement:** Where HIPAA applies, cloud processing of protected health information requires a business associate agreement, and Elithia publicly describes encryption, regional hosting, and GDPR controls without publishing its U.S. agreement terms ([HHS cloud guidance](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/special-topics/health-information-technology/cloud-computing/index.html), [Elithia security statement](https://elithia.com/)).
- **Insurer distribution:** [Inferred]: Agency contracts establish a channel to physician experts, while insurer and administrator sales would put Elithia up against Wisedocs’ existing carrier and administrator deployments ([YC company profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/elithia), [Wisedocs](https://www.wisedocs.ai/company/about-us)).
- **Entity identification:** YC lists San Francisco and one team member, Elithia’s LinkedIn lists London and 2–10 employees, Companies House records Minerva Labs Limited’s UK incorporation in December 2025, and a separate Romanian clinic operator uses the Elithia name ([YC company profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/elithia), [Elithia LinkedIn](https://uk.linkedin.com/company/elithia), [Companies House](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/16933495), [Romanian trademark office](https://osim.ro/images/Publicatii/Marci/2025/cereri_admise_15122025.pdf)).
