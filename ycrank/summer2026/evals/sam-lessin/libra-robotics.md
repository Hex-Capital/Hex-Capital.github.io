# libra-robotics -- Sam Lessin Evaluation

I start with three blanks: no proposed post-money valuation, no competing-term-sheet count, and no evidence that this is the first scarce check. The “dossier” contains only a statement that live research was intended; it supplies no resulting facts about libra-robotics. Under my price gate, financing silence earns exactly the absent-evidence prior. It does not prove an auction, but it certainly does not prove cheap non-consensus access.

The same problem kills the venture case. There is no named robotics hypothesis, finite experiment, metric, deadline, or use-of-funds plan. “Robotics” could mean a difficult physical-system breakthrough or a fashionable wrapper around commodity hardware; the record gives me no basis to distinguish them. I cannot underwrite capital whose truth-revealing job has not been stated.

There is also no documented product, deployment, paid pilot, proprietary dataset, embedded customer workflow, repeat usage, or purchase. Pre-seed companies should not be punished for lacking scale, but one informative receipt—a working prototype in a real operating environment, a named LOI, or repeated use—would matter. Here there is not even evidence from which to identify the two systems being joined, much less proof that the founders have shipped on both sides.

The strongest bull case is that public silence at pre-seed can hide exceptional technical work and customer access. That is true, but none of my known exceptions rests on hidden potential: each required unusually hard external evidence such as adoption, conversion, retention, or an acquisition offer. Libra-robotics provides no such override. Nor can I test the physical-world software pattern, founder accountability, model dependency, or whether the company is forcing a service business into a venture narrative, because the necessary facts are absent.

My verifiable flip condition is a signed paid pilot showing that libra-robotics’ deployed system replaces a named customer workflow and produces a measured economic result. Until that exists in the record, I am not investing my own money. This is a pass on available evidence, not a conclusion that the underlying company is bad.
