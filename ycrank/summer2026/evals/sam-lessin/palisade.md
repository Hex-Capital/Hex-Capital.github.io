# Palisade -- Sam Lessin Evaluation

The first three numbers I need are absent: post-money valuation, competing-term-sheet count, and the amount being raised. That matters because Palisade sits inside an already fashionable AI-commerce category, opposite Rep AI, Constructor, and Nosto, while relying partly on computer-use capabilities available from Anthropic. Without terms, I cannot tell whether I am buying a cheap, non-consensus experiment or paying YC-auction pricing for a public thesis.

The real signal is the more than $2.7 million in marketplace GMV Palisade says it drove since the Summer 2026 batch began. Shorebreak and Vaulted Atelier are live in production, and the product occupies the transaction workflow: it remembers searches, contacts buyers by email or SMS, updates CRM records, and navigates toward checkout. This is stronger than audience or integration theater because Palisade claims a causal commercial outcome. The missing pieces are attribution, retained usage, and how much of that GMV becomes Palisade revenue.

There is a credible translation layer here. Buyer, seller, and marketplace staff agents could coordinate parties whose information normally remains fragmented across catalogs, booking systems, CRMs, and abandoned sessions. Jonathan Salama has worked on Amazon traffic and marketing, built consumer-shopping agents at Sage AI Labs, and co-created Lulus TV to roughly one million subscribers. Those are meaningful receipts across commerce infrastructure and consumer attention, although the dossier does not establish that he previously owned a scaled marketplace operation.

The bull case stings: one founder, two production deployments, and $2.7 million of attributed GMV in weeks may be the rare operating evidence that overrides an expensive or fashionable AI round. Palisade could be discovering that the durable asset is not the agent model but the persistent purchase-intent history embedded across the marketplace workflow. A one-person team with no active job postings also looks far more capital-efficient than the usual plan to hire twenty people and manufacture Series A metrics.

I cannot underwrite that exception yet. Paid plans exist, but there is no disclosed revenue, gross margin, burn, retention, conversion baseline, or evidence that customers would keep paying after an agent error. Nor does the dossier name what the round will prove, by what date, or why the experiment cannot be financed from customer receipts. If the next foundation-model release commoditizes navigation, Palisade is left competing on integrations and sales execution against incumbents with $14.4 million to more than $85 million already raised.

My base case is that within 36 months Palisade becomes a narrower agent layer for high-consideration marketplaces, reaches roughly $1 million–$3 million ARR, and abandons the broader claim that buyer, seller, and staff agents constitute a horizontal marketplace operating system. I am not writing a check today. The single flip condition is a sub-$10 million post-money financing plan explicitly tied to converting Shorebreak and Vaulted Atelier into 90-day retained, positive-contribution-margin annual contracts by a fixed date; that would simultaneously establish scarce pricing and a truth-revealing venture moment.
