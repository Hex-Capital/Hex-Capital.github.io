## The Idea

- **Problem:** Government adjudicators process millions of permit, license, patent, drug-approval, grant, and benefits applications using paper and legacy software, leaving applicants waiting months or years ([Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/stratum-industries), Aug 2026).
- **Approach:** Stratum reads each application, evaluates it against an agency-selected rubric, routes individual steps to different models, requests missing information, and presents a cited summary for a human officer’s decision ([company website](https://stratumindustries.co/), Aug 2026).
- **Differentiation:** Stratum targets cross-domain application adjudication at federal, state, and local levels, whereas GovWell concentrates on municipal permitting and licensing, Polimorphic on constituent CRM and communications, and Clariti on community-development permitting and plan review ([Stratum](https://stratumindustries.co/), [GovWell](https://govwell.com/), [Polimorphic](https://www.polimorphic.com/), [Clariti](https://www.claritisoftware.com/), Aug 2026).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: The demo-request form and absence of public pricing indicate agency contracts negotiated through direct sales rather than self-service subscriptions ([Stratum demo page](https://stratumindustries.co/demo), Aug 2026).
- **TAM/SAM:** The broad global smart-government market was $34.4 billion in 2023 and is projected to grow at a 20.2% CAGR through 2030, while no public data found for a U.S. application-adjudication SAM ([Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/horizon/outlook/smart-government-market-size/global), 2024).
- **GTM / Distribution:** [Inferred]: Founder-led agency sales and referrals are the initial path because the launch asks government adjudication personnel to request meetings and others to introduce agency operators ([YC Launch](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/SBY-stratum-industries-clearing-the-entire-u-s-government-backlog), Jul 2026).

## Key Risks

- **High-impact AI compliance:** Federal AI that materially affects rights, benefits, or safety requires pre-deployment testing, impact assessments, human oversight, fail-safes, and appeal processes; Stratum states that humans retain final decisions and every step is auditable ([OMB M-25-21](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/M-25-21-Accelerating-Federal-Use-of-AI-through-Innovation-Governance-and-Public-Trust.pdf), Apr 2025; [company website](https://stratumindustries.co/), Aug 2026).
- **Accuracy across heterogeneous programs:** OMB identifies inaccurate, misleading, unreliable, and discriminatory outputs as AI risks, while Stratum proposes serving applications ranging from patents and drug approvals to disability benefits under different rubrics ([OMB M-25-21](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/M-25-21-Accelerating-Federal-Use-of-AI-through-Innovation-Governance-and-Public-Trust.pdf), Apr 2025; [company website](https://stratumindustries.co/), Aug 2026).
- **Competitive channel overlap:** GovWell reports more than 150 agencies and 5,000 government processes, while Polimorphic and Clariti have raised $28 million each to sell overlapping government workflow products ([GovWell](https://govwell.com/), Aug 2026; [Polimorphic](https://www.polimorphic.com/articles/ai-startup-polimorphic-raises-18-6-million-led-by-general-catalyst), Sep 2025; [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/clariti), Aug 2026).
- **Name collision:** [Inferred]: Search discovery may be affected by an unrelated Bangalore industrial-supply business using the same “Stratum Industries” name and the stratum-industries.com domain ([unrelated company website](https://www.stratum-industries.com/), Aug 2026).
