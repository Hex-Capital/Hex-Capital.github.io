## The Idea

- **Problem:** Raven targets people who spend work time switching among phone apps to handle email, calendars, calls and research themselves ([Launch YC, Nov 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Whh-raven-the-next-phone-for-the-world-of-agents)).
- **Approach:** Users delegate a task from the phone, agents act across tools such as Gmail, Slack and Google Calendar, and Raven surfaces the result or presents a draft for approval ([Raven product page](https://ravenphone.com/)).
- **Differentiation:** Raven combines its own agents with Codex and Claude in a handset interface, Samsung’s Galaxy S26 integrates Bixby, Gemini and Perplexity, and Phone Harness gives an external agent control of an existing or cloud-hosted phone ([Raven product page](https://ravenphone.com/), [Samsung Mobile Press, Feb 2026](https://api.samsungmobilepress.com/press-releases/samsung-unveils-galaxy-s26-series-intuitive-galaxy-ai), [Phone Harness YC profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/phone-harness)).
- **Business Model:** Raven lists a refundable $199 pre-order covering use of the handset and the first three months after activation, followed by a projected $99–$199 per month for the phone, AI features and cellular service, with the handset returned when membership ends ([Raven checkout and terms, Oct 2026](https://ravenphone.com/checkout/terms/)).
- **TAM/SAM:** The broader GenAI-capable smartphone market was forecast at approximately 486 million shipments in 2026, calculated from 45% of 1.08 billion global smartphone shipments, with share rising from 36% in 2025 ([Counterpoint Research, Jun 2026](https://counterpointresearch.com/default.htm/insights/genai-smartphone-share-to-rise-to-45-percent-of-global-shipments-in-2026)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Usmanov initially described the Sintem handset as a device for employees, and Raven opened public direct pre-orders on November 4 alongside a free waitlist and a device inquiry form for individuals and teams ([Isa Usmanov on LinkedIn, via search snippet](https://de.linkedin.com/in/isausmanov), [RuntimeWire, Nov 2026](https://runtimewire.com/article/raven-opens-preorders-agent-phone-isa-usmanov), [Raven waitlist](https://ravenphone.com/waitlist/), [Raven device inquiry](https://ravenphone.com/reach-out/)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** No defensibility signals found in public sources.
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Saved workflows, conversation history and user-created apps could raise switching costs if users depend on them for recurring work, with retention and usage data still undisclosed ([Raven product page](https://ravenphone.com/)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** Phone Harness already offers agent control of existing devices, and Nothing lets handset users generate home-screen widgets from prompts, making parts of Raven’s proposition available through separate products ([Phone Harness](https://phone-harness.com/), [Nothing Essential Apps update, Oct 2026](https://nothing.community/d/63586-essential-apps-update-october-2026)).

## Key Risks

- **Hardware delivery:** Raven is still working with hardware partners and has published neither final specifications nor a commercial ship date, with full refunds available before shipment ([Raven checkout](https://ravenphone.com/checkout/)).
- **Agent reliability:** [Inferred]: Cross-app tasks can fail when an integration changes or an agent misreads context, while Raven’s terms describe integrations as subject to change and its product page shows approval steps for some actions ([Raven terms](https://ravenphone.com/checkout/terms/), [Raven product page](https://ravenphone.com/)).
- **Access to personal data:** [Inferred]: Email, calendar and call handling put sensitive information within reach of the device and its connected services, whose privacy disclosures Raven says it will provide before use ([Raven product page](https://ravenphone.com/), [Raven terms](https://ravenphone.com/checkout/terms/)).
- **Incumbent substitution:** [Inferred]: Galaxy S26 owners can delegate supported multi-step tasks to Gemini from the side button, reducing the need to switch handsets for those workflows ([Google, Feb 2026](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/android/samsung-unpacked-2026/)).
- **Name collision:** [Inferred]: The unrelated Raven PBX also uses “Raven Phone,” creating potential confusion in searches for the handset ([Raven PBX downloads](https://ravenpbx.com/downloads), [Raven product page](https://ravenphone.com/)).
