# Frontrunner -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Frontrunner’s strongest route is a strategic acquisition by a sales or revenue operations platform. Its shared graph connects CRM, email, calendar, product, and call data so agents can act on buying signals and feed sales outcomes back into later workflows. Zoom’s acquisition of Common Room establishes that a platform has bought adjacent capabilities. A buyer could acquire Frontrunner to add cross-system workflow execution and feedback to its own product. That rationale is an inference, and the memo provides no priced transaction benchmark. (The Idea, Market & Traction)

Foresight Health’s CEO reported seven meetings in 48 hours after onboarding. Frontrunner separately reported a 15% higher new-business win rate. These are early usage signals. The seed-stage milestone is repeatable incremental qualified pipeline against customers’ existing stacks across several retained, paying deployments. The founders’ GTM engineering experience supports running that test, while paid retention, runway, and the capital required remain open. A software acquisition is plausible, with assignment of customer agreements, rights to use customer data, and continued API access still to verify. The memo identifies no independent liquidity route for this holder. (Market & Traction, Founders & Team, Defensibility, Key Risks)

The benchmark assumes a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, 5% ownership diluted to 3%, and no follow-ons. The 5× objective is $2.5 million in cash within eight years, requiring about $83.3 million in distributable equity value at 3%. The sale scenarios assume full holder participation, all-cash payment within eight years, and a pro rata waterfall with no senior claims.

- **Modest sale:** A hypothetical $50 million enterprise value yields $1.5 million to the holder with zero net debt and deal adjustments. Any deductions reduce that payout.
- **Strong sale:** A hypothetical $100 million enterprise value yields $3 million under the same assumptions. The holder clears the objective with up to about $16.7 million in net debt and downward deal adjustments. With $20 million in deductions, distributable equity falls to $80 million and the holder receives $2.4 million.
- **Prolonged private:** Continued operation with reinvestment and no eligible share purchase or funded payout yields zero realized cash within eight years.

The memo leaves sale pricing and the actual holder waterfall open. These scenarios show what would clear the hurdle.

### Countercase

Zoom already bundles Common Room’s buyer signals and agents with sales conversation data. Clay offers configurable workflows, and Unify offers account research and intent-triggered outbound. A buyer may be able to use capabilities it already owns or can source elsewhere, especially if Frontrunner’s agents continue to use stale data or stop early in live workflows. That weakens the case for paying an acquisition premium. The memo establishes neither a structural closing block nor a supported sale-price ceiling below the holder’s hurdle, so the Pass gate remains open pending evidence. (Market & Traction, Defensibility, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Would verified incremental pipeline across retained paying customers give a strategic buyer an economic reason to pay enough to leave at least $83.3 million in distributable equity value? Production comparisons against customers’ existing stacks, paired with a buyer-side estimate of the value of owning the product versus building it, would answer that question. A supported price range that clears the hurdle would move the action to Reach out. A supported ceiling materially below it would trigger Pass.
