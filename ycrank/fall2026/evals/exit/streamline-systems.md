# Streamline Systems -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

A strategic acquisition by a drone manufacturer or navigation supplier is Streamline’s strongest route to holder liquidity. Streamline reports that its retrofit module works with existing drones and that it demonstrated autonomous flight without satellite positioning. Owning a qualified version of the module could save an acquirer development and integration time. The memo contains no measured savings or evidence of acquisition interest. (*The Idea*, *Defensibility*, *Key Risks*)

The next operating milestone is independent qualification on an OEM platform followed by repeat orders outside the Drone Dominance Program. Streamline reports more than $80,000 in contracted module revenue and deposits reserving more than $87,000 worth of modules. The overlap and deposit amount are undisclosed. The memo lists $500,000 in YC financing, while shipment volumes, cash collection, and the capital needed to reach the milestone remain unclear. (*The Idea*, *Market & Traction*, *Key Risks*)

The shared benchmark assumes a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, 5% initial ownership diluted to 3%, and no follow-ons. A 5× gross cash return requires $2.5 million within eight years, or about $83.3 million in distributable equity value at 3%. Both sale scenarios below assume $5 million in combined net debt and deal costs, no senior claims diverting proceeds, and an eligible holder selling their entire stake for cash at closing within eight years. The sale prices are illustrative assumptions. Buyer pricing and holder terms are unverified.

| Scenario | Proceeds bridge | Gross cash to holder |
|---|---|---:|
| Modest sale | $50M enterprise value less $5M in adjustments leaves $45M for pro rata distribution | $1.35M (2.7×) |
| Strong sale | $100M enterprise value less $5M in adjustments leaves $95M for pro rata distribution | $2.85M (5.7×) |
| Prolonged private | Operating cash is reinvested, with no eligible secondary sale or payout within eight years | $0 |

In the strong case, another $11.7 million in deductions from the pro rata pool would erase the margin above the hurdle. The memo supplies no evidence of an independent route to holder cash.

### Countercase

An OEM could meet its navigation needs through a supply contract. Established navigation suppliers give it options, and Streamline’s disclosed demand comes from manufacturers competing in a single procurement program. With independent flight performance and repeat shipments unverified, Streamline has yet to show why an OEM would pay an acquisition premium. This warrants Watch. The memo establishes neither a structural closing barrier nor a credible sale-price ceiling below the holder’s hurdle. (*Defensibility*, *Market & Traction*, *Key Risks*)

### Decisive diligence question

What price could a strategic buyer justify once Streamline earns independent OEM qualification and repeat orders outside Drone Dominance? A buyer-backed valuation range tied to that milestone would move the action to Reach out if expected cash to the eligible holder clears $2.5 million within eight years after dilution, deal adjustments, and senior claims. A supported upper bound materially below the hurdle would move the action to Pass.
