## The Idea

- **Problem:** Caladh targets drone threats to ports, naval bases, and subsea infrastructure, identifying nets, marine mammals, and kinetic responses as existing alternatives ([Caladh](https://caladh.com), Nov 2026).
- **Approach:** Eclipse denies unmanned vehicles’ navigation, communications, and sensing from buoys or tow-fish, while seabed-deployed Cloak degrades and deceives underwater sensing ([Caladh](https://caladh.com), Nov 2026).
- **Differentiation:** Caladh advertises non-kinetic denial and concealment, while Sonardyne’s Sentinel emphasizes underwater detection and Leonardo DRS’s M-MEP addresses aerial threats from maritime platforms ([Caladh](https://caladh.com); [Sonardyne](https://www.sonardyne.com/product/sentinel/); [Leonardo DRS](https://www.leonardodrs.com/news/press-releases/leonardo-drs-launches-new-maritime-counter-uas-capability-to-defeat-aerial-unmanned-threats-at-sea/), accessed Nov 2026).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Hardware sales with deployment services are a possible monetization path; published pricing: No public data found.
- **TAM/SAM:** The adjacent global maritime counter-drone market is estimated at $1.98 billion in 2026, reaching $4.2 billion by 2030 at 20.7% CAGR, without separately sizing Caladh’s underwater segment ([The Business Research Company, 2026](https://www.thebusinessresearchcompany.com/report/maritime-counter-drone-system-global-market-report)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Government and industry buyers can request briefings, site assessments, and pilots ([Caladh](https://caladh.com), Nov 2026).
