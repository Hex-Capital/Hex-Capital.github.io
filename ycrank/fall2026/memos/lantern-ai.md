## The Idea

- **Problem:** Enterprise recruiters use applicant tracking systems (ATSs), notes, and browser tabs to manage hiring, and Lantern says hiring managers’ criteria get lost between handoffs from sourcing through interviews ([Launch YC, Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WZB-lantern-agentic-recruiting-platform-for-the-future)).
- **Approach:** Lantern combines a live graph of professional and career signals with an employer-specific model of hiring criteria, feedback, and decisions that its sourcing, application review, interview, note-taking, and analytics agents share ([Lantern](https://lantern.md/)).
- **Differentiation:** Ashby builds interviewing and automation into its own ATS, Gem centers sourcing and outreach on its recruiting CRM, and Juicebox emphasizes natural-language candidate search, while Lantern applies employer-approved changes to hiring criteria across agents that can work on top of an existing ATS ([Ashby](https://www.ashbyhq.com/blog/culture/ashby-one-2026-keynote), [Gem](https://help.gem.com/external/gem-ai-features-overview), [Juicebox](https://juicebox.ai/blog/series-b), [Lantern](https://lantern.md/)).
- **Business Model:** Enterprise subscription fees and usage limits are set by customer agreement, and Lantern People, the separate agent-facing service, offers a three-day trial followed by paid plans with credits for paid research endpoints ([Lantern Terms, Sep 2026](https://lantern.md/terms-of-service), [Lantern People](https://people.lantern.md/)).
- **TAM/SAM:** Mordor Intelligence estimates the broader global recruitment software market at $3.77B in 2026 and forecasts 7.85% CAGR through 2031, with no public SAM estimate found for Lantern’s specific segment ([Mordor Intelligence, 2026](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/recruitment-software-market)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Lantern books enterprise demos and describes selling an initial workflow, expanding accounts through adjacent modules, then offering to replace the customer’s ATS ([Lantern](https://lantern.md/), [Thomas Chan via Lantern LinkedIn, Nov 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ai-lantern/)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Lantern reports a graph covering one billion professionals and captures employer-approved criteria and debrief feedback for reuse across its agents ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/lantern-ai), [Lantern](https://lantern.md/)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Accumulated hiring decisions could raise switching costs if customers depend on that history across workflows, but the company’s product documentation supplies no comparative performance or migration data ([Lantern](https://lantern.md/)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** Ashby previews scheduled agents and has acquired AI interviewer technology for its ATS, while Workday has announced a talent acquisition agent and embedded candidate grading in its recruiting workflow ([Ashby, May 2026](https://www.ashbyhq.com/blog/culture/ashby-one-2026-keynote), [Workday, Oct 2026](https://investor.workday.com/news-and-events/press-releases/news-details/2026/Workday-Launches-Autopilot-Agents-That-Get-Real-Work-Done-Across-HR-Finance-and-IT/default.aspx), [Workday, Nov 2026](https://blog.workday.com/en-us/screen-candidates-50-faster-workday-recruiting.html)).

## Key Risks

- **Incumbent substitution:** ATS vendors can sell overlapping automation inside the customer’s existing system, as Ashby is doing with its assistant and native AI interviewer ([Ashby, May 2026](https://www.ashbyhq.com/blog/culture/ashby-one-2026-keynote)).
- **Assessment validity:** Lantern’s terms flag incomplete or inaccurate AI assessments and require human review of every final employment decision ([Lantern Terms, Sep 2026](https://lantern.md/terms-of-service)).
- **Hiring compliance:** Covered uses in New York City require bias audits and candidate notices, and Lantern assigns customers responsibility for compliant hiring criteria, notices, and decisions ([NYC DCWP](https://www.nyc.gov/site/dca/about/automated-employment-decision-tools.page), [Lantern Terms](https://lantern.md/terms-of-service)).
- **Integration coverage:** Analytics depends on the ATS records available through each integration, and Lantern checks connection methods and data flows during demos ([Lantern Analytics](https://lantern.md/analytics), [Lantern product FAQ](https://lantern.md/#product-answers)).
- **Name collision:** Search results include a Lantern extension for fundraising and warm introductions published by Jason Yeh, which can misattribute store metrics to the recruiting company ([Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/lantern/gcddjhpbkggofheoaeeipclpgllijkfp), [YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/lantern-ai)).
