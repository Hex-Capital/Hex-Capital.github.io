# Reconero -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

The strongest route is a strategic sale to a records or dispatch software vendor. Reconero says its product reads existing systems, links draft narratives to timestamped video, and fills agency forms for officer review. A vendor could buy that capability to expand what it sells to departments if the workflow saves time and works across agencies. The memo records a public launch and YC backing. The founders report consulting more than 100 officers and receiving feedback from several units that tested an earlier beta. That supports a plan for paid production pilots, though the capital and time needed for repeatable deployments remain unclear. The memo provides no buyer interest, company revenue, or transaction terms. (*The Idea*, *Market & Traction*, *Key Facts*)

Under the shared benchmark, a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation starts at 5% and dilutes to 3% with no follow-ons. A 5× gross cash return within eight years requires $2.5 million to the holder, or about $83.3 million in distributable equity value. The following are hurdle illustrations. Both sale cases assume the holder is eligible to sell the full stake and receives cash at closing pro rata, with no additional preference leakage.

- **Modest sale.** At $40 million in enterprise value less $5 million in net debt and deal adjustments, $35 million in distributable equity yields $1.05 million to the holder, or 2.1×.
- **Strong sale.** At $100 million in enterprise value less $10 million in net debt and deal adjustments, $90 million in distributable equity yields $2.7 million, or 5.4×. If the holder can sell only 80% of the stake at that price, cash received falls to $2.16 million, or 4.32×.
- **Prolonged private.** Continued operation with cash reinvested yields zero realized proceeds within eight years. The memo gives no evidence of an eligible secondary or funded payout. (*Market & Traction*, *Key Facts*)

### Countercase

Axon already bundles report drafting into its evidence platform, and Code Four already offers source-linked drafting across camera vendors. Those substitutes weaken a buyer’s reason to pay for Reconero. Reconero has disclosed no measured time savings, and a trial of a competing tool found no improvement in report-writing speed. The countercase supports Watch. A structural barrier to a sale or a supported price range that leaves the holder materially short would trigger Pass, and the memo establishes neither. (*Defensibility*, *Key Risks*)

### Decisive diligence question

Can Reconero show repeatable time savings without sacrificing report accuracy in paid deployments, enough for a records or dispatch vendor to justify buying the product at a price that clears the holder’s cash hurdle? Measured results across agencies and a buyer-side build-versus-buy assessment supporting at least $83.3 million in distributable equity value under the stated assumptions would move the action to Reach out.
