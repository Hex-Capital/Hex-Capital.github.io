# Collar -- Exit Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

A strategic acquisition by an investor-relations platform is Collar’s strongest supported exit route. Collar says it can flag conflicting figures and surface citation-backed answers from approved materials during live investor calls. An incumbent could add those capabilities to its existing investor targeting and earnings-preparation tools. Collar describes one anonymized example in which preparation fell from three weeks to about one day. Whether customers consistently see that benefit remains unverified. (*The Idea*, *Market & Traction*)

FactSet’s $120.2 million acquisition of Irwin shows that an adjacent investor-relations asset can sell at a price above the shared liquidity hurdle. Irwin’s focus on ownership monitoring and targeting limits what its sale price can establish for Collar’s newer live-call workflow. (*Market & Traction*)

The shared benchmark assumes a $500,000 check at a $10 million post-money valuation, 5% starting ownership diluted to 3%, and no follow-ons. Earning 5× in gross cash within eight years requires $2.5 million to the holder, or about $83.3 million in distributable equity at 3%. The sale scenarios assume closing within eight years, full holder eligibility, sale of the entire stake, cash payment at closing, and pro rata distribution with no senior preference claims. The prices are illustrative.

| Scenario | Enterprise-to-equity bridge | Gross cash to holder |
|-----------|-----------------------------|----------------------|
| Modest sale | $50M enterprise value becomes $50M distributable equity assuming no net debt or deal deductions | $1.50M (3.0×) |
| Strong sale | $100M enterprise value becomes $100M distributable equity on the same assumptions. With $20M in combined net debt and deal deductions, equity falls to $80M. | $3.00M (6.0×), or $2.40M (4.8×) after deductions |
| Prolonged private | Collar continues operating and reinvests available cash, with no eligible share purchase or distribution within eight years | $0 realized |

A concrete seed-stage milestone is converting compliance-gated prospects into paying customers that use the product across earnings cycles. Collar reports $1.4 million in deals pending compliance. The memo records $500,000 in seed funding, while conversion, current revenue dollars, runway, and the capital needed to reach the milestone remain unknown. Acquisition permissions and holder rights are unverified, and the memo gives no evidence of a separate liquidity route. (*Market & Traction*, *Key Risks*)

### Countercase

Incumbents may be able to build enough of Collar’s workflow themselves. Q4 has launched an AI-native IR CRM with automated interaction capture, and Notified has added market intelligence to its earnings-preparation tools. If those products satisfy customers, or compliance review stalls Collar’s deals, an incumbent has less reason to buy Collar at a price that clears the holder’s hurdle. The memo establishes neither a ready substitute for Collar’s live-call workflow nor a credible ceiling on sale proceeds, so the Pass gate remains open. (*Defensibility*, *Key Risks*)

### Decisive diligence question

Can at least three of Collar’s compliance-gated prospects become paying customers that document preparation time savings across two consecutive earnings cycles? Signed contracts and customer-confirmed usage and time savings would move the action to **Reach out** to test what an incumbent would pay for that capability. Until then, **Watch**.
