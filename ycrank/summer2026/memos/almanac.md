## The Idea

- **Problem:** Almanac targets company knowledge, decisions, customer context and outstanding work fragmented across Slack, Gmail, Calendar, Docs, GitHub and meeting notes, where employees otherwise retrieve information from the original tools ([Almanac website](https://usealmanac.com/), Aug 2026; [Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/almanac), Aug 2026).
- **Approach:** Connected accounts continuously populate a source-linked company wiki, while an assistant reached through Slack or iMessage uses integrations, a terminal and its own browser to answer questions and perform tasks ([Almanac website](https://usealmanac.com/), Aug 2026).
- **Differentiation:** Glean centers on enterprise search, a knowledge graph and 250 connectors, Dust lets teams build governed agents across more than 100 tools, and Sana combines search, agents and learning, while Almanac packages a continuously maintained wiki with iMessage access and a dedicated computer for tools lacking integrations ([Glean](https://www.glean.com/press/glean-raises-150m-series-f-at-7-2b-valuation-to-accelerate-enterprise-ai-agent-innovation-globally), Jun 2025; [Dust](https://rss.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/18/3296726/0/en/dust-raises-40m-to-make-ai-multiplayer-inside-the-enterprise.html), May 2026; [Workday](https://investor.workday.com/news-and-events/press-releases/news-details/2025/Workday-Signs-Definitive-Agreement-to-Acquire-Sana-09-16-2025/default.aspx), Sep 2025; [Almanac](https://usealmanac.com/), Aug 2026).
- **Business Model:** One plan costs $250 per user monthly after a three-day trial and includes 2,500 shared requests monthly, while enterprise pricing is custom and adds SSO, rollout support and custom controls ([Almanac pricing](https://usealmanac.com/pricing), Aug 2026).
- **TAM/SAM:** AI-powered knowledge-base software was valued at $15.36 billion in 2025 and forecast to grow at an 8.57% CAGR to $27.33 billion by 2032; no public SAM data was found for Almanac’s narrower company-assistant segment ([Research and Markets](https://www.researchandmarkets.com/reports/6123413/ai-powered-knowledge-base-software-market), 2026, via search snippet).
- **GTM / Distribution:** [Inferred]: The three-day trial, per-user plan and enterprise contact path indicate self-service team adoption followed by sales-assisted expansion into larger deployments ([Almanac pricing](https://usealmanac.com/pricing), Aug 2026).

## Key Risks

- **Product-transition risk:** [Inferred]: YC and company social pages recently described CodeAlmanac’s coding-agent wiki, while the current website now describes a company-wide executive assistant, so the prior product’s 791 GitHub stars cannot be treated as current-product adoption ([GitHub](https://github.com/AlmanacCode/codealmanac), Aug 2026; [Almanac](https://usealmanac.com/), Aug 2026).
- **Autonomous-action and data risk:** Almanac can read, create, transmit, publish or delete data through connected accounts, while its terms state that AI outputs and actions may be incomplete or inaccurate; mitigation includes revocable connections, source links and confirmation before sending, spending or committing ([Almanac Terms](https://usealmanac.com/terms), Aug 2026; [Almanac FAQ](https://usealmanac.com/), Aug 2026).
- **Third-party dependency:** Connected-service providers may change or discontinue APIs, permissions or features; Almanac mitigates part of this dependency by operating websites through its managed browser when no integration exists ([Almanac Terms](https://usealmanac.com/terms), Aug 2026; [Almanac website](https://usealmanac.com/), Aug 2026).
- **Competitive overlap:** Glean has 250 connectors and more than $100 million ARR, while Dust serves more than 3,000 organizations with 300,000 deployed agents, creating overlapping enterprise knowledge-and-action products ([Glean](https://www.glean.com/press/glean-raises-150m-series-f-at-7-2b-valuation-to-accelerate-enterprise-ai-agent-innovation-globally), Jun 2025; [Dust](https://rss.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/18/3296726/0/en/dust-raises-40m-to-make-ai-multiplayer-inside-the-enterprise.html), May 2026).
- **Name ambiguity:** [Inferred]: Searches for “Almanac” also surface unrelated brands such as the @Almanac_Sport sports-journalism account, increasing disambiguation requirements; the usealmanac.com domain and YC S26 profile provide verification ([X](https://x.com/Almanac_Sport), Aug 2026; [Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/almanac), Aug 2026).
