# Gradon -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

**Path B — New Economies.** Gradon’s strongest case is that civil engineering firms could profitably accept plan-set work they previously declined because the fee could not cover manual drafting, calculations, and licensed review. That market opening is a scenario assumption. Gradon reports completing a project in 180 hours against a 750-hour manual estimate. The implied 570-hour saving assumes comparable scope and quality. The memo confirms licensed approval is required and leaves open how much review, rework, and exception handling the 180 hours included. The relevant test is whether the full cost of an approved plan set, including those costs and an allocation for the seat fee and setup, falls below the fee a firm can earn on work it previously declined. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

The CEO reports mid-six-figure contracted ARR in four weeks and an initial civil engineering customer using Gradon for plan sets, CAD, and reports. These are early demand signals, with buyer-verified payment, repeat use, and newly accepted work still open. The team launched the AEC product after shipping Vendo and brings relevant software, mechanical design, and civil engineering experience. Gradon sells unlimited-use seats and describes at least a week of on-site deployment work. Expansion depends on whether seat revenue covers setup, support, and variable processing costs as usage rises. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Founders & Team, Key Risks)

### Countercase

Gradon may make existing projects faster while firms continue accepting the same work. If licensed review and corrections absorb enough of the reported savings, previously declined jobs could still be uneconomic. Under model parity, Gradon’s firm-specific standards, learned corrections, and configured workflows could give customers a reason to stay. Autodesk’s assistant preview in Revit and Civil 3D gives it a route to overlapping automation inside the tools Gradon uses. Buyer preference under functional parity remains untested. Both gates remain open, supporting Watch. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

### Decisive diligence question

Will an independent civil engineering firm pay for repeat plan-set work it previously declined and choose Gradon after testing a functionally equivalent alternative? Move to Reach out if a representative trial shows that the firm can profitably deliver licensed-approved work after all costs and still chooses Gradon at a price that supports Gradon’s delivery economics. Pass if representative evidence shows the work remains uneconomic or buyers can switch and retain the same outcome.
