# Zomma -- Elad Gil Evaluation

Anthropic’s October 2024 computer-use release and OpenAI’s January 2025 Operator launch make **Zomma** a recently opened workflow-infrastructure bet with unusually relevant finance operators, but no disclosed revenue curve. That market-catalyst strength paired with revenue silence is **Zomma’s** signature: the puck moved, yet the dossier does not show that Zomma is skating faster than Zalos, Eloquent AI, or Stacks.

**Stripe** is the high rung on market re-opening because APIs replaced merchant-account integration precisely as e-commerce compounded. **Zomma** shares the load-bearing mechanism: new computer-use models can finally traverse the custodian portals, desktop applications, and PDFs that APIs never reached. **Zomma** diverges because Anthropic and OpenAI supplied the catalyst to every developer, while Stripe controlled a differentiated interface; Zalos’s March 2026 financing and Eloquent AI’s $0.5M ARR show that several entrants recognized this window simultaneously.

**Lyft** is the low rung in my revenue-velocity decision record because category growth did not prove that the second network would capture attractive economics; activity could be a false signal without share inversion. **Zomma** reports unnamed hedge funds, asset managers, and fintech firms using reconciliation, KYC, onboarding, and reporting workflows, but Zomma discloses neither revenue nor usage growth. A “long waitlist,” 188 LinkedIn followers, and workshop attendance do not establish buying behavior. For an AI workflow product, the absence of a curve keeps Zomma much closer to false signal than Harvey-style “just working.”

**Jihyun Kim** helped launch a $1B multi-strategy hedge fund, while Cashel Fitzgerald built systems supporting $300M+ annual revenue and $20B+ transaction volume. Those artifacts give **Zomma** more shipping credibility than credential-only founders: Kim knows the workflow, Fitzgerald knows production financial systems, and Varun Arumugam brings ML and verification experience from Bronco AI and Apple. Over 18 months, that combination can compound through rapid correction loops on real customer desktops, but **Zomma** provides no Perplexity-like evidence that the founders ship new requested functionality between conversations.

**Zalos** has raised $3.6M for overlapping CFO computer agents, while Stacks has raised $35M across enterprise finance workflows. For **Zomma** to flip into a Stripe-like index position, Zomma would need to become the control and audit layer through which investment firms run every approval-gated desktop workflow, independent of custodian or model provider. The current forward-deployed workshops and customer-specific workflow maps point toward services-heavy deployments, not automatic category-wide distribution; proprietary correction histories could change that assessment, but the dossier labels them inferred rather than demonstrated.

**Color Genomics** worked because commodity sequencing had already happened and the remaining bet was workflow and distribution on top of the hard thing. **Zomma** wants the same architecture on top of frontier-model computer use, but Zomma has not yet isolated one miracle: reliable execution through UI changes, resistance to indirect prompt injection, and enterprise trust while SOC 2 remains in progress are independent requirements. Human approval reduces the consequence of errors, but it does not prove that the underlying automation is reliable or economical enough to scale.

**$7.78B** of agentic AI in financial services growing at a projected 41.12% CAGR gives **Zomma** room to build a meaningful business without winning all finance automation. My falsifiable 36-month forecast is that by July 2029, Zomma reaches $3M-$8M ARR concentrated in investment-operations deployments but remains a forward-deployed vertical vendor rather than the default computer-use layer for finance. **Zomma’s** catalyst and founder-market fit should produce customers; its missing curve, shared foundation-model dependency, and crowded distribution path should prevent index-like dominance.

**Unnamed hedge funds and asset managers already using Zomma** form the strongest opposing case: Kim’s direct hedge-fund operating history, approval-gated design, and the technical mix across ML, verification, and production finance could make Zomma a Color-style exception where workflow data becomes the moat. Zomma could then compound customer-specific corrections into better reliability faster than horizontal competitors. The dossier still gives no revenue, retention, exclusive data, or defensibility signal, so I would not write the personal check today. Verified $1M ARR across at least ten customers while sustaining greater than 20% monthly growth for three consecutive months would flip that verdict.
