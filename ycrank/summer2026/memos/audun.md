## The Idea

- **Problem:** Creditors rely on manual debt-collection processes, while debtors receive scripted outreach that may not account for disputes, hardship, or affordable repayment schedules ([Audun website, Aug 2026](https://getaudun.com/); [company LinkedIn, Jul 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/company/auduncollection)).
- **Approach:** Audun automates reminders across debtor-preferred channels, negotiates and schedules payment plans, records the interaction history, and escalates disputes or vulnerability cases to a human handler ([Audun website, Aug 2026](https://getaudun.com/)).
- **Differentiation:** [Inferred]: Altur concentrates on autonomous voice and text agents for banks, and AgentCollect on branded B2B recovery, whereas Audun presents a creditor-and-debtor workflow spanning pre-collection reminders, negotiation, plans, payouts, and human escalation ([Altur YC profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/altur); [AgentCollect YC profile](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/agentcollect); [Audun website](https://getaudun.com/)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Audun could charge creditors a platform fee, recovery-based fee, or combination of the two because it offers an end-to-end collection service, but no pricing is publicly displayed.
- **TAM/SAM:** Global debt-collection software was valued at $5.98 billion in 2025 and forecast to grow at a 9.72% CAGR through 2034, while the European segment was estimated at $2.5 billion in 2025 with an 8.6% forecast CAGR ([Fortune Business Insights, Jul 2026](https://www.fortunebusinessinsights.com/debt-collection-software-market-102966); [6Wresearch, Dec 2025](https://www.6wresearch.com/industry-report/europe-debt-collection-software-market)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Audun uses a creditor-facing “book a walkthrough” call to action, indicating direct sales to organizations managing overdue accounts ([Audun website, Aug 2026](https://getaudun.com/)).

## Key Risks

- **Licensing:** Audun’s advertised collection on behalf of creditors may constitute third-party collection, for which Norwegian agencies require a Finanstilsynet licence; Audun’s licence status could not be verified, although its human escalation and audit-history features may support supervised processes ([Finanstilsynet](https://www.finanstilsynet.no/en/about-us/finanstilsynets-main-goal/); [Audun website](https://getaudun.com/)).
- **Regulatory transition:** Norway enacted a replacement Debt Collection Act in May 2026, with commencement to be determined, creating implementation work around collection conduct and debtor protections ([Lovdata, May 2026](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2026-05-22-19/kap1)).
- **Automated debtor decisions:** GDPR generally restricts solely automated decisions with legal or similarly material effects, which may affect automated payment-plan or escalation logic; Audun states that disputes and vulnerability cases are handed to humans ([Datatilsynet](https://www.datatilsynet.no/rettigheter-og-plikter/den-registrertes-rettigheter/rettar-ved-automatiserte-avgjerder/); [Audun website](https://getaudun.com/)).
- **Brand disambiguation:** “Audun” is also the name of a Villeroy & Boch tableware collection, producing unrelated search results and potential discovery ambiguity; no public mitigation was found ([Villeroy & Boch campaign archive](https://milled.com/villeroy-boch.com/limited-edition-audun-maison-Rv7PeChVrjJFVC0T)).
