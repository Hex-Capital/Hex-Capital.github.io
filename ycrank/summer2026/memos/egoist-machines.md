## The Idea

- **Problem:** People using multiple AI apps accumulate separate profiles in ChatGPT, Claude, and other services, leaving them to re-explain preferences, history, and goals to each new tool ([Y Combinator, Aug 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/egoist-machines)).
- **Approach:** AI Passport collects proposed context in an inbox for user approval, lets applications request revocable access to specified categories, and records receipts showing which application read what and when ([Y Combinator, Aug 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/egoist-machines)).
- **Differentiation:** Unlike provider-specific ChatGPT memory and developer-controlled platforms such as Mem0 and Letta, Egoist places context in a user-controlled card with field-level requests, duration limits, approval, and access receipts ([OpenAI, Apr 2025](https://openai.com/index/memory-and-new-controls-for-chatgpt/); [Mem0, Oct 2025](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/mem0-raises-24m-series-a-to-build-memory-layer-for-ai-agents-302597157.html); [Letta, Sep 2024](https://www.letta.com/blog/announcing-letta/); [Egoist developer page, Aug 2026](https://ego.ist/developer)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Egoist advertises the consumer Passport as free for life and solicits developer pilots without publishing business pricing, indicating a possible paid developer or application-access model ([Egoist website, Aug 2026](https://ego.ist/); [Egoist developer page, Aug 2026](https://ego.ist/developer)).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** Distribution currently combines a consumer waitlist, direct outreach to consumer-AI developers, and founder-led promotion through Erin McGurk’s social channels and planned office and university visits ([GlobeNewswire, Jul 2026](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/13/3326210/0/en/y-combinator-backed-egoist-machines-launches-ai-passport-the-only-way-to-securely-store-and-share-your-personal-data-across-ai-assistants-like-chatgpt-and-claude.html); [Erin Meryl, Aug 2026](https://erinmerylstudy.com/); [LinkedIn, Jul 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/erin-mcgurk_do-you-want-a-free-bagel-if-youre-not-activity-7477210248395816961-3BUK)).

## Key Risks

- **Ecosystem coordination:** Passport’s value depends on applications requesting and honoring its passes, while public access remains organized through a waitlist and developer request form with no disclosed integration-partner count ([Egoist terms, Aug 2026](https://ego.ist/terms-of-use); [developer page, Aug 2026](https://ego.ist/developer)).
- **Security concentration:** Passport may process health, fitness, identity, preferences, OAuth credentials, and other personal context, making account or infrastructure compromise consequential; the privacy policy says no security method can be guaranteed absolutely ([Egoist privacy policy, Aug 2026](https://ego.ist/privacy-policy)).
- **Platform substitution and dependency:** OpenAI and Anthropic operate native memory systems and control APIs needed for cross-service access, allowing them to restrict integrations or add overlapping portability controls ([OpenAI, Jun 2026](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-memory-dreaming/); [Anthropic, Sep 2025](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-sonnet-4-5)).
- **Consent workload:** The announced workflow asks users to review new information and sharing requests, potentially shifting repeated onboarding into recurring approval decisions, although the company says users can set defaults for non-sensitive requests ([GlobeNewswire, Jul 2026](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/07/13/3326210/0/en/y-combinator-backed-egoist-machines-launches-ai-passport-the-only-way-to-securely-store-and-share-your-personal-data-across-ai-assistants-like-chatgpt-and-claude.html)).
