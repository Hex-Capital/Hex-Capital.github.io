# Hesper AI -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Medium

### Company case

**Usage-based expansion** is Hesper’s strongest path. An MGA claims team could start by routing a subset of P&C claims through Hesper, then increase paid claim volume if the agents reliably gather records, verify providers, and return evidence-linked findings for adjuster review. Hesper quotes pricing based on insurance line, claim volume, and agents deployed. The memo identifies integrations with Guidewire, Duck Creek, and Majesco that could let buyers expand use within their existing claims systems. Reusing those integrations across markets is plausible. Local coverage rules, access to provider records, and AI oversight could still require material changes. (The Idea, Defensibility, Key Risks)

Hesper reports paying production customers and more than $5 million in claim value processed. A founder reports that one MGA saw 82% less adjuster time per claim and $1.2 million in fraud identified over two weeks. The memo provides no account-level records connecting that result to payment, repeat use, or rising paid volume. For a pre-seed company, the reported workflow benefit makes the path credible enough to watch. The first unproven link is whether an MGA routes a growing share of its regular claims through Hesper once promotional terms end. (Market & Traction, Key Facts)

### Countercase

A founder-led rollout could deliver strong results on a limited batch of claims while requiring customer-specific setup and ongoing exception handling. The memo describes founder demos and a launch offer of 200 free claims and 90 days without a platform fee for eligible buyers. It gives no account-level route from demo or application to paid production, and no delivery-cost measurements for the next claim. The short MGA result leaves that countercase open. The structural downside gate does not apply because the memo does not establish that costs rise in proportion to volume or that expansion requires rebuilding the product. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Facts)

### Decisive diligence question

Can an MGA coming off the launch offer increase paid claim volume over successive billing periods at its normal claims cadence, with incremental revenue exceeding the added costs of verification, inference, and support? Account-level invoices, claim-routing logs, and delivery-cost records showing that pattern would move the action to **Reach out**. If the records show only initial paid use, keep **Watch**.
