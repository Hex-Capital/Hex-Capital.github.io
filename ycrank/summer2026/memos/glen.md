## The Idea

- **Problem:** Organizational context is distributed among agent sessions, Slack, GitHub, tickets, calls, and documents, forcing workplace agents to query individual sources and reconstruct decisions without their underlying rationale ([Glen website, Aug. 2026](https://www.tryglen.com/)).
- **Approach:** Glen continuously captures work from those sources, synthesizes it into an organization-wide store, injects relevant prior work into agent prompts, and distills repeated methods into reusable skills ([Glen website, Aug. 2026](https://www.tryglen.com/)).
- **Differentiation:** Glen combines automatic work capture and skill distillation, while Glean centers search and assistants over enterprise knowledge, Dust provides shared human-agent workspaces, Zep exposes a temporal context graph, and Mem0 supplies portable application memory ([Glen](https://www.tryglen.com/), [Glean](https://www.glean.com/press/glean-achieves-100m-arr-in-three-years-delivering-true-ai-roi-to-the-enterprise), [Dust](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/18/3296726/0/en/dust-raises-40m-to-make-ai-multiplayer-inside-the-enterprise.html), [S&P Global on Zep](https://www.getzep.com/downloads/451Research_Reprint_ZepSoftware_10APR2026.pdf), [Mem0](https://mem0.ai/series-a)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Paid B2B subscriptions are the likely monetization path because Glen’s terms reference paid plans and its subprocessor list identifies Stripe for subscription billing, although no public prices or tiers are displayed ([Terms](https://www.tryglen.com/terms), [subprocessors](https://www.tryglen.com/sub-processors)).
- **TAM/SAM:** The global enterprise-search market was $5.86 billion in 2025 and is forecast to reach $12.76 billion in 2034 at a 9.03% CAGR, while no Glen-specific SAM estimate was found ([Fortune Business Insights, July 2026](https://www.fortunebusinessinsights.com/enterprise-search-market-117155)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Glen uses a work-email waitlist, wave-based onboarding, founder-led calls, and design-partner access rather than public self-service signup ([Glen website, Aug. 2026](https://www.tryglen.com/)).

## Key Risks

- **Competitive overlap:** Dust reports more than 3,000 organizations, 51,000 monthly active users, and 300,000 deployed agents for a shared enterprise-agent product addressing the same fragmented-context problem ([Dust, May 2026](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/05/18/3296726/0/en/dust-raises-40m-to-make-ai-multiplayer-inside-the-enterprise.html)).
- **Sensitive-data processing:** Glen stores prompts, conversations, and organizational observations and sends customer content through OpenAI, Vercel, Neon, and Inngest, with mitigation through row-level isolation, per-observation access control, private mode, encryption, and a stated prohibition on training its own models with customer content ([Privacy Policy](https://www.tryglen.com/privacy), [subprocessors](https://www.tryglen.com/sub-processors)).
- **Context accuracy:** Glen’s terms state that recalled memory, summaries, and other outputs may be inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated, while the product provides source-linked decision histories for user verification ([Terms](https://www.tryglen.com/terms), [Glen website](https://www.tryglen.com/)).
- **Coverage limitations:** Glen says it reads public Slack channels and shared team transcripts, which can omit decisions contained in private channels or sessions, while private mode deliberately excludes sensitive sessions from storage ([Glen website, Aug. 2026](https://www.tryglen.com/)).
- **Name ambiguity:** “Glen” is shared with unrelated organizations, creating search-discovery ambiguity mitigated by the tryglen.com domain and tryglen LinkedIn handle ([Glen LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/tryglen), [unrelated GLEN organization](https://ie.linkedin.com/company/glen---gay-and-lesbian-equality-network)).
