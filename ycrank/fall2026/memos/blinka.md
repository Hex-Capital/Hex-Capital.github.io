## The Idea

- **Problem:** US-incorporated businesses operating abroad face account-access and fragmented-payment problems; Blinka’s CEO reports that another banking platform closed his foreign-operated LLC’s account and returned its balance by check ([Valora Analitik, Oct 2026](https://www.valoraanalitik.com/asi-es-la-empresa-que-dos-colombianos-crearon-en-ee-uu-y-que-ya-ha-procesado-mas-de-us10-millones-en-pagos/)).
- **Approach:** Blinka combines partner-bank USD accounts, ACH/wires, local-currency payouts, cards, treasury investments, invoicing, and accounting automation in one interface ([Blinka, accessed Oct 27, 2026](https://blinka.co)).
- **Differentiation:** [Inferred]: Blinka’s positioning centers on US companies operated abroad, compared with Mercury’s general business banking, Airwallex’s global financial infrastructure, and Slash’s industry-specific workflows ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/blinka), [Mercury](https://mercury.com/blog/series-d-announcement), [Airwallex](https://www.airwallex.com/en-au/who-we-are), [Slash](https://www.slash.com/company/about)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Account and payment-service fees appear to be monetization paths because its terms authorize service charges and account deductions, while placing the fee schedule inside the platform rather than publishing public tiers ([Blinka Terms, Aug 2026](https://docs.blinka.co/legal/terms-of-service)).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** Blinka promotes account opening through founder conversations, a LegalOS visa-fee partnership, and sponsorship of founder events including Mexico Tech Week ([Blinka LinkedIn, accessed Oct 27, 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/company/blinka-tech)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** [Inferred]: Multi-entity financial integration may create switching costs, with one reported customer managing over 200 entities through one portal, although retention and migration data are unavailable ([Valora Analitik, Oct 2026](https://www.valoraanalitik.com/asi-es-la-empresa-que-dos-colombianos-crearon-en-ee-uu-y-que-ya-ha-procesado-mas-de-us10-millones-en-pagos/)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Embedded vendor, invoicing, and accounting workflows could deepen switching costs, but public evidence does not establish that outcome ([Blinka](https://blinka.co)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** [Inferred]: Existing business-banking providers could reproduce much of the bundle because Slash already offers global USD accounts, stablecoin payments, invoicing, and accounting automation; replication time and cost are unknown ([Slash, Apr 2026](https://www.slash.com/blog/series-c)).

## Key Risks

- **Provider dependency:** [Inferred]: Banking, card issuance, and stablecoin payouts depend on external providers including Portage Bank, Third National, and Mural Pay, exposing service continuity to partner availability and approvals ([Blinka](https://blinka.co), [Partner Disclosures](https://docs.blinka.co/legal/partner-disclosures)).
- **Eligibility mismatch:** LinkedIn advertises access for non-US entities, while the published platform terms require a US business entity or US-operating sole proprietor, leaving public eligibility descriptions inconsistent ([LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/blinka-tech), [Terms, Aug 2026](https://docs.blinka.co/legal/terms-of-service)).
- **Investment and settlement exposure:** Treasury investments can lose value and stablecoins carry network, contract, and regulatory risks; Blinka distinguishes investments from insured deposits ([Blinka disclosures](https://blinka.co)).
- **Attribution ambiguity:** Earlier Blinka lending products and an unrelated Blinka social-shopping app create potential confusion over historical revenue and app metrics ([Pygma](https://pygma.substack.com/p/we-bet-on-a-lawyer-and-a-drop-out), [unrelated app](https://blinkaapp.com/)).
