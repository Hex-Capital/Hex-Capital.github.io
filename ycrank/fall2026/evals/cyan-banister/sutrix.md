# Sutrix -- Cyan Banister Evaluation

In 2032, I want a researcher to change a study definition and get a revised dataset with every inclusion, exclusion, and disputed field traceable to its source. Sutrix’s recorded reconciliation decisions are a small, useful piece of that world. But getting there requires reliable production connections and study logic that survives reuse across institutions. The dossier establishes neither transition: FHIR connections are currently sandboxes, and reusable definitions are documented without evidence of cross-institution reuse. That future is my daydream; I cannot score it as something the company has already demonstrated.

The founders give me more to hold onto. Ritvik has actually run clinical research, including a 300-participant concussion study; Supratik brings software engineering and a prior Kairo exit. Four years of building together, including work at HSS, matters more to me than their school names. They have encountered the people and institutional machinery this product must accommodate. That is substantial first-believer evidence, although the record does not establish a lifelong obsession or show how they recruit beyond their existing partnership. Their earlier research reach belongs in the founder assessment, not in Sutrix’s customer count.

Why does preparing a study take weeks? Here the answer is concrete: disconnected EHR, REDCap, and wearable records leave researchers manually joining, checking, and reconciling information. Sutrix could return control over that preparation to the research team. This is a real operating wound, although the dossier identifies labor and delay rather than an artificial restriction or a gatekeeper collecting a quantified rent. Source-linked fields, approved mappings, and reviewer decisions also put the product beneath the final analysis. There is useful infrastructure here, but no demonstrated exclusive asset or dependency that makes Sutrix difficult to replace.

People have paid the team to solve this problem. The reported $150K-plus revenue, 10-plus paying institutions, and work across 50-plus studies are meaningful at this stage. The distinction that changes my check-writing decision is what those customers purchased. The founder describes opening access to software previously used to deliver research work; individually scoped engagements remain the documented commercial model. Customers paying for completed datasets proves pain and willingness to spend. It does not yet establish repeated use of the software by researchers themselves. I would also leave the claimed 28% improvement in treatment plans out of my conviction without its measurement method.

The strongest case against my hesitation is that delivering the work manually can reveal exactly what durable software must do. That fits my preference for unglamorous workflow repair and concealed infrastructure. But the same history can produce a capable research-services business whose hardest decisions remain with the founders. Layer Health and Dyania already address overlapping abstraction workflows; Sutrix needs its accumulated definitions and reconciliation records to make subsequent studies easier without proportionate staff effort. My willingness to back companies before revenue does not resolve that question: those exceptions require unusually strong founder and technical evidence supporting the future mechanism.

I am **Neutral** today. The PHI approval requirements, institutional agreements, and review gates are sensible safeguards, although their implementation still matters; the dossier establishes no severe privacy violation. It names a CEO and CTO and supplies no evidence of a leadership shell game or control deadlock. My hesitation comes from the product’s remaining distance, not an identified fatal gate. One verifiable result would flip me toward Invest: three existing institutions independently completing a second paid study using retained definitions and provenance, with documented preparation savings and declining Sutrix staff hours per study. That would show the team is building a layer researchers can operate.
