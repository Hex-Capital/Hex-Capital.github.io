# Anywhere -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Pass  
**Evidence confidence:** Medium

### Company case

Anywhere brings saved places, visits, and friends’ shared locations into one map. The company describes Fenn recommending places and connected agents adding pins. That workflow consumes AI and map services, so the building-blocks path scores zero. The memo identifies no constrained input that Anywhere supplies to a growing AI workload. (The Idea, Key Risks)

The industry-enabled path also scores zero. Consumers already save places in map lists and share recommendations in chats. Anywhere could reduce the work of finding those recommendations and turning them into plans, but the memo leaves open whether the savings make any previously impractical outing or planning frequency viable. (The Idea, Market & Traction)

Account-level place history and reciprocal friend sharing could create switching costs. That is an inference, with retention and friend-network density still unmeasured and conversational discovery already available in Google Maps. Existing workarounds and YC-reported launch signups support early interest. The listed 2.8/5 rating comes from nine ratings and offers limited evidence about repeat demand. The shipped iPhone app and Delaroche’s prior map delivery at Zenly and Life360 support execution credit. The free app has no disclosed revenue-to-cost bridge for serving another active group. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Founders & Team, The Idea)

### Countercase

If the effort of piecing together recommendations routinely causes friend groups to abandon outings, an agent that can read and update a shared map could make those outings practical at a higher frequency. Even with equally capable models, the outcome could still depend on access to the group’s place history and permissioned location data. The described product supports that mechanism in principle. Launch signups leave the change in actual outings untested, so the countercase does not clear the outreach gate. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction)

### Decisive diligence question

Can Anywhere make recurring outings practical for friend groups that regularly abandon plans because coordination takes too much time? A comparison with those groups’ existing chats and map lists, with access to equally capable AI in both workflows, would answer it if groups using Anywhere repeatedly completed outings they otherwise would have dropped and spent less time coordinating each one. That result would change the action to Reach out.
