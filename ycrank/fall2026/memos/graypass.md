## The Idea

- **Problem:** Financial platforms, business software, and developer tools can authenticate someone at login yet lack fresh evidence of control when a payment, permission change, or data export occurs later ([GrayPass announcement, October 2026](https://markets.businessinsider.com/news/stocks/graypass-introduces-post-login-security-to-check-who-is-still-in-control-before-critical-actions-1036654166)).
- **Approach:** GrayPass combines typing, pointer, and scrolling patterns with identity, device, session, and transaction context, evaluates action policies, and returns decisions plus short-lived signed proof for allowed actions ([GrayPass](https://graypass.com/)).
- **Differentiation:** GrayPass emphasizes application-enforced, action-bound authorization, whereas BioCatch emphasizes banking fraud intelligence, TypingDNA typing-based authentication, and Twosense continuous session authentication ([GrayPass](https://graypass.com/), [BioCatch](https://www.biocatch.com/company), [TypingDNA](https://www.typingdna.com/), [Twosense](https://www.twosense.ai/)).
- **Business Model:** Pricing starts at $500/month, with paid evaluations or annual agreements metered by assured actions or active Brainprint months under negotiated contracts ([evaluation form](https://graypass.com/evaluate), [terms](https://graypass.com/terms), accessed October 27, 2026).
- **TAM/SAM:** The broader behavioral biometrics market is estimated at $3.8B in 2026, reaching $16.2B by 2033 at 23.0% CAGR; GrayPass-specific SAM: No public data found ([Grand View Research, 2026](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/behavioral-biometrics-market)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Founder-led outbound, in-person demonstrations, and workflow-specific evaluations are documented acquisition channels ([CEO LinkedIn, 2026, via search snippet](https://www.linkedin.com/in/aravmathur); [evaluation form](https://graypass.com/evaluate)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** GrayPass claims ownership of its SDK, API, and models; exclusive datasets or granted patents: No public data found ([GrayPass terms](https://graypass.com/terms)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Embedded action policies and enrolled behavioral templates could create migration work, although public evidence does not establish customer retention or switching costs ([platform](https://graypass.com/platform)).
- **Market structure:** [Inferred]: No structural barrier identified at this stage, given existing behavioral authentication APIs and continuous authentication platforms ([TypingDNA](https://www.typingdna.com/), [Twosense](https://www.twosense.ai/)).
- **Commoditization risk:** TypingDNA already provides authentication APIs and Twosense connects behavioral mismatches to policy remediation, overlapping GrayPass’s inputs and enforcement integrations ([TypingDNA](https://www.typingdna.com/), [Twosense](https://www.twosense.ai/)).

## Key Risks

- **Technical validation:** Behavioral continuity remains experimental and unapproved for live enforcement, while externally measured accuracy and latency are unavailable; shadow evaluation is offered ([platform](https://graypass.com/platform), [terms](https://graypass.com/terms)).
- **Endpoint compromise:** GrayPass states that behavioral evidence cannot withstand a fully compromised endpoint and cannot independently establish identity, intent, or permission; other identity signals remain required ([platform](https://graypass.com/platform)).
- **Integration coverage:** [Inferred]: Applications must enforce decisions before protected actions, so omitted enforcement hooks leave gaps; registered actions initially run in shadow mode ([platform](https://graypass.com/platform)).
- **Consent requirements:** GrayPass requires written notice and affirmative consent before collecting Illinois users’ behavioral signals, with deletion and revocation controls provided ([GrayPass BIPA policy](https://graypass.com/legal/bipa)).
