# Hickory & Company -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Pass  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Hickory earns zero on both AGI paths. Its compliance agents use AI to retrieve evidence and draft case records. The memo identifies no scarce input that constrains an AI workload or buildout. For the second path, Hickory describes an existing workflow in which compliance staff gather evidence across legacy tools, request data from IT, and document decisions. Its agents could reduce that labor, with qualified staff still approving decisions. Hickory claims 10× faster queue processing, but supplies no benchmark or evidence that the savings make a previously unaffordable investigation or review cadence viable. (The Idea, Key Risks)

Institution-specific policies, cited evidence, and action logs could create switching costs if banks rely on them in recurring audits. The memo provides no retention evidence, and competing products already offer investigation and documentation agents. The public launch and conference appearance establish market activity, while the memo records no buyer trials, commitments, or paid demand. The team brings banking and fintech experience, including a compliance lead who used an internal AI tool at Seis. Dependable delivery to an external institution remains unverified. Pricing, integration effort, reviewer time, and rework costs are unknown, so the speed claim cannot establish economics for the next deployment. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Founders & Team, Key Risks, Key Facts)

### Countercase

A community bank might be able to investigate a class of alerts it currently defers because staff spend too much time collecting evidence. That is a scenario assumption grounded in Hickory’s description of manual backlogs. Even with access to equally capable models, the bank would still need a workflow that applies its policies, traces evidence, and records qualified approval. The memo supplies no buyer baseline showing which investigations are deferred for cost reasons, and Hickory’s stated account-data restrictions could limit the cases it can handle. The scenario therefore leaves both path scores at zero and triggers Pass. (The Idea, Key Risks)
