# GrayPass -- Hypergrowth Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Usage-based expansion is GrayPass’s strongest path. The likely first buyer is the security or engineering team at an application that repeatedly authorizes sensitive actions such as payouts. GrayPass documents a workflow in which the buyer starts with one protected action in shadow mode and can then enforce decisions through its server-side authorization logic. The same job and core API could carry across markets, with local changes to consent and data handling where required, as GrayPass’s Illinois policy illustrates. Cross-market adoption remains unverified. (The Idea; Key Risks)

If the first workflow proves valuable in production, an account could add more protected actions and increase billable usage. GrayPass’s terms describe allowances metered by assured actions or active Brainprint months, so expansion would depend on added usage translating into additional revenue. The memo records an evaluation process and pricing starting at $500 per month. GrayPass reports more than 900,000 customer-app sessions and $50,000 in October 2026 revenue as of launch. Those figures provide early activity and revenue signals, with no buyer-level link between discovery, paid activation, and production use. They also leave first-segment fit, repeat paid usage, and incremental contribution unverified. (The Idea; Market & Traction; Key Facts)

### Countercase

The first link likely to break is adding a second paid workflow after the first succeeds. Each protected action requires customer-side authorization work, the core decision API is in beta, and behavioral continuity signals are limited to sandbox and shadow evaluation. The reported sessions could therefore reflect evaluation activity while each additional production workflow still requires substantial manual integration. The structural downside gate does not apply because the memo gives no rollout-effort measurements showing that delivery costs rise in proportion to revenue or that integrations cannot be reused. (Key Risks; Market & Traction; Key Facts)

### Decisive diligence question

Can an early paying customer add a second protected workflow that generates recurring billable usage through a reusable integration? Move to Reach out if one account’s rollout and billing records show the second workflow running in production, adding billed usage over its normal operating cadence, and taking materially less implementation and support effort than the first.
