## The Idea

- **Problem:** Sellers into manufacturing and insurance use contact exports and manual research to find buyers, and a stale list can miss the interval before a buyer selects a supplier ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WhX-clean-real-time-company-data-for-sales-teams-and-their-agents), [EO](https://www.eomag.io/article/clean-pavan-kumar)).
- **Approach:** Clean reads the seller’s website and documents, assembles account timelines from 1,000+ sources such as permits, shipments and filings, then identifies a buying window and the decision-maker with the underlying records attached ([Clean](https://www.tryclean.ai/)).
- **Differentiation:** Clean emphasizes dated operational records, while Apollo offers contact data and sequencing, Clay offers configurable enrichment workflows, 6sense scores digital intent, and Salesmotion emphasizes account monitoring inside CRM ([Clean](https://www.tryclean.ai/), [Clay](https://www.clay.com/blog/series-d), [6sense](https://6sense.com/newsroom/6sense-surpasses-200m-arr-driven-by-sales-intelligence-innovation-and-market-share-expansion/), [Salesmotion](https://salesmotion.io/about)).
- **Business Model:** Clean sells access through demos and reports recurring revenue, with no public pricing tiers or billing unit found ([Clean Contact](https://www.tryclean.ai/contact), [The American Bazaar](https://americanbazaaronline.com/2026/10/21/indian-american-student-tejas-gupta-on-building-clean-490431/)).
- **TAM/SAM:** No public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment ([Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/sales-intelligence-market) projects the broader sales intelligence market at $4.4B in 2026 and 10.1% CAGR through 2033).
- **GTM / Distribution:** The CEO describes personalized outbound and founder-hosted events, and Clean offers demos and API/MCP access for sales-agent builders ([Pavan Kumar on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/pavankumarny), [Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WhX-clean-real-time-company-data-for-sales-teams-and-their-agents)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** Clean reports combining its own datasets with licensed data and linking individual signals to their source records ([Clean](https://www.tryclean.ai/)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Linking dated events to closed deals could improve sector-specific timing models, but Clean has published customer outcomes without evidence of a cross-customer learning loop ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WhX-clean-real-time-company-data-for-sales-teams-and-their-agents)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage.
- **Commoditization risk:** Salesmotion already aggregates filings, news and hiring signals into account intelligence, and Apollo offers AI account research and outreach in the same product ([Salesmotion](https://salesmotion.io/about), [Apollo](https://www.apollo.io/our-story)).

## Key Risks

- **Prediction accuracy:** [Inferred]: Incomplete events can produce mistimed recommendations, and Clean’s aircraft example shows a supplier already selected when outreach began ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/WhX-clean-real-time-company-data-for-sales-teams-and-their-agents)).
- **Data dependency:** [Inferred]: Changes to licensed data access could reduce contact coverage, with source links and contact verification available for checking records ([Clean](https://www.tryclean.ai/)).
- **Bundled competition:** [Inferred]: Sales teams can get account signals inside existing CRM workflows through vendors such as Salesmotion, reducing the need for another interface ([Salesmotion](https://salesmotion.io/about)).
- **Name ambiguity:** Product Hunt lists an unrelated Mac utility called Clean alongside this team’s prior coding IDE, making name-only attribution unreliable ([Mac utility](https://www.producthunt.com/products/clean), [Clean coding IDE](https://www.producthunt.com/products/clean-4)).
