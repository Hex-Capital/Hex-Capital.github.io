## The Idea

- **Problem:** Vehicle builders and defense primes without in-house electrical integration expertise must coordinate separate battery-pack and power-electronics suppliers, which SPM identifies as a source of development delays ([Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/super-powers-mobility-spm)).
- **Approach:** SPM designs modular packs and power electronics in-house so customers can add storage capacity and run external equipment from the same system ([SPM Power Export Kit](https://spm.us/pek), [Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Wdz-spm-batteries-are-the-bottleneck-to-american-energy-dominance)).
- **Differentiation:** SPM offers custom packs, vehicle integration and modular power export in one engagement, overlapping with Inventus Power’s custom packs and chargers, Bren-Tronics’ portable batteries and charging systems, and New Use Energy’s case-based solar and battery stations ([SPM](https://spm.us/), [Inventus Power](https://inventuspower.com/market/military/), [EnerSys](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1289308/000128930824000023/exhibit991-enersysannounce.htm), [New Use Energy](https://newuseenergy.com/)).
- **Business Model:** SPM quotes individual units through fleet orders and offers separately contracted engineering work for custom packs, power systems and vehicles ([SPM How to Buy](https://spm.us/how-to-buy)).
- **TAM/SAM:** Grand View Research estimates the broader global military battery market at $1.6 billion in 2026 with 4.3% CAGR through 2033 ([Grand View Research, 2026](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/military-battery-market-report)); no public TAM/SAM data found for this specific segment.
- **GTM / Distribution:** SPM says direct battery and power-system sales began in August 2026, its YC profile dates the pivot to September 2026, and its launch describes selling to primes while pursuing prime-contractor status in 2027 ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Wdz-spm-batteries-are-the-bottleneck-to-american-energy-dominance), [Y Combinator](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/super-powers-mobility-spm)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** SPM reports a patented pack-design method and in-house pack and power-electronics integration; the launch post supplies no patent number ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Wdz-spm-batteries-are-the-bottleneck-to-american-energy-dominance)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Customer-specific integration and validation could create switching costs if designs move into repeat production; public disclosures cover contract participation and bookings ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Wdz-spm-batteries-are-the-bottleneck-to-american-energy-dominance)).
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier identified at this stage; Inventus Power already designs and manufactures complete military battery and power systems ([Inventus Power](https://inventuspower.com/market/military/)).
- **Commoditization risk:** [Inferred]: Existing pack makers can compete for pack-only orders using their existing design, manufacturing and testing capabilities; the effort required to match SPM’s vehicle integration is unknown ([Inventus Power](https://inventuspower.com/custom-battery-packs/), [SPM](https://spm.us/custom-development)).

## Key Risks

- **Prime-contractor dependence:** [Inferred]: Subcontract sales depend on primes winning programs and placing supplier orders, while SPM’s direct prime-contractor route was targeted for 2027 ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Wdz-spm-batteries-are-the-bottleneck-to-american-energy-dominance)).
- **Cell sourcing:** [Inferred]: SPM buys cells externally, so evolving defense sourcing rules could narrow eligible supply; SPM says its pack designs can accommodate different cells ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Wdz-spm-batteries-are-the-bottleneck-to-american-energy-dominance), [10 U.S.C. § 4865](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t10/s4865/)).
- **Production scale-up:** [Inferred]: Moving from low-volume builds to automated production adds delivery risk; SPM says it is expanding Pittsburgh capacity and reported bookings into February 2027 ([Launch YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/Wdz-spm-batteries-are-the-bottleneck-to-american-energy-dominance)).
