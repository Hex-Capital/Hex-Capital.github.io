## The Idea

- **Problem:** American hardware, robotics, medical-device, and industrial-equipment companies often wait weeks for PCBs from overseas suppliers, with limited control over quality and supply-chain timing ([Trinity College Dublin, Jun 2026](https://www.tcd.ie/news_events/articles/2026/provenmetal-y-combinator/)).
- **Approach:** ProvenMetal checks parts against the BOM, manages fabrication and assembly through partners, performs AOI/X-ray inspection, and provides a parts, process, and testing record with each order ([ProvenMetal capabilities, Aug 2026](https://provenmetal.com/capabilities)).
- **Differentiation:** ProvenMetal combines a five-day fastest full-board turn and in-KiCad sourcing with managed component procurement and testing, versus MacroFab’s 100-plus-factory network, CircuitHub’s automated 1-to-10,000-unit model, and Sierra Circuits’ one-day fabrication service ([ProvenMetal](https://provenmetal.com/), [MacroFab](https://ir.trinitycap.com/node/8741/pdf), [CircuitHub](https://www.circuithub.com/post/circuithub-series-a), [Sierra Circuits](https://www.protoexpress.com/)).
- **Business Model:** Orders use custom per-project quotes with no expedite fee, a five-day fastest or seven-day standard turn, and a lower-price option for longer schedules; no numerical prices are published ([ProvenMetal, Aug 2026](https://provenmetal.com/)).
- **TAM/SAM:** The North American PCB market was $16.12 billion in 2025 and is forecast to grow at a 5.48% CAGR through 2034, while no public SAM estimate was found for U.S. quick-turn turnkey PCBA ([Fortune Business Insights, Jul 2026](https://www.fortunebusinessinsights.com/printed-circuit-board-market-104947)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** Direct ordering targets drone and defense teams, while a KiCad plugin places BOM sourcing inside engineers’ design workflow and an Altium plugin is planned ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/provenmetal), [ProvenMetal plugins, Aug 2026](https://provenmetal.com/plugins)).

## Defensibility

- **Moat today:** The KiCad 9+ plugin links projects to ProvenMetal accounts, checks live distributor inventory and lead times, and returns sourcing results in about one minute, but no installation or retention figures are public ([ProvenMetal KiCad plugin, Aug 2026](https://provenmetal.com/plugins/kicad)).
- **Future moat:** [Inferred]: Repeated BOM, revision, supplier-performance, and inspection data could improve quoting and create workflow switching costs, but no repeat-customer, data-volume, or proprietary-model evidence is public.
- **Market structure:** No structural barrier is identified because manufacturing is performed at Network PCB, which also accepts direct RFQs for sourcing, DFM, fabrication, assembly, and testing ([ProvenMetal capabilities](https://provenmetal.com/capabilities), [Network PCB](https://networkpcb.com/)).
- **Commoditization risk:** Network PCB advertises three-day turnkey service, Sierra Circuits advertises one-day fabrication, and MacroFab provides digital quoting across more than 100 North American factories ([Network PCB](https://networkpcb.com/value-proposition.html), [Sierra Circuits](https://www.protoexpress.com/), [MacroFab](https://ir.trinitycap.com/node/8741/pdf)).

## Key Risks

- **Partner concentration and disintermediation:** Assembly is performed at Network PCB’s San Jose facility, while Network PCB independently accepts customer RFQs for substantially overlapping services; ProvenMetal’s mitigation is one-hour quoting, program management, and EDA integration ([ProvenMetal capabilities](https://provenmetal.com/capabilities), [Network PCB](https://networkpcb.com/request)).
- **Offering transition:** June 2026 reporting described an AI-enabled benchtop X-ray inspection product, whereas the August capabilities sheet describes turnkey PCBA through Network PCB, so the five early LOIs cannot be publicly tied to the current service configuration ([Patch](https://ie.linkedin.com/company/joinpatch), [ProvenMetal capabilities](https://provenmetal.com/capabilities)).
- **Domestic-origin scope:** Assembly is domestic, but fabrication may use overseas suppliers unless a fully domestic route is selected; ProvenMetal documents the route at order time ([ProvenMetal capabilities](https://provenmetal.com/capabilities)).
- **Turnaround competition:** Network PCB advertises three-day turnkey service and Sierra Circuits advertises one-day fabrication, compared with ProvenMetal’s five-day fastest complete turn; ProvenMetal offers no expedite fee and pre-order ship dates ([Network PCB](https://networkpcb.com/value-proposition.html), [Sierra Circuits](https://www.protoexpress.com/), [ProvenMetal](https://provenmetal.com/)).
- **High-reliability execution:** Aerospace, defense, and medical work depends on the selected partner route and certification scope; ProvenMetal lists AOI, X-ray, AS9100, ISO 13485, ITAR, and NIST 800-171 capabilities with certificates available on request ([ProvenMetal capabilities](https://provenmetal.com/capabilities)).
