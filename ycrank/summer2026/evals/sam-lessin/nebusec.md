# NebuSec -- Sam Lessin Evaluation

NebuSec has credible security researchers. My underwriting starts with the deal: post-money unknown, competing term sheets unknown, amount needed to reach a self-supporting business unknown. YC and Team Ignite backing establish that capital has arrived; they establish neither an auction nor a cheap entry. I use the missing-evidence default for terms. The dossier also supplies no round-funded hypothesis with a deadline and pass/fail metric. Finding vulnerabilities is an operating activity. What specific uncertainty does my check resolve, and why does resolving it dramatically change the business’s value?

The founders are the strongest part of this investment. Published working exploits, team-reported Google vulnerability rewards exceeding $400,000, and Frank Wu’s automated-repair work are receipts beyond academic credentials. More importantly, [PingCAP confirms an investigation completed by October 23, 2026](https://www.pingcap.com/trust-hub/security/ceo-note-october-2026/). That connects vulnerability research to an enterprise’s actual security problem. NebuSec fits my pattern of backing people who translate between systems: here, exploit researchers and engineering organizations that must investigate and remediate vulnerabilities. I give substantial founder credit for demonstrated work on both sides.

The unresolved question is who owns the resulting access. PingCAP’s engagement establishes enterprise use, but the dossier does not establish payment, repeat purchases, or that an owned distribution channel caused the engagement. The 1,700 GitHub stars and 490 LinkedIn followers supply no such conversion evidence either. Published research can build trust; whether that trust produces recurring purchases is still unproven. Likewise, accumulated vulnerability cases could become proprietary infrastructure, but exclusive data rights are explicitly unestablished. This dimension gets its default rather than credit for a dark pool I have imagined.

The product creates a particularly sharp tradeoff. NebuSec reports [88% maximum coverage on 76 known vulnerabilities](https://nebusec.ai/research/nebu-benchmark/), against 80% for Codex Security and 74% for Claude Security. That is useful technical evidence, with vendor-reported and known-bug limitations. It does not establish a durable advantage against the next model release. Semgrep and Aikido already combine overlapping detection and remediation capabilities, while dynamic exploit verification is disabled by default in NebuSec. My AI red flag is relevant: a temporary detection lead offers fragile underwriting. Researcher-led investigation supplies a more tangible source of value, although its labor requirements also matter to margins.

The strongest objection to passing is that enterprise trust may compound before a recurring-revenue dashboard exists. A company facing a real incident selected this team, and the founders have shipped exploits rather than merely promising autonomous defense. That could support an excellent specialist business and eventually a repeatable product. I checked the logic behind my known exceptions: unusually strong paid conversion and retention can outweigh AI concerns, while hard external demand can justify flexibility on price. NebuSec has meaningful external validation, but the dossier does not establish those stronger overrides. Its scan quotes and subscription tiers show a charging mechanism; without receipts, delivery costs, burn, or runway, they leave the cash-economics dimension at default.

I pass on the current evidence. I would happily believe these founders can build a valuable security business; I cannot yet identify the priced experiment my personal capital should finance. Delete the next round and the dossier gives me no way to calculate survival. One verifiable flip condition would reopen the decision: a documented sub-$10 million post-money financing package funding a six-month test of five enterprise customers buying repeat scans at positive contribution margin, with sufficient runway to reach that result. That would connect the team’s demonstrated capability to an actual venture moment.
