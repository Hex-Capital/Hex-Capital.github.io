# Halmos Labs -- AGI Evaluation

**Action:** Watch  
**Evidence confidence:** Low

### Company case

Physical Validation connects AI research software with external labs that quote work by project. The supported industry mechanism is cheaper access to physical validation. A plausible seed-stage scenario is that a small R&D team defers a study because specialist scoping time and lab fees together exceed its budget. AI-assisted scoping could reduce staff time and avoid unnecessary evaluations. Even with access to equally capable models, the buyer still needs a lab to perform the work and deliver a usable result. (The Idea)

Halmos reports that its earlier Halmos Bio workflow needed 30% fewer evaluations on average to match the best result in repeated bioprocessing simulations. That supports the proposed cost lever at an early stage. The memo gives no measured wet-lab savings or evidence that a buyer crossed an affordability threshold. The building-block path scores zero because the memo identifies no AI buildout workload constrained by scarce lab capacity and gives no evidence that demand growth would outpace alternative supply. (The Idea, Market & Traction, Key Risks)

Halmos controls catalogue redistribution and reviews supplier corrections, giving it some control over the sourcing workflow. A food-tech team reportedly revised its experiment plan after a Halmos Bio collaboration, an early sign of buyer engagement with experiment design. The Physical Validation launch and the founders’ relevant modeling experience support seed-stage execution credit. The memo records a future readout for its demonstrations and gives no paid Physical Validation deployments, platform fees, or delivery costs from which to estimate contribution per completed project. (Defensibility, Market & Traction, Founders & Team, Key Facts)

### Countercase

Physical Validation could make existing lab procurement easier while serving the same buyers and uses. Science Exchange already combines supplier relationships and procurement with an AI assistant. External labs control execution, and their fees and any rework could absorb the savings from better scoping. The early simulation result supports a testable mechanism, while the unresolved cost and adoption gap warrants Watch. (The Idea, Defensibility, Market & Traction, Key Risks)
