# Reason Machines -- Elad Gil Evaluation

Coding agents are moving from interactive assistance to delegated execution; engineering teams now buy completed work, and the controlling variable is accepted pull requests per hour of human supervision. Reason Machines addresses that transition directly. Its dossier identifies interruptions, environment management, testing, and review as the remaining manual work. The causal thesis is credible: removing those bottlenecks expands how much engineering one person can supervise. What remains unproven is whether Reason removes enough supervision to become the preferred operating layer when established coding products offer similar delegation.

The economic evidence is narrower than the market thesis. Reason’s October 2026 benchmark reports **22% lower cost per solved task than Codex and 10% below Pi**, using GPT-6 Astra. That is a measurable improvement, supported by disclosed methodology and job records, but it remains a company-run comparison. It does not establish lower cost per customer-accepted production change. At **$50 or $200 per person monthly**, inference savings could improve Reason’s economics or customer value; the dossier does not establish which captures the benefit. I give credit for a quantified technical improvement without extrapolating it into a margin transformation.

The strongest argument for investing is that the product already extends beyond generating code. A persistent controller, separate execution machines, testing, and pull-request delivery form an operational loop. Better models should improve completion inside that architecture. The demand evidence also deserves attention: on **October 22, 2026**, the founder reported **10,000 users and 300 active customer Slack channels over the preceding 14 days**. Three hundred active channels could indicate teams integrating Reason into daily work rather than merely trying a demo. The reporting does not distinguish onboarding conversations from recurring delegated work, so I treat it as meaningful early interest rather than established product-market fit.

The competitive structure makes that distinction decisive. Cognition already offers shared agent context and external agents; Factory offers model choice and controlled deployment. Reason therefore enters a category with emerging leaders, rather than an empty coordination layer. Its cache-preserving harness may be useful engineering, but the harness builds on Pi and the messaging engine is permissively licensed. Those facts do not eliminate a business; they put more weight on workflow ownership and distribution. Saved environments and reusable skills could create switching costs, but the dossier labels that retention mechanism as inferred. I cannot underwrite it as an existing moat.

Adi Singh’s prior IDE company, Dereference, supplies relevant startup learning. The founders’ reported history of building and living together across four cities, followed by leaving university to build Ara, supplies concrete commitment. Those facts strengthen the team assessment independently of school or employer names. My pattern of backing multiple layers of a technology transition supports taking this team seriously, but coverage across a wave is not sufficient justification for a personal check. The known exceptions for frontier research or scarce model-building talent do not apply on the evidence provided. Conversely, Reason’s documented execution workflow keeps it out of the simplest thin-wrapper rejection, and there is no documented fundraising-status or founder-liquidity red flag.

I would monitor Reason Machines and keep my money uncommitted today. The product is substantive, the founders have relevant learning, and the launch response could become a distribution advantage. The unresolved issue is whether independent coordination retains value as bundled agents improve. **My flip condition is a verifiable eight-week cohort of ten paying engineering teams in which at least seven repeatedly merge Reason-generated pull requests with fewer human intervention minutes per accepted change than their previous coding-agent workflow.** That would connect early interest to the single economic variable that matters.
