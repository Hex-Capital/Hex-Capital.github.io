## The Idea

- **Problem:** Fleet depots rely on technicians for routine maintenance, with oil changes competing for scarce labor and delaying vehicle availability ([YC, accessed Oct 2026](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/rodeo-robotics)).
- **Approach:** Robots perform maintenance with human assistance while teleoperation and instrumented hand tools collect training demonstrations ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/rodeo-robotics)).
- **Differentiation:** Rodeo starts with oil changes, whereas PitPro automates tire changes and UVeye automates inspections ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/rodeo-robotics); [PitPro](https://www.pitproauto.com/); [UVeye](https://uveye.com/about/)).
- **Business Model:** [Inferred]: Charging fleets for maintenance services fits the described human-assisted deployment strategy; public pricing: No public data found ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/rodeo-robotics)).
- **TAM/SAM:** The broader US automotive aftermarket is forecast at $599.7 billion in 2026, growing 5.4% that year, but includes parts and services beyond Rodeo’s depot segment; segment-specific SAM: No public data found ([Auto Care Association, June 2026](https://www.autocare.org/news/latest-news/details/2026/06/11/new-report--5.4--growth-expected-for-total-auto-care-industry-in-2026--reaching--676.5-billion-in-2029)).
- **GTM / Distribution:** [Inferred]: Direct sales to centralized fleet depots follow the stated deployment focus; Website not accessible at time of research, after two access attempts on October 27, 2026 ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/rodeo-robotics)).

## Key Risks

- **Technical feasibility:** [Inferred]: Maintenance policies may fail to transfer across varied vehicle hardware; Rodeo acknowledges this variation and retains human intervention as mitigation ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/rodeo-robotics)).
- **Human-service economics:** [Inferred]: Technician assistance could limit labor savings if intervention remains frequent; Rodeo describes increasing automation to improve margins, without published intervention rates ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/rodeo-robotics)).
- **Service-information reliability:** [Inferred]: Fragmented, unreliable service information could complicate correct task execution across models ([YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/rodeo-robotics)).
- **Name confusion:** An unrelated Breck School robotics team also uses “Rodeo Robotics,” creating a source-attribution risk; its school affiliation distinguishes it from the startup ([Breck Robotics](https://breckrobotics.org/rodeo)).
